Cù Liū Bái Cài (Vinegar-Glazed Napa Cabbage) 醋溜白菜

▾

Surprise me with

Cu liu bai cai, napa cabbage flash-fried with vinegar and dried chilies, is a classic of Beijing and northern home cooking, where cabbage is the staple winter vegetable.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 6 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

  • 600 g1.3 lbNapa cabbage stems, sliced
  • 45 ml3 tbspChinkiang vinegar
  • 6 g1/4 ozDried chilies
  • 2 g1 tspSichuan peppercorns
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 10 g2 tspSugar
  • 15 ml1 tbspCornstarch slurry
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Cut the cabbage stems into 4 cm pieces, separating the leafy tops; pat dry.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok over high heat and fry the peppercorns and chilies for 15 seconds.
  3. Add the cabbage stems and stir-fry 2 minutes until they begin to turn translucent.
  4. Add the soy sauce, vinegar and sugar and toss for 30 seconds.
  5. Add the slurry, stir until glossy and serve at once.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking makes the cabbage limp.
  • Adding the vinegar too early loses its aroma.

Authentic tips

  • Use very high heat so the cabbage keeps its crunch.
  • The dish should be sour, slightly sweet and a little peppery.

Substitutions

  • Savoy or green cabbage replaces napa cabbage.
  • Reduce the chilies for a milder dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; good cold.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or buns
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
