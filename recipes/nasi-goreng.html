Nasi Goreng

▾

Surprise me with

Nasi goreng ("fried rice") is often called Indonesia's national dish, distinguished from other Asian fried rices by its use of kecap manis (sweet soy sauce) and shrimp paste, traditionally made with day-old rice to avoid a mushy result.

Method

  1. Pound the shallots, garlic, chilies and shrimp paste into a coarse paste. Break up the cold rice so there are no clumps.
  2. Heat the oil in a flat-bottom wok over medium heat and fry the paste for 2 minutes until fragrant and darker; the toasted shrimp paste gives nasi goreng its savory depth.
  3. Raise the heat to high, add the chicken and stir-fry for 2 to 3 minutes until cooked, then the shrimp for 1 minute.
  4. Add the rice and toss for 2 to 3 minutes, pressing out clumps, until hot. Add the kecap manis, soy sauce, salt and pepper and toss for 2 minutes more, until every grain is evenly brown and slightly smoky.
  5. Fry the eggs sunny-side up with crisp edges. Serve the rice topped with an egg, with cucumber, tomato, krupuk and fried shallots.

Common mistakes

  • Using freshly cooked, warm rice, which clumps and turns mushy when fried — day-old, cold rice fries far better.
  • Adding too much kecap manis at once without tasting — it's sweeter and thicker than regular soy sauce.

Authentic tips

  • Spreading freshly cooked rice on a tray and refrigerating for an hour is the single biggest factor in good fried rice texture.
  • A fried egg with a runny yolk on top, broken into the rice at the table, is considered essential by most Indonesian cooks.

Substitutions

  • No kecap manis — mix regular soy sauce with a little brown sugar or molasses to approximate the sweetness and thickness.
  • Beef, or just vegetables for a vegetarian version (omitting shrimp paste), both work well.

Storage

Leftovers: Reheats well in a hot pan; avoid the microwave, which makes the rice gummy.

Freezing: Freezes reasonably well for up to a month; reheat in a hot pan rather than thawing slowly.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Fried egg, pickles and prawn crackers
