Steamed Pork with Chopped Chili 剁椒蒸肉

▾

Surprise me with

This vivid, fiery Hunan steaming technique piles a thick layer of fermented chopped chili over sliced pork, letting the steam carry the chili's aroma deep into the meat — a technique also used with chicken and fish across the region.

Method

  1. Arrange sliced pork in a single layer on a heatproof plate.
  2. Mix red and green chopped chili with garlic, ginger, soy sauce, and wine.
  3. Spread the chili mixture evenly over the pork.
  4. Steam over high heat, covered, 15–18 minutes until the pork is cooked through.
  5. Drizzle with sesame oil before serving.

Common mistakes

  • Steaming at too low a heat, which extends cooking time and leaves the pork tougher.
  • Slicing the pork too thick, which prevents even, quick cooking under the chili topping.

Authentic tips

  • Slicing the pork thin ensures fast, even cooking under the piled-on chili topping.
  • Both red and green duo jiao are usually sold together specifically for this style of dish.

Substitutions

  • Fresh minced chilies with rice vinegar approximate duo jiao.
  • Pork loin replaces shoulder for a leaner, slightly less tender result.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat by re-steaming for 8 minutes.

Freezing: Not recommended.
