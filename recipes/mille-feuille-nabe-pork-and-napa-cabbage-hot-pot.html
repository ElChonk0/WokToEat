Mille-Feuille Nabe (Pork and Napa Cabbage Hot Pot) ミルフィーユ鍋

▾

Surprise me with

Mille-feuille nabe, layers of napa cabbage and pork belly packed upright in a pot so they look like pastry layers, is a modern Japanese home hot pot popular since the 2010s.

  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Layer the napa cabbage leaves and pork belly slices alternately: cabbage, pork, cabbage, pork, finishing with cabbage, in a stack of about four layers.
  2. Cut the stack crosswise into 5 cm (2 in) blocks and pack them upright, cut sides facing up, tightly around a shallow Dutch oven or donabe so they form a spiral. Tuck the shiitake and ginger into the center.
  3. Mix the dashi, sake, soy sauce, mirin and salt and pour it over the layers.
  4. Cover and bring to a boil over medium heat, then simmer for 15 to 20 minutes, until the cabbage is soft and the pork is cooked through. The pork fat and cabbage juices enrich the broth as it cooks.
  5. Bring the pot to the table and serve with ponzu for dipping. Add udon or rice to the leftover broth to finish.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Packing loosely makes the layers fall over.
  • Overboiling turns the cabbage to mush.

Authentic tips

  • Finish with udon or rice and egg in the broth (zosui).
  • A portable burner at the table keeps it warm.

Substitutions

  • Thinly sliced beef replaces pork.
  • Add perilla or shiso leaves between layers.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: This is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea or sake
