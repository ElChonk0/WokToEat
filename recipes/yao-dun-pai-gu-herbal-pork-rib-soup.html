Yào Dùn Pái Gǔ (Taiwanese Herbal Pork Rib Soup) 藥燉排骨

Yao dun pai gu, pork ribs in a Chinese-herb broth with angelica, licorice and wolfberry, is a Taiwanese night-market and winter restorative soup.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 90 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Taiwan Pork LunchDinnerBraise / stewSoup

  • 800 g1.75 lbPork ribs
  • 30 g1 ozHerbal packet (angelica, licorice, dang shen)
  • 10 g1 tbspGoji berries
  • 60 ml4 tbspRice wine
  • 1.5 L6 cupsWater
  • 8 g1.5 tspSalt
  • 30 g2 tbspGinger

Method

  1. Cover the ribs with cold water, boil 3 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  2. Return to a clean pot with the herb packet, ginger and 1.5 L water and bring to a boil; skim.
  3. Cover and simmer gently for 75 minutes until the ribs are tender.
  4. Add the rice wine and goji for the last 5 minutes.
  5. Season with the salt.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard makes the broth cloudy.
  • Too much herb packet makes the soup bitter.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with a dipping sauce of soy sauce and chili.
  • Herbal packets are sold at Chinese markets.

Substitutions

  • Pork neck bones replace ribs.
  • Pre-mixed herbal packets are sold at Chinese markets.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; the broth improves.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Warm ginger tea
