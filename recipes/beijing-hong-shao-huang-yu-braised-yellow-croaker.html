Hóng Shāo Huáng Yú Beijing Style (Beijing Braised Yellow Croaker) 北京红烧黄鱼

▾

Surprise me with

Beijing kitchens use soy-braising for fish in the same style as for meats, producing a deep brown, savory sauce that goes well with steamed bread or rice.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Medium
  • Draft Beijing Seafood LunchDinnerBraise / stew30 min or less

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 800 g1.75 lbYellow croaker, cleaned
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspDark soy sauce
  • 15 g1 tbspSweet bean sauce
  • 15 g1 tbspSugar
  • 50 g4 tbspGinger, garlic and scallion
  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Pat the croaker dry and fry in the oil over medium-high heat 2 minutes per side until golden.
  2. Add the ginger, garlic, scallion and sweet bean sauce and fry 30 seconds.
  3. Add the soy sauces, sugar, wine and 250 ml water and bring to a boil.
  4. Cover and simmer 8 minutes, then uncover and reduce the sauce for 3 minutes.

Common mistakes

  • Turning the fish too soon tears the skin.
  • Too much dark soy sauce makes it bitter.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with steamed buns.
  • Use mackerel if croaker is unavailable.

Substitutions

  • Cod steaks or whole sea bream replace the croaker.
  • Reduce the sweet bean sauce for a lighter sauce.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat gently.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
