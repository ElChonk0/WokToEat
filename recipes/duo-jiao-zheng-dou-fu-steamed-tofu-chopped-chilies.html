Duò Jiāo Zhēng Dòu Fu (Steamed Tofu with Chopped Chilies) 剁椒蒸豆腐

▾

Surprise me with

The chopped chili topping made famous on Hunan fish heads works just as well on silken tofu, a simpler vegetarian dish.

  • Prep 5 min
  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 500 g1.1 lbSoft tofu, sliced
  • 80 g5 tbspSalted chopped chilies (duo jiao)
  • 30 g2 tbspGarlic and ginger
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 30 g2 tbspScallion
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Drain the soft tofu on paper towels for 10 minutes, then slice into 1.5 cm slabs and arrange on a plate.
  2. Spread the chopped chilies, garlic and ginger over the tofu.
  3. Steam over high heat for 8-10 minutes.
  4. Pour off any liquid, drizzle with the soy sauce and scatter over the scallion.
  5. Pour hot oil over the scallion to sizzle.

Common mistakes

  • Adding salt: the chilies are already salty.
  • Oversteaming makes the tofu spongy.

Authentic tips

  • Fresh red chilies add a brighter color.
  • Silken tofu steams more delicately but breaks easily.

Substitutions

  • Firm tofu holds its shape better.
  • Eggplant slices replace the tofu.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
