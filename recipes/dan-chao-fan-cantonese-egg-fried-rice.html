Dàn Chǎo Fàn (Cantonese Egg Fried Rice) 蛋炒饭

▾

Surprise me with

Egg fried rice is a Cantonese staple of day-old rice stir-fried over high heat with beaten egg and green onion, judged by whether each grain is separate, dry and lightly coated in egg.

Method

  1. Break up the rice with wet hands so no clumps remain. Day-old rice, or rice cooked with slightly less water and fluffed while hot, keeps the grains firm and separate.
  2. Cook the carrot, peas and corn with a splash of water in the microwave for 2 minutes, or blanch them for 1 minute, and drain them very well; wet vegetables make soggy rice. Beat the eggs with their salt.
  3. Heat a flat-bottom wok over high heat until it barely smokes, lower to medium-low and add 2 tablespoons of oil. When it shimmers, pour in the eggs and stir gently for 20 to 30 seconds until soft and silky but still wet.
  4. Add the rice straight on top and keep scooping from the bottom and turning for about 2 minutes, pressing out clumps, so the soft egg coats the grains.
  5. Raise the heat to high, add the vegetables and stir-fry 2 to 3 minutes, until the rice is hot and you hear it crackle.
  6. Lower the heat, pour the soy sauces over the rice and add the salt, sugar and pepper, then turn the heat back to high and toss 1 to 2 minutes until evenly seasoned. Add the last tablespoon of oil, the green onion and sesame oil, toss for 30 seconds and serve.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Using fresh, warm rice gives mushy fried rice; use day-old or chilled rice.
  • Too low a heat steams the rice instead of searing it.

Authentic tips

  • Add the egg to the rice first for the classic "golden coat" variation.
  • Cook in two batches in a home wok.

Substitutions

  • Cauliflower rice replaces rice for a low-carb version.
  • Add shrimp or char siu to make a main dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator; reheat in a hot pan.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Blanched choy sum with oyster sauce
  • Soup: Clear winter melon soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot Chinese tea
