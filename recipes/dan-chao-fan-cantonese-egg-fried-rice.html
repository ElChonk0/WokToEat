Dan Chao Fan (Cantonese Egg Fried Rice) 蛋炒饭

Egg fried rice is a Cantonese staple of day-old rice stir-fried over high heat with beaten egg and green onion, judged by whether each grain is separate, dry and lightly coated in egg.

  • Cook 10 min

  • 5 ml1 tspSesame oil
  • 100 g3.5 ozPeas, frozen

Method

  1. Break up the cold rice with your hands so there are no clumps.
  2. Beat the eggs with a pinch of the salt.
  3. Heat 30 ml of the oil in a wok over very high heat until smoking, pour in the eggs, and scramble for 20 seconds until barely set, then lift out.
  4. Add the remaining oil, then the rice, and toss for 3 minutes, pressing and flipping, until the grains are hot and begin to jump in the wok.
  5. Return the egg, add the peas, remaining salt, white pepper and soy sauce, and toss for 1 minute.
  6. Finish with the green onion and sesame oil, and serve at once.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Using fresh, warm rice gives mushy fried rice; use day-old or chilled rice.
  • Too low a heat steams the rice instead of searing it.

Authentic tips

  • Add the egg to the rice first for the classic "golden coat" variation.
  • Cook in two batches in a home wok.

Substitutions

  • Cauliflower rice replaces rice for a low-carb version.
  • Add shrimp or char siu to make a main dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator; reheat in a hot pan.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Blanched choy sum with oyster sauce
  • Soup: Clear winter melon soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot Chinese tea
