Sān Bēi Xuě Yú (Three Cup Cod) 三杯鱈魚

▾

Surprise me with

Three cup, equal parts sesame oil, soy sauce and rice wine with basil, is a Taiwanese braising method best known for chicken; it also works with firm fish.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 500 g1 lb 2 ozCod loin, in 5 cm pieces
  • 45 ml3 tbspToasted sesame oil
  • 40 g1.5 ozGinger, sliced thick
  • 8 cloves8 clovesGarlic cloves, whole
  • 45 ml3 tbspSoy sauce
  • 45 ml3 tbspRice wine (mijiu)
  • 10 g2 tspRock sugar
  • 15 g1 tbspRed chilies, sliced
  • 30 g1 ozFresh Thai basil

Method

  1. Pat the cod dry and sear it in 1 tablespoon of the sesame oil in a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat for 2 minutes per side, until golden. Lift it out; it finishes in the sauce.
  2. Add the remaining sesame oil over medium-low heat and fry the ginger slices for 3 to 4 minutes until they curl and brown at the edges. Add the garlic and fry for 2 minutes until golden. Low heat keeps the sesame oil from turning bitter.
  3. Add the rock sugar (or sugar) and let it melt for 30 seconds, then pour in the soy sauce and rice wine. The three 'cups' are sesame oil, soy sauce and rice wine.
  4. Return the cod and simmer gently for 3 to 4 minutes, spooning the sauce over, until the fish flakes and the sauce is glossy. Shake the pan rather than stirring so the fish stays whole.
  5. Turn off the heat, toss in the basil and chilies, cover for 30 seconds so the basil wilts in the steam, and serve with rice.

Cook’s notes

Written for cod loin.

Common mistakes

  • Basil added early blackens and loses its aroma.
  • Using high heat from the start burns the sesame oil.

Authentic tips

  • Use toasted sesame oil for the right fragrance.
  • A small Dutch oven works in place of a clay pot on any stove.

Substitutions

  • Haddock or black cod replaces the cod.
  • Tofu replaces the fish for a vegetarian version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
