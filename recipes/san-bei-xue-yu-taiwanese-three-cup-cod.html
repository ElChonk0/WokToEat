Sān Bēi Xuě Yú (Three Cup Cod) 三杯鱈魚

Three cup, equal parts sesame oil, soy sauce and rice wine with basil, is a Taiwanese braising method best known for chicken; it also works with firm fish.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 500 g1 lb 2 ozCod loin, in 5 cm pieces
  • 45 ml3 tbspToasted sesame oil
  • 40 g1.5 ozGinger, sliced thick
  • 8 cloves8 clovesGarlic cloves, whole
  • 45 ml3 tbspSoy sauce
  • 45 ml3 tbspRice wine (mijiu)
  • 10 g2 tspRock sugar
  • 15 g1 tbspRed chilies, sliced
  • 30 g1 ozFresh Thai basil

Method

  1. Pat the cod dry. Heat the sesame oil in a small Dutch oven or heavy pan over medium heat and fry the ginger and garlic for 3 minutes until golden.
  2. Add the cod and sear 1 minute per side without breaking it.
  3. Add the soy sauce, rice wine, sugar and chilies, cover, and simmer on medium-low for 6 minutes.
  4. Uncover, increase the heat to medium-high, and spoon the sauce over the fish for 2 minutes until syrupy.
  5. Stir in the basil off the heat and serve at once.

Cook’s notes

Written for cod loin.

Common mistakes

  • Basil added early blackens and loses its aroma.
  • Using high heat from the start burns the sesame oil.

Authentic tips

  • Use toasted sesame oil for the right fragrance.
  • A small Dutch oven works in place of a clay pot on any stove.

Substitutions

  • Haddock or black cod replaces the cod.
  • Tofu replaces the fish for a vegetarian version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
