Vegetarian Curry Laksa

▾

Surprise me with

Curry laksa, noodles in a spicy coconut broth, is a Peranakan and Malay classic; the vegetarian version uses tofu puffs and vegetables.

  • 300 g10 ozRice noodles
  • 400 ml1.75 cupsCoconut milk
  • 90 g6 tbspLaksa paste (vegetarian)
  • 150 g5 ozTofu puffs
  • 100 g3.5 ozBean sprouts
  • 500 ml2 cupsVegetable stock
  • 22Boiled egg
  • 15 g1/2 ozLaksa leaves

Method

  1. Fry the laksa paste in a little oil for 3 minutes until fragrant.
  2. Add the stock and coconut milk and simmer gently for 10 minutes.
  3. Add the tofu puffs and simmer 5 minutes.
  4. Blanch the noodles for 2 minutes and divide among bowls.
  5. Pour over the broth and top with sprouts, egg halves and laksa leaves.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard splits the coconut milk.
  • Overcooked noodles go soft.

Authentic tips

  • Check the laksa paste for shrimp paste.
  • Add sambal at the table.

Substitutions

  • Check the laksa paste for shrimp paste.
  • Tofu puffs can be pan-fried first.

Storage

Leftovers: Store noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 3 days.

Freezing: Freeze the broth for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sambal
  • Soup: Not needed; the noodle soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
