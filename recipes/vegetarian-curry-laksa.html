Vegetarian Curry Laksa

▾

Surprise me with

Curry laksa, noodles in a spicy coconut broth, is a Peranakan and Malay classic; the vegetarian version uses tofu puffs and vegetables.

Method

  1. Blend the chilies, shallots, garlic, candlenuts, lemongrass, galangal, turmeric and coriander into a smooth paste with a little of the oil.
  2. Fry the paste in the remaining oil in a Dutch oven over medium-low heat for 10 to 15 minutes, stirring, until it is dark and fragrant and the oil separates. Without shrimp paste or dried shrimp, a long, thorough fry is what gives the broth its depth.
  3. Add the stock and tofu puffs and simmer for 10 minutes, so the tofu soaks up the broth.
  4. Stir in the coconut milk, soy sauce, sugar and salt and bring to a gentle simmer; do not let it boil hard.
  5. Cook the noodles, blanch the bean sprouts and divide among bowls. Ladle the hot laksa over and top with the eggs and laksa leaves, with lime on the side.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard splits the coconut milk.
  • Overcooked noodles go soft.

Authentic tips

  • Check the laksa paste for shrimp paste.
  • Add sambal at the table.

Substitutions

  • Check the laksa paste for shrimp paste.
  • Tofu puffs can be pan-fried first.

Storage

Leftovers: Store noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 3 days.

Freezing: Freeze the broth for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sambal
  • Soup: Not needed; the noodle soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
