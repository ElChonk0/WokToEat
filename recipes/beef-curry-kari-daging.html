Beef Curry (Kari Daging)

A Malaysian home-style beef curry reflecting the country's Indian and Malay culinary fusion, kari daging simmers beef in a fragrant spice paste and coconut milk until tender — a staple at Malay family gatherings.

  • Cook 1.5 hr

Method

  1. Mix curry powder with a little water to form a paste.
  2. Fry shallot, garlic, ginger, cinnamon, and star anise in oil until fragrant.
  3. Add the curry paste and fry 2–3 minutes until deeply aromatic.
  4. Add beef and stir to coat, cooking 3–4 minutes.
  5. Add coconut milk, bring to a simmer, cover, and braise 1 to 1.25 hours until the beef is nearly tender.
  6. Add potatoes and continue simmering 20–25 minutes until tender.

Common mistakes

  • Rushing the spice-frying step, which is essential for developing the curry's full aroma before liquid is added.
  • Boiling the curry hard, which can cause the coconut milk to separate into an oily texture.

Authentic tips

  • Toasting the curry paste in oil until the oil separates slightly is a key step for deep flavor.
  • This curry, like many braises, tastes even better the next day.

Substitutions

  • Short rib adds richness.
  • A pressure cooker cuts the braise to 45 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; better the next day.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months without the potatoes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or roti canai
  • Soup: Acar
  • Dessert: Ais kacang
  • Beverage: Teh tarik
