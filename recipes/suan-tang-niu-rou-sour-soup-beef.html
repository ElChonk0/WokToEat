Suān Tāng Niú Ròu (Sour Soup Beef Hot Pot) 酸汤牛肉

▾

Surprise me with

Sour soup, made with fermented tomatoes and chilies, is a specialty of the Miao and Dong peoples of Guizhou and Yunnan, used to cook beef and fish.

  • Cook 30 min

  • 600 g1.3 lbBeef, thin slices
  • 400 g14 ozTomatoes
  • 60 g2 ozPickled chilies
  • 40 g3 tbspGinger and garlic
  • 1 L4 cupsStock
  • 8 g1.5 tspSalt
  • 20 g2/3 ozCilantro

Method

  1. Heat a little oil in a pot over medium heat and cook the tomatoes, pickled chilies, ginger and garlic for 10 minutes until they collapse into a saucy base.
  2. Add the stock and salt, bring to a boil and simmer 10 minutes.
  3. Slice the beef very thin against the grain.
  4. Bring the broth to a boil and cook the beef in batches for 30-45 seconds until just cooked.
  5. Serve in the pot with cilantro.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the beef toughens it.
  • Skipping the tomato cooking-down gives a thin, raw-tasting broth.

Authentic tips

  • Freeze the beef for 30 minutes first to make it easier to slice thin.
  • Add tofu or greens for a fuller pot.

Substitutions

  • Canned tomatoes plus a splash of rice vinegar approximate fermented tomato soup.
  • Lamb or fish also work in the sour broth.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; use leftover broth for noodles.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; the pot broth serves as soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
