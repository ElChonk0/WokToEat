Kuay Teow Neua Toon (Thai Stewed Beef Noodle Soup) ก๋วยเตี๋ยวเนื้อตุ๋น

Stewed beef noodles, brisket simmered with Chinese spices and served in a clear broth, are a Thai-Chinese noodle shop staple of Bangkok.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 2.5 hr
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 350 g12 ozRice noodles, fresh
  • 500 g1.1 lbBeef brisket
  • 200 g7 ozDaikon
  • 15 g1/2 ozCinnamon, star anise, cilantro root
  • 45 ml3 tbspSoy sauce
  • 200 g7 ozBean sprouts and Chinese broccoli
  • 15 g1/2 ozFried garlic
  • 2 L8 cupsWater

Method

  1. Blanch the brisket in boiling water for 5 minutes; drain and rinse.
  2. Simmer in 2 L water with the spices, cilantro root and soy sauce for 2 hours until tender.
  3. Add the daikon for the last 30 minutes.
  4. Slice the beef; blanch the noodles and vegetables.
  5. Assemble in bowls, ladle on the broth and top with fried garlic.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the blanch clouds the broth.
  • Boiling hard toughens the brisket.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with a dip of chili, vinegar and fish sauce.
  • Make ahead for a deeper flavor.

Substitutions

  • Short rib or chuck replace brisket.
  • A pressure cooker cuts the simmer to 50 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Store noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 5 days.

Freezing: Freeze the broth and beef for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Chili vinegar and fish sauce
  • Soup: Not needed; the noodle soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
