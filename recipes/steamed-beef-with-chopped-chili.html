Steamed Beef with Chopped Chili 剁椒蒸牛肉

▾

Surprise me with

Applying Hunan's signature steaming technique to beef, this dish piles fermented chopped chili over thin beef slices, letting the steam carry the chili's aroma into the meat for a vivid, fiery result.

Method

  1. Marinate beef slices in soy sauce, wine, and cornstarch for 10 minutes.
  2. Arrange beef in a single layer on a heatproof plate.
  3. Mix red and green chopped chili with garlic and ginger; spread evenly over the beef.
  4. Steam over high heat, covered, 10–12 minutes until the beef is just cooked through.
  5. Drizzle with sesame oil before serving.

Common mistakes

  • Steaming for too long, which toughens the beef given how thin and quick-cooking it is.
  • Slicing the beef too thick, which prevents even, quick cooking under the chili topping.

Authentic tips

  • Slicing the beef thin and against the grain ensures fast, even cooking and a tender bite.
  • Both red and green duo jiao are usually sold together specifically for this style of dish.

Substitutions

  • Fresh minced chilies with rice vinegar approximate duo jiao.
  • Pork or chicken can be prepared the same way.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; re-steam briefly for 5 minutes.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Light egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Dragon fruit
  • Beverage: Iced green tea
