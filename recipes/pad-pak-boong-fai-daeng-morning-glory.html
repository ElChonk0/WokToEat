Pad Pak Boong Fai Daeng (Stir-Fried Morning Glory) ผัดผักบุ้งไฟแดง

Pad pak boong fai daeng, "morning glory on a red fire," is a Thai street-stall favorite, the wok flaring as the vegetable is tossed with garlic and chili.

Method

  1. Wash the water spinach and drain well. Put it in a bowl with the garlic, chilies, soybean paste, soy sauce, mushroom sauce, sugar and water, so everything goes into the wok at once.
  2. Heat the oil in a flat-bottom wok over the highest heat until it smokes. Fai daeng means 'red fire'; at street stalls the oil flares up into the wok.
  3. Tip the whole bowl in at once and toss rapidly with tongs or a spatula.
  4. Stir-fry for 45 to 60 seconds, just until the leaves wilt and the stems are still crunchy.
  5. Serve right away with rice; the stems lose their crunch if it sits.

Common mistakes

  • Wet greens spatter and steam.
  • Overcooking makes it limp.

Authentic tips

  • The flare-up is part of the show on gas; on electric or induction get the flat-bottom wok very hot and toss the sauce in at the end for a similar char.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • Spinach or pea shoots replace water spinach.
  • Add oyster sauce for a non-vegetarian version.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Soup: Tom yum
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
