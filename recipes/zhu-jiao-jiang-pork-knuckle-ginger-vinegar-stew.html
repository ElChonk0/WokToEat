Zhū Jiǎo Jiāng (Pork Knuckle in Ginger and Black Vinegar) 猪脚姜

Pork trotters and eggs stewed with old ginger in sweet black vinegar is the essential Cantonese confinement dish for new mothers, also shared with visitors to celebrate a birth.

  • Cook 3 hr

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 1.5 kg3.3 lbPork knuckle or trotters, chopped into pieces
  • 500 g1.1 lbOld ginger, peeled and smashed
  • 30 ml2 tbspSesame oil
  • 1 L4 cupsSweetened black vinegar (Chinese)
  • 120 ml1/2 cupRice vinegar
  • 60 g1/4 cupBrown slab sugar or brown sugar
  • 66Hard-boiled eggs, peeled

Method

  1. Dry-fry the ginger in a Dutch oven over medium heat for 10 minutes until it shrinks and smells spicy, then add the sesame oil and fry 5 minutes more.
  2. Blanch the pork in boiling water for 5 minutes, drain and rinse; scrape off any hairs.
  3. Add the black vinegar, rice vinegar and sugar to the ginger and bring to a boil, then simmer 1 hour. Do not use an aluminium or cast-iron pot without enamel.
  4. Add the pork and simmer covered for 1.5 to 2 hours until the skin is very soft and gelatinous.
  5. Add the eggs for the last 30 minutes. It is best rested overnight and reheated.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Bare metal pots react with the vinegar; use enamel, stainless or a clay pot.
  • Young ginger lacks the heat this dish needs; use mature, fibrous ginger.

Authentic tips

  • The vinegar stock is often kept and reheated over days, adding more eggs.
  • Pat Chun is the classic Hong Kong sweet vinegar brand for this dish.

Substitutions

  • Pork hocks or ham hocks work; trim extra fat.
  • A pressure cooker cooks the pork in 45 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps a week in the refrigerator and improves.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months without the eggs.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Dried scallop and chicken soup
  • Dessert: Red bean soup
  • Beverage: Warm water
