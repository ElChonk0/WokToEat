Jiǔ Cài Chǎo Dàn (Scrambled Eggs with Garlic Chives) 韭菜炒蛋

▾

Surprise me with

Eggs scrambled with garlic chives is one of the simplest and most common home dishes across southern China, made in minutes for a family meal.

  • Cook 5 min

  • 66Eggs
  • 220 g8 ozGarlic chives, cut into 2 cm (3/4 in) lengths
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt
  • 4 g1 tspSugar
  • 0.5 g1/4 tspGround white pepper
  • 5 ml1 tspRice vinegar
  • 3 g1 tspPotato starch or cornstarch
  • 10 ml2 tspWater
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Soak the chives in water for 3 to 4 minutes, rinse, trim the ends and cut them into short lengths. Crack the eggs one at a time into a bowl and add the salt, sugar, pepper and vinegar. Dissolve the starch in the water and stir it in, then beat until bubbles form. The starch keeps the eggs tender and stops them weeping.
  2. Heat a flat-bottom wok over high heat until it smokes, lower to low and add 1 teaspoon of oil. Add the chives, raise to medium and stir-fry for about 1 minute until fragrant and just wilted. Fold them into the eggs.
  3. Wipe the wok clean so no bits stick, then reheat it over high heat until smoking and add 2 tablespoons of oil. Re-beat the eggs and pour them in.
  4. Stir for about 15 seconds until the bottom sets, then turn the eggs over in large pieces and cook 40 seconds. Drizzle the last of the oil around the edge, stir for 10 seconds and turn off the heat.
  5. Plate immediately, while the eggs are still soft; they keep cooking from their own heat.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the chives turns them stringy and dull.
  • Stirring constantly gives small, dry curds.

Authentic tips

  • Plenty of oil is what gives the eggs their fluffy, golden edges.
  • Yellow chives make a delicate, more expensive version.

Substitutions

  • Green onions or tomatoes replace chives.
  • Add a handful of dried shrimp for a non-vegetarian version.

Storage

Leftovers: Best eaten at once; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Seaweed and egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
