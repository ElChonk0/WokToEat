Jiǔ Cài Chǎo Dàn (Scrambled Eggs with Garlic Chives) 韭菜炒蛋

▾

Surprise me with

Eggs scrambled with garlic chives is one of the simplest and most common home dishes across southern China, made in minutes for a family meal.

  • Prep 5 min
  • Cook 5 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 88Eggs
  • 200 g7 ozGarlic chives, cut into 3 cm lengths
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt
  • 1 g1/2 tspGround white pepper
  • 10 ml2 tspShaoxing wine
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 10 ml2 tspLight soy sauce

Method

  1. Beat the eggs with the salt, pepper and wine.
  2. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a flat-bottom wok over high heat and stir-fry the chives for 30 seconds until bright green. Tip them into the eggs.
  3. Heat the remaining oil over medium-high heat until shimmering.
  4. Pour in the egg mixture, let it set for 5 seconds, then push and fold in big curds for about 1 minute until just set but still moist.
  5. Drizzle with the soy sauce and serve.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the chives turns them stringy and dull.
  • Stirring constantly gives small, dry curds.

Authentic tips

  • Plenty of oil is what gives the eggs their fluffy, golden edges.
  • Yellow chives make a delicate, more expensive version.

Substitutions

  • Green onions or tomatoes replace chives.
  • Add a handful of dried shrimp for a non-vegetarian version.

Storage

Leftovers: Best eaten at once; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Seaweed and egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
