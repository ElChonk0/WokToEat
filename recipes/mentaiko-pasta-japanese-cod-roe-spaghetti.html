Mentaiko Pasta (Japanese Cod Roe Spaghetti) 明太子パスタ

▾

Surprise me with

Mentaiko pasta is a Japanese wafu pasta of spaghetti tossed in a creamy sauce of spicy pollock roe, butter and soy sauce, topped with shredded nori and shiso.

Method

  1. Boil the spaghetti in well-salted water until al dente, and keep 60 ml of the cooking water.
  2. Melt the butter in a large pan over low heat, add the garlic and cook for 30 seconds without browning.
  3. Add the cream and soy sauce and warm for 1 minute, then remove from the heat.
  4. Stir in three-quarters of the mentaiko and the pasta water, then add the hot spaghetti and toss for 1 minute until coated.
  5. Divide onto plates, top with the remaining mentaiko, the nori, shiso and pepper.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking the mentaiko over heat makes it dry and grainy; the pasta heat is enough.
  • Skipping the pasta water gives a thick, claggy sauce.

Authentic tips

  • Mix the mentaiko with a spoon of mayonnaise for a creamier sauce.
  • Pollock roe is usually sold salted; taste before adding soy sauce.

Substitutions

  • Salted cod roe (tarako) replaces mentaiko.
  • Cream cheese replaces cream.

Storage

Leftovers: Best eaten at once; reheat gently with a splash of milk.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Green salad with ponzu dressing
  • Soup: Miso soup
  • Dessert: Purin
  • Beverage: Iced green tea
