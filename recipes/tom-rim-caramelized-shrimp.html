Tôm Rim (Caramelized Shrimp)

▾

Surprise me with

Tom rim, shrimp simmered in caramel, fish sauce and black pepper until sticky, is a Vietnamese home staple eaten with rice.

  • Cook 12 min

  • 700 g1.5 lbShrimp, shell on (or peeled), deveined
  • 30 ml2 tbspFish sauce
  • 25 g2 tbspSugar
  • 15 ml1 tbspNeutral oil
  • 40 g1.5 ozShallots and garlic, minced
  • 60 ml1/4 cupCoconut water or water
  • 2 g1 tspBlack pepper
  • 30 g1 ozGreen onion, sliced

Method

  1. Toss the shrimp with half the fish sauce and leave for 10 minutes. Pat dry.
  2. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium heat and fry the shallots and garlic for 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Add the shrimp and cook for 1 to 2 minutes, turning, until they turn pink.
  4. Add the sugar, remaining fish sauce and coconut water and simmer over medium heat for 5 to 7 minutes, turning, until the liquid reduces to a thick, sticky caramel that coats the shrimp. Shell-on shrimp absorb the caramel into the shells, which many people eat.
  5. Finish with a generous amount of black pepper and green onion, and serve with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the caramel makes it bitter.
  • Overcooking the shrimp makes them rubbery.

Authentic tips

  • Pork belly can be braised the same way.
  • Eat with the shells on if small.

Substitutions

  • Pork belly replaces shrimp for ca-rim style.
  • Peeled shrimp cook faster.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and boiled greens
  • Soup: Canh
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
