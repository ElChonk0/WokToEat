Xiāng Xī Jiàng Niú Ròu (Western Hunan Sauced Beef) 湘西酱牛肉

▾

Surprise me with

Beef braised in a soy and spice sauce and sliced cold is a staple of Xiangxi's Tujia and Miao communities, where cattle are raised in the hills.

  • Cook 2 hr

  • 700 g1.5 lbBeef shank
  • 90 ml6 tbspSoy sauce
  • 30 g2 tbspRock sugar
  • 15 g1/2 ozStar anise, cinnamon, Sichuan pepper
  • 10 g1/3 ozDried chilies
  • 60 g4 tbspGinger and scallion
  • 1.2 L5 cupsWater

Method

  1. Cover the shank with cold water, bring to a boil for 5 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  2. Return to the pot with the soy sauce, rock sugar, spices, chilies, ginger, scallion and the water.
  3. Bring to a boil, reduce to a bare simmer, cover and cook 100 minutes until a skewer slides in easily.
  4. Cool the beef in its liquid, then chill for 1 hour.
  5. Slice thinly across the grain and serve with some of the strained braising liquid.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard makes the shank tough and stringy.
  • Slicing warm tears the beef; chill first.

Authentic tips

  • Keep the braising liquid for another batch; it improves each time.
  • Serve cold with chili dip for a typical Xiangxi snack.

Substitutions

  • Brisket replaces shank.
  • A pressure cooker cuts the braise to 50 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated in the braising liquid; slice cold.

Freezing: Freezes well for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or steamed buns
  • Soup: Pickled vegetables
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
