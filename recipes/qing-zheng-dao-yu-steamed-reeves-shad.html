Qīng Zhēng Dāo Yú (Steamed Yangtze Anchovy) 清蒸刀鱼

▾

Surprise me with

Yangtze anchovy (dao yu), a slender spring fish of the Yangtze, is a seasonal prize of the Wuhu region, steamed simply with ginger and scallion.

  • 600 g1.3 lbYangtze anchovy (dao yu), or small silvery fish such as smelt
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 2 g1/3 tspSalt
  • 20 g3/4 ozGinger and green onion, for steaming
  • 20 g3/4 ozGinger and green onion, julienned, for topping
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Score the fish on both sides, rub it with the Shaoxing wine and a pinch of salt and lay it on a heatproof plate over a few slices of ginger and green onion, so steam can circulate underneath.
  2. Steam over vigorously boiling water, covered, for 5 to 6 minutes, until the flesh at the thickest part flakes from the bone. Fish from Chaohu and the Yangtze is prized for its delicate sweetness, so the seasoning is kept light.
  3. Pour off the steaming liquid, which tastes fishy, and discard the cooked aromatics.
  4. Lay fresh julienned ginger and green onion on the fish and pour the soy sauce around it.
  5. Heat the oil until it just smokes and pour it over the aromatics so they sizzle. Serve at once.

Common mistakes

  • Oversteaming dries the delicate flesh.
  • Rough handling breaks the fish apart.

Authentic tips

  • The fish has many fine bones; eat carefully.
  • Use small silvery fish such as sardines if you cannot find Yangtze anchovy.

Substitutions

  • Small sardines replace Yangtze anchovy.
  • Ginger and scallion strips are essential.

Storage

Leftovers: Eat within the hour.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
