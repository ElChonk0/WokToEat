Hủ Tiếu Nam Vang (Phnom Penh-Style Noodle Soup)

▾

Surprise me with

Hu tieu Nam Vang, a clear pork-bone noodle soup with minced pork, shrimp and liver, is a southern Vietnamese breakfast showing Khmer-Chinese influence.

Method

  1. Blanch the bones in boiling water for 5 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  2. Simmer in 2 L water with the daikon and dried squid for 45 minutes; skim and season with the fish sauce.
  3. Cook the noodles in a separate pot of boiling water for 2-3 minutes and divide among bowls.
  4. Poach the pork and shrimp in the broth for 2 minutes.
  5. Add to the noodles, ladle on the broth and top with chives, celery and fried garlic.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking the noodles in the broth makes it cloudy.
  • Overcooking the shrimp makes them rubbery.

Authentic tips

  • Add liver and quail eggs for the traditional bowl.
  • Serve with lime and chilies.

Substitutions

  • Chicken stock replaces pork bones.
  • Fresh rice noodles replace dried.

Storage

Leftovers: Store noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 4 days.

Freezing: Freeze the broth for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Lime wedges and chilies
  • Soup: Not needed; the soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
