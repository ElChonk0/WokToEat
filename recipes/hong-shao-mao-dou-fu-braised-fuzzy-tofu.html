Hóng Shāo Máo Dòu Fu (Braised Fuzzy Tofu) 红烧毛豆腐

▾

Braised mao doufu is the home version of the Huizhou specialty, simmered in a soy-based sauce with chilies and scallion.

Method

  1. Mao doufu is tofu covered in a soft white mold, fermented for a few days in Huizhou's mountain villages. Fry it in the oil in a skillet over medium heat for 2 to 3 minutes per side until golden; the fuzz collapses into a crisp skin.
  2. Push the tofu aside and fry the ginger, garlic and chilies for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the soy sauces, sugar and water and simmer for 5 minutes, turning the tofu once, until the sauce reduces and the tofu turns soft and creamy inside.
  4. Scatter with green onion.
  5. Serve hot. Mao doufu tastes rich and savory, a little like a mild cheese.

Common mistakes

  • Stirring too much breaks the tofu.
  • Too much water makes a soupy sauce.

Authentic tips

  • Pan-fry first so the tofu keeps its shape.
  • Taste before adding salt.

Substitutions

  • Stinky tofu replaces mao doufu.
  • Firm tofu plus fermented bean curd approximates the flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.
