Niú Ròu Hú Luó Bo Shuǐ Jiǎo (Beef and Carrot Dumplings) 牛肉胡萝卜水饺

▾

Surprise me with

Beef dumplings are a staple of Beijing's Hui Muslim community, often paired with carrot or celery for sweetness.

  • Prep 60 min
  • Cook 8 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 200 g7 ozCarrots, grated
  • 30 g2 tbspGinger and scallion
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspSesame oil
  • 5 g1 tspSalt

Method

  1. Squeeze excess water from the grated carrot and mix it with the beef, ginger, scallion, soy sauce, sesame oil and salt; stir in one direction.
  2. Add 2 tablespoons of water gradually to keep the filling juicy.
  3. Place a tablespoon of filling in each wrapper, fold and pleat to seal.
  4. Boil in plenty of water 6-7 minutes until the dumplings float and the filling is cooked through.
  5. Serve with black vinegar and ginger.

Common mistakes

  • Overmixing makes the filling tough.
  • Overfilling splits the dumplings.

Authentic tips

  • Carrot adds sweetness and moisture.
  • Chill the filling before wrapping if it is sticky.

Substitutions

  • Ground lamb replaces beef for a more traditional version.
  • Grated daikon can replace some of the carrot.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; pan-fry leftovers.

Freezing: Freeze uncooked on a tray for 2 months; boil from frozen for 8 minutes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Black vinegar and chili oil dip
  • Soup: Hot and sour soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
