Málà Féi Niú (Sichuan Hot Pot Fatty Beef) 麻辣肥牛

▾

Surprise me with

Hot pot is Chongqing's signature meal; thin-sliced fatty beef is the classic item cooked in the mala broth, here adapted into a pan dish.

  • 45 ml3 tbspBeef tallow or oil

Method

  1. Heat the tallow or oil in a wide pan over medium heat and fry the dried chilies and peppercorns for 20 seconds until fragrant.
  2. Add the doubanjiang, garlic and ginger and cook 1 minute until the oil turns red.
  3. Pour in 300 ml water, add the onion and bring to a simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Add the enoki, then lay the beef in the broth for 40-60 seconds until just turned gray.
  5. Serve straight from the pan with rice; the broth is rich and meant to be spooned over.

Common mistakes

  • Leaving the beef in more than a minute makes the thin slices tough.
  • Using a thick cut gives a chewy, ungainly result; the slices should be paper-thin.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with extra dipping oil of sesame oil and garlic.
  • Choose well-marbled beef such as ribeye or short plate for the most flavor.

Substitutions

  • Thinly sliced lamb takes the same sauce.
  • Pork belly slices make a richer version.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; reheated fatty beef turns greasy.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Blanched greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold plum juice
