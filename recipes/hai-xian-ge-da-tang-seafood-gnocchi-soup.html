Hai Xian Ge Da Tang (Seafood Dough Drop Soup) 海鲜疙瘩汤

Ge da tang, a soup of hand-dropped dough bits, is a Shandong comfort food; the Jiaodong coastal version adds shrimp, clams and fish.

  • Cook 15 min

Method

  1. Mix the flour with the egg and 90 ml water into a shaggy dough, then rub between your fingers into small crumbs.
  2. Bring the fish stock to a boil with the ginger and tomatoes and simmer 5 minutes.
  3. Add the clams and cook 2 minutes until they open, then the shrimp for 1 minute.
  4. Drop in the dough crumbs, stirring, and simmer 5 minutes until tender.
  5. Season with the salt and finish with the sesame oil and scallion.

Common mistakes

  • Dough crumbs that are too big cook unevenly.
  • Overcooking the shrimp makes them tough.

Authentic tips

  • Slightly lumpy dough is traditional.
  • Add the seafood last so it stays tender.

Substitutions

  • Fish fillets replace some of the shrimp.
  • Spinach adds color.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated; the dough drops swell.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed buns
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
