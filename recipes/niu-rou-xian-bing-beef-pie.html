Niú Ròu Xiàn Bǐng (Beijing Beef Pies) 牛肉馅饼

▾

Surprise me with

Beef-filled pan-fried pies are a staple of Beijing's Hui Muslim cooking, sold in shops and eaten for breakfast.

  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 100 g3.5 ozScallions
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 10 ml2 tspSesame oil
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Mix the flour and hot water into a shaggy dough, knead 5 minutes and rest, covered, 30 minutes.
  2. Mix the beef with the scallions, soy sauce and sesame oil, stirring in one direction until sticky.
  3. Divide the dough into 4 pieces, roll each into a 14 cm circle, add a quarter of the filling and gather the edges to seal; flatten gently.
  4. Heat the oil in a pan over medium heat and fry the pies 4-5 minutes per side until golden and cooked through.
  5. Cut into wedges and serve hot.

Common mistakes

  • Overfilling tears the dough.
  • Too much heat browns the outside before the filling cooks.

Authentic tips

  • Keep the pies thin so they cook through.
  • Beef from the neck or shoulder gives the juiciest filling.

Substitutions

  • Ground lamb is a traditional alternative.
  • Chopped cabbage in the filling lightens the pie.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot dry pan.

Freezing: Freeze cooked pies for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Millet porridge
  • Soup: Pickled vegetables
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
