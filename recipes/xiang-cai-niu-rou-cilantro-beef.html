Xiāng Cài Niú Ròu (Yunnan Cilantro Beef) 香菜牛肉

Cilantro is eaten as a vegetable in Yunnan, and stir-frying it with beef is a common home and restaurant dish.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 8 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Yunnan Beef LunchDinnerStir-frySpicy30 min or less

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 450 g1 lbBeef sirloin, sliced
  • 150 g5 ozCilantro, lots
  • 40 g3 tbspGarlic and chilies
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 10 g2 tspCornstarch
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 2 g1 tspPepper

Method

  1. Slice the beef 4 mm thick across the grain and marinate with the soy sauce, cornstarch and pepper for 10 minutes.
  2. Heat half the oil in a wok over high heat until smoking and sear the beef 1 minute; remove.
  3. Add the remaining oil, garlic and chilies for 15 seconds.
  4. Return the beef and toss 30 seconds.
  5. Fold in the cilantro, cut into 5 cm lengths, for 10 seconds just to wilt.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the beef makes it tough.
  • Cooking the cilantro too long turns it dull.

Authentic tips

  • Use the stems; they carry the best flavor.
  • Serve at once.

Substitutions

  • Pork takes the same method.
  • Add sliced celery for crunch.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the cilantro darkens.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
