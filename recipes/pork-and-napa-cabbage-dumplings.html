Pork and Napa Cabbage Dumplings 白菜猪肉饺子

▾

Surprise me with

The most classic dumpling filling in northern China, pork and napa cabbage dumplings are a Lunar New Year staple, believed to bring good fortune when eaten at midnight — a fixture of nearly every northern Chinese household.

Method

  1. Toss the cabbage or vegetables with salt and leave for 15 minutes, then squeeze out as much water as you can; wet filling makes leaky dumplings.
  2. Mix the pork with the ginger, soy sauce, Shaoxing wine and pepper. Add the Sichuan pepper water a tablespoon at a time, stirring in one direction, until it is absorbed and the filling turns sticky and springy. Fold in the vegetables, green onion and sesame oil.
  3. Put a heaping teaspoon of filling in the center of a wrapper, fold it in half and pinch the middle, then pleat or press the edges to seal tightly.
  4. Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil and add 15 to 20 dumplings, stirring so they do not stick. When the water returns to a boil, add 250 ml (1 cup) of cold water; repeat twice more. They are done when they float and look puffed, 6 to 8 minutes.
  5. Lift them out with a slotted spoon and serve with black vinegar, crushed garlic and chili oil. Dumplings (jiaozi) are the Lunar New Year food of northern China.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the salting-and-squeezing step for the cabbage, which leaves the filling watery and hard to wrap.
  • Overfilling the wrappers, which makes sealing difficult and dumplings prone to bursting.

Authentic tips

  • Mixing the filling in one consistent direction helps develop a cohesive, less crumbly texture.
  • Extra dumplings freeze beautifully uncooked — freeze on a tray first, then bag once solid.

Substitutions

  • Ground beef or lamb are traditional northern Chinese alternatives to pork.
  • Chives can replace or supplement green onion for a more traditional flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Cooked dumplings keep 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes excellently uncooked for up to 3 months; cook directly from frozen with a couple extra minutes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Black vinegar and chili oil dip
  • Soup: Hot and sour soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot jasmine tea
