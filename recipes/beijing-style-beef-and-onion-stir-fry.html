Beijing-Style Beef and Onion Stir-Fry 洋葱炒牛肉

▾

Surprise me with

A simple, satisfying Beijing home-style stir-fry pairing tender beef with sweet onion, seasoned lightly with soy sauce — an everyday dish valued for its speed and comforting flavor.

  • 500 g1 lbFlank steak, sliced thin against the grain
  • 22Onion, sliced
  • 3 cloves3 clovesGarlic, sliced
  • 20 ml4 tspSoy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 10 ml2 tspCornstarch
  • 5 g1 tspSugar

Method

  1. Marinate beef in soy sauce and cornstarch for 15 minutes.
  2. Stir-fry beef in hot oil over high heat until just browned; remove and set aside.
  3. Fry garlic briefly, then add onion, stir-frying 2–3 minutes until just starting to soften but still with some bite.
  4. Return beef to the wok with wine and sugar; toss over high heat until combined.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the onion, which loses its pleasant sweetness and crunch.
  • Overcooking the beef, which turns it tough — sear quickly over very high heat.

Authentic tips

  • This dish is intentionally simple — the sweetness of well-cooked onion is the main flavor to showcase.
  • Slicing the beef thinly against the grain is essential for tenderness in this quick stir-fry.

Substitutions

  • Chicken thigh takes the same sauce.
  • A mix of red and yellow onions adds color.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.
