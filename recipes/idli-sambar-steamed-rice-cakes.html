Idli Sambar (Steamed Rice Cakes with Lentil Stew)

▾

Surprise me with

Idli, steamed fermented rice and lentil cakes, are the staple South Indian breakfast, served with sambar and coconut chutney across Tamil Nadu and Karnataka.

  • 400 g14 ozIdli batter
  • 120 g4 ozToor dal
  • 250 g9 ozVegetables (carrot, drumstick, onion)
  • 15 g1/2 ozTamarind
  • 15 g1/2 ozSambar powder
  • 6 g2 tspMustard seeds and curry leaves
  • 120 ml1/2 cupCoconut chutney
  • 15 ml1 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Grease the idli molds and fill with the batter.
  2. Steam over boiling water for 12 minutes until a toothpick comes out clean.
  3. Cook the dal until soft with the vegetables, tamarind and sambar powder and 800 ml water, about 25 minutes.
  4. Temper the mustard seeds and curry leaves in the oil and pour into the sambar.
  5. Serve the idlis with the sambar and chutney.

Common mistakes

  • Opening the steamer early deflates the idli.
  • Too-thin batter gives flat idlis.

Authentic tips

  • Ferment the batter overnight in a warm place.
  • Serve hot.

Substitutions

  • Ready-made batter saves time.
  • Idlis can be steamed in a muffin tin.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; re-steam for 5 minutes.

Freezing: Freeze idlis for 2 months.

Serve with
