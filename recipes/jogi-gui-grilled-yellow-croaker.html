Jogi Gui (Grilled Yellow Croaker) 조기구이

▾

Surprise me with

Salted grilled croaker (jogi gui) is a traditional Korean breakfast and table staple, served with rice and soup.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 600 g1.3 lbSalted croaker
  • 15 ml1 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Rinse the croaker and pat very dry.
  2. Brush the pan or grill with oil.
  3. Cook over medium heat 5-6 minutes per side until the skin is golden.
  4. Serve with lemon.
  5. Eat with rice and soup.

Common mistakes

  • Wet fish sticks.
  • Moving the fish too soon tears the skin.

Authentic tips

  • Salted croaker is sold dried at Korean markets.
  • Use a nonstick pan for easier turning.

Substitutions

  • Small mackerel replaces croaker.
  • A broiler replaces the grill.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and banchan
  • Soup: Doenjang jjigae
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
