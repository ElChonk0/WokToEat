Unagi no Kabayaki (Grilled Eel) 鰻の蒲焼き

▾

Surprise me with

Kabayaki, eel split, grilled and glazed with sweet soy, has been enjoyed in Japan since the Edo period, and eel is traditionally eaten on the hottest days of summer.

Method

  1. Simmer the soy sauce, mirin, sugar and sake for 8 minutes until syrupy.
  2. Pat the eel dry and cut into rectangles.
  3. Grill or broil skin-side down 4 minutes, flip and cook 3 minutes.
  4. Brush with the sauce and grill 1 minute; repeat 2-3 times until glossy.
  5. Slice and serve over the hot rice with sansho pepper.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the sugary sauce; brush only at the end.
  • Overcooking makes the eel dry.

Authentic tips

  • Pre-cooked frozen eel only needs reheating and glazing.
  • Serve with miso soup and pickles.

Substitutions

  • Frozen pre-grilled eel from Asian markets saves effort.
  • Salmon or mackerel takes the same sauce.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat gently.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickles
  • Soup: Clear soup with mitsuba
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot sencha
