Niu Nan Fan (Taiwanese Beef Brisket Rice) 牛腩飯

Braised beef brisket spooned over rice, with greens and a boiled egg, is a Taiwanese lunch-shop staple that brings the flavors of beef noodle soup to a rice bowl.

  • Cook 2 hr

  • 650 g1.4 lbBeef brisket, cubed
  • 30 g2 tbspDoubanjiang
  • 60 ml4 tbspSoy sauce
  • 20 g2 tbspRock sugar
  • 80 g5 tbspStar anise, ginger, scallion
  • 300 g10.5 ozDaikon
  • 500 g1.1 lbCooked rice
  • 1 L4 cupsWater

Method

  1. Cover the brisket with cold water, boil 5 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  2. Fry the doubanjiang and aromatics in a little oil for 1 minute until the oil is red.
  3. Add the brisket, soy sauce, sugar and water, cover and simmer 90 minutes.
  4. Add the daikon and cook 30 minutes more.
  5. Spoon over the rice and serve with greens.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard toughens the beef.
  • Adding daikon too early makes it dissolve.

Authentic tips

  • Better the next day.
  • Add a boiled egg.

Substitutions

  • Short rib replaces brisket.
  • A pressure cooker cuts the braise to 50 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months without the daikon.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Blanched greens
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Oolong tea
