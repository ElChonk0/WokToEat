Pork Momos (Himalayan Steamed Dumplings)

▾

Surprise me with

Momos, steamed dumplings that came to India with Tibetan and Nepali communities, are a street food of Darjeeling, Sikkim and Delhi.

  • Cook 15 min

Method

  1. Mix the pork with the onion, garlic, ginger, cilantro, soy sauce and sesame oil until sticky, adding 3 tablespoons of water.
  2. Place a spoonful in each wrapper and pleat closed.
  3. Oil a steamer and arrange the momos without touching.
  4. Steam over high heat for 12 minutes.
  5. Serve hot with the chili chutney.

Common mistakes

  • Dry filling makes dry momos; add the water.
  • Overfilling splits the wrappers.

Authentic tips

  • Wrappers must be thin; roll them yourself if possible.
  • Fry leftovers.

Substitutions

  • Ground chicken replaces pork.
  • Chopped cabbage lightens the filling.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; re-steam or pan-fry.

Freezing: Freeze uncooked on a tray for 2 months; steam from frozen for 15 minutes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Spicy tomato chutney
  • Soup: Clear broth
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Butter tea
