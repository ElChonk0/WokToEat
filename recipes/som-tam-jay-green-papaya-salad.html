Som Tam Jay (Green Papaya Salad)

▾

Surprise me with

Som tam, pounded green papaya salad, comes from Isaan in northeastern Thailand and Laos; this vegetarian version swaps fish sauce for soy sauce and salt.

  • 400 g14 ozGreen papaya, shredded
  • 100 g3.5 ozCherry tomatoes
  • 80 g3 ozLong beans
  • 30 g1 ozGarlic and chilies
  • 60 ml4 tbspLime juice
  • 20 g4 tspPalm sugar
  • 15 ml1 tbspSoy sauce
  • 40 g1.5 ozRoasted peanuts

Method

  1. Pound the garlic and chilies lightly in a mortar.
  2. Add the long beans and tomatoes and bruise lightly.
  3. Add the lime juice, palm sugar and soy sauce and stir to dissolve.
  4. Add the shredded papaya and pound gently while mixing.
  5. Top with peanuts and serve at once.

Common mistakes

  • Over-pounding makes it mushy.
  • Waiting makes the salad watery.

Authentic tips

  • Adjust the heat to taste.
  • Serve with sticky rice.

Substitutions

  • Shredded green mango or raw carrot replace papaya.
  • Reduce the chilies for a milder dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the salad waters out.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sticky rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
