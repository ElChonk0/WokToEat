Dōng Gū Là Cháng Zhēng Jī (Steamed Chicken with Mushrooms and Chinese Sausage) 冬菇腊肠蒸鸡

▾

Surprise me with

Steaming marinated chicken on a plate with shiitake and lap cheong is everyday Cantonese home cooking: the sausage fat and mushroom juices form a sauce that is spooned over rice.

Method

  1. Soak the shiitake in warm water for 20 minutes and the cloud ear and goji for 10 to 15 minutes, until fully soft. Squeeze the mushrooms dry, trim the stems and halve them; tear the cloud ear into pieces. Partly soaked mushrooms stay leathery after steaming.
  2. Toss the chicken with the chicken wine and half the ginger and leave for 10 minutes to freshen it. Mix the oyster sauce, soy sauce, sugar, salt, pepper, marinade wine and cornstarch, taste it, then stir it into the chicken with the sesame oil, neutral oil, shiitake, remaining ginger and green onion whites. Marinate 30 minutes; much longer makes the meat firm.
  3. Spread the cloud ear on a rimmed heatproof plate, then the chicken in a single layer with a slight dip in the center, so it steams evenly. Lay the sausage slices over the top, where their fat bastes the chicken, and scatter the goji.
  4. Set a rack in a flat-bottom wok or steamer, add water and bring it to a full rolling boil before the plate goes in; starting in cold water throws off the timing. Steam covered over high heat for 15 to 18 minutes, until the juices from the thickest piece run clear and the meat pulls from the bone.
  5. Lift the plate out carefully, scatter with the green onion greens and serve straight from the plate, spooning the juices over rice.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Piling the chicken up causes uneven cooking; use one layer.
  • Letting the steamer run dry; top up with boiling water if needed.

Authentic tips

  • Cook it on top of the rice in the last 20 minutes of a pot of rice for a one-pot version.
  • Chopping through the bone in small pieces is traditional and lets the marrow flavor the sauce.

Substitutions

  • Boneless thighs: steam 12 to 14 minutes.
  • Add a few dried red dates and goji berries for a homely variation.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; re-steam for 8 minutes.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and garlic bok choy
  • Soup: Spinach tofu soup
  • Dessert: Steamed milk pudding
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
