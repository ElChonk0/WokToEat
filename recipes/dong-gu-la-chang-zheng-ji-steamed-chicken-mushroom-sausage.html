Dong Gu La Chang Zheng Ji (Steamed Chicken with Mushrooms and Chinese Sausage) 冬菇腊肠蒸鸡

Steaming marinated chicken on a plate with shiitake and lap cheong is everyday Cantonese home cooking: the sausage fat and mushroom juices form a sauce that is spooned over rice.

  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Mix the chicken with the soy sauce, oyster sauce, wine, sugar and cornstarch and marinate for 20 minutes, then stir in the oil.
  2. Spread the chicken in a single layer on a heatproof plate with a rim, tuck the mushrooms between the pieces and lay the sausage and ginger over the top.
  3. Set up a steamer or a flat-bottom wok with a rack and boiling water. Steam the plate, covered, over high heat for 18 to 20 minutes until the chicken is cooked through to the bone.
  4. Drizzle with sesame oil and scatter with green onion.
  5. Serve straight from the plate with the juices spooned over rice.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Piling the chicken up causes uneven cooking; use one layer.
  • Letting the steamer run dry; top up with boiling water if needed.

Authentic tips

  • Cook it on top of the rice in the last 20 minutes of a pot of rice for a one-pot version.
  • Chopping through the bone in small pieces is traditional and lets the marrow flavor the sauce.

Substitutions

  • Boneless thighs: steam 12 to 14 minutes.
  • Add a few dried red dates and goji berries for a homely variation.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; re-steam for 8 minutes.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and garlic bok choy
  • Soup: Spinach tofu soup
  • Dessert: Steamed milk pudding
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
