Massaman Chicken Curry

▾

Surprise me with

Massaman curry blends Thai technique with spices introduced through historical trade with Persian and Indian merchants — cinnamon, cardamom, and cloves give it a warmer, less fiery profile than other Thai curries.

Method

  1. Simmer the thicker coconut cream with the curry paste until fragrant and the oil separates, 3–4 minutes.
  2. Add chicken and stir to coat, cooking 3–4 minutes.
  3. Add remaining coconut milk, potatoes, and onion; bring to a simmer.
  4. Cover and braise gently 20–25 minutes until the chicken and potatoes are tender.
  5. Stir in peanuts, fish sauce, palm sugar, and tamarind paste; simmer uncovered 5 minutes more to combine flavors.
  6. Taste and adjust seasoning before serving.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling too vigorously once the potatoes are added, which can break them apart before the chicken finishes cooking.
  • Skipping the tamarind, which provides the subtle sourness that balances the dish's richness.

Authentic tips

  • Massaman is meant to be milder and sweeter than other Thai curries — resist adding extra chili if aiming for authenticity.
  • This curry, like many braises, tastes even better the next day once flavors have had time to meld.

Substitutions

  • Bone-in thighs add flavor; simmer 10 minutes longer.
  • Sweet potato replaces some of the potato.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; better the next day.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months without the potatoes.
