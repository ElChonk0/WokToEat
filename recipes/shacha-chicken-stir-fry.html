Shacha Chicken Stir-Fry 沙茶鸡

▾

Surprise me with

Shacha sauce, a savoury paste of dried shrimp, brill fish, garlic and spices, came to Xiamen with returning Southeast Asian Chinese and became southern Fujian’s all-purpose stir-fry sauce.

  • Cook 10 min

  • 45 g3 tbspShacha sauce
  • 11Onion, sliced
  • 11Green bell pepper, sliced
  • 3 cloves3 clovesGarlic, sliced
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 10 g1 tbspCornstarch
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Toss the chicken with soy sauce, wine and cornstarch; rest 10 minutes.
  2. Sear the chicken in half the oil over high heat until browned and just cooked; remove.
  3. Add the remaining oil, the garlic and the shacha sauce and fry 30 seconds until fragrant.
  4. Add onion and pepper and stir-fry 2 minutes, keeping some crunch.
  5. Return the chicken with the sugar and a splash of water and toss until glossy.

Common mistakes

  • Adding shacha to a dry, smoking wok, which burns the dried seafood in it; fry it briefly in oil.

Authentic tips

  • Stir the jar well before measuring: the oil separates and the flavour sits in the solids.

Substitutions

  • Beef or pork take the same sauce.
  • Peanut butter plus a little dried shrimp powder approximates shacha.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot oolong tea
