Hé Lán Dòu Chǎo Xiā Rén (Shrimp and Snow Peas) 荷兰豆炒虾仁

▾

Surprise me with

Velveted shrimp with crisp snow peas in a clear garlic sauce is an everyday Cantonese stir-fry that shows off fresh ingredients.

  • Cook 8 min

Method

  1. Toss the shrimp with the salt and pepper, then the cornstarch. String the snow peas on both sides, soak them in cold water for 5 minutes to crisp them, and dry. Mix the sauce salt, sugar, fish sauce, cornstarch, water and half the wine.
  2. Bring a pot of water to a boil with the blanching salt and a splash of oil. Blanch the celery and carrot for 30 seconds, the snow peas for 30 seconds, then the mushrooms briefly. Drain while still bright green.
  3. Wipe the wok dry and heat it over high heat for 40 seconds, add 1 tablespoon of oil and wait 15 seconds. Stir-fry the shrimp for 40 to 50 seconds, lifting them out the moment they turn opaque; they finish in the sauce.
  4. Add the remaining oil and fry the ginger and garlic for 5 seconds, the green onion for 10 seconds, then the blanched vegetables for 10 seconds. Return the shrimp and splash in the rest of the wine.
  5. Stir the sauce and pour it in, tossing for 10 to 15 seconds until glossy. Turn off the heat, add a dash of sesame oil, toss and serve.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooked snow peas lose their snap; they need only a minute.
  • Soy sauce would muddy the clear, white sauce.

Authentic tips

  • Rinsing the shrimp in salted water, then drying them, gives a crisp bite.
  • Use sugar snap peas when snow peas are out of season.

Substitutions

  • Scallops replace shrimp.
  • Add water chestnut slices for crunch.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Spinach tofu soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
