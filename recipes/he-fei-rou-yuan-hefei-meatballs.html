Héféi Ròu Yuán (Hefei Pork Meatballs) 合肥肉圆

▾

Surprise me with

Fried pork meatballs in a savory soup, sometimes with a boiled egg, are a Hefei and central Anhui specialty and a Spring Festival dish.

  • Cook 25 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Method

  1. Mix the pork with the ginger, soy sauce, Shaoxing wine and salt, then add the ginger-scallion water a little at a time, stirring hard in one direction until absorbed. Add the egg, water chestnuts, green onion and cornstarch and slap the mixture against the bowl until springy.
  2. Shape into walnut-size balls with wet hands.
  3. Heat the oil to 170 C (340 F) and fry the meatballs in batches for 4 to 5 minutes until golden and cooked through. Drain.
  4. Simmer the cabbage in the stock for 5 minutes, then add the meatballs and simmer for 5 minutes more.
  5. Serve the meatballs on the cabbage with a little broth, or eat them straight from the fryer as a snack.

Common mistakes

  • Overmixing makes them dense.
  • Frying at too low a temperature makes them greasy.

Authentic tips

  • Chilling the mixture helps shape the balls.
  • Add a halved boiled egg to each bowl for the traditional version.

Substitutions

  • Ground chicken replaces some pork.
  • Bake the balls at 220 C (425 F) instead of frying.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat in the broth.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; the meatballs sit in broth
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
