Fúlíjí Shāo Jī (Fuliji Roast Chicken) 符离集烧鸡

▾

Surprise me with

Fuliji roast chicken, from a town in northern Anhui, is a famous deli chicken fried and then simmered in spices until tender; it is eaten cold and is considered one of the best-known chicken dishes in China.

  • Cook 90 min

  • 1.4 kg3 lbWhole chicken
  • 60 ml4 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspDark soy sauce
  • 20 g1.5 tbspRock sugar
  • 15 g1/2 ozSpice mix (star anise, fennel, clove, cinnamon)
  • 60 g4 tbspGinger and scallion
  • 30 g2 tbspHoney
  • 400 ml1.75 cupsNeutral oil for frying

Method

  1. Rub the chicken with half the soy sauce and brush the skin with the honey; let it dry 20 minutes.
  2. Heat the oil to 180 C (350 F) in a wok or deep pot and fry the chicken, turning, for 3-4 minutes until the skin is deep golden; drain.
  3. In a pot just large enough, bring 1.5 L water, the remaining soy sauces, rock sugar, spices, ginger and scallion to a boil and simmer 10 minutes.
  4. Lower in the chicken, cover and cook on the lowest heat for 75 minutes until the meat is so tender it nearly falls from the bone.
  5. Cool the chicken in the stock for 30 minutes, lift out carefully and serve at room temperature.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard tears the skin and toughens the meat; keep the stock barely moving.
  • Skipping the frying gives pale skin that does not take on the characteristic color.

Authentic tips

  • Fuliji chicken is traditionally eaten cold, torn from the bone.
  • The stock can be reused as a master sauce.

Substitutions

  • Chicken legs only cook in 60 minutes.
  • Skip the frying and roast at 220 C (425 F) for 15 minutes before braising.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; serve cold, torn from the bone.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed buns, torn chicken eaten by hand
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
