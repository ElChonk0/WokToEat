Suān Nǎi Huáng Guā (Uyghur Yogurt and Cucumber Salad) 酸奶黄瓜

▾

Cool yogurt and cucumber salad cuts through the rich grilled and fried foods of Xinjiang's tables.

Ingredients

  • 300 g1.25 cupsThick plain yogurt (full-fat)
  • 400 g14 ozCucumbers, diced
  • 2 g1/3 tspSalt, for the cucumbers
  • 1 clove1 cloveGarlic, grated
  • 15 g1/2 ozDill, chopped
  • 5 g1 tbspMint (optional)
  • 1 gpinchSalt, for the yogurt
  • 15 ml1 tbspOlive oil

Method

  1. Toss the cucumbers with salt and leave for 10 minutes, then drain off the liquid so the salad does not turn watery.
  2. Whisk the yogurt with the garlic and a pinch of salt.
  3. Fold in the cucumbers, dill and mint.
  4. Chill for 15 minutes.
  5. Drizzle with olive oil and serve cold, alongside polo or kebabs.

Common mistakes

  • Watery cucumber thins the yogurt; drain it.
  • Adding the dressing too early makes it soggy.

Authentic tips

  • Thick strained yogurt works best.
  • Serve with rich meat dishes.

Substitutions

  • Thick Greek yogurt works well.
  • Fresh mint replaces dill.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated; drain before serving.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Alongside grilled skewers or polo
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Hami melon
  • Beverage: Black tea
