Suān Nǎi Huáng Guā (Uyghur Yogurt and Cucumber Salad) 酸奶黄瓜

▾

Cool yogurt and cucumber salad cuts through the rich grilled and fried foods of Xinjiang's tables.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 0 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 300 g1.25 cupsYogurt
  • 400 g14 ozCucumbers, diced
  • 10 g2 tspGarlic
  • 15 g1/2 ozDill
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt
  • 15 ml1 tbspOlive oil

Method

  1. Salt the diced cucumber lightly and drain 10 minutes; pat dry.
  2. Mix the yogurt, garlic and salt.
  3. Fold in the cucumber and dill.
  4. Chill 15 minutes.
  5. Drizzle with olive oil to serve.

Common mistakes

  • Watery cucumber thins the yogurt; drain it.
  • Adding the dressing too early makes it soggy.

Authentic tips

  • Thick strained yogurt works best.
  • Serve with rich meat dishes.

Substitutions

  • Thick Greek yogurt works well.
  • Fresh mint replaces dill.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated; drain before serving.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Alongside grilled skewers or polo
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Hami melon
  • Beverage: Black tea
