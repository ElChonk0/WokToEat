Kongnamul Guk (Bean Sprout Soup) 콩나물국

▾

Surprise me with

Kongnamul guk, a clear soup of soybean sprouts, is a staple Korean breakfast and hangover remedy.

  • Cook 15 min

  • 300 g10 ozSoybean sprouts
  • 1.2 L5 cupsKelp stock
  • 15 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 15 ml1 tbspSoy sauce
  • 5 g1 tspSalt
  • 30 g2 tbspScallion
  • 10 g2 tspChili (optional)

Method

  1. Rinse the sprouts.
  2. Bring the stock to a boil with the garlic.
  3. Add the sprouts, cover and simmer 10 minutes; do not lift the lid at first, which can cause a raw smell.
  4. Season with the soy sauce and salt.
  5. Add the scallion and chili and serve.

Common mistakes

  • Lifting the lid early leaves a raw bean smell.
  • Overcooking makes the sprouts limp.

Authentic tips

  • Often served as a morning-after remedy.
  • Add anchovy for depth.

Substitutions

  • Anchovy stock is traditional.
  • Add chili flakes for a spicy version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
