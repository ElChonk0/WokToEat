Bāo Shāo Yú (Dai Banana Leaf Fish) 芭蕉叶包烧鱼

The Dai people of southern Yunnan wrap fish with herbs and chili in banana leaves and cook it over coals, a technique that infuses the fish with a gentle smokiness.

Method

  1. Pound the lemongrass, garlic, shallot, chilies, herbs, salt and fish sauce to a coarse paste.
  2. Soften the banana leaves by dipping them in boiling water for 30 seconds or laying them briefly in a hot dry skillet.
  3. Coat the fish in the paste and wrap in the leaves, folding in the ends and securing with toothpicks.
  4. Grill over medium heat or bake at 200 C (400 F) for 20 minutes, turning once.
  5. Unwrap at the table and serve with lime.

Common mistakes

  • Unsoftened leaves tear when folded.
  • Too hot a grill burns the leaves before the fish cooks.

Authentic tips

  • Foil works if you cannot find banana leaves, though you lose the aroma.
  • Serve with sticky rice.

Substitutions

  • Foil replaces banana leaves, with less aroma.
  • Snapper or sea bass replaces the fish.

Storage

Leftovers: Eat within the hour.

Freezing: Not recommended.
