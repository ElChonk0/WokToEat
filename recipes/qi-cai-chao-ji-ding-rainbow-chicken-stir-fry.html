Qī Cǎi Chǎo Jī Dīng (Rainbow Chicken Stir-Fry) 七彩炒鸡丁

▾

Surprise me with

Diced chicken stir-fried with many colors of vegetables is everyday Cantonese home cooking, built on the restaurant technique of velveting the meat first.

Method

  1. Mix the chicken with its soy sauce, oyster sauce, wine, salt, sugar, cornstarch and water, squeezing it for 1 to 2 minutes until almost no liquid is left. Cut all the vegetables to the same small dice as the chicken so they cook evenly and look neat. Mix the slurry.
  2. Heat a flat-bottom wok over high heat until it just smokes. Add 2 tablespoons of oil and half the garlic and ginger for 15 seconds, then the chicken. Stir-fry for 1 to 2 minutes, just until it turns white outside; it finishes cooking later. Lift it out.
  3. Add the remaining oil and the rest of the garlic and ginger for 15 seconds. Add the carrot, celery and baby corn and toss for 1 minute, then the peppers and mushrooms for 1 minute more, keeping everything moving so it stays bright and crisp.
  4. Splash the wine around the edge of the wok and add the vegetable oyster sauce, sugar and salt, tossing to coat.
  5. Return the chicken with 2 tablespoons of water, cover and cook for 1 to 2 minutes until the liquid bubbles and the chicken is cooked through.
  6. Uncover, stir the slurry and pour it in, tossing a few times until the sauce turns glossy and clings to every piece. Turn off the heat, drizzle with sesame oil and serve straight away.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Cutting the vegetables different sizes makes them cook unevenly; dice everything like the chicken.
  • Overcrowding the wok stews the chicken.

Authentic tips

  • Blanching the firm vegetables first is the restaurant way to keep the stir-fry quick and bright.
  • On induction, stir-fry in two batches to keep the heat up.

Substitutions

  • Use any mix of colorful vegetables: snow peas, corn, zucchini.
  • Shrimp or pork replace chicken.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.
