Qi Cai Chao Ji Ding (Rainbow Chicken Stir-Fry) 七彩炒鸡丁

Diced chicken stir-fried with many colors of vegetables is everyday Cantonese home cooking, built on the restaurant technique of velveting the meat first.

  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Mix the chicken with the soy sauce, wine and cornstarch, then a teaspoon of oil, and marinate 15 minutes. Mix the oyster sauce, soy sauce, sugar, water and cornstarch.
  2. Blanch the carrot and celery in boiling water for 1 minute and drain.
  3. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a flat-bottom wok over high heat and stir-fry the chicken for 3 to 4 minutes until just cooked. Lift out.
  4. Add the remaining oil and stir-fry the garlic, ginger, mushrooms and peppers for 1 minute, then the blanched vegetables for 1 minute.
  5. Return the chicken, stir the sauce in and toss until glossy, then finish with sesame oil.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Cutting the vegetables different sizes makes them cook unevenly; dice everything like the chicken.
  • Overcrowding the wok stews the chicken.

Authentic tips

  • Blanching the firm vegetables first is the restaurant way to keep the stir-fry quick and bright.
  • On induction, stir-fry in two batches to keep the heat up.

Substitutions

  • Use any mix of colorful vegetables: snow peas, corn, zucchini.
  • Shrimp or pork replace chicken.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.
