Xiāng Shì Chǎo Tǔ Dòu Sī (Hunan Spicy Shredded Potato) 湘式辣椒炒土豆丝

▾

Hunan's version of stir-fried potato shreds emphasizes fresh chilies and a smoky heat rather than vinegar.

Ingredients

  • 600 g1.3 lbPotatoes, julienned
  • 80 g3 ozFresh chilies
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 10 g2 tspFermented black beans
  • 10 ml2 tspLight soy sauce
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Cut the potatoes into fine matchsticks and rinse in several changes of water until clear; drain well.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok over high heat and fry the chilies, garlic and black beans for 20 seconds.
  3. Add the potato and stir-fry over high heat 3 minutes until translucent but crisp.
  4. Add the soy sauce and salt, toss 20 seconds and serve.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the rinse leaves the starch on, so the strips stick and turn gluey.
  • Overcooking turns the potato soft; it should keep a slight crunch.

Authentic tips

  • Cut the strips as evenly as possible.
  • Add a splash of vinegar at the end for brightness.

Substitutions

  • Sweet potato gives a sweeter version.
  • Green pepper adds color.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated; best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Tofu soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
