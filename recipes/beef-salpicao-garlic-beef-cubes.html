Beef Salpicao (Filipino Garlic Beef Cubes)

▾

Surprise me with

Salpicao, beef cubes seared in garlic and olive oil, is a Spanish-influenced Filipino dish popularized in restaurants.

Method

  1. Marinate the beef with the Worcestershire, soy sauce, oyster sauce and pepper for 30 minutes.
  2. Fry the garlic in the olive oil in a skillet over medium-low heat for 4 to 5 minutes, stirring, until golden and crisp. Lift it out with a slotted spoon and keep the oil.
  3. Raise the heat to high and sear the beef in the garlic oil in a single layer for 1 to 2 minutes per side, until browned outside and pink inside. Do it in batches; crowding steams the beef.
  4. Return all the beef with the marinade and butter and toss for 30 seconds until glossy.
  5. Toss in most of the fried garlic and the parsley, season with salt and top with the rest of the garlic. Serve with garlic rice.

Common mistakes

  • Crowding the pan steams the beef.
  • Burning the garlic makes it bitter; add it at the end.

Authentic tips

  • Tenderloin is classic; sirloin is cheaper.
  • Cook it medium-rare for tenderness.

Substitutions

  • Sirloin is cheaper than tenderloin.
  • Mushrooms add earthiness.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; reheat gently.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Garlic rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Red wine or iced tea
