Beef Salpicao (Filipino Garlic Beef Cubes)

▾

Surprise me with

Salpicao, beef cubes seared in garlic and olive oil, is a Spanish-influenced Filipino dish popularized in restaurants.

  • 15 g1/2 ozParsley
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt

Method

  1. Toss the beef cubes with the Worcestershire and soy sauce, pepper and salt; marinate 30 minutes.
  2. Heat the olive oil in a large pan over very high heat until smoking.
  3. Sear the beef in batches for 1-2 minutes, turning, then remove.
  4. Add the garlic and butter to the pan and fry 1 minute until golden.
  5. Return the beef, toss with parsley and serve at once with garlic rice.

Common mistakes

  • Crowding the pan steams the beef.
  • Burning the garlic makes it bitter; add it at the end.

Authentic tips

  • Tenderloin is classic; sirloin is cheaper.
  • Cook it medium-rare for tenderness.

Substitutions

  • Sirloin is cheaper than tenderloin.
  • Mushrooms add earthiness.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; reheat gently.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Garlic rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Red wine or iced tea
