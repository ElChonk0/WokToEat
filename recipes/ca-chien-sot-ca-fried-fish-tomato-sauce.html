Cá Chiên Sốt Cà (Fried Fish in Tomato Sauce)

▾

Surprise me with

Fried fish in a sweet-sour tomato sauce is a Vietnamese home dish eaten with rice and herbs.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 800 g1.75 lbWhole fish or fillets
  • 400 g14 ozTomatoes
  • 30 ml2 tbspFish sauce
  • 10 g2 tspSugar
  • 40 g3 tbspGarlic and shallot
  • 30 g2 tbspScallion
  • 90 ml6 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Pat the fish dry and season with salt.
  2. Fry in the oil over medium-high heat 4 minutes per side until crisp; set aside.
  3. Fry the garlic and shallot 30 seconds, add the tomatoes and cook 6 minutes.
  4. Add the fish sauce and sugar and simmer 8 minutes.
  5. Pour over the fish and top with scallion.

Common mistakes

  • Wet fish spatters and doesn't crisp.
  • Pouring the sauce too early softens the crust.

Authentic tips

  • Serve at once.
  • Add a little tamarind for extra tang.

Substitutions

  • Fillets fry in less time.
  • Canned tomatoes replace fresh.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and herbs
  • Soup: Canh
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
