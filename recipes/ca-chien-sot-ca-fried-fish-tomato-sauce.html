Cá Chiên Sốt Cà (Fried Fish in Tomato Sauce)

▾

Surprise me with

Fried fish in a sweet-sour tomato sauce is a Vietnamese home dish eaten with rice and herbs.

  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 800 g1.75 lbWhole fish (tilapia or snapper) or thick fillets
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt
  • 90 ml6 tbspNeutral oil
  • 33Shallots, minced
  • 3 cloves3 clovesGarlic, minced
  • 400 g14 ozRipe tomatoes, chopped
  • 15 g1 tbspTomato paste
  • 30 ml2 tbspFish sauce
  • 10 g2 tspSugar
  • 120 ml1/2 cupWater
  • 30 g1 ozGreen onion, cut into lengths
  • to tasteto tasteBlack pepper and dill (optional)

Method

  1. Score the fish, rub it with salt and pat very dry so it does not spatter.
  2. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat and fry the fish for 4 to 6 minutes per side, until golden and crisp. Lift it onto a platter.
  3. Pour off all but 2 tablespoons of oil and fry the shallots and garlic for 1 minute, then the tomatoes and tomato paste for 5 to 6 minutes, mashing, until they collapse into a thick sauce.
  4. Add the fish sauce, sugar and water and simmer for 3 minutes until glossy.
  5. Return the fish and spoon the sauce over, simmer for 1 to 2 minutes, then add the green onion and pepper. Serve with rice; an everyday Vietnamese family dish.

Common mistakes

  • Wet fish spatters and doesn't crisp.
  • Pouring the sauce too early softens the crust.

Authentic tips

  • Serve at once.
  • Add a little tamarind for extra tang.

Substitutions

  • Fillets fry in less time.
  • Canned tomatoes replace fresh.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and herbs
  • Soup: Canh
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
