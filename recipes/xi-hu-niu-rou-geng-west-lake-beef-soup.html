Xī Hú Niú Ròu Gēng (West Lake Beef Soup) 西湖牛肉羹

▾

Surprise me with

West Lake beef soup, a thick soup of minced beef, egg white and cilantro, is a fixture of Cantonese banquets and restaurant menus; its thick, egg-ribboned body is typical of Cantonese geng (thickened soups).

Method

  1. Rinse the minced beef in a bowl of water, drain and squeeze it dry, then mix it with the beef cornstarch, salt, sugar, pepper and water until it turns smooth and loose.
  2. Bring a small pot of water to a boil and add the beef, breaking up the clumps with chopsticks for 30 to 40 seconds until it turns pale. Drain it. Par-cooking the beef separately keeps the soup clear instead of gray and scummy.
  3. Bring the stock, water and mushrooms to a boil in a covered pot over high heat. Meanwhile mix the slurry, and beat the egg whites until loose and frothy.
  4. Stir in the beef, then pour in the slurry slowly while stirring, until the soup is thick enough to coat a spoon. It thickens more as it cools, so stop just short of how thick you want it.
  5. Add the tofu and bring back to a gentle simmer. Pour in the egg whites in a thin stream while stirring slowly in one direction, so they set into fine white wisps; do not let the soup boil hard. Skim off any foam.
  6. Turn off the heat and season with the salt, sugar, pepper and sesame oil. Ladle into bowls and scatter with cilantro.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Adding raw beef straight to the soup clouds it with grey scum.
  • Boiling hard after adding the egg whites breaks them into specks.

Authentic tips

  • Hand-mincing the beef with a cleaver gives a better texture than ground beef.
  • A drizzle of Worcestershire sauce at the table is a popular Hong Kong touch.

Substitutions

  • Ground beef works if you blanch it the same way.
  • Use whole eggs instead of whites.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator; reheat gently.

Freezing: Not recommended; the thickened soup turns watery.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Fried rice
