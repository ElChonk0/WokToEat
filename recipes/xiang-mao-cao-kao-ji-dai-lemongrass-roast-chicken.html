Xiāng Máo Cǎo Kǎo Jī (Dai Lemongrass Roast Chicken) 香茅草烤鸡

▾

Surprise me with

Dai cooks in Xishuangbanna stuff chicken with lemongrass, herbs and chilies and roast or grill it, a dish with flavors shared with Thailand and Laos.

  • Prep 20 min (plus 2 hr marinating)
  • Cook 45 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 1.2 kg2.6 lbChicken legs or a small spatchcocked chicken
  • 80 g3 ozLemongrass, minced, plus whole stalks
  • 60 g2 ozGarlic and shallots
  • 55Bird's eye chilies
  • 8 g1.5 tspSalt
  • 15 ml1 tbspNeutral oil
  • 30 g1 ozCilantro and mint
  • 22Lime

Method

  1. Pound the minced lemongrass, garlic, shallots, chilies and salt into a paste and mix in the oil.
  2. Slash the chicken and rub the paste all over and into the cuts. Stuff some whole lemongrass stalks under it and marinate for 2 hours.
  3. Grill the chicken over medium charcoal, or clamp it in a grill basket, turning, for 35 to 45 minutes until charred and cooked through (74 C / 165 F). In the oven, roast at 200 C (400 F) for 40 minutes, then broil to char.
  4. Rest for 5 minutes.
  5. Chop and serve with lime, herbs and sticky rice.

Common mistakes

  • Not slashing the meat gives a flavorful skin but bland flesh.
  • Too little salt in the paste leaves the chicken flat.

Authentic tips

  • Dai cooks grill the chicken over charcoal for extra smoke.
  • Lemongrass should be pounded fine; use only the tender inner part.

Substitutions

  • Chicken thighs cook in 30 minutes at 200 C (400 F).
  • Grill over charcoal for the authentic Dai flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; serve cold or reheat in an oven.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sticky rice in a banana leaf
  • Soup: Sour bamboo shoot soup
  • Dessert: Fresh pineapple
  • Beverage: Chilled lemongrass tea
