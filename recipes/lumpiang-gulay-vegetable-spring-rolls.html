Lumpiang Gulay (Vegetable Spring Rolls)

▾

Surprise me with

Lumpia, spring rolls, came to the Philippines with Hokkien traders; lumpiang gulay is the meatless version.

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a flat-bottom wok and saute the garlic and onion for 2 minutes, then the tofu and sweet potato for 3 minutes.
  2. Add the carrot and green beans for 2 minutes, then the cabbage and bean sprouts for 1 minute. Season with soy sauce, salt and pepper.
  3. Spread the filling on a tray and let it cool and drain completely; wet filling tears the wrappers and makes them soggy.
  4. Put 2 heaping tablespoons of filling near one corner of a wrapper, fold the corner over, fold in the sides and roll up tightly. Seal with water.
  5. Heat the frying oil to 175 C (345 F) and fry the rolls in batches for 3 to 4 minutes until golden and crisp. Drain on a rack and serve with spiced vinegar.

Common mistakes

  • Wet filling makes the wrappers burst.
  • Oil that is too cool makes them greasy.

Authentic tips

  • Cool the filling completely.
  • Make ahead and freeze.

Substitutions

  • Air-fry at 200 C (400 F) for a lighter version.
  • Add shredded carrot and bean sprouts.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; recrisp in a hot oven.

Freezing: Freeze uncooked rolls for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sweet chili sauce
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
