Lumpiang Gulay (Vegetable Spring Rolls)

▾

Surprise me with

Lumpia, spring rolls, came to the Philippines with Hokkien traders; lumpiang gulay is the meatless version.

  • Cook 10 min

  • 1616Lumpia wrappers
  • 400 g14 ozCabbage, carrot, green beans
  • 150 g5 ozFirm tofu, minced
  • 40 g3 tbspGarlic and onion
  • 15 ml1 tbspSoy sauce
  • 60 ml4 tbspSweet chili sauce
  • 300 ml1.25 cupsNeutral oil for frying

Method

  1. Stir-fry the garlic and onion, then the cabbage, carrot, beans and tofu for 4 minutes; season with the soy sauce and cool.
  2. Place 2 tablespoons of filling on each wrapper.
  3. Roll tightly, folding in the sides, and seal with water.
  4. Fry in oil at 170 C (340 F) for 3-4 minutes until golden.
  5. Serve with sweet chili sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Wet filling makes the wrappers burst.
  • Oil that is too cool makes them greasy.

Authentic tips

  • Cool the filling completely.
  • Make ahead and freeze.

Substitutions

  • Air-fry at 200 C (400 F) for a lighter version.
  • Add shredded carrot and bean sprouts.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; recrisp in a hot oven.

Freezing: Freeze uncooked rolls for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sweet chili sauce
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
