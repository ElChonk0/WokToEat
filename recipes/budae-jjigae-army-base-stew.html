Budae Jjigae (Army Base Stew) 부대찌개

Budae jjigae, a stew of sausage, Spam, kimchi and instant noodles, arose in the 1950s after the Korean War when cooks used surplus U.S. army rations.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 25 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 200 g7 ozSpam or luncheon meat
  • 200 g7 ozSausages
  • 200 g7 ozKimchi
  • 200 g7 ozTofu
  • 22Instant ramen
  • 45 g3 tbspGochugaru and gochujang
  • 100 g3.5 ozBaked beans
  • 1 L4 cupsStock

Method

  1. Cut the Spam, sausages and tofu into slices.
  2. Arrange the kimchi, Spam, sausages, tofu and beans in a wide pot.
  3. Mix the gochugaru and gochujang with a ladle of stock and spoon over the center.
  4. Pour in the rest of the stock, bring to a boil and simmer 12 minutes.
  5. Add the ramen noodles for the last 3-4 minutes and serve straight from the pot.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooked noodles go soft.
  • Too much stock makes the stew thin.

Authentic tips

  • A slice of American cheese is a traditional add-on.
  • Use the ramen seasoning sparingly; the dish is salty already.

Substitutions

  • Hot dogs replace sausages.
  • Leave out the instant ramen seasoning to control the salt.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the noodles soften.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; the stew is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
