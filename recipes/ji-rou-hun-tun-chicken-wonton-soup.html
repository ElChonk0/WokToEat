Jī Ròu Hún Tún (Shanghai Chicken Wonton Soup) 鸡肉馄饨

▾

Surprise me with

Wontons are a Shanghai breakfast and snack staple; chicken wontons are a lighter alternative to pork in a clear broth.

  • 4040Wonton wrappers (thin, Shanghai style)
  • 400 g14 ozChicken thighs, minced
  • 10 g2 tspGinger, grated
  • 30 g1 ozGreen onion, minced
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 10 ml2 tspShaoxing wine
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt
  • 3 g1/2 tspSugar
  • 1 g1/2 tspWhite pepper
  • 45 ml3 tbspWater, for the filling
  • 10 ml2 tspSesame oil
  • 1 L4 cupsChicken stock
  • 15 g1/2 ozDried laver seaweed and tiny dried shrimp, for the broth
  • 11Egg, made into a thin omelet and shredded
  • 15 g1/2 ozGreen onion, sliced

Method

  1. Mix the chicken with the ginger, soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, salt, sugar and pepper. Add the water a tablespoon at a time, stirring in one direction until absorbed and the filling is sticky; this keeps chicken wontons juicy. Stir in the green onion and sesame oil.
  2. Put a teaspoon of filling near one corner of a wrapper, roll it up loosely, then bring the ends together and pinch to seal, so the wonton looks like a little cap.
  3. Put a pinch of laver, dried shrimp, white pepper and a drop of sesame oil in each bowl. Bring the stock to a boil.
  4. Boil the wontons in plenty of water for 3 to 4 minutes, until they float and the filling is cooked.
  5. Ladle the boiling stock into the bowls so the laver softens, add the wontons, and top with shredded egg and green onion.

Common mistakes

  • Overfilling splits the wrappers.
  • Cooking the wontons in the soup clouds the broth with starch.

Authentic tips

  • Whisk the filling in one direction for a bouncy texture.
  • Serve with a few drops of black vinegar and chili oil.

Substitutions

  • Ground pork or shrimp replaces the chicken.
  • Shepherd's purse or spinach makes a vegetable filling.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated; store wontons and broth separately.

Freezing: Freeze uncooked wontons on a tray for 2 months; boil from frozen for 5 minutes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pan-fried buns
  • Soup: Not needed; the wonton soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Orange slices
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
