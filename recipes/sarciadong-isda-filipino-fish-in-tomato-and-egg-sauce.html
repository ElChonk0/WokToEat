Sarciadong Isda (Fish in Tomato and Egg Sauce)

▾

Surprise me with

Sarciado is a Filipino home dish of fried fish simmered in a tomato sauce, with beaten egg stirred in at the end to make it rich.

Method

  1. Season the trout with the salt, pat dry, and dust with the flour.
  2. Heat the oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat and fry the fish 2 minutes per side until golden; lift out.
  3. In the same pan sauté the onion and garlic for 3 minutes, then add the tomatoes and cook 5 minutes until soft.
  4. Add the water, fish sauce and pepper and simmer 3 minutes.
  5. Return the fish, pour the beaten eggs over the sauce, let set for 30 seconds, then fold gently; serve with scallions and rice.

Cook’s notes

Written for rainbow trout fillets.

Common mistakes

  • Stirring the eggs too fast makes them break into small bits; fold slowly.
  • Soft tomatoes make a watery sauce; cook them down.

Authentic tips

  • Ripe tomatoes make the best sauce.
  • Trout is delicate, so fold it carefully.

Substitutions

  • Cod loin or haddock replaces the trout.
  • Canned tomatoes replace fresh.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Calamansi juice
