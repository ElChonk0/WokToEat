Yuān Yāng Chǎo Fàn (Yin Yang Fried Rice) 鸳鸯炒饭

▾

Surprise me with

Yin yang fried rice, egg fried rice divided by a white cream sauce on one side and a red tomato sauce on the other, is a Hong Kong banquet and restaurant showpiece.

Method

  1. Mix the egg yolks into the cold rice with your hands, so every grain is coated and the fried rice comes out evenly yellow. Mix the chicken with its salt, cornstarch and water. Mix the ketchup, sugar, vinegar, red-sauce salt and stock in one bowl, and divide the cornstarch and water into two slurries.
  2. Heat a flat-bottom wok over high heat for 1 minute, add 1 tablespoon of oil and fry the rice for about 3 minutes, pressing and tossing until the grains are separate and golden. Season with the rice salt, toss 30 seconds, then add the green onion. Press the rice level on a large platter.
  3. For the red side, reheat the wok with 1 tablespoon of oil and stir-fry the chicken for 30 to 40 seconds, then add half the onion and the tomato for 40 seconds. Pour in the red sauce, lower the heat and stir in one slurry slowly until thick and glossy. Cook 1 minute and transfer to a bowl.
  4. For the white side, wipe out the wok, heat 1 tablespoon of oil over low heat and cook the shrimp for 20 seconds with the rest of the onion. Add the white-sauce stock and salt, bring to a simmer and stir in the second slurry until thick.
  5. Pour the egg whites into the white sauce in a thin stream without stirring for 20 to 30 seconds, so they set into soft white clouds, then fold gently. Turn off the heat and add the peas and the last of the oil.
  6. Lay a strip of foil in an S-shape across the rice as a divider, spoon the red sauce on one side and the white sauce on the other, then lift the foil away. Serve at once.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Sauces that are too thin run together; thicken them well.
  • Boiling after the egg white goes in makes it rubbery.

Authentic tips

  • Use a spatula held upright down the middle to keep the sauces apart as you pour.
  • The plain rice should be lightly seasoned so the sauces lead.

Substitutions

  • All shrimp or all chicken for both sides.
  • Crab meat in the white sauce for a luxurious version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Garlic gai lan
  • Soup: Chicken and sweetcorn soup
  • Dessert: Mango pudding
  • Beverage: Iced lemon tea
