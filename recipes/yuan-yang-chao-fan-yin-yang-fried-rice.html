Yuān Yāng Chǎo Fàn (Yin Yang Fried Rice) 鸳鸯炒饭

▾

Surprise me with

Yin yang fried rice, egg fried rice divided by a white cream sauce on one side and a red tomato sauce on the other, is a Hong Kong banquet and restaurant showpiece.

Method

  1. Make egg fried rice: heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a flat-bottom wok over high heat, add the eggs then the rice, toss 4 minutes with the salt and green onion. Mound it on a platter.
  2. Toss the chicken and shrimp with the cornstarch. Stir-fry the chicken in 1 tablespoon of oil for 3 minutes, lift out, then the shrimp for 1 minute.
  3. Red sauce: simmer the ketchup, sugar and water, add the chicken and peas, and thicken with half the cornstarch mixed with water.
  4. White sauce: bring the stock to a simmer, add the shrimp, thicken with the remaining cornstarch, then stir in the egg white off the heat so it forms white strands.
  5. Pour the red sauce over one half of the rice and the white sauce over the other, divided by a line of green onion.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Sauces that are too thin run together; thicken them well.
  • Boiling after the egg white goes in makes it rubbery.

Authentic tips

  • Use a spatula held upright down the middle to keep the sauces apart as you pour.
  • The plain rice should be lightly seasoned so the sauces lead.

Substitutions

  • All shrimp or all chicken for both sides.
  • Crab meat in the white sauce for a luxurious version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Garlic gai lan
  • Soup: Chicken and sweetcorn soup
  • Dessert: Mango pudding
  • Beverage: Iced lemon tea
