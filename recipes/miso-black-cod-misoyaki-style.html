Miso Black Cod (Misoyaki-Style)

▾

Surprise me with

A signature Japanese preparation, misoyaki marinates rich, buttery black cod in a sweetened white miso paste for a day or two before broiling — the sugar in the marinade caramelizes into a deeply lacquered, almost custardy crust.

  • Prep 15 min (plus 2-3 days marinating)
  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 700g1.5 lbBlack cod (sablefish) fillets, skin-on
  • 150g2/3 cupWhite miso (saikyo miso)
  • 60ml1/4 cupMirin
  • 60ml1/4 cupSake
  • 45g3 tbspSugar

Method

  1. Bring the sake and mirin to a boil for 20 seconds to cook off the alcohol, then turn the heat to low and whisk in the miso until smooth. Add the sugar and stir until dissolved. Cool completely.
  2. Pat the fish dry, lightly salt it for 30 minutes, then pat dry again; this firms the flesh. Coat the fillets all over with the miso marinade.
  3. Cover and refrigerate for 2 to 3 days. The long cure is what seasons the rich, oily fish all the way through.
  4. Heat the oven to 200 C (400 F) and set a rack under the broiler. Wipe off the excess marinade with your fingers; any left behind will burn.
  5. Sear the fillets skin-side down in a lightly oiled ovenproof skillet over high heat until browned, then roast for 10 to 12 minutes until the fish flakes.
  6. Finish under the broiler for 1 to 2 minutes until the top caramelizes in dark spots. Serve with pickled ginger shoots (hajikami).

Common mistakes

  • Not scraping off excess miso before broiling: the sugar in the marinade burns quickly under a broiler, so wipe most of it away before the fish goes under the heat.

Authentic tips

  • Saikyo miso (sweet, pale) is the traditional choice and worth seeking out over regular white miso for the most authentic result.

Substitutions

  • No mirin: a mix of sake and a touch more sugar approximates it.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days; reheat gently to avoid drying out.

Freezing: The marinated raw fish freezes well for up to a month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and pickled ginger
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot sencha
