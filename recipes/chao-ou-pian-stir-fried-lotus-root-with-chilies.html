Chao Ou Pian (Stir-Fried Lotus Root with Chilies) 炒藕片

Hunan's lakes yield abundant lotus root, which is sliced and stir-fried with chilies and vinegar for a crunchy, tangy dish.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 8 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Hunan Tofu & Vegetarian LunchDinnerStir-frySpicyVegetarianVegan30 min or less

Ingredients

  • 500 g1.1 lbLotus root, sliced
  • 60 g2 ozFresh red and green chilies
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 15 ml1 tbspChinkiang vinegar
  • 10 ml2 tspLight soy sauce
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Peel the lotus root and slice into 3 mm rounds; soak in cold water with a splash of vinegar for 10 minutes to stop browning, then drain.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok over high heat and fry the garlic and chilies for 15 seconds.
  3. Add the lotus root and stir-fry 3-4 minutes until crisp-tender.
  4. Add the vinegar, soy sauce and salt and toss 30 seconds.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking makes lotus root soft and loses its crunch.
  • Skipping the soak leaves it brown and starchy.

Authentic tips

  • Rinse off the starch for a clean, crisp bite.
  • Jicama can replace lotus root.

Substitutions

  • Jicama replaces lotus root.
  • Water chestnuts add similar crunch.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; good cold.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Tofu soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
