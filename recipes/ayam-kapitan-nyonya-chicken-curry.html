Ayam Kapitan (Nyonya Chicken Curry)

Ayam kapitan, a mild Nyonya (Peranakan) coconut curry with a dry spice paste, is a Penang and Malacca specialty.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 40 min
  • Difficulty Medium
  • Draft Malaysia / Singapore Chicken LunchDinnerBraise / stewCurrySpicy

  • 900 g2 lbChicken, bone-in, chopped
  • 200 ml3/4 cupCoconut milk
  • 100 g3.5 ozShallot, garlic, ginger
  • 40 g1.5 ozTurmeric and lemongrass
  • 44Kaffir lime leaves
  • 20 g3/4 ozChilies
  • 10 g1/3 ozTamarind
  • 6 g1 tspSalt
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Blend the shallot, garlic, ginger, turmeric, lemongrass and chilies into a thick paste.
  2. Fry the paste in the oil over medium heat for 8 minutes until fragrant and the oil separates.
  3. Add the chicken and cook 5 minutes until coated.
  4. Pour in the coconut milk and 200 ml water, add the lime leaves and tamarind, and simmer gently for 25 minutes.
  5. Season with the salt and serve.

Common mistakes

  • Under-frying the paste leaves a raw taste.
  • Boiling hard splits the coconut milk.

Authentic tips

  • Kapitan is a dry curry; reduce until the sauce clings.
  • Better the next day.

Substitutions

  • Boneless thighs shorten the cooking by 10 minutes.
  • Kaffir lime leaves can be replaced by a squeeze of lime.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; better the next day.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.
