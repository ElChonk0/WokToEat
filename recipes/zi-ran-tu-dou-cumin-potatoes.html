Zī Rán Tǔ Dòu (Cumin Potatoes) 孜然土豆

Cumin, the signature spice of Xinjiang, is rubbed on grilled lamb and also on roasted potatoes, a popular street snack in Urumqi.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 600 g1.3 lbPotatoes, cubed
  • 8 g1 tbspCumin seeds, ground
  • 5 g2 tspChili flakes
  • 6 g1 tspSalt
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 60 ml4 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Cut the potatoes into 2 cm cubes and boil 5 minutes; drain and steam dry.
  2. Heat the oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat.
  3. Fry the potatoes 10 minutes, turning, until crisp and golden.
  4. Add the garlic for 20 seconds.
  5. Toss with the cumin, chili flakes and salt.

Common mistakes

  • Wet potatoes spatter and don't crisp.
  • Cumin burns if added too early.

Authentic tips

  • Parboiling gives a fluffy middle.
  • Serve as a snack or side.

Substitutions

  • Sweet potatoes replace white potatoes.
  • Air-fry at 200 C (400 F) after parboiling.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; recrisp in a hot oven.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Nang flatbread
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Hami melon
  • Beverage: Black tea
