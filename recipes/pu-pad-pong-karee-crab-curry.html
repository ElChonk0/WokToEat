Pu Pad Pong Karee (Thai Stir-Fried Crab in Yellow Curry)

▾

Surprise me with

Pu pad pong karee, crab stir-fried with curry powder, egg and evaporated milk, is a Bangkok seafood-restaurant favorite shaped by the Indian and Chinese influences on Thai cooking.

Method

  1. Whisk the eggs with the evaporated milk, chili paste, curry powder, oyster sauce, soy sauce and sugar.
  2. Heat the oil in a large flat-bottom wok over medium-high heat and fry the garlic for 15 seconds, then the onion for 1 minute.
  3. Add the crab and toss for 1 minute, then pour in the stock, cover and cook for 6 to 8 minutes, until the shells are red and the meat is opaque.
  4. Pour the egg mixture over the crab and leave it for 15 seconds to begin setting, then fold gently until it forms soft, creamy curds. Do not overcook the egg; the sauce should be silky.
  5. Toss in the celery, green onion and chili for 30 seconds and serve at once with rice. The dish comes from Bangkok's Chinese-Thai seafood restaurants.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking makes the crab dry.
  • Adding the eggs over high heat scrambles them.

Authentic tips

  • Soft-shell crab also works.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • Soft-shell crab or large shrimp replace crab.
  • Curry powder from a Thai or Indian market gives the best flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Jasmine rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
