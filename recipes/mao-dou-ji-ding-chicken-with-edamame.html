Máo Dòu Jī Dīng (Diced Chicken with Edamame) 毛豆鸡丁

▾

Surprise me with

Edamame, called mao dou, is a Jiangnan summer vegetable, and stir-fried with diced chicken it makes a quick, light dish.

Method

  1. Toss the chicken with the salt, Shaoxing wine, egg white and cornstarch and leave for 15 minutes. Velveting keeps it tender and pale.
  2. Blanch the edamame in salted boiling water for 2 minutes until just tender, then cool in cold water so they stay bright green.
  3. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a flat-bottom wok over medium-high heat and stir-fry the chicken for 2 to 3 minutes until just cooked. Lift it out.
  4. Add the remaining oil and the ginger for 15 seconds, then the edamame for 1 minute.
  5. Return the chicken with the soy sauce, sugar and stock and toss for 30 seconds until glossy. Finish with sesame oil. Edamame is a beloved summer vegetable in Shanghai.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the chicken makes it dry; it finishes cooking when it returns.
  • Skipping the blanching leaves frozen edamame cold in the center.

Authentic tips

  • Add a pinch of sugar to bring out the sweetness of the edamame.
  • Fresh, in-season edamame is far better than frozen.

Substitutions

  • Frozen shelled edamame needs no pre-cooking beyond 1 minute.
  • Green peas replace the edamame.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
