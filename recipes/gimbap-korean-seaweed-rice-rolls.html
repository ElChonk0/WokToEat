Gimbap (Korean Seaweed Rice Rolls) 김밥

▾

Surprise me with

Gimbap, rice and fillings rolled in roasted seaweed and seasoned with sesame oil, is the standard Korean picnic, school-trip and quick-breakfast food, sold at snack shops all over the country.

  • 800 g5 cupsShort-grain rice, cooked slightly dry, warm

  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt, for the rice
  • 44Roasted seaweed sheets (gim) for gimbap

  • 44Yellow pickled radish (danmuji), in strips
  • 44Seasoned burdock root strips (optional)
  • 100 g3.5 ozFish cake sheets, cut into strips
  • 5 ml1 tspLight soy sauce, for the fish cake
  • 2 g1/2 tspSugar, for the fish cake
  • 15 ml1 tbspNeutral oil
  • 5 ml1 tspSesame oil, for brushing
  • 5 g2 tspToasted sesame seeds

Method

  1. Cook the rice with a little less water than usual so it is firm, then fold in the sesame oil and salt while it is still warm. Cover it with a damp cloth; warm rice spreads and sticks better than cold.
  2. Blanch the spinach for 30 seconds, cool it in cold water, squeeze it dry and toss with the sesame oil and a pinch of salt. Stir-fry the carrot in a little oil over medium-high heat for 1 to 2 minutes with a pinch of salt, until just soft.
  3. Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt and cook them as a thick omelet in a small nonstick pan over medium-low heat, folding it over while the top is still slightly runny. Cool and cut into long strips. Warm the ham and crab sticks in the pan for a minute, then stir-fry the fish cake strips over medium heat for 2 minutes with the soy sauce and sugar.
  4. Lay a sheet of gim shiny side down on a bamboo mat. Spread a thin, even layer of rice over it with wet fingers, about 200 g (1 1/4 cups), leaving a 4 cm (1.5 in) strip bare at the far edge.
  5. Line up a strip of each filling across the center of the rice. Lift the near edge of the mat and roll it over the fillings, tucking them in tightly, then keep rolling, pressing firmly, until the bare edge seals the roll. A few grains of rice on the edge help it stick.
  6. Brush the roll with sesame oil and sprinkle with sesame seeds. Cut it into 1.5 cm (1/2 in) slices with a sharp, wet knife, wiping the blade between cuts. Season the fillings lightly; the pickled radish and burdock are salty.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Spreading the rice too thick makes rolls that split; keep it a thin, even layer.
  • Wet fillings soak the seaweed; squeeze the spinach thoroughly.

Authentic tips

  • Wipe the knife with a damp cloth between slices for clean cuts.
  • Leftover gimbap is traditionally dipped in egg and pan-fried the next day.

Substitutions

  • Bulgogi beef or canned tuna mixed with mayonnaise replace the ham.
  • Use fried tofu strips and skip the crab and fish cake for a vegetarian roll.

Storage

Leftovers: Best the same day; refrigerated rice hardens, so dip slices in beaten egg and pan-fry them.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Kimchi
  • Soup: Eomuk guk (fish cake soup)
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
