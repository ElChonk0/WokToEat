Ba Yu Shui Jiao (Spanish Mackerel Dumplings) 鲅鱼水饺

Spanish mackerel dumplings are a Jiaodong Peninsula specialty, filled with minced fish and chives and eaten in spring when mackerel is in season.

  • Prep 45 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Method

  1. Mince the mackerel by hand or pulse in a food processor; mix with the ginger, soy sauce, sesame oil, salt and oil, stirring in one direction until sticky.
  2. Add 2 tablespoons of water gradually and fold in the chives.
  3. Place a tablespoon of filling in each wrapper, fold and pleat.
  4. Boil in plenty of water 6-7 minutes until they float.
  5. Serve with vinegar and ginger.

Common mistakes

  • Overmixing makes the filling tough.
  • Overfilling splits the dumplings.

Authentic tips

  • Add the chives last so they stay fresh.
  • Cod or any firm white fish works.

Substitutions

  • Cod or mahi-mahi replaces mackerel.
  • Pork mixed in softens the fish flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; pan-fry leftovers.

Freezing: Freeze uncooked on a tray for 2 months; boil from frozen for 8 minutes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Black vinegar and ginger dip
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
