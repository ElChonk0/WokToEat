Ayam Percik (Kelantan Grilled Chicken)

▾

Surprise me with

Ayam percik, chicken grilled and basted with a spiced coconut sauce, is a specialty of Kelantan on Malaysia's east coast.

  • Cook 35 min

  • 5 g2 tspTurmeric
  • 15 g1 tbspPalm sugar
  • 8 g1.5 tspSalt

Method

  1. Blend the lemongrass, galangal, shallot, garlic, chilies and turmeric to a paste.
  2. Rub half the paste over the chicken and marinate 1 hour.
  3. Simmer the remaining paste with the coconut milk, sugar and salt for 15 minutes until thick.
  4. Grill or roast the chicken at 200 C (400 F) for 30 minutes, basting with the sauce every 10 minutes.
  5. Serve with the remaining sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling the sauce hard splits the coconut milk.
  • Basting too early burns the sugar.

Authentic tips

  • Charcoal grilling gives the best smoky flavor.
  • Serve with rice or ketupat.

Substitutions

  • A broiler or barbecue replaces charcoal.
  • Light coconut milk makes a lighter sauce.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat in an oven.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Nasi kerabu or steamed rice
  • Soup: Cucumber salad
  • Dessert: Cendol
  • Beverage: Teh tarik
