Sup Tulang (Malay Spicy Beef Bone Soup)

▾

Surprise me with

Sup tulang, beef shin bones simmered in a spiced soup, is a Malay mamak favorite where the marrow is sucked out of the bones.

  • Cook 3 hr

  • 900 g2 lbBeef bones
  • 100 g3.5 ozShallot, garlic, ginger
  • 30 g1 ozChilies
  • 10 g1/3 ozStar anise, cinnamon, cloves
  • 12 g4 tspCoriander and cumin
  • 150 g5 ozTomato
  • 10 g2 tspSalt
  • 2.5 L10 cupsWater

Method

  1. Cover the bones and shin with cold water, boil 5 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  2. Blend the shallot, garlic, ginger and chilies and fry with the whole spices and ground coriander and cumin for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the bones, shin, tomato and 2.5 L water; bring to a boil and skim.
  4. Cover and simmer gently for 2.5 hours until the meat is tender.
  5. Season with the salt and serve with bread or rice and a straw for the marrow.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the blanch gives a cloudy, scummy soup.
  • Boiling hard toughens the meat.

Authentic tips

  • Suck the marrow through the bone.
  • Serve with white bread for dipping.

Substitutions

  • Oxtail or short ribs replace beef shin.
  • A pressure cooker cuts the simmer to 1 hour.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; the broth improves.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: White bread or steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; the soup is the main
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Teh tarik
