Woo Tau Zheng Pai Gu (Steamed Spare Ribs with Taro) 芋头蒸排骨

▾

Surprise me with

Small pieces of spare rib steamed over taro in a black bean or garlic marinade is a dim sum and home dish; the taro soaks up the meat juices.

  • Prep 30 min (plus 30 min marinating)
  • Cook 25 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Rinse the black beans three times and soak them in warm water for 5 minutes. Soak the mandarin peel until soft, scrape off the pith and mince it. Peel the membrane from the back of the ribs if it is still attached, and cut them into bite-size pieces.
  2. Mix the cornstarch, salt, sugar, oyster sauce, soy sauces, wine, water and pepper, stir in the garlic, ginger, mandarin peel and sesame oil, and massage it into the ribs. Leave for 30 to 60 minutes.
  3. Peel the taro wearing gloves, cut it into cubes and toss it with the taro soy sauce. Spread it over the bottom of a rimmed heatproof dish. Keeping the taro underneath means it soaks up the rib juices as they steam.
  4. Crush half the black beans into the ribs, mix in the neutral oil, and spread the ribs over the taro. Scatter the whole beans and chili on top.
  5. Set a rack in a flat-bottom wok or steamer and bring the water to a full boil before the dish goes in, so the timing holds. Steam covered over high heat for 20 to 25 minutes, until a chopstick slides easily into the taro and the meat is opaque at the bone.
  6. Lift the dish out carefully, scatter with green onion and serve with rice.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Thick taro chunks stay hard; cut them about 2 cm (3/4 in).
  • Skipping the soak leaves the steaming juices cloudy.

Authentic tips

  • Fry the taro briefly first for a richer, firmer version.
  • Steaming on top of rice in a pot makes it a one-pot meal.

Substitutions

  • Potato or pumpkin replace taro.
  • Leave out the black beans for a plain garlic version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; re-steam.

Freezing: Freezes raw in the marinade for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Winter melon soup
  • Dessert: Egg tarts
  • Beverage: Pu-erh tea
