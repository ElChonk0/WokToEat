Cōng Kǎo Xuě Yú (Scallion-Braised Cod) 葱烤鳕鱼

▾

Surprise me with

Cong kao, slowly braising fish in a sweet soy sauce with large amounts of scallion, is a Shanghai cold dish and a banquet appetizer.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 600 g1 lb 5 ozIcelandic cod fillet, in 5 cm pieces
  • 200 g7 ozScallions, cut in 6 cm lengths
  • 20 g3/4 ozGinger, sliced
  • 60 ml4 tbspNeutral oil
  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 45 ml3 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 10 ml2 tspDark soy sauce
  • 25 g2 tbspSugar
  • 15 ml1 tbspRice vinegar
  • 150 ml2/3 cupWater

Method

  1. Pat the cod very dry. Heat the oil in a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat and sear the cod for 2 minutes per side until golden. Lift it out.
  2. Lower the heat to medium-low and add the scallions and ginger. Fry them slowly for 8 to 10 minutes, turning, until the scallions are deeply browned and soft. Slow-fried scallions (cong kao) give the sauce its sweet, smoky depth.
  3. Add the Shaoxing wine around the edge of the pan, then the soy sauces, sugar and water, and bring to a simmer.
  4. Return the cod, lay some scallions over it and simmer gently for 4 to 5 minutes, spooning the sauce over, until the fish flakes. Shake the pan rather than stirring.
  5. Add the vinegar, raise the heat for 1 minute to reduce the sauce to a glaze, and serve.

Cook’s notes

Written for Icelandic cod fillet. The Shanghai original is cong kao fish, braised with plenty of scallions, usually using crucian carp.

Common mistakes

  • A heavy hand on the stirring breaks the cod; baste instead.
  • Too little sugar makes the sauce sharp and flat.

Authentic tips

  • The scallions should be soft and sweet, almost caramelized.
  • It tastes even better at room temperature the next day.

Substitutions

  • Haddock or black cod replaces the cod.
  • Leeks replace some of the scallions.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator and is good cold.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and cold cucumber
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
