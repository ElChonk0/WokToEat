Dǎi Xiāng Máo Cǎo Dòu Fu (Dai Lemongrass Tofu) 傣味香茅草豆腐

▾

Surprise me with

Lemongrass, mint and chili define Dai cuisine from Xishuangbanna, and they make a fragrant marinade for grilled or pan-fried tofu.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 15 min

  • 500 g1.1 lbFirm tofu, sliced
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 2 stalks2 stalksLemongrass, finely minced
  • 4 cloves4 clovesGarlic
  • 44Bird's eye chilies
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 2 g1/3 tspSalt
  • 30 ml2 tbspLime juice
  • 40 g1.5 ozMint and cilantro

Method

  1. Press and pat the tofu dry. Fry it in 2 tablespoons of the oil in a skillet over medium-high heat for 3 minutes per side until golden. Lift it out.
  2. Pound the lemongrass, garlic and chilies coarsely in a mortar.
  3. Fry the paste in the remaining oil for 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Return the tofu with the soy sauce and salt and toss to coat.
  5. Turn off the heat, add the lime juice and herbs and serve.

Common mistakes

  • Wet tofu spatters and won't crisp.
  • Adding lime in the pan cooks off its freshness.

Authentic tips

  • Press the tofu well for the best crust.
  • Serve with sticky rice.

Substitutions

  • Extra-firm tofu holds up best.
  • Add chopped peanuts.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sticky rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh pineapple
  • Beverage: Chilled tea
