Dōng'ān Jī (Dong'an Chicken) 东安鸡

▾

Surprise me with

Dong'an chicken, poached chicken shredded and stir-fried with vinegar, ginger and chili, is named for Dong'an County in Hunan and is one of the province's best-known dishes.

Method

  1. Simmer the chicken in water with a few slices of the ginger and half the wine for 20 minutes, until cooked through. Cool it in the broth for 10 minutes, then pull the meat into thick shreds.
  2. Cut the rest of the ginger into fine matchsticks.
  3. Heat the oil in a wok over medium-high heat and fry the ginger for 1 minute until fragrant, then the dried chilies for 15 seconds.
  4. Add the chicken and toss 1 minute, then add the remaining wine, soy sauce and vinegar around the edge of the wok so they sizzle.
  5. Toss for 30 seconds, finish with the scallion and serve warm or at room temperature.

Common mistakes

  • Over-poaching dries the meat; pull it as soon as the juices run clear.
  • Too little ginger loses the point of the dish; the ginger and vinegar should be unmistakable.

Authentic tips

  • Add the vinegar at the end, around the edge of the hot wok, for the most aroma.
  • The dish is traditionally served warm with a fiery chili-vinegar dip.

Substitutions

  • Chicken breast works if poached gently first and torn.
  • Use less ginger for a milder dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated; good cold or at room temperature.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Pear slices
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
