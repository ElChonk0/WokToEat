Yú Xiāng Yú Kuài (Fish-Fragrant Fish Chunks) 鱼香鱼块

▾

Surprise me with

Yu xiang, the "fish-fragrant" flavor of pickled chili, garlic, ginger, sugar and vinegar, contains no fish and is a Sichuan sauce; here it is spooned over crisp-fried fish chunks.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 600 g1 lb 5 ozHaddock fillet, in 4 cm chunks
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 40 g1/3 cupCornstarch, for dredging
  • 120 ml1/2 cupNeutral oil, for frying
  • 40 g1.5 ozPickled red chilies (pao hong la jiao), minced
  • 15 g1 tbspDoubanjiang
  • 4 cloves4 clovesGarlic, minced
  • 15 g1/2 ozGinger, minced
  • 30 g1 ozScallions, finely sliced
  • 20 g4 tspSugar
  • 30 ml2 tbspBlack vinegar
  • 20 ml4 tspLight soy sauce
  • 100 ml1/3 cupChicken stock
  • 8 g1 tbspCornstarch, for the sauce

Method

  1. Season the haddock with the salt and wine, rest 10 minutes, pat dry and dredge in the cornstarch.
  2. Heat the oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat and pan-fry the fish for 2 to 3 minutes per side until golden; lift out to a plate.
  3. In the same pan fry the pickled chilies and doubanjiang over medium heat for 1 minute until the oil is red, then add the garlic and ginger for 20 seconds.
  4. Add the sugar, vinegar, soy sauce and stock, bring to a boil, and thicken with the cornstarch mixed with 2 tablespoons water.
  5. Return the fish to the sauce for 20 seconds, turning gently, and finish with the scallions.

Cook’s notes

Written for haddock fillet. The firm white fish stands in for the carp or catfish used in Sichuan.

Common mistakes

  • Moving the fish too early tears the coating; let it release from the pan.
  • Cooking the sauce too long loses the vinegar brightness.

Authentic tips

  • The sauce should taste sweet, sour, salty and mildly spicy in balance.
  • Haddock is delicate, so turn it once with a fish spatula.

Substitutions

  • Cod loin replaces the haddock.
  • Fresh red chilies and extra doubanjiang replace the pickled chilies.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and blanched greens
  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Fresh orange
  • Beverage: Hot barley tea
