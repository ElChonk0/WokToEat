Kinpira Gobo (Braised Burdock and Carrot) きんぴらごぼう

Kinpira, a sweet-spicy stir-fry of root vegetables, is a Japanese home staple and bento side dish named for a folk hero of strength.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 200 g7 ozBurdock root, julienned
  • 100 g3.5 ozCarrot, julienned
  • 30 ml2 tbspSoy sauce
  • 30 ml2 tbspMirin
  • 10 g2 tspSugar
  • 15 ml1 tbspSesame oil
  • 1 g1/2 tspChili flakes
  • 10 g1 tbspSesame seeds

Method

  1. Julienne the burdock and carrot; soak the burdock in cold water for 5 minutes and drain.
  2. Heat the sesame oil in a pan and fry the burdock 3 minutes.
  3. Add the carrot and chili and cook 2 minutes.
  4. Add the soy sauce, mirin and sugar and cook over medium heat 3-4 minutes until glazed and nearly dry.
  5. Finish with the sesame seeds.

Common mistakes

  • Soaking too long washes out flavor.
  • Overcooking makes it soft; keep some crunch.

Authentic tips

  • Burdock is sold at Japanese and Korean markets.
  • Serve cold or warm as a bento side.

Substitutions

  • Carrot alone is a milder substitute.
  • Lotus root also works.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; served cold.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Miso soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
