Natto Gohan (Natto over Rice) 納豆ご飯

Fermented soybean natto over rice is the classic Japanese breakfast, valued for its protein and probiotics.

  • Prep 5 min
  • Cook 0 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Stir the natto 30 times with chopsticks until sticky and stringy.
  2. Add the soy sauce and mustard from the packet.
  3. Divide the warm rice between bowls.
  4. Spoon the natto on top.
  5. Top with scallion and an egg yolk.

Common mistakes

  • Stirring too little leaves the flavor flat.
  • Cold rice dulls the aroma.

Authentic tips

  • Add a dash of vinegar to reduce the smell.
  • A classic Japanese breakfast.

Substitutions

  • Add kimchi for a Korean-inspired version.
  • Plain soy sauce and mustard are all you need.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
