Natto Gohan (Natto over Rice) 納豆ご飯

▾

Surprise me with

Fermented soybean natto over rice is the classic Japanese breakfast, valued for its protein and probiotics.

  • 22Egg yolks, very fresh
  • 2 g1 sheetShredded nori (optional)

Method

  1. Tip the natto into a bowl and stir vigorously with chopsticks in one direction for about a minute, until it is frothy and full of long strings. Stirring before seasoning develops the savory flavor and fluffy texture.
  2. Add the packet sauce or soy sauce and the mustard and stir for 30 seconds more.
  3. Mix in most of the green onion.
  4. Spoon the natto over bowls of hot rice and make a small hollow for the egg yolk.
  5. Top with the yolk, the remaining green onion and nori, and mix before eating. It is a classic Japanese breakfast.

Common mistakes

  • Stirring too little leaves the flavor flat.
  • Cold rice dulls the aroma.

Authentic tips

  • Add a dash of vinegar to reduce the smell.

Substitutions

  • Add kimchi for a Korean-inspired version.
  • Plain soy sauce and mustard are all you need.
