Chǎo Dì Guā Yè (Stir-Fried Sweet Potato Leaves) 炒地瓜叶

▾

Surprise me with

Sweet potato leaves are a staple Fujian green, stir-fried with garlic, a humble vegetable that fed generations during lean years.

  • Cook 5 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 500 g1.1 lbSweet potato leaves
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine

Method

  1. Pick the leaves and tender tips from the stems, discarding tough stalks; wash and drain well.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok over high heat until smoking and fry the garlic for 10 seconds.
  3. Add the leaves and the wine and toss 2 minutes until just wilted.
  4. Season with the salt and serve immediately.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking turns the leaves slimy and dark.
  • Wet leaves spatter and steam.

Authentic tips

  • Use young tips for the most tender result.
  • Cook quickly over very high heat.

Substitutions

  • Spinach or pea shoots replace the leaves.
  • Add a splash of rice vinegar.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.
