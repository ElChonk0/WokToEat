Irtysh River Grilled Fish 额尔齐斯河烤鱼

▾

Surprise me with

Fish from the cold Irtysh River in northern Xinjiang are a regional specialty, traditionally grilled whole over coals with cumin, chili and salt.

  • Cook 30 min

Method

  1. Score the fish deeply on both sides and rub with the salt, cumin, chili and garlic; leave 20 minutes.
  2. Slice the onion and green pepper and spread on a tray.
  3. Lay the fish on top, brush with oil and roast at 220 C (425 F) for 25 minutes, turning once, until the skin blisters and the flesh flakes.
  4. Squeeze the lemon over.
  5. Serve over the charred vegetables.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking dries the fish.
  • Not scoring leaves the flesh underseasoned.

Authentic tips

  • Over charcoal it is traditional.
  • Trout is the closest widely available fish.

Substitutions

  • Trout is the closest widely available fish.
  • Oven-roast at 220 C (425 F) if no grill.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.
