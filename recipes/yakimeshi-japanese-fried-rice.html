Yakimeshi (Japanese Fried Rice) 焼き飯

▾

Surprise me with

Yakimeshi, or chahan, is the Japanese take on Chinese fried rice with chashu or ham, egg and green onion, served in ramen shops and at home.

Method

  1. Break up the rice with your hands.
  2. Heat the oil in a flat-bottom wok or large skillet over high heat, pour in the eggs, then immediately add the rice and toss so the egg coats the grains.
  3. Stir-fry 3 minutes until the rice is hot and separate.
  4. Add the pork, salt and pepper and toss 1 minute, then pour the soy sauce around the hot edge of the pan.
  5. Add the green onion and butter, toss 30 seconds and serve.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Fresh, warm rice turns sticky.
  • Too much soy sauce makes it wet.

Authentic tips

  • Pack a portion into a small bowl and invert it onto the plate, ramen-shop style.
  • A pinch of dashi powder adds umami.

Substitutions

  • Bacon or kamaboko replace chashu.
  • Add a few chopped pickled ginger strips.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Gyoza
  • Soup: Shoyu ramen or miso soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
