Dōngběi Lā Pí (Cold Mung Bean Starch Sheet Salad) 东北拉皮

▾

Surprise me with

La pi, translucent mung bean starch sheets tossed with cucumber, carrot and sesame sauce, is a popular cold dish in Dongbei, served in summer.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 5 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 300 g10 ozMung bean starch sheets, soaked
  • 250 g9 ozCucumber and carrot, julienned
  • 22Eggs, thin omelet strips
  • 30 g2 tbspSesame paste
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspRice vinegar
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 10 ml2 tspSesame oil

Method

  1. Soak the starch sheets in warm water for 15 minutes until pliable; cut into 2 cm strips and rinse.
  2. Whisk the sesame paste with the soy sauce, vinegar, garlic and sesame oil, thinning with 2 tablespoons of water.
  3. Cook the eggs as a thin omelet and cut into strips.
  4. Toss the sheets with the cucumber, carrot and the sauce.
  5. Top with the egg strips and serve cold.

Common mistakes

  • Over-soaking makes the sheets mushy.
  • A thick sauce clumps; thin it with water.

Authentic tips

  • Serve chilled.
  • Add a spoonful of chili oil if you like heat.

Substitutions

  • Cold glass noodles replace the sheets.
  • Peanut butter replaces sesame paste.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated; dress just before serving.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed buns
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
