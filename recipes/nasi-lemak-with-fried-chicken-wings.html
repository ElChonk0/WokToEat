Nasi Lemak with Fried Chicken Wings

▾

Surprise me with

Nasi lemak, rice cooked in coconut milk and served with sambal, anchovies, peanuts, egg and cucumber, is a national dish of Malaysia and a staple of Singapore hawker centres; fried chicken wings are a common addition.

Method

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs clear, then cook it with the coconut milk, water, pandan, ginger and salt in a rice cooker or covered pot over low heat for 18 minutes. Rest it covered for 10 minutes and fluff.
  2. Blend the lemongrass and garlic with a splash of water to a paste, mix it with the turmeric, coriander and salt, and massage into the chicken wings. Marinate for at least 30 minutes, then toss with the cornstarch.
  3. Blend the soaked chilies, shallots, garlic, belacan and tamarind into a paste. Fry it in the oil over medium heat for 15 minutes, stirring often, until the oil separates and the sambal turns deep red, then stir in the sugar and a pinch of salt.
  4. Heat the frying oil to 170 C (340 F) and fry the wings in two batches for 10 to 12 minutes each until deep golden and cooked through, then drain on a rack.
  5. Slice the eggs and cucumber. Plate the rice with the wings, a generous spoonful of sambal, the egg, cucumber, peanuts and anchovies.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking the coconut rice at a hard boil scorches the bottom; keep the heat low.
  • Frying the sambal too fast leaves it raw and sharp; it needs a slow 15 minutes until the oil separates.

Authentic tips

  • Fry the sambal ahead, because it improves overnight and keeps 5 days.
  • Kerak, the crispy bits scraped from the fried-chicken pan, is a prized topping.

Substitutions

  • Chicken thigh pieces replace wings: fry them for 15 minutes.
  • Skip the belacan and add 15 ml of fish sauce for a version with less funk.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator; reheat the chicken in a hot oven so it crisps.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months; thaw overnight and reheat the rice with a sprinkle of water.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Extra sambal and fried tempeh
  • Soup: Not needed; the plate is a full meal
  • Dessert: Kuih lapis
  • Beverage: Hot teh tarik
