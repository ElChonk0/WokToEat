Cháo Gà (Vietnamese Chicken Congee)

▾

Surprise me with

Chao ga, rice porridge with shredded chicken, ginger and herbs, is a Vietnamese comfort and breakfast food, also given to the sick.

Method

  1. Toast the drained rice in a dry pan over medium heat for 4 to 5 minutes until fragrant and slightly golden. Toasting gives chao a nutty depth and helps the grains break down.
  2. Simmer the chicken with the water, sliced ginger and onion for 30 minutes, skimming, until cooked. Lift out the chicken, shred the meat and return the bones to the pot for 30 minutes more, then strain.
  3. Add the rice to the broth and simmer, stirring often, for 40 to 50 minutes, until the grains burst and the congee is thick and creamy. Add water as needed.
  4. Season with fish sauce and salt.
  5. Ladle into bowls and top with the shredded chicken, julienned ginger, green onion, cilantro, fried shallots, black pepper and a squeeze of lime. Often served with a ginger-fish sauce salad of the chicken on the side.

Common mistakes

  • Not stirring causes sticking.
  • Salting early makes the rice firm.

Authentic tips

  • Add a little black pepper and ginger slivers.
  • Serve with youtiao for dipping.

Substitutions

  • Leftover cooked rice shortens the cooking to 25 minutes.
  • Poached chicken breast also works.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; loosen with water.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Fried dough sticks
  • Soup: Not needed; the congee is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
