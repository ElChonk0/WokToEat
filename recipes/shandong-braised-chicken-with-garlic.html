Shandong Braised Chicken with Garlic 蒜香焖鸡

▾

Surprise me with

Shandong cuisine is known for its heavy, confident use of garlic and scallion, and this braise cooks whole cloves of garlic until soft and sweet alongside soy-glazed chicken, a Shandong home-cooking staple.

  • Cook 30 min

  • 250 ml1 cupChicken stock or water

Method

  1. Brown chicken pieces in oil until golden; add ginger, stirring briefly.
  2. Add whole garlic cloves and stir-fry 1–2 minutes until lightly golden on the edges.
  3. Add both soy sauces, wine, and sugar, tossing to coat.
  4. Add stock, bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer, cover, and braise 20 minutes.
  5. Uncover and reduce the sauce 5–10 minutes until the garlic is soft and the sauce glossy.
  6. Serve hot, garlic cloves and all.

Common mistakes

  • Using minced garlic instead of whole cloves, which loses the intended contrast of soft, sweet whole garlic against the chicken.
  • Rushing the braise — the garlic needs the full simmering time to soften and mellow.

Authentic tips

  • The whole garlic cloves become almost creamy and lose their raw bite entirely by the end of braising — don't be shy with the quantity.
  • This dish keeps and reheats exceptionally well as the garlic continues to mellow.

Substitutions

  • Shallots can be added alongside the garlic for extra sweetness.
  • Bone-in drumsticks work with the same method.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; flavor improves.

Freezing: Freezes well for up to 2 months.

Serve with

  • Soup: Millet porridge
  • Dessert: Fresh pear
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
