Gamja-jeon (Korean Potato Pancakes) 감자전

Gamja-jeon, crisp pancakes of grated potato bound by their own starch, are a specialty of potato-growing Gangwon Province, served with soy dipping sauce and makgeolli.

Method

  1. Grate the potatoes finely (a blender with a splash of water also works) and tip into a sieve set over a bowl. Press out the liquid and leave it to stand 10 minutes.
  2. Pour off the water from the bowl carefully, keeping the white potato starch that has settled at the bottom, and mix it back into the grated potato with the salt.
  3. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a non-stick skillet over medium heat and spoon in small rounds of the mixture, flattening them to about 1 cm (1/2 in). Top each with a little green onion and chili.
  4. Cook 4 minutes until crisp and golden underneath, flip and cook 3 to 4 minutes more, adding oil as needed. Repeat with the rest.
  5. Mix the soy sauce, vinegar and gochugaru for dipping and serve the pancakes hot.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Throwing away the settled starch makes the pancakes fall apart.
  • Too high a heat browns the outside before the potato cooks through.

Authentic tips

  • Waxy-starchy all-purpose potatoes give the best chewy-crisp texture.
  • Grate just before cooking; the potato discolors as it stands.

Substitutions

  • Add a little grated onion or zucchini for flavor.
  • A spoon of potato starch rescues a mixture that is too wet.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; reheat leftovers in a dry skillet.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month after cooking.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Kimchi
  • Soup: Kongnamul guk (bean sprout soup)
  • Dessert: Steamed sweet potatoes
  • Beverage: Makgeolli
