Jiang Bao Ji Ding (Sweet Bean Sauce Diced Chicken) 酱爆鸡丁

Jiang bao, flash-fried in sweet bean sauce, is a northern Chinese technique that Shandong cooks use for diced chicken with peppers and nuts.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 500 g1.1 lbChicken thighs, diced
  • 45 g3 tbspSweet bean sauce
  • 150 g5 ozBell peppers
  • 40 g1.5 ozPeanuts
  • 40 g3 tbspGinger, garlic, scallion
  • 15 g1 tbspCornstarch
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Toss the diced chicken with the cornstarch and wine and 1 tablespoon of water; leave 10 minutes. Dice the peppers to the same size.
  2. Heat half the oil in a wok over high heat and stir-fry the chicken 2 minutes until golden; remove.
  3. Add the remaining oil, ginger, garlic and scallion for 15 seconds, then the sweet bean sauce with 2 tablespoons of water; stir 30 seconds until glossy.
  4. Return the chicken, add the peppers and peanuts and toss for 1 minute until coated.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the sauce makes it bitter; add a splash of water and keep stirring.
  • Overcooking dries out the chicken.

Authentic tips

  • Cook the sauce until it darkens slightly for the best flavor.
  • Add the peanuts last for crunch.

Substitutions

  • Hoisin thinned with a little soy sauce replaces sweet bean sauce.
  • Cashews replace peanuts.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or thin pancakes
  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
