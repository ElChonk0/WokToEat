Jiàng Yóu Jī (Beijing Soy-Braised Chicken) 酱油鸡

▾

Surprise me with

Soy-braised chicken, sold at Beijing delis and eaten cold, is a staple northern dish simmered in a master sauce with star anise.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 40 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Beijing Chicken LunchDinnerBraise / stew

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 1 kg2.2 lbChicken legs, bone-in
  • 90 ml6 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 30 ml2 tbspDark soy sauce
  • 30 g2 tbspSugar
  • 60 ml4 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 30 g1 ozStar anise, cinnamon, ginger
  • 600 ml2.5 cupsWater

Method

  1. Bring the water, both soy sauces, sugar, wine, star anise, cinnamon and ginger to a boil in a pot just large enough for the chicken; simmer 10 minutes.
  2. Add the chicken legs, return to a gentle simmer, cover and cook 30 minutes, turning once.
  3. Turn off the heat and steep the chicken in the sauce for 15 minutes.
  4. Lift out, chop through the bone and moisten with spoonfuls of the sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard tightens the meat and clouds the sauce.
  • Skipping the steep leaves the chicken less flavorful and less juicy.

Authentic tips

  • Reuse the sauce as a master stock: strain, refrigerate and top up with fresh aromatics.
  • Serve cold with steamed buns, as in Beijing delis.

Substitutions

  • Whole bone-in thighs work with the same timing.
  • Hard-boiled eggs added for the last 15 minutes soak up the sauce.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated in the sauce.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Blanched greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
