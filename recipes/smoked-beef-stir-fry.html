Smoked Beef Stir-Fry 烟熏牛肉炒辣椒

▾

Surprise me with

Applying Hunan's love of smoked and cured meats to beef, this dish approximates the region's traditional smoking process with a quick dry-sear before stir-frying with chili for a deeply savory result.

  • Cook 12 min

Method

  1. Toss beef slices with smoked paprika and let sit 10 minutes.
  2. Sear the beef in a hot, dry pan without oil until lightly charred at the edges, 2–3 minutes; remove.
  3. Add a little oil to the pan, then fry garlic until fragrant.
  4. Add green chili and stir-fry 2 minutes until slightly blistered.
  5. Return beef to the pan with soy sauce and wine; toss over high heat until combined.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the dry-sear step, which is what stands in for the traditional smoking process and adds depth.
  • Overcrowding the pan, which steams the beef instead of properly charring it.

Authentic tips

  • A well-seasoned cast-iron pan gets closest to the smoky char this dish approximates.
  • Smoked paprika is a reasonable modern shortcut for the traditional smoking process.

Substitutions

  • Pork cooks the same way with equally good results.
  • A drop of liquid smoke in the marinade intensifies the smokiness.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.
