Méi Cài Kòu Ròu (Steamed Pork with Preserved Greens) 梅菜扣肉

▾

Surprise me with

Mei cai kou rou, braised pork belly layered with preserved mustard greens, is a Hakka classic shared by Hunan and Guangdong, with deep, caramelized flavor.

  • 500 g1.1 lbPork belly, skin-on
  • 200 g7 ozPreserved mustard greens (mei cai)
  • 30 ml2 tbspDark soy sauce
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 20 g4 tspSugar
  • 22Star anise
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Cover the pork with water, add the star anise, bring to a boil and simmer 30 minutes; drain and dry.
  2. Rub the skin with the dark soy and fry it skin-side down in a little oil until deep brown, taking care of spatter; rest 10 minutes and slice 5 mm thick.
  3. Rinse the mei cai well and chop. Stir-fry it with the sugar and light soy sauce for 3 minutes.
  4. Lay the pork slices skin-side down in a bowl, pack the mei cai on top and steam over high heat for 90 minutes.
  5. Invert the bowl onto a plate so the pork sits on top of the greens.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the frying prevents the skin from taking on the wrinkled, caramelized look.
  • Not rinsing the mei cai makes the dish too salty.

Authentic tips

  • Steam in a wide bowl so the juices collect around the greens.
  • Better the next day; it reheats well by steaming.

Substitutions

  • Pork shoulder works but is less rich; steam 30 minutes less.
  • Pork ribs replace the belly for a leaner version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; re-steam for 15 minutes.

Freezing: Freezes well for 2 months; re-steam from frozen for 25 minutes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
