Méi Cài Kòu Ròu (Steamed Pork with Preserved Greens) 梅菜扣肉

▾

Surprise me with

Mei cai kou rou, braised pork belly layered with preserved mustard greens, is a Hakka classic shared by Hunan and Guangdong, with deep, caramelized flavor.

  • 600 g1.3 lbPork belly, skin-on, in one piece
  • 15 ml1 tbspDark soy sauce, for coloring the skin
  • 120 ml1/2 cupNeutral oil, for frying
  • 150 g5 ozPreserved mustard greens (mei cai), soaked and rinsed
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspDark soy sauce, for the sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 20 g1.5 tbspSugar
  • 15 g1 tbspFermented red bean curd (optional)
  • 22Star anise
  • 3 cloves3 clovesGarlic, minced

Method

  1. Simmer the pork belly whole for 20 minutes, then drain, pat dry and prick the skin all over. Brush the skin with dark soy sauce.
  2. Heat the oil in a deep pan and fry the pork skin-side down, covered with a spatter guard, for 3 to 5 minutes until the skin blisters and turns dark brown. Then soak it in cold water for 10 minutes; this wrinkles the skin so it turns tender and 'tiger-striped'.
  3. Slice the pork 5 mm (1/4 in) thick and arrange it skin-side down in a heatproof bowl in overlapping rows. Mix the soy sauces, Shaoxing wine, sugar, red bean curd and garlic and pour over.
  4. Squeeze the mei cai dry and stir-fry it for 3 minutes in a dry pan with a pinch of sugar until fragrant, then pack it on top of the pork.
  5. Steam over boiling water for 1.5 to 2 hours, until the pork is meltingly tender.
  6. Pour off the juices, invert the bowl onto a plate, and pour the juices back over. Serve with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the frying prevents the skin from taking on the wrinkled, caramelized look.
  • Not rinsing the mei cai makes the dish too salty.

Authentic tips

  • Steam in a wide bowl so the juices collect around the greens.
  • Better the next day; it reheats well by steaming.

Substitutions

  • Pork shoulder works but is less rich; steam 30 minutes less.
  • Pork ribs replace the belly for a leaner version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; re-steam for 15 minutes.

Freezing: Freezes well for 2 months; re-steam from frozen for 25 minutes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
