Tokwa (Fried Tofu in Soy-Vinegar Sauce)

▾

Surprise me with

Tokwa, fried tofu with soy-vinegar dipping sauce, is a Filipino staple, usually paired with pork in tokwa't baboy; this meatless version stands alone.

  • Cook 10 min

Method

  1. Press the tofu for 15 minutes and pat dry, then cut it into cubes.
  2. Heat the oil to 180 C (350 F) in a Dutch oven and fry the tofu in batches for 5 to 6 minutes until golden and crisp. Drain.
  3. Mix the vinegar, soy sauce, water, sugar, red onion, green onion, garlic, chilies and pepper. Leave for 10 minutes so the onion and garlic flavor the vinegar.
  4. Pour the sauce over the hot tofu, or serve it on the side for dipping.
  5. Tokwa't baboy pairs it with boiled pork; this tofu-only version is a classic pulutan and side dish.

Common mistakes

  • Wet tofu spatters.
  • Sauce too early softens the crust.

Authentic tips

  • Pair with pork for tokwa't baboy.
  • Serve right away.

Substitutions

  • Air-fry at 200 C (400 F) for a lighter dish.
  • Extra-firm tofu fries best.

Storage
