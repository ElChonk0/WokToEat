La Pi Zi Ji (Xinjiang Chili Chicken) 辣皮子鸡

La pi zi, a paste of dried chilies pounded with Sichuan pepper, flavors Xinjiang chicken dishes that blend Han and Uyghur traditions.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 30 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 25 g1 ozDried chilies
  • 6 g2 tspSichuan peppercorns
  • 50 g4 tbspGinger and garlic
  • 250 g9 ozPeppers and onion
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 60 ml4 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Soak the dried chilies and Sichuan peppercorns in hot water for 10 minutes, drain and pound to a coarse paste.
  2. Fry the chicken in the oil in a wok over medium-high heat for 8 minutes until golden.
  3. Add the chili paste, ginger and garlic and stir 2 minutes.
  4. Add the soy sauce and 100 ml water, cover and simmer 15 minutes.
  5. Add the peppers and onion and cook 5 minutes until just tender.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the chili paste turns the dish bitter.
  • Too much water dilutes the heat.

Authentic tips

  • Use bone-in chicken for flavor.
  • Serve over noodles.

Substitutions

  • Lamb is the traditional meat; braise for 40 minutes.
  • Reduce the chilies for a milder dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or nang
  • Soup: Cucumber salad
  • Dessert: Watermelon
  • Beverage: Black tea
