Cháng Shā Sù Mǐ Fěn (Changsha Vegetarian Rice Noodles) 长沙素米粉

▾

Surprise me with

Rice noodles (mi fen) in a spicy, savory broth are a Changsha breakfast institution, eaten at noodle shops from early morning.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 400 g14 ozRice noodles, fresh or soaked
  • 1.2 L5 cupsDried shiitake and kelp stock
  • 80 g3 ozPickled long beans, chopped
  • 30 ml2 tbspChili oil
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 40 g1.5 ozScallion and cilantro

Method

  1. Simmer the stock gently for 10 minutes to marry the flavors.
  2. Blanch the noodles in boiling water for 1-2 minutes, drain and divide among four bowls.
  3. Put the garlic, soy sauce and chili oil in each bowl.
  4. Ladle in the hot stock and top with the pickled beans, scallion and cilantro.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the noodles makes them mushy.
  • A weak stock gives a flat bowl; simmer dried shiitake with the stock.

Authentic tips

  • Dried shiitake and kelp give a savory vegetarian stock.
  • Chili oil is essential.

Substitutions

  • Fried tofu cubes add protein.
  • Crushed peanuts add crunch.

Storage

Leftovers: Store the noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 4 days.

Freezing: Freeze the broth for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled long beans
  • Soup: Not needed; the noodle soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
