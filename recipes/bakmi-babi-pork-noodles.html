Bakmi Babi (Chinese-Indonesian Pork Noodles)

Bakmi, egg noodles with minced pork, is a Chinese-Indonesian street dish found in the Chinatowns of Jakarta, Medan and Manado.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 45 ml3 tbspSoy sauce and kecap manis
  • 10 ml2 tspSesame oil
  • 150 g5 ozChoy sum
  • 15 g1 tbspFried shallots

Method

  1. Fry the garlic and shallot in a little oil for 1 minute.
  2. Add the pork and cook 5 minutes until browned; add the soy sauce and kecap manis and cook 2 minutes more.
  3. Boil the noodles 2-3 minutes; drain and toss with the sesame oil.
  4. Blanch the choy sum 1 minute.
  5. Top the noodles with the pork, choy sum and fried shallots; serve with soup alongside.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooked noodles go soft.
  • Not draining the noodles makes the dish watery.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with chili sauce and pickled green chilies.
  • Often served with a bowl of clear broth.

Substitutions

  • Chicken replaces pork for a halal version.
  • Fresh egg noodles are best.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled green chilies
  • Soup: Clear broth
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Es teh
