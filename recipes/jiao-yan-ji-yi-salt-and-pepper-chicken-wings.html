Jiao Yan Ji Yi (Salt-and-Pepper Chicken Wings) 椒盐鸡翼

Salt-and-pepper (jiu yim) frying is a Cantonese restaurant technique: the food is fried crisp, then tossed in a hot wok with garlic, chili and spiced salt; wings are one of its most popular forms.

Method

  1. Pat the wings very dry, then mix with the salt, sugar and Shaoxing wine and marinate for 30 minutes. Mix in the egg, then toss with the cornstarch until each piece is evenly coated.
  2. Toast the salt in a dry flat-bottom wok over medium heat for 2 minutes until it smells faintly toasty, then mix it with the white pepper and five-spice. Wipe out the wok.
  3. Heat the oil in the wok or a Dutch oven to 165 C (330 F). Fry the wings in two batches for 8 to 9 minutes until cooked through, then lift out. Raise the oil to 190 C (375 F) and fry them all again for 1 to 2 minutes until deep golden and crisp.
  4. Pour off all but 1 tablespoon of oil. Over medium-high heat fry the garlic, chili and green onion for 30 seconds until fragrant but not brown.
  5. Return the wings, sprinkle with the spiced salt and toss for 30 seconds until coated. Serve at once.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Wet wings spatter and never crisp; dry them well before marinating.
  • Burning the garlic in the final toss makes it bitter; keep it to 30 seconds.

Authentic tips

  • The second fry at higher heat is what gives the restaurant-style shatter.
  • Make extra spiced salt; it keeps for months in a jar.

Substitutions

  • Air fryer: spray the coated wings with oil and cook at 200 C (400 F) for 22 to 25 minutes, turning once, then do the garlic toss in a pan.
  • Drumettes alone, or boneless thigh pieces fried for 5 minutes, work the same way.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; reheat in a 220 C (425 F) oven or air fryer for 8 minutes to re-crisp.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months after frying; reheat from frozen in a hot oven.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed jasmine rice and stir-fried choy sum
  • Soup: Winter melon soup
  • Dessert: Mango pudding
  • Beverage: Cold beer or jasmine tea
