Chángshā Chòu Dòu Fu (Changsha Stinky Tofu) 长沙臭豆腐

▾

Surprise me with

Changsha's black stinky tofu, brined and fried until crisp outside and soft inside, is among the most famous street foods of Hunan, associated with Huogongdian, a street food and temple.

Method

  1. Pat the tofu very dry; cut into 4 cm squares if large.
  2. Heat the oil to 180 C (350 F) in a deep pot.
  3. Fry the tofu in two batches for 4-5 minutes until the surface is dark and crisp.
  4. Mix the garlic, soy sauce, vinegar and chili oil for the dipping sauce.
  5. Poke a hole in each piece with a chopstick, fill with the sauce and top with cilantro.

Common mistakes

  • Wet tofu spatters dangerously; dry it thoroughly.
  • Frying at too low a temperature gives a greasy, soggy crust.

Authentic tips

  • Buy ready-made stinky tofu from an Asian market; making it takes days.
  • Ventilate the kitchen: the smell is part of the experience.

Substitutions

  • Firm tofu with a spoonful of fermented bean curd approximates the flavor.
  • Bake at 220 C (425 F) for a lighter version.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; reheat in a hot oven to recrisp.

Freezing: Not recommended.
