Bendi Goreng Sambal (Okra Stir-Fried in Sambal)

▾

Surprise me with

Okra stir-fried in sambal is a Malaysian rice-table staple, its slime tamed by quick frying over high heat.

  • 400 g14 ozOkra
  • 40 g1.5 ozChilies
  • 40 g3 tbspGarlic and shallot
  • 80 g3 ozTomato
  • 10 g2 tspTamarind
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Cut the okra into 3 cm pieces.
  2. Fry in hot oil for 2-3 minutes until lightly browned and the slime sets; remove.
  3. Fry the blended chilies, shallot and garlic for 5 minutes until thick.
  4. Add the tomato, tamarind and sugar and cook 3 minutes.
  5. Return the okra and toss 1 minute.

Common mistakes

  • Wet okra stays slimy.
  • Overcooking makes it soft.

Authentic tips

  • Pat the okra dry before frying.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • Green beans replace okra.
  • Dry okra before frying.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
