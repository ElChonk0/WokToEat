Chòu Dòu Fu (Taiwanese Fried Stinky Tofu) 臭豆腐

▾

Surprise me with

Stinky tofu, fermented in brine until pungent and deep-fried until crisp, is a Taiwanese night-market icon, served with pickled cabbage and a sweet-spicy sauce.

  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 400 g14 ozFermented stinky tofu
  • 150 g5 ozPickled cabbage (pao cai)
  • 45 ml3 tbspGarlic soy sauce
  • 30 ml2 tbspChili sauce
  • 500 ml2 cupsNeutral oil for frying
  • 15 g1/2 ozCilantro

Method

  1. Pat the stinky tofu very dry.
  2. Heat the oil to 180 C (350 F).
  3. Fry 4-5 minutes until the skin is crisp and deep golden; drain.
  4. Cut open and fill with the garlic soy and chili sauce.
  5. Serve with the pickled cabbage and cilantro.

Common mistakes

  • Wet tofu spatters.
  • Low oil temperature makes it greasy.

Authentic tips

  • The smell is strong; fry with the extractor on.
  • Fresh stinky tofu is sold at Asian markets.

Substitutions

  • Firm tofu plus fermented bean curd approximates the flavor.
  • Bake at 220 C (425 F) for a lighter version.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; recrisp in a hot oven.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled cabbage
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Shaved ice
  • Beverage: Winter melon tea
