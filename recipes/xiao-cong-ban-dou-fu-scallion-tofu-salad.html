Xiǎo Cōng Bàn Dòu Fu (Scallion and Tofu Salad) 小葱拌豆腐

▾

Surprise me with

Xiao cong ban dou fu, chilled tofu with chopped scallion, salt and sesame oil, is a northern Chinese idiom, often cited for being as plain and clear as the dish itself.

  • Cook 3 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 450 g1 lbSoft tofu
  • 80 g3 ozScallions, finely chopped
  • 15 ml1 tbspSesame oil
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt

Method

  1. Blanch the tofu in simmering water for 1 minute, drain, and chill.
  2. Place the tofu on a plate, breaking it into large pieces with a spoon.
  3. Scatter the scallions on top.
  4. Drizzle with the soy sauce, salt and sesame oil.

Common mistakes

  • Not draining the tofu makes the dish watery.
  • Over-salting hides the plain flavor.

Authentic tips

  • Heat the sesame oil and pour it over the scallion for more aroma.
  • Silken tofu gives a delicate texture.

Substitutions

  • Firm tofu works but is less delicate.
  • Add century egg or sesame paste for variation.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the tofu waters out.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed buns or rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
