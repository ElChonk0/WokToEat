Dì Guā Zhōu (Sweet Potato Congee) 地瓜粥

▾

Surprise me with

Sweet potato congee, rice porridge with chunks of sweet potato, is a Taiwanese comfort food, often eaten with pickles and salted egg.

  • Prep 5 min
  • Cook 40 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Taiwan Tofu & Vegetarian LunchDinnerSoupRiceVegetarianVegan

Ingredients

  • 150 g3/4 cupRice
  • 300 g10 ozSweet potato, cubed
  • 1.5 L6 cupsWater
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt

Method

  1. Rinse the rice.
  2. Bring it to a boil with the water and simmer 20 minutes, stirring occasionally.
  3. Add the sweet potato chunks and salt.
  4. Cook 20 minutes more until the rice is creamy and the potato is soft.
  5. Serve with pickles and salted egg.

Common mistakes

  • Not stirring causes sticking.
  • Adding the sweet potato too early makes it dissolve.

Authentic tips

  • Add more water for a thinner congee.
  • A traditional comfort breakfast.

Substitutions

  • Taro replaces sweet potato.
  • More water gives a thinner congee.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; loosen with water.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickles and salted egg
  • Soup: Not needed; the congee is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
