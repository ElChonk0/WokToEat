Dì Guā Zhōu (Sweet Potato Congee) 地瓜粥

▾

Surprise me with

Sweet potato congee, rice porridge with chunks of sweet potato, is a Taiwanese comfort food, often eaten with pickles and salted egg.

  • Cook 45 min

  • 150 g3/4 cupJasmine or short-grain rice, rinsed
  • 400 g14 ozSweet potato (yellow or orange), peeled and cubed
  • 1.5 L6 cupsWater
  • 2 g1/3 tspSalt
  • 200 g7 ozPickled cucumbers, fermented tofu, braised peanuts and greens, for serving

Method

  1. Bring the water to a boil, add the rice and stir so it does not stick.
  2. Simmer over low heat, partly covered, for 20 minutes, stirring now and then.
  3. Add the sweet potato and simmer for 15 to 20 minutes more, until it is very soft and the rice has broken down into a loose, creamy porridge. Sweet potato congee was everyday food in Taiwan when rice was scarce, and now a comforting classic.
  4. Season with just a pinch of salt, letting the sweet potato's sweetness come through.
  5. Serve with small side dishes: pickled cucumbers, fermented tofu, braised peanuts and stir-fried greens.

Common mistakes

  • Not stirring causes sticking.
  • Adding the sweet potato too early makes it dissolve.

Authentic tips

  • Add more water for a thinner congee.
  • A traditional comfort breakfast.

Substitutions

  • Taro replaces sweet potato.
  • More water gives a thinner congee.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; loosen with water.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickles and salted egg
  • Soup: Not needed; the congee is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
