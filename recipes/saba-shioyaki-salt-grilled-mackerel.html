Saba Shioyaki (Salt-Grilled Mackerel) 鯖の塩焼き

Salt-grilled mackerel is a beloved Japanese home and izakaya dish, valued for its richness and the way its skin crisps over a flame.

  • Prep 5 min
  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Sprinkle the mackerel with the salt and rest for 15 minutes.
  2. Pat dry thoroughly.
  3. Heat the grill or broiler to high.
  4. Grill skin-side up 6-8 minutes until the skin is blistered and crisp.
  5. Serve with grated daikon, lemon and a drizzle of soy sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Not drying leaves the skin steamed.
  • Overcooking dries the flesh.

Authentic tips

  • Fresh mackerel needs no extra oil.
  • Rinse excess salt if it forms a crust.

Substitutions

  • Horse mackerel or sardines take the same method.
  • Canned mackerel is a poor stand-in; avoid.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; flake into rice.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and pickles
  • Soup: Miso soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot sencha
