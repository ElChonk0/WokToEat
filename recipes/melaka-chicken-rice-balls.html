Melaka Chicken Rice Balls

▾

Surprise me with

Chicken rice balls are a Hakka-influenced Melaka specialty in which the rice from the chicken rice is shaped into golf-ball-size spheres; they are now rare in Singapore.

  • Cook 1 hr 15 min

Method

  1. Rub the chicken with a little salt, rinse and pat dry. Bring the water to a boil with the ginger, green onion and the rest of the salt, lower in the chicken and return to a boil, then cover and keep at a bare simmer for 15 minutes. Turn off the heat and leave covered 15 minutes more, until it reads 74 C (165 F) by the bone. Plunge it into ice water for 5 minutes and keep the broth.
  2. Heat the chicken fat in a pot over medium heat and fry the garlic until golden, then add the rinsed rice and stir for 2 minutes until coated. Add 520 ml (2 1/4 cups) of the broth and the pandan, bring to a boil, cover and cook on low for 15 minutes, then rest 10 minutes.
  3. While the rice is still very hot, wet your hands with salted water and squeeze about 3 tablespoons of rice at a time firmly into balls the size of a golf ball, rolling and pressing until they hold together. The hot rice's starch is what binds them; cold rice will not stick. Cover them so they stay warm and soft.
  4. Blend the chilies, grated ginger, garlic and lime juice with a pinch of salt, sugar and a few spoons of broth into the chili sauce.
  5. Chop the chicken, brush it with sesame oil and drizzle with soy sauce mixed with a spoon of broth. Serve with the rice balls, cucumber, chili sauce and a bowl of hot broth.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Shaping cold rice gives balls that crumble; shape them while the rice is hot.
  • Boiling the chicken hard toughens it and clouds the stock; keep it at a bare simmer.

Authentic tips

  • Keep a bowl of water beside you to dip your hands and stop the rice sticking.
  • Use the ice bath so the skin turns smooth and the meat stays juicy.

Substitutions

  • Boneless thighs shorten the poach to 18 minutes.
  • A bottled chili sauce works if you skip the fresh one.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; steam the rice balls for 5 minutes.

Freezing: The rice balls freeze for 2 months; steam from frozen for 12 minutes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Blanched choy sum
  • Soup: The poaching stock with a pinch of white pepper
  • Dessert: Cendol
  • Beverage: Hot Chinese tea
