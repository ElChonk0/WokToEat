Beef Nihari (Slow-Cooked Mughlai Beef Stew)

Nihari, a slow-cooked beef shank stew with a rich gravy, originated in Old Delhi's Mughal kitchens and is traditionally eaten for breakfast with naan.

Method

  1. Brown the shank and bones in the ghee in a heavy pot for 8 minutes.
  2. Add the nihari masala, ginger, garlic, fried onion, salt and water and bring to a boil.
  3. Cover and simmer on very low heat for 3 hours until the meat falls off the bone.
  4. Mix the flour with a little water and stir in to thicken; simmer 10 minutes.
  5. Serve topped with ginger julienne, lemon, green chilies and cilantro with naan.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard makes the meat tough.
  • Lumpy flour paste: mix it to a smooth slurry.

Authentic tips

  • Traditionally cooked overnight.
  • Eaten for breakfast with naan.

Substitutions

  • Lamb shank is traditional.
  • A pressure cooker cuts the cooking to 75 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; the flavor improves.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Naan
  • Soup: Not needed; the stew is the main
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Masala chai
