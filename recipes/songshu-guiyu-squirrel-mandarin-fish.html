Sōngshǔ Guìyú (Squirrel Mandarin Fish) 松鼠桂鱼

▾

Surprise me with

Squirrel fish is a Suzhou banquet classic: the fish is scored, fried until its flesh fans out like a squirrel's tail, and sauced with sweet-and-sour tomato.

  • Cook 15 min

  • 15 g1 tbspGarlic, minced

Method

  1. Cut off the head and set it aside. Slice along both sides of the backbone from the head end to the tail, keeping the two fillets attached at the tail, and cut out the backbone. Remove the rib bones.
  2. Lay the fillets skin-side down and score the flesh in a deep diamond pattern, down to the skin but not through it, at an angle. When fried, the cuts open like a squirrel's fur.
  3. Rub with salt and Shaoxing wine, then dredge thoroughly in cornstarch, opening the cuts so starch gets into every one. Coat the head too.
  4. Heat the oil to 180 C (350 F) in a large wok or Dutch oven. Holding the tail, lower the fish in skin-side down, pouring hot oil over the top so it curls into shape. Fry for 4 to 5 minutes until golden and crisp, then fry the head. Drain and arrange on a platter.
  5. Fry the garlic in 2 tablespoons of oil, add the ketchup and fry for 30 seconds, then the sugar, vinegar, soy sauce, water, peas and carrots. Thicken with the slurry and stir in a spoon of hot frying oil for gloss.
  6. Pour the sauce over the fish at the table so it sizzles (said to sound like a squirrel), and scatter with pine nuts. A Suzhou banquet classic.

Common mistakes

  • Scoring too shallowly means the flesh will not open into the squirrel's tail.
  • A sauce that has stood makes the crust soggy; pour it over at the table.

Authentic tips

  • Dry the fish very well before coating.
  • Fry the fish twice for extra crispness.

Substitutions

  • Whole sea bass or tilapia replaces the mandarin fish.
  • Ketchup is a common modern shortcut; use tomato paste plus sugar for a deeper sauce.

Storage

Leftovers: Best eaten at once; the crust softens within 20 minutes.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot jasmine tea
