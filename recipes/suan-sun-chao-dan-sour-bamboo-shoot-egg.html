Suān Sǔn Chǎo Dàn (Sour Bamboo Shoot and Egg Stir-Fry) 酸笋炒蛋

▾

Surprise me with

Fermented sour bamboo shoots are a Dai pantry staple, adding a sharp, funky tang to stir-fries and soups.

  • Cook 8 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 200 g7 ozSour bamboo shoots, rinsed
  • 55Eggs
  • 20 g2/3 ozChilies
  • 15 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 15 g1/2 ozCilantro

Method

  1. Rinse the sour bamboo shoots and chop; squeeze dry.
  2. Heat half the oil in a wok and fry the chilies and garlic 15 seconds.
  3. Add the shoots and stir-fry 3 minutes.
  4. Push to the side, add the remaining oil and scramble in the beaten eggs.
  5. Mix everything together and garnish with cilantro.

Common mistakes

  • Not rinsing the shoots makes the dish too sour.
  • Overcooking the eggs makes them dry.

Authentic tips

  • Sour bamboo shoots are sold at Chinese and Southeast Asian markets.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • Rinsed sauerkraut replaces sour bamboo shoots.
  • Add minced pork for a non-vegetarian version.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Pu'er tea
