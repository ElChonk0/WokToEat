Sān Bēi Dòu Fu (Three-Cup Tofu) 三杯豆腐

▾

Surprise me with

San bei, "three cups" of soy sauce, rice wine and sesame oil, is a Taiwanese braise that came to the island with Jiangxi settlers; tofu takes the sauce beautifully.

  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 45 ml3 tbspRice wine

  • 50 g3 tbspGinger
  • 40 g3 tbspGarlic
  • 40 g1.5 ozThai basil
  • 15 g1 tbspChilies

Method

  1. Press the tofu 15 minutes and cut into cubes.
  2. Pan-fry the tofu in a little oil until golden on all sides, 6 minutes; remove.
  3. Heat the sesame oil in a wok and fry the ginger, garlic and chilies 1 minute.
  4. Return the tofu with the soy sauce, wine and sugar and cook over high heat until the sauce is nearly gone.
  5. Fold in the basil and serve.

Common mistakes

  • Wet tofu doesn't crisp.
  • Burning the sauce makes it bitter.

Authentic tips

  • Use a clay pot for the traditional presentation, or a small Dutch oven on induction.
  • The basil goes in last.

Substitutions

  • Mushrooms or eggplant take the same sauce.
  • Pressed tofu soaks up the most sauce.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Oolong tea
