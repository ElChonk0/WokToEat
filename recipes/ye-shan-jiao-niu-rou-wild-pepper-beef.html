Yě Shān Jiāo Niú Ròu (Wild Pepper Beef) 野山椒牛肉

▾

Pickled wild mountain peppers give Hunan's beef stir-fries a sharp, floral heat.

  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 500 g1.1 lbBeef sirloin, sliced
  • 80 g3 ozPickled wild peppers
  • 30 g2 tbspGarlic and ginger
  • 100 g3.5 ozCelery
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 10 g2 tspCornstarch
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Slice the beef 4 mm thick across the grain and toss with the soy sauce, cornstarch and 1 tablespoon of water; leave 15 minutes. Chop the pickled peppers and slice the celery.
  2. Heat half the oil in a wok over high heat and sear the beef for 45 seconds, stirring; remove.
  3. Add the remaining oil, then the pickled peppers, garlic and ginger and fry 1 minute until the kitchen smells sharp and floral.
  4. Return the beef with the celery and a splash of the pickling brine and toss 45 seconds.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the beef toughens it; it should still be slightly pink when it leaves the pan.
  • Adding too much brine makes the dish soupy.

Authentic tips

  • Pickled wild mountain peppers have a floral, fruity edge; pickled jalapenos are the closest substitute.
  • Slice the celery on the diagonal for even cooking.

Substitutions

  • Pickled jalapenos replace wild peppers.
  • Pork loin takes the same method.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
