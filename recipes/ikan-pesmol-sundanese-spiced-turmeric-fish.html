Ikan Pesmol (Sundanese Spiced Turmeric Fish)

Pesmol is a West Javan preparation in which fried fish is simmered in a turmeric, nutmeg and tamarind sauce until the sauce is absorbed.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 25 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 600 g1 lb 5 ozCod loin, in 5 cm pieces

  • 100 g3.5 ozTomato, chopped

  • 300 ml1 1/4 cupsWater
  • 1 g1/4 tspNutmeg, grated
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt, for the sauce

Method

  1. Season the cod with the 3 g salt and lime juice, rest 10 minutes, pat dry.
  2. Heat the oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat and fry the cod 2 minutes per side until lightly golden; lift out.
  3. Blend the shallots, garlic, turmeric, ginger, candlenuts and chilies and fry the paste in the same pan with the lemongrass and bay leaves over medium heat for 5 minutes.
  4. Add the tomato, tamarind, water, nutmeg and salt and simmer 5 minutes.
  5. Return the fish, simmer gently for 5 to 6 minutes, spooning the sauce over, until the sauce thickens.

Cook’s notes

Written for cod loin. Sundanese cooks use carp or tilapia.

Common mistakes

  • Too much stirring breaks the fish; baste instead.
  • Under-frying the paste leaves a raw spice taste.

Authentic tips

  • Pesmol should end with a thick, clinging sauce.
  • A light hand with nutmeg is enough.

Substitutions

  • Haddock replaces the cod.
  • Ground turmeric (2 g) replaces fresh turmeric.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and lalap vegetables
  • Soup: Sayur asem
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Es teh
