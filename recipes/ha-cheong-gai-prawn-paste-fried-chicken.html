Ha Cheong Gai (Prawn Paste Fried Chicken) 虾酱鸡

▾

Surprise me with

Prawn paste chicken, ha cheong gai, is a Chinese-Malaysian and Singaporean zi char dish of chicken marinated in fermented shrimp paste and deep-fried until the crust blisters and turns deeply savoury.

  • Prep 20 min (plus 1 hr marinating)
  • Cook 20 min

Method

  1. Dissolve the sugar in the hot water, then stir in the prawn paste, oyster sauce, wine, ginger, pepper and sesame oil. Coat the wings and marinate for at least 1 hour, ideally overnight. The fermented paste is pungent raw but turns deeply savory and fragrant in the oil.
  2. Whisk the cornstarch, flour, baking powder, egg and water into a smooth batter and mix it into the wings.
  3. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven to 150 C (300 F). Fry the wings in small batches for 6 to 8 minutes, until cooked through and golden. Do not crowd the pot. Drain on a rack and rest for 10 to 15 minutes.
  4. Raise the oil to 190 C (375 F) and fry the wings again for 1 to 2 minutes, until deep reddish-brown and very crisp. The hotter second fry sets the crust.
  5. Drain and serve hot on lettuce with lime wedges and chili sauce.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Too much flour hides the flavor of the paste; use a thin coating.
  • Frying at one temperature gives either a raw center or a burnt crust; fry low, then refry hot.

Authentic tips

  • Do not add salt, because the prawn paste is salty enough.
  • The smell is strong when frying, so use the extractor fan and open a window.

Substitutions

  • Chicken thigh pieces replace wings: fry 12 minutes.
  • Fermented shrimp paste (belacan) mixed with oil replaces har cheong in a pinch.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator; recrisp in a 200 C oven for 8 minutes.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months once fried; reheat in the oven.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and stir-fried greens
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer or iced lemon tea
