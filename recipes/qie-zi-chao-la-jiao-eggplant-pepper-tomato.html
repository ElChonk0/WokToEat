Qié Zi Chǎo Là Jiāo (Xinjiang Eggplant, Pepper and Tomato Stir-Fry) 茄子炒辣子

▾

Eggplant, peppers and tomato stir-fried together is a staple Uyghur and Hui home dish, served over rice or with flatbread.

  • 400 g14 ozEggplant, in chunks
  • 200 g7 ozGreen peppers
  • 250 g9 ozTomatoes
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 3 g1 tspCumin
  • 3 g1 tspChili flakes
  • 75 ml5 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Cut the eggplant into chunks, salt lightly and rest 10 minutes; pat dry.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok over medium-high heat and fry the eggplant 5 minutes until golden and soft; remove.
  3. Fry the garlic, cumin and chili 20 seconds.
  4. Add the peppers and tomatoes and cook 5 minutes into a sauce.
  5. Return the eggplant and simmer 5 minutes.

Common mistakes

  • Eggplant soaks up oil if added to a cool pan.
  • Over-stirring breaks it up.

Authentic tips

  • Serve over rice or with flatbread.
  • Salt the eggplant first to reduce oil absorption.

Substitutions

  • Zucchini replaces eggplant.
  • Fresh tomatoes should be ripe; canned can fill in.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Nang flatbread or steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Hami melon
  • Beverage: Black tea
