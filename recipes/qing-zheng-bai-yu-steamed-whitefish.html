Qīng Zhēng Bái Yú (Steamed Chaohu Whitefish) 清蒸白鱼

▾

Surprise me with

Chaohu whitefish, a slender silvery lake fish, is traditionally steamed with ginger and scallion to preserve its delicate flavor.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 800 g1.75 lbWhole white fish or bream, cleaned
  • 50 g4 tbspGinger and scallion
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Scale and clean the fish, score the sides and rub with the wine; lay on a plate over a few ginger slices.
  2. Steam over high heat 10-12 minutes until the flesh at the thickest part is just opaque.
  3. Pour off the steaming liquid.
  4. Top with the julienned ginger and scallion and drizzle with the soy sauce.
  5. Heat the oil until smoking and pour it over the aromatics so they sizzle.

Common mistakes

  • Oversteaming dries the fish.
  • Skipping the hot oil leaves the aromatics raw.

Authentic tips

  • Use a fish about 800 g so it cooks evenly.
  • Freshness is everything.

Substitutions

  • Bream or sea bass replaces whitefish.
  • Fillets steam in 6-8 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Eat within the hour.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
