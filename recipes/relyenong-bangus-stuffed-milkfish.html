Relyenong Bangus (Stuffed Milkfish)

▾

Surprise me with

Relyenong bangus, milkfish deboned, stuffed with its own flaked meat, vegetables and raisins, and fried, is a festive Filipino dish.

  • 30 ml2 tbspCalamansi juice
  • 90 ml6 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Debone the fish through the belly slit, keeping the skin intact; flake the flesh and discard the bones.
  2. Fry the garlic and onion in a little oil, add the carrots, peas and raisins and cook 5 minutes.
  3. Add the flaked fish, soy sauce and calamansi and cook 5 minutes; cool and mix in the eggs.
  4. Stuff the skin and stitch or tie closed.
  5. Fry in oil at 170 C (340 F) for 5-6 minutes per side until golden; slice and serve.

Common mistakes

  • Overstuffing splits the skin.
  • Oil that is too hot browns the skin before the filling cooks.

Authentic tips

  • Ask the fishmonger to debone the fish.
  • Serve with banana ketchup.

Substitutions

  • Ask the fishmonger to debone the fish.
  • Tilapia replaces milkfish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Banana ketchup and rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Leche flan
  • Beverage: Calamansi juice
