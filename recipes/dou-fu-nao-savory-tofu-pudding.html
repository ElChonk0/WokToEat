Dòu Fu Nǎo (Beijing Savory Tofu Pudding) 豆腐脑

▾

Dou fu nao, silken tofu in a thick savory sauce of mushrooms and wood ear, is a Beijing breakfast classic eaten with fried dough sticks.

Ingredients

  • 500 g1.1 lbSilken tofu
  • 40 g1.5 ozDried shiitake and wood ear, soaked
  • 400 ml1.75 cupsVegetable stock
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 45 ml3 tbspCornstarch slurry
  • 10 ml2 tspSesame oil
  • 10 ml2 tspChili oil
  • 15 g1/2 ozCilantro

Method

  1. Soak the mushrooms and wood ear until soft, slice them, and simmer in the stock with the soy sauce for 8 minutes.
  2. Stir in the slurry gradually until the sauce thickens to a loose gravy.
  3. Heat the silken tofu gently in a pan of barely simmering water for 3 minutes.
  4. Scoop the tofu into bowls in large, thin curls.
  5. Ladle over the sauce and finish with sesame oil, chili oil and cilantro.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling the tofu makes it spongy.
  • Adding the slurry too fast creates lumps.

Authentic tips

  • Scoop with a flat spoon for wide, thin curls.
  • Serve with fried dough sticks.

Substitutions

  • Soft tofu replaces silken tofu if it isn't available.
  • A vegetarian mushroom gravy replaces the traditional meat gravy.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the tofu pudding firms up when cold.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Youtiao (fried dough sticks)
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot soy milk
