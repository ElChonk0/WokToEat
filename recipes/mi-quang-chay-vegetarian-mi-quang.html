Mì Quảng Chay (Vegetarian Mi Quang)

▾

Surprise me with

Mi quang, turmeric noodles with a small amount of rich broth, is the signature dish of Quang Nam in central Vietnam; the chay version uses mushrooms and tofu.

Method

  1. Fry the tofu in 2 tablespoons of oil over medium-high heat for 6 to 8 minutes until golden. Lift it out.
  2. Add the remaining oil and fry the shallots, garlic and turmeric for 1 minute, then the mushrooms for 3 to 4 minutes until browned.
  3. Return the tofu, add the soy sauce, sugar, salt and broth and simmer for 10 minutes into a small amount of concentrated golden broth; mi quang is moistened, not swimming.
  4. Cook the noodles and divide among bowls over lettuce, herbs and sprouts.
  5. Ladle over a little broth with the tofu and mushrooms and top with peanuts and broken rice crackers. Serve with lime and chili.

Common mistakes

  • Too much broth makes it soup, not mi quang.
  • Overcooked noodles go soft.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with lime and chilies.
  • Rice crackers are essential.

Substitutions

  • Fresh wide rice noodles replace turmeric noodles.
  • Crushed peanuts and rice crackers are essential.

Storage

Leftovers: Store noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 3 days.

Freezing: Freeze the broth for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Rice crackers, herbs and chili
  • Soup: The dish has a little broth
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
