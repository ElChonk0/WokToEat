Mì Quảng Chay (Vegetarian Mi Quang)

▾

Surprise me with

Mi quang, turmeric noodles with a small amount of rich broth, is the signature dish of Quang Nam in central Vietnam; the chay version uses mushrooms and tofu.

  • Cook 20 min

  • 5 g2 tspTurmeric
  • 40 g1.5 ozRoasted peanuts
  • 500 ml2 cupsVegetable broth
  • 150 g5 ozHerbs and sprouts
  • 44Rice crackers
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt

Method

  1. Fry the tofu and mushrooms with the turmeric in the oil for 6 minutes.
  2. Add the broth and salt and simmer 8 minutes; the broth should be thick and intense.
  3. Cook the noodles in boiling water 1 minute and divide among bowls.
  4. Add a little broth, enough to moisten but not cover.
  5. Top with herbs, peanuts and crumbled rice crackers.

Common mistakes

  • Too much broth makes it soup, not mi quang.
  • Overcooked noodles go soft.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with lime and chilies.
  • Rice crackers are essential.

Substitutions

  • Fresh wide rice noodles replace turmeric noodles.
  • Crushed peanuts and rice crackers are essential.

Storage

Leftovers: Store noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 3 days.

Freezing: Freeze the broth for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Rice crackers, herbs and chili
  • Soup: The dish has a little broth
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
