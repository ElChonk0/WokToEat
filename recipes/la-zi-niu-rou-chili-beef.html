Là Zi Niú Ròu (Xinjiang Chili Beef) 辣子牛肉

La zi, spicy stir-fry in Xinjiang, uses dried chilies, Sichuan pepper and cumin on beef, blending Han and Uyghur flavors.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 500 g1.1 lbBeef, sliced
  • 25 g1 ozDried chilies
  • 5 g2 tspSichuan pepper
  • 5 g2 tspCumin
  • 40 g3 tbspGarlic and ginger
  • 150 g5 ozPeppers
  • 60 ml4 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Slice the beef thin against the grain and toss with a little soy sauce and cornstarch.
  2. Heat half the oil in a wok over high heat and sear the beef 1 minute; remove.
  3. Add the remaining oil, then the dried chilies, Sichuan pepper, garlic and ginger for 30 seconds.
  4. Add the peppers and stir-fry 2 minutes.
  5. Return the beef with the cumin and toss 1 minute.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the chilies makes the dish bitter.
  • Overcooking the beef makes it tough.

Authentic tips

  • Open the window; the chili fumes are strong.
  • Serve with rice or flatbread.

Substitutions

  • Lamb is the traditional meat.
  • Reduce the chilies for a milder dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or nang
  • Soup: Cucumber salad
  • Dessert: Watermelon
  • Beverage: Black tea
