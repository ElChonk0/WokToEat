Hunan-Style Stir-Fried Pork with Chili 辣椒炒肉

▾

Surprise me with

A simple, fiery everyday stir-fry that showcases Hunan's love of fresh green chilies, cooked hot and fast with thin-sliced pork for a dish found on nearly every Hunanese home dinner table.

  • Cook 10 min

Method

  1. Marinate pork slices in soy sauce and half the wine for 10 minutes.
  2. Stir-fry pork in hot oil over high heat until just cooked through; remove and set aside.
  3. In the same wok, fry garlic, ginger, and black beans until fragrant.
  4. Add green chilies and stir-fry 2–3 minutes until slightly blistered.
  5. Return pork to the wok with remaining wine and sugar; toss over high heat until combined.

Common mistakes

  • Using bell peppers instead of a genuinely spicy green chili, which loses the dish's characteristic heat.
  • Overcooking the chilies, which softens them beyond the intended slight char and crunch.

Authentic tips

  • This dish is meant to be quite spicy and simple — don't over-season beyond the core ingredients.
  • High heat and quick cooking are essential; the whole stir-fry should take only a few minutes.

Substitutions

  • Serrano or jalapeño peppers work if Hunan-style chilies aren't available.
  • Beef can replace pork with the same method.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended — texture of the chilies suffers.

Serve with

  • Beverage: Cold beer
