Là Ròu Chǎo Sǔn (Cured Pork with Bamboo Shoots) 腊肉炒笋

▾

Surprise me with

Cured pork stir-fried with bamboo shoots pairs two staples of Anhui's mountains; the shoots balance the intensely savory meat.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

  • 250 g9 ozCured pork belly, sliced
  • 400 g14 ozBamboo shoots, sliced
  • 40 g3 tbspGarlic and chilies
  • 5 ml1 tspLight soy sauce
  • 3 g1/2 tspSugar
  • 15 ml1 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Slice the bamboo shoots and blanch for 5 minutes; drain.
  2. Rinse the cured pork, steam it 15 minutes to soften and slice thinly.
  3. Fry the cured pork in a dry wok over medium heat for 3-4 minutes until the fat renders and the edges are crisp.
  4. Add the oil, garlic and chilies for 20 seconds, then the bamboo shoots and stir-fry 3 minutes.
  5. Season lightly with the soy sauce and sugar.

Common mistakes

  • Over-salting: the cured pork is salty already.
  • Skipping the blanch leaves bitterness in the shoots.

Authentic tips

  • Taste before adding soy sauce.
  • Bacon stands in for cured pork.

Substitutions

  • Smoked bacon replaces cured pork belly.
  • Leeks or garlic shoots replace bamboo shoots.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
