Bò Tái Chanh (Vietnamese Lime-Cured Beef Salad)

▾

Surprise me with

Bo tai chanh, thin raw beef cured in lime juice and tossed with onion, herbs and peanuts, is a southern Vietnamese salad and a popular bar snack.

  • 400 g14 ozBeef sirloin, paper-thin
  • 90 ml6 tbspLime juice
  • 100 g3.5 ozRed onion
  • 40 g1.5 ozRau ram and mint
  • 15 g1/2 ozChilies
  • 15 ml1 tbspFish sauce
  • 60 g2 ozRoasted peanuts and shrimp chips

Method

  1. Freeze the beef 30 minutes and slice very thin against the grain.
  2. Toss with the lime juice and fish sauce in a bowl and cure 3-5 minutes until the surface turns pale.
  3. Add the red onion, rau ram, mint and chilies.
  4. Toss lightly.
  5. Top with the peanuts and shrimp chips and serve at once.

Common mistakes

  • Curing too long makes the beef dry and gray.
  • Using old beef is unsafe for raw preparations; buy it fresh.

Authentic tips

  • Use only very fresh, good quality beef.
  • Serve as a drinking snack.

Substitutions

  • Use only very fresh beef.
  • Sliced and briefly seared beef is a cooked alternative.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Shrimp chips or steamed rice
  • Soup: Canh
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
