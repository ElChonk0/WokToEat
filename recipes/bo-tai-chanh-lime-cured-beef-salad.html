Bò Tái Chanh (Vietnamese Lime-Cured Beef Salad)

▾

Surprise me with

Bo tai chanh, thin raw beef cured in lime juice and tossed with onion, herbs and peanuts, is a southern Vietnamese salad and a popular bar snack.

  • 400 g14 ozBeef sirloin or eye round, partly frozen and sliced paper-thin
  • 90 ml1/3 cupLime juice, for curing
  • 100 g3.5 ozRed onion, very thinly sliced

  • 30 g1/4 cupRoasted peanuts, crushed
  • 10 g1 tbspFried shallots
  • 30 g1 ozShrimp chips (banh phong tom), for serving

Method

  1. Partly freeze the beef for 30 minutes and slice it as thinly as possible across the grain.
  2. Toss the beef with the lime juice and leave for 5 to 10 minutes, until the outside turns pale and opaque but the center is still pink. Squeeze out the excess juice firmly; this is what keeps the beef tender and not mushy.
  3. Soak the red onion in the vinegar for 10 minutes to soften its bite.
  4. Mix the fish sauce, sugar, garlic and chilies until the sugar dissolves, then toss with the beef, onion and herbs.
  5. Top with peanuts and fried shallots and serve at once with shrimp chips for scooping. Use the freshest beef you can buy for this dish.

Common mistakes

  • Curing too long makes the beef dry and gray.
  • Using old beef is unsafe for raw preparations; buy it fresh.

Authentic tips

  • Use only very fresh, good quality beef.
  • Serve as a drinking snack.

Substitutions

  • Use only very fresh beef.
  • Sliced and briefly seared beef is a cooked alternative.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Shrimp chips or steamed rice
  • Soup: Canh
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
