Niàng Qié Zi (Hakka Stuffed Eggplant) 酿茄子

▾

Surprise me with

Stuffed vegetables are part of the Hakka yong tau foo tradition, in which eggplant, bitter melon, mushrooms or tofu are filled with minced pork and salted fish and then fried or braised.

Method

  1. Mix the pork, salted fish, green onion, ginger, filling cornstarch, soy sauce, wine, white pepper and sesame oil, stirring in one direction until the filling turns sticky.
  2. Cut the eggplants into 6 cm sections and make a deep slit through each, without cutting through the base. Dust the cut sides with a little cornstarch.
  3. Press the filling into the slits and smooth the tops.
  4. Heat the oil in a wide pan over medium heat and fry the stuffed eggplant, filling-side down first, for 3 to 4 minutes per side until golden.
  5. Add the water, oyster sauce and soy sauce, cover and braise for 8 minutes, until the eggplant is soft, then stir the sauce cornstarch with a spoon of water, pour it in and cook for 1 minute until glossy.

Common mistakes

  • Overstuffing makes the filling burst out; pack it level with the cut.
  • Starting with a hot pan burns the eggplant before the pork cooks; use medium heat.

Authentic tips

  • The dusting of cornstarch helps the filling stay attached.
  • Soak the cut eggplant in salted water for 5 minutes to prevent browning.

Substitutions

  • Bitter melon rings or shiitake caps replace eggplant.
  • Minced shrimp replaces the salted fish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; reheat in a covered pan.

Freezing: Not recommended, because the eggplant turns mushy.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear winter melon soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot jasmine tea
