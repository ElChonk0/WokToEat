Ji Zhou (Cantonese Chicken Congee) 鸡粥

Congee (jook) is the everyday rice porridge of southern China, eaten for breakfast and when unwell; Cantonese cooks break the rice down until it is silky and cook chicken in it.

  • Cook 1.5 hr

Method

  1. Rinse the rice, drain well and mix with the oil and salt; leave for 15 minutes. This helps the grains break down.
  2. Bring the water to a boil in a Dutch oven or large pot, add the rice, return to a boil, then lower to a steady simmer. Cook partly covered for 1 hour, stirring every 10 minutes and scraping the bottom, until the grains have burst and the congee is thick and silky.
  3. Meanwhile take the chicken off the bone, cut it into bite-size pieces and mix with half the ginger, the wine, salt and cornstarch. Simmer the bones in the congee for flavor.
  4. Remove the bones, add the chicken and remaining ginger and simmer for 8 to 10 minutes until the chicken is cooked through. Thin with boiling water if it gets too thick.
  5. Season with salt and white pepper and serve topped with green onion, cilantro and a few drops of sesame oil.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Not stirring lets the rice catch and scorch on the bottom, especially on electric and induction hobs.
  • Adding the chicken at the start overcooks it; it needs only 10 minutes.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with youtiao (fried dough sticks) to dip, the classic pairing.
  • A clay pot is the traditional vessel; a heavy Dutch oven holds the simmer just as evenly.

Substitutions

  • Leftover cooked rice makes congee in 30 minutes: use 400 g with 1.5 L water.
  • Add sliced shiitake or a century egg with the chicken.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; it sets firm, so reheat with a splash of water, stirring.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months; the texture loosens slightly but is fine reheated with stirring.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Youtiao (Chinese fried dough)
  • Soup: Not needed; the congee is the soup
  • Dessert: Sweet tofu pudding (dou fu fa)
  • Beverage: Soy milk or hot tea
