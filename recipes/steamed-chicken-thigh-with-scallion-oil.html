Steamed Chicken Thigh with Scallion Oil 葱油蒸鸡

▾

Surprise me with

A minimalist Jiangnan preparation that trusts good chicken and fresh scallion oil to carry the whole dish — simplicity is the point, echoing the region's preference for letting quality ingredients speak for themselves.

  • Cook 15 min

  • 600 g1.3 lbChicken thighs, boneless, sliced
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 8 g1 tbspCornstarch
  • 10 g1/3 ozGinger, julienned
  • 44Dried shiitake, soaked and sliced (optional)
  • 4 stalks4 stalksGreen onion, finely shredded
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 3 g1/2 tspSugar

Method

  1. Toss the chicken with the salt, Shaoxing wine, cornstarch and ginger and leave for 15 minutes. The starch keeps the meat silky during steaming.
  2. Spread the chicken and shiitake in a single layer on a heatproof plate.
  3. Steam over boiling water in a flat-bottom wok, covered, for 12 to 15 minutes, until cooked through (74 C / 165 F).
  4. Pour off half the steaming juices, mix the rest with the soy sauce and sugar and pour back over the chicken. Pile the shredded green onion on top.
  5. Heat the oil until it just smokes and pour it over the green onion so it sizzles and releases its fragrance. Serve at once.

Common mistakes

  • Over-steaming, which is easy to do with such a simple, exposed preparation — check doneness at the 10-minute mark.
  • Pouring the oil before it's hot enough, which fails to properly bloom the aromatics.

Authentic tips

  • This dish lives or dies by the quality of the final scallion oil — use fresh green onion and good oil.
  • Slicing the chicken thin and even ensures fast, uniform steaming.

Substitutions

  • Chicken breast works if steamed 3 minutes less.
  • Bone-in thighs give a richer broth; steam 10 minutes longer.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; best cold with the oil spooned over.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot jasmine tea
