Udang Saus Padang (Shrimp in Padang Sauce)

▾

Surprise me with

Padang-style sauce, fiery and tomato-based, is a Minangkabau restaurant standard, served over fried shrimp, fish or eggs.

  • Cook 15 min

  • 20 g1 tbspGinger
  • 10 g2 tspSugar
  • 30 ml2 tbspCornstarch slurry
  • 60 ml4 tbspNeutral oil
  • 5 g1 tspSalt

Method

  1. Fry the shrimp in a little oil for 2 minutes; set aside.
  2. Fry the blended chilies, shallot, garlic and ginger for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the tomatoes, sugar, salt and 100 ml water and simmer 5 minutes.
  4. Stir in the slurry to thicken.
  5. Add the shrimp and cook 2 minutes.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the shrimp makes them tough.
  • Thick slurry makes the sauce gluey.

Authentic tips

  • Also excellent on fried fish or eggs.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • Fried fish or hard-boiled eggs also work in the sauce.
  • Reduce the chilies for a milder dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the sauce keeps 3 days.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Es teh
