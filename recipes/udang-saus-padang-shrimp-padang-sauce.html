Udang Saus Padang (Shrimp in Padang Sauce)

▾

Surprise me with

Padang-style sauce, fiery and tomato-based, is a Minangkabau restaurant standard, served over fried shrimp, fish or eggs.

  • Cook 15 min

  • 2 stalks2 stalksGreen onion, sliced

Method

  1. Blend the shallots, garlic, chilies and ginger into a paste.
  2. Heat the oil in a flat-bottom wok over medium heat and fry the paste for 5 minutes until fragrant, then add the onion for 1 minute.
  3. Add the ketchup, chili sauce, oyster sauce, sugar, salt and water and bring to a boil.
  4. Add the shrimp and tomatoes and cook for 2 to 3 minutes, until the shrimp are pink.
  5. Stir in the cornstarch slurry, then drizzle in the egg, stirring gently so it forms ribbons in the thick sauce. Toss in the green onion and serve.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the shrimp makes them tough.
  • Thick slurry makes the sauce gluey.

Authentic tips

  • Also excellent on fried fish or eggs.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • Fried fish or hard-boiled eggs also work in the sauce.
  • Reduce the chilies for a milder dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the sauce keeps 3 days.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Es teh
