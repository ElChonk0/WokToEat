Hù Shì Hēi Jiāo Niú Pái (Shanghai Pepper Steak) 沪式黑椒牛排

▾

Shanghai's Western-style restaurants adapted steak with a black pepper sauce to local tastes in the 20th century, giving rise to a sizzling-plate steak popular today.

  • 600 g1.3 lbBeef sirloin steaks, 1.5 cm (1/2 in) thick
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce, for the marinade
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 8 g1 tbspCornstarch

  • 8 g1 tbspBlack peppercorns, coarsely crushed
  • 30 g2 tbspButter
  • 11Onion, sliced
  • 2 cloves2 clovesGarlic, minced
  • 150 ml2/3 cupBeef stock
  • 15 ml1 tbspWorcestershire sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce, for the sauce
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 5 g2 tspCornstarch, for the sauce, mixed with 2 tbsp water

Method

  1. Pound the steaks lightly with the back of a cleaver to tenderize them. Marinate with the soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, cornstarch and baking soda for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat half the butter in a heavy skillet over high heat and sear the steaks for 1.5 to 2 minutes per side, until browned but pink inside. Rest them on a plate.
  3. Lower the heat, add the remaining butter and the crushed pepper and toast for 30 seconds until fragrant. Add the onion and garlic and cook for 2 minutes.
  4. Add the stock, Worcestershire, soy sauce and sugar, simmer for 1 minute, and thicken with the slurry until glossy.
  5. Return the steaks with their juices, turn them in the sauce for 30 seconds and serve. 'Haipai' Western-style cooking like this came from Shanghai's old cafés.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the rest makes the juices run out.
  • Burning the pepper makes the sauce bitter.

Authentic tips

  • For the sizzling effect, serve on a preheated cast-iron plate.
  • Crush the peppercorns coarsely in a mortar.

Substitutions

  • Ribeye or strip steak replaces the sirloin.
  • Coarsely crushed pepper gives more bite than ground.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; reheat gently so the steak doesn't overcook.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Fries or rice
  • Soup: Russian-style borscht
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot black tea
