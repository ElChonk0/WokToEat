Xiāng Cháng (Taiwanese Sweet Pork Sausage) 香腸

▾

Surprise me with

Taiwanese sausage, sweet and garlicky with a hint of rice wine, is sold from night-market grills and eaten with raw garlic and pickles.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Taiwan Pork LunchDinner

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 300 g10.5 ozTaiwanese sausages
  • 60 g2 ozRaw garlic cloves
  • 100 g3.5 ozPickled cucumber
  • 15 ml1 tbspRice wine
  • 15 ml1 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Prick the sausages in a few places.
  2. Heat the oil in a pan over medium heat.
  3. Cook the sausages for 12 minutes, turning, until browned and cooked through; add the wine in the last minute.
  4. Slice on the diagonal.
  5. Serve with raw garlic and pickled cucumber.

Common mistakes

  • High heat burns the sugary casing before the inside cooks.
  • Pricing too much lets the fat out.

Authentic tips

  • Raw garlic is the traditional pairing.
  • Grill over charcoal for the full night-market flavor.

Substitutions

  • Chinese lap cheong in a pinch, cooked 10 minutes.
  • Add a drizzle of rice wine for the last minute.
  • No grill: bake at 190°C (375°F) for 20 minutes on a rack, turning once, then broil 2 minutes to brown; or pan-sear over medium heat.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat in a pan.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months raw or cooked.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Raw garlic and pickled cucumber
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
