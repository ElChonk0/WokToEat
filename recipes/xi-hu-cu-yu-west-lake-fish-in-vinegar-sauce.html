Xī Hú Cù Yú (West Lake Fish in Vinegar Sauce) 西湖醋鱼

▾

Surprise me with

West Lake vinegar fish is Hangzhou's most famous dish: a grass carp poached whole and glazed with a sweet-sour sauce, attributed to a Song-dynasty tale of two brothers and a corrupt official.

  • 800 g1.75 lbWhole grass carp
  • 40 g3 tbspGinger, sliced and julienned
  • 60 ml4 tbspZhenjiang (Chinkiang) vinegar
  • 45 g3 tbspSugar
  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 30 ml2 tbspCornstarch slurry
  • 300 ml1.25 cupsWater

Method

  1. Bring 1.5 L water to a boil with half the ginger and the wine; cut the fish into halves lengthwise if needed to fit.
  2. Lower the fish into the water, return to a bare simmer and cook 6-8 minutes until just cooked; lift it onto a plate, cut side up.
  3. Reduce 100 ml of the poaching liquid with the soy sauce, sugar and vinegar in a small pan for 2 minutes.
  4. Stir in the slurry until glossy and pour over the fish; scatter with the julienned ginger.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling the fish hard breaks the delicate flesh.
  • A thin sauce will not cling; reduce it before thickening.

Authentic tips

  • Serve warm or at room temperature.
  • Use rice vinegar plus a splash of balsamic if Chinkiang vinegar is unavailable.

Substitutions

  • Sea bass or large trout replaces grass carp.
  • Use a little Worcestershire to balance if the vinegar is sharp.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the sauce keeps 3 days separately.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Longjing tea
