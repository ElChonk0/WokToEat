Xī Hú Cù Yú (West Lake Fish in Vinegar Sauce) 西湖醋鱼

▾

Surprise me with

West Lake vinegar fish is Hangzhou's most famous dish: a grass carp poached whole and glazed with a sweet-sour sauce, attributed to a Song-dynasty tale of two brothers and a corrupt official.

  • 800 g1.75 lbWhole grass carp, cleaned
  • 2 L8 cupsWater, for poaching
  • 20 g3/4 ozGinger, sliced, for poaching
  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 20 g3/4 ozGinger, finely julienned or minced, for the sauce
  • 60 ml1/4 cupZhenjiang (Chinkiang) vinegar
  • 45 g3.5 tbspSugar
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 300 ml1.25 cupsPoaching water
  • 15 g2 tbspCornstarch, mixed with 4 tbsp water

Method

  1. Split the fish in half lengthwise from the head to the tail, leaving the two halves joined at the belly, so it opens flat like a book. Score the thicker half twice so it cooks evenly.
  2. Bring the water to a rolling boil with the sliced ginger, then turn off the heat. Slide in the fish skin-side up, cover and poach in the residual heat for 3 to 4 minutes, until the fins stand up and the eye turns white. Gentle poaching is what gives the flesh its crab-like tenderness.
  3. Lift the fish carefully onto a platter, keeping 300 ml (1.25 cups) of the poaching water.
  4. In a small pan, bring the poaching water to a boil with the Shaoxing wine, soy sauce, sugar and most of the vinegar. Stir in the slurry until thick and glossy, then add the rest of the vinegar.
  5. Pour the sauce over the fish and scatter with the julienned ginger. Hangzhou's most famous dish, named for West Lake.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling the fish hard breaks the delicate flesh.
  • A thin sauce will not cling; reduce it before thickening.

Authentic tips

  • Serve warm or at room temperature.
  • Use rice vinegar plus a splash of balsamic if Chinkiang vinegar is unavailable.

Substitutions

  • Sea bass or large trout replaces grass carp.
  • Use a little Worcestershire to balance if the vinegar is sharp.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the sauce keeps 3 days separately.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Longjing tea
