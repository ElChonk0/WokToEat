Yáo Zhù Jī Tāng (Dried Scallop and Chicken Soup) 瑶柱鸡汤

▾

Surprise me with

Slow-simmered clear soups (lou fo tong) are central to Cantonese home cooking; dried scallops (conpoy) give this chicken soup a deep, sweet savor.

  • Cook 2 hr

Method

  1. Blanch the chicken in boiling water for 3 minutes, drain and rinse; pull off most of the skin to keep the soup clear and light.
  2. Put the chicken, scallops with their soaking water, mushrooms, ginger and dates in a large pot with the water.
  3. Bring to a boil, skim, then simmer gently, partly covered, for 1.5 to 2 hours.
  4. Add the goji berries for the last 10 minutes.
  5. Season with salt and serve the clear soup first, then the chicken and scallops dipped in soy sauce.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard clouds the soup; keep it at a lazy simmer.
  • Salting early concentrates as the soup reduces; season at the end.

Authentic tips

  • Cantonese families drink the soup before the meal and eat the soup ingredients (tong liu) as a side.
  • A slow cooker on low for 6 hours works well.

Substitutions

  • Pork ribs or lean pork replace chicken.
  • Add winter melon or carrot for sweetness.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and stir-fried greens
  • Soup: This is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh oranges
  • Beverage: Hot tea
