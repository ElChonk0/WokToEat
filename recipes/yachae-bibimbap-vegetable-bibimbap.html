Yachae Bibimbap (Vegetable Bibimbap) 야채비빔밥

▾

Surprise me with

Bibimbap, rice mixed with vegetables and gochujang, is Korea's best-known rice dish; the Jeonju version is the most celebrated, and vegetable bibimbap is its classic form.

Method

  1. Stir the gochujang, sugar, water and 1 tablespoon of sesame oil into a smooth sauce.
  2. Blanch the bean sprouts for 1 minute and the spinach for 40 seconds in boiling salted water, cooling each in cold water and squeezing it dry. Season each with a pinch of salt, a little garlic and sesame oil.
  3. Heat a skillet over medium-high heat with a little oil and stir-fry the carrot and zucchini separately for 1 minute each with a pinch of salt. Then cook the mushrooms and tofu with the soy sauce for 2 minutes until browned. Keeping each vegetable separate keeps the colors distinct.
  4. Fry the eggs sunny-side up so the yolks stay runny.
  5. Divide the rice among bowls, arrange the vegetables and tofu in sections on top and set an egg in the center. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and serve with the gochujang sauce to mix in at the table.

Common mistakes

  • Soggy vegetables: squeeze out water.
  • Cold rice dulls the dish.

Authentic tips

  • Hot stone bowl (dolsot) gives a crispy base.
  • Add a tablespoon of sesame oil.

Substitutions

  • Add shredded zucchini, radish and fernbrake.
  • A hot stone bowl gives a crisp rice crust.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps the namul 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Kimchi
  • Soup: Doenjang soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
