Yachae Bibimbap (Vegetable Bibimbap) 야채비빔밥

▾

Surprise me with

Bibimbap, rice mixed with vegetables and gochujang, is Korea's best-known rice dish; the Jeonju version is the most celebrated, and vegetable bibimbap is its classic form.

  • Cook 15 min

Method

  1. Blanch the spinach and sprouts separately 1 minute; squeeze and season with sesame oil and salt.
  2. Sauté the carrot, zucchini and shiitake separately in a little oil with salt, 2 minutes each.
  3. Fry the eggs sunny-side up.
  4. Divide the warm rice between bowls and arrange the vegetables around the edge.
  5. Top with the egg and gochujang and mix at the table.

Common mistakes

  • Soggy vegetables: squeeze out water.
  • Cold rice dulls the dish.

Authentic tips

  • Hot stone bowl (dolsot) gives a crispy base.
  • Add a tablespoon of sesame oil.

Substitutions

  • Add shredded zucchini, radish and fernbrake.
  • A hot stone bowl gives a crisp rice crust.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps the namul 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Kimchi
  • Soup: Doenjang soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
