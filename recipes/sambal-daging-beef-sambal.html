Sambal Daging (Malay Beef in Sambal)

Sambal daging, beef cooked in a rich chili paste, is a Malay rice-table staple, often served at weddings and feasts.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 40 min
  • Difficulty Medium
  • Draft Malaysia / Singapore Beef LunchDinnerBraise / stewSpicy

  • 600 g1.3 lbBeef, sliced
  • 80 g3 ozChilies
  • 100 g3.5 ozShallot and garlic
  • 15 g1/2 ozTamarind
  • 15 g1 tbspPalm sugar
  • 100 g3.5 ozTomato
  • 5 g1 tspSalt
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 200 ml3/4 cupWater

Method

  1. Blend the chilies, shallot, garlic and tomato into a paste.
  2. Fry the paste in the oil over medium heat for 10 minutes until the oil separates.
  3. Add the beef and cook 5 minutes.
  4. Pour in the water, cover and simmer 30 minutes until tender.
  5. Season with the tamarind, sugar and salt.

Common mistakes

  • Under-frying the paste leaves it raw and sharp.
  • Boiling hard toughens the beef.

Authentic tips

  • Cook until the sauce clings.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • Chicken or lamb take the same sambal.
  • Reduce the chilies for a milder dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Nasi lemak or steamed rice
  • Soup: Cucumber salad
  • Dessert: Kuih
  • Beverage: Teh tarik
