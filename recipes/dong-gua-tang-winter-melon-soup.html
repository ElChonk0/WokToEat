Dōng Guā Tāng (Winter Melon and Mushroom Soup) 冬瓜汤

▾

Surprise me with

Cooling soups such as winter melon are a pillar of Cantonese home cooking, taken in the heat of summer to balance the body in line with traditional Chinese food principles.

  • Cook 30 min

  • 600 g1.3 lbWinter melon, peeled and cut into 3 cm (1 in) chunks
  • 66Dried shiitake mushrooms
  • 10 g1/3 ozDried kombu (optional)
  • 20 g2/3 ozGinger, sliced
  • 10 g1 tbspDried goji berries
  • 1.2 L5 cupsWater
  • 5 g1 tspSalt
  • 0.5 g1/4 tspGround white pepper
  • 3 ml1/2 tspSesame oil
  • 20 g2 tbspScallion, chopped

Method

  1. Rinse the shiitake and soak them in 250 ml (1 cup) of the water for 30 minutes, then slice them, keeping the soaking water. Wipe the kombu with a damp cloth.
  2. Peel the winter melon thickly, scoop out the seeds and soft core and cut it into even chunks so they all turn tender at the same time.
  3. Put the mushrooms, their soaking water, the remaining water, kombu and ginger in a pot and bring slowly to a simmer over medium heat. Lift out the kombu just before it boils, so the broth stays clean rather than slimy.
  4. Add the winter melon and simmer, partly covered, for 15 to 20 minutes, until it turns translucent and a chopstick slides through. Do not boil it hard, or the melon breaks down and clouds the soup.
  5. Add the goji berries for the last 3 minutes, so they plump without going mushy. Season with the salt and pepper, add a few drops of sesame oil and serve topped with scallion.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking until the melon disintegrates.
  • Skipping the mushrooms, which supply savory depth.

Authentic tips

  • The melon should be translucent but still hold its shape.
  • Use the mushroom soaking water in the soup.

Substitutions

  • Chayote or zucchini replace winter melon; cook 10 minutes less.
  • Add tofu cubes for protein.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat gently.

Freezing: Freezes acceptably for 1 month, but the melon softens.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot chrysanthemum tea
