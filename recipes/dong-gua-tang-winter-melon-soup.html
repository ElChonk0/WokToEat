Dōng Guā Tāng (Winter Melon and Mushroom Soup) 冬瓜汤

▾

Surprise me with

Cooling soups such as winter melon are a pillar of Cantonese home cooking, taken in the heat of summer to balance the body in line with traditional Chinese food principles.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 30 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 600 g1.3 lbWinter melon, peeled and cubed
  • 30 g1 ozDried shiitake mushrooms
  • 20 g1 tbspGinger
  • 10 g1 tbspDried goji berries
  • 1.2 L5 cupsWater or kombu stock
  • 5 g1 tspSalt
  • 20 g2 tbspScallion

Method

  1. Soak the dried shiitake in warm water for 30 minutes, squeeze, trim the stems and slice; keep the soaking liquid.
  2. Bring the water or stock and the mushroom liquid to a boil with the ginger and shiitake and simmer 10 minutes.
  3. Add the cubed winter melon and simmer 15 minutes, until the cubes turn translucent but still hold their shape.
  4. Add the goji berries and salt and cook 3 minutes more.
  5. Ladle into bowls and garnish with scallion.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking until the melon disintegrates.
  • Skipping the mushrooms, which supply savory depth.

Authentic tips

  • The melon should be translucent but still hold its shape.
  • Use the mushroom soaking water in the soup.

Substitutions

  • Chayote or zucchini replace winter melon; cook 10 minutes less.
  • Add tofu cubes for protein.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat gently.

Freezing: Freezes acceptably for 1 month, but the melon softens.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot chrysanthemum tea
