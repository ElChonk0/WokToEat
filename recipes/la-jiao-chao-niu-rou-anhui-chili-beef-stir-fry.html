La Jiao Chao Niu Rou (Anhui Chili Beef Stir-Fry) 辣椒炒牛肉

Anhui cooks use fresh chilies in quick stir-fries of beef, an everyday home dish.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 8 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Anhui Beef LunchDinnerStir-fryDeep-friedSpicy30 min or less

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 500 g1.1 lbBeef sirloin, sliced
  • 150 g5 ozGreen and red chilies
  • 30 g2 tbspGarlic and ginger
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 10 g2 tspCornstarch
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Slice the beef 4 mm thick across the grain and toss with the soy sauce, cornstarch and wine; leave 15 minutes. Cut the chilies into 3 cm pieces.
  2. Heat half the oil in a wok over high heat until smoking and sear the beef 45 seconds; remove.
  3. Add the remaining oil, garlic and ginger for 15 seconds, then the chilies and stir-fry 2 minutes until blistered.
  4. Return the beef and toss 30 seconds.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking makes the beef tough.
  • A crowded or cool pan steams the beef.

Authentic tips

  • Use a mix of mild and hot chilies.
  • Serve over rice.

Substitutions

  • Pork loin takes the same method.
  • Mix mild and hot chilies for balance.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
