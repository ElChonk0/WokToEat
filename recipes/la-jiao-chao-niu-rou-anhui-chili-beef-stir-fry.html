Là Jiāo Chǎo Niú Ròu (Anhui Chili Beef Stir-Fry) 辣椒炒牛肉

▾

Anhui cooks use fresh chilies in quick stir-fries of beef, an everyday home dish.

  • Cook 8 min

  • 500 g1.1 lbBeef sirloin, sliced thin
  • 10 ml2 tspLight soy sauce, for the marinade
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 10 g1 tbspCornstarch
  • 150 g5 ozGreen and red chilies, sliced
  • 2 g1/3 tspSalt
  • 30 g1 ozGarlic and ginger, sliced
  • 10 ml2 tspLight soy sauce, for the sauce
  • 2 g1/2 tspSugar
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Toss the beef with the marinade soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, cornstarch and a tablespoon of water and leave for 15 minutes.
  2. Dry-fry the chilies in the wok with the salt over medium heat for 2 minutes until blistered. Lift them out.
  3. Heat the oil over high heat and stir-fry the beef for 1 to 2 minutes until just cooked. Push aside.
  4. Fry the garlic and ginger for 15 seconds.
  5. Return the chilies with the sauce soy sauce and sugar and toss over high heat for 30 seconds. Serve with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking makes the beef tough.
  • A crowded or cool pan steams the beef.

Authentic tips

  • Use a mix of mild and hot chilies.
  • Serve over rice.

Substitutions

  • Pork loin takes the same method.
  • Mix mild and hot chilies for balance.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
