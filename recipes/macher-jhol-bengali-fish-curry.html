Macher Jhol (Bengali Fish Curry)

▾

Surprise me with

Macher jhol, a light turmeric and nigella fish stew, is the everyday Bengali fish curry, eaten with rice across West Bengal and Bangladesh.

  • 5 g2 tspTurmeric
  • 3 g1 tspNigella seeds
  • 20 g1 tbspGinger
  • 15 g1/2 ozGreen chilies
  • 60 ml4 tbspMustard oil
  • 5 g1 tspSalt

Method

  1. Rub the fish with turmeric and salt and fry lightly in the mustard oil for 2 minutes per side; remove.
  2. Temper the nigella seeds in the oil, then add ginger, tomato and potatoes.
  3. Add 400 ml water and simmer 10 minutes.
  4. Return the fish and chilies and cook 6 minutes.
  5. Serve with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Overfrying hardens the fish.
  • Stirring breaks it.

Authentic tips

  • Mustard oil must be heated until it stops smelling raw.
  • Rohu is traditional.

Substitutions

  • Tilapia or catfish replace rohu.
  • Cauliflower is a classic addition.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Cucumber salad
  • Dessert: Mishti doi
  • Beverage: Black tea
