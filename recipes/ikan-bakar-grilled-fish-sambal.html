Ikan Bakar (Indonesian Grilled Fish with Sambal)

▾

Surprise me with

Ikan bakar, fish grilled over coconut husk or charcoal and brushed with sweet soy, is the quintessential Indonesian coastal dish, found from Jimbaran Bay to Makassar.

Method

  1. Score the fish deeply on both sides, rub with lime juice and salt and leave for 15 minutes.
  2. Blend the shallots, garlic, turmeric, ginger, candlenuts and chilies and fry the paste in the oil for 5 minutes until fragrant. Cool, then mix half of it with the kecap manis and melted butter to make the basting sauce.
  3. Rub the remaining paste into the cuts and cavity of the fish.
  4. Grill the fish in an oiled fish basket over medium charcoal, or on a rack under a broiler, for 8 to 10 minutes per side, brushing with the basting sauce every few minutes, until charred and the flesh flakes at the backbone. Turning it only once keeps the skin from tearing.
  5. Serve with sambal, rice and raw vegetables (lalapan).

Common mistakes

  • Moving the fish too soon tears the skin.
  • Burning the sweet soy; brush only in the last minutes.

Authentic tips

  • An oiled grill basket prevents sticking.
  • Coconut husk charcoal gives a distinctive smoke.

Substitutions

  • A broiler replaces the charcoal grill.
  • Fillets cook in 6-8 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice, kangkung and sambal
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Es kelapa muda
