Dàn Huā Tāng (Egg Drop Soup) 蛋花汤

▾

Surprise me with

Egg drop soup, egg beaten into hot thickened stock to form silky ribbons, is a simple everyday soup across southern China and a fixture of Cantonese restaurant menus abroad.

Method

  1. Beat the eggs with the water using a fork in an upward, lifting motion until smooth. Mix the slurry. Dice the tofu small and chop the carrot.
  2. Bring the stock and ginger to a boil in a large pot over high heat, add the tofu, carrot and corn and return to a boil. Fish out the ginger.
  3. Turn off the heat and stir in the slurry a little at a time with a ladle; adding it off the heat stops lumps forming. Turn the heat back to high and bring the soup back to a boil, when it will turn glossy and lightly thick.
  4. Turn the heat to low. Stir the soup in a steady circle, reaching right to the bottom, and drizzle in the eggs in a thin stream from a height. Keep stirring gently for a few seconds as they set into fine, silky ribbons.
  5. Season with the salt, sugar and pepper, taste, then add the sesame oil. Serve topped with green onion.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Pouring the egg into rapidly boiling soup breaks it into cloudy crumbs.
  • Stirring right after adding the egg breaks up the ribbons.

Authentic tips

  • Thickening the soup before adding the egg is what keeps the ribbons suspended.
  • A pinch of sugar rounds out a vegetable stock.

Substitutions

  • Chicken stock is the usual restaurant base, though the soup is then no longer vegetarian.
  • Seaweed egg drop soup (zi cai dan hua tang): leave out the tofu, corn and cornstarch, and add 10 g torn dried laver and 1 tablespoon of light soy sauce 1 minute before the egg.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator; reheat gently without boiling.

Freezing: Not recommended; the egg turns rubbery.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Fried rice or chow mein
  • Soup: This is the soup course
  • Dessert: Almond jelly
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
