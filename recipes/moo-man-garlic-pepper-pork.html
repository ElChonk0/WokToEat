Moo Man (Garlic Pepper Pork) หมูกระเทียมพริกไทย

▾

Surprise me with

A simple, garlicky Thai favorite, moo man marinates pork in garlic and white pepper before frying until crisp — a common accompaniment to rice porridge or a standalone dish with steamed rice.

  • Prep 15 min (plus 1 hr marinating)
  • Cook 12 min

Method

  1. Pound garlic, cilantro root, and white pepper into a rough paste.
  2. Combine the paste with pork, fish sauce, sugar, and oyster sauce; marinate at least 1 hour.
  3. Fry the marinated pork in a hot pan with a little oil until browned and slightly crisp at the edges, 8–10 minutes.
  4. Garnish with cilantro leaves and serve.

Common mistakes

  • Rushing the marinating time, which doesn't allow the garlic and pepper flavors to fully penetrate the meat.
  • Cooking over too gentle heat, which steams the pork instead of letting it brown and crisp.

Authentic tips

  • This dish relies on generous amounts of garlic — don't be shy with the quantity.
  • Frying until genuinely crisp at the edges, not just cooked through, is key to the dish's texture.

Substitutions

  • Chicken thigh works well with the same marinade and method.
  • This dish is also excellent served alongside Thai rice porridge (khao tom) for breakfast.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot pan to re-crisp.

Freezing: Freezes well, marinated and raw, for up to a month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Jasmine rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
