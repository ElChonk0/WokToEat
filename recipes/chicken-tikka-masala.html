Chicken Tikka Masala

▾

Surprise me with

Chicken tikka masala, grilled chicken in a spiced tomato-cream sauce, is a hybrid dish popularized in Britain by South Asian restaurants and counted among the most popular curries in the UK.

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken in yogurt and tikka spices for 2 hours, then grill or broil it for 8-10 minutes until charred at the edges.
  2. Melt the butter in a pan and fry the onion until golden, 8 minutes.
  3. Add the garlic, ginger, garam masala and chili and fry 1 minute, then add the tomato puree and salt.
  4. Simmer 15 minutes until the oil separates, then stir in the cream.
  5. Add the chicken and simmer 5 minutes.

Common mistakes

  • Undercooked onions give a raw taste.
  • Boiling after adding the cream can split it.

Authentic tips

  • Finish with kasuri methi.
  • Serve with naan or rice.

Substitutions

  • Boneless thighs work best; breast dries out.
  • Store-bought tikka paste speeds the marinade.
  • No grill: spread the marinated chicken on a foil-lined sheet pan and roast at 230°C (450°F) for 12 to 15 minutes, or broil 8 to 10 minutes, turning once, until charred at the edges.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; thin with water.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months; add the cream after thawing.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Basmati rice or naan
  • Soup: Cucumber raita
  • Dessert: Gulab jamun
  • Beverage: Mango lassi
