Yín Yú Gēng (Whitebait Soup) 银鱼羹

▾

Surprise me with

Whitebait soup, a thickened egg-flower soup, is a gentle Anhui dish that showcases the tender silvery fish of Chaohu Lake.

  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 200 g7 ozWhitebait
  • 22Eggs
  • 800 ml3.5 cupsChicken stock
  • 45 ml3 tbspCornstarch slurry
  • 20 g1 ozHam
  • 5 g1 tspSalt and white pepper
  • 20 g2 tbspScallion

Method

  1. Bring the stock to a boil in a pot.
  2. Add the whitebait and shredded ham and simmer 2 minutes.
  3. Stir in the slurry a little at a time until the soup thickens to a loose chowder.
  4. Slowly pour in the beaten eggs in a thin stream, stirring once.
  5. Season with the salt and pepper and garnish with scallion.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the eggs too fast makes clumps; stream them in.
  • An over-thick slurry makes the soup gluey.

Authentic tips

  • Stir gently for fine ribbons of egg.
  • Serve hot.

Substitutions

  • Small shrimp replace whitebait.
  • Add crab meat for a richer soup.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
