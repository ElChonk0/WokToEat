Yín Yú Gēng (Whitebait Soup) 银鱼羹

▾

Surprise me with

Whitebait soup, a thickened egg-flower soup, is a gentle Anhui dish that showcases the tender silvery fish of Chaohu Lake.

  • Cook 10 min

Ingredients

  • 200 g7 ozWhitebait (silverfish)
  • 900 ml3.75 cupsChicken stock
  • 20 g3/4 ozCured ham, finely shredded
  • 33Dried shiitake, soaked and finely shredded
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt
  • 1 g1/2 tspWhite pepper
  • 25 g3 tbspCornstarch, mixed with 4 tbsp water
  • 22Eggs (whites only, or whole), beaten
  • 20 g3/4 ozGreen onion and cilantro, chopped
  • 5 ml1 tspSesame oil

Method

  1. Rinse the whitebait and drain.
  2. Bring the stock to a boil with the ham and shiitake and simmer for 5 minutes. Season with salt and white pepper.
  3. Stir in the cornstarch slurry until the soup thickens enough to coat a spoon.
  4. Add the whitebait and return to a gentle simmer for 1 minute, then drizzle in the beaten egg while stirring slowly so it forms fine ribbons.
  5. Finish with green onion, cilantro and sesame oil.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the eggs too fast makes clumps; stream them in.
  • An over-thick slurry makes the soup gluey.

Authentic tips

  • Stir gently for fine ribbons of egg.
  • Serve hot.

Substitutions

  • Small shrimp replace whitebait.
  • Add crab meat for a richer soup.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
