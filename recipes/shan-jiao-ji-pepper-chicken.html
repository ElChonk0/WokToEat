Shān Jiāo Jī (Anhui Mountain Pepper Chicken) 山椒鸡

▾

Surprise me with

Pickled mountain peppers add fiery sourness to chicken in the highlands of Anhui and neighboring regions.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 600 g1.3 lbChicken thighs, sliced
  • 80 g3 ozPickled mountain peppers
  • 40 g3 tbspGarlic and ginger
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 g1 tbspCornstarch
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Slice the chicken thin and toss with the soy sauce, cornstarch, wine and 1 tablespoon of water; leave 10 minutes. Chop the pickled peppers.
  2. Heat half the oil in a wok over high heat and stir-fry the chicken 2 minutes until golden; remove.
  3. Add the remaining oil, then the pickled peppers, garlic and ginger and fry 1 minute until sharp and fragrant.
  4. Return the chicken with a splash of the pickling brine and toss 1 minute.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the peppers turns the dish bitter.
  • Overcooking the chicken dries it.

Authentic tips

  • Use the brine for extra sourness.
  • Pickled jalapenos stand in for the mountain peppers.

Substitutions

  • Pickled jalapenos replace pickled mountain peppers.
  • Pork loin takes the same method.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
