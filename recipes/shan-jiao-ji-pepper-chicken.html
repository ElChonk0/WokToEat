Shān Jiāo Jī (Anhui Mountain Pepper Chicken) 山椒鸡

▾

Surprise me with

Pickled mountain peppers add fiery sourness to chicken in the highlands of Anhui and neighboring regions.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Ingredients

  • 600 g1.3 lbChicken thighs, boneless, sliced
  • 10 ml2 tspLight soy sauce, for the marinade
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 10 g1 tbspCornstarch
  • 80 g3 ozPickled mountain peppers (pao shan jiao), chopped, with a little brine
  • 40 g1.5 ozGarlic and ginger, sliced
  • 100 g3.5 ozCelery or green pepper, sliced
  • 5 ml1 tspLight soy sauce, for the sauce
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Toss the chicken with the marinade soy sauce, Shaoxing wine and cornstarch.
  2. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a flat-bottom wok over high heat and stir-fry the chicken for 3 minutes until cooked. Lift it out.
  3. Add the remaining oil and fry the garlic, ginger and pickled peppers for 1 minute until fragrant and tangy.
  4. Add the celery for 1 minute.
  5. Return the chicken with a splash of pepper brine and the sauce soy sauce and toss for 30 seconds. The pickled peppers bring a sharp, sour heat.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the peppers turns the dish bitter.
  • Overcooking the chicken dries it.

Authentic tips

  • Use the brine for extra sourness.
  • Pickled jalapenos stand in for the mountain peppers.

Substitutions

  • Pickled jalapenos replace pickled mountain peppers.
  • Pork loin takes the same method.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
