Bibim Naengmyeon (Spicy Cold Buckwheat Noodles) 비빔냉면

▾

Surprise me with

Bibim naengmyeon, chewy buckwheat noodles tossed in a sweet, spicy chili sauce and topped with beef, pear and egg, is the spicy partner of mul naengmyeon and a Hamheung specialty.

Method

  1. Simmer the beef in the water for 50 minutes until tender. Chill it and slice very thinly; keep the broth for mul naengmyeon or soup.
  2. Mix the gochujang, gochugaru, grated pear, vinegar, sugar, soy sauce, garlic and sesame oil, and refrigerate.
  3. Boil the noodles for 30 to 60 seconds, then rinse vigorously in ice water and drain.
  4. Twist the noodles into bowls and top with sauce, beef, cucumber, pear slices and half an egg.
  5. Sprinkle with sesame seeds; cut the noodles with scissors at the table and mix.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking turns the noodles soft; they should be very chewy.
  • Warm noodles make the sauce slide off; chill them in ice water.

Authentic tips

  • Restaurants serve a cup of hot beef broth on the side.
  • Make the sauce a day ahead so the flavors meld.

Substitutions

  • Soba noodles in place of naengmyeon noodles.
  • Leave out the beef for a lighter dish.

Storage

Leftovers: The beef and sauce keep 4 days; cook noodles fresh.

Freezing: The cooked beef freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled radish (mu-saengchae)
  • Soup: Hot beef broth
  • Dessert: Sliced melon
  • Beverage: Cold barley tea
