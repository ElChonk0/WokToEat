Bibim Naengmyeon (Spicy Cold Buckwheat Noodles) 비빔냉면

▾

Surprise me with

Bibim naengmyeon, chewy buckwheat noodles tossed in a sweet, spicy chili sauce and topped with beef, pear and egg, is the spicy partner of mul naengmyeon and a Hamheung specialty.

  • Cook 1 hr

Method

  1. Simmer the beef in the water with the onion, garlic and ginger, skimming, for about 1 hour until tender. Cool it in the broth, then slice it thinly; chill the broth and keep 60 ml (4 tbsp) for the sauce.
  2. Toss the radish strips with the vinegar, sugar and salt and leave for 20 minutes to pickle lightly.
  3. Mix the gochugaru, gochujang, grated pear, sugar, vinegar, soy sauce, garlic, cold broth and sesame oil into a smooth sauce. It tastes best made a few hours ahead and chilled, so the chili flakes soften and the flavors meld.
  4. Boil the noodles according to the packet, usually 1 to 2 minutes, stirring to separate them. Drain and plunge them into ice water, rubbing them between your hands under cold running water until they are very cold and no longer starchy. This gives naengmyeon its springy chew.
  5. Squeeze out the water, coil the noodles in chilled bowls, spoon the sauce on top and arrange the beef, radish, cucumber, pear slices and half an egg on each. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and mix everything together at the table, snipping the long noodles with scissors.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking turns the noodles soft; they should be very chewy.
  • Warm noodles make the sauce slide off; chill them in ice water.

Authentic tips

  • Restaurants serve a cup of hot beef broth on the side.
  • Make the sauce a day ahead so the flavors meld.

Substitutions

  • Soba noodles in place of naengmyeon noodles.
  • Leave out the beef for a lighter dish.

Storage

Leftovers: The beef and sauce keep 4 days; cook noodles fresh.

Freezing: The cooked beef freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled radish (mu-saengchae)
  • Soup: Hot beef broth
  • Dessert: Sliced melon
  • Beverage: Cold barley tea
