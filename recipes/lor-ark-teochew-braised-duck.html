Lor Ark (Teochew Braised Duck) 卤鸭

▾

Surprise me with

Lor ark is a Teochew dish of duck braised in a rich soy, galangal and spice stock; hawkers in Singapore and Malaysia serve it with rice, tofu and eggs from the same pot.

Method

  1. Rub the duck inside and out with salt, leave for 15 minutes and rinse. Scald it in boiling water for 3 to 5 minutes, then pluck off any remaining pin feathers. Use a young Pekin or Long Island duck; tough stewing ducks stay chewy.
  2. Heat the oil in a large Dutch oven over medium heat and fry the garlic, galangal and cilantro roots for 3 minutes until fragrant. Add the rock sugar and stir until it melts and turns light amber, then add the star anise, cinnamon and cloves.
  3. Add the duck and turn it in the caramel and spices for 2 to 3 minutes to color it, then add both soy sauces and the water, so the duck is mostly submerged.
  4. Bring to a boil, then cover and simmer gently for 1 to 1.5 hours, turning the duck every 20 minutes and spooning the liquid over, until a chopstick slides easily into the thigh. Add the eggs and tofu for the last 30 minutes.
  5. Lift out the duck and rest it for 10 minutes. Thicken a quarter of the braising liquid with a little starch slurry if you like a gravy; save the rest as a master stock.
  6. Chop the duck through the bone, arrange it with the halved eggs and tofu, spoon the gravy over and serve with rice, cilantro and garlic-chili vinegar.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling the braise hard toughens the duck; keep it at a gentle simmer.
  • Skimming too little leaves a greasy sauce; skim the fat from the surface.

Authentic tips

  • The master stock can be reused: strain, boil and freeze it.
  • Leave the duck in the sauce for an hour after cooking for deeper flavor.

Substitutions

  • Duck legs replace a whole duck.
  • Pork belly or chicken thighs braise in the same sauce.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days in the refrigerator in its sauce.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice, pickled green chilies and a chili-garlic dip
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot Chinese tea
