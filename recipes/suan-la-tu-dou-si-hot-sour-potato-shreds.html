Suān Là Tǔ Dòu Sī (Hot and Sour Shredded Potato) 酸辣土豆丝

▾

Surprise me with

Hot and sour potato shreds, crunchy matchsticks stir-fried with vinegar and chili, are an everyday dish in Sichuan and across northern and western China.

  • 600 g1.3 lbPotatoes, julienned
  • 8 g1/4 ozDried chilies
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 30 ml2 tbspChinkiang vinegar
  • 10 ml2 tspLight soy sauce
  • 2 g1 tspSichuan peppercorns
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Peel the potatoes and cut into very fine matchsticks (a mandoline helps).
  2. Rinse the potato in several changes of cold water until the water runs clear, then soak for 10 minutes; drain very well.
  3. Heat the oil in a wok over high heat and fry the chilies and peppercorns for 15 seconds.
  4. Add the garlic and potato and stir-fry over high heat for 2-3 minutes, until the strips turn translucent but are still crisp.
  5. Add the soy sauce, vinegar and salt, toss 20 seconds and serve.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the rinse leaves the starch on the potato, so the strips stick and turn gluey.
  • Overcooking makes the potato soft; it should keep a slight crunch.

Authentic tips

  • Add the vinegar at the end for brightness.
  • Cut the potato as evenly as possible.

Substitutions

  • Sweet potato makes a sweeter, softer dish.
  • Green pepper adds color.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated; best eaten fresh for crunch.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Tofu soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
