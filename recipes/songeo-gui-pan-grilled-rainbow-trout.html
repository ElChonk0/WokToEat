Songeo Gui (Pan-Grilled Rainbow Trout) 송어구이

▾

Surprise me with

Rainbow trout (songeo) is farmed in the cold mountain streams of Gangwon Province, where it is eaten raw as hoe or grilled with salt or a light soy marinade.

  • Cook 10 min

Method

  1. Pat the trout dry and score the skin side two or three times. Mix the soy sauce, rice wine, sesame oil, garlic, ginger and pepper and brush it over the flesh side only; leave for 15 minutes.
  2. Wipe the skin dry again with a paper towel. Dry skin is what crisps and keeps the fish from sticking.
  3. Heat the oil in a large nonstick or cast-iron skillet over medium-high heat. Lay the fillets in skin-side down, pressing them flat with a spatula for the first 20 seconds so they do not curl.
  4. Cook without moving for 4 to 5 minutes, until the skin is crisp and golden and the flesh is opaque most of the way up. Turn and cook the flesh side for 1 minute, brushing on any remaining marinade, which caramelizes quickly.
  5. Serve skin-side up, scattered with scallions and sesame seeds, with lemon wedges and rice.

Cook’s notes

Written for rainbow trout fillets (skin-on).

Common mistakes

  • A wet fillet sticks and tears; pat it dry before it goes in the pan.
  • Marinating longer than 30 minutes makes the thin flesh salty and firm.

Authentic tips

  • A cast-iron or carbon-steel skillet gives the best crisp skin on gas, electric or induction.
  • Wrap the cooked fillet in lettuce with ssamjang for a quick ssam.

Substitutions

  • Salmon replaces the trout.
  • A whole cleaned trout works; add 3 minutes per side.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and spinach namul
  • Soup: Doenjang jjigae
  • Dessert: Sliced pear
  • Beverage: Barley tea (boricha)
