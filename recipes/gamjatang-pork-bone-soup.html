Gamjatang (Pork Bone and Potato Soup) 감자탕

▾

Surprise me with

Gamjatang, a spicy soup of pork neck bones, potato and perilla, is a Korean late-night favorite with origins in the Incheon area.

Method

  1. Soak the bones in cold water for at least 1 hour, changing the water once, to draw out the blood. Put them in a pot of cold water, bring to a boil and boil for 7 to 8 minutes, then drain, rinse each bone and scrub the pot. This parboil gives a clean broth.
  2. Return the bones to the pot with the measured water, onion, whole garlic, ginger and green onion white. Boil uncovered over medium-high heat for 10 minutes, then cover and cook over medium-low for 50 to 60 minutes, until the meat is tender and pulls from the bone and the broth turns cloudy. Top up with hot water if it drops below the bones.
  3. Mix the gochugaru, gochujang, doenjang, soup soy sauce and minced garlic into a seasoning paste. Blanch the cabbage in boiling water for 4 to 5 minutes, squeeze it dry, cut it into pieces and toss with 1 tablespoon of the paste. Parboil the potatoes in the same water until nearly tender.
  4. Lift out and discard the aromatics, keeping about 1.2 L (5 cups) of broth with the bones. Add the cabbage, potatoes and the remaining seasoning paste and boil for 10 minutes so the flavors blend.
  5. Add the perilla leaves and green onion, boil 2 minutes more, then stir in the ground perilla seeds, which thicken the broth and give its nutty depth. Season with salt and pepper.
  6. Serve bubbling in bowls with rice and kimchi, with a dish on the table for the bones.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the soak leaves a gamey, dark broth.
  • Adding the potatoes too early makes them disintegrate.

Authentic tips

  • Perilla seeds give a nutty, earthy depth.
  • Serve with rice and kimchi.

Substitutions

  • Beef bones replace pork bones.
  • Ground perilla seeds are essential; sesame seeds are a distant substitute.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; reheat gently.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months without the potatoes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and kimchi
  • Soup: Not needed; the stew is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
