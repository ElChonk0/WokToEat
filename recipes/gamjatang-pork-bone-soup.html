Gamjatang (Pork Bone and Potato Soup) 감자탕

▾

Surprise me with

Gamjatang, a spicy soup of pork neck bones, potato and perilla, is a Korean late-night favorite with origins in the Incheon area.

  • Cook 2 hr

  • 1 kg2.2 lbPork neck bones

  • 200 g7 ozNapa cabbage leaves, blanched
  • 30 g1 ozGochugaru
  • 30 g1 ozDoenjang
  • 60 g4 tbspGarlic and ginger
  • 30 g1 ozPerilla seeds, ground
  • 6 g1 tspSalt
  • 2.5 L10 cupsWater

Method

  1. Soak the bones in cold water for 1 hour, changing the water once, to draw out blood.
  2. Boil the bones in fresh water for 10 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  3. Return to the pot with 2.5 L water, the doenjang, ginger and half the garlic and simmer, covered, for 90 minutes until the meat falls off the bone.
  4. Add the potatoes, cabbage and gochugaru and simmer 25 minutes.
  5. Stir in the remaining garlic and perilla seeds and season with the salt.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the soak leaves a gamey, dark broth.
  • Adding the potatoes too early makes them disintegrate.

Authentic tips

  • Perilla seeds give a nutty, earthy depth.
  • Serve with rice and kimchi.

Substitutions

  • Beef bones replace pork bones.
  • Ground perilla seeds are essential; sesame seeds are a distant substitute.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; reheat gently.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months without the potatoes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and kimchi
  • Soup: Not needed; the stew is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
