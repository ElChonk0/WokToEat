Táinán Xiā Juǎn (Tainan Shrimp Rolls) 蝦捲

▾

Surprise me with

Shrimp rolls, minced shrimp and pork wrapped in tofu skin and fried, are a Tainan banquet appetizer and street snack.

  • Prep 30 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 30 g2 tbspScallion and ginger
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 5 ml1 tspSesame oil
  • 400 ml1.75 cupsNeutral oil for frying

Method

  1. Mix the shrimp, pork, water chestnuts, scallion, ginger, soy sauce and sesame oil until sticky.
  2. Soften the tofu skin with a damp cloth and cut into squares.
  3. Roll the filling in the skin into tight cylinders, sealing with a dab of water.
  4. Fry at 170 C (340 F) for 5 minutes until golden and cooked through.
  5. Drain and slice; serve with sweet chili sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Loose rolls open in the oil.
  • Oil that is too hot browns the skin before the filling cooks.

Authentic tips

  • Water chestnuts add crunch.
  • Make ahead and freeze.

Substitutions

  • Spring roll wrappers replace tofu skin.
  • Steam first, then pan-fry, for a lighter roll.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; recrisp in a hot oven.

Freezing: Freeze uncooked rolls for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Oolong tea
