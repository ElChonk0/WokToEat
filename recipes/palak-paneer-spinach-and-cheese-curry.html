Palak Paneer (Spinach and Paneer Curry)

▾

Surprise me with

Palak paneer, paneer cheese in a spiced spinach gravy, is one of North India's best-known vegetarian dishes, a staple of Punjabi cooking.

Method

  1. Blanch the spinach and green chilies in boiling water for 1 to 2 minutes, just until wilted, then plunge into ice water. Quick blanching and chilling keeps the bright green color. Blend to a smooth puree.
  2. Soak the paneer in hot salted water for 10 minutes to soften it, or sear it in a little ghee until golden.
  3. Heat the ghee in a pan and fry the cumin seeds for 20 seconds, then the onion for 6 to 8 minutes until golden. Add the ginger-garlic paste for 1 minute, then the tomato, coriander, cumin, chili and salt for 5 minutes until soft.
  4. Add the spinach puree and simmer for 3 to 4 minutes; longer and it turns olive-drab.
  5. Add the paneer, garam masala, crushed kasuri methi and cream and simmer for 2 minutes. Serve with roti or naan.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the spinach dulls the color.
  • Boiling the paneer makes it rubbery.

Authentic tips

  • Soak the paneer in warm water first for softness.
  • Tofu is a vegan substitute.

Substitutions

  • Firm tofu replaces paneer for a vegan version.
  • Frozen spinach works; thaw and squeeze.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; the color dulls.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months; add cream after thawing.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Naan or basmati rice
  • Soup: Cucumber raita
  • Dessert: Kheer
  • Beverage: Masala chai
