Teochew Steamed Cod with Sour Plum

▾

Surprise me with

Teochew steamed fish, seasoned with salted sour plum, tomato, preserved vegetable and shiitake, is a dish of the Teochew community in Singapore and Malaysia, known for its clean, tart broth.

Method

  1. Pat the cod dry and lay it on a deep heatproof plate on a bed of half the ginger, mushrooms and mustard greens.
  2. Mash the sour plums lightly with the garlic and scatter them over the fish with the tomatoes and the rest of the ginger, mushrooms and greens. Pour the water, soy sauce, sugar and pepper around the fish. The sour plum and salted greens give a clean, tangy, savory broth typical of Teochew steaming.
  3. Set a rack in a flat-bottom wok or steamer and bring the water to a full boil before the plate goes in. Steam covered over high heat for 8 to 10 minutes for thick loin pieces, until the fish flakes easily and is opaque in the center.
  4. Heat the oil until it shimmers and pour it over the fish so it sizzles.
  5. Scatter with scallions and cilantro and serve at once, spooning the tangy broth over rice or porridge.

Cook’s notes

Written for cod loin. The Teochew original uses pomfret or other whole fish.

Common mistakes

  • Salted plums vary in saltiness, so taste before adding soy sauce.
  • Over-steaming dries the cod.

Authentic tips

  • The sauce on the plate should be tart and light; spoon it over rice.
  • Use the thickest cut of cod so it stays moist.

Substitutions

  • Haddock or Chilean sea bass replaces the cod.
  • Pickled green plums replace the sour plum.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and stir-fried greens
  • Soup: The sauce doubles as soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot Chinese tea
