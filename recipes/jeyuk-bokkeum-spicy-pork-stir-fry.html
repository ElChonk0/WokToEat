Jeyuk Bokkeum (Spicy Pork Bulgogi) 제육볶음

▾

Surprise me with

Jeyuk bokkeum, also sold as spicy pork bulgogi (dwaeji bulgogi), is thin pork marinated in gochujang and seared over high heat; it is one of the most common Korean home and lunch-restaurant dishes.

  • Prep 20 min (plus 30 min marinating)
  • Cook 10 min

Method

  1. Mix the gochujang, gochugaru, soy sauce, grated pear, sugar, garlic and ginger into a marinade.
  2. Coat the pork thoroughly and marinate at least 30 minutes, or up to overnight in the refrigerator.
  3. Heat a wide skillet or wok over high heat and cook the pork and onion in two batches, spread out, for 7 to 8 minutes, stirring only now and then, until charred in spots and cooked through.
  4. Drizzle with the sesame oil and toss.
  5. Garnish with sesame seeds and green onion and serve with rice and lettuce leaves for wrapping.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking over gentle heat or crowding the pan, which stews the pork instead of charring it.
  • Marinating with pear for more than 24 hours, which turns the meat mushy.

Authentic tips

  • Grated Asian pear sweetens and tenderizes; it is what makes the grilled bulgogi-style version.
  • Serve as ssam, wrapped in lettuce with rice and ssamjang.

Substitutions

  • Chicken thigh works with the same marinade and method.
  • No grill or very hot pan: broil in a single layer on a foil-lined sheet pan for 5 to 6 minutes, turn, and broil 3 to 4 minutes more until charred.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; great in a rice bowl.

Freezing: Freezes well, marinated and raw, for up to a month.
