Jeyuk Bokkeum (Spicy Pork Stir-Fry) 제육볶음

A fiery, gochujang-based pork stir-fry beloved as both a home-cooked meal and a popular restaurant dish, jeyuk bokkeum balances sweet, spicy, and savory notes in a quick, high-heat preparation.

  • Prep 20 min (plus 30 min marinating)
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Combine gochujang, gochugaru, soy sauce, sugar, garlic, and ginger for the marinade.
  2. Coat pork thoroughly and marinate at least 30 minutes.
  3. Heat a pan over high heat and stir-fry pork and onion together until charred in spots and cooked through, 7–8 minutes.
  4. Drizzle with sesame oil and garnish with green onion before serving.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking over too gentle heat, which misses the char that gives this dish its signature smoky edge.
  • Not marinating long enough, since the gochujang-based marinade needs time to penetrate the meat.

Authentic tips

  • High, quick heat is essential; this dish should sear and char, not stew in its own liquid.
  • Serve wrapped in lettuce leaves with rice and ssamjang for a traditional presentation.

Substitutions

  • Chicken thigh works well with the same marinade and method.
  • Reduce gochugaru for a milder, more kid-friendly version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; great in a rice bowl.

Freezing: Freezes well, marinated and raw, for up to a month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice with lettuce wraps
  • Soup: Doenjang jjigae
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
