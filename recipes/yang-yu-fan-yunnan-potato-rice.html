Yáng Yù Fàn (Yunnan Potato Rice) 洋芋饭

▾

Surprise me with

Yangyu, as potatoes are known in Yunnan, are cooked in rice with chilies and herbs in a hearty one-pot dish common in the mountains.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 25 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 300 g1.5 cupsShort-grain rice
  • 400 g14 ozPotatoes, diced
  • 100 g3.5 ozMushrooms
  • 20 g2/3 ozChilies
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 450 ml1.75 cupsWater
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Rinse the rice and drain.
  2. Heat the oil in a heavy pot and fry the diced potato and mushrooms 5 minutes until lightly golden.
  3. Add the rice, chilies and soy sauce and stir 1 minute.
  4. Pour in the water, bring to a boil, cover and cook on low heat 18-20 minutes.
  5. Rest 10 minutes off the heat, then fluff.

Common mistakes

  • Lifting the lid during cooking lets the steam escape.
  • Cutting the potatoes too big leaves them raw.

Authentic tips

  • Add a spoonful of chili oil at the table.
  • Diced cured ham is the traditional addition for non-vegetarians.

Substitutions

  • Cured ham is traditional for non-vegetarians.
  • Sticky rice replaces short-grain rice.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat with a splash of water.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled vegetables
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Pu'er tea
