Fǔ Rǔ Tōng Cài (Water Spinach with Fermented Tofu) 腐乳通菜

Water spinach stir-fried with fermented bean curd (fu ru) is a Cantonese home favorite, the funky, salty paste turning a plain summer vegetable into a rich dish.

  • Prep 5 min
  • Cook 5 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 500 g1.1 lbWater spinach (tong choi), cut
  • 40 g3 cubesWhite fermented bean curd
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 10 g2 tspChili (optional)
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Cut the water spinach into 6 cm lengths, keeping stems and leaves separate, and wash and drain thoroughly.
  2. Mash the fermented bean curd with the wine and 1 tablespoon of water into a smooth paste.
  3. Heat the oil in a wok over very high heat until smoking and fry the garlic and chili for 15 seconds.
  4. Add the stems and toss 30 seconds, then the leaves, and toss 30 seconds more.
  5. Pour in the bean curd paste and toss until the leaves just wilt, about 20 seconds; serve at once.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking too long, which turns the stems limp.
  • Adding extra salt; the fermented tofu is salty.

Authentic tips

  • Use a very hot wok to keep the stems crisp.
  • Red fermented tofu gives a spicier, deeper flavor.

Substitutions

  • Ong choy, kangkung or spinach all work.
  • Miso thinned with a little wine stands in for the fermented tofu.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the greens go limp within an hour.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or congee
  • Soup: Steamed tofu
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot jasmine tea
