Zī Rán Dòu Fu Chuàn (Cumin Tofu Skewers) 孜然豆腐串

▾

Surprise me with

Cumin-dusted skewers are the signature street food of Xinjiang; lamb is the classic, and tofu skewers are the vegetarian equivalent at night markets and barbecue restaurants across China.

Method

  1. Press the tofu 20 minutes and cut into 3 cm cubes; soak the skewers in water for 20 minutes.
  2. Toss the tofu with the soy sauce and 15 ml oil.
  3. Thread the tofu and onion onto the skewers.
  4. Grill over medium-high heat, or in a hot ridged pan, for 8-10 minutes, turning, until golden and charred at the edges, brushing with the remaining oil.
  5. Dust generously with the cumin, chili flakes and salt and serve at once.

Common mistakes

  • Soft tofu falls apart on the skewer; use extra-firm and press it.
  • Spice applied before grilling burns; add it at the end.

Authentic tips

  • Freshly toasted, ground cumin makes the difference.
  • Serve with flatbread (nang) and hot tea.

Substitutions

  • Lamb or beef take the same spicing.
  • King oyster mushrooms replace tofu.
  • No grill: lay the skewers on a wire rack over a foil-lined sheet pan and broil on high about 10 cm (4 in) from the element for 4 minutes per side, or roast at 230°C (450°F) for 12 to 15 minutes, turning once, until charred at the edges and cooked through.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; reheat in a hot pan.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Nang flatbread
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Hami melon
  • Beverage: Black tea
