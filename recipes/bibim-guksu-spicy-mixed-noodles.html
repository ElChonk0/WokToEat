Bibim Guksu (Spicy Mixed Wheat Noodles) 비빔국수

▾

Surprise me with

Bibim guksu, thin wheat noodles tossed in sweet-sour gochujang sauce with kimchi and cucumber, is a Korean summer staple ready in ten minutes.

  • 350 g12 ozSomyeon (thin wheat noodles)
  • 45 g3 tbspGochujang
  • 5 g2 tspGochugaru (Korean chili flakes)
  • 30 ml2 tbspRice vinegar
  • 20 g1.5 tbspSugar

  • 15 ml1 tbspSesame oil
  • 2 cloves2 clovesGarlic, minced
  • 150 g5 ozVegan kimchi, chopped
  • 11Cucumber, julienned
  • 50 g2 ozLettuce, shredded
  • 22Eggs, hard-boiled and halved
  • 5 g2 tspToasted sesame seeds

Method

  1. Mix the gochujang, gochugaru, vinegar, sugar, soy sauce, garlic and half the sesame oil into a sauce and chill it. It keeps for up to two weeks, and the flavor improves after a few hours.
  2. Cut the cucumber into matchsticks, shred the lettuce and chop the kimchi. Chill the vegetables so everything is cold.
  3. Boil the noodles according to the packet, usually 3 to 4 minutes, adding a cup of cold water when the pot foams up. Drain and plunge them into cold water.
  4. Rinse the noodles under icy water, rubbing them between your hands, until very cold and no longer starchy. Squeeze out the water and coil them into mounds. Somyeon turns soggy quickly, so do this just before serving.
  5. Toss the noodles with the remaining sesame oil, top them with the kimchi, vegetables and half an egg, spoon the sauce over and sprinkle with sesame seeds. Mix everything together at the table.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Unrinsed noodles turn gluey.
  • Too much vinegar overwhelms; taste the sauce first.

Authentic tips

  • A spoon of kimchi juice adds depth to the sauce.
  • Serve with grilled meats for a summer barbecue.

Substitutions

  • Regular kimchi if it does not need to be vegetarian.
  • Soba noodles replace somyeon.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Grilled pork belly
  • Soup: Eomuk-tang
  • Dessert: Watermelon
  • Beverage: Cold barley tea
