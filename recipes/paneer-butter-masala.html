Paneer Butter Masala

▾

Surprise me with

Paneer butter masala, cheese in a rich tomato-butter gravy, was created in Delhi's Punjabi restaurants and is among India's most ordered vegetarian dishes.

  • 300 g10.5 ozPaneer, cubed
  • 500 g1.1 lbTomatoes
  • 40 g3 tbspButter
  • 40 g1.5 ozCashews
  • 60 ml1/4 cupCream
  • 30 g1 ozGinger and garlic
  • 12 g1/2 ozKashmiri chili, garam masala
  • 3 g1 tspKasuri methi
  • 5 g1 tspSalt

Method

  1. Simmer the tomatoes, cashews, ginger and garlic with 150 ml water for 15 minutes; blend until smooth.
  2. Melt the butter, fry the chili and garam masala for 20 seconds and add the puree and salt.
  3. Simmer 10 minutes until thick.
  4. Add the paneer and cook 5 minutes.
  5. Stir in the cream and crushed kasuri methi.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling after adding the cream can split it.
  • Overcooking the paneer makes it rubbery.

Authentic tips

  • Soak the paneer in warm water first.
  • Serve with naan.

Substitutions

  • Firm tofu replaces paneer.
  • Cashew paste can replace cream.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; thin with water.

Freezing: Freezes the gravy for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Naan or jeera rice
  • Soup: Cucumber raita
  • Dessert: Gulab jamun
  • Beverage: Masala chai
