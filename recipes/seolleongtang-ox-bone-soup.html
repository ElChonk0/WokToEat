Seolleongtang (Ox Bone Soup) 설렁탕

▾

Seolleongtang, a milky ox-bone broth with brisket and noodles, is a traditional Seoul dish served plain and seasoned at the table with salt, scallion and pepper.

Method

  1. Soak the bones and the brisket separately in cold water for about 1 hour, changing the water once, to draw out the blood. Rinse.
  2. Put the bones in a large pot of cold water, bring to a boil over high heat and boil for 5 minutes. Drain, rinse the bones and scrub the pot clean. This first boil removes the scum that would gray the broth.
  3. Cover the bones with fresh cold water by about 5 cm (2 in) and bring to a boil. Keep it at a steady, rolling medium boil, not a gentle simmer, for about 5 hours, topping up with boiling water as it reduces. The vigorous boil is what emulsifies the marrow and collagen and turns the broth milky white; if it is not milky after 2 to 3 hours, turn the heat up.
  4. Add the brisket and boil for 1.5 to 2 hours more, until tender. Lift it out, let it cool and slice it thinly.
  5. Strain the broth. For the richest result, chill it overnight and lift off the fat. The bones can be boiled again in fresh water for another 3 to 5 hours and the two batches combined.
  6. Cook the noodles, rinse and divide them among bowls with the sliced beef. Pour the steaming broth over and serve with rice, scallion, salt and pepper on the table; the broth is left unsalted so each person seasons their own.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the soak gives a murky, gamey broth.
  • Seasoning the pot early; the dish is seasoned at the table.

Authentic tips

  • A pressure cooker cuts the time to 2 hours.
  • Serve with kkakdugi (radish kimchi).

Substitutions

  • A pressure cooker cuts the simmer to 2 hours.
  • Ready-made bone broth saves hours.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; the broth sets to a jelly.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Rice and kkakdugi (radish kimchi)
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
