Laing (Bicol Taro Leaves in Coconut Milk)

▾

Surprise me with

Laing, dried taro leaves simmered in coconut milk and chilies, is a classic of Bicol, the Philippines' region of fiery coconut dishes.

  • 150 g5 ozTofu, fried
  • 5 g1 tspSalt
  • 15 ml1 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Fry the garlic, onion and ginger in the oil for 3 minutes.
  2. Add the dried taro leaves and coconut milk, bring to a gentle simmer and do not stir for 10 minutes.
  3. Simmer 30 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the oil separates and the sauce is thick.
  4. Add the chilies and tofu.
  5. Season with the salt and simmer 5 minutes.

Common mistakes

  • Stirring early or undercooking the taro leaves leaves an itchy throat; cook fully.
  • Boiling hard splits the coconut milk.

Authentic tips

  • Dried taro leaves must be cooked long.
  • Spicy by tradition; add more chilies.

Substitutions

  • Spinach replaces taro leaves, but the texture changes.
  • Add pork for the traditional version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; the flavor improves.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Coconut water
