Bánh Xèo Chay (Vegetarian Crispy Crepe)

▾

Surprise me with

Banh xeo, the crisp turmeric crepe of southern Vietnam, is made vegetarian with tofu and mushrooms in temple kitchens.

Method

  1. Whisk the rice flour, turmeric, salt, coconut milk and water into a thin batter and stir in the green onion. Rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Make a vegan nuoc cham (nuoc cham chay): dissolve the sugar in the warm water, then add the soy sauce, lime juice, garlic and chili. Soy sauce stands in for fish sauce.
  3. Heat a teaspoon of oil in a 25 cm (10 in) nonstick skillet over medium-high heat and fry a little tofu, mushroom and onion for 2 minutes until golden.
  4. Stir the batter and pour in a ladleful, swirling to coat the pan thinly. Scatter with bean sprouts, cover and cook for 2 minutes.
  5. Uncover, drizzle oil around the edge and cook for 3 to 4 minutes more until crisp and golden. Fold and slide out. Repeat.
  6. Wrap pieces in lettuce and herbs and dip in the sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Thick batter doesn't crisp.
  • Pan not hot enough means no sizzle.

Authentic tips

  • Wrap in lettuce with herbs.
  • Beer in the batter adds crispness.

Substitutions

  • Beer in the batter adds crispness.
  • Bean sprouts and mushrooms fill the center.
