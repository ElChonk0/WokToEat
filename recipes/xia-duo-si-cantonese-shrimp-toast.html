Xia Duo Si (Cantonese Shrimp Toast) 虾多士

▾

Surprise me with

Shrimp toast, bread spread with shrimp paste and fried crisp, is a Cantonese dim sum and banquet appetizer whose name borrows the English "toast" (do si).

  • Cook 15 min

  • 11Egg white
  • 2 g1/2 tspSalt
  • 2 g1/2 tspSugar
  • 0.5 g1/4 tspGround white pepper

  • 5 ml1 tspSesame oil
  • 2 stalks2 stalksGreen onion, finely chopped
  • 6 slices6 slicesSliced white bread, slightly stale
  • 30 g1/4 cupWhite sesame seeds
  • 500 ml2 cupsNeutral oil, for frying

Method

  1. Pat the shrimp very dry, then chop and smash them with the flat of a cleaver into a coarse paste, leaving a few small pieces for texture. Mix in the fatback, egg white, salt, sugar, pepper and cornstarch, stirring hard in one direction for 1 to 2 minutes until sticky. Stir in the sesame oil and green onion and chill for 15 minutes.
  2. Trim the crusts off the bread and cut each slice into 4 triangles. Slightly stale bread absorbs less oil and holds the paste better.
  3. Spread a generous layer of shrimp paste on each triangle, about 6 mm (1/4 in) thick, pressing it down firmly and smoothing it right to the edges so it does not lift off in the oil. Press the paste side into the sesame seeds.
  4. Heat the oil in a flat-bottom wok or Dutch oven to 170 C (340 F). Fry the toasts paste-side down in batches for 1.5 to 2 minutes, until the sesame is golden and the shrimp has turned pink and set, then turn them and fry the bread side for 30 to 60 seconds until golden.
  5. Drain upright on a rack so the oil runs off, and serve hot with sweet chili sauce.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Fresh, soft bread soaks up oil; use day-old bread.
  • Too hot oil burns the sesame before the shrimp cooks.

Authentic tips

  • Chill the spread toasts 15 minutes so the paste sticks firmly.
  • Air fryer: spray and cook at 190 C (375 F) for 8 minutes.

Substitutions

  • Add a little minced pork fat for a richer paste.
  • Use baguette slices for a thicker toast.

Storage

Leftovers: Best eaten hot; re-crisp in a hot oven.

Freezing: Freeze spread, uncooked toasts for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sweet chili sauce
  • Soup: Hot and sour soup
  • Dessert: Mango pudding
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
