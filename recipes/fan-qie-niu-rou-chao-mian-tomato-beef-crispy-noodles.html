Fān Qié Niú Ròu Chǎo Miàn (Tomato Beef over Crispy Noodles) 番茄牛肉炒面

Velveted beef in a sweet-sour tomato gravy poured over a crisp cake of pan-fried egg noodles is a Hong Kong diner and Toisanese restaurant classic.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 400 g14 ozFresh thin egg noodles (Hong Kong style)
  • 400 g14 ozBeef flank steak, thinly sliced
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 1 g1/4 tspBaking soda
  • 8 g1 tbspCornstarch, for the beef
  • 75 ml5 tbspNeutral oil
  • 44Tomatoes, cut into wedges
  • 11Onion, cut into wedges
  • 2 stalks2 stalksGreen onion, cut into lengths
  • 60 ml1/4 cupKetchup
  • 30 g2 tbspSugar
  • 15 ml1 tbspOyster sauce
  • 10 ml2 tspWorcestershire sauce
  • 250 ml1 cupWater
  • 15 g1.5 tbspCornstarch, for the sauce

Method

  1. Mix the beef with the soy sauce, baking soda, cornstarch and a teaspoon of oil and rest 15 minutes. Blanch the noodles 30 seconds and drain very dry.
  2. Heat 3 tablespoons of oil in a large non-stick skillet over medium heat, press in the noodles as one flat cake and fry 5 minutes per side until golden and crisp. Slide onto a platter.
  3. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a flat-bottom wok over high heat and sear the beef for 1 minute until just browned. Lift out.
  4. Add the last tablespoon of oil and the onion and tomatoes and cook 3 minutes until the tomatoes slump. Add the ketchup, sugar, oyster sauce, Worcestershire and water and simmer 2 minutes, then thicken with the cornstarch mixed with a little water.
  5. Return the beef and green onion, then pour everything over the crispy noodles and serve at once.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Wet noodles never crisp; drain and air-dry them first.
  • Overcooking the beef in the sauce makes it tough; add it back at the end.

Authentic tips

  • The contrast of crunchy and gravy-soaked noodles is the point, so pour the sauce just before eating.
  • A little Worcestershire is the Hong Kong diner signature.

Substitutions

  • Serve the tomato beef over rice instead.
  • Chicken thigh replaces beef.

Storage

Leftovers: Store the sauce separately for 3 days; re-crisp noodles in a pan.

Freezing: The tomato beef sauce freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Blanched gai lan
  • Soup: Hong Kong borscht
  • Dessert: Egg tarts
  • Beverage: Hong Kong milk tea
