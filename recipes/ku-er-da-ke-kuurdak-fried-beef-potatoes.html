Kuurdak (Kazakh Fried Beef and Potatoes) 库尔达克

Kuurdak, fried meat with potatoes and onion, is a traditional Kazakh dish enjoyed by the Kazakh communities of northern Xinjiang.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 40 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Xinjiang Beef LunchDinnerStir-fry

  • 550 g1.2 lbBeef, cubed
  • 400 g14 ozPotatoes
  • 200 g7 ozOnion
  • 8 g1.5 tspSalt and pepper
  • 2 g3/4 tspCumin
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 20 g3/4 ozCilantro or dill
  • 150 ml2/3 cupWater

Method

  1. Brown the beef in the oil in a heavy pot over medium-high heat for 8 minutes.
  2. Add the onion and cook 8 minutes until soft and golden.
  3. Add the potato wedges, salt, cumin and water, cover and cook 30 minutes over low heat until the beef is tender.
  4. Uncover and cook 5 minutes to crisp the potatoes.
  5. Season with pepper and finish with the herbs.

Common mistakes

  • Rinsing the beef before frying prevents browning; pat it dry.
  • High heat after adding the water burns the bottom.

Authentic tips

  • Traditionally made with horse meat or lamb.
  • Serve with flatbread.

Substitutions

  • Lamb or horse meat is traditional.
  • Add sliced onion at the end for sweetness.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot pan.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months without the potatoes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Nang flatbread
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Hami melon
  • Beverage: Black tea with milk
