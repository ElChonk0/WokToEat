Cōng Yóu Bǐng (Taiwanese Scallion Pancake) 蔥油餅

▾

Surprise me with

Scallion pancakes, flaky layered flatbreads, are among Taiwan's most popular breakfast and street foods, often served with egg or basil.

  • 300 g2.5 cupsAll-purpose flour
  • 150 ml2/3 cupHot water (about 80 C / 175 F)
  • 60 ml1/4 cupCold water
  • 5 g1 tspSalt
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil, for the roux
  • 15 g2 tbspFlour, for the roux
  • 1 g1/2 tspWhite pepper (optional)
  • 100 g3.5 ozGreen onions, finely chopped
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil, for frying
  • 45 ml3 tbspSoy sauce and chili oil, for dipping

Method

  1. Stir the hot water into the flour with chopsticks, then the cold water, and knead for 5 minutes into a soft, smooth dough. Hot water gives a tender, chewy pancake. Cover and rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Make the oil roux: heat the oil until hot and pour it over the flour, salt and pepper in a bowl, stirring into a loose paste. It spreads more evenly than plain oil and makes distinct flaky layers.
  3. Divide the dough into 4. Roll each piece into a thin rectangle, spread with the roux and scatter with green onion. Roll it up tightly like a cigar, then coil it into a snail and tuck the end under. Rest for 10 minutes.
  4. Press and roll each coil gently into a round about 5 mm (1/4 in) thick, without pressing so hard that the onions burst through.
  5. Heat the frying oil in a skillet over medium heat and fry each pancake for 3 to 4 minutes per side, until golden and crisp.
  6. While it is still in the pan, push the edges toward the center with two spatulas to fluff up the layers. Cut into wedges and serve with soy sauce and chili oil.

Common mistakes

  • Cold water gives a tough dough.
  • High heat burns the outside before the layers cook.

Authentic tips

  • Hot water gives a tender, layered pancake.
  • Add an egg for a Taiwanese breakfast version.

Substitutions

  • Store-bought frozen pancakes are a good shortcut.
  • Whole wheat flour replaces some flour.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days; reheat in a hot dry pan.

Freezing: Freeze uncooked rounds between parchment for 3 months; cook from frozen.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Add an egg or basil
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot soy milk
