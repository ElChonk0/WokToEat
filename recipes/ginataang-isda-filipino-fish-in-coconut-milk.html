Ginataang Isda (Fish in Coconut Milk)

▾

Surprise me with

Ginataan, cooking in coconut milk with ginger and chilies, is a widely loved Filipino technique used for fish, seafood and vegetables.

  • 80 g3 ozPechay or spinach
  • 1 g1/4 tspBlack pepper

Method

  1. Combine the coconut milk, water, ginger, garlic and onion in a wide pan and bring to a gentle simmer for 6 minutes.
  2. Add the eggplant and chilies and simmer 6 minutes until the eggplant is tender.
  3. Season with the fish sauce and pepper and slide in the fish.
  4. Cover and simmer gently for 6 to 8 minutes until the fish is cooked, then add the pechay for 1 minute.
  5. Serve with rice.

Cook’s notes

Written for haddock fillet.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling the coconut milk hard splits it.
  • Stirring flakes the delicate haddock.

Authentic tips

  • Use full-fat coconut milk for a rich sauce.
  • Add a squeeze of calamansi at the table.

Substitutions

  • Cod loin replaces the haddock.
  • Squash replaces the eggplant.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Calamansi juice
