Dwaeji Galbi (Korean BBQ Pork Ribs) 돼지갈비

Dwaeji galbi, sweet soy-marinated pork ribs grilled at the table, is a mainstay of Korean barbecue restaurants and cheaper than beef galbi.

  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Method

  1. Soak the ribs in cold water 20 minutes, drain and pat dry.
  2. Mix the soy sauce, sugar, pear, onion, garlic, ginger, mirin, sesame oil and pepper, coat the ribs and marinate at least 4 hours or overnight.
  3. Heat a cast-iron grill pan or skillet over medium heat.
  4. Cook the ribs 4 to 5 minutes per side until caramelized and cooked through, turning often and lowering the heat if the sugar burns.
  5. Cut into pieces with scissors, scatter with green onion and serve with lettuce and ssamjang.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • High heat burns the sweet marinade before the pork cooks.
  • Short marinating leaves the meat bland.

Authentic tips

  • Oven or broiler: broil on a rack 6 to 8 minutes per side, basting.
  • A spicy version adds gochujang and gochugaru to the marinade.

Substitutions

  • Boneless pork shoulder slices.
  • Grated apple replaces pear.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freezes raw in the marinade for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Lettuce, ssamjang and rice
  • Soup: Doenjang jjigae
  • Dessert: Sikhye
  • Beverage: Soju or cold beer
