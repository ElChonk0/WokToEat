Dwaeji Galbi (Korean BBQ Pork Ribs) 돼지갈비

▾

Surprise me with

Dwaeji galbi, sweet soy-marinated pork ribs grilled at the table, is a mainstay of Korean barbecue restaurants and cheaper than beef galbi.

  • Prep 20 min (plus 8 hr marinating)
  • Cook 1 hr 45 min

Method

  1. Pull the thin membrane off the back of the ribs with a paper towel for grip, then cut the rack in half so it fits your pan.
  2. Mix the soy sauce, water, rice wine, honey, sugar, pear, onion, garlic, ginger, sesame oil and pepper. Coat the ribs, cover and refrigerate for at least 8 hours, ideally 24, turning once. The fruit and long marinade tenderize the meat and carry the flavor to the bone.
  3. Heat the oven to 160 C (325 F). Lay the ribs meat-side down in a foil-lined roasting pan with the marinade, cover tightly with foil and bake for 1.5 hours, or 2 hours for fall-off-the-bone ribs.
  4. Uncover and pour the pan juices into a small pan. Boil them over medium-high heat for 5 to 8 minutes, until reduced to a thick, glossy sauce.
  5. Turn the ribs meat-side up and brush them with the sauce. Broil them on a high rack, or grill over medium heat, for about 5 minutes per side, basting again, until sticky and lightly charred at the edges. Watch closely, as the sugar catches.
  6. Cut between the bones, scatter with green onion and serve with lettuce, ssamjang and rice. The ribs can be baked a day ahead and charred just before serving.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • High heat burns the sweet marinade before the pork cooks.
  • Short marinating leaves the meat bland.

Authentic tips

  • Oven or broiler: broil on a rack 6 to 8 minutes per side, basting.
  • A spicy version adds gochujang and gochugaru to the marinade.

Substitutions

  • Boneless pork shoulder slices.
  • Grated apple replaces pear.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freezes raw in the marinade for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Lettuce, ssamjang and rice
  • Soup: Doenjang jjigae
  • Dessert: Sikhye
  • Beverage: Soju or cold beer
