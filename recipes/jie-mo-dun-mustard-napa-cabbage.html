Jiè Mò Dūn (Beijing Mustard Cabbage) 芥末墩

▾

Surprise me with

Jie mo dun, napa cabbage rolled up and dressed with a sharp mustard-vinegar sauce, is an old Beijing cold dish traditionally served at New Year to cut through rich meals.

  • Cook 5 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 500 g1.1 lbNapa cabbage, thick stems
  • 10 g1 tbspMustard powder
  • 60 ml4 tbspRice vinegar
  • 30 g2 tbspSugar
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt
  • 60 ml4 tbspHot water

Method

  1. Cut the cabbage stems into 8 cm lengths and blanch for 30 seconds in boiling water; drain and chill in ice water.
  2. Stir the mustard powder, hot water, vinegar, sugar and salt together, cover and leave 15 minutes to develop the heat.
  3. Roll or stack the cabbage in a shallow dish and pour the mustard sauce over.
  4. Cover tightly and chill at least 1 hour, then slice or serve in sections.

Common mistakes

  • Uncovered mustard loses its pungency.
  • Too little rest gives a flat flavor.

Authentic tips

  • Cover the dish, as the fumes are strong.
  • Serve cold alongside rich dishes.

Substitutions

  • Wasabi powder replaces mustard powder.
  • Serve at once or chill 1 hour for stronger flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; best chilled.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Served cold as an appetizer before rich mains
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
