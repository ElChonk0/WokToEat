Tǔ Dòu Dùn Niú Ròu (Xinjiang Beef and Potato Stew) 土豆炖牛肉

▾

Surprise me with

Beef stewed with potatoes, peppers and tomatoes is an everyday home dish across Xinjiang.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 90 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Xinjiang Beef LunchDinnerBraise / stew

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 650 g1.4 lbBeef brisket, cubed
  • 450 g1 lbPotatoes
  • 300 g10.5 ozTomatoes and peppers
  • 100 g3.5 ozOnion and garlic
  • 3 g1 tspCumin
  • 700 ml3 cupsWater
  • 8 g1.5 tspSalt
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Brown the beef in the oil in a heavy pot over medium-high heat for 8 minutes.
  2. Add the onion, garlic and cumin and fry 3 minutes.
  3. Add the tomatoes, water and salt, bring to a boil, cover and simmer 60 minutes.
  4. Add the potato chunks and cook 20 minutes more until tender.
  5. Add the peppers for the last 5 minutes.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the potatoes too early makes them dissolve.
  • Boiling hard toughens the beef.

Authentic tips

  • Better the next day.
  • Serve with flatbread.

Substitutions

  • Lamb shoulder is the traditional meat.
  • Chuck replaces brisket.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months without the potatoes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Nang flatbread
  • Soup: Not needed; the stew is its own soup
  • Dessert: Hami melon
  • Beverage: Black tea
