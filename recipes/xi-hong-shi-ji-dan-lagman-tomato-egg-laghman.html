Xī Hóng Shì Jī Dàn Lagman (Tomato and Egg Laghman) 番茄鸡蛋拌面

▾

Surprise me with

Laghman, hand-pulled noodles with a stir-fried topping, is the staple of Uyghur home cooking; the vegetarian version with tomato, egg and peppers is common in Xinjiang households.

  • 350 g2.75 cupsAll-purpose flour, for hand-pulled noodles (or 450 g fresh thick noodles)
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt, for the dough
  • 180 ml3/4 cupWater, for the dough
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 44Eggs, beaten
  • 100 g3.5 ozOnion and garlic, sliced
  • 400 g14 ozTomatoes, chopped

  • 150 g5 ozGreen peppers, sliced
  • 3 g1 tspGround cumin
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt
  • 120 ml1/2 cupWater

Method

  1. Mix the flour, salt and water into a firm dough and knead for 10 minutes until smooth. Rest for 30 minutes, knead again, then divide into finger-thick ropes, coat with oil, coil on a plate and cover for at least 1 hour. The long rest is what lets the dough stretch.
  2. Scramble the eggs in a tablespoon of oil into soft curds and lift them out.
  3. Fry the onion and garlic in the remaining oil for 1 minute, then the tomato paste, tomatoes and peppers for 4 minutes until saucy. Add the cumin, soy sauce, salt and water and simmer for 3 minutes, then fold in the eggs.
  4. Bring a large pot of water to a boil. Take each rope, pull and stretch it gently between your hands, swinging and slapping it against the counter, until it is long and about 3 mm thick, and drop it straight into the water. Cook for 2 to 3 minutes until the noodles float.
  5. Drain the noodles, pile on plates and spoon the tomato-egg topping over. Serve with black vinegar.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooked eggs go rubbery.
  • A watery sauce does not coat the noodles; reduce it.

Authentic tips

  • Tomato-egg is also a classic over rice.
  • Eggs make this lacto-ovo vegetarian.

Substitutions

  • Fresh egg noodles or udon replace hand-pulled noodles.
  • Tofu replaces egg for a vegan version.

Storage

Leftovers: Store sauce and noodles separately; the sauce keeps 4 days.

Freezing: The sauce freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled vegetables
  • Soup: Not needed; the noodles are the meal
  • Dessert: Hami melon
  • Beverage: Black tea
