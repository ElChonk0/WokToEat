Xī Hóng Shì Jī Dàn Lagman (Tomato and Egg Laghman) 番茄鸡蛋拌面

▾

Surprise me with

Laghman, hand-pulled noodles with a stir-fried topping, is the staple of Uyghur home cooking; the vegetarian version with tomato, egg and peppers is common in Xinjiang households.

  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 350 g12 ozHand-pulled or wheat noodles, fresh
  • 400 g14 ozTomatoes
  • 44Eggs
  • 150 g5 ozGreen peppers
  • 100 g3.5 ozOnion and garlic
  • 3 g1 tspCumin
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Heat half the oil in a wok and scramble the eggs into soft curds; remove.
  2. Add the remaining oil, onion, garlic and cumin and fry 2 minutes.
  3. Add the tomatoes, peppers, soy sauce and salt and simmer 10 minutes until saucy.
  4. Stir in the eggs.
  5. Boil the noodles 3-4 minutes, drain and top with the sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooked eggs go rubbery.
  • A watery sauce does not coat the noodles; reduce it.

Authentic tips

  • Tomato-egg is also a classic over rice.
  • Eggs make this lacto-ovo vegetarian.

Substitutions

  • Fresh egg noodles or udon replace hand-pulled noodles.
  • Tofu replaces egg for a vegan version.

Storage

Leftovers: Store sauce and noodles separately; the sauce keeps 4 days.

Freezing: The sauce freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled vegetables
  • Soup: Not needed; the noodles are the meal
  • Dessert: Hami melon
  • Beverage: Black tea
