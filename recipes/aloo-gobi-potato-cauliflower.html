Aloo Gobi (Potato and Cauliflower)

▾

Surprise me with

Aloo gobi, dry-spiced potato and cauliflower, is among the most popular vegetarian dishes of North India and the Indian diaspora.

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a wide, heavy skillet over medium heat and fry the potatoes for 5 to 6 minutes until golden at the edges. Add the cauliflower and fry for 5 minutes more until lightly browned in spots. Lift them out. Browning first keeps the vegetables from turning mushy.
  2. In the same oil, fry the cumin seeds for 20 seconds, then the onion for 5 minutes until golden. Add the ginger-garlic paste and chilies for 1 minute.
  3. Add the turmeric, coriander, cumin and chili with a splash of water, then the tomato and salt. Cook for 4 to 5 minutes until the tomato breaks down.
  4. Return the potatoes and cauliflower and toss to coat. Cover and cook over low heat for 10 to 12 minutes, stirring gently once or twice, until tender. Add only a sprinkle of water if it sticks; aloo gobi is a dry dish.
  5. Uncover, sprinkle with garam masala and amchur and toss over medium heat for 2 minutes. Scatter with cilantro and ginger and serve with roti.

Common mistakes

  • Too much water makes it mushy; it should be dry.
  • Stirring a lot breaks the cauliflower.

Authentic tips

  • Cut the vegetables the same size.
  • Serve with roti.

Substitutions

  • Peas can join the dish.
  • Cut the vegetables evenly.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Roti or paratha
  • Soup: Cucumber raita
  • Dessert: Kheer
  • Beverage: Masala chai
