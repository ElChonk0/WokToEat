Aloo Gobi (Potato and Cauliflower)

▾

Surprise me with

Aloo gobi, dry-spiced potato and cauliflower, is among the most popular vegetarian dishes of North India and the Indian diaspora.

  • 3 g1 tspGaram masala
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 6 g1 tspSalt

Method

  1. Heat the oil and fry the cumin seeds for 20 seconds.
  2. Add the onion and fry until golden, 6 minutes; add the ginger and garlic for 1 minute.
  3. Add the turmeric, coriander and tomato and cook 4 minutes.
  4. Add the potato and cauliflower with the salt and a splash of water, cover and cook 15 minutes.
  5. Uncover, finish with the garam masala and cook 3 minutes.

Common mistakes

  • Too much water makes it mushy; it should be dry.
  • Stirring a lot breaks the cauliflower.

Authentic tips

  • Cut the vegetables the same size.
  • Serve with roti.

Substitutions

  • Peas can join the dish.
  • Cut the vegetables evenly.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Roti or paratha
  • Soup: Cucumber raita
  • Dessert: Kheer
  • Beverage: Masala chai
