Goan Fish Curry

Goan fish curry, a tangy coconut and kokum curry, is the staple dish of Goa; it blends local, Konkani and Portuguese influences.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 25 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Method

  1. Fry the onion and spices in the oil for 5 minutes until golden.
  2. Add the garlic, ginger and tomato and cook 5 minutes.
  3. Pour in the tamarind, coconut milk and 200 ml water and simmer 8 minutes.
  4. Add the fish steaks and cook gently 8 minutes.
  5. Season with salt and serve with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Stirring breaks the fish.
  • Boiling hard splits the coconut milk.

Authentic tips

  • Kokum gives the authentic sourness.
  • Better the next day.

Substitutions

  • Kokum replaces tamarind for the authentic sourness.
  • Shrimp take the same curry.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; the flavor improves.

Freezing: Not recommended.
