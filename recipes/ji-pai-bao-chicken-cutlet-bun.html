Jiān Jī Pái Bāo (Chicken Cutlet Bun) 雞排包

▾

Surprise me with

Taiwanese breakfast shops serve a range of filled buns and sandwiches; a fried chicken cutlet with cabbage and a sweet-savory sauce is a common choice.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Method

  1. Pound the chicken to 1 cm, season with salt and pepper and coat in starch or breadcrumbs.
  2. Fry in 2 cm of oil at 180 C (350 F) for 4 minutes per side until golden; drain.
  3. Fry the eggs in a little oil.
  4. Toast the buns split-side down in a dry pan.
  5. Fill with the cutlet, cabbage, egg, mayonnaise and sweet chili sauce.

Common mistakes

  • A cold pan makes the cutlet greasy.
  • Wet cabbage makes the bun soggy; drain it.

Authentic tips

  • Cut the cutlet in half to fit the bun.
  • Serve with soy milk for a Taiwanese breakfast.

Substitutions

  • Brioche buns replace soft buns.
  • Air-fry the cutlet for a lighter sandwich.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the bun softens.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Fries
  • Soup: Soy milk
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot soy milk
