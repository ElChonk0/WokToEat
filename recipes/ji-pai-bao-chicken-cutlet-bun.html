Jiān Jī Pái Bāo (Chicken Cutlet Bun) 雞排包

▾

Surprise me with

Taiwanese breakfast shops serve a range of filled buns and sandwiches; a fried chicken cutlet with cabbage and a sweet-savory sauce is a common choice.

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken with the soy sauce, garlic, five-spice, sugar and egg for at least 30 minutes.
  2. Press the chicken into the coarse sweet potato starch on both sides and rest for 5 minutes so the starch hydrates; this gives the knobbly, crunchy crust of Taiwanese fried chicken.
  3. Heat the oil to 170 C (340 F) and fry the cutlets for 4 to 5 minutes until cooked through, then raise the heat to 190 C (375 F) for the last 30 seconds to crisp. Drain and sprinkle with pepper salt.
  4. Toast the buns lightly.
  5. Fill each bun with shredded cabbage, a cutlet, mayonnaise and sweet chili sauce. Serve at once.

Common mistakes

  • A cold pan makes the cutlet greasy.
  • Wet cabbage makes the bun soggy; drain it.

Authentic tips

  • Cut the cutlet in half to fit the bun.
  • Serve with soy milk for a Taiwanese breakfast.

Substitutions

  • Brioche buns replace soft buns.
  • Air-fry the cutlet for a lighter sandwich.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the bun softens.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Fries
  • Soup: Soy milk
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot soy milk
