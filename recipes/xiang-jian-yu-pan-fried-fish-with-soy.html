Xiāng Jiān Yú (Pan-Fried Fish with Soy and Scallion) 香煎鱼

A whole fish or fillets pan-fried crisp and dressed with sweet soy and scallion is a staple of the Cantonese family dinner table.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Cantonese Seafood LunchDinner30 min or less

  • 800 g1.75 lbTilapia or sea bream, whole and cleaned, or 4 fillets
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt
  • 20 g2/3 ozGinger, sliced
  • 15 g1.5 tbspCornstarch
  • 60 ml1/4 cupNeutral oil
  • 3 stalks3 stalksGreen onion, julienned
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 8 g2 tspSugar
  • 45 ml3 tbspWater

Method

  1. Score a whole fish 2 or 3 times on each side. Rub with salt and pat completely dry, then dust thinly with cornstarch.
  2. Heat the oil in a large non-stick or carbon-steel skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering and fry the ginger 30 seconds.
  3. Lay the fish in and fry without moving for 5 to 6 minutes until the skin is golden and releases. Turn carefully and fry the other side 4 to 5 minutes (fillets need 3 minutes per side).
  4. Move the fish to a plate. Pour off most of the oil and add the soy sauce, sugar and water to the pan, bubbling for 30 seconds.
  5. Pour the sauce over the fish and top with the green onion.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • A damp fish sticks and tears; dry it thoroughly.
  • Moving the fish too soon rips the skin.

Authentic tips

  • Rubbing the hot pan with a slice of ginger before adding the oil helps stop sticking.
  • Salmon fillets are a popular modern version: cook them skin-side down 5 to 6 minutes, turn for 1 to 2 minutes, and spoon the sauce around rather than over the skin.

Substitutions

  • Salmon, cod, trout or black cod fillets.
  • Add a little chopped chili to the sauce.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and stir-fried greens
  • Soup: Bok choy and pork soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
