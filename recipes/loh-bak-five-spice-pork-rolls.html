Loh Bak (Five-Spice Pork Rolls) 卤肉

▾

Surprise me with

A Teochew-Chinese dish popular in both Malaysia and Singapore, loh bak wraps five-spice seasoned ground pork in bean curd skin before frying until crisp — a popular hawker-stall side dish or snack.

Method

  1. Mix the pork, shrimp, water chestnuts, shallots, carrot, green onion, five-spice, soy sauce, fish sauce, sugar, salt, pepper, egg and cornstarch, stirring hard in one direction until sticky. Chill for 30 minutes; the filling firms up and is easier to roll.
  2. Wipe the bean curd skins with a damp cloth to soften them and cut them into pieces about 15 x 20 cm (6 x 8 in).
  3. Spread a line of filling along one edge, about 2.5 cm (1 in) thick, fold in the sides and roll it up firmly, sealing the edge with egg white. Make 8 rolls.
  4. Steam the rolls seam-side down, not touching, over boiling water for 10 to 12 minutes, until the filling is cooked through. Let them cool completely on a rack. Steaming first cooks the filling evenly, so the frying only crisps the skin.
  5. Heat the oil to 175 C (350 F) and deep-fry the cooled rolls for 2 to 3 minutes until golden and crisp. Drain, rest 2 minutes and slice on an angle. Serve with sweet sauce and chili sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the steaming step and frying directly, which risks an undercooked interior with a burnt exterior.
  • Rolling too loosely, which lets the rolls fall apart during frying.

Authentic tips

  • Bean curd skin is fragile when dry — dampening it slightly with a wet cloth makes it much easier to roll without tearing.
  • The two-stage cooking (steam, then fry) ensures the filling is fully cooked while achieving a crisp exterior.

Substitutions

  • Ground chicken can replace pork for a lighter filling.
  • No bean curd skin: spring roll wrappers work as a substitute, though texture differs.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in an oven to restore crispness.

Freezing: Freezes well, steamed and uncooked, for up to a month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sweet chili sauce and chili dip
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
