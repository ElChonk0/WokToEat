Fúzhōu Jī Juǎn (Fuzhou Chicken Rolls) 福州鸡卷

▾

Surprise me with

Fuzhou cooks wrap minced meat and vegetables in thin sheets and fry or steam them, a banquet and festival dish.

Method

  1. Mix the chicken, pork fat, water chestnuts, green onion, ginger, soy sauce, fish sauce, five-spice, sugar, starch and egg white until sticky.
  2. Wipe the tofu skin with a damp cloth, lay a line of filling along one edge and roll up tightly, sealing the edge with a little egg white.
  3. Steam the rolls for 12 minutes to cook the filling.
  4. Fry in oil at 170 C (340 F) for 3 to 4 minutes until golden and crisp.
  5. Slice on an angle and serve hot, with sweet chili or garlic vinegar.

Common mistakes

  • Overfilling makes the rolls burst when fried.
  • Frying without steaming first can leave the center undercooked.

Authentic tips

  • Steaming first makes the rolls hold together.
  • Serve with sweet chili sauce.

Substitutions

  • Spring roll wrappers replace tofu skin.
  • Steam the rolls first, then pan-fry for a lighter version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; recrisp in a hot oven.

Freezing: Freeze uncooked rolls for 2 months; fry from frozen.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot oolong tea
