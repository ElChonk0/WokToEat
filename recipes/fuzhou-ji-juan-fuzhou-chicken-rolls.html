Fúzhōu Jī Juǎn (Fuzhou Chicken Rolls) 福州鸡卷

▾

Surprise me with

Fuzhou cooks wrap minced meat and vegetables in thin sheets and fry or steam them, a banquet and festival dish.

Method

  1. Mix the minced chicken with the water chestnuts, scallion, ginger and soy sauce; stir in one direction until sticky.
  2. Soften the tofu skin sheets by wiping with a damp cloth, lay one flat and spoon a quarter of the filling in a line along one edge.
  3. Roll up tightly, folding in the sides, and seal the seam with egg white; repeat.
  4. Steam the rolls over high heat for 12 minutes until the filling is firm; cool 10 minutes.
  5. Heat the oil to 175 C (350 F) and fry the rolls 2-3 minutes until golden; drain, slice on the diagonal and serve.

Common mistakes

  • Overfilling makes the rolls burst when fried.
  • Frying without steaming first can leave the center undercooked.

Authentic tips

  • Steaming first makes the rolls hold together.
  • Serve with sweet chili sauce.

Substitutions

  • Spring roll wrappers replace tofu skin.
  • Steam the rolls first, then pan-fry for a lighter version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; recrisp in a hot oven.

Freezing: Freeze uncooked rolls for 2 months; fry from frozen.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot oolong tea
