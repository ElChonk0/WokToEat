Dal Tadka (Tempered Yellow Lentils)

▾

Surprise me with

Dal tadka, lentils finished with sizzling spiced ghee, is the everyday dal of North India, eaten with rice or roti.

Method

  1. Pressure-cook the dal with the water and turmeric for 3 to 4 whistles, or simmer it, partly covered, for 30 to 40 minutes, until completely soft. Whisk until smooth and creamy.
  2. Heat the masala ghee in a pot and cook the onion for 5 minutes, then the ginger, green chili and tomato for 4 minutes until soft. Add the dal and salt, and water to thin it to a pourable consistency, and simmer for 5 minutes.
  3. Make the tadka: heat the tadka ghee in a small pan until hot, add the cumin seeds and let them sizzle for 10 seconds, then the garlic until golden, then the dried chilies and asafoetida for 5 seconds.
  4. Take it off the heat, add the chili powder (it burns if the pan is on the heat) and pour the sizzling tadka over the dal at once.
  5. Stir in the lemon juice and cilantro and serve with rice or roti. The tempering, done last, is what makes dal taste vibrant.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the garlic makes the tadka bitter.
  • Adding salt too late leaves it bland.

Authentic tips

  • The tadka should sizzle loudly when poured.
  • Serve with rice or roti.

Substitutions

  • Masoor or moong dal replace toor.
  • Oil replaces ghee for a vegan dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; thin with water.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Basmati rice or roti
  • Soup: Cucumber raita
  • Dessert: Kheer
  • Beverage: Masala chai
