Dal Tadka (Tempered Yellow Lentils)

▾

Surprise me with

Dal tadka, lentils finished with sizzling spiced ghee, is the everyday dal of North India, eaten with rice or roti.

  • 200 g7 ozDried toor or yellow lentils
  • 3 g1 tspTurmeric
  • 200 g7 ozTomato and onion
  • 30 g2 tbspGhee
  • 15 g1/2 ozCumin seeds, garlic, dried chilies
  • 1 gpinchAsafoetida
  • 15 g1/2 ozCilantro
  • 6 g1 tspSalt

Method

  1. Rinse the lentils and simmer with the turmeric, tomato and 1 L water for 25 minutes until soft.
  2. Mash lightly and season with the salt.
  3. Heat the ghee in a small pan and fry the cumin seeds until they sizzle.
  4. Add the garlic, chilies and asafoetida for 20 seconds until golden.
  5. Pour over the dal and garnish with cilantro.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the garlic makes the tadka bitter.
  • Adding salt too late leaves it bland.

Authentic tips

  • The tadka should sizzle loudly when poured.
  • Serve with rice or roti.

Substitutions

  • Masoor or moong dal replace toor.
  • Oil replaces ghee for a vegan dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; thin with water.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Basmati rice or roti
  • Soup: Cucumber raita
  • Dessert: Kheer
  • Beverage: Masala chai
