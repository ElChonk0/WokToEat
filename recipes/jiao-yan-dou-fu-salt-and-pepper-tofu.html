Jiāo Yán Dòu Fu (Salt-and-Pepper Tofu) 椒盐豆腐

▾

Surprise me with

Salt-and-pepper tofu, cubes fried until crisp and tossed with chili, garlic and pepper salt, is a Cantonese restaurant staple adapted from the salt-and-pepper treatment of squid and pork chops.

  • 450 g1 lbFirm tofu, cut into 2.5 cm (1 in) cubes
  • 5 g1 tspSalt, for the tofu water
  • 50 g6 tbspCornstarch
  • 500 ml2 cupsNeutral oil for frying
  • 22Dried chili (optional)
  • 3 cloves3 clovesGarlic, minced
  • 50 g2 ozRed chilies or mini bell pepper, diced
  • 3 stalks3 stalksScallions, white parts diced
  • 8 g2 tspGarlic salt
  • 1 g1/4 tspSalt
  • 1 g1/2 tspWhite pepper

Method

  1. Bring a pot of water to a boil with the salt, add the tofu cubes, cover and cook 3 minutes, then uncover over low heat for 2 minutes. Drain and let the cubes cool and steam dry. Parboiling firms the tofu so it holds together in the oil and seasons it lightly.
  2. Pat the cubes dry, then toss them gently in the cornstarch, dusting with a little more until every side is coated. Use cornstarch, not flour, for a crust that stays crisp. Lay them out in a single layer so they do not stick together.
  3. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven or flat-bottom wok to 190 C (375 F). Fry the tofu in batches, leaving it undisturbed until the crust sets, then for about 5 minutes in all, turning, until golden and crisp. Keep the oil between 180 and 190 C (360 and 375 F). Drain on a rack.
  4. Mix the garlic salt, salt and white pepper. Pour off all but 2 tablespoons of oil, fry the dried chili for 8 seconds and remove it, then the garlic for 15 seconds and the chilies and scallions for 20 to 25 seconds.
  5. Return the tofu and toss for 10 seconds, then keep tossing while you sprinkle in the seasoning a little at a time until evenly coated. Serve at once.

Common mistakes

  • Not drying the tofu, which prevents crisping.
  • Letting the tofu sit after tossing, which makes it soggy.

Authentic tips

  • Press the tofu 20 minutes under a weight for the crispest result.
  • Serve immediately.

Substitutions

  • Extra-firm tofu or tempeh both work.
  • Bake at 220 C (425 F) for 25 minutes for a lighter version.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; recrisp in a hot oven for 8 minutes the next day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh pineapple
  • Beverage: Hot jasmine tea
