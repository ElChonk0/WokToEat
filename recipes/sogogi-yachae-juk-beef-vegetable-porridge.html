Sogogi Yachae Juk (Beef and Vegetable Rice Porridge) 소고기 야채죽

▾

Surprise me with

Juk, Korean rice porridge, is a traditional breakfast and the food given to anyone unwell; beef and finely diced vegetables make it a filling one-pot meal.

Method

  1. Soak the rice for 30 minutes, drain it and crush it roughly with a rolling pin or pulse it briefly in a food processor, so it breaks down into a smoother porridge.
  2. Heat the sesame oil in a heavy pot over medium heat. Stir-fry the beef and garlic for 2 minutes, until the beef loses its color, then add the rice and stir for 2 to 3 minutes, until the grains turn translucent at the edges.
  3. Add the water and bring to a boil, then lower the heat and simmer for 20 minutes, stirring often and scraping the bottom so it does not catch.
  4. Add the carrot, zucchini, onion and mushrooms and simmer for 10 to 15 minutes more, stirring, until the rice is soft and creamy and the vegetables are tender. Thin with a little hot water if it gets too thick.
  5. Season with the soup soy sauce and salt and serve topped with green onion and crumbled seaweed, with kimchi on the side.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Not stirring lets the rice stick and scorch, especially on electric hobs.
  • Adding salt early makes the rice tighten; season at the end.

Authentic tips

  • Lightly crushing the soaked rice speeds up the cooking.
  • Serve with jangjorim or kimchi on the side.

Substitutions

  • Cooked rice (400 g) makes it in 15 minutes.
  • Chicken or shrimp replace the beef.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; loosen with water when reheating.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Kimchi and soy-braised beef
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
