Daging Masak Hitam (Malay Black Beef Stew)

▾

Surprise me with

Masak hitam, beef braised in a dark, sweet soy sauce with spices, is a Malay festive dish of the Hari Raya table.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 2 hr
  • Difficulty Medium
  • Draft Malaysia / Singapore Beef LunchDinnerBraise / stewSpicySlow (2 hr+ cooking)

Ingredients

  • 700 g1.5 lbBeef brisket, cubed
  • 90 ml6 tbspKecap manis
  • 100 g3.5 ozShallot, garlic, ginger
  • 8 g3 tspStar anise, cinnamon, cardamom
  • 30 g1 ozChilies
  • 15 g1/2 ozTamarind
  • 600 ml2.5 cupsWater

Method

  1. Blend the shallot, garlic, ginger and chilies and fry with the whole spices in a little oil for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the beef and cook 5 minutes until browned.
  3. Add the kecap manis, tamarind and water, cover and simmer for 100 minutes until very tender.
  4. Uncover and reduce the sauce 10 minutes until thick and dark.
  5. Serve with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the kecap manis makes it bitter.
  • Boiling hard toughens the beef.

Authentic tips

  • Better the next day.
  • Add potatoes in the last 30 minutes.

Substitutions

  • Short rib replaces brisket.
  • A pressure cooker cuts the braise to 55 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Nasi minyak or steamed rice
  • Soup: Acar
  • Dessert: Kuih
  • Beverage: Teh tarik
