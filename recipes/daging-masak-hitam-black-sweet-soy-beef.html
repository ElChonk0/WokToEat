Daging Masak Hitam (Malay Black Beef Stew)

▾

Surprise me with

Masak hitam, beef braised in a dark, sweet soy sauce with spices, is a Malay festive dish of the Hari Raya table.

  • Cook 2 hr

Method

  1. Blend the shallots, garlic, ginger and soaked chilies into a paste.
  2. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven over medium heat and fry the star anise, cinnamon, cardamom and lemongrass for 30 seconds, then the paste for 8 to 10 minutes, stirring, until dark and the oil separates.
  3. Add the beef and stir to coat for 5 minutes, then add the kecap manis, dark soy, tamarind, palm sugar and water. Bring to a boil.
  4. Cover and simmer gently for 1.5 hours, stirring now and then, until the beef is tender.
  5. Uncover and simmer for 15 to 20 minutes more, stirring as it thickens, until the sauce has reduced to a glossy, nearly black glaze that clings to the beef; masak hitam means 'cooked black'. Add the onion rings for the last 5 minutes and season with salt. Serve with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the kecap manis makes it bitter.
  • Boiling hard toughens the beef.

Authentic tips

  • Better the next day.
  • Add potatoes in the last 30 minutes.

Substitutions

  • Short rib replaces brisket.
  • A pressure cooker cuts the braise to 55 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Nasi minyak or steamed rice
  • Soup: Acar
  • Dessert: Kuih
  • Beverage: Teh tarik
