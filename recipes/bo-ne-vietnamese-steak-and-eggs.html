Bò Né (Vietnamese Sizzling Steak and Eggs)

▾

Surprise me with

Bo ne, steak and fried eggs served on a sizzling plate with pate and bread, is a popular Saigon breakfast showing the French influence.

  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 400 g14 ozBeef steak, sliced
  • 44Eggs
  • 80 g3 ozPate
  • 22Baguette
  • 150 g5 ozOnion and tomato
  • 30 ml2 tbspSoy and Maggi
  • 30 g2 tbspButter
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt

Method

  1. Season the beef with salt and pepper.
  2. Heat the butter in a cast-iron pan and sear the beef and onion for 2 minutes.
  3. Fry the eggs on the side.
  4. Serve on a hot plate with the pate, tomato and baguette.
  5. Dip the bread in the sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the beef makes it tough.
  • Cold plates cool the dish quickly.

Authentic tips

  • Serve sizzling on a hot cast-iron plate.
  • Add Maggi sauce for the Saigon flavor.

Substitutions

  • Ribeye or strip steak replaces sirloin.
  • Skip the pate for a lighter dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Baguette for dipping
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Vietnamese iced coffee
