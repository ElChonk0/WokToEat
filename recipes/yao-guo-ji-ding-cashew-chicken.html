Yāo Guǒ Jī Dīng (Cashew Chicken) 腰果鸡丁

▾

Surprise me with

Cashew chicken, diced chicken stir-fried with crunchy cashews and vegetables, is a Cantonese restaurant classic that spread across Chinese-American menus.

Method

  1. Toast the cashews on a tray at 180 C (350 F) for 5 to 7 minutes, or in a dry pan over medium-low heat, until golden and fragrant, then cool them. Toasting ahead is what keeps them crunchy in the sauce.
  2. Mix the chicken with the marinade oyster sauce, cornstarch and water until the liquid is fully absorbed, then stir in the marinade oil. Mix the stock, soy sauces, honey, hoisin, vinegar, sesame oil and white pepper in a jug, and the slurry in a cup. The cooking takes only a few minutes, so have everything ready before the wok goes on.
  3. Heat a flat-bottom wok over high heat until it just smokes and swirl in 2 tablespoons of oil. Spread the chicken in a single layer and sear for 1 minute without moving, then turn and sear 1 minute more, until browned outside and about 80% cooked. Lift it out.
  4. Add the remaining oil and the ginger for 5 seconds, then the garlic, celery, bell pepper, water chestnuts and green onion. Stir-fry for 30 seconds to 1 minute, keeping the vegetables crisp.
  5. Pour the wine around the hot edge of the wok and stir for 10 seconds, then add the sauce, scraping the sides. Return the chicken and its juices and bring to a simmer.
  6. Stir in about three quarters of the slurry and simmer until the sauce thickens and turns glossy, adding the rest if you like it thicker. Toss in the cashews and serve straight away over rice.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the cashews while the sauce is bubbling softens them; fold them in last.
  • Crowding the wok steams the chicken, so cook it in two batches if the pan is small.

Authentic tips

  • Toast raw cashews in a dry pan for 5 minutes if you cannot find roasted ones.
  • Cut the chicken and vegetables the same size so they finish together.

Substitutions

  • Roasted peanuts or almonds replace the cashews; add them at the end so they stay crisp.
  • Chicken breast works if you velvet it with cornstarch and cook it 1 minute less.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; the cashews soften, so reheat in a hot wok and add a few fresh nuts.

Freezing: Not recommended; the nuts and celery turn soggy.

Serve with
