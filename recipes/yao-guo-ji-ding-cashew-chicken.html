Yāo Guǒ Jī Dīng (Cashew Chicken) 腰果鸡丁

Cashew chicken, diced chicken stir-fried with crunchy cashews and vegetables, is a Cantonese restaurant classic that spread across Chinese-American menus.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 8 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

  • 500 g1.1 lbChicken thighs, diced
  • 80 g3 ozRoasted cashews
  • 250 g9 ozCelery and bell pepper
  • 30 g2 tbspGarlic and ginger
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspOyster sauce
  • 15 g1 tbspCornstarch
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Toss the diced chicken with 15 ml of the soy sauce, the cornstarch and 1 tablespoon of water; leave 10 minutes.
  2. Heat half the oil in a wok over high heat until it shimmers, then stir-fry the chicken 2-3 minutes until golden at the edges but not cooked through; remove.
  3. Add the remaining oil, then the garlic and ginger, and stir-fry 15 seconds until fragrant.
  4. Add the celery and bell pepper and toss 1-2 minutes, keeping them crisp.
  5. Return the chicken with the remaining soy sauce and the oyster sauce and cook 1 minute until glossy and cooked through.
  6. Fold in the cashews off the heat and serve at once.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the cashews while the sauce is bubbling softens them; fold them in last.
  • Crowding the wok steams the chicken, so cook it in two batches if the pan is small.

Authentic tips

  • Toast raw cashews in a dry pan for 5 minutes if you cannot find roasted ones.
  • Cut the chicken and vegetables the same size so they finish together.

Substitutions

  • Roasted peanuts or almonds replace the cashews; add them at the end so they stay crisp.
  • Chicken breast works if you velvet it with cornstarch and cook it 1 minute less.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; the cashews soften, so reheat in a hot wok and add a few fresh nuts.

Freezing: Not recommended; the nuts and celery turn soggy.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Mango pomelo sago
  • Beverage: Hot jasmine tea
