Lú Sǔn Chǎo Dài Zi (Scallops and Asparagus Stir-Fry) 芦笋炒带子

Seared scallops with asparagus in a light sauce is a modern Cantonese banquet and restaurant dish.

Method

  1. Put the scallops in a bowl, pour over the soaking wine and boiling water and leave for 1 minute, then drain and pat them very dry. This firms them up so they need less time in the wok and stay plump. Mix the oyster sauce, salt, sugar, cornstarch and water.
  2. Snap the woody ends off the asparagus and cut it on a sharp diagonal. Bring a pot of water to a boil with the blanching salt and a splash of oil, blanch the asparagus for 30 seconds and the carrot for 20 seconds, then plunge them into cold water and drain. The brief blanch keeps them bright green and crisp.
  3. Heat a flat-bottom wok over high heat with the oil and fry the garlic and ginger for 15 seconds. Lower to medium, lay in the scallops and sear without moving for 45 to 60 seconds until golden underneath, then turn and cook 30 to 40 seconds more.
  4. Add the onion for 30 seconds, then raise the heat and add the asparagus and carrot, tossing for 30 to 50 seconds until hot.
  5. Stir the sauce and add it gradually, tossing until it coats everything with a light gloss; thin with a spoon of water if needed. Turn off the heat, drizzle with sesame oil and serve.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Wet scallops will not brown; dry them well.
  • Overcooked scallops turn rubbery; sear fast.

Authentic tips

  • Use the thinnest asparagus spears you can find.
  • Add a few slices of yellow bell pepper for color.

Substitutions

  • Shrimp replace scallops.
  • Snow peas or gai lan replace asparagus.

Storage

Leftovers: Best eaten at once.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Winter melon seafood soup
  • Dessert: Almond jelly
  • Beverage: Chilled white wine or tea
