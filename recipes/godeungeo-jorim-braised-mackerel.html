Godeungeo Jorim (Braised Mackerel with Radish) 고등어조림

Godeungeo jorim, mackerel braised with Korean radish in a spicy gochugaru sauce, is a classic Korean home dish.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 30 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Korea Seafood LunchDinnerBraise / stewSpicy

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 700 g1.5 lbMackerel, cut into pieces
  • 300 g10.5 ozKorean radish
  • 30 g1 ozGochugaru
  • 45 ml3 tbspSoy sauce
  • 30 g1 ozGochujang
  • 40 g1.5 ozGarlic and ginger
  • 15 g1 tbspSugar
  • 300 ml1.25 cupsWater

Method

  1. Slice the radish into thick half-moons and lay them over the bottom of a wide pot.
  2. Mix the soy sauce, gochugaru, gochujang, garlic, ginger, sugar and water and pour over the radish; bring to a boil and cook 10 minutes.
  3. Rinse the mackerel and lay on top.
  4. Cover and simmer 20 minutes, spooning the sauce over.
  5. Serve with the radish and sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Stirring breaks up the fish.
  • Skipping the rinse leaves a fishy smell.

Authentic tips

  • Add green chilies for extra heat.
  • Radish absorbs the sauce and is half the pleasure.

Substitutions

  • Saury or cod replace mackerel.
  • Kimchi added to the braise is a common variation.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and kimchi
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
