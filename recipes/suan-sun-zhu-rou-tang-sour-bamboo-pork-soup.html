Suān Sǔn Zhū Ròu Tāng (Sour Bamboo Shoot Pork Soup) 酸笋煮猪肉

Sour bamboo shoot soups are part of the cooking of Yunnan's Dai and other minorities; pork adds richness to the tangy broth.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 40 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Yunnan Pork LunchDinnerSoupSpicy

Ingredients

  • 600 g1.3 lbPork ribs or belly
  • 150 g5 ozSour bamboo shoots, rinsed
  • 20 g2/3 ozChilies
  • 40 g3 tbspGinger and garlic
  • 1 L4 cupsWater
  • 6 g1 tspSalt
  • 15 g1/2 ozCilantro

Method

  1. Cover the pork with cold water, boil 3 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  2. Return to a clean pot with 1 L water and the ginger and simmer gently 25 minutes.
  3. Rinse the sour bamboo shoots and add with the chilies and garlic.
  4. Simmer 10 minutes more.
  5. Season with the salt and top with cilantro.

Common mistakes

  • Not rinsing the shoots makes the soup too sour.
  • Boiling hard makes the broth cloudy.

Authentic tips

  • Skim the fat for a lighter soup.
  • Serve with plain rice.

Substitutions

  • Rinsed sauerkraut plus a squeeze of lime replaces sour bamboo shoots.
  • Spare ribs replace pork belly.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
