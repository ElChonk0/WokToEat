Escabeche (Filipino Sweet-and-Sour Fish)

▾

Surprise me with

Escabeche, fried fish topped with a sweet-and-sour vegetable sauce, arrived with Spanish influence and is a Filipino fiesta staple.

Method

  1. Score the fish deeply, rub with salt and pat very dry. Dust lightly with cornstarch.
  2. Heat the oil in a large skillet over medium-high heat and fry the fish for 5 to 6 minutes per side until golden and crisp. Drain and lay it on a platter.
  3. Pour off all but 2 tablespoons of oil and stir-fry the ginger, garlic and onion for 1 minute, then the carrot and peppers for 1 minute until just tender-crisp.
  4. Add the vinegar and let it boil for 1 minute without stirring, then the sugar, water and ketchup. Simmer for 2 minutes and thicken with the slurry.
  5. Pour the sweet-sour sauce and vegetables over the fish and serve at once with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Pouring the sauce too early makes the skin soggy.
  • Burning the sugar.

Authentic tips

  • Use a whole tilapia or lapu-lapu.
  • Serve immediately.

Substitutions

  • Fillets fry in less time.
  • Tilapia is the classic fish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Leche flan
  • Beverage: Calamansi juice
