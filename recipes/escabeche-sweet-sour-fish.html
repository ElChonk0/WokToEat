Escabeche (Filipino Sweet-and-Sour Fish)

▾

Surprise me with

Escabeche, fried fish topped with a sweet-and-sour vegetable sauce, arrived with Spanish influence and is a Filipino fiesta staple.

  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 45 g3 tbspSugar
  • 20 g1 tbspGinger
  • 30 g2 tbspCornstarch
  • 400 ml1.75 cupsNeutral oil for frying
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt

Method

  1. Season the fish with the salt and dust with the cornstarch.
  2. Fry in oil at 180 C (350 F) for 6-8 minutes, turning once, until crisp; drain.
  3. Simmer the vinegar, sugar, ginger, carrots, pepper and onion with 100 ml water for 5 minutes.
  4. Thicken with a cornstarch slurry.
  5. Pour over the fish and serve.

Common mistakes

  • Pouring the sauce too early makes the skin soggy.
  • Burning the sugar.

Authentic tips

  • Use a whole tilapia or lapu-lapu.
  • Serve immediately.

Substitutions

  • Fillets fry in less time.
  • Tilapia is the classic fish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Leche flan
  • Beverage: Calamansi juice
