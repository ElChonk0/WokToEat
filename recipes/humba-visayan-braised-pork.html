Humba (Visayan Braised Pork Belly)

▾

Surprise me with

Humba, pork belly braised in soy sauce, vinegar, black beans and brown sugar, is a Visayan dish with Chinese influences, a sweeter relative of adobo.

  • Cook 2 hr

  • 500 g1.1 lbPork belly, cubed
  • 90 ml6 tbspSoy sauce
  • 60 ml4 tbspVinegar
  • 45 g3 tbspBrown sugar
  • 20 g3/4 ozFermented black beans
  • 40 g1.5 ozGarlic and bay
  • 100 g3.5 ozBanana blossom (optional)
  • 500 ml2 cupsWater

Method

  1. Brown the pork belly cubes in a dry heavy pot over medium heat for 8 minutes.
  2. Add the garlic, soy sauce, vinegar, sugar, black beans and bay leaves; do not stir for 2 minutes after adding the vinegar.
  3. Add the water, bring to a boil, cover and simmer on low for 90 minutes until tender.
  4. Add the banana blossom for the last 20 minutes.
  5. Uncover and reduce the sauce until syrupy.

Common mistakes

  • Stirring right after adding vinegar leaves a raw sour edge.
  • Boiling hard toughens the pork.

Authentic tips

  • Add fried saba bananas or boiled eggs.
  • Better the next day.

Substitutions

  • Pork shoulder gives a leaner dish.
  • Fried saba bananas are a classic addition.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Calamansi juice
