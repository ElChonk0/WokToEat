Zi Ran Ji Rou Chuan (Cumin Chicken Skewers) 孜然鸡肉串

Skewers dusted with cumin and chili are Xinjiang's signature street food; lamb is the classic, but chicken skewers are a common alternative.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 700 g1.5 lbChicken thighs, cubed
  • 10 g1 tbspCumin seeds, ground
  • 6 g2 tspChili flakes
  • 8 g1.5 tspSalt
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil
  • 1212Bamboo skewers

Method

  1. Mix the soy sauce, oil and half the cumin and toss with the chicken; marinate at least 30 minutes.
  2. Soak bamboo skewers in water for 20 minutes and thread the chicken tightly.
  3. Grill over high heat (or a very hot ridged pan) for 8-10 minutes, turning every 2 minutes, until charred in spots and cooked through.
  4. Sprinkle with the remaining cumin, chili flakes and salt.
  5. Serve straight off the grill.

Common mistakes

  • Soaked skewers still burn if the heat is too low and cooking takes too long.
  • Overcooking dries the chicken.

Authentic tips

  • Toast the cumin seeds and grind them fresh.
  • Lamb is the traditional meat; lamb shoulder works with the same method.

Substitutions

  • Lamb is the traditional meat; use shoulder with the same method.
  • Bone-in thighs on larger skewers are juicier.
  • No grill: lay the skewers on a wire rack over a foil-lined sheet pan and broil on high about 10 cm (4 in) from the element for 4 to 5 minutes per side, or roast at 230°C (450°F) for 12 to 15 minutes, turning once, until charred at the edges and cooked through.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot pan.

Freezing: Freeze raw marinated skewers for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Nang flatbread
  • Soup: Cucumber yogurt salad
  • Dessert: Hami melon
  • Beverage: Black tea
