Shāndōng Hóng Shāo Niú Wěi (Shandong Braised Oxtail) 山东红烧牛尾

▾

Braised oxtail is a northern banquet dish valued for its gelatin and rich flavor.

Method

  1. Soak the oxtail in cold water for 1 hour to draw out the blood, then blanch it for 10 minutes and rinse.
  2. Heat the oil and rock sugar in a Dutch oven over low heat until it melts into an amber caramel, then turn the oxtail in it until glossy.
  3. Add the ginger, green onion, garlic and spices, then the Shaoxing wine and soy sauces.
  4. Add the hot water, bring to a boil, cover and simmer over low heat for 2.5 to 3 hours, until the meat falls from the bone. Add the carrots for the last 30 minutes.
  5. Uncover and reduce for 10 to 15 minutes, until the sauce is thick and coats the oxtail. Skim off the fat before serving.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard toughens the meat.
  • Skipping the blanching clouds the sauce.

Authentic tips

  • A pressure cooker does the job in 50 minutes.
  • Better the next day.

Substitutions

  • A pressure cooker cuts the braise to 60 minutes.
  • Beef short rib replaces oxtail.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Blanched greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
