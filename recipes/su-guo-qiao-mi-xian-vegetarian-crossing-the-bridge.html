Sù Guò Qiáo Mǐ Xiàn (Vegetarian Crossing-the-Bridge Noodles) 素过桥米线

▾

Surprise me with

Crossing-the-bridge noodles, rice noodles served with a bowl of boiling broth and raw ingredients that cook at the table, are Yunnan's most famous dish; the legend says a scholar's wife carried it across a bridge to keep it hot.

Method

  1. Bring the mushroom broth to a rolling boil in a large pot.
  2. Arrange the raw tofu, greens, mushrooms and eggs (cracked into small bowls) on plates; blanch the noodles for 30 seconds.
  3. Pour the boiling broth into large bowls and float a layer of oil on the surface to keep it hot.
  4. Add the ingredients to the broth at the table in order: tofu and mushrooms first, then the eggs, greens and noodles last.
  5. Season with the chili oil and scallion.

Common mistakes

  • The broth must be boiling when it reaches the table or the raw ingredients will not cook.
  • Adding the noodles first leaves them soggy.

Authentic tips

  • Quail eggs are traditional; a raw hen's egg works.
  • Eggs make this lacto-ovo vegetarian; omit them for vegan.

Substitutions

  • Raw egg is optional; omit for a vegan version.
  • Soft tofu replaces firm tofu.

Storage

Leftovers: Store the broth and ingredients separately; the broth keeps 3 days.

Freezing: Freeze the broth for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled vegetables
  • Soup: Not needed; the soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Pu'er tea
