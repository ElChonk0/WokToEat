Huī Shì Hóng Shāo Zhū Tí (Hui-Style Braised Pig Trotters) 徽式红烧猪蹄

▾

Surprise me with

Braised trotters, collagen-rich and glossy, are a Hui banquet and winter dish with local soy sauce and mountain bamboo.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 2 hr
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 1.2 kg2.6 lbPig trotters, chopped
  • 60 ml4 tbspSoy sauce
  • 30 g2 tbspRock sugar
  • 80 g5 tbspGinger, scallion, garlic
  • 8 g3 tspStar anise and cinnamon
  • 60 ml4 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 800 ml3.5 cupsWater

Method

  1. Cut the trotters into 5 cm pieces, cover with cold water, boil 5 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  2. Melt the rock sugar in a dry pot over low heat until amber, add the trotters and turn to coat.
  3. Add the ginger, scallion, garlic, star anise, cinnamon, soy sauce, wine and water.
  4. Bring to a boil, cover and simmer on low for 90 minutes until the skin is soft and sticky.
  5. Uncover and boil 8 minutes to reduce the sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the blanching leaves a scummy sauce.
  • Burning the sugar makes the dish bitter.

Authentic tips

  • Cook until the skin yields to a spoon.
  • A pressure cooker does it in 35 minutes.

Substitutions

  • Pork hock replaces trotters.
  • A pressure cooker cuts the braise to 50 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; the sauce sets to a jelly.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Blanched greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
