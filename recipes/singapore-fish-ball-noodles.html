Singapore Fish Ball Noodles

▾

Surprise me with

Fish ball noodles, bouncy fish balls with egg noodles dressed in vinegar and chili, are a Singapore hawker breakfast of Teochew origin.

  • Cook 15 min

  • 150 g5 ozChoy sum
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 20 g2 tbspScallion

Method

  1. Simmer the stock gently with the fish balls for 5 minutes.
  2. Blanch the noodles for 1 minute and the choy sum for 1 minute.
  3. Toss the noodles with the vinegar, chili sauce and soy sauce.
  4. Divide among bowls with the fish balls and greens.
  5. Serve with the soup on the side; top with shallots and scallion.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the noodles loses their bounce.
  • Boiling the fish balls hard makes them rubbery.

Authentic tips

  • Fishcake slices are a classic addition.
  • Serve dry (with sauce) or in soup.

Substitutions

  • Spaghetti replaces mee pok.
  • Fish cake slices are a classic addition.

Storage

Leftovers: Store noodles and soup separately; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Chili sauce
