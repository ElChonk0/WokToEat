Jiāng Mǐ Chǎo Fàn (Ginger Egg Fried Rice) 姜米炒饭

Fried rice with a generous amount of finely minced ginger and no soy sauce is a warming Cantonese home dish, often cooked for children and new mothers.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Squeeze some of the juice out of the minced ginger so it fries crisp rather than steams.
  2. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a flat-bottom wok over medium heat and fry the ginger for 3 to 4 minutes until golden and fragrant. Lift out.
  3. Add the remaining oil over high heat, pour in the eggs, stir once and add the rice before they set. Toss 3 minutes until hot and separate.
  4. Return the ginger, season with salt and pepper and toss 1 minute.
  5. Add the green onion and serve.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Wet ginger steams and stays sharp; squeeze it and fry it slowly.
  • Soy sauce would hide the ginger; this rice stays pale.

Authentic tips

  • The squeezed ginger juice can be added to soup or tea.
  • Mince the ginger by hand for the best texture.

Substitutions

  • Add diced chicken for a fuller meal.
  • Add a little fish sauce for depth if it does not need to be vegetarian.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Garlic broccoli
  • Soup: Seaweed and egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
