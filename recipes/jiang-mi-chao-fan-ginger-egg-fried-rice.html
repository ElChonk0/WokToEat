Jiāng Mǐ Chǎo Fàn (Ginger Egg Fried Rice) 姜米炒饭

▾

Surprise me with

Fried rice with a generous amount of finely minced ginger and no soy sauce is a warming Cantonese home dish, often cooked for children and new mothers.

Method

  1. Break up the rice with wet hands so no clumps remain. Mince the ginger very finely; leaving the skin on gives more fragrance. Beat the eggs with their salt.
  2. Heat a flat-bottom wok over the highest heat, add 2 tablespoons of oil and swirl, then pour in the eggs and stir for 45 to 60 seconds until only partly set. Lift them out; they finish later and stay tender.
  3. Add the remaining oil and the ginger and fry over medium heat for about 1 minute, until golden at the edges and very fragrant. This toasted ginger is the whole point of the dish.
  4. Add the rice, spread it out with the spatula and leave it for 30 seconds, then stir-fry for about 1 minute over high heat, pressing out any clumps.
  5. Add the salt and pepper and toss for 30 seconds, then return the eggs, breaking them up, and fold for about 2 minutes until the rice is hot and every grain is coated.
  6. Add the green onion and toss for 1 minute more, then fluff and serve.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Wet ginger steams and stays sharp; squeeze it and fry it slowly.
  • Soy sauce would hide the ginger; this rice stays pale.

Authentic tips

  • The squeezed ginger juice can be added to soup or tea.
  • Mince the ginger by hand for the best texture.

Substitutions

  • Add diced chicken for a fuller meal.
  • Add a little fish sauce for depth if it does not need to be vegetarian.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Garlic broccoli
  • Soup: Seaweed and egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
