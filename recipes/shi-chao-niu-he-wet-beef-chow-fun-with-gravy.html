Shī Chǎo Niú Hé (Beef Chow Fun with Gravy) 湿炒牛河

▾

Surprise me with

"Wet" chow fun, seared rice noodles under a glossy beef and vegetable gravy, is the sister of dry-fried beef ho fun and a Cantonese restaurant standard.

Method

  1. Soak the beef slices in cold water for a few minutes, drain and squeeze dry. Massage in the beef salt, sugar, pepper, soy sauces, oyster sauce, cornstarch and water, then 1 tablespoon of the oil. Mix the gravy oyster sauce, soy sauces, sugar, stock and cornstarch. Peel the noodles apart on a dry surface.
  2. Heat a dry flat-bottom wok over high heat, add 1 tablespoon of oil and lower to medium. Spread the noodles out and fry undisturbed for about 1 minute, flip and fry 30 to 40 seconds more, until lightly charred. Add the noodle soy sauce, toss gently and spread on a platter.
  3. Reheat the wok with 3 tablespoons of oil, lower the heat to medium and add the beef. Cook for about 1 minute, stirring gently, until 60 to 70% cooked, then lift it out and drain. Gentler heat keeps the beef tender.
  4. Pour off all but a thin film of oil. Fry the garlic and ginger for 10 seconds, the black beans for 20 to 30 seconds, then the onion, peppers and greens for 1 minute, adding a splash of water so the greens steam to tender-crisp.
  5. Return the beef for 15 to 20 seconds, then stir the gravy and pour it in. Simmer for 30 to 60 seconds until it thickens and turns glossy; thin with a little water if it gets too heavy. Add the sesame oil.
  6. Pour the beef and gravy over the noodles and serve at once.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Cold, stiff noodles break; warm them briefly in a microwave to soften before frying.
  • A thin gravy makes the noodles soggy; it should be thick and glossy.

Authentic tips

  • Some restaurants stir a beaten egg into the gravy (wat dan ngau ho).
  • On induction, fry the noodles in two batches to keep the char.

Substitutions

  • Dried wide rice noodles, soaked, if fresh are unavailable.
  • Chicken or seafood replace beef.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator; the noodles soften.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled vegetables
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Mango pudding
  • Beverage: Iced lemon tea
