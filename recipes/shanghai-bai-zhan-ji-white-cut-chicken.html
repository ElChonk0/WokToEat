Shanghai Bái Zhǎn Jī (Shanghai White-Cut Chicken) 白斩鸡

Bai zhan ji, chicken poached gently and served at room temperature with a ginger-soy dip, is a Shanghai and Jiangnan cold dish that prizes tender, juicy flesh.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 30 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 1.4 kg3 lbWhole chicken
  • 60 g4 tbspGinger and scallion
  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 45 ml3 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 10 ml2 tspSesame oil
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 8 g1.5 tspSalt

Method

  1. Bring a stockpot of water to a boil with the ginger, scallion and wine. Lower in the chicken breast-side down, making sure it is submerged.
  2. Return to a bare simmer for 5 minutes, then cover, turn off the heat and let the chicken steep 25 minutes.
  3. Check that the thigh joint is just cooked (75 C / 165 F) and plunge the chicken into a bowl of ice water for 10 minutes to firm the skin.
  4. Chop through the bone into pieces and arrange on a plate.
  5. Stir together the soy sauce, sesame oil, sugar and salt with some minced ginger for the dipping sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling the chicken makes it dry; the gentle steep keeps it moist.
  • Skipping the ice bath gives a loose, rubbery skin.

Authentic tips

  • A slightly pink near the bone is fine if the juices run clear, but cook it through for safety.
  • Use the poaching liquid as an excellent stock.

Substitutions

  • A small whole poussin cooks in about 20 minutes.
  • Drumsticks and thighs only make a more forgiving dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; serve cold with the ginger-scallion sauce.

Freezing: Not recommended; the poached chicken turns watery.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Plain rice with ginger-scallion sauce
  • Soup: Clear chicken broth
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot jasmine tea
