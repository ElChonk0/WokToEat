Taiwanese Beef Noodle Soup 牛肉麵

▾

Surprise me with

Considered by many Taiwanese to be their unofficial national dish, niu rou mian slow-braises beef in a five-spice, doubanjiang, and soy broth until deeply savory — a dish so beloved that Taipei holds an annual competition dedicated to it.

Method

  1. Blanch the beef in boiling water for 5 minutes, drain and rinse.
  2. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven over medium heat and fry the ginger, garlic, green onion and shallots for 3 minutes until golden. Add the doubanjiang and fry for 1 to 2 minutes until the oil turns red, then the tomato for 2 minutes.
  3. Add the beef, rice wine, soy sauces and rock sugar and toss for 2 minutes until coated.
  4. Add the water and the spices (in a spice bag if you have one), bring to a boil, then cover and simmer gently for 2 to 2.5 hours, until the beef is very tender. Remove the spices and skim the fat, keeping some for flavor.
  5. Cook the noodles according to the packet and blanch the bok choy in the same water.
  6. Divide the noodles among bowls, add the beef and bok choy and ladle over the hot broth. Top with pickled mustard greens and green onion. This red-braised version was born in Taiwan's military villages after 1949.

Common mistakes

  • Rushing the braise — beef shank needs real time to become properly tender and for the broth to develop depth.
  • Skipping the initial blanch, which leaves the broth cloudier and slightly muddier in flavor.

Authentic tips

  • This broth freezes and reheats exceptionally well; a double batch is well worth the long braising time.
  • Pickled mustard greens add a sour, crunchy contrast that cuts through the richness of the broth — don't skip them.

Substitutions

  • Beef brisket or short rib can substitute for shank with a similar braising time.
  • For a milder version, reduce the doubanjiang by half.

Storage

Leftovers: The broth and beef improve overnight; store noodles separately.

Freezing: The braised beef and broth freeze excellently for up to 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled mustard greens
  • Soup: Not needed; the noodle soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Oolong tea
