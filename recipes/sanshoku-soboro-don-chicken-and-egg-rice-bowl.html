Sanshoku Soboro Don (Three-Color Ground Chicken Bowl) 三色そぼろ丼

▾

Surprise me with

Soboro don, rice topped with sweet-soy ground chicken, fine scrambled egg and green vegetables, is a classic Japanese bento and home meal.

Method

  1. Put the chicken, soy sauce, mirin, sake, sugar and ginger in a cold pan and stir with 4 chopsticks to break it up before heating.
  2. Cook over medium heat, stirring constantly, for 6 to 8 minutes until fine, crumbly and the liquid is nearly gone.
  3. Beat the eggs with the sugar and salt and cook in a non-stick pan over low heat, stirring with chopsticks, into fine dry curds.
  4. Slice the blanched snow peas into thin strips.
  5. Arrange the chicken, egg and greens in three sections over bowls of rice.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Heating the chicken before stirring makes lumps; break it up cold.
  • High heat makes the egg rubbery rather than fine.

Authentic tips

  • Four chopsticks held together are the Japanese trick for fine soboro.
  • Excellent cold in bento boxes.

Substitutions

  • Ground pork or beef work the same way.
  • Spinach or peas for the green.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; the toppings pack well.

Freezing: The chicken soboro freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled ginger
  • Soup: Miso soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Green tea
