Sanshoku Soboro Don (Three-Color Ground Chicken Bowl) 三色そぼろ丼

▾

Surprise me with

Soboro don, rice topped with sweet-soy ground chicken, fine scrambled egg and green vegetables, is a classic Japanese bento and home meal.

Method

  1. Put the ground chicken, soy sauce, mirin, sake, sugar and ginger in a cold saucepan and stir with 4 or 5 chopsticks held together until evenly mixed, before turning on the heat. Starting cold and stirring constantly is what gives fine, fluffy soboro instead of clumps.
  2. Cook over medium heat, stirring with the chopsticks constantly, for 5 to 6 minutes, until the chicken is cooked and crumbled finely and most of the liquid has evaporated.
  3. Beat the eggs with the sugar and salt. Cook them in a nonstick pan over medium-low heat, stirring constantly with several chopsticks, until they set into fine, dry, golden crumbs.
  4. Blanch the snow peas for 1 minute in salted water, cool them in ice water and slice them thinly on an angle.
  5. Fill bowls with rice and arrange the chicken, egg and green peas in three neat sections (sanshoku means 'three colors'). Spoon a little of the chicken juices over the rice.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Heating the chicken before stirring makes lumps; break it up cold.
  • High heat makes the egg rubbery rather than fine.

Authentic tips

  • Four chopsticks held together are the Japanese trick for fine soboro.
  • Excellent cold in bento boxes.

Substitutions

  • Ground pork or beef work the same way.
  • Spinach or peas for the green.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; the toppings pack well.

Freezing: The chicken soboro freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled ginger
  • Soup: Miso soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Green tea
