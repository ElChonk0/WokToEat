Mèn Zhū Jiǎo (Hakka Braised Pork Trotters) 焖猪脚

▾

Braised pig trotters are a Hakka dish of long-simmered pork feet with mushrooms and soy-based sauces, valued for the gelatinous skin.

Method

  1. Blanch the trotters in boiling water for 8 minutes, drain, rinse and pat dry.
  2. Heat the oil in a heavy pot, brown the trotters over medium-high heat for 6 minutes, and lift out.
  3. Fry the ginger, garlic, star anise and cinnamon in the same pot for 1 minute, add the rock sugar and cook for 1 minute until it melts and turns amber.
  4. Return the trotters, add both soy sauces, the wine, mushrooms and water, bring to a boil, cover and simmer on low for 1 hour 30 minutes.
  5. Uncover and simmer for 15 to 20 minutes, until the sauce is glossy and the skin is soft enough to cut with a spoon.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the blanching leaves a gamy smell and a cloudy sauce.
  • Boiling hard toughens the skin; keep the braise at a gentle simmer.

Authentic tips

  • Rinse the mushrooms well and keep the soaking liquid for the braise.
  • Chill the pot overnight and skim the fat for a cleaner sauce.

Substitutions

  • Pork hock replaces trotters if you cannot find them.
  • Add peeled peanuts for a nutty variation.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days in the refrigerator; the jelly melts as it reheats.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months with the sauce.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and blanched choy sum
  • Soup: Not needed; the braise is rich
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot pu-erh tea
