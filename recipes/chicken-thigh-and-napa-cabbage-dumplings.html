Chicken Thigh and Napa Cabbage Dumplings 鸡肉饺子

▾

Surprise me with

Dumplings are central to northern Chinese cuisine, especially around Beijing and Shandong; this chicken-and-napa-cabbage filling is a lighter, everyday alternative to the more common pork filling.

Method

  1. Toss the cabbage with a little salt and leave for 15 minutes, then squeeze out as much water as you can; wet filling makes leaky dumplings.
  2. Mix the chicken with the ginger, soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, salt and pepper. Add the Sichuan pepper water a tablespoon at a time, stirring hard in one direction, until it is absorbed and the filling turns sticky and springy. This makes juicy dumplings. Fold in the vegetables and sesame oil.
  3. Put a heaping teaspoon of filling in the center of a wrapper, fold it in half, pinch the middle and pleat or press the edges to seal tightly.
  4. Bring a large pot of water to a rolling boil and add 15 to 20 dumplings, stirring gently so they do not stick. When it returns to a boil, add 250 ml (1 cup) of cold water; repeat twice. They are done when they float and look puffed, 6 to 8 minutes.
  5. Lift out with a slotted spoon and serve with black vinegar, garlic and chili oil. Shandong is famous for its dumplings, made thick-skinned and generously filled.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the salting-and-squeezing step for the cabbage, which leaves the filling watery and hard to wrap.
  • Overfilling the wrappers, which makes sealing difficult and dumplings prone to bursting during cooking.

Authentic tips

  • Mixing the filling in one consistent direction (not back and forth) helps develop a cohesive, less crumbly texture.
  • Extra dumplings freeze beautifully uncooked — freeze on a tray first, then bag once solid, to prevent sticking.

Substitutions

  • Ground turkey works as a substitute with a similar result.
  • Chives can replace or supplement green onion for a more traditional flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Cooked dumplings keep 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes excellently uncooked for up to 3 months; cook directly from frozen, adding a couple extra minutes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Black vinegar and garlic dip
  • Soup: Hot and sour soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
