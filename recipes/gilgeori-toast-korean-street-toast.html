Gilgeori Toast (Korean Street Toast) 길거리 토스트

▾

Gilgeori toast, a buttery pan-fried sandwich of cabbage omelet, ham, cheese and a sweet sauce, is the classic Korean street breakfast sold from carts near subway stations.

Method

  1. Melt a little butter in a nonstick skillet over medium heat and toast the bread for 1 to 2 minutes per side until golden. Set aside.
  2. For each sandwich, put a quarter of the vegetables in a deep bowl, keeping a pinch of each aside, add an egg with a pinch of salt and pepper and mash with a fork for about 20 seconds, until the cabbage wilts and the mixture holds together. This keeps the omelet from falling apart in the pan.
  3. Melt more butter over medium heat, scoop in the mixture and shape it into a square roughly the size of the bread. Scatter the reserved raw vegetables on top for crunch and cook for about 3 minutes until set and golden underneath.
  4. Flip, add a little butter around the edge and cook for about 2 minutes more. Lay a slice of ham beside it to warm for the last minute.
  5. Build each toast: bread, omelet, ham and cheese, a generous sprinkle of sugar, a squeeze of ketchup and mayonnaise, then the second slice. The sweet-salty contrast is the point of the street version. Serve hot, wrapped in paper.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • A thick omelet stays raw in the middle; keep it to 1.5 cm.
  • Skipping the sugar loses the street-cart flavor.

Authentic tips

  • The sprinkle of sugar is the signature of the Seoul street version.
  • Press each sandwich briefly in the pan to melt the cheese.

Substitutions

  • Bacon replaces ham.
  • Leave out ham for a vegetarian toast.
