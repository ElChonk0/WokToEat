Gilgeori Toast (Korean Street Toast) 길거리 토스트

▾

Surprise me with

Gilgeori toast, a buttery pan-fried sandwich of cabbage omelet, ham, cheese and a sweet sauce, is the classic Korean street breakfast sold from carts near subway stations.

Method

  1. Beat the eggs with the salt and mix in the cabbage, carrot and green onion.
  2. Melt a little butter in a skillet over medium heat and pour in a quarter of the egg mixture, shaping it into a square the size of a bread slice. Cook 2 minutes per side.
  3. Toast the bread in butter in another pan until golden.
  4. Layer on one slice: omelet, ham, cheese, a sprinkle of sugar, ketchup and mayonnaise, then top with a second slice.
  5. Repeat for 4 sandwiches and serve warm, wrapped in paper.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • A thick omelet stays raw in the middle; keep it to 1.5 cm.
  • Skipping the sugar loses the street-cart flavor.

Authentic tips

  • The sprinkle of sugar is the signature of the Seoul street version.
  • Press each sandwich briefly in the pan to melt the cheese.

Substitutions

  • Bacon replaces ham.
  • Leave out ham for a vegetarian toast.
