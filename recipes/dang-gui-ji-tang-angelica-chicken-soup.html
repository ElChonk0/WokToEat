Dāng Guī Jī Tāng (Angelica Root Chicken Soup) 當歸雞湯

▾

Surprise me with

Dang gui, angelica root, is a warming herb used in Taiwanese tonic soups, especially in winter and after childbirth.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 60 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 15 g1/2 ozDang gui slices
  • 15 g1 tbspGoji berries
  • 20 g2/3 ozRed dates
  • 40 g3 tbspGinger
  • 60 ml4 tbspRice wine
  • 1.5 L6 cupsWater
  • 6 g1 tspSalt

Method

  1. Cover the chicken with cold water, boil 3 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  2. Return to a clean pot with the dang gui, red dates, ginger and 1.5 L water; bring to a boil and skim.
  3. Cover and simmer gently for 50 minutes.
  4. Add the rice wine and goji and simmer 5 minutes.
  5. Season with the salt and serve.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard makes the soup cloudy and bitter.
  • Too much dang gui makes the broth medicinal; use measured amounts.

Authentic tips

  • Avoid in pregnancy without advice from your doctor.
  • Serve with rice or noodles.

Substitutions

  • Skip the dang gui if you dislike the medicinal taste and use more ginger.
  • Chicken drumsticks only shorten the simmer by 10 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; reheat gently.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sesame oil noodles
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Warm ginger tea
