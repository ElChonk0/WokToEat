Sayur Lodeh (Vegetable Coconut Stew)

▾

Surprise me with

Sayur lodeh, vegetables in a mild coconut milk soup, is a Javanese everyday dish, often eaten with lontong rice cakes.

Method

  1. Blend the shallots, garlic, candlenuts, chilies, coriander and turmeric into a paste.
  2. Heat the oil in a pot over medium heat and fry the spice paste with the galangal, lemongrass and bay leaves, stirring often, for 5 to 6 minutes, until it darkens, smells cooked rather than raw and the oil starts to separate. Under-fried paste tastes sharp and grassy.
  3. Add the water and bring to a boil, then add the jackfruit or chayote and the tempeh and simmer for 8 to 10 minutes until almost tender.
  4. Add the long beans, eggplant and cabbage, then pour in the coconut milk and season with salt and palm sugar. Simmer gently, stirring often so the coconut milk does not split, for 5 to 8 minutes until all the vegetables are tender.
  5. Serve with rice; this is the vegan version of a Javanese staple that is often made with shrimp paste.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard splits the coconut milk.
  • Overcooked vegetables go mushy.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with lontong.
  • A mild dish; add chilies if you like.

Substitutions

  • Any seasonal vegetables replace the list.
  • Tofu adds protein.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or lontong
  • Soup: Not needed; the stew is its own soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Es teh
