Sayur Lodeh (Vegetable Coconut Stew)

▾

Surprise me with

Sayur lodeh, vegetables in a mild coconut milk soup, is a Javanese everyday dish, often eaten with lontong rice cakes.

  • Cook 25 min

  • 200 g7 ozLong beans and cabbage
  • 150 g5 ozTempeh, cubed
  • 50 g3 tbspShallot and garlic
  • 30 g1 ozTurmeric, galangal, lemongrass
  • 22Bay leaf
  • 10 g2 tspSalt and palm sugar

Method

  1. Fry the shallot, garlic, turmeric, galangal and lemongrass paste in a little oil for 3 minutes.
  2. Add the coconut milk, 400 ml water and the bay leaves; bring to a gentle simmer.
  3. Add the jackfruit or squash and long beans and cook 10 minutes.
  4. Add the cabbage and tempeh and cook 5 minutes.
  5. Season with salt and palm sugar.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard splits the coconut milk.
  • Overcooked vegetables go mushy.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with lontong.
  • A mild dish; add chilies if you like.

Substitutions

  • Any seasonal vegetables replace the list.
  • Tofu adds protein.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or lontong
  • Soup: Not needed; the stew is its own soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Es teh
