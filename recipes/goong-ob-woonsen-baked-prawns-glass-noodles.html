Goong Ob Woonsen (Baked Prawns with Glass Noodles)

Goong ob woonsen, prawns baked on glass noodles with ginger and pepper, is a Chinese-Thai restaurant dish, often cooked in a clay pot.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 600 g1.3 lbLarge prawns, shell on
  • 100 g3.5 ozDried glass noodles
  • 40 g1.5 ozGinger
  • 40 g1.5 ozGarlic
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 30 ml2 tbspOyster sauce
  • 2 g3/4 tspPepper
  • 30 g1 ozScallion
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Soak the glass noodles in warm water for 10 minutes and drain.
  2. Place the noodles and sliced ginger in a Dutch oven or lidded casserole.
  3. Mix the soy sauce, oyster sauce, oil, garlic and pepper with 4 tablespoons water.
  4. Arrange the prawns on the noodles and pour the sauce over.
  5. Cover and bake at 200 C (400 F) for 15 minutes, or steam, until the prawns are pink; top with scallion.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking makes the prawns rubbery.
  • Dry noodles absorb all the sauce; add enough liquid.

Authentic tips

  • Serve in the pot.
  • Add cilantro root to the sauce for authentic flavor.

Substitutions

  • Large shrimp replace prawns.
  • A Dutch oven replaces the clay pot on any stove, induction included.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Jasmine rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
