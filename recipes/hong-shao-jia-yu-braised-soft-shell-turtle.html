Hóng Shāo Jiǎ Yú (Braised Soft-Shell Turtle) 红烧甲鱼

▾

Surprise me with

Soft-shelled turtle is a traditional banquet and tonic dish in Anhui and the Yangtze region, braised until the gelatinous skirt turns tender.

  • Prep 30 min
  • Cook 1.5 hr

Method

  1. Blanch the turtle pieces in water at about 80 C (175 F) for 1 to 2 minutes, then rub off the thin, dark outer skin from the shell and legs. Blanch again in boiling water with ginger and green onion for 3 minutes and rinse.
  2. Fry the pork belly in the oil in a Dutch oven until it renders, then the ginger and garlic for 1 minute.
  3. Add the rock sugar and let it caramelize, then the turtle, and turn it until glossy.
  4. Add the Shaoxing wine, soy sauces and hot water, cover and simmer over low heat for 1 hour, until the meat and the gelatinous edge (qun bian) are soft.
  5. Uncover and reduce until the sauce is thick and glossy. Scatter with green onion; a Huizhou banquet dish.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the blanch leaves a muddy smell.
  • Boiling hard makes the meat stringy.

Authentic tips

  • Buy from a reliable source and have it cleaned.
  • Chicken thighs can substitute for a milder version.

Substitutions

  • Chicken thighs replace the turtle for a milder version.
  • Buy from a reliable source and have it cleaned.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat gently.

Freezing: Not recommended.

  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
