Hóng Shāo Jiǎ Yú (Braised Soft-Shell Turtle) 红烧甲鱼

▾

Surprise me with

Soft-shelled turtle is a traditional banquet and tonic dish in Anhui and the Yangtze region, braised until the gelatinous skirt turns tender.

  • Prep 30 min
  • Cook 60 min
  • Difficulty Hard

  • 60 ml4 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 15 g1 tbspSugar
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Blanch the turtle pieces in boiling water for 2 minutes; drain, scrape off any skin and rinse.
  2. Heat the oil in a heavy pot and fry the pork, ginger and garlic 3 minutes.
  3. Add the turtle, wine, both soy sauces, sugar and 600 ml water and bring to a boil.
  4. Cover and simmer on low 50 minutes until the meat and gelatinous skirt are tender.
  5. Uncover and reduce the sauce 5 minutes.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the blanch leaves a muddy smell.
  • Boiling hard makes the meat stringy.

Authentic tips

  • Buy from a reliable source and have it cleaned.
  • Chicken thighs can substitute for a milder version.

Substitutions

  • Chicken thighs replace the turtle for a milder version.
  • Buy from a reliable source and have it cleaned.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat gently.

Freezing: Not recommended.
