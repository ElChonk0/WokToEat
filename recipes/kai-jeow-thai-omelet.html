Kai Jeow (Thai Crispy Omelet)

▾

Surprise me with

Kai jeow, a crisp, puffy omelet fried in plenty of hot oil, is Thailand's comfort food and a standard breakfast over rice.

Method

  1. Beat the eggs hard with the soy sauce, lime juice, cornstarch and water for about a minute until frothy. The acid and starch help the omelet puff and crisp. Stir in the green onion.
  2. Heat the oil in a flat-bottom wok over high heat until it just begins to smoke.
  3. Hold the bowl about 30 cm (1 ft) above the wok and pour in half the egg in a stream. It will puff and bubble instantly. Pushing the edges toward the center, fry for 1 minute until the underside is golden.
  4. Flip and fry for 30 seconds more, until golden, crisp at the edges and still soft in the center.
  5. Drain on paper towels and repeat. Serve over hot rice with Sriracha, a favorite quick meal across Thailand.

Common mistakes

  • Not hot enough oil makes a flat, greasy omelet.
  • Overcooking makes it dry.

Authentic tips

Substitutions

  • Add minced pork for a classic version.
  • Fry in a wok with plenty of oil for puffiness.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; reheated omelet loses its puff.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Jasmine rice and sriracha
  • Soup: Clear soup
