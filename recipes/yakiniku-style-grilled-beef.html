Yakiniku-Style Grilled Beef 焼肉

▾

Surprise me with

Yakiniku ("grilled meat") describes Japan's tableside grilling tradition, influenced by Korean barbecue, where thinly sliced beef is marinated or seasoned simply and grilled quickly over high heat.

Method

  1. Make the yakiniku sauce: simmer the soy sauce, mirin, sake and sugar for 1 minute, take it off the heat and stir in the pear, garlic, ginger, sesame seeds and sesame oil. Cool.
  2. Toss half the sauce with the beef and leave for 15 to 30 minutes. The grated pear tenderizes and sweetens the meat; keep the rest of the sauce for dipping.
  3. Heat a tabletop grill, cast-iron grill pan or skillet over high heat until very hot and oil it lightly.
  4. Grill the beef a few slices at a time for 30 to 60 seconds per side, until browned at the edges and just cooked. Crowding the pan steams the meat instead of searing it.
  5. Dip each slice in the reserved sauce and eat with rice, lettuce and green onion.

Common mistakes

  • Overcrowding the grill or pan, which steams the beef instead of achieving a proper sear.
  • Overcooking the thin slices, which toughens them quickly — they need only seconds per side.

Authentic tips

  • Very high heat and extremely brief cooking times are essential; yakiniku beef should be seared, not cooked through slowly.
  • This dish is traditionally cooked and eaten in batches at the table, keeping each portion fresh off the grill.

Substitutions

  • Pork belly is another popular protein for yakiniku-style grilling.
  • No grill: spread the meat in a single layer on a foil-lined sheet pan and broil on the top rack for 1 to 2 minutes, flip, and cook 1 minute more until charred at the edges; work in two batches so it browns instead of steaming. A very hot cast-iron pan also works.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat gently to avoid toughening.

Freezing: Freezes well, marinated and raw, for up to a month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice, lettuce wraps and kimchi
  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
