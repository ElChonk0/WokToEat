Butter Chicken Thigh (Murgh Makhani)

▾

Surprise me with

Invented in 1950s Delhi at the Moti Mahal restaurant as a way to use leftover tandoori chicken in a rich tomato-butter sauce, butter chicken has since become one of the most popular Indian dishes worldwide.

Method

  1. Mix the yogurt, marinade ginger-garlic paste, chili, garam masala, lemon juice and salt and coat the chicken. Marinate for at least 1 hour.
  2. Grill or broil the chicken on a rack 10 cm (4 in) from the heat for 10 to 12 minutes, turning once, until charred at the edges. Or sear it in the oil in a hot cast-iron skillet. The char stands in for the tandoor.
  3. Simmer the tomatoes, onion, sauce ginger-garlic paste and cashews with 120 ml (1/2 cup) water for 15 minutes until soft, then blend until completely smooth and strain for a silky sauce.
  4. Melt the butter in a pan over medium heat, add the chili and fry for 10 seconds, then pour in the tomato puree. Simmer for 10 minutes, stirring, until it thickens and darkens.
  5. Add the chicken, garam masala, salt and sugar and simmer for 5 minutes. Crush the kasuri methi between your palms over the pot and stir in the cream.
  6. Simmer for 2 minutes more, top with a knob of butter and a swirl of cream, and serve with naan. Butter chicken was created at Moti Mahal in Delhi.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the marination, which is what gives the chicken its characteristic tenderness and background flavor.
  • Adding the cream too early and letting it boil hard, which can cause it to split.

Authentic tips

  • Blending the tomato sauce until silky-smooth is what gives restaurant-style butter chicken its signature texture.
  • Kashmiri chili powder gives vibrant color without excessive heat — worth seeking out over regular chili powder.

Substitutions

  • Coconut cream replaces heavy cream for a dairy-light version.
  • Tandoori-style chicken can be roasted in an oven at 230 C (450 F).

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; thin with a splash of water when reheating.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months; add the cream fresh after thawing.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Garlic naan and basmati rice
  • Soup: Cucumber raita
  • Dessert: Gulab jamun
  • Beverage: Mango lassi
