Kè Jiā Jiǔ Niàng Jī (Hakka Wine Chicken) 客家酒酿鸡

Hakka communities of western Fujian cook chicken with glutinous rice wine, a warming dish served to new mothers and at winter gatherings.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 35 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Fujian Chicken LunchDinnerBraise / stew

  • 900 g2 lbChicken thighs, chopped
  • 250 ml1 cupGlutinous rice wine
  • 60 g4 tbspGinger
  • 30 ml2 tbspSesame oil
  • 10 g1 tbspGoji berries
  • 6 g1 tspSalt
  • 200 ml3/4 cupWater

Method

  1. Heat the sesame oil in a heavy pot over medium heat and fry the ginger slices for 2 minutes until golden and fragrant.
  2. Add the chicken and brown for 6-8 minutes, turning, until the skin is golden.
  3. Pour in the glutinous rice wine and water, add the goji berries and bring to a boil.
  4. Cover, reduce to a gentle simmer and cook 25 minutes until the chicken is tender.
  5. Season with the salt and serve hot in bowls with the broth.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling the wine fiercely drives off the flavor and leaves a harsh alcohol note; simmer gently.
  • Too much sesame oil at high heat turns bitter; keep the heat medium.

Authentic tips

  • Use sweet glutinous rice wine (jiu niang) for the traditional flavor; Shaoxing wine with a spoonful of sugar is a substitute.
  • Traditionally served to new mothers.

Substitutions

  • Shaoxing wine plus a spoonful of sugar replaces glutinous rice wine.
  • Bone-in drumsticks add flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat gently.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Warm ginger tea
