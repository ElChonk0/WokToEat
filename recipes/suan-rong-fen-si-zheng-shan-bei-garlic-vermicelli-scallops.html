Suàn Róng Fěn Sī Zhēng Shàn Bèi (Garlic Vermicelli Steamed Scallops) 蒜蓉粉丝蒸扇贝

▾

Scallops steamed on the half shell over glass noodles and garlic are a Yantai and Qingdao seafood-restaurant staple.

  • Cook 10 min

  • 1212Scallops on the half shell
  • 100 g3.5 ozGlass noodles, soaked
  • 50 g4 tbspGarlic, minced
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 30 g2 tbspScallions
  • 60 ml4 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Soak the glass noodles in warm water for 15 minutes; drain and cut into 10 cm pieces.
  2. Place a small nest of noodles in each scallop shell and set a scallop on top.
  3. Fry the garlic in the oil over medium heat for 1-2 minutes until pale gold; stir in the soy sauce and wine and take off the heat.
  4. Spoon the garlic mixture over the scallops.
  5. Steam over high heat for 6-8 minutes until the scallops are opaque; top with scallions.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the scallops makes them rubbery.
  • Burning the garlic makes it bitter.

Authentic tips

  • Use the freshest scallops you can find.
  • Serve straight from the steamer.

Substitutions

  • Large shrimp on shells replace scallops.
  • Mussels also work with the same topping.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; eat within the hour.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
