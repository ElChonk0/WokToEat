Canh Chua Cá (Mekong Sour Fish Soup)

Canh chua is the signature sour soup of Vietnam's Mekong Delta, balancing tamarind, pineapple, tomato and herbs in a clear fish broth.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 120 g4 ozPineapple
  • 150 g5 ozTomatoes
  • 100 g3.5 ozBac ha (taro stem)
  • 80 g3 ozBean sprouts
  • 30 ml2 tbspFish sauce
  • 20 g2/3 ozRice paddy herb and cilantro

Method

  1. Simmer the stock with the tamarind for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the pineapple and tomato and cook 3 minutes.
  3. Add the fish steaks and cook gently 6 minutes.
  4. Add the bac ha and sprouts and cook 1 minute.
  5. Season with the fish sauce and top with herbs.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard breaks the fish.
  • Overcooking the sprouts makes them limp.

Authentic tips

  • Balance sweet, sour and salty.
  • Rau om (rice paddy herb) is the authentic herb.

Substitutions

  • Shrimp replaces fish.
  • Pineapple and tomato both go in.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
