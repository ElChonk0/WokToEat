Canh Chua Cá (Mekong Sour Fish Soup)

▾

Surprise me with

Canh chua is the signature sour soup of Vietnam's Mekong Delta, balancing tamarind, pineapple, tomato and herbs in a clear fish broth.

Method

  1. Marinate the fish with the fish sauce for 15 minutes.
  2. Fry the garlic in the oil until golden and crisp; keep it aside for the garnish.
  3. Bring the water to a boil, add the tamarind liquid, pineapple and sugar and simmer for 5 minutes. Season with fish sauce; the broth should be sour, sweet and salty all at once.
  4. Add the fish and simmer gently for 6 to 8 minutes until cooked. Add the tomatoes, bac ha and okra for 2 minutes.
  5. Turn off the heat and add the bean sprouts, herbs, chili and fried garlic with its oil. Serve with rice and ca kho to, the classic southern pairing.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard breaks the fish.
  • Overcooking the sprouts makes them limp.

Authentic tips

  • Balance sweet, sour and salty.
  • Rau om (rice paddy herb) is the authentic herb.

Substitutions

  • Shrimp replaces fish.
  • Pineapple and tomato both go in.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
