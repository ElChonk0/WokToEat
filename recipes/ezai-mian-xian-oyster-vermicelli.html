O-a-mi-sua (Oyster Vermicelli) 蚵仔麵線

Oyster vermicelli, a thick bonito-scented soup of wheat vermicelli with oysters, is a Taiwanese street food often eaten at breakfast.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 200 g7 ozFine wheat vermicelli
  • 250 g9 ozShucked oysters
  • 1.2 L5 cupsDried bonito stock
  • 60 ml4 tbspSweet potato starch slurry
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 15 ml1 tbspBlack vinegar
  • 15 g1/2 ozCilantro
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce

Method

  1. Bring the stock to a simmer and add the vermicelli; cook 8-10 minutes until soft.
  2. Stir in the slurry and simmer 1 minute until thick.
  3. Add the oysters and cook 2 minutes until plump.
  4. Season with the soy sauce.
  5. Ladle into bowls and top with garlic, vinegar and cilantro.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooked oysters turn rubbery.
  • Adding the slurry too fast makes lumps.

Authentic tips

  • Thick, starchy soup is the goal.
  • Add pork intestines for the traditional version.

Substitutions

  • Cooked pork intestine is traditional.
  • Dried bonito stock can be replaced with chicken stock.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the vermicelli thickens as it sits, so add water when reheating.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Fried dough sticks
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Winter melon tea
