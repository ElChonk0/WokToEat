Kkanpunggi (Spicy Garlic Fried Chicken) 깐풍기

▾

Surprise me with

Kkanpunggi, fried chicken tossed in a dry, sweet-sour garlic and chili sauce, is a Korean-Chinese classic adapted from the Chinese ganpengji.

Method

  1. Season the chicken with salt, pepper and ginger, mix in the egg and coat with potato starch.
  2. Fry in oil at 170 C (340 F) for 5 to 6 minutes, rest, then refry at 185 C (365 F) for 2 minutes until very crisp.
  3. Mix the soy sauce, vinegar, sugar, oyster sauce and water.
  4. In a flat-bottom wok with a tablespoon of oil, fry the garlic, chilies and green onion over medium heat for 1 minute.
  5. Pour in the sauce, bubble 30 seconds until slightly reduced, add the chicken and toss over high heat until the sauce is absorbed. Serve at once.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Too much sauce makes it soggy; the sauce should coat and mostly evaporate.
  • Burnt garlic makes it bitter.

Authentic tips

  • The name refers to the dry-fried style, so the finished dish should look glossy but not saucy.
  • Shrimp make kkanpung-saeu.

Substitutions

  • Air fryer: cook the coated chicken at 200 C (400 F) for 18 minutes.
  • Leave out dried chilies for less heat.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days; re-crisp in a hot oven.

Freezing: Not recommended.
