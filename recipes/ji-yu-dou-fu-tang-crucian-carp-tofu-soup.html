Jì Yú Dòu Fu Tāng (Crucian Carp and Tofu Soup) 鲫鱼豆腐汤

▾

Surprise me with

Crucian carp simmered with tofu into a creamy white soup is a home remedy and everyday dish across Anhui and the Yangtze.

  • Cook 30 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 600 g1.3 lbCrucian carp, cleaned
  • 300 g10 ozTofu
  • 30 g2 tbspGinger
  • 30 g2 tbspScallion
  • 1 L4 cupsWater
  • 6 g1 tspSalt
  • 1 g1/4 tspWhite pepper
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Pat the carp dry and score the sides.
  2. Heat the oil in a pot over medium-high heat and fry the fish 2 minutes per side until golden.
  3. Add the ginger and 1 L boiling water and simmer hard for 20 minutes until the broth is milky.
  4. Add the tofu, salt and pepper and simmer 5 minutes.
  5. Garnish with scallion.

Common mistakes

  • Using cold water prevents the milky color.
  • Not frying the fish gives a pale, fishy broth.

Authentic tips

  • A hard simmer makes the broth milky.
  • Crucian carp has many fine bones; strain the soup if serving children.

Substitutions

  • Tilapia replaces crucian carp.
  • Strain the soup if serving children.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freeze the broth only.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
