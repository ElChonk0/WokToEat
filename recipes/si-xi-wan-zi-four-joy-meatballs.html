Sì Xǐ Wán Zi (Four Joy Meatballs) 四喜丸子

Si xi wan zi, four large braised meatballs symbolizing good fortune, joy, wealth and longevity, are a Beijing banquet dish eaten at New Year and weddings.

  • Prep 30 min
  • Cook 40 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 300 g10 ozNapa cabbage

Method

  1. Mix the pork with the egg, cornstarch, water chestnuts, half the soy sauce and the ginger and scallion; stir in one direction until sticky and chill 20 minutes.
  2. Divide into four large balls and roll smooth in wet hands.
  3. Pan-fry the meatballs in a little oil over medium heat for 5-6 minutes, turning, until browned all over.
  4. Add the remaining soy sauce, the dark soy, sugar and 400 ml water; cover and simmer 25 minutes.
  5. Add the cabbage around the meatballs for the last 10 minutes, then uncover and reduce the sauce until glossy.

Common mistakes

  • Overworking the pork makes the meatballs dense.
  • Boiling hard breaks them up.

Authentic tips

  • Chilling the mixture makes shaping easier.
  • Serve over rice with the cabbage and sauce.

Substitutions

  • Ground chicken thigh replaces some of the pork for a lighter version.
  • Napa cabbage can be replaced by bok choy.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; reheat in the sauce.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months; thaw overnight and reheat gently.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
