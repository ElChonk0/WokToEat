Goan Beef Cutlets

▾

Surprise me with

Cutlets, minced meat with potato and spices, coated in breadcrumbs and fried, are a Goan Catholic snack and tea-time favorite shaped by Portuguese influence.

Method

  1. Cook the beef with the ginger-garlic paste, turmeric, chili, half the garam masala, vinegar, salt and 60 ml (1/4 cup) water in a covered pan for 15 minutes, then uncover and cook until completely dry. Dry filling keeps the cutlets from falling apart.
  2. Cool, then mix with the mashed potato, onion, green chilies, cilantro, mint and the remaining garam masala.
  3. Shape into 12 oval patties about 1.5 cm (1/2 in) thick. Chill for 30 minutes to firm.
  4. Dip each in beaten egg, then press into the breadcrumbs.
  5. Shallow-fry in the oil over medium heat for 3 minutes per side until deep golden and crisp. Serve with onion salad and ketchup; a staple of Goan Christmas and parties.

Common mistakes

  • Wet mixture falls apart; cook the beef dry.
  • Oil that is too hot browns before heating through.

Authentic tips

  • Chill the shaped cutlets 30 minutes.
  • Serve with ketchup and green chutney.

Substitutions

  • Ground chicken or lamb replace beef.
  • Bake at 220 C (425 F) for a lighter version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot oven.

Freezing: Freeze cooked cutlets for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Green chutney and bread
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Masala chai
