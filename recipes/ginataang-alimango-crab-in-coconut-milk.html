Ginataang Alimango (Crab in Coconut Milk)

▾

Surprise me with

Ginataan, dishes stewed in coconut milk, are a staple of Bicol and the Visayas; mud crab in coconut milk with chilies is a coastal celebration dish.

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat and saute the garlic, onion and ginger for 3 minutes.
  2. Pour in the coconut milk and bring to a gentle simmer, then add the squash and cook for 8 to 10 minutes until it starts to soften and thicken the sauce.
  3. Add the crabs, cover and cook for 8 to 10 minutes, turning once, until the shells turn bright orange.
  4. Add the long beans, chilies, coconut cream and fish sauce and simmer uncovered for 5 minutes, until the sauce is rich and slightly oily.
  5. Stir in the moringa leaves until wilted and serve with rice. Expect to eat with your hands.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard splits the coconut milk.
  • Overcooked crab is dry.

Authentic tips

  • Crack the claws first.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • Shrimp replace crab.
  • Chilies can be omitted for a mild dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Coconut water
