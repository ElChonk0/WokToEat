Gambas (Filipino Garlic Shrimp)

▾

Surprise me with

Gambas, shrimp in garlic and chili oil, arrived with Spanish influence and is a Filipino bar favorite.

  • Cook 8 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 600 g1.3 lbShrimp
  • 60 g4 tbspGarlic
  • 90 ml6 tbspOlive oil
  • 15 g1/2 ozChilies
  • 3 g1 tspPaprika
  • 30 ml2 tbspLemon juice
  • 5 g1 tspSalt
  • 15 g1/2 ozParsley

Method

  1. Heat the olive oil with the sliced garlic and chilies in a pan over medium heat for 2 minutes until the garlic is pale gold.
  2. Add the shrimp and paprika.
  3. Cook 3 minutes, turning once, until the shrimp are pink.
  4. Season with the salt and lemon juice.
  5. Sprinkle with parsley and serve sizzling with bread.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the garlic makes it bitter.
  • Overcooking makes the shrimp rubbery.

Authentic tips

  • Serve in a clay dish at the table.
  • Dip in the oil with bread.

Substitutions

  • Mushrooms replace shrimp for a vegetarian version.
  • Serve with crusty bread.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Garlic bread or rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
