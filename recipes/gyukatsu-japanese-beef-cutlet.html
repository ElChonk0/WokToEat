Gyukatsu (Japanese Beef Cutlet) 牛カツ

▾

Surprise me with

Gyukatsu is a Japanese breaded beef cutlet fried very briefly so the center stays rare, served with a hot stone or sauce to finish cooking each slice at the table.

Method

  1. Let the beef sit at room temperature for 20 minutes, then season it with the salt and pepper.
  2. Dredge in the flour, dip in the egg, and press firmly into the panko so the crust covers every side.
  3. Heat the oil to 180 C (350 F) in a heavy pot.
  4. Fry the cutlets for 1 minute per side until the crust is golden but the center is rare, then rest on a rack for 2 minutes.
  5. Slice into 1.5 cm pieces and serve with the daikon, ponzu, wasabi, cabbage and rice, cooking each slice briefly on a hot pan or stone if you want it more done.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Frying longer than 2 minutes in total cooks the beef through and defeats the dish.
  • Oil that is too cool makes the crust greasy and soft.

Authentic tips

  • Use a good-quality, well-marbled cut since it is eaten rare.
  • Chill the breaded cutlets for 10 minutes so the crust sticks.

Substitutions

  • Pork loin replaces beef, but cook it fully, 4 minutes per side.
  • Soy sauce and sea salt replace ponzu.

Storage

Leftovers: Best eaten at once; do not store.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Shredded cabbage and miso soup
  • Soup: Miso soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold barley tea
