Yakitori (Grilled Chicken Skewers) 焼き鳥

▾

Surprise me with

Yakitori, bite-size chicken grilled on skewers, became popular in Japan during the Meiji era and is now found at izakaya across the country.

Method

  1. Simmer the soy sauce, mirin, sake and sugar in a small pan for 8 minutes until syrupy; this is the tare.
  2. Cut the thighs into 3 cm pieces and the scallions into 3 cm lengths.
  3. Thread the chicken onto skewers alternating with scallion, 3-4 pieces each; soak bamboo skewers 20 minutes first.
  4. Grill over medium-high heat (or under a hot broiler) for 8-10 minutes, turning and brushing with tare every 2 minutes, until glossy and cooked through.
  5. Serve with shichimi pepper.

Common mistakes

  • Brushing with tare too early burns the sugar before the chicken cooks.
  • Using chicken breast gives dry skewers; thigh stays juicy.

Authentic tips

  • Leave the skin on for charred, crisp edges.
  • Binchotan charcoal gives the real izakaya flavor.

Substitutions

  • Thigh stays juicier than breast; add a little chicken skin for authentic flavor.
  • A broiler on its highest setting replaces charcoal.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot pan or oven.

Freezing: Freeze raw skewered chicken for 2 months; grill from thawed.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and pickles
  • Soup: Miso soup
  • Dessert: Matcha ice cream
  • Beverage: Cold beer or sencha
