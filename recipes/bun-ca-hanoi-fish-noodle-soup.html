Bún Cá (Hanoi Fish Noodle Soup)

▾

Surprise me with

Bun ca, vermicelli in a clear dill and turmeric fish broth with fried fish, is a classic Hanoi breakfast.

  • 5 g2 tspGround turmeric

  • 400 g14 ozRice vermicelli

  • 100 g3.5 ozWater spinach or celery, blanched

Method

  1. Marinate the fish with the turmeric, galangal and fish sauce for 30 minutes.
  2. Fry the fish in the oil over medium-high heat for 3 to 4 minutes per side until golden and crisp. Drain.
  3. In a pot, fry the shallots in a spoonful of the frying oil, then the tomatoes for 3 minutes until soft. Add the stock and simmer for 10 minutes.
  4. Season with fish sauce, vinegar and sugar so the broth is clear, gently sour and savory. Stir in a little shrimp paste if you like the traditional Hanoi funk.
  5. Divide the vermicelli and greens among bowls, add the fried fish and plenty of dill and green onion, and ladle over the boiling broth.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the fish breaks it.
  • Undissolved shrimp paste is gritty.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with extra dill and chilies.
  • A Hanoi breakfast favorite.

Substitutions

  • Cod replaces the firm fish.
  • Crab paste adds richness.

Storage

Leftovers: Store noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 2 days.

Freezing: Freeze the broth for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Herbs and shrimp paste
  • Soup: Not needed; the soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
