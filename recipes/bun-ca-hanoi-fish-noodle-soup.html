Bún Cá (Hanoi Fish Noodle Soup)

Bun ca, vermicelli in a clear dill and turmeric fish broth with fried fish, is a classic Hanoi breakfast.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 25 min
  • Difficulty Medium
  • Draft Vietnam Seafood BreakfastLunchDinnerSoupNoodlesShellfish

  • 500 g1.1 lbFirm fish, fried
  • 400 g14 ozRice vermicelli
  • 200 g7 ozTomatoes
  • 100 g3.5 ozDill and scallion
  • 1.5 L6 cupsFish stock
  • 5 g2 tspTurmeric
  • 30 ml2 tbspFish sauce
  • 15 g1 tbspShrimp paste

Method

  1. Marinate the fish with turmeric and a little fish sauce and fry in a little oil 3 minutes per side.
  2. Simmer the stock with the tomatoes for 10 minutes.
  3. Season with the fish sauce and dissolved shrimp paste.
  4. Cook the vermicelli, drain and divide among bowls.
  5. Add the fish and dill and ladle on the broth.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the fish breaks it.
  • Undissolved shrimp paste is gritty.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with extra dill and chilies.
  • A Hanoi breakfast favorite.

Substitutions

  • Cod replaces the firm fish.
  • Crab paste adds richness.

Storage

Leftovers: Store noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 2 days.

Freezing: Freeze the broth for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Herbs and shrimp paste
  • Soup: Not needed; the soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
