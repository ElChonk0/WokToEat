Zhī Má Jī (Sesame Chicken) 芝麻鸡

▾

Surprise me with

Sesame-crusted chicken, deep-fried and served with a sweet-savory sauce, is a staple of northern banquet and Chinese-American restaurant menus.

Method

  1. Toss the chicken with the marinade soy sauce, Shaoxing wine and egg, then with the cornstarch until coated in a thick batter.
  2. Heat the oil to 170 C (340 F) in a Dutch oven and fry the chicken in batches for 4 to 5 minutes until cooked. Raise the oil to 190 C (375 F) and fry again for 1 minute until crisp. Drain.
  3. Simmer the sauce soy sauce, honey, sugar, vinegar, water and garlic in a flat-bottom wok for 1 to 2 minutes until it thickens into a syrup that coats a spoon.
  4. Turn off the heat, add the chicken and toss quickly until glazed.
  5. Scatter with the sesame seeds and drizzle with sesame oil. Serve at once, while the coating is still crisp.

Common mistakes

  • Oil that is too hot burns the sesame before the chicken is cooked; keep it at medium.
  • Saucing too early softens the coating.

Authentic tips

  • Black and white sesame together look striking.
  • Bake at 200 C (400 F) for 20 minutes for a lighter version.

Substitutions

  • Boneless thigh strips work; cook 2 minutes less.
  • Baking at 220 C (425 F) on a rack replaces frying.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day and recrisps in a hot oven.

Freezing: Not recommended.
