Duo Jiao Yu Tou (Steamed Fish Head with Chopped Chilies) 剁椒鱼头

Duo jiao yu tou, a fish head blanketed in salted chopped red chilies and steamed, is Hunan's best-known dish; it is credited to the Qing-era Hunan scholar Huang Zongxian and is now served in Hunan restaurants worldwide.

Method

  1. Rub the fish head with wine and a pinch of salt and lay it on ginger slices on a plate.
  2. Cover the fish with the chopped chilies, garlic, minced ginger and black beans.
  3. Steam over high heat for 15 minutes until the flesh near the bone is opaque.
  4. Top with scallions and pour smoking-hot oil over the fish.

Common mistakes

  • Oversteaming, which dries out the cheek meat.
  • Adding extra salt; the chopped chilies are already salty.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with rice or the traditional hua juan steamed rolls to soak up the sauce.
  • Use fresh, bright red chilies for the best color.

Substitutions

  • A large whole fish or thick cod fillets replace the head.
  • Add fresh chilies for more heat.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated; the fish firms up when cold.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or plain noodles to finish the sauce
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
