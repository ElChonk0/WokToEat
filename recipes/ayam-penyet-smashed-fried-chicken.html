Ayam Penyet (Smashed Fried Chicken with Sambal)

▾

Surprise me with

Ayam penyet, fried chicken smashed with a pestle and served with sambal, comes from East Java and is a staple of Indonesian warung.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 30 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 700 g1.5 lbChicken thighs, bone-in
  • 8 g3 tspTurmeric and coriander
  • 40 g1.5 ozGarlic and shallot
  • 90 g3 ozSambal
  • 150 g5 ozTempeh and tofu, fried
  • 8 g1.5 tspSalt
  • 400 ml1.75 cupsNeutral oil for frying
  • 400 ml1.75 cupsWater

Method

  1. Simmer the chicken in the water with the turmeric, coriander, garlic, shallot and salt for 20 minutes until cooked; drain and pat dry.
  2. Heat the oil to 175 C (350 F) and fry the chicken 5-6 minutes, turning, until golden and crisp.
  3. Fry the tempeh and tofu until golden.
  4. Place the chicken on a board and smash each piece lightly with a pestle or the flat of a cleaver until the meat splits.
  5. Serve with the sambal, fried tempeh and tofu, rice and fresh cucumber.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the pat-dry spatters hot oil.
  • Smashing too hard shreds the meat.

Authentic tips

  • The smashing helps the sambal cling.
  • Serve with raw cabbage and basil.

Substitutions

  • Boneless thighs cook in less time.
  • Air-fry at 200 C (400 F) after poaching for a lighter dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; recrisp in a hot oven.

Freezing: Freezes the poached chicken for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice, raw cabbage, cucumber and fried tempeh
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Es teh manis
