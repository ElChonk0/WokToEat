Zhū Ròu Dà Cōng Shuǐ Jiǎo (Pork and Scallion Dumplings) 猪肉大葱水饺

▾

Surprise me with

Large scallions are a Shandong specialty, and pork and scallion dumplings are the classic filling in the province, eaten at New Year.

  • Cook 8 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 20 g1 tbspGinger
  • 45 ml3 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspSesame oil
  • 5 g1 tspSalt

Method

  1. Mix the pork with the ginger, soy sauce, sesame oil and salt, stirring in one direction and adding 4 tablespoons of water gradually until sticky.
  2. Fold in the chopped scallions just before filling.
  3. Place a tablespoon of filling in each wrapper, fold and pleat.
  4. Boil in plenty of water for 6-7 minutes until they float and the filling is cooked.
  5. Serve with black vinegar and a little chili oil.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the scallions early makes the filling watery.
  • Overfilling splits the wrappers.

Authentic tips

  • Shandong's large scallions are sweet and mild; regular scallions work too.
  • Dumplings freeze well uncooked.

Substitutions

  • Ground chicken thigh replaces the pork.
  • Green onion tops replace large scallions.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; pan-fry leftovers.

Freezing: Freeze uncooked on a tray for 2 months; boil from frozen for 8 minutes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Black vinegar dip
  • Soup: Hot and sour soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
