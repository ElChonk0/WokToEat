Dǎi Kǎo Zhū Ròu (Dai Lemongrass Grilled Pork) 傣味烤猪肉

▾

Surprise me with

Dai cooks grill pork marinated with lemongrass, cilantro and chilies, a technique that mirrors the grilled pork of neighboring Laos and Thailand.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 700 g1.5 lbPork neck or shoulder, sliced
  • 60 g2 ozLemongrass
  • 50 g3 tbspGarlic and shallot
  • 20 g2/3 ozChilies
  • 30 g1 ozCilantro
  • 30 ml2 tbspFish sauce
  • 10 g2 tspSugar

Method

  1. Pound the lemongrass, garlic, shallot, chilies, fish sauce and sugar to a coarse paste.
  2. Toss with the sliced pork and marinate 2 hours in the refrigerator.
  3. Grill over high heat (or in a hot ridged pan) for 4-5 minutes per side until charred at the edges and cooked through.
  4. Rest 3 minutes and slice.
  5. Serve with cilantro and lime wedges.

Common mistakes

  • Marinating in too much liquid steams rather than chars.
  • Cooking on low heat dries the pork.

Authentic tips

  • Pork neck stays juiciest.
  • Serve with sticky rice and a chili dip.

Substitutions

  • Pork belly slices make a richer version.
  • A broiler replaces the charcoal grill.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot pan.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sticky rice and fresh herbs
  • Soup: Sour bamboo shoot soup
  • Dessert: Fresh pineapple
  • Beverage: Chilled tea
