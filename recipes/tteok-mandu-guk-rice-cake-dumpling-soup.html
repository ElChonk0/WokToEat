Tteok Mandu Guk (Rice Cake and Dumpling Soup) 떡만둣국

▾

Surprise me with

Tteokguk, sliced rice cake soup, is eaten on the morning of Lunar New Year, when finishing a bowl is said to add a year to your age; dumplings are added in many families, especially in the north.

Method

  1. Soak the rice cakes in cold water for 20 minutes if they are hard. Brown the beef with half the soup soy sauce in a large pot over medium heat for 2 to 3 minutes, then add the water, bring to a boil and skim. Cover and boil gently for 15 minutes to make a light beef broth.
  2. Meanwhile steam the dumplings on a damp cloth or parchment for 8 to 10 minutes, until cooked through. Steaming them separately keeps their starch out of the soup, so it stays clear, and keeps the wrappers chewy.
  3. Separate the eggs and fry the whites and yolks as thin sheets in a lightly oiled pan over medium-low heat without browning them. Cut them into thin strips.
  4. Add the garlic and drained rice cakes to the broth and boil for 5 to 8 minutes, until the rice cakes are soft and float.
  5. Add the dumplings, return to a boil and season with the rest of the soup soy sauce, salt and pepper.
  6. Ladle into bowls and top with the egg strips, green onion, seaweed and a few drops of sesame oil.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking the rice cakes too long makes them mushy and clouds the broth.
  • Using dark or regular soy sauce darkens the broth; soup soy sauce keeps it pale.

Authentic tips

  • Anchovy broth is a common quick alternative to beef broth.
  • Soak dried rice cake slices for 20 minutes so they cook evenly.

Substitutions

  • Skip the dumplings for plain tteokguk.
  • Light soy sauce plus extra salt replaces soup soy sauce.

Storage

Leftovers: Keep the broth separately for 3 days; cook rice cakes fresh.

Freezing: The broth and beef freeze for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Kimchi and japchae
  • Soup: This is the soup
  • Dessert: Yakgwa (honey cookies)
  • Beverage: Sikhye (rice punch)
