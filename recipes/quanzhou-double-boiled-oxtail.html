Quanzhou Herbal Oxtail Soup 炖牛尾

▾

Surprise me with

In Quanzhou’s beef houses, oxtail is double-boiled with mild Chinese herbs until the meat is gelatinous and the broth turns gently sweet: a restorative bowl eaten on its own or with rice.

Method

  1. Blanch the oxtail 8 minutes, drain, and rinse each piece clean.
  2. Put the oxtail, ginger, red dates, dang shen and wine in a heavy pot (or a heatproof bowl inside a steamer) with the water.
  3. Bring just to a boil, skim, then keep at the barest simmer, covered, for 2.5–3 hours.
  4. Add the goji berries for the last 10 minutes and season with salt only at the end.
  5. Skim the fat and serve in bowls with the broth.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard, which clouds the broth; double-boiled soups should stay clear.

Authentic tips

  • Make it a day ahead: chilled overnight, the fat sets on top and lifts off in one piece.

Substitutions

  • Beef shank with marrow bones can replace oxtail; reduce to 2 hours.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days; the broth sets to a jelly when cold.

Freezing: Freezes excellently for up to 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
