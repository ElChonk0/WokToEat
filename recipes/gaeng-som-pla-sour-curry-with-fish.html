Gaeng Som Pla (Southern Thai Sour Curry with Fish)

Gaeng som, a sour and spicy curry with no coconut milk, is a staple of Thai home cooking, especially in the south and central regions.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Medium
  • Draft Thailand Seafood LunchDinnerCurrySpicy

  • 600 g1.3 lbFish steaks
  • 60 g4 tbspSour curry paste
  • 45 g3 tbspTamarind paste
  • 30 ml2 tbspFish sauce
  • 15 g1 tbspPalm sugar
  • 300 g10 ozDaikon and long beans
  • 800 ml3.5 cupsWater
  • 15 g1/2 ozCilantro

Method

  1. Bring the water to a boil with the sour curry paste.
  2. Add the daikon and long beans and cook 8 minutes.
  3. Add the fish steaks and cook 5 minutes.
  4. Season with the tamarind, fish sauce and sugar.
  5. Garnish with cilantro and serve with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking makes the fish dry.
  • Adding the tamarind too early makes it bitter.

Authentic tips

  • Add cabbage or cauliflower.
  • Southern versions use turmeric and are fiery.

Substitutions

  • Shrimp replaces fish.
  • Cauliflower and cabbage replace daikon.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freeze the broth only.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Jasmine rice
  • Soup: Not needed; the curry is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
