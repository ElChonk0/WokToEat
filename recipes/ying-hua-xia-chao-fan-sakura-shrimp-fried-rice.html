Yīng Huā Xiā Chǎo Fàn (Sakura Shrimp Fried Rice) 櫻花蝦炒飯

▾

Surprise me with

Sakura shrimp, tiny pink shrimp caught off Donggang in southern Taiwan, are dried and toasted to flavor fried rice.

Method

  1. Break up the cold rice so the grains separate. Dry-toast the sakura shrimp in the wok over low heat for 1 to 2 minutes until fragrant and crisp, then lift them out. Toasting releases their sweet, briny aroma; they come from Donggang in southern Taiwan.
  2. Heat the oil in the wok over high heat, pour in the beaten eggs and stir for 10 seconds, then add the rice before the egg sets and toss to coat the grains.
  3. Stir-fry for 2 to 3 minutes, pressing out clumps, until the rice is hot and separate. Add the garlic and the scallion whites for 30 seconds.
  4. Pour the soy sauce around the edge of the wok so it sizzles, add most of the sakura shrimp and toss for 1 minute.
  5. Finish with the scallion greens and sesame oil, and scatter the remaining shrimp on top.

Common mistakes

  • Wet rice makes mushy fried rice; use cold, day-old rice.
  • Burning the shrimp makes them bitter.

Authentic tips

  • Sakura shrimp are available dried at Japanese and Taiwanese markets.
  • Use a hot wok.

Substitutions

  • Dried baby shrimp replace sakura shrimp.
  • Day-old rice is essential.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot pan.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Clear soup
  • Soup: Not needed; the rice is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Oolong tea
