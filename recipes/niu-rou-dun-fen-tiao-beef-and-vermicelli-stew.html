Niú Ròu Dùn Fěn Tiáo (Beef and Sweet Potato Noodle Stew) 牛肉炖粉条

▾

Beef stewed with chewy sweet potato noodles and cabbage is a warming winter dish of northern Anhui and the Huaihe plain.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 2.5 hr
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Blanch the beef in boiling water for 5 minutes, starting from cold water, then drain and rinse.
  2. Heat a tablespoon of oil in a Dutch oven or clay pot over medium heat and fry the ginger, green onion, garlic and star anise for 1 minute.
  3. Add the rock sugar and stir over low heat until it melts into an amber caramel, then turn the beef in it until glossy. Hui cooking is known for 'heavy oil, heavy color and attention to the fire'.
  4. Pour the Shaoxing wine around the edge of the pot, add the soy sauces and hot water to almost cover. Bring to a boil, then cover and simmer over low heat for 1.5 hours, until tender.
  5. Add the cabbage and simmer for 10 minutes, then the vermicelli for 10 minutes, until clear and swollen with the sauce.
  6. Uncover and reduce over medium-high heat for 5 to 10 minutes, basting, until the sauce is thick and glossy.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the noodles makes them mushy.
  • Not enough liquid: the noodles absorb a lot.

Authentic tips

  • Soak the noodles first.
  • Serve in the pot.

Substitutions

  • Glass noodles replace sweet potato vermicelli; add them last.
  • Chuck replaces brisket.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; the noodles absorb the broth.

Freezing: Freeze without the noodles for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; the stew is its own soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
