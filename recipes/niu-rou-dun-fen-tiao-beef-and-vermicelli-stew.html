Niú Ròu Dùn Fěn Tiáo (Beef and Sweet Potato Noodle Stew) 牛肉炖粉条

▾

Beef stewed with chewy sweet potato noodles and cabbage is a warming winter dish of northern Anhui and the Huaihe plain.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 90 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 700 g1.5 lbBeef brisket, cubed
  • 150 g5 ozSweet potato vermicelli, soaked
  • 250 g9 ozNapa cabbage
  • 45 ml3 tbspSoy sauce
  • 60 g4 tbspGinger, scallion, garlic
  • 22Star anise
  • 800 ml3.5 cupsWater

Method

  1. Soak the sweet potato vermicelli in warm water for 20 minutes. Cut the brisket into 4 cm cubes, blanch 5 minutes, drain and rinse.
  2. Brown the beef in a heavy pot with the ginger, scallion and garlic.
  3. Add the soy sauce, star anise and 800 ml water; bring to a boil, cover and simmer 60 minutes.
  4. Add the cabbage and vermicelli and cook 10 minutes until the noodles are soft and glossy.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the noodles makes them mushy.
  • Not enough liquid: the noodles absorb a lot.

Authentic tips

  • Soak the noodles first.
  • Serve in the pot.

Substitutions

  • Glass noodles replace sweet potato vermicelli; add them last.
  • Chuck replaces brisket.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; the noodles absorb the broth.

Freezing: Freeze without the noodles for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; the stew is its own soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
