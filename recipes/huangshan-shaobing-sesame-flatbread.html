Huángshān Shāobǐng (Sesame Flatbread) 黄山烧饼

▾

Surprise me with

Crisp sesame flatbread, baked on the walls of a clay oven or in a pan, is a traditional Anhui breakfast and snack served with congee.

  • Cook 20 min

  • 40 g1/3 cupSesame seeds
  • 15 ml1 tbspWater, for brushing

Method

  1. Mix the flour, yeast, salt and warm water into a soft dough, knead for 5 minutes and leave for 45 minutes until risen.
  2. Mix the oil and flour into a paste. Mix the chopped mustard greens with the green onion and sugar for the filling. Huangshan shaobing are called 'crab-shell' cakes for their crisp, layered shell.
  3. Divide the dough into 8 pieces, roll each into a thin oval, spread with oil paste, roll up and coil into a spiral. Flatten it, put a spoon of filling in the center, enclose it and flatten into a thin round.
  4. Brush the tops with water and press them into the sesame seeds.
  5. Bake on a tray at 220 C (425 F) for 15 to 20 minutes, or cook in a covered skillet over low heat for 5 to 6 minutes per side, until golden and crisp.

Common mistakes

  • Dough that is too dry bakes hard.
  • Not brushing with water means the sesame seeds fall off.

Authentic tips

  • Let the shaped dough rest 10 minutes before baking.
  • Serve with congee or soy milk.

Substitutions

  • Instant yeast works without proofing; use the same amount.
  • Cooking on a hot griddle replaces baking.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days at room temperature; reheat in a hot oven.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Congee or soy milk
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
