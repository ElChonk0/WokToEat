Huángshān Shāobǐng (Sesame Flatbread) 黄山烧饼

▾

Surprise me with

Crisp sesame flatbread, baked on the walls of a clay oven or in a pan, is a traditional Anhui breakfast and snack served with congee.

  • Prep 30 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Ingredients

  • 300 g2.5 cupsFlour
  • 160 ml2/3 cupWarm water
  • 40 g1.5 ozSesame seeds
  • 60 g2 ozScallions
  • 5 g1 tspSalt
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 3 g1 tspYeast

Method

  1. Mix the flour, yeast, salt and warm water into a soft dough, knead 8 minutes and rest, covered, 1 hour until puffy.
  2. Roll the dough into a 30 x 40 cm rectangle, brush with the oil and scatter with the scallions.
  3. Roll up like a log, cut into 6 pieces and press each flat into a 10 cm disc.
  4. Brush the tops with water and press into the sesame seeds.
  5. Bake at 220 C (425 F) for 15 minutes until golden and crisp.

Common mistakes

  • Dough that is too dry bakes hard.
  • Not brushing with water means the sesame seeds fall off.

Authentic tips

  • Let the shaped dough rest 10 minutes before baking.
  • Serve with congee or soy milk.

Substitutions

  • Instant yeast works without proofing; use the same amount.
  • Cooking on a hot griddle replaces baking.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days at room temperature; reheat in a hot oven.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Congee or soy milk
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
