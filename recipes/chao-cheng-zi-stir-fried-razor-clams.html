Chǎo Chēng Zi (Stir-Fried Razor Clams) 炒蛏子

Razor clams (cheng zi) are a prized Fujian shellfish, stir-fried briefly with garlic, ginger and wine.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 6 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Fujian Seafood LunchDinnerStir-fry30 min or lessShellfish

  • 800 g1.75 lbRazor clams, cleaned
  • 40 g3 tbspGinger and garlic
  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 20 g2 tbspScallion
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Soak the razor clams in salted water for 30 minutes, rinse well and drain; if long, cut into 5 cm sections.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok over very high heat and fry the ginger and garlic for 15 seconds.
  3. Add the clams and the wine and toss over high heat, covering for 1-2 minutes.
  4. Add the soy sauce and scallion and toss 30 seconds until the shells open and the meat is just cooked.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking toughens the clams; they cook in minutes.
  • Not purging the sand leaves a gritty dish.

Authentic tips

  • Cook only until the shells open.
  • Substitute regular clams if razor clams are unavailable.

Substitutions

  • Manila clams or mussels replace razor clams.
  • Add a little chili for heat.

Storage

Leftovers: Eat immediately.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
