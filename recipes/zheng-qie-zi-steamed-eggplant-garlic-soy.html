Zheng Qie Zi (Steamed Eggplant with Garlic Soy Dressing) 蒸茄子

Steaming is the light Cantonese way with eggplant: the strips turn creamy without oil and are dressed with garlic, soy and sizzling oil.

  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Lay the eggplant skin-side down on a heatproof plate.
  2. Steam over high heat for 10 to 12 minutes until completely soft. Pour off any liquid.
  3. Mix the soy sauce, vinegar, sugar and sesame oil and spoon it over the eggplant.
  4. Pile the garlic, green onion and chili on top.
  5. Heat the oil until it just smokes and pour it over the aromatics so they sizzle. Serve warm or at room temperature.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Under-steamed eggplant is spongy; it should be silky.
  • Leaving the steaming water on the plate dilutes the dressing.

Authentic tips

  • Keep the skin side down so the eggplant keeps its purple color.
  • It is also good cold as a summer side.

Substitutions

  • Globe eggplant, peeled and cut into batons; steam 15 minutes.
  • Add minced pork on top before steaming for a non-vegetarian version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator; serve cold.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice with a main dish
  • Soup: Spinach tofu soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
