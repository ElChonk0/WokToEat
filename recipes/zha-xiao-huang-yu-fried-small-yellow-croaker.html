Zhà Xiǎo Huáng Yú (Fried Small Yellow Croaker) 炸小黄鱼

▾

Surprise me with

Fried small yellow croaker, seasoned with pepper salt, is a common home dish and bar snack in northern China, where small whole fish are fried until crisp enough to eat bones and all.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 700 g1.5 lbSmall yellow croaker, cleaned
  • 60 g1/2 cupFlour
  • 5 g1 tspSalt
  • 1 g1/4 tspWhite pepper
  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 400 ml1.75 cupsNeutral oil for frying
  • 44Lemon wedges

Method

  1. Marinate the fish with the wine, salt and white pepper for 15 minutes; pat very dry.
  2. Dredge the fish in the flour and shake off the excess.
  3. Heat the oil to 180 C (350 F) and fry in batches for 3-4 minutes until golden and crisp.
  4. Fry again for 30 seconds to make them extra crisp; drain on a rack.
  5. Serve hot with lemon.

Common mistakes

  • Wet fish spatter and steam.
  • Overcrowding the pot lowers the temperature.

Authentic tips

  • Fry twice for crunch so you can eat the bones.
  • Serve with pepper salt for dipping.

Substitutions

  • Smelts or whitebait replace the croaker.
  • Air-fry at 200 C (400 F) for 10 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; eat while hot and crisp.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Lemon wedges
  • Beverage: Cold beer
