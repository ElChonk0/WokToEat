Kè Jiā Xiǎo Chǎo (Hakka Stir-Fry) 客家小炒

▾

Surprise me with

Hakka stir-fry combines strips of pork, tofu and dried cuttlefish with celery and scallions, a way of making a savory dish from preserved and pantry ingredients.

  • Prep 25 min
  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 200 g7 ozPork belly, cut into matchsticks
  • 40 g1.5 ozDried cuttlefish, soaked and shredded
  • 200 g7 ozFirm tofu, cut into batons
  • 100 g3.5 ozChinese celery or celery, cut into 4 cm pieces
  • 40 g1.5 ozGreen onion, cut into 4 cm lengths
  • 3 cloves3 clovesGarlic, sliced
  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 2 g1/3 tspSalt
  • 1 g1/4 tspWhite pepper

Method

  1. Soak the dried cuttlefish in cold water for 2 hours until soft, then shred it and pat dry.
  2. Fry the tofu batons in 30 ml of the oil in a wok over medium-high heat for 4 minutes until golden on the edges, then lift out.
  3. Add the remaining oil and the pork, and stir-fry over high heat for 3 minutes until the fat renders and the edges crisp.
  4. Add the garlic and cuttlefish, stir-fry for 1 minute until fragrant, then splash in the wine around the rim of the wok.
  5. Add the celery, green onion and tofu, season with the soy sauce, salt and white pepper, and toss for 1 minute until the celery is just crisp.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the celery too early turns it limp and bitter; add it last.
  • Crowding the wok steams the pork; cook it over high heat in a single layer.

Authentic tips

  • Cut everything into similar matchsticks so it cooks evenly.
  • Taste before adding salt, because the cuttlefish and soy sauce are already salty.

Substitutions

  • Dried squid replaces dried cuttlefish.
  • Smoked tofu adds a deeper flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended, because the tofu and celery go watery.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear pork rib soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot oolong tea
