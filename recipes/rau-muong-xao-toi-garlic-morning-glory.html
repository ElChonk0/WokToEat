Rau Muống Xào Tỏi (Garlic Morning Glory)

Rau muong, water spinach, is Vietnam's everyday green, stir-fried with garlic over high heat as a side at nearly every meal.

  • Prep 5 min
  • Cook 4 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Vietnam Tofu & Vegetarian LunchDinnerStir-fryVegetarianVegan30 min or less

  • 500 g1.1 lbWater spinach

  • 15 ml1 tbspSoy sauce
  • 3 g1/2 tspSugar
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Cut the water spinach into 8 cm lengths and drain well.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok until smoking and fry the garlic 10 seconds.
  3. Add the stems and toss 1 minute, then the leaves.
  4. Season with the soy sauce, sugar and salt.
  5. Toss 1 minute and serve at once.

Common mistakes

  • Wet greens spatter and steam.
  • Overcooking makes it limp.

Authentic tips

  • Stems first as they take longer.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • Spinach or pea shoots replace water spinach.
  • Add fish sauce for a non-vegetarian version.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Canh
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
