Bosten Lake Stewed Fish 博斯腾湖炖鱼

▾

Surprise me with

Bosten Lake, Xinjiang's largest freshwater lake, supplies local fish that are stewed with potato, tomato and chili in a single pot.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 30 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 900 g2 lbLake fish, cut into chunks
  • 300 g10 ozPotatoes
  • 200 g7 ozTomatoes
  • 200 g7 ozOnion and green pepper
  • 30 g2 tbspGarlic
  • 10 g1 tbspCumin and chili
  • 300 ml1.25 cupsWater
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a wide pot and fry the onion, garlic, cumin and chili 3 minutes.
  2. Add the tomatoes and cook 5 minutes into a sauce.
  3. Add the potato chunks and water, cover and simmer 12 minutes.
  4. Add the fish and green pepper and simmer gently 10 minutes until the fish is just cooked.
  5. Season with salt and serve from the pot.

Common mistakes

  • Stirring breaks the fish.
  • Adding the fish too early overcooks it.

Authentic tips

  • Use a firm freshwater fish such as carp.
  • Serve with flatbread.

Substitutions

  • Carp or catfish replace lake fish.
  • Add green chilies for heat.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freeze the broth only.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Nang flatbread
  • Soup: Not needed; the stew is its own soup
  • Dessert: Hami melon
  • Beverage: Black tea
