Babi Asam Manis (Sweet-Sour Braised Pork Ribs)

A homestyle Chinese-Indonesian dish, babi asam manis braises pork ribs in a tamarind-sweetened sauce, balancing sour and sweet notes distinct from the fried version of sweet and sour pork.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 35 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Blanch rib pieces in boiling water 3 minutes; drain.
  2. Fry shallot and garlic in a little oil until fragrant.
  3. Add ribs and stir to coat, cooking 2–3 minutes.
  4. Add tamarind paste, palm sugar, kecap manis, tomato, and water; bring to a simmer.
  5. Cover and braise 20 minutes, then uncover and reduce 10–15 minutes until the sauce thickens and coats the ribs.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the initial blanch, which leaves the sauce cloudier and slightly muddier in flavor.
  • Using too little tamarind, which is essential to this dish's distinctive sour-sweet balance.

Authentic tips

  • Taste and adjust the balance of sour (tamarind) against sweet (palm sugar and kecap manis) before serving.
  • This dish is a great make-ahead option, since the flavor deepens as it sits.

Substitutions

  • Baby back ribs cook faster; reduce by 10 minutes.
  • Pineapple adds extra sweetness.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; reheat in the sauce.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.
