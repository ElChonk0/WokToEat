Dòu Chǐ Chǎo Ròu (Black Bean Pork) 豆豉炒肉

▾

Surprise me with

Fermented black beans, douchi, are a Hunan pantry staple, and stir-frying pork with them and fresh chilies is a daily home dish.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 500 g1.1 lbPork shoulder, sliced
  • 20 g1.5 tbspFermented black beans
  • 100 g3.5 ozFresh chilies
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Slice the pork thin and toss with the wine and half the soy sauce. Cut the chilies into 2 cm pieces.
  2. Heat half the oil in a wok over high heat and stir-fry the pork 2-3 minutes until browned; remove.
  3. Add the remaining oil, black beans and garlic and fry 20 seconds.
  4. Add the chilies and stir-fry 2 minutes until blistered.
  5. Return the pork with the remaining soy sauce and toss 1 minute.

Common mistakes

  • Overcrowding the wok steams the pork.
  • Over-salting: black beans and soy are both salty.

Authentic tips

  • Use fatty pork shoulder for juiciness.
  • Rinse the beans if they are very salty.

Substitutions

  • Beef sirloin takes the same method.
  • Firm tofu cubes replace the pork for a vegetarian dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
