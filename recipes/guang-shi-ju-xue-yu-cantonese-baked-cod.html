Guǎng Shì Jú Xuě Yú (Cantonese Baked Cod with Ginger and Scallion) 广式焗鳕鱼

▾

Surprise me with

Cantonese home cooks apply the flavors of steamed fish, ginger, scallion, soy and sizzling oil, to fillets cooked in the oven, a modern convenience version of a classic.

  • Cook 20 min

Method

  1. Mince the garlic, ginger and green onion whites very finely, almost to a paste, so the coating clings to the fish. Mix in the garlic salt, sugar, pepper, cornstarch and wine. Stir the dip soy sauces, vinegar, sugar and chili sauce in a small bowl.
  2. Pat the cod completely dry; any moisture stops the coating sticking and the fish from browning. Rub the paste evenly over all sides, then toss with 1 tablespoon of the oil.
  3. Heat the oven to 220 C (425 F) with a rack low in the oven. Line a tray with foil, brush it with oil and lay the fillets on it with space between them.
  4. Bake for 12 to 15 minutes on the low rack, drizzle the remaining oil over the tops, then move the tray to the top shelf for 3 to 5 minutes until the garlic crust turns golden. An air fryer at 190 C (375 F) for 11 to 12 minutes works too.
  5. Pull the fish when it reads 55 to 60 C (130 to 140 F) in the center and flakes when pressed; cod turns rubbery quickly beyond that. Rest 2 minutes, scatter with cilantro and serve with the dipping sauce.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Leaving the fishy cooking liquid in the dish muddies the sauce; pour it off.
  • Oil that is not hot enough will not release the aroma of the ginger and scallion.

Authentic tips

  • This is the same finish as Cantonese steamed fish; steaming the fillets for 8 to 10 minutes works too.
  • Use the thickest loin pieces you can find so they stay moist.

Substitutions

  • Haddock, halibut or black cod work the same way.
  • No Shaoxing wine: use dry sherry.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator; reheat gently, covered.

Freezing: Not recommended after cooking.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and garlic gai lan
  • Soup: Winter melon soup
  • Dessert: Sweet red bean soup
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
