Dàlǐ Shā Guō Yú (Dali Clay Pot Fish) 大理砂锅鱼

Dali's clay pot fish is a signature of the Bai people near Erhai Lake: fresh lake fish simmered at the table with tofu, ham and vegetables.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 25 min
  • Difficulty Medium
  • Draft Yunnan Seafood LunchDinnerBraise / stew

  • 800 g1.75 lbFish, cut into chunks
  • 200 g7 ozTofu
  • 40 g1.5 ozHam
  • 40 g3 tbspGinger and scallion
  • 150 g5 ozTomato
  • 1 L4 cupsWater or stock
  • 6 g1 tspSalt
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Cut the fish into thick chunks and pat dry; cut the tofu into 3 cm cubes and the tomato into wedges.
  2. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven or heavy pot and fry the ginger 30 seconds.
  3. Add the tomato, ham and stock; bring to a boil and simmer 5 minutes.
  4. Add the fish and tofu and simmer gently 10 minutes until the fish is just cooked.
  5. Season with the salt and garnish with scallion; serve in the pot.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard breaks up the fish.
  • Over-salting hides the delicate lake-fish flavor.

Authentic tips

  • Use a firm freshwater fish such as carp or catfish.
  • Serve with chili dip.

Substitutions

  • Carp or catfish are the closest to lake fish.
  • Add napa cabbage and glass noodles.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freeze the broth only.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; the pot is its own soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Pu'er tea
