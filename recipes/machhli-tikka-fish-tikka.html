Machhli Tikka (Tandoori Fish Tikka)

▾

Surprise me with

Fish tikka, firm fish marinated in yogurt and spices and cooked in a tandoor, is a Punjabi restaurant classic.

  • Prep 20 min (plus 1 hr marinating)
  • Cook 12 min

  • 800 g1.75 lbFirm white fish (monkfish, cod or kingfish), cut into large chunks
  • 30 ml2 tbspLemon juice
  • 6 g1 tspSalt
  • 150 g2/3 cupHung (strained) yogurt
  • 30 g2 tbspGinger-garlic paste
  • 6 g2 tspKashmiri chili powder
  • 3 g1 tspGaram masala
  • 2 g1/2 tspAjwain (carom seeds)
  • 1 g1/2 tspGround turmeric
  • 15 g2 tbspGram flour, toasted
  • 30 ml2 tbspMustard oil
  • 15 g1 tbspButter, melted, for basting
  • 3 g1 tspChaat masala

Method

  1. Toss the fish with the lemon juice and half the salt and leave for 15 minutes. Pat dry.
  2. Toast the gram flour in a dry pan for 2 minutes until nutty. Heat the mustard oil until it smokes, cool slightly and mix with the yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, chili, garam masala, ajwain, turmeric, toasted gram flour and remaining salt. The gram flour binds the marinade so it does not slide off delicate fish.
  3. Coat the fish and marinate for 1 hour; longer and the acid softens it too much.
  4. Thread onto skewers and grill or broil on high, 10 cm (4 in) from the heat, for 8 to 10 minutes, turning once and basting with butter, until charred at the edges and just cooked.
  5. Sprinkle with chaat masala and serve with lemon and mint chutney.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking dries the fish.
  • Too long in lemon makes the fish mushy.

Authentic tips

  • Use firm fish like halibut or catfish.
  • Serve with onion rings and chutney.

Substitutions

  • Salmon or halibut replace the white fish.
  • A broiler replaces the tandoor.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Naan and mint chutney
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Kulfi
  • Beverage: Masala chai
