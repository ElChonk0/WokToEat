Tamagoyaki (Japanese Rolled Omelet) 卵焼き

▾

Surprise me with

Tamagoyaki, a layered, sweet-savory rolled omelet, is a staple of Japanese breakfast and bento boxes.

Method

  1. Whisk the eggs with the sugar, soy sauce, mirin and dashi until just combined.
  2. Heat a rectangular pan, oil it lightly and pour in a thin layer.
  3. When it is half set, roll it to one side.
  4. Oil the pan again and pour in more egg, lifting the roll so egg flows underneath; roll again, repeating 3-4 times.
  5. Rest the omelet for 2 minutes and slice.

Common mistakes

  • High heat burns the sugary egg.
  • Filling the pan too deep makes it hard to roll.

Authentic tips

  • A round pan works; trim the edges.
  • Add dashi for a juicy texture.

Substitutions

  • A round pan works; fold and trim the edges.
  • Grated daikon on the side is traditional.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; good cold in bento.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Miso soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
