Sun Gan Shao Ji (Dried Bamboo Shoot Braised Chicken) 笋干烧鸡

Dried bamboo shoots are a staple of Huizhou's mountain cooking, adding a chewy texture and savory flavor to braised chicken.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 40 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Anhui Chicken LunchDinnerBraise / stew
350 kcal24 g protein12 g carbs23 g fat700 mg sodiumper serving

Ingredients

  • 800 g1.75 lbChicken thighs, chopped
  • 100 g3.5 ozDried bamboo shoots, soaked
  • 45 ml3 tbspSoy sauce
  • 10 g2 tspSugar
  • 40 g3 tbspGinger and garlic
  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 400 ml1.75 cupsWater
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Soak the dried bamboo shoots in water overnight (or boil 40 minutes), rinse and cut into 4 cm pieces.
  2. Brown the chicken in the oil over medium-high heat 8 minutes.
  3. Add the ginger, garlic, soy sauce, sugar and wine and stir 2 minutes.
  4. Add the bamboo shoots and 400 ml water, cover and simmer 30 minutes.
  5. Uncover and reduce the sauce 5 minutes.

Common mistakes

  • Not soaking the shoots long enough leaves them tough and bitter.
  • Salting too early: the soy sauce provides enough.

Authentic tips

  • Dried bamboo shoots absorb the braise and become chewy and savory.
  • Soak overnight for the best texture.

Substitutions

  • Canned bamboo shoots shorten the preparation but lack the chew.
  • Boneless thighs cook 10 minutes faster.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; the flavor improves.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
