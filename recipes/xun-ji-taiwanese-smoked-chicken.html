Xūn Jī (Taiwanese Smoked Chicken) 燻雞

▾

Surprise me with

Smoked chicken, braised, then smoked over sugar and tea, is a common Taiwanese night-market and holiday dish.

  • Cook 1.5 hr

  • 25 g1 ozStar anise, ginger and green onion

  • 20 g1/4 cupBlack tea leaves, for smoking
  • 40 g3 tbspBrown sugar, for smoking
  • 10 ml2 tspSesame oil, for brushing

Method

  1. Simmer the soy sauce, rice wine, sugar, star anise, ginger, green onion and water for 10 minutes. Add the chicken, breast down, and poach at a bare simmer for 30 to 35 minutes, turning once, then turn off the heat and leave it in the liquid for 20 minutes, until it reaches 74 C (165 F) in the thigh.
  2. Lift the chicken out and pat it dry.
  3. Line a flat-bottom wok and its lid with heavy foil. Mix the rice, tea leaves and brown sugar and spread them in the bottom. Set a rack above them and put the chicken on it.
  4. Cover tightly, heat over medium-high until it starts to smoke, then lower to medium and smoke for 12 to 15 minutes. Turn off the heat and leave covered for 5 minutes. Open a window; the sugar caramelizes into thick smoke.
  5. Brush the chicken with sesame oil, rest for 10 minutes, then chop and serve cold or at room temperature.

Common mistakes

  • Over-smoking turns the skin bitter.
  • Poor ventilation: turn on the extractor fan.

Authentic tips

  • Pat the poached chicken dry so the skin takes the smoke.
  • Reduce the braising liquid as a dipping sauce.

Substitutions

  • Chicken legs smoke in the same time.
  • A stovetop foil-lined pot replaces a smoker.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; serve cold.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Oolong tea
