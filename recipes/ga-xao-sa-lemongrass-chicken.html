Gà Xào Sả (Lemongrass Chicken)

A fast home stir-fry built on the bright, citrusy aroma of fresh lemongrass paired with chicken and a touch of chili — a staple weeknight dish across Vietnamese households.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Vietnam Chicken LunchDinnerStir-fry30 min or less

  • 2 stalks2 stalksLemongrass, minced
  • 10g2 clovesGarlic, minced
  • 22Shallot, minced
  • 30ml2 tbspFish sauce
  • 15g1 tbspSugar
  • 11Chili, sliced (optional)
  • 30ml2 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Marinate chicken briefly (15–20 minutes if time allows) with half the lemongrass, garlic, fish sauce, and sugar.
  2. Heat oil over medium-high heat; add shallot and remaining lemongrass and garlic, frying until fragrant, about 1 minute.
  3. Add chicken and cook, stirring occasionally, 8–10 minutes until browned and cooked through.
  4. Add chili in the last minute if using.
  5. Garnish with scallion and serve over rice.

Common mistakes

  • Mincing lemongrass too coarsely: tough, fibrous pieces are unpleasant to bite — mince very finely or use a food processor.

Authentic tips

  • Letting the chicken sit undisturbed for a minute or two at a time helps it caramelize rather than just steam.

Substitutions

  • Pork or beef take the same marinade.
  • Dried lemongrass is a poor substitute; use fresh or frozen.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Canh
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
