Hambagu (Japanese Hamburg Steak) ハンバーグ

▾

Surprise me with

Hambagu, a juicy ground-meat patty with a demi-glace or ketchup sauce, is a yoshoku (Western-style Japanese) classic of diners and home cooking.

  • 60 ml4 tbspKetchup and Worcestershire
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Soften the onion in butter for 6 minutes and cool.
  2. Soak the panko in milk, then mix with the beef, pork, onion, egg, salt and nutmeg until sticky; chill 20 minutes.
  3. Shape into 4 patties and press a dimple in the center of each.
  4. Fry in the oil over medium-high heat for 3 minutes until browned, flip, cover and cook over low heat 8 minutes.
  5. Pour the ketchup and Worcestershire into the pan with a splash of water, simmer 2 minutes and pour over.

Common mistakes

  • Overworking makes dense patties.
  • Skipping the dimple makes them bulge.

Authentic tips

  • A mix of beef and pork keeps them juicy.
  • Serve with rice and salad.

Substitutions

  • All beef works; add a spoonful of milk for juiciness.
  • Tonkatsu sauce replaces ketchup and Worcestershire.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat in the sauce.

Freezing: Freezes cooked patties for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and green salad
  • Soup: Miso soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
