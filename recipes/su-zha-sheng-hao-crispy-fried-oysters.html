Sū Zhà Shēng Háo (Crispy Fried Oysters) 酥炸生蚝

▾

Surprise me with

Plump oysters in a light, crisp batter are a Cantonese restaurant and banquet dish, especially in winter when oysters are best.

Method

  1. Toss the oysters gently with the cleaning flour, then rinse under cold water to remove grit and slime. Blanch for 15 seconds, drain and pat very dry.
  2. Season the oysters with the ginger, wine and salt.
  3. Whisk the batter flour, cornstarch, baking powder, ice water and 1 tablespoon of oil into a smooth batter.
  4. Heat the frying oil in a Dutch oven to 180 C (350 F). Dip the oysters in the batter and fry in batches for 2 to 3 minutes until puffed and golden.
  5. Drain and serve with spiced salt and Worcestershire or sweet chili sauce.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the quick blanch lets the oysters release water and spatter in the oil.
  • Over-frying shrinks the oysters to nothing.

Authentic tips

  • The flour scrub is the Cantonese way to clean oysters.
  • Keep the batter cold for the crispest shell.

Substitutions

  • Shrimp or squid in the same batter.
  • Serve on shredded lettuce with lemon.

Storage

Leftovers: Best eaten at once.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Soup: Winter melon soup
  • Dessert: Sweet red bean soup
