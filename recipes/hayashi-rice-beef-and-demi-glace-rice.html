Hayashi Raisu (Hayashi Beef Stew Rice) ハヤシライス

Hayashi rice, beef and onions in a demi-glace tomato sauce over rice, is a Japanese yoshoku dish that competes with curry rice in popularity.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 40 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 500 g1.1 lbBeef, thin slices
  • 300 g10 ozOnions
  • 150 g5 ozMushrooms
  • 60 g2 ozTomato paste and ketchup
  • 120 ml1/2 cupRed wine
  • 400 ml1.75 cupsDemi-glace or stock
  • 600 g21 ozCooked rice
  • 30 g2 tbspButter

Method

  1. Melt the butter in a heavy pot and cook the onions slowly for 12 minutes until deep golden.
  2. Add the beef, mushrooms and tomato paste and cook 4 minutes.
  3. Pour in the red wine and reduce by half.
  4. Add the demi-glace or stock and ketchup and simmer 25 minutes until thick.
  5. Spoon over the rice and serve.

Common mistakes

  • Under-caramelized onions give a thin, acidic sauce.
  • Overcooking the beef toughens it; use well-marbled slices.

Authentic tips

  • A pinch of sugar rounds out the sauce.
  • Better the next day.

Substitutions

  • Ready-made hayashi roux blocks save time.
  • Stew meat replaces thin slices; braise 40 minutes longer.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes the sauce for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Green salad
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Black tea
