Hayashi Raisu (Hayashi Beef Stew Rice) ハヤシライス

▾

Surprise me with

Hayashi rice, beef and onions in a demi-glace tomato sauce over rice, is a Japanese yoshoku dish that competes with curry rice in popularity.

Method

  1. Season the beef with salt and pepper and dust it with the flour, which thickens the sauce later.
  2. Melt half the butter in a Dutch oven over medium heat and cook the onions for 10 to 15 minutes until deep golden, then add the garlic for 30 seconds. Browned onions give hayashi sauce its sweetness and color.
  3. Push the onions aside, add the remaining butter and the beef and cook for 2 to 3 minutes until no longer pink. Add the mushrooms for 2 minutes.
  4. Pour in the wine and boil for 2 minutes, scraping the bottom, until reduced by half. Add the tomatoes, stock, ketchup, tonkatsu sauce, soy sauce, sugar and bay leaf.
  5. Simmer uncovered over low heat for 20 to 25 minutes, stirring now and then, until the sauce is thick, glossy and dark brown. Season and serve over rice.

Common mistakes

  • Under-caramelized onions give a thin, acidic sauce.
  • Overcooking the beef toughens it; use well-marbled slices.

Authentic tips

  • A pinch of sugar rounds out the sauce.
  • Better the next day.

Substitutions

  • Ready-made hayashi roux blocks save time.
  • Stew meat replaces thin slices; braise 40 minutes longer.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes the sauce for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Green salad
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Black tea
