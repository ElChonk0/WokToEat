Dōngběi Luàn Dùn (Northeastern Vegetable Stew) 东北乱炖

Luan dun, "chaotic stew," is a Dongbei one-pot dish of beans, potatoes and cabbage simmered with soybean paste, born of cold winters and sparse produce.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 30 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 300 g10 ozPotatoes
  • 200 g7 ozGreen beans
  • 200 g7 ozCabbage
  • 150 g5 ozTomatoes
  • 45 g3 tbspSoybean paste
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 500 ml2 cupsWater
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Peel the potatoes and cut into large chunks; trim the beans and chop the cabbage and tomatoes.
  2. Heat the oil in a pot over medium heat and fry the soybean paste and garlic 1 minute.
  3. Add the potatoes and beans and stir 2 minutes, then pour in 500 ml water.
  4. Cover and simmer 15 minutes until the potatoes start to soften.
  5. Add the cabbage and tomatoes and cook 8 minutes until everything is tender.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the vegetables makes them mushy.
  • Too much soybean paste makes it too salty.

Authentic tips

  • Add the vegetables in order of cooking time.
  • Serve with steamed buns.

Substitutions

  • Any seasonal vegetables replace the list.
  • Add tofu or pork belly for richness.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months without the potatoes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or cornbread
  • Soup: Not needed; the stew is its own soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
