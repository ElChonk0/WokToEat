Gaeng Massaman Jay (Vegetarian Massaman Curry)

▾

Surprise me with

Massaman curry, a mild, spiced curry with Persian and Malay influences, is among the world's most celebrated dishes; potatoes, peanuts and tofu make a hearty vegetarian version.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 30 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

  • 400 g14 ozPotatoes
  • 250 g9 ozFirm tofu
  • 60 g4 tbspMassaman curry paste (vegetarian)
  • 400 ml1.75 cupsCoconut milk
  • 100 g3.5 ozOnion
  • 40 g1.5 ozRoasted peanuts
  • 20 g1.5 tbspTamarind
  • 15 g1 tbspPalm sugar

Method

  1. Fry the massaman paste in the thick coconut cream for 2 minutes.
  2. Add the potatoes, onion and remaining coconut milk with 100 ml water.
  3. Simmer 20 minutes until the potatoes are tender.
  4. Add the tofu, tamarind, sugar and peanuts and simmer 5 minutes.
  5. Taste for sweet, sour and salty balance.

Common mistakes

  • Cutting the potatoes too small makes them dissolve.
  • Boiling hard splits the coconut milk.

Authentic tips

  • Check the paste for shrimp paste.
  • Serve with jasmine rice.

Substitutions

  • Sweet potato replaces some potato.
  • Check the paste for shrimp paste.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; better the next day.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months without the potatoes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Jasmine rice
  • Soup: Cucumber salad
  • Dessert: Mango sticky rice
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
