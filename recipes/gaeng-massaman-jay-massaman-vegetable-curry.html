Gaeng Massaman Jay (Vegetarian Massaman Curry)

▾

Surprise me with

Massaman curry, a mild, spiced curry with Persian and Malay influences, is among the world's most celebrated dishes; potatoes, peanuts and tofu make a hearty vegetarian version.

  • Cook 30 min

Method

  1. Brown the tofu in the oil in a skillet over medium-high heat for 5 to 6 minutes until golden. Set aside.
  2. Heat the thick top of the coconut milk in a Dutch oven over medium heat until the oil separates, then fry the curry paste for 2 to 3 minutes until fragrant.
  3. Add the rest of the coconut milk, the stock, cinnamon, cardamom, potatoes, carrot, onion and peanuts. Simmer, partly covered, for 20 to 25 minutes until the potatoes are tender and starting to thicken the sauce.
  4. Add the tofu and season with tamarind, palm sugar and soy sauce until sweet, salty and gently sour.
  5. Simmer for 5 minutes more, then rest for 10 minutes before serving with jasmine rice.

Common mistakes

  • Cutting the potatoes too small makes them dissolve.
  • Boiling hard splits the coconut milk.

Authentic tips

  • Check the paste for shrimp paste.
  • Serve with jasmine rice.

Substitutions

  • Sweet potato replaces some potato.
  • Check the paste for shrimp paste.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; better the next day.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months without the potatoes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Jasmine rice
  • Soup: Cucumber salad
  • Dessert: Mango sticky rice
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
