Chicken 65

A Tamil Nadu bar-food classic of uncertain naming origin, chicken 65 marinates and fries chicken before tossing it in a punchy curry-leaf, chili, and garlic tempering — spicy, tangy, and deeply addictive.

Method

  1. Marinate chicken in yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, chili powder, and turmeric for at least 1 hour.
  2. Toss the marinated chicken in a mix of cornstarch and rice flour to coat.
  3. Heat oil to 175°C (350°F) and fry chicken in batches until golden and crisp, 5–6 minutes; drain.
  4. In a separate pan, temper a little oil with curry leaves, green chilies, and minced garlic until fragrant and the curry leaves crisp.
  5. Toss the fried chicken through the tempering mixture until well coated.
  6. Serve immediately, hot and crisp.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the tempering step and just serving the fried chicken plain, which misses the dish's signature curry-leaf aroma.
  • Frying at too low a temperature, which produces a greasy rather than crisp coating.

Authentic tips

  • The combination of cornstarch and rice flour gives an extra-crisp coating compared to either alone.
  • Fresh curry leaves fried until crisp are essential to the dish's characteristic aroma — don't substitute dried.

Substitutions

  • No rice flour: all-purpose flour mixed with cornstarch works, though texture is slightly less crisp.
  • For a milder version, reduce the chili powder and use fewer green chilies in the tempering.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1–2 days but loses crispness.

Freezing: Freezes well, fried and untempered, for up to a month; re-fry and temper fresh from frozen.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Lemon wedges and onion rings
  • Soup: Rasam
  • Dessert: Gulab jamun
  • Beverage: Cold beer or lime soda
