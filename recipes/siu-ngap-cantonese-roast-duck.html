Siu Ngap (Cantonese Roast Duck) 烧鸭

▾

Surprise me with

Cantonese roast duck is a siu mei dish of whole duck with a spiced cavity and a dried, lacquered skin, roasted until the skin is crisp and the meat stays juicy, and chopped to order in Hong Kong barbecue shops.

Method

  1. Pat the duck dry, trim the neck skin, and mix the hoisin, soy sauce, wine, sugar, five-spice, garlic, ginger, star anise and salt into a paste.
  2. Spoon the paste into the cavity, then sew or skewer the opening shut so the marinade stays inside.
  3. Scald the skin with the boiling water, ladling it over until the skin tightens and turns white.
  4. Dissolve the maltose and vinegar in the hot water and brush it all over the duck. Hang or place the duck uncovered on a rack in the refrigerator for 12 to 24 hours until the skin feels dry and papery.
  5. Roast on a rack above a tray at 220 C (425 F) for 20 minutes, then reduce to 180 C (350 F) and roast for 1 hour until the skin is deep mahogany and the thigh reaches 74 C (165 F).
  6. Rest for 15 minutes, pour the juices from the cavity into a bowl for sauce, and chop the duck through the bone into pieces. Serve with the cavity juices and the plum sauce.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the air-drying gives soft, leathery skin; give it at least 12 hours.
  • A closed cavity that is not sealed lets the marinade leak and the flavor escape.

Authentic tips

  • A fan blowing on the duck in the refrigerator dries it faster.
  • Roast on a rack so the fat drips away and the skin crisps all round.

Substitutions

  • Two duck legs and thighs replace a whole duck: roast 50 minutes.
  • Honey replaces maltose.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; crisp in a 200 C oven for 10 minutes.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months; thaw overnight.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and blanched choy sum
  • Soup: Winter melon soup
  • Dessert: Fresh orange wedges
  • Beverage: Hot pu-erh tea
