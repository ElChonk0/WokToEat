Cá Nướng Giấy Bạc (Foil-Baked Lemongrass Trout)

▾

Surprise me with

Fish seasoned with lemongrass, garlic and fish sauce and sealed in foil to bake or grill is a popular Vietnamese restaurant and home dish, eaten wrapped in rice paper with herbs.

Method

  1. Mix the lemongrass, garlic, shallots, fish sauce, sugar, turmeric, chilies and oil into a paste.
  2. Lay each fillet on a sheet of foil, spread the paste over it and top with the scallions and dill.
  3. Fold the foil into sealed parcels, leaving a little space above the fish for steam.
  4. Bake at 200 C (400 F) or on a medium grill for 12 to 15 minutes, until the fish flakes. Open the parcels for the last 2 minutes on the grill to char the top lightly.
  5. Serve at the table with rice paper, vermicelli, herbs and dipping sauce, so everyone can roll their own.

Cook’s notes

Written for rainbow trout fillets. Vietnamese cooks use snakehead, catfish or sea fish.

Common mistakes

  • Leaky foil dries the fish; crimp the edges tightly.
  • Over-baking dries out the thin fillets.

Authentic tips

  • A charcoal grill gives smoke, but the oven works on any stove.
  • Serve with nuoc cham.

Substitutions

  • Haddock or cod loin replaces the trout.
  • Banana leaves under the foil add aroma.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Rice paper, herbs and vermicelli
  • Soup: Canh chua
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
