Xiān Xiā Cháng Fěn (Shrimp Rice Noodle Rolls) 鲜虾肠粉

Cheung fun are silky steamed rice-noodle sheets wrapped around fillings; shrimp is the classic version found in Cantonese dim sum restaurants.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Method

  1. Whisk the rice flour, wheat starch, cornstarch, 450 ml water and a pinch of salt until smooth; rest 20 minutes and stir again before using.
  2. Oil a shallow heatproof tray, ladle in a thin layer of batter (about 3 mm) and scatter over a few shrimp.
  3. Steam over high heat for 3 minutes until the sheet is translucent and the shrimp are pink.
  4. Loosen with a scraper and roll the sheet into a log; repeat with the remaining batter.
  5. Mix the soy sauce, sugar, sesame oil and 60 ml hot water and pour over the rolls just before serving.

Common mistakes

  • A batter that has settled makes thick, rubbery sheets, so stir before every ladle.
  • Overcooking makes the shrimp tough; 3 minutes is enough.

Authentic tips

  • Serve immediately for the silkiest texture.
  • Slice the shrimp in half lengthwise if large so they cook evenly.

Substitutions

  • Char siu or beef make a classic alternative filling.
  • Chopped scallion and dried shrimp add flavor to the batter.

Storage

Leftovers: Best within an hour; leftovers keep 1 day and re-steam for 3 minutes.

Freezing: Not recommended; the rice rolls turn hard.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Congee
  • Soup: Clear chicken soup
  • Dessert: Sweet red bean soup
  • Beverage: Hot jasmine tea
