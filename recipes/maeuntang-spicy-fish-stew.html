Maeuntang (Spicy Fish Stew) 매운탕

▾

Surprise me with

Maeuntang is a Korean spicy fish stew, a hearty pot of fish, radish and tofu in a chili broth, enjoyed after drinking.

Method

  1. Soak the clams in salted water for at least 30 minutes so they spit out sand. Clean the fish, scraping off any blood along the backbone, and cut it into large pieces, keeping the head and bones for flavor. Mix the gochugaru, gochujang, doenjang, soup soy sauce, garlic and ginger into a seasoning.
  2. Bring the broth to a boil with the radish and seasoning over high heat and boil for about 3 minutes, until the radish starts to soften.
  3. Add the fish, clams and tofu, return to a boil, then lower to medium and simmer for about 5 minutes, until the clams open and the fish is nearly cooked. Skim off the foam and shake the pot rather than stirring, so the fish stays whole.
  4. Add the zucchini and green onion and boil for 1 to 2 minutes. Season with salt and pepper.
  5. Lay the crown daisy on top, turn off the heat and serve at once, bubbling, with rice. Pull the fish while it is just underdone; it finishes in the hot broth.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking makes the fish fall apart.
  • Skipping the skimming leaves a murky broth.

Authentic tips

  • Use fresh whole fish with bones for broth.
  • Add a few shrimp for extra sweetness.

Substitutions

  • Cod fillets and bones replace whole fish.
  • Add shrimp for sweetness.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and kimchi
  • Soup: Not needed; the stew is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Soju or barley tea
