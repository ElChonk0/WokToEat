Bún Chay (Vegetarian Vermicelli Bowl)

▾

Surprise me with

Bun, rice vermicelli bowls with herbs and a sweet-sour dressing, are a Vietnamese lunch staple; the chay version uses crisp tofu.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 300 g10 ozRice vermicelli
  • 300 g10 ozFried tofu
  • 150 g5 ozLettuce, mint, cilantro
  • 200 g7 ozCucumber and pickled carrots
  • 40 g1.5 ozRoasted peanuts
  • 120 ml1/2 cupSoy-lime dressing
  • 15 g1 tbspFried shallots

Method

  1. Cook the vermicelli, rinse in cold water and drain.
  2. Fry the tofu cubes until golden if not already fried.
  3. Arrange the vermicelli, tofu, lettuce, herbs, cucumber and pickled carrots in bowls.
  4. Pour on the dressing.
  5. Top with peanuts and fried shallots.

Common mistakes

  • Warm noodles wilt the lettuce.
  • Dressing too early makes it soggy.

Authentic tips

  • Add spring rolls on top.
  • Toss at the table.

Substitutions

  • Baked tofu replaces fried.
  • Spring rolls on top make it a feast.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated; assemble fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Soy-lime dressing is included
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
