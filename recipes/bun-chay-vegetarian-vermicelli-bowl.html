Bún Chay (Vegetarian Vermicelli Bowl)

▾

Surprise me with

Bun, rice vermicelli bowls with herbs and a sweet-sour dressing, are a Vietnamese lunch staple; the chay version uses crisp tofu.

Method

  1. Press the tofu, cut into cubes and toss with the soy sauce. Fry in the oil over medium-high heat for 6 to 8 minutes until golden on all sides, adding the lemongrass for the last 2 minutes until crisp.
  2. Make a vegan nuoc cham (nuoc cham chay): dissolve the sugar in the warm water, then add the soy sauce, lime juice, garlic and chili. Soy sauce stands in for fish sauce.
  3. Fill bowls with lettuce, herbs, sprouts and cucumber, then the vermicelli.
  4. Top with the lemongrass tofu and pickles.
  5. Scatter with peanuts and fried shallots and pour the dressing over. Toss before eating.

Common mistakes

  • Warm noodles wilt the lettuce.
  • Dressing too early makes it soggy.

Authentic tips

  • Add spring rolls on top.
  • Toss at the table.

Substitutions

  • Baked tofu replaces fried.
  • Spring rolls on top make it a feast.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated; assemble fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Soy-lime dressing is included
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
