Huī Shì Jī Sī Tāng Miàn (Hui-Style Chicken Noodle Soup) 徽式鸡丝汤面

▾

Hand-made noodles in a clear chicken broth topped with shredded chicken and mushrooms are a simple comfort food of Anhui.

  • Cook 30 min

Method

  1. Simmer the chicken with the ginger and green onion in the water for 30 minutes, skimming. Lift it out and shred the meat; return the bones for 20 minutes more and strain.
  2. Add the shiitake and ham to the broth and simmer for 10 minutes. Season with salt and soy sauce.
  3. Cook the noodles and blanch the bok choy.
  4. Divide among bowls, top with shredded chicken and ladle over the broth.
  5. Finish with sesame oil and white pepper.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking the noodles in the broth makes it starchy and cloudy.
  • A rolling boil toughens the chicken.

Authentic tips

  • Skim the broth for clarity.
  • Hand-pulled noodles are traditional.

Substitutions

  • Bone-in chicken gives a richer broth.
  • Fresh udon replaces wheat noodles.

Storage

Leftovers: Store noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 3 days.

Freezing: Freeze the broth for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled vegetables
  • Soup: Not needed; the noodle soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
