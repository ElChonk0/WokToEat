Yāo Guǒ Xiā Rén (Cashew Shrimp) 腰果虾仁

▾

Surprise me with

Stir-fried shrimp with toasted cashews and crisp vegetables in a light sauce is a Cantonese restaurant dish that became a takeout classic overseas.

Method

  1. Toss the shrimp with the cornstarch, fish sauce and pepper. Mix the oyster sauce, sugar, salt, sauce wine, cornstarch and water in a cup.
  2. Bring a pot of water to a boil. Blanch the carrot for 15 to 20 seconds, add the celery and mushroom for 40 seconds, then the snow peas for 20 to 30 seconds. Lift them into cold water for 2 minutes to keep them crisp and bright, then drain well. Blanch the shrimp in the same water for 30 to 40 seconds until just pink and drain.
  3. Heat a flat-bottom wok over high heat for 20 to 30 seconds, then lower the heat, add the oil and fry the garlic and ginger for 20 to 30 seconds. Raise to medium and add the green onion whites for 20 seconds.
  4. Turn the heat to high, add the vegetables for 20 to 30 seconds, then the shrimp for 20 to 30 seconds, and splash the wine around the edge.
  5. Lower the heat, stir the sauce in and toss for 20 to 30 seconds until glossy. Turn off the heat, stir in the sesame oil and serve topped with the cashews, added last so they stay crunchy.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooked shrimp turn rubbery; take them out as soon as they curl.
  • Adding the cashews early makes them soft.

Authentic tips

  • The egg-white coating (velveting) keeps the shrimp plump.
  • Cut the vegetables to the size of the cashews so everything eats together.

Substitutions

  • Chicken thigh, diced, for the takeout cashew chicken.
  • Roasted peanuts replace cashews.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator; the cashews soften.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed jasmine rice
  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Mango pudding
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
