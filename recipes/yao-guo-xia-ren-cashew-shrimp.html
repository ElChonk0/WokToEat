Yāo Guǒ Xiā Rén (Cashew Shrimp) 腰果虾仁

Stir-fried shrimp with toasted cashews and crisp vegetables in a light sauce is a Cantonese restaurant dish that became a takeout classic overseas.

  • Cook 10 min

Method

  1. Pat the shrimp dry and mix with the salt, egg white and cornstarch; refrigerate 15 minutes. Mix the oyster sauce, soy sauce, sugar, stock and cornstarch in a cup.
  2. Toast the cashews in a dry flat-bottom wok over medium-low heat for 4 to 5 minutes, stirring, until golden. Set aside.
  3. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil over high heat and cook the shrimp for 1 to 2 minutes until just pink. Lift out.
  4. Add the remaining oil and stir-fry the celery, carrot and onion for 2 minutes, then the garlic and ginger for 30 seconds. Splash in the wine.
  5. Stir the sauce, pour it in and bubble until glossy, then return the shrimp and cashews, toss for 30 seconds and finish with sesame oil.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooked shrimp turn rubbery; take them out as soon as they curl.
  • Adding the cashews early makes them soft.

Authentic tips

  • The egg-white coating (velveting) keeps the shrimp plump.
  • Cut the vegetables to the size of the cashews so everything eats together.

Substitutions

  • Chicken thigh, diced, for the takeout cashew chicken.
  • Roasted peanuts replace cashews.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator; the cashews soften.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed jasmine rice
  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Mango pudding
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
