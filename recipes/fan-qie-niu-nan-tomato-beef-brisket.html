Fān Qié Niú Nǎn (Tomato Beef Brisket) 番茄牛腩

▾

Tomato-braised beef brisket is a home-style dish found across China, particularly Sichuan, with a sweet-sour tomato sauce.

Method

  1. Cut the brisket into 4 cm cubes, cover with cold water, boil 5 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  2. Heat the oil in a heavy pot and fry the ginger and scallion for 1 minute. Add the chopped tomatoes and cook 8 minutes, mashing, until they break down into a thick paste.
  3. Add the brisket, soy sauce, sugar, star anise and water; bring to a boil, then reduce to the lowest simmer.
  4. Cover and cook for 90 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the beef is tender.
  5. Uncover, raise the heat and reduce the sauce for 10 minutes until it clings to the meat.

Common mistakes

  • Using underripe tomatoes gives a sharp sauce; add a little extra sugar if the fruit is pale.
  • Boiling hard instead of simmering makes the beef stringy.

Authentic tips

  • Brisket with some fat keeps the stew silky.
  • Better the next day, once the flavors have melded.

Substitutions

  • Short rib replaces the brisket; add 20 minutes.
  • A pressure cooker cuts the braise to 45 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Better the next day; keeps 4 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes well for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
