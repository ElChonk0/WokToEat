Fān Qié Niú Nǎn (Tomato Beef Brisket) 番茄牛腩

▾

Tomato-braised beef brisket is a home-style dish found across China, particularly Sichuan, with a sweet-sour tomato sauce.

  • 700 g1.5 lbBeef brisket, cut into 4 cm (1.5 in) cubes
  • 60 g2 ozGinger, sliced, and green onion, for blanching and braising

  • 15 g1 tbspRock or white sugar
  • 800 ml3.5 cupsHot water
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt
  • to tasteto tasteGreen onion, sliced, for garnish

Method

  1. Put the brisket in cold water with a few slices of ginger and a splash of Shaoxing wine, bring to a boil and boil for 3 minutes. Drain and rinse off the scum.
  2. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven over medium heat and fry the remaining ginger, green onion and star anise for 1 minute. Add the beef and the rest of the wine and stir for 2 minutes.
  3. Add the hot water, soy sauces and sugar, bring to a boil, then cover and simmer gently for 1.5 hours, until the beef is nearly tender. Hot water rather than cold keeps the meat from seizing.
  4. Meanwhile, fry the tomato paste and half the tomatoes in a skillet with a little oil over medium heat for 5 to 8 minutes, mashing, until they collapse into a thick sauce.
  5. Stir the tomato sauce into the beef and simmer uncovered for 20 minutes. Add the remaining tomato wedges for the last 5 minutes so some keep their shape.
  6. Season with salt and scatter with green onion. Serve with rice or over noodles.

Common mistakes

  • Using underripe tomatoes gives a sharp sauce; add a little extra sugar if the fruit is pale.
  • Boiling hard instead of simmering makes the beef stringy.

Authentic tips

  • Brisket with some fat keeps the stew silky.
  • Better the next day, once the flavors have melded.

Substitutions

  • Short rib replaces the brisket; add 20 minutes.
  • A pressure cooker cuts the braise to 45 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Better the next day; keeps 4 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes well for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
