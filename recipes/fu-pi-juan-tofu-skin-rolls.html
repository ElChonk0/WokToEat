Fǔ Pí Juǎn (Anhui Tofu Skin Rolls) 腐皮卷

▾

Surprise me with

Tofu skin, the film skimmed from heated soy milk, is a prized ingredient in Anhui and Jiangnan; rolled around mushrooms and vegetables, it becomes a crisp, savory dish.

Method

  1. Stir-fry the mushrooms, wood ear, carrot, cabbage and bamboo shoots in the oil for 3 minutes, season with soy sauce, salt and sesame oil, and cool. Drain off any liquid.
  2. Wipe the tofu skin sheets with a damp cloth to soften them and cut them into rectangles.
  3. Lay a line of filling along one edge, roll up tightly, tucking in the sides, and seal with flour paste.
  4. Heat the oil to 170 C (340 F) and fry the rolls for 3 to 4 minutes, turning, until golden and crisp.
  5. Drain, slice on an angle and serve hot.

Common mistakes

  • Overfilling splits the rolls.
  • Dry tofu skin cracks; keep it damp.

Authentic tips

  • Roll tightly like a spring roll.
  • Steam them first if you want a softer version.

Substitutions

  • Spring roll wrappers replace tofu skin.
  • Steam them first for a softer version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; recrisp in a hot oven.

Freezing: Freeze uncooked rolls for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
