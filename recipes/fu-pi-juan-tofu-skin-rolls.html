Fǔ Pí Juǎn (Anhui Tofu Skin Rolls) 腐皮卷

▾

Surprise me with

Tofu skin, the film skimmed from heated soy milk, is a prized ingredient in Anhui and Jiangnan; rolled around mushrooms and vegetables, it becomes a crisp, savory dish.

  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce

  • 400 ml1.75 cupsNeutral oil for frying

Method

  1. Soak the shiitake and wood ear until soft, squeeze and mince; stir-fry with the carrot and cabbage in a little oil for 3 minutes; season with the soy sauce and cool.
  2. Soften the tofu skin sheets by wiping with a damp cloth and cut each in half.
  3. Spoon filling along one edge of each sheet, fold in the sides and roll up tightly; seal with the cornstarch paste.
  4. Heat the oil to 170 C (340 F) and fry the rolls in batches for 3-4 minutes until golden; drain.
  5. Slice on the diagonal and serve with soy sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Overfilling splits the rolls.
  • Dry tofu skin cracks; keep it damp.

Authentic tips

  • Roll tightly like a spring roll.
  • Steam them first if you want a softer version.

Substitutions

  • Spring roll wrappers replace tofu skin.
  • Steam them first for a softer version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; recrisp in a hot oven.

Freezing: Freeze uncooked rolls for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
