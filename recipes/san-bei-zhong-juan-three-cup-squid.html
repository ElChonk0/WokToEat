Sān Bēi Zhōng Juǎn (Three-Cup Squid) 三杯中捲

▾

Surprise me with

Three-cup cooking, with equal parts soy sauce, rice wine and sesame oil plus basil, is a Taiwanese technique usually used for chicken and applied to squid at coastal restaurants.

  • 600 g1.3 lbSquid, cleaned and cut
  • 45 ml3 tbspSoy sauce
  • 45 ml3 tbspRice wine
  • 45 ml3 tbspSesame oil
  • 50 g3 tbspGinger
  • 40 g3 tbspGarlic
  • 40 g1.5 ozThai basil
  • 15 g1 tbspChilies

Method

  1. Cut the squid into rings or scored pieces and pat dry.
  2. Heat the sesame oil in a wok over medium heat and fry the ginger, garlic and chilies 1 minute.
  3. Turn the heat to high, add the squid, soy sauce and wine, and cook 2-3 minutes until it curls and the sauce thickens.
  4. Take off the heat and fold in the basil.
  5. Serve at once.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking the squid too long makes it rubbery.
  • Burning the sesame oil makes it bitter.

Authentic tips

  • Cook the squid hot and fast.
  • Add a pinch of sugar for balance.

Substitutions

  • Cuttlefish or large shrimp replace squid.
  • Reduce the sesame oil for a lighter dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
