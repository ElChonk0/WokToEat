Niú Ròu Xiàn Bǐng (Taiwanese Pan-Fried Beef Pie) 牛肉餡餅

▾

Surprise me with

Pan-fried meat pies were brought to Taiwan by mainland soldiers after 1949, and beef pies are a street and breakfast food in Taipei.

  • Cook 15 min

Method

  1. Mix the flour with the hot water into a dough, knead 5 minutes and rest, covered, 30 minutes.
  2. Mix the beef, scallions, soy sauce and sesame oil, stirring in one direction.
  3. Divide the dough in 8, flatten each, fill and pinch closed; press into 1.5 cm discs.
  4. Pan-fry in the oil over medium heat for 4 minutes per side until golden.
  5. Cover for 3 minutes to steam through; serve with vinegar.

Common mistakes

  • Overfilling tears the dough.
  • Cooking too fast burns the outside and leaves the inside raw.

Authentic tips

  • Cover the pan briefly to cook the filling through.
  • Serve with soy milk.

Substitutions

  • Ground pork replaces beef.
  • Chopped cabbage in the filling lightens the pie.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot dry pan.

Freezing: Freeze cooked pies for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Soy milk
  • Soup: Pickled vegetables
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot soy milk
