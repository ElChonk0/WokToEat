Bánh Cuốn (Steamed Rice Rolls with Pork)

Banh cuon, silky steamed rice-batter rolls filled with pork and wood ear, are a northern Vietnamese breakfast, a specialty of Thanh Tri in Hanoi.

  • Prep 40 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 80 g3 ozWood ear mushrooms and shallots
  • 60 ml4 tbspFish sauce dipping sauce
  • 15 g1/2 ozFried shallots

Method

  1. Whisk the flours, water and a pinch of salt into a thin batter and rest 30 minutes.
  2. Fry the pork with the chopped wood ear and shallots for 6 minutes until cooked.
  3. Ladle a thin layer of batter onto a steamer cloth or nonstick pan, cover and steam 1-2 minutes until translucent.
  4. Place a spoonful of filling on the sheet, roll up and lift off.
  5. Serve with the dipping sauce and fried shallots.

Common mistakes

  • Thick batter makes thick, rubbery rolls.
  • Overfilling splits the sheets.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with cha lua (Vietnamese sausage) and herbs.
  • Hanoi cooks steam the sheets on a cloth.

Substitutions

  • Store-bought banh cuon sheets save time.
  • Shrimp replaces pork.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day, re-steamed.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Cha lua (pork roll) and herbs
  • Soup: Canh
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
