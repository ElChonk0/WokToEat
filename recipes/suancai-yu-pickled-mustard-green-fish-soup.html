Suāncài Yú (Fish in Pickled Mustard Green Soup) 酸菜鱼

▾

Surprise me with

Suancai yu, fish in sour pickled mustard greens, has roots in Chongqing river-fish cooking and became a national phenomenon in the 2010s; the tangy, slightly spicy broth is a lighter alternative to boiled fish.

  • 700 g1.5 lbFish fillets (grass carp or basa), sliced 6 mm (1/4 in)
  • 300 g10 ozFish bones and head (optional, for the broth)
  • 10 ml2 tspSalt and Shaoxing wine, for the fish
  • 11Egg white
  • 15 g2 tbspCornstarch
  • 250 g9 ozPickled mustard greens (suan cai), rinsed and sliced
  • 30 g1 ozPickled chilies (optional)
  • 40 g1.5 ozGinger and garlic, sliced
  • 1 L4 cupsWater or fish stock
  • 2 g1 tspWhite pepper
  • 3 g1/2 tspSugar
  • 10 g1/3 ozDried chilies
  • 5 g2 tspSichuan peppercorns
  • 20 g3/4 ozGreen onion, sliced
  • 75 ml5 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Toss the fish slices with a pinch of salt, the Shaoxing wine (or a splash of water), the egg white and the cornstarch until coated in a thin film. Velveting keeps the delicate fish whole and silky in the broth.
  2. Squeeze the rinsed pickled greens dry. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a pot over medium heat and fry the fish bones (if using) for 2 minutes, then the ginger, garlic, pickled greens and pickled chilies for 3 minutes until the greens smell sour and fragrant.
  3. Add the water, bring to a boil and simmer for 10 minutes; the broth turns cloudy and tangy. Season with white pepper and sugar, and taste before adding salt, since the pickles are salty.
  4. Lift out the bones. Lower the heat to a gentle simmer and add the fish a slice at a time. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes until just opaque, then pour into a deep bowl.
  5. Top with the dried chilies, peppercorns and green onion. Heat the remaining oil until smoking and pour it over to release their aroma.

Common mistakes

  • Rinsing the pickled greens too much, which removes their characteristic sourness.
  • Letting the broth boil hard once the fish is in.

Authentic tips

  • Fish bones in the broth give it body and a milky look.

Substitutions

  • Cod, tilapia or catfish replace grass carp.
  • A little sugar softens unusually sharp pickled greens.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated; reheat gently so the fish stays intact.

Freezing: Freeze the broth without the fish for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Orange slices
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
