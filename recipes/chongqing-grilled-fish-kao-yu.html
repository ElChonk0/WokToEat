Chongqing Grilled Fish 重庆烤鱼

Chongqing grilled fish emerged in Wanzhou in the 1990s: a whole river fish is roasted, then simmered in a spicy mala sauce with vegetables in a shared pan over a flame.

  • Prep 25 min
  • Cook 30 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 1.2 kg2.6 lbWhole fish (grass carp or tilapia), butterflied
  • 30 g2 tbspSalt, white pepper and Shaoxing wine for the marinade
  • 45 g3 tbspDoubanjiang
  • 25 g1 ozDried chilies
  • 10 g1 tbspSichuan peppercorns
  • 60 g4 tbspGarlic and ginger
  • 400 g14 ozLotus root, potato and bean sprouts
  • 300 ml1.25 cupsStock
  • 80 ml5 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Marinate the fish with salt, pepper and wine for 20 minutes.
  2. Roast at 220 C (425 F) for 20 minutes until the skin is crisp.
  3. Fry the doubanjiang, garlic, ginger, chilies and peppercorns in oil, then add the stock and vegetables and simmer 8 minutes.
  4. Place the fish on top and spoon the sauce around it; serve in the pan.

Common mistakes

  • Under-roasting the fish, which leaves the skin flabby in the sauce.
  • Adding too much stock, which dilutes the concentrated flavor.

Authentic tips

  • Sturdy vegetables go in first; add leafy ones last.

Substitutions

  • Tilapia or catfish replace carp.
  • Reduce the chilies for a milder dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated; the skin softens.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Cold cucumber salad
  • Dessert: Chilled watermelon
  • Beverage: Cold beer
