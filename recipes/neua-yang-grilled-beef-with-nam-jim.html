Neua Yang (Thai Grilled Beef with Jaew Dipping Sauce)

▾

Surprise me with

Neua yang, grilled marinated beef, is eaten across Thailand, especially Isaan, with a spicy tamarind-chili dipping sauce called nam jim jaew.

  • Cook 12 min

Method

  1. Pound the garlic and coriander root and mix with the soy sauce, oyster sauce and sugar; marinate the beef 1 hour.
  2. Grill over high heat for 3 minutes per side for medium-rare.
  3. Mix the tamarind, lime, fish sauce, chili flakes and toasted rice powder for the dipping sauce.
  4. Rest the beef 5 minutes.
  5. Slice across the grain and serve with the sauce and sticky rice.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking too long makes the beef tough.
  • Slicing with the grain leaves it chewy.

Authentic tips

  • Add fresh herbs to the dip.
  • Flank is traditional.

Substitutions

  • Flank steak takes the marinade best.
  • No grill: broil on a rack about 8 cm (3 in) from the element for 3 to 4 minutes per side for medium-rare, then rest 5 minutes before slicing; a hot ridged pan also works.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; slice cold into salad.

Freezing: Freezes cooked beef for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sticky rice and papaya salad
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Singha beer
