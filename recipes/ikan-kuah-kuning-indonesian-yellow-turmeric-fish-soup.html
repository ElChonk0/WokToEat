Ikan Kuah Kuning (Indonesian Yellow Turmeric Fish Soup)

Kuah kuning, a light turmeric-yellow broth with lemongrass and galangal, is cooked across eastern Indonesia, especially Maluku and Papua, with local fish.

Method

  1. Blend the shallots, garlic, ginger, turmeric and candlenuts into a smooth paste with a little water.
  2. Heat the oil in a pot over medium heat and fry the paste with the lemongrass, galangal and lime leaves for 4 to 5 minutes until fragrant.
  3. Add the water and salt and bring to a simmer for 8 minutes.
  4. Add the fish and chilies and simmer gently for 6 to 8 minutes until cooked.
  5. Stir in the lime juice and basil and serve with rice.

Cook’s notes

Written for Chilean sea bass. Indonesian cooks use snapper or tuna.

Common mistakes

  • A hard boil makes the broth cloudy and the fish dry.
  • Skipping the paste frying leaves a raw spice taste.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with sambal on the side.
  • Fresh turmeric gives a brighter color than powder.

Substitutions

  • Cod loin or haddock replaces the sea bass.
  • Ground turmeric (3 g) replaces fresh turmeric.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and sambal
  • Soup: The recipe is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Es teh
