Zhū Jiǎo Fàn (Taiwanese Braised Pig's Trotter Rice) 豬腳飯

▾

Surprise me with

Braised pig's trotter rice is a Taiwanese comfort food, associated with wishes for good fortune, sold at stalls across the island.

  • Cook 2 hr
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 90 ml6 tbspSoy sauce
  • 30 g2 tbspRock sugar
  • 8 g3 tspStar anise and cinnamon
  • 60 ml4 tbspRice wine
  • 500 g17 ozCooked rice
  • 150 g5 ozMustard greens
  • 1 L4 cupsWater

Method

  1. Chop the trotters, cover with cold water, boil 5 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  2. Simmer in the soy sauce, rock sugar, wine, spices and 1 L water, covered, for 90 minutes until tender.
  3. Lift out the trotters and skim the fat from the sauce.
  4. Spoon the trotters and sauce over the rice.
  5. Serve with blanched mustard greens.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard toughens the skin.
  • Skipping the blanching clouds the sauce.

Authentic tips

  • It is eaten for luck, often with mian xian noodles.
  • A pressure cooker takes 40 minutes.

Substitutions

  • Pork hock replaces trotters.
  • A pressure cooker cuts the braise to 50 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; the sauce sets.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Blanched mustard greens
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Oolong tea
