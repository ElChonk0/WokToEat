Gaeng Keow Wan Tofu (Thai Green Curry with Tofu)

▾

Surprise me with

Gaeng keow wan, sweet green curry, is a central Thai curry that dates to the early 20th century; tofu and Thai eggplant make a vegetarian version.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 400 ml1.75 cupsCoconut milk
  • 250 g9 ozThai eggplant and bamboo shoots
  • 44Kaffir lime leaves
  • 15 ml1 tbspSoy sauce
  • 15 g1 tbspPalm sugar
  • 30 g1 ozThai basil

Method

  1. Press the tofu 15 minutes and cut into cubes.
  2. Fry the green curry paste in the thick coconut cream for 2 minutes until the oil separates.
  3. Add the remaining coconut milk, eggplant and bamboo shoots and simmer 8 minutes.
  4. Add the tofu, soy sauce and sugar and simmer 8 minutes more.
  5. Take off the heat and add the lime leaves and basil.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard splits the coconut milk.
  • Overcooking the eggplant makes it mushy.

Authentic tips

  • Check the paste: many commercial pastes contain shrimp paste.
  • Serve with jasmine rice.

Substitutions

  • Store-bought paste saves time; check for shrimp paste.
  • Zucchini and bamboo shoots replace Thai eggplant.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; thin with coconut milk.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Jasmine rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Mango sticky rice
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
