Gaeng Keow Wan Tofu (Thai Green Curry with Tofu)

▾

Surprise me with

Gaeng keow wan, sweet green curry, is a central Thai curry that dates to the early 20th century; tofu and Thai eggplant make a vegetarian version.

  • Cook 20 min

Method

  1. Brown the tofu in the oil in a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat for 5 to 6 minutes, turning, until golden on several sides. Browning keeps it from falling apart in the curry.
  2. Heat the thick top of the coconut milk in a pot over medium heat for 3 to 5 minutes until the oil separates, then fry the curry paste in it for 2 to 3 minutes until fragrant. Check the label: many curry pastes contain shrimp paste.
  3. Add the remaining coconut milk and the stock, bring to a gentle simmer and add the eggplant, bamboo shoots and lime leaves. Simmer for 6 to 8 minutes until the eggplant is tender.
  4. Add the tofu and season with soy sauce, salt and palm sugar, standing in for fish sauce.
  5. Turn off the heat, stir in the basil and chili and serve with jasmine rice.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard splits the coconut milk.
  • Overcooking the eggplant makes it mushy.

Authentic tips

  • Check the paste: many commercial pastes contain shrimp paste.
  • Serve with jasmine rice.

Substitutions

  • Store-bought paste saves time; check for shrimp paste.
  • Zucchini and bamboo shoots replace Thai eggplant.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; thin with coconut milk.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Jasmine rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Mango sticky rice
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
