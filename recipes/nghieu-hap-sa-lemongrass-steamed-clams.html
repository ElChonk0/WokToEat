Nghêu Hấp Sả (Lemongrass Steamed Clams)

▾

Surprise me with

Clams steamed with lemongrass and chili are a favorite southern Vietnamese beer snack and seafood-restaurant dish.

  • Cook 8 min

  • 15 ml1 tbspFish sauce
  • 20 g2/3 ozThai basil
  • 120 ml1/2 cupWater

Method

  1. Soak the clams in salted water for 30 minutes to purge sand; scrub and rinse.
  2. Boil the lemongrass and ginger with the water in a wide pot 3 minutes.
  3. Add the clams, cover and steam 4-5 minutes until they open.
  4. Discard any that stay closed.
  5. Add the chilies, fish sauce and basil, toss and serve with the broth.

Common mistakes

  • Not purging leaves grit.
  • Overcooking makes the clams tough.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with lime, salt and pepper.
  • Lemongrass should be bruised before using.

Substitutions

  • Mussels replace clams.
  • Add beer to the steaming liquid.

Storage

Leftovers: Eat immediately.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Baguette to soak up the broth
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
