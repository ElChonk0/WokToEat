Pepes Ikan (Fish Steamed in Banana Leaf)

▾

Surprise me with

Pepes, spiced fish wrapped in banana leaf and steamed or grilled, is a Sundanese (West Java) specialty.

  • Cook 30 min

  • 700 g1.5 lbFish fillets (mackerel or tilapia)
  • 44Banana leaves
  • 60 g2 ozShallot and garlic
  • 20 g3/4 ozChilies
  • 20 g3/4 ozTurmeric and galangal
  • 100 g3.5 ozLemongrass, tomato, basil
  • 6 g1 tspSalt
  • 15 ml1 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Pound the shallot, garlic, chilies, turmeric, galangal and salt into a paste with the oil.
  2. Soften the banana leaves in boiling water for 30 seconds or briefly in a hot dry skillet, then cut into squares.
  3. Coat the fish in the paste, place on a leaf with the lemongrass, tomato and basil, and fold into a packet, securing with toothpicks.
  4. Steam over high heat for 25 minutes.
  5. Grill briefly for 2-3 minutes per side for char and smoke; serve in the leaf.

Common mistakes

  • Unsoftened leaves tear.
  • Overcooking dries the fish.

Authentic tips

  • Foil works if you can't find leaves.
  • Serve with rice and raw vegetables.

Substitutions

  • Foil replaces banana leaves, with less aroma.
  • Tilapia or mackerel both work.
  • No grill: after steaming, broil the packets 2 to 3 minutes per side, or bake at 200°C (400°F) for 10 minutes, for char and smoke.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and raw vegetables
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Es teh
