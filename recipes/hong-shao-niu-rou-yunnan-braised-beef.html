Yúnnán Hóng Shāo Niú Ròu (Yunnan Red-Braised Beef) 云南红烧牛肉

▾

Surprise me with

Beef braised with Yunnan chilies, ginger and local soy is a mountain-village dish, rich and slightly spicy.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 2 hr
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 700 g1.5 lbBeef brisket, cubed
  • 10 g1/3 ozDried chilies
  • 60 ml4 tbspSoy sauce
  • 20 g2 tbspRock sugar
  • 60 g4 tbspGinger, scallion, garlic
  • 22Star anise
  • 800 ml3.5 cupsWater

Method

  1. Cover the beef with cold water, boil 5 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  2. Melt the rock sugar in a heavy pot over low heat until amber, add the beef and turn to coat.
  3. Add the ginger, scallion, garlic, dried chilies, star anise and soy sauce, then the water.
  4. Bring to a boil, cover and simmer on low for 90 minutes until very tender.
  5. Uncover and boil 8-10 minutes until the sauce is thick.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard toughens the beef.
  • Burning the sugar makes the dish bitter.

Authentic tips

  • Better the next day.
  • Add daikon or potatoes in the last 30 minutes.

Substitutions

  • Shank replaces brisket.
  • Add daikon for the last 30 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Pu'er tea
