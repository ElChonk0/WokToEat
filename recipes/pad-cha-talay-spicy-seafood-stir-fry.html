Pad Cha Talay (Spicy Seafood Stir-Fry with Holy Basil)

▾

Surprise me with

Pad cha talay is a fiery Thai seafood stir-fry with krachai (fingerroot), peppercorns and basil, built for coastal restaurants.

  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 700 g1.5 lbMixed seafood (shrimp, squid, mussels)
  • 40 g1.5 ozKrachai or galangal
  • 15 g1/2 ozGreen peppercorns
  • 20 g2/3 ozBird's eye chilies
  • 30 g2 tbspGarlic
  • 40 g1.5 ozHoly basil
  • 30 ml2 tbspFish sauce
  • 30 ml2 tbspOyster sauce
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Pound the garlic, chilies and krachai.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok over very high heat and fry the paste with the peppercorns 30 seconds.
  3. Add the seafood and sauces and cook 3 minutes.
  4. Take off the heat and fold in the basil.
  5. Serve at once.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking makes seafood rubbery.
  • Crowding the wok steams the seafood.

Authentic tips

  • Cook in two batches for best char.
  • Fresh green peppercorns add a distinctive aroma.

Substitutions

  • Any mix of shrimp, squid and mussels works.
  • Fresh green peppercorns add a distinctive aroma.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Jasmine rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Singha beer
