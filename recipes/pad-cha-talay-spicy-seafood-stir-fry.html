Pad Cha Talay (Spicy Seafood Stir-Fry with Holy Basil)

▾

Surprise me with

Pad cha talay is a fiery Thai seafood stir-fry with krachai (fingerroot), peppercorns and basil, built for coastal restaurants.

  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 30 ml2 tbspOyster sauce
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 30 ml2 tbspWater
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Score the squid in a crosshatch and cut into pieces; pat all the seafood dry. Pound the garlic and chilies into a coarse paste.
  2. Heat the oil in a flat-bottom wok over medium-high heat and fry the paste for 30 seconds, then add the krachai and peppercorns and stir for 30 seconds until fragrant.
  3. Raise the heat to high, add the mussels and shrimp and stir-fry for 1 minute, then add the squid.
  4. Add the oyster sauce, fish sauce, sugar and water and toss for 1 to 2 minutes, until the shrimp are pink, the mussels open and the squid curls. Seafood cooks fast; pull it as soon as it is done.
  5. Turn off the heat, toss in the lime leaves and holy basil and serve with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking makes seafood rubbery.
  • Crowding the wok steams the seafood.

Authentic tips

  • Cook in two batches for best char.
  • Fresh green peppercorns add a distinctive aroma.

Substitutions

  • Any mix of shrimp, squid and mussels works.
  • Fresh green peppercorns add a distinctive aroma.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Jasmine rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Singha beer
