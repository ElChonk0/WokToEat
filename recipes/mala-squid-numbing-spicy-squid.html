Mala Squid (Numbing-Spicy Stir-Fried Squid) 麻辣鱿鱼

▾

Surprise me with

Stir-fried squid with chili and Sichuan pepper is a popular street and restaurant dish across Sichuan and Chongqing, showing the region's mala flavor on quickly cooked seafood.

  • Cook 8 min

  • 600 g1.3 lbSquid, cleaned and scored
  • 15 g1/2 ozDried chilies
  • 5 g2 tspSichuan peppercorns
  • 30 g2 tbspGarlic and ginger
  • 200 g7 ozCelery and green peppers, sliced
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Score the squid body in a crosshatch pattern and cut into 3 cm pieces. Slice the celery and green peppers.
  2. Blanch the squid in boiling water for 15 seconds until it curls; drain very well and pat dry.
  3. Heat the oil in a wok over medium-high heat and fry the chilies and peppercorns for 20 seconds until fragrant but not black.
  4. Add the garlic, ginger, celery and peppers and stir-fry 1 minute over high heat.
  5. Add the squid, soy sauce and wine and toss over high heat for 30 seconds; serve immediately.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking the squid longer than a minute turns it rubbery.
  • Wet squid cools the pan and steams instead of stir-frying.

Authentic tips

  • Scoring helps it curl and take up the sauce.
  • A hot wok is essential; heat it until smoking before adding the oil.

Substitutions

  • Cuttlefish or shrimp replace the squid.
  • Fried tofu puffs add bulk.

Storage

Leftovers: Eat immediately; reheated squid turns rubbery.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
