Satay Ayam (Malaysian Chicken Satay)

Satay is a hawker and street staple of Malaysia and Singapore; Malay satay is often sweeter and more fragrant with lemongrass and turmeric.

  • Prep 30 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Blend the lemongrass, galangal, turmeric, shallot, garlic and palm sugar into a paste.
  2. Marinate the cubed chicken for at least 2 hours.
  3. Thread onto soaked bamboo skewers.
  4. Grill over medium-high heat 8-10 minutes, turning and basting with oil, until charred and cooked through.
  5. Serve with the peanut sauce, cucumber and onion.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking dries the chicken.
  • Dry skewers burn; soak them.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with ketupat (rice cakes).
  • Brush with coconut oil while grilling.

Substitutions

  • Beef or lamb take the same marinade.
  • Peanut butter plus chili replaces homemade peanut sauce.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat under a broiler.

Freezing: Freeze raw skewers for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Ketupat and onion-cucumber

  • Dessert: Cendol
