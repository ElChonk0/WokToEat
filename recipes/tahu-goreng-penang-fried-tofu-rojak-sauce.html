Tahu Goreng (Malaysian Fried Tofu with Peanut Sauce)

▾

Surprise me with

Tau fu goreng, fried tofu with sweet chili and peanut sauce, is a Malaysian hawker snack found at Penang and Singapore food centers.

Method

  1. Pat the tofu very dry and sprinkle with salt. Dry tofu spits less and forms a crisper skin.
  2. Pound the chilies and garlic into a paste, then simmer them with the tamarind, palm sugar, kecap manis, water and sauce salt for 3 minutes. Stir in most of the peanuts. The sauce should be thick, sweet, tangy and spicy.
  3. Heat the oil in a flat-bottom wok or Dutch oven to 180 C (350 F) and deep-fry the tofu for 6 to 8 minutes, turning, until deep golden and crisp outside and soft inside. Drain on a rack.
  4. Cut each piece in half diagonally and slit a pocket into the cut side. Stuff the pockets with cucumber and bean sprouts.
  5. Pour the sauce over and scatter with the remaining peanuts. Serve at once, while the tofu is crisp.

Common mistakes

  • Wet tofu doesn't crisp.
  • Sauce too early makes the tofu soggy.

Authentic tips

  • Serve at once.
  • Add pineapple for a rojak twist.

Substitutions

  • Air-fry at 200 C (400 F) for a lighter dish.
  • Peanut butter plus chili replaces homemade sauce.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.
