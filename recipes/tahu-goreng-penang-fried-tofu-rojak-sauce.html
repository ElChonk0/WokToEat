Tahu Goreng (Malaysian Fried Tofu with Peanut Sauce)

▾

Surprise me with

Tau fu goreng, fried tofu with sweet chili and peanut sauce, is a Malaysian hawker snack found at Penang and Singapore food centers.

  • Cook 10 min

  • 200 g7 ozCucumber and bean sprouts
  • 120 ml1/2 cupPeanut sauce
  • 45 ml3 tbspSweet chili sauce
  • 15 g1/2 ozChilies
  • 40 g1.5 ozCrushed peanuts
  • 300 ml1.25 cupsNeutral oil for frying

Method

  1. Press the tofu 15 minutes and cut into cubes.
  2. Fry in oil at 180 C (350 F) for 5 minutes until crisp and golden; drain.
  3. Arrange on a plate with the cucumber and bean sprouts.
  4. Pour over the peanut sauce and sweet chili sauce.
  5. Sprinkle with the chilies and crushed peanuts.

Common mistakes

  • Wet tofu doesn't crisp.
  • Sauce too early makes the tofu soggy.

Authentic tips

  • Serve at once.
  • Add pineapple for a rojak twist.

Substitutions

  • Air-fry at 200 C (400 F) for a lighter dish.
  • Peanut butter plus chili replaces homemade sauce.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
