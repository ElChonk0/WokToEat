Lontong Sayur (Rice Cakes in Coconut Vegetable Curry)

▾

Surprise me with

Lontong sayur is an Indonesian breakfast dish of compressed rice cakes in a coconut milk vegetable curry, usually served with fried tofu, egg and crackers.

Method

  1. Blend the shallots, garlic, candlenuts and turmeric into a smooth paste.
  2. Heat the oil in a pot over medium heat and fry the paste with the galangal, lemongrass and bay leaves for 6 to 8 minutes, until fragrant and the oil separates.
  3. Add the water and bring to a boil, then add the chayote and simmer for 5 minutes. Add the long beans and tofu.
  4. Pour in the coconut milk, season with salt and sugar and simmer gently, stirring often so it does not split, for 8 to 10 minutes until the vegetables are tender.
  5. Divide the sliced lontong among bowls, ladle over the curry and add a halved egg, fried shallots and sambal. A favorite Betawi and Padang breakfast.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling the coconut milk hard splits it; keep it at a gentle simmer.
  • Over-cooking the vegetables turns them mushy; add them in stages.

Authentic tips

  • Buy ready-made lontong or compress cooked rice in banana leaf or a clean container.
  • Serve with crackers for crunch.

Substitutions

  • Ketupat or cooked rice replaces lontong.
  • Tempeh replaces tofu.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator; reheat the curry gently.

Freezing: The curry freezes for 1 month without the rice cakes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Prawn crackers (krupuk)
  • Soup: Not needed; the dish is a curry soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot sweet tea
