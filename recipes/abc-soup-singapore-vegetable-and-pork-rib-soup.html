ABC Soup (Singapore Vegetable and Pork Rib Soup)

▾

Surprise me with

ABC soup is a Singapore and Malaysian home soup of pork ribs simmered with carrot, potato, tomato, corn and onion; the name is said to come from the vitamins A, B and C.

Method

  1. Put the ribs in a pot of cold water, bring to a boil and boil for 5 minutes, then drain and rinse them under cold water. Blanching keeps the soup clear and clean-tasting.
  2. Return the ribs to the cleaned pot with the water, ginger, red dates, onion, carrot and corn. Bring to a boil, skim, then lower to a gentle simmer, partly covered.
  3. Simmer for 45 minutes, then add the potatoes and simmer for 30 to 40 minutes more, until the ribs are tender and the potatoes are soft enough to break with a spoon; a few crumbling potatoes thicken the broth slightly.
  4. Add the tomatoes for the last 10 minutes, so they soften and tint the soup without falling apart entirely.
  5. Season with the salt and white pepper. The soup is sweet from the vegetables and dates, and is even better made a few hours ahead and reheated, or held hot in a thermal pot.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the potatoes too early makes them dissolve and the soup starchy.
  • Boiling hard makes the broth cloudy and the ribs tough.

Authentic tips

  • Use mature carrots and sweet tomatoes for the best flavor.
  • Add the tomatoes with the potatoes for a bright acidity.

Substitutions

  • Chicken pieces replace ribs.
  • Celery and cabbage can replace some of the other vegetables.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and chili-soy dip
  • Soup: The dish is a soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot water or barley water
