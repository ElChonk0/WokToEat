Sate Ayam (Chicken Satay with Peanut Sauce)

▾

Surprise me with

Satay, grilled skewered meat, is Indonesia's best-known street food, believed to have developed from Indian kebabs sold by Javanese traders; chicken with peanut sauce is the most popular version.

Method

  1. Mix the marinade kecap manis, shallots, garlic, coriander, turmeric and oil and toss with the chicken. Marinate for at least 1 hour.
  2. Make the peanut sauce: fry the garlic and chilies in a little oil for 2 minutes, then add the ground peanuts, palm sugar and water and simmer, stirring, for 5 to 8 minutes until thick. Season with a pinch of salt, a spoon of kecap manis and a squeeze of lime.
  3. Thread the chicken onto the soaked skewers, 4 or 5 pieces each, keeping the bottom of the skewer bare.
  4. Grill over hot charcoal or a grill pan over high heat for 6 to 8 minutes, turning and brushing with the basting kecap manis mixed with oil, until charred at the edges and cooked through.
  5. Pour the peanut sauce over and top with a drizzle of kecap manis, fried shallots and lime. Serve with lontong or rice.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking dries the chicken; thigh is much more forgiving than breast.
  • Burning the sugar in the marinade; keep the grill medium.

Authentic tips

  • Cut the pieces evenly so they cook at the same rate.
  • Serve with sliced shallot, cucumber and sambal.

Substitutions

  • Pork or beef take the same marinade.
  • Peanut butter thinned with coconut milk replaces homemade sauce.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat under a broiler.

Freezing: Freeze raw skewered chicken for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Lontong or steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Es campur
  • Beverage: Es teh
