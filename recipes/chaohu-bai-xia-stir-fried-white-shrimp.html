Cháohú Bái Xiā (Chaohu White Shrimp) 巢湖白虾

▾

Surprise me with

Small translucent white shrimp from Chaohu Lake are prized for their sweetness and are usually cooked very simply.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 8 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 600 g1.3 lbSmall white shrimp
  • 20 g1 tbspGinger
  • 30 g2 tbspScallion
  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 5 g1 tspSalt
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Rinse the shrimp and drain well; pat dry.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok over high heat and fry the ginger 10 seconds.
  3. Add the shrimp and stir-fry 1 minute until they turn pink.
  4. Add the wine and salt and toss 1 minute more; finish with the scallions.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking makes the small shrimp tough.
  • Too much seasoning masks their sweetness.

Authentic tips

  • The shrimp are eaten shell and all when small.
  • Fresh shrimp need very little seasoning.

Substitutions

  • Small peeled shrimp replace whole river shrimp.
  • Add a little rice vinegar at the end.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
