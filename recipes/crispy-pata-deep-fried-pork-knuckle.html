Crispy Pata (Deep-Fried Pork Knuckle)

▾

Surprise me with

Crispy pata, pork knuckle boiled until tender and then deep-fried until the skin blisters, is a Filipino party dish served with vinegar-soy dip.

  • Cook 2.5 hr

  • 1.2 kg2.6 lbPork knuckle (pata)
  • 40 g1.5 ozGarlic, bay leaves, peppercorns
  • 20 g1.5 tbspSalt
  • 120 ml1/2 cupVinegar-soy dip
  • 1.5 L6 cupsNeutral oil for frying
  • 100 g3.5 ozOnion

Method

  1. Boil the knuckle with the garlic, bay leaves, peppercorns, onion and salt in enough water to cover for 90 minutes until tender but intact.
  2. Lift out carefully, pat dry and refrigerate uncovered overnight to dry the skin.
  3. Heat the oil to 180 C (350 F) in a deep pot.
  4. Lower in the pata (very carefully; it spatters) and fry 10-15 minutes, turning, until the skin blisters and turns golden.
  5. Chop and serve at once with the dip.

Common mistakes

  • Wet skin makes the hot oil spatter violently; dry it well and use a lid or splatter screen.
  • Skipping the overnight rest gives chewy rather than crisp skin.

Authentic tips

  • An air fryer or oven at 230 C (450 F) is a safer alternative.
  • Serve with a vinegar-soy dip and lechon sauce.

Substitutions

  • An air fryer or oven at 230 C (450 F) is safer than deep-frying.
  • Pork hock replaces pata.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; recrisp in a hot oven.

Freezing: Freezes the boiled knuckle for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and atchara
  • Soup: Sinigang
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
