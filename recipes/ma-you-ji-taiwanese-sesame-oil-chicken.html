Má Yóu Jī (Taiwanese Sesame Oil Chicken) 麻油雞

▾

Surprise me with

Ma you ji is a Taiwanese chicken soup of old ginger fried in black sesame oil and simmered with rice wine, traditionally eaten in winter and by new mothers.

  • Cook 45 min

  • 900 g2 lbChicken thighs, bone-in, cut into chunks
  • 60 ml1/4 cupBlack sesame oil
  • 100 g3.5 ozOld ginger, sliced
  • 500 ml2 cupsRice wine (Taiwanese mi jiu)
  • 500 ml2 cupsWater
  • 88Dried red dates
  • 10 g1 tbspGoji berries
  • 6 g1 tspSalt

Method

  1. Heat the sesame oil in a heavy pot over low heat and fry the ginger slices for 5 to 6 minutes until golden at the edges and fragrant. Do not let them blacken.
  2. Raise the heat to medium-high, add the chicken and stir-fry for 5 minutes until the surface turns white and lightly golden.
  3. Pour in the rice wine and water, add the red dates, and bring to a boil.
  4. Reduce to a simmer, cover and cook for 30 minutes, until the chicken is tender and the alcohol smell has cooked off.
  5. Add the goji berries and salt, simmer for 3 minutes, and serve in bowls.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the ginger or sesame oil makes the broth bitter; keep the heat low at the start.
  • Cooking over very high heat after adding the wine can scorch it; simmer gently.

Authentic tips

  • Use black sesame oil, which has a deeper, smokier flavor than toasted white sesame oil.
  • Leave the soup to sit for an hour: the flavor deepens.

Substitutions

  • Shaoxing wine replaces rice wine; reduce it to 300 ml and add 200 ml more water.
  • Boneless thighs shorten the simmer to 20 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; reheat gently.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Mian xian (thin wheat noodles) or steamed rice
  • Soup: The dish is a soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot ginger tea
