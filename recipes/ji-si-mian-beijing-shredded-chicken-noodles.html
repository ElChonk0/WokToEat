Jī Sī Miàn (Shredded Chicken Noodles) 鸡丝面

▾

Surprise me with

Chicken noodle soup in a clear, savory broth is a northern Chinese comfort dish, common in Beijing's noodle shops.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 25 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Simmer the chicken in the water with the ginger for 20 minutes until cooked; lift out and shred.
  2. Season the broth with the soy sauce and a pinch of salt and keep it hot.
  3. Cook the noodles in a separate pot of boiling water until just tender; drain and divide among bowls.
  4. Add the cabbage to the broth for 2 minutes until tender.
  5. Ladle the broth and cabbage over the noodles, top with chicken, scallion and a few drops of sesame oil.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking the noodles in the broth clouds it and makes them mushy.
  • A boiling broth makes the chicken tough.

Authentic tips

  • Strain the broth for clarity.
  • Add a spoonful of chili oil at the table.

Substitutions

  • Bone-in chicken makes a richer broth; poach 15 minutes longer.
  • Spinach replaces napa cabbage.

Storage

Leftovers: Store noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 3 days.

Freezing: Freeze the broth for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled vegetables
  • Soup: Not needed; the noodle soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
