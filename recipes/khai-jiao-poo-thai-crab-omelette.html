Khai Jiao Poo (Thai Crab Omelette) ไข่เจียวปู

▾

Surprise me with

Khai jiao poo is a Thai omelette of eggs deep-fried until puffed and golden around a filling of crab meat; it is a staple of Bangkok restaurants and street stalls.

Method

  1. Beat the eggs with the fish sauce, soy sauce, white pepper and cornstarch mixed with a tablespoon of water. Beat hard for a minute to whip in air; the cornstarch helps the omelet puff and stay crisp.
  2. Fold in the crab and green onion.
  3. Heat the oil in a flat-bottom wok over high heat until it just starts to smoke, about 200 C (390 F). A lot of very hot oil is what makes a Thai omelet puff up with lacy, crisp edges.
  4. Hold the bowl high and pour the eggs into the center of the oil in a steady stream. They will puff dramatically. Fry for 1 to 2 minutes, pushing the edges in, until the bottom is golden.
  5. Flip it carefully and fry for 30 to 60 seconds more until golden and crisp. Drain on paper towels and serve over rice with Sriracha or sweet chili sauce.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Oil that is not hot enough gives a flat, greasy omelette; heat it to 190 C.
  • Overmixing knocks out the air; fold the crab in gently.

Authentic tips

  • A drop of fish sauce poured from a height makes the omelette lighter.
  • Use fresh jumbo lump crab if you can.

Substitutions

  • Shrimp or minced pork replaces crab.
  • Use 60 ml oil in a pan for a shallow-fried version.

Storage

Leftovers: Best eaten at once.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed jasmine rice and sriracha
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced Thai tea
