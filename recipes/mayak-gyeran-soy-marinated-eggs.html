Mayak Gyeran (Soy-Marinated Eggs) 마약계란

▾

Surprise me with

Mayak gyeran, jammy soft-boiled eggs steeped in sweet soy with chili and scallion, became a viral Korean side dish in the 2010s; "mayak" is slang for addictive.

Method

  1. Bring a pot of water to a boil, lower in the cold eggs and boil for 6.5 minutes for jammy yolks.
  2. Move the eggs into ice water for 5 minutes, then peel them carefully.
  3. Stir the soy sauce, water, sugar and syrup until the sugar dissolves, then add the garlic, green onion, chilies, onion, sesame seeds and sesame oil.
  4. Put the eggs in a container that fits them snugly and pour the marinade over so they are covered.
  5. Refrigerate for at least 6 hours, ideally overnight, turning once. Serve halved over hot rice with a spoon of the marinade.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Eggs straight from the refrigerator into boiling water can crack; lower them gently with a spoon.
  • Leaving them more than 3 days makes the whites rubbery and very salty.

Authentic tips

  • Serve over rice with a little marinade, sesame oil and a sheet of seaweed for a two-minute meal.
  • Reuse the marinade once for a second batch within a week.

Substitutions

  • Quail eggs: boil 2.5 minutes and marinate 4 hours.
  • Halve the chilies for a milder batch.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator in the marinade.

Freezing: Not recommended; egg whites turn rubbery.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and roasted seaweed
  • Soup: Kongnamul guk (bean sprout soup)
  • Dessert: Fresh strawberries
  • Beverage: Barley tea
