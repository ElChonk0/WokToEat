Lo Mai Fan (Cantonese Sticky Rice with Chinese Sausage) 糯米饭

Lo mai fan, glutinous rice stir-fried with Chinese sausage, dried shrimp and mushrooms, is a Cantonese winter dish and a Lunar New Year and Thanksgiving-table favorite in Chinese American families.

  • Cook 45 min

Method

  1. Drain the rice well. Spread it on a cloth-lined steamer and steam over high heat for 25 minutes until mostly tender.
  2. Heat the oil in a flat-bottom wok over medium heat and fry the sausage for 2 minutes until its fat runs, then the shrimp, mushrooms and shallots for 2 minutes.
  3. Add the steamed rice and toss, then add the soy sauces, oyster sauce and sugar and toss until evenly colored.
  4. Sprinkle in the mushroom water a little at a time, tossing and pressing over low heat for 8 to 10 minutes, until the rice is fully tender and sticky.
  5. Fold in the green onion and cilantro and serve.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Adding all the liquid at once makes it mushy; sprinkle it in gradually.
  • Using high heat at the end burns the sticky rice onto the wok.

Authentic tips

  • A rice cooker can do the whole thing: add everything with 300 ml liquid and use the normal setting.
  • Toasted peanuts are a common addition.

Substitutions

  • Chicken thigh, diced, replaces some of the sausage.
  • Add chestnuts for a festive version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days in the refrigerator; re-steam or microwave with a sprinkle of water.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Garlic gai lan
  • Soup: Dried scallop and chicken soup
  • Dessert: Sweet red bean soup
  • Beverage: Pu-erh tea
