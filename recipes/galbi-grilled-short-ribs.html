Galbi (Grilled Short Ribs) 갈비

▾

Surprise me with

Galbi grills marinated short ribs, traditionally scored (flanken-cut) to maximize marinade absorption and grilling surface — a centerpiece dish of Korean barbecue restaurants and celebratory meals.

Method

  1. Soak the ribs in cold water for 30 minutes to draw out blood and bone dust, then rinse and pat dry. Trim off excess fat. If using thick English-cut ribs, butterfly the meat into a long, thin strip 6 mm to 1 cm (1/4 to 1/2 in) thick, still attached to the bone.
  2. Mix the soy sauce, water, sugar, honey, rice wine, pear, onion, garlic, ginger, sesame oil and pepper until the sugar dissolves, then stir in the green onion.
  3. Coat the ribs in the marinade, cover and refrigerate for at least 6 hours, ideally overnight; thicker cuts need longer.
  4. Heat a grill to medium-high, or a broiler with the rack about 15 cm (6 in) from the heat. Shake off the excess marinade, which burns.
  5. Grill for 2 to 3 minutes per side, turning once, until charred at the edges and just cooked through. Under a broiler, allow 5 to 6 minutes on the first side and 3 to 4 on the second. Charcoal gives the classic smoky flavor, but gas or a grill pan works.
  6. Snip the meat into pieces with kitchen scissors and serve with rice, lettuce, ssamjang and kimchi.

Common mistakes

  • Grilling over too high heat, which burns the sugary marinade before the meat cooks through.
  • Not scoring the ribs, which slows marinade penetration and can make the meat tougher to eat off the bone.

Authentic tips

  • Flanken-cut short ribs (cut across the bone into thin strips) are the traditional cut for galbi and grill much faster than English-cut ribs.
  • Longer marinating (up to 24 hours) gives noticeably more tender, flavorful results.

Substitutions

  • Boneless short rib or even flank steak can work with the same marinade, adjusting grilling time.
  • No grill: a hot cast-iron pan or broiler both work as substitutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes well, marinated and raw, for up to a month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice with lettuce wraps and kimchi
  • Soup: Doenjang jjigae
  • Dessert: Sliced pear
  • Beverage: Soju or barley tea
