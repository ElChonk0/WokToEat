Chingri Malai Curry (Bengali Prawn Coconut Curry)

Chingri malai curry, prawns in a rich coconut milk gravy with warm spices, is a Bengali festive dish traditionally made with large freshwater prawns.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Medium
  • Draft India Seafood LunchDinnerCurrySpicyShellfishDairy

  • 700 g1.5 lbLarge prawns
  • 400 ml1.75 cupsCoconut milk
  • 150 g5 ozOnion
  • 40 g3 tbspGinger and garlic
  • 5 g2 tspGaram masala
  • 3 g1 tspTurmeric
  • 15 g1/2 ozGreen chilies
  • 30 g2 tbspGhee
  • 5 g1 tspSalt

Method

  1. Fry the onion, ginger and garlic in the ghee for 6 minutes until soft and golden.
  2. Add the turmeric, chilies and coconut milk and simmer 8 minutes.
  3. Add the prawns and season with salt.
  4. Simmer 6 minutes until the prawns curl and turn pink.
  5. Finish with the garam masala.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking makes the prawns rubbery.
  • Boiling hard splits the coconut milk.

Authentic tips

  • Add a pinch of sugar.
  • Serve with plain rice.

Substitutions

  • Large shrimp replace prawns.
  • Light coconut milk makes a lighter version.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Cucumber salad
  • Dessert: Mishti doi
  • Beverage: Black tea
