Chingri Malai Curry (Bengali Prawn Coconut Curry)

▾

Surprise me with

Chingri malai curry, prawns in a rich coconut milk gravy with warm spices, is a Bengali festive dish traditionally made with large freshwater prawns.

  • 700 g1.5 lbLarge prawns (head-on if possible), shelled with tails on
  • 2 g3/4 tspGround turmeric
  • 5 g1 tspSalt
  • 30 ml2 tbspMustard oil, for frying the prawns
  • 30 g2 tbspGhee
  • 22Bay leaves
  • 66Whole garam masala (cardamom, cinnamon, cloves)
  • 150 g5 ozOnion, grated or pureed
  • 20 g1 tbsp + 1 tspGinger paste
  • 10 g2 tspGarlic paste (optional)
  • 3 g1 tspKashmiri chili powder
  • 2 g1 tspGround cumin
  • 400 ml1 canCoconut milk
  • 33Green chilies, slit
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 2 g1/2 tspGaram masala powder

Method

  1. Rub the prawns with half the turmeric and a pinch of salt. Heat the mustard oil until it smokes, cool it slightly, and fry the prawns for 1 minute per side until just pink. Lift them out; the heads give the curry richness, so press them lightly.
  2. Add the ghee to the pan with the bay leaves and whole garam masala and fry for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the onion paste and cook over medium heat for 6 to 8 minutes, stirring, until it turns golden and the raw smell goes. Add the ginger and garlic pastes, chili, cumin and remaining turmeric for 1 minute.
  4. Pour in the coconut milk with the salt, sugar and green chilies and simmer gently for 5 minutes, stirring, until it thickens slightly. Do not boil hard.
  5. Return the prawns and simmer for 2 to 3 minutes until cooked through. Finish with garam masala powder and a spoon of ghee. A Bengali celebration dish, served with steamed rice.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking makes the prawns rubbery.
  • Boiling hard splits the coconut milk.

Authentic tips

  • Add a pinch of sugar.
  • Serve with plain rice.

Substitutions

  • Large shrimp replace prawns.
  • Light coconut milk makes a lighter version.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Cucumber salad
  • Dessert: Mishti doi
  • Beverage: Black tea
