Cháng Tīng Dòu Fu (Changting Hakka Tofu) 长汀豆腐

▾

Surprise me with

Changting in western Fujian is a Hakka town known for its tofu, which is fried or stuffed and simmered in a simple broth.

  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Pat the tofu dry and cut into 3 cm cubes. Soak the mushrooms in warm water for 30 minutes, slice and keep the liquid.
  2. Heat the oil in a pan and fry the tofu 8 minutes, turning, until golden on all sides; lift out.
  3. Add the ginger and mushrooms and fry 1 minute.
  4. Add the stock and mushroom liquid, return the tofu and simmer 8 minutes.
  5. Season with the soy sauce and finish with the scallion and sesame oil.

Common mistakes

  • Breaking the tofu by stirring too much.
  • Over-salting: the soy sauce is salty.

Authentic tips

  • Pan-fry the tofu well so it holds its shape.
  • Use the mushroom soaking liquid for depth.

Substitutions

  • Fried tofu puffs soak up the sauce.
  • Fresh shiitake replace dried.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
