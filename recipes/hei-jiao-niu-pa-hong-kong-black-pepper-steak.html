Hei Jiao Niu Pa (Hong Kong Black Pepper Steak) 黑椒牛扒

▾

Surprise me with

Thin, tenderized steak with black pepper sauce served on a sizzling plate or with spaghetti is a staple of Hong Kong Western-style diners (sai chaan).

  • Cook 15 min

Method

  1. Pat the steaks dry and trim off any sinew. Snip the fat edge every 2 cm so the steaks lie flat, and pound both sides lightly with the back of a knife. Season with the salt, garlic salt and pepper, spread the oyster sauce over both sides and dredge lightly in cornstarch.
  2. Heat a heavy stainless or cast-iron skillet over high heat for 2 to 3 minutes until very hot. Add 1 tablespoon of oil, then hold each steak fat-edge down in the pan for about 1 minute to render and brown the fat.
  3. Lay the steaks flat, add the rest of the oil and turn the heat to medium. Cook for 2 minutes, turn, 1.5 minutes, turn, 2 minutes, turn, 1 minute, for medium; turning often builds an even crust. Adjust for thickness: about 55 C (130 F) inside for medium-rare. Rest on a warm plate.
  4. Lower the heat to medium, melt the butter in the pan and cook the onion for 2 minutes until soft, then the garlic and coarse pepper for 30 seconds, until the pepper smells toasty.
  5. Add the oyster sauce, Worcestershire, ketchup, sugar and stock and simmer for 2 minutes. Stir in the sauce cornstarch mixed with 1 tablespoon of water and cook until glossy and thick enough to coat a spoon. Add the steak resting juices.
  6. Serve the steaks on hot plates with the black pepper sauce poured over at the table so it sizzles, with rice or spaghetti and vegetables on the side.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Fine pre-ground pepper tastes dusty; grind it coarsely.
  • Cutting the steak straight away loses its juices; rest it 3 minutes.

Authentic tips

  • Heat a cast-iron skillet in a 250 C (480 F) oven to recreate the sizzling plate.
  • Serve with spaghetti or rice and a fried egg, diner style.

Substitutions

  • Pork chops work with the same marinade and sauce.
  • Mushroom sauce: swap the pepper for sliced button mushrooms.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; slice thinly and reheat in the sauce.

Freezing: The sauce freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Spaghetti or rice with corn and peas
  • Soup: Hong Kong borscht
  • Dessert: Red bean ice
  • Beverage: Iced milk tea
