Buddha's Delight 罗汉斋

Lo han zhai, a vegetarian braise of mushrooms, tofu, bean thread and vegetables, is a traditional Cantonese dish eaten by Buddhist monks and at Lunar New Year, when many families avoid meat on the first day.

Ingredients

  • 40 g1.5 ozDried shiitake mushrooms, soaked
  • 200 g7 ozFirm tofu, cubed and fried
  • 200 g7 ozNapa cabbage
  • 150 g5 ozCarrots and bamboo shoots
  • 30 g1 ozWood ear mushrooms
  • 60 g2 ozBean thread noodles, soaked
  • 45 ml3 tbspVegetarian mushroom sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Soak the dried shiitake in 300 ml hot water for 30 minutes; squeeze, trim the stems and slice, keeping the soaking liquid. Soak the wood ear 15 minutes and tear into pieces, and soak the bean thread noodles 5 minutes.
  2. If the tofu is not already fried, pan-fry the cubes in 1 tablespoon of the oil until golden on all sides; set aside.
  3. Heat the remaining oil in a wok and stir-fry the shiitake, carrot and bamboo shoots for 3 minutes.
  4. Add the cabbage, tofu and wood ear with 250 ml of the mushroom liquid, the mushroom sauce and soy sauce; cover and simmer 10 minutes until the cabbage is tender.
  5. Stir in the noodles and cook 3 minutes until they absorb the sauce; serve hot.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the cabbage into mush.
  • Discarding the mushroom soaking liquid, which carries the flavor.

Authentic tips

  • Traditional recipes include up to 18 ingredients for the 18 luohan; use what you have.
  • Fermented bean curd added to the sauce gives a deeper, savory note.

Substitutions

  • Use any mix of seasonal vegetables.
  • Add lotus seeds or ginkgo nuts for a festive version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps three days in the fridge and tastes better the next day.

Freezing: Freezes acceptably, though the cabbage softens.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable broth
  • Dessert: Sweet red bean soup
  • Beverage: Hot jasmine tea
