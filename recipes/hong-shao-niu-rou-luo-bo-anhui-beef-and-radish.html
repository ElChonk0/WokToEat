Huī Shì Hóng Shāo Niú Ròu Luó Bo (Hui-Style Beef and Radish Braise) 徽式萝卜烧牛肉

▾

Beef braised with radish is a Hui winter dish, in which the radish soaks up the savory sauce.

Method

  1. Cut the brisket into 4 cm cubes, blanch 5 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  2. Melt the rock sugar in a heavy pot over low heat until amber, add the beef and turn to coat.
  3. Add the ginger, scallion, garlic, star anise, soy sauce and 800 ml water, bring to a boil, cover and simmer 60 minutes.
  4. Peel the daikon, cut into thick chunks and add; simmer 30 minutes until translucent and the beef is tender.
  5. Uncover and reduce the sauce 5 minutes.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the daikon too soon makes it fall apart.
  • Boiling hard toughens the beef.

Authentic tips

  • The daikon should be sweet and soaked in the sauce.
  • Serve over rice.

Substitutions

  • Shank replaces brisket.
  • Carrot can join the daikon.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months without the daikon.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
