Qīng Cài Xiāng Gū (Baby Bok Choy with Shiitake) 青菜香菇

▾

Surprise me with

Qing cai xiang gu, baby bok choy stir-fried with shiitake mushrooms, is the iconic Shanghai home vegetable dish, showing the region's love of tender local greens.

  • 500 g1.1 lbShanghai bok choy (qing cai)
  • 40 g1.5 ozDried shiitake mushrooms, soaked
  • 15 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Soak the dried shiitake in warm water for 30 minutes, squeeze and slice; keep 3 tablespoons of the soaking liquid.
  2. Halve the bok choy lengthwise and rinse well, drying thoroughly.
  3. Heat the oil in a wok over high heat and fry the garlic and mushrooms for 1 minute.
  4. Add the bok choy, the mushroom liquid, soy sauce, sugar and salt.
  5. Toss over high heat for 2-3 minutes until the stems are tender-crisp.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking turns the greens olive-colored and mushy.
  • Wet greens steam rather than stir-fry.

Authentic tips

  • Shanghai bok choy is milder and sweeter than the standard variety.
  • Save the mushroom water for another dish.

Substitutions

  • Baby bok choy replaces the Shanghai bok choy.
  • Fresh shiitake replace the dried ones.

Storage

Leftovers: Best the same day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Tofu soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
