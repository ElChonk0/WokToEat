Gỏi Gà (Vietnamese Chicken and Cabbage Salad)

▾

Surprise me with

Goi ga, shredded chicken tossed with cabbage, herbs, peanuts and a sweet-sour fish sauce dressing, is a Vietnamese party and weeknight dish.

  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 600 g1.3 lbChicken thighs, poached
  • 300 g10 ozCabbage, shredded
  • 100 g3.5 ozCarrots
  • 40 g1.5 ozRau ram or mint
  • 45 ml3 tbspFish sauce
  • 45 ml3 tbspLime juice
  • 30 g1 ozSugar and chilies
  • 40 g1.5 ozRoasted peanuts

Method

  1. Poach the chicken in simmering water for 18 minutes, then cool and shred.
  2. Mix the fish sauce, lime juice, sugar and chilies until the sugar dissolves.
  3. Toss the cabbage and carrots with the dressing and let sit 10 minutes.
  4. Add the herbs and chicken.
  5. Top with peanuts and serve.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling toughens the chicken.
  • Dressing too early wilts the cabbage excessively.

Authentic tips

  • Rau ram gives the authentic Vietnamese flavor.
  • Add fried shallots for crunch.

Substitutions

  • Rotisserie chicken is a quick shortcut.
  • Red cabbage adds color.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day if the dressing is kept separate.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Shrimp chips or steamed rice
  • Soup: Canh
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
