Mala Beef Stir-Fry 麻辣牛肉

▾

Surprise me with

A numbing-spicy stir-fry built on the classic Sichuan mala (numbing-hot) flavor profile, this dish combines chili and Sichuan peppercorn with quickly seared beef for an intensely aromatic weeknight dish.

Method

  1. Marinate beef in soy sauce and cornstarch for 15 minutes.
  2. Stir-fry dried chilies and Sichuan peppercorns in oil over medium heat until fragrant, about 30 seconds.
  3. Add beef and stir-fry over high heat until just browned, 2–3 minutes; remove.
  4. Add garlic, ginger, and green onion to the wok, stir-frying briefly.
  5. Return beef to the wok with wine, tossing everything together over high heat until combined.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the dried chilies and peppercorns, which turns the whole dish bitter.
  • Overcooking the beef, which turns it tough — sear it quickly over very high heat.

Authentic tips

  • Toasting the chilies and peppercorns briefly in oil is the flavor foundation of the whole dish — don't rush it.
  • Slicing the beef thinly against the grain is essential for tenderness given the quick cooking time.

Substitutions

  • Chicken thigh works with the same method.
  • Reduce the dried chilies by half for a family-friendly version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.
