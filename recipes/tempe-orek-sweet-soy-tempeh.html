Tempe Orek (Sweet and Spicy Tempeh)

▾

Surprise me with

Tempe, fermented soybean cake, was invented in Java and is Indonesia's best-known contribution to plant protein; orek is a sweet, spicy stir-fry of tempeh with kecap manis.

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a flat-bottom wok to 170 C (340 F) and fry the tempeh in batches for 4 to 5 minutes until golden and crisp. Drain.
  2. Pour off all but 2 tablespoons of oil. Fry the shallots, garlic, chilies, galangal and leaves over medium heat for 2 to 3 minutes until fragrant and golden.
  3. Add the kecap manis, palm sugar, tamarind, salt and water and bubble for 1 to 2 minutes until it thickens into a sticky glaze.
  4. Turn off the heat, add the tempeh and toss until every piece is coated.
  5. Serve with rice. Tempe orek keeps well and is a lunchbox staple across Java.

Common mistakes

  • Soggy tempeh: fry until truly crisp.
  • Burning the sugar.

Authentic tips

  • Add peanuts or anchovies for a non-vegetarian version.
  • Keeps well.

Substitutions

  • Add peanuts or anchovies for crunch.
  • Air-fry the tempeh for a lighter dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.
