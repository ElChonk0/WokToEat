Tempe Orek (Sweet and Spicy Tempeh)

Tempe, fermented soybean cake, was invented in Java and is Indonesia's best-known contribution to plant protein; orek is a sweet, spicy stir-fry of tempeh with kecap manis.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 45 ml3 tbspKecap manis
  • 50 g3 tbspShallot and garlic
  • 20 g2/3 ozChilies

  • 10 g2 tspPalm sugar
  • 150 ml2/3 cupNeutral oil for frying

Method

  1. Slice the tempeh into thin matchsticks.
  2. Fry in the oil in a wok over medium-high heat for 5-6 minutes until crisp and golden; drain.
  3. Pour off most of the oil, then fry the shallot, garlic, chilies and lime leaves 1 minute.
  4. Add the kecap manis, palm sugar and 2 tablespoons water.
  5. Toss the tempeh in the sauce until sticky.

Common mistakes

  • Soggy tempeh: fry until truly crisp.
  • Burning the sugar.

Authentic tips

  • Add peanuts or anchovies for a non-vegetarian version.
  • Keeps well.

Substitutions

  • Add peanuts or anchovies for crunch.
  • Air-fry the tempeh for a lighter dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.
