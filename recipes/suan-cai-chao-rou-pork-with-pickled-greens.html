Suān Cài Chǎo Ròu (Pork with Pickled Greens) 酸菜炒肉

▾

Surprise me with

Pickled mustard greens, a Yunnan pantry staple, are stir-fried with pork and chilies to make a sharp, salty accompaniment to rice.

  • Cook 10 min

  • 300 g11 ozPork belly, sliced
  • 250 g9 ozPickled mustard greens
  • 30 g1 ozChilies
  • 20 g4 clovesGarlic
  • 5 ml1 tspLight soy sauce
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 15 ml1 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Rinse the pickled greens, squeeze dry and chop.
  2. Fry the belly slices in a dry wok over medium-high heat for 4 minutes until browned and the fat renders; add the oil only if needed.
  3. Add the garlic and chilies for 20 seconds.
  4. Add the pickles and sugar and stir-fry 3 minutes until the moisture evaporates.
  5. Taste and season lightly with soy sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Not rinsing the pickles makes the dish far too salty.
  • A wet pan steams the pork instead of browning it.

Authentic tips

  • Press the pickles dry for a sharper flavor.
  • Serve with plain rice.

Substitutions

  • Rinsed sauerkraut replaces the pickled greens.
  • Pork shoulder gives a leaner dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
