Kansi (Bacolod Sour Beef Shank Soup)

▾

Surprise me with

Kansi, a sour beef-shank soup soured with batwan fruit and flavored with lemongrass, is a specialty of Bacolod and Negros, a cross between bulalo and sinigang.

  • Cook 3 hr

  • 600 g1.3 lbBeef shank
  • 700 g1.5 lbBeef bones, with marrow
  • 45 g1.5 ozBatwan or tamarind
  • 80 g3 ozLemongrass and ginger
  • 100 g3.5 ozOnion
  • 150 g5 ozJackfruit (optional)
  • 30 ml2 tbspFish sauce
  • 2.5 L10 cupsWater

Method

  1. Cover the shank and bones with cold water, boil 5 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  2. Return to a clean pot with 2.5 L water, the onion, ginger and bruised lemongrass; bring to a boil and skim.
  3. Cover and simmer gently for 2.5 hours until tender.
  4. Add the jackfruit and cook 15 minutes.
  5. Add the batwan or tamarind and fish sauce and simmer 5 minutes; adjust for sour and salty.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the sour fruit too early dulls its brightness.
  • Skipping the blanch gives a cloudy soup.

Authentic tips

  • Batwan is a Visayan fruit; tamarind is a close substitute.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • Tamarind replaces batwan.
  • Short rib replaces shank.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Calamansi juice
