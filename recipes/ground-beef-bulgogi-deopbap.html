Ground Beef Bulgogi Deopbap (Ground Beef Bulgogi Rice Bowl) 불고기 덮밥

▾

Surprise me with

Bulgogi seasoning (soy, sugar, garlic and sesame) is used with minced beef in Korean home cooking as a fast, cheap stand-in for sliced grilled bulgogi, served as a rice bowl (deopbap).

Method

  1. Mix the soy sauce, sugar, pear, mirin, sesame oil, garlic and pepper. Stir it into the beef with the onion and green onion and leave for 15 to 30 minutes.
  2. Heat a large skillet over medium-high heat with the oil, add the beef and break it up with a spatula. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes until it has lost its pink color and starts to brown.
  3. Add the carrot, mushrooms and water and stir-fry for 2 to 3 minutes until the vegetables are just tender. Keep some saucy liquid in the pan; it soaks into the rice and is the point of a deopbap (topped rice bowl).
  4. Meanwhile fry the eggs sunny-side up in a separate pan.
  5. Spoon the beef and its sauce over bowls of hot rice, top each with an egg and sprinkle with sesame seeds.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Stirring the beef constantly stops it browning; let it sit and sear before breaking it up.
  • Not draining the fat leaves the sauce greasy.

Authentic tips

  • Grated pear is the traditional bulgogi tenderizer and adds a fruity sweetness.
  • Add a spoon of gochujang to the sauce for a spicy version.

Substitutions

  • Ground pork or turkey work the same way.
  • No pear: grated apple or 1 extra tablespoon of sugar.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days in the refrigerator; good for packed lunches.

Freezing: The cooked beef freezes well for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Kimchi and cucumber salad (oi muchim)
  • Soup: Miyeok guk (seaweed soup)
  • Dessert: Hotteok
  • Beverage: Barley tea
