Hù Shì Gā Lí Niú Ròu (Shanghai Curry Beef and Potato) 沪式咖喱牛肉

▾

Surprise me with

Curry reached Shanghai through Cantonese and Hong Kong restaurants and British influence, and a mild curry of beef and potato is a Shanghai-style home and cafe dish.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 60 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 600 g1.3 lbBeef brisket, cubed
  • 400 g14 ozPotatoes and carrots
  • 150 g5 ozOnion
  • 20 g2 tbspCurry powder
  • 120 ml1/2 cupCoconut milk
  • 15 g1 tbspFlour
  • 600 ml2.5 cupsWater
  • 6 g1 tspSalt

Method

  1. Brown the brisket cubes in a heavy pot over medium-high heat for 5 minutes; remove.
  2. In the same pot cook the onion 5 minutes until soft, then stir in the curry powder and flour for 1 minute.
  3. Return the beef, add 600 ml water, bring to a boil, then cover and simmer 40 minutes.
  4. Add the potatoes and carrots and cook 20 minutes more.
  5. Stir in the coconut milk and salt and simmer 5 minutes until the sauce thickens.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the curry powder makes it bitter; cook it just 1 minute.
  • Adding the vegetables too soon makes them mushy.

Authentic tips

  • Shanghai-style curry is mild and slightly sweet; a spoonful of sugar is in character.
  • Serve over rice.

Substitutions

  • Chuck replaces the brisket.
  • Evaporated milk replaces the coconut milk for a more traditional taste.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; better the next day.

Freezing: Freezes well for 2 months without the potatoes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or bread
  • Soup: Cucumber salad
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
