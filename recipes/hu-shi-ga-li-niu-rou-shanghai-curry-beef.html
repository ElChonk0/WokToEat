Hù Shì Gā Lí Niú Ròu (Shanghai Curry Beef and Potato) 沪式咖喱牛肉

▾

Surprise me with

Curry reached Shanghai through Cantonese and Hong Kong restaurants and British influence, and a mild curry of beef and potato is a Shanghai-style home and cafe dish.

  • Cook 2 hr

  • 600 g1.3 lbBeef brisket, cubed
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil
  • 11Onion, diced
  • 10 g1/3 ozGinger, sliced
  • 20 g2 tbspCurry powder (mild, Madras style)
  • 15 g2 tbspAll-purpose flour
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 800 ml3.5 cupsWater
  • 250 g9 ozPotatoes, cut into chunks
  • 150 g5 ozCarrots, cut into chunks
  • 120 ml1/2 cupCoconut milk or evaporated milk
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 10 g2 tspSugar
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt

Method

  1. Blanch the brisket in boiling water for 5 minutes, drain and rinse.
  2. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven over medium heat and cook the onion and ginger for 5 minutes until soft and golden. Stir in the curry powder and flour and fry for 1 minute until fragrant; frying the curry in oil blooms it and the flour thickens the gravy.
  3. Add the beef and Shaoxing wine and toss to coat, then the water. Bring to a boil, cover and simmer over low heat for 1.25 hours until nearly tender.
  4. Add the potatoes and carrots and simmer for 25 to 30 minutes until soft; some potato breaks down and thickens the sauce.
  5. Stir in the coconut milk, soy sauce, sugar and salt and simmer for 5 minutes. Shanghai curry, a legacy of the city's foreign concessions, is mild, golden and slightly sweet. Serve with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the curry powder makes it bitter; cook it just 1 minute.
  • Adding the vegetables too soon makes them mushy.

Authentic tips

  • Shanghai-style curry is mild and slightly sweet; a spoonful of sugar is in character.
  • Serve over rice.

Substitutions

  • Chuck replaces the brisket.
  • Evaporated milk replaces the coconut milk for a more traditional taste.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; better the next day.

Freezing: Freezes well for 2 months without the potatoes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or bread
  • Soup: Cucumber salad
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
