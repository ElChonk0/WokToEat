Yīng Zuǐ Dòu Dùn Jī (Chickpea Chicken Stew) 鹰嘴豆炖鸡

▾

Surprise me with

Chickpeas are a staple of Uyghur and Central Asian cooking, simmered with chicken in a spiced tomato stew.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 45 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Brown the chicken in the oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat for 6 to 8 minutes.
  2. Add the onion and garlic for 3 minutes, then the tomatoes and spices for 3 minutes until soft.
  3. Add the chickpeas and water, bring to a boil, cover and simmer for 30 minutes until the chicken is tender. Xinjiang chickpeas, 'eagle-beak beans' (ying zui dou), are grown around Mulei.
  4. Mash a few chickpeas against the pot to thicken the sauce and season with salt.
  5. Scatter with cilantro and serve with naan.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the browning gives a pale stew.
  • Too much water dilutes the sauce.

Authentic tips

  • Canned chickpeas are fine; drain and rinse.
  • Serve with flatbread.

Substitutions

  • Canned chickpeas replace cooked; rinse well.
  • Lamb is the traditional meat.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; the flavor improves.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Nang flatbread
  • Soup: Not needed; the stew is its own soup
  • Dessert: Hami melon
  • Beverage: Black tea
