Yīng Zuǐ Dòu Dùn Jī (Chickpea Chicken Stew) 鹰嘴豆炖鸡

▾

Surprise me with

Chickpeas are a staple of Uyghur and Central Asian cooking, simmered with chicken in a spiced tomato stew.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 45 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Xinjiang Chicken LunchDinnerBraise / stew

Ingredients

  • 900 g2 lbChicken, chopped
  • 300 g10 ozChickpeas, cooked
  • 300 g10 ozTomatoes and onion
  • 8 g3 tspCumin and coriander
  • 30 g2 tbspGarlic
  • 8 g1.5 tspSalt
  • 500 ml2 cupsWater
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Brown the chicken in the oil in a heavy pot over medium-high heat for 8 minutes; remove.
  2. Fry the onion, garlic, cumin and coriander 3 minutes.
  3. Add the tomatoes and cook 5 minutes into a sauce.
  4. Return the chicken with the chickpeas, salt and water; cover and simmer 30 minutes.
  5. Uncover and cook 5 minutes to thicken.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the browning gives a pale stew.
  • Too much water dilutes the sauce.

Authentic tips

  • Canned chickpeas are fine; drain and rinse.
  • Serve with flatbread.

Substitutions

  • Canned chickpeas replace cooked; rinse well.
  • Lamb is the traditional meat.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; the flavor improves.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Nang flatbread
  • Soup: Not needed; the stew is its own soup
  • Dessert: Hami melon
  • Beverage: Black tea
