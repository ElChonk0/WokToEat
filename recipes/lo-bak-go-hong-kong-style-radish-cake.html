Lo Bak Go (Hong Kong-Style Radish Cake) 萝卜糕

▾

Surprise me with

Lo bak go is a Cantonese dim sum cake of grated white radish, rice flour, Chinese sausage and dried shrimp, steamed and then pan-fried in slices until crisp.

  • Cook 1 hr

Method

  1. Soak the dried shrimp and mushrooms in warm water for 10 minutes, then mince them. Dice the sausage, keeping a few slices for the top. Stir the rice flour into the water until smooth.
  2. Heat a flat-bottom wok over high heat with the oil until it shimmers. Fry the garlic, shallots and shrimp for 30 seconds, then the sausage and mushrooms for 30 seconds, until fragrant.
  3. Add the radish, cover and cook for 6 to 7 minutes, stirring once or twice, until it is translucent and has released its juice. Season with the salt, sugar, pepper and sesame oil, cover and cook 2 minutes more until the liquid boils. That juice is part of the batter, so do not drain it.
  4. Line the base of a 20 cm (8 in) cake pan with parchment and oil the sides. Lower the heat and pour the rice flour mixture into the wok gradually, stirring constantly, then keep stirring for about 2 minutes until the mixture is thick and pasty.
  5. Spread it evenly in the pan, smooth the top and brush it with oil. Set a rack in the wok with water just below it and bring to a full boil.
  6. Steam covered over high heat for 20 minutes, then medium heat for 20 to 25 minutes, topping up with boiling water as needed, until a chopstick pushed into the center comes out clean. Scatter the reserved sausage and green onion on top for the last minute.
  7. Cool to room temperature, then refrigerate for at least 4 hours or overnight. It must be completely cold and set, or it falls apart in the pan.
  8. Turn out and cut into rectangles 1.5 cm (1/2 in) thick. Pan-fry in the frying oil over medium-high heat for 4 to 6 minutes per side, without moving, until golden and crisp. Serve with oyster sauce or chili sauce.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Slicing the cake while warm makes it fall apart; chill it first.
  • Too much water in the batter gives a soft cake that does not fry crisp.

Authentic tips

  • Squeeze a little of the water from the radish if it is very juicy.
  • A thin coating of oil in the pan lets the surface crisp evenly.

Substitutions

  • Bacon replaces Chinese sausage.
  • Skip the dried shrimp for a version with only sausage and mushrooms.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days in the refrigerator; fry before eating.

Freezing: The steamed, unfried cake freezes for 2 months; thaw before slicing.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Chili sauce and hoisin
  • Soup: Clear congee
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot jasmine tea
