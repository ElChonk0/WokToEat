Lo Bak Go (Hong Kong-Style Radish Cake) 萝卜糕

Lo bak go is a Cantonese dim sum cake of grated white radish, rice flour, Chinese sausage and dried shrimp, steamed and then pan-fried in slices until crisp.

  • Cook 1 hr 30 min

Ingredients

  • 800 g1.75 lbWhite radish (daikon), grated
  • 200 g1 2/3 cupsRice flour
  • 30 g1/4 cupCornstarch
  • 300 ml1 1/4 cupsWater
  • 100 g3.5 ozChinese sausage (lap cheong), diced
  • 30 g1 ozDried shrimp, soaked and chopped
  • 20 g3/4 ozDried shiitake mushrooms, soaked and diced
  • 40 g1.5 ozShallots, minced
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 8 g1.5 tspSalt
  • 2 g1/2 tspWhite pepper
  • 10 g2 tspSugar
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil, for pan-frying
  • 30 ml2 tbspOyster sauce, for serving

Method

  1. Fry the sausage in 15 ml of the oil over medium heat for 3 minutes until the fat renders, then add the shrimp, mushrooms and shallots for 2 minutes and set aside.
  2. Simmer the grated radish with 100 ml of the water and the salt, covered, for 10 minutes until translucent and soft.
  3. Whisk the rice flour, cornstarch, remaining water, pepper and sugar into a smooth batter, then stir in the radish with its liquid and the fried filling.
  4. Pour into an oiled 20 cm square pan, cover with foil and steam over high heat for 1 hour until a skewer comes out clean.
  5. Cool the cake completely, ideally overnight in the refrigerator, then cut into slices 2 cm thick.
  6. Pan-fry the slices in the remaining oil over medium-high heat for 3 minutes per side until golden and crisp, and serve with the oyster sauce.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Slicing the cake while warm makes it fall apart; chill it first.
  • Too much water in the batter gives a soft cake that does not fry crisp.

Authentic tips

  • Squeeze a little of the water from the radish if it is very juicy.
  • A thin coating of oil in the pan lets the surface crisp evenly.

Substitutions

  • Bacon replaces Chinese sausage.
  • Skip the dried shrimp for a version with only sausage and mushrooms.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days in the refrigerator; fry before eating.

Freezing: The steamed, unfried cake freezes for 2 months; thaw before slicing.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Chili sauce and hoisin
  • Soup: Clear congee
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot jasmine tea
