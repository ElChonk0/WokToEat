Ayam Buah Keluak (Nyonya Chicken with Black Nuts)

▾

Surprise me with

Ayam buah keluak is a Peranakan dish of chicken braised in a sour, spiced tamarind gravy with buah keluak, black nuts whose filling is mixed with spiced meat and stuffed back into the shells.

Method

  1. Crack the soaked buah keluak shells and scoop out the black flesh. Mix the flesh with the ground pork, a pinch of salt and a splash of tamarind, and stuff the mixture back into the shells.
  2. Blend the shallots, garlic, both chilies, candlenuts, lemongrass, galangal, turmeric and belacan into a smooth paste.
  3. Fry the paste in the oil over medium heat for 10 minutes until fragrant and the oil separates.
  4. Add the chicken, stir for 3 minutes to coat, then add the tamarind, water and the stuffed nuts.
  5. Bring to a boil, then simmer gently, covered, for 45 minutes until the chicken is tender and the gravy is thick and dark.
  6. Season with the sugar and salt, and serve with steamed rice.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Using unsoaked or bad nuts can give a bitter, harsh taste; taste a nut first and discard any that smell sour.
  • A thin gravy means too little frying; cook the paste until the oil separates.

Authentic tips

  • Buy ready-to-use buah keluak from a Peranakan grocer; they come soaked and boiled.
  • Better the next day, after the gravy has rested.

Substitutions

  • Pork ribs replace chicken.
  • Mushrooms and a spoonful of dark chocolate-like miso give a similar earthiness if you cannot find the nuts.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days in the refrigerator; the flavor improves.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and cucumber salad
  • Soup: Not needed; the gravy is rich
  • Dessert: Kueh
  • Beverage: Iced teh
