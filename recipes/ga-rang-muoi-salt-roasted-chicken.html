Gà Rang Muối (Vietnamese Salt-Roasted Chicken)

▾

Surprise me with

Ga rang muoi, chicken pan-roasted with a coating of pepper and salt, is a Vietnamese restaurant favorite, often paired with lime and pepper dip.

  • Cook 25 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken with the fish sauce and garlic for 30 minutes, then pat dry and dust with cornstarch.
  2. Heat the oil to 170 C (340 F) and fry the chicken in batches for 8 to 10 minutes until golden and cooked through. Drain. Pour off all but 2 tablespoons of oil.
  3. Fry the garlic, shallot and lemongrass in the oil over medium heat for 2 minutes until golden, then add the lime leaves, salt, pepper, sugar and chili and toss for 30 seconds until fragrant and sandy.
  4. Add the chicken and toss over medium heat for 1 to 2 minutes until every piece is coated in the salty, crunchy mixture. Rang muoi means 'roasted in salt'.
  5. Serve with lime wedges, as a drinking snack or with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the garlic makes the dish bitter; add it at the end.
  • Overcooking dries the chicken.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with a dip of lime juice, salt and black pepper.
  • Use bone-in thighs for the juiciest result.

Substitutions

  • Bone-in drumsticks work with 10 extra minutes.
  • Shrimp take the same seasoning.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot pan.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice with lime-pepper-salt dip

  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
