Xiǎo Mǐ Là Chǎo Jī (Yunnan Fiery Chili Chicken) 小米辣炒鸡

▾

Surprise me with

Xiaomila, tiny and fiery Yunnan chilies, flavor stir-fried chicken across the province, from Kunming to Dali.

  • Cook 12 min

  • 600 g1.3 lbChicken thighs, boneless, sliced
  • 10 ml2 tspLight soy sauce, for the marinade
  • 10 g1 tbspCornstarch
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 40 g1.5 ozXiao mi la (bird's eye chilies), chopped
  • 40 g1.5 ozGarlic and ginger, sliced
  • 10 ml2 tspLight soy sauce, for the sauce
  • 2 g1/3 tspSalt
  • 20 g3/4 ozCilantro

Method

  1. Toss the chicken with the marinade soy sauce, cornstarch and a tablespoon of water.
  2. Heat the oil in a flat-bottom wok over high heat and stir-fry the chicken for 3 to 4 minutes until cooked and browned.
  3. Add the garlic, ginger and chilies and stir-fry for 1 minute. Xiao mi la, Yunnan's tiny, fiercely hot chilies, are the heart of the dish.
  4. Season with the sauce soy sauce and salt.
  5. Toss in the cilantro and serve with plenty of rice.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the chilies fills the kitchen with harsh fumes.
  • Overcooking the chicken dries it out.

Authentic tips

  • Xiaomila are very hot; substitute bird's eye chilies and reduce the quantity if needed.
  • Serve with a lot of rice.

Substitutions

  • Bird's eye chilies replace xiaomila; use fewer.
  • Pork loin takes the same method.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Cooling cucumber salad
  • Dessert: Fresh watermelon
  • Beverage: Chilled tea
