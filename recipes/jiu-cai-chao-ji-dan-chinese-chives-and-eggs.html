Jiǔ Cài Chǎo Jī Dàn (Chinese Chives and Scrambled Eggs) 韭菜炒鸡蛋

▾

Chinese chives and eggs is a simple northern home dish and the classic filling for jiaozi and pancakes, beloved for the chives' mild garlicky flavor.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 6 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 300 g10 ozChinese chives, cut
  • 66Eggs
  • 5 g1 tspSalt
  • 10 ml2 tspShaoxing wine
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Cut the chives into 4 cm lengths and beat the eggs with a pinch of salt and the wine.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok over high heat and pour in the eggs.
  3. Let them set for 10 seconds, then stir gently into large soft curds, about 40 seconds.
  4. Add the chives and toss for 20 seconds until just wilted.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the eggs makes them rubbery.
  • Cooking the chives too long loses their flavor.

Authentic tips

  • Serve right away.
  • The classic filling for jiaozi and pancakes.

Substitutions

  • Garlic chives or scallions replace Chinese chives.
  • Add dried shrimp for the classic version.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
