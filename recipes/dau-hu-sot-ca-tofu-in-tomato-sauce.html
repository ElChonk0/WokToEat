Đậu Hũ Sốt Cà (Tofu in Tomato Sauce)

▾

Surprise me with

Dau hu sot ca, fried tofu simmered in a sweet and tangy tomato sauce, is a Vietnamese home staple eaten with rice, found at Buddhist temple kitchens and family tables alike.

  • 30 ml2 tbspSoy sauce
  • 10 g2 tspSugar

  • 60 ml4 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Press the tofu 15 minutes, cut into cubes and pat dry.
  2. Fry in the oil over medium-high heat 6 minutes until golden; remove.
  3. Fry the shallot and garlic 30 seconds and add the tomatoes; cook down 8 minutes.
  4. Add the soy sauce, sugar and tofu.
  5. Simmer 5 minutes and add scallion.

Common mistakes

  • Wet tofu doesn't crisp.
  • Underripe tomatoes make a sour sauce.

Authentic tips

  • Ripe tomatoes give the best sauce.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • Fried tofu puffs soak up the sauce.
  • Canned tomatoes replace fresh.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Canh
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
