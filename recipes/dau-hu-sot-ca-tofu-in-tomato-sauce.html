Đậu Hũ Sốt Cà (Tofu in Tomato Sauce)

▾

Surprise me with

Dau hu sot ca, fried tofu simmered in a sweet and tangy tomato sauce, is a Vietnamese home staple eaten with rice, found at Buddhist temple kitchens and family tables alike.

  • Cook 20 min

  • 22Shallots, minced

  • 400 g14 ozRipe tomatoes, chopped
  • 15 g1 tbspTomato paste (optional)
  • 30 ml2 tbspSoy sauce
  • 10 g2 tspSugar
  • 2 g1/3 tspSalt
  • 120 ml1/2 cupWater
  • 30 g1 ozGreen onion, sliced
  • 1 g1/2 tspBlack pepper

Method

  1. Press the tofu and pat dry. Fry it in the oil in a skillet over medium-high heat for 8 to 10 minutes, turning, until golden on all sides. Lift it out.
  2. Pour off all but 2 tablespoons of oil and fry the shallots and garlic for 1 minute.
  3. Add the tomatoes and tomato paste and cook for 6 to 8 minutes, mashing, until they break down into a thick sauce.
  4. Add the soy sauce, sugar, salt and water and simmer for 2 minutes, then return the tofu and simmer for 3 to 5 minutes so it absorbs the sauce.
  5. Finish with green onion and pepper and serve with rice. A Buddhist vegetarian classic eaten across Vietnam.

Common mistakes

  • Wet tofu doesn't crisp.
  • Underripe tomatoes make a sour sauce.

Authentic tips

  • Ripe tomatoes give the best sauce.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • Fried tofu puffs soak up the sauce.
  • Canned tomatoes replace fresh.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Canh
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
