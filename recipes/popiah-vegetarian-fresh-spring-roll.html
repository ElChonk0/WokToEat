Popiah (Vegetarian Fresh Spring Rolls)

▾

Surprise me with

Popiah, thin crepes wrapped around cooked jicama and fresh vegetables, came to Singapore and Malaysia with Hokkien immigrants; the vegetarian version leaves out the pork and shrimp.

Method

  1. Braise the jicama in a little oil with the sweet sauce for 20 minutes until soft; drain well.
  2. Make the egg strips from a thin omelet.
  3. Lay a popiah skin flat and spread with the sweet and chili sauces.
  4. Layer the lettuce, sprouts, jicama, egg, tofu and peanuts.
  5. Fold in the sides and roll tightly.

Common mistakes

  • Wet filling makes the skin tear.
  • Overfilling splits the roll.

Authentic tips

  • Drain the jicama well.
  • Serve at room temperature.

Substitutions

  • Lettuce cups replace wrappers.
  • Omit the egg for a vegan dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Chili sauce
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
