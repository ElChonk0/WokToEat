Popiah (Vegetarian Fresh Spring Rolls)

▾

Surprise me with

Popiah, thin crepes wrapped around cooked jicama and fresh vegetables, came to Singapore and Malaysia with Hokkien immigrants; the vegetarian version leaves out the pork and shrimp.

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a pan over medium heat and fry the garlic for 10 seconds, then add the jicama and carrot with the soy sauce, salt and pepper. Cover and braise for 15 to 20 minutes, stirring now and then, until very soft and sweet.
  2. Drain the filling well in a sieve and keep the juice aside; wet filling tears the delicate skin and makes the roll soggy.
  3. Lay a popiah skin flat and brush the center with sweet sauce and a little chili sauce. Lay a lettuce leaf on top to protect the skin.
  4. Add some bean sprouts, a few tablespoons of filling, tofu, egg strips, crushed peanuts, fried shallots and cilantro.
  5. Fold in the sides, then roll up tightly from the bottom like a burrito. Cut into pieces and eat straight away; assemble only as many as you will eat, as the skins soften quickly.

Common mistakes

  • Wet filling makes the skin tear.
  • Overfilling splits the roll.

Authentic tips

  • Drain the jicama well.
  • Serve at room temperature.

Substitutions

  • Lettuce cups replace wrappers.
  • Omit the egg for a vegan dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.
