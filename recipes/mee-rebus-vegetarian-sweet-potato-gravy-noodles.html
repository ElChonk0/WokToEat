Mee Rebus (Vegetarian Sweet Potato Gravy Noodles)

▾

Surprise me with

Mee rebus, "boiled noodles", is a Malay and Singaporean dish of yellow noodles in a thick sweet potato and spiced gravy, usually topped with egg, tofu and lime.

Method

  1. Steam or boil the sweet potato until very soft, about 10 minutes, then mash it smooth with a little of the stock.
  2. Blend the shallots, garlic, soaked chilies and half the peanuts into a paste. Fry it in the oil over medium-low heat for 8 to 10 minutes until dark and the oil separates, then add the coriander, cumin and tauco and fry for 2 minutes more.
  3. Add the stock, tamarind and palm sugar and bring to a boil, then stir in the mashed sweet potato and simmer for 10 to 15 minutes, stirring often so it does not catch, until thick, smooth and slightly sweet. The sweet potato is what thickens mee rebus into its signature velvety gravy.
  4. Grind the remaining peanuts coarsely, stir them in and season with the salt. Thin with stock if needed; it should coat the noodles generously.
  5. Blanch the noodles and bean sprouts and divide among bowls. Ladle the hot gravy over and top with the fried tofu, halved eggs, green chilies and fried shallots. Squeeze over lime to serve.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Leaving the gravy unstirred lets it catch and burn; stir often as it thickens.
  • A raw spice paste tastes harsh; fry it until the oil separates.

Authentic tips

  • Add a splash of the noodle water to loosen the gravy before serving.
  • Traditional versions use dried shrimp; this one is vegetarian.

Substitutions

  • Pumpkin replaces some of the sweet potato.
  • Tofu puffs replace fried tofu.

Storage

Leftovers: The gravy keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; loosen with water.

Freezing: The gravy freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Extra sliced green chilies and lime
  • Soup: Not needed; the gravy is rich
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced teh
