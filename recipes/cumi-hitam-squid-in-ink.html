Cumi Hitam (Squid in Its Ink)

Cumi hitam, squid cooked in its own ink with garlic and chilies, is a Madurese and East Javanese coastal dish.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Medium
  • Draft Indonesia Seafood LunchDinnerBraise / stewSpicyShellfish

  • 700 g1.5 lbWhole squid with ink
  • 60 g4 tbspShallot and garlic
  • 30 g1 ozChilies
  • 150 g5 ozTomato
  • 30 ml2 tbspLime juice
  • 15 ml1 tbspKecap manis
  • 5 g1 tspSalt
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Clean the squid, keeping the ink sacs; cut into rings.
  2. Fry the shallot, garlic and chilies in the oil for 3 minutes.
  3. Add the tomato and cook 4 minutes.
  4. Add the squid and ink with 100 ml water and simmer 10 minutes.
  5. Season with the lime juice, kecap manis and salt.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking too long makes the squid rubbery.
  • Cooking too short leaves it tough; either 2 minutes or 20.

Authentic tips

  • Ink sacs are sold in some fishmongers; sachets also work.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • Ink sachets are sold at fishmongers.
  • Cooking time is 5 minutes or 25; avoid the middle.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
