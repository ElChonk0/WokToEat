Gudeg Nangka (Yogyakarta Jackfruit Stew)

▾

Surprise me with

Gudeg, young jackfruit simmered for hours in coconut milk and palm sugar, is the signature dish of Yogyakarta and a traditional Javanese breakfast.

  • Cook 3 hr

Method

  1. If using fresh jackfruit, oil your hands and knife (the sap is sticky), cut it into chunks and boil for 10 minutes. Drain.
  2. Blend the shallots, garlic, candlenuts and coriander into a paste.
  3. Line a heavy Dutch oven with the teak leaves (or put in the tea bags), which give gudeg its red-brown color. Add the jackfruit, paste, galangal, bay leaves, palm sugar, salt, water and coconut milk.
  4. Bring to a boil, then add the eggs and tofu. Simmer uncovered over very low heat for 2.5 to 3 hours, stirring gently every 30 minutes, until the liquid has almost completely reduced and the jackfruit is dark, soft and sweet. Remove the tea bags after an hour.
  5. Rest for 30 minutes. Gudeg from Yogyakarta is sweet, mellow and deep brown; serve it with rice and sambal goreng krecek.

Common mistakes

  • High heat burns the sugar.
  • Not enough liquid sticks.

Authentic tips

  • Teak leaves give the traditional brown color.
  • Serve with krecek and sambal.

Substitutions

  • Canned young jackfruit replaces fresh; rinse well.
  • Teak leaves give color; black tea bags replace them.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; the flavor improves.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice, krecek and sambal
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
