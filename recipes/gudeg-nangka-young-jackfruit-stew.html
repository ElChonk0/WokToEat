Gudeg Nangka (Yogyakarta Jackfruit Stew)

▾

Surprise me with

Gudeg, young jackfruit simmered for hours in coconut milk and palm sugar, is the signature dish of Yogyakarta and a traditional Javanese breakfast.

  • Cook 90 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 400 ml1.75 cupsCoconut milk
  • 60 g4 tbspPalm sugar
  • 60 g4 tbspShallot, garlic, candlenut
  • 15 g1/2 ozBay leaves, galangal
  • 10 g1/3 ozTeak leaves or tea
  • 44Boiled eggs
  • 200 g7 ozFried tofu
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt

Method

  1. Fry the shallot, garlic and candlenut paste with the galangal in a little oil for 4 minutes.
  2. Add the jackfruit, coconut milk, palm sugar, bay leaves, teak leaves or tea and 400 ml water.
  3. Simmer on very low heat for 90 minutes, stirring occasionally, until dark brown and nearly dry.
  4. Add the boiled eggs and fried tofu for the last 20 minutes.
  5. Season with salt and serve with rice.

Common mistakes

  • High heat burns the sugar.
  • Not enough liquid sticks.

Authentic tips

  • Teak leaves give the traditional brown color.
  • Serve with krecek and sambal.

Substitutions

  • Canned young jackfruit replaces fresh; rinse well.
  • Teak leaves give color; black tea bags replace them.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; the flavor improves.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice, krecek and sambal
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
