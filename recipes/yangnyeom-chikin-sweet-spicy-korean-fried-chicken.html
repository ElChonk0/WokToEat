Yangnyeom Chikin (Sweet and Spicy Korean Fried Chicken) 양념치킨

▾

Surprise me with

Yangnyeom chicken, double-fried chicken glazed in a sticky gochujang sauce, was popularized by Korean fried-chicken chains in the early 1980s and is now one of the country's most-ordered foods.

Method

  1. Season the chicken with the salt, pepper and grated garlic. Whisk the potato starch, flour and baking powder with the ice water into a thin batter and coat the chicken.
  2. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven to 170 C (340 F). Fry the chicken in batches for 8 to 10 minutes until pale golden and cooked through to 74 C (165 F), then rest it on a rack for 5 minutes.
  3. Raise the oil to 185 C (365 F) and fry all the chicken again for 2 to 3 minutes until deep golden and very crisp.
  4. Melt the butter in a flat-bottom wok or large skillet over medium heat, fry the minced garlic for 30 seconds, then add the gochujang, ketchup, syrup, soy, sugar and gochugaru. Simmer for 1 to 2 minutes, stirring, until it bubbles and thickens.
  5. Turn off the heat, add the chicken and toss until glossy all over. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and serve at once.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the second fry leaves a crust that softens within minutes of saucing.
  • Simmering the sauce too long makes it a hard candy that will not coat.

Authentic tips

  • Korean shops serve it with cubes of pickled radish (chikin-mu) and beer, the pairing called chimaek.
  • Order half-and-half at home: sauce half the chicken and leave half plain fried.

Substitutions

  • Air fryer: spray the coated chicken with oil and cook at 200 C (400 F) for 25 minutes, turning once, then toss in the sauce.
  • Use boneless thigh pieces; fry them for 5 to 6 minutes the first time.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; reheat in a 200 C (400 F) oven or air fryer for 8 minutes.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month; reheat from frozen in a hot oven.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled radish cubes (chikin-mu) and shredded cabbage salad
  • Soup: Kongnamul guk (bean sprout soup)
  • Dessert: Bingsu (shaved ice)
  • Beverage: Cold lager
