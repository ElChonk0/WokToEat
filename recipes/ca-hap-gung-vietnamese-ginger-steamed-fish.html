Cá Hấp Gừng (Ginger-Steamed Fish)

▾

Surprise me with

Steaming fish with ginger and scallions is a simple Vietnamese home dish that shows off the freshness of the fish, finished with a soy-fish sauce dressing.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 600 g1 lb 5 ozChilean sea bass fillets, about 150 g each
  • 40 g1.5 ozGinger, cut in fine strips
  • 30 g1 ozScallions, cut in fine strips
  • 20 ml4 tspFish sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspSoy sauce
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 30 ml2 tbspWater
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil
  • 5 g1 tspRed chili, sliced
  • 10 g1/3 ozCilantro
  • 1 g1/4 tspBlack pepper

Method

  1. Pat the fillets dry, season with the pepper, and lay on a heatproof plate with half the ginger on top.
  2. Steam over high heat for 8 to 10 minutes until the flesh is opaque and flakes at the thickest part.
  3. Meanwhile, warm the fish sauce, soy sauce, sugar and water until the sugar dissolves.
  4. Pour away the steaming liquid, discard the cooked ginger, and top the fish with the fresh ginger, scallions and chili.
  5. Heat the oil until smoking, pour it over, and spoon the dressing around the fish; garnish with cilantro.

Cook’s notes

Written for Chilean sea bass.

Common mistakes

  • Over-steaming dries the fish; check at 8 minutes.
  • Leaving the liquid on the plate dilutes the dressing.

Authentic tips

  • Let the steam come to a full boil before adding the plate.
  • Serve straight away.

Substitutions

  • Cod loin replaces the sea bass.
  • Rainbow trout fillets steam in 6 to 8 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and herbs
  • Soup: Canh chua
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
