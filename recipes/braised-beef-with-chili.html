Braised Beef with Chili 辣烧牛肉

▾

Surprise me with

A hearty Hunanese braise that pairs beef with dried and fresh chilies in a savory, mildly sweet sauce — slower and more comforting than the region's typical quick stir-fries.

  • Cook 1.5 hr

Method

  1. Blanch beef in boiling water 3 minutes; drain.
  2. Brown beef in a little oil until golden; add garlic, ginger, and dried chilies, stirring briefly.
  3. Add soy sauce, sugar, and wine, stirring to coat.
  4. Add water or stock, bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer, cover, and braise 1 to 1.25 hours until the beef is tender.
  5. Uncover, add fresh chili, and reduce 10 minutes more until the sauce thickens.

Common mistakes

  • Rushing the braise — beef shank or chuck needs real time to become properly tender.
  • Adding fresh chili too early, which loses its bright color and crisp bite by serving time.

Authentic tips

  • This braise, like most, tastes even better the next day once the flavors have melded.
  • Adding fresh chili near the end preserves its color and gives textural contrast against the tender beef.

Substitutions

  • Brisket replaces shank with a similar braising time.
  • Reduce the chilies for a milder dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes well for up to 2 months.
