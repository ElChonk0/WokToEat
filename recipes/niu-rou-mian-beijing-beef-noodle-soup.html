Niú Ròu Miàn (Beijing Beef Noodle Soup) 牛肉面

▾

Surprise me with

Beef noodle soup in a clear, savory broth is a staple of Beijing's Hui Muslim noodle shops.

  • Cook 2 hr

  • 45 ml3 tbspSoy sauce
  • 40 g1.5 ozCilantro and chili oil
  • 2 L8 cupsWater

Method

  1. Blanch the beef in boiling water for 5 minutes; drain and rinse.
  2. Return to the pot with the water, ginger, scallion, star anise and Sichuan pepper and simmer gently for 90 minutes until tender.
  3. Lift out the beef, slice thinly across the grain, and season the broth with the soy sauce.
  4. Cook the noodles in a separate pot, drain and divide among bowls.
  5. Add the beef, ladle over the hot broth and top with cilantro and chili oil.

Common mistakes

  • Cloudy broth comes from skipping the blanch.
  • Overcooking the noodles in the broth makes them mushy.

Authentic tips

  • Add a few slices of daikon to the broth for sweetness.
  • Serve with pickled mustard greens.

Substitutions

  • Brisket replaces shank with a similar simmer.
  • Ready-made beef stock saves an hour.

Storage

Leftovers: Store noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 4 days.

Freezing: Freeze the broth and beef for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled mustard greens
  • Soup: Not needed; the noodle soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
