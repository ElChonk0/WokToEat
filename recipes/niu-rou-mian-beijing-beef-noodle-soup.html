Niú Ròu Miàn (Beijing Beef Noodle Soup) 牛肉面

▾

Surprise me with

Beef noodle soup in a clear, savory broth is a staple of Beijing's Hui Muslim noodle shops.

  • Cook 2.5 hr

Method

  1. Blanch the beef for 5 minutes and rinse.
  2. Heat the oil and rock sugar in a Dutch oven over low heat until the sugar melts and turns amber, then turn the beef in it until glossy. Add the ginger, green onion and spices.
  3. Pour in the Shaoxing wine, soy sauces and water, bring to a boil, then cover and simmer gently for 2 hours, until tender. Season the broth with salt.
  4. Cook the noodles and blanch the greens in the same water.
  5. Divide the noodles and greens among bowls, add the sliced beef and ladle over the hot broth. Finish with cilantro, green onion and chili oil.

Common mistakes

  • Cloudy broth comes from skipping the blanch.
  • Overcooking the noodles in the broth makes them mushy.

Authentic tips

  • Add a few slices of daikon to the broth for sweetness.
  • Serve with pickled mustard greens.

Substitutions

  • Brisket replaces shank with a similar simmer.
  • Ready-made beef stock saves an hour.

Storage

Leftovers: Store noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 4 days.

Freezing: Freeze the broth and beef for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled mustard greens
  • Soup: Not needed; the noodle soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
