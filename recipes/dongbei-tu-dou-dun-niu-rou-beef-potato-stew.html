Dōngběi Tǔ Dòu Niú Ròu Dùn (Northeastern Beef and Potato Stew) 东北土豆炖牛肉

Beef stewed with potatoes and sweet potato noodles is a Dongbei winter staple.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 90 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Shandong Beef LunchDinnerBraise / stew

Ingredients

  • 800 g1.75 lbBeef brisket, cubed
  • 400 g14 ozPotatoes
  • 100 g3.5 ozSweet potato noodles
  • 30 g2 tbspSoybean paste
  • 60 g4 tbspGinger, scallion, garlic
  • 30 ml2 tbspSoy sauce
  • 800 ml3.5 cupsWater

Method

  1. Soak the sweet potato noodles in warm water for 20 minutes. Cut the brisket into 4 cm cubes, blanch 5 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  2. Brown the beef with the soybean paste in a heavy pot over medium heat for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the ginger, scallion, garlic, soy sauce and 800 ml water, bring to a boil, cover and simmer 60 minutes.
  4. Add the potatoes in large chunks and cook 15 minutes, then the noodles for 10 minutes more.
  5. Season and serve.

Common mistakes

  • Cutting the potatoes small makes them fall apart.
  • Boiling hard makes the beef stringy.

Authentic tips

  • Add the noodles late so they hold their texture.
  • Serve over rice.

Substitutions

  • Chuck replaces brisket with a shorter stew.
  • Add carrots for sweetness.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months without the potatoes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or cornbread
  • Soup: Not needed; the stew is its own soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
