Bo Luc Lac (Shaking Beef)

▾

Surprise me with

Named for the motion of shaking the wok or pan while searing, bo luc lac cubes tender beef and sears it quickly in a garlicky sauce, traditionally served over watercress and rice with a lime-pepper dip.

Method

  1. Marinate beef cubes in soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sugar for 15 minutes.
  2. Heat a pan or wok very hot with a little oil and garlic; sear the beef in batches, shaking the pan frequently, 2–3 minutes per batch until browned but still pink inside.
  3. Stir in butter at the very end for a glossy finish.
  4. Serve over a bed of watercress and red onion with rice, alongside a lime-black pepper dipping sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Overcrowding the pan, which steams the beef instead of searing it properly.
  • Overcooking the beef, since this dish is traditionally served with the cubes still pink and tender in the center.

Authentic tips

  • Cooking in small batches over very high heat is essential — the pan should never be overcrowded.
  • The lime-black pepper dipping sauce is a distinctly Vietnamese touch that cuts through the richness of the beef.

Substitutions

  • Chuck or ribeye can substitute for sirloin, though tenderer cuts work best given the quick cooking time.
  • Arugula can substitute for watercress if unavailable.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat gently to avoid overcooking further.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with
