Wǔ Xiāng Dòu Gān Bàn Qín Cài (Five-Spice Dried Tofu with Celery) 五香豆干拌芹菜

▾

Surprise me with

Chewy five-spice pressed tofu tossed with crisp celery and sesame oil is a staple Jiangnan cold dish, sold at delis and served as an appetizer on the home table.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 5 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 300 g10 ozFive-spice pressed tofu (dou gan), shredded
  • 250 g9 ozCelery, thinly sliced
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspSesame oil
  • 10 ml2 tspRice vinegar
  • 3 g1/2 tspSugar
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt

Method

  1. Cut the celery on the diagonal into thin slices.
  2. Blanch in boiling water for 30 seconds, then plunge into cold water and drain well.
  3. Shred the tofu into thin strips.
  4. Toss the tofu and celery with the soy sauce, sesame oil, vinegar, sugar and salt.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the celery loses its crunch.
  • Dressing early wilts the celery.

Authentic tips

  • Serve chilled.
  • Shred the tofu thinly so it soaks up the dressing.

Substitutions

  • Firm tofu pan-fried and cubed replaces dried tofu.
  • Toasted peanuts add crunch.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; drain before serving.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
