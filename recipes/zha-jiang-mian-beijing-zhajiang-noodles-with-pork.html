Beijing Zha Jiang Mian with Pork 北京炸酱面

▾

Surprise me with

Zha jiang mian is the defining noodle of Beijing: wheat noodles topped with a pork and fermented soybean sauce and a heap of fresh shredded vegetables.

  • 300 g10 ozPork belly, diced small (or fatty ground pork)
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 90 g1/3 cupYellow soybean paste (huangjiang)
  • 30 g2 tbspSweet bean sauce (tianmianjiang)
  • 150 ml2/3 cupWater
  • 40 g1.5 ozGinger and green onion, minced
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 450 g1 lbHand-pulled or thick wheat noodles
  • 150 g5 ozCucumber, julienned
  • 200 g7 ozBean sprouts, radish, celery and edamame, blanched (cai ma)
  • 88Garlic cloves, raw, for serving

Method

  1. Mix the yellow soybean paste and sweet bean sauce with the water until smooth.
  2. Heat the oil in a flat-bottom wok over medium heat and fry the pork belly for 5 to 6 minutes until it renders and the edges turn golden. Add the ginger and green onion for 30 seconds.
  3. Pour in the bean paste mixture with the Shaoxing wine and sugar.
  4. Lower the heat and fry, stirring often, for 15 minutes, until the sauce is thick, dark and glossy and the oil pools at the edges. This slow frying (zha) is what the dish is named for.
  5. Cook the noodles and rinse in cold water for a chewier texture, if you like. Serve each bowl with a spoonful of sauce and a heap of cucumber and blanched vegetables, with raw garlic on the side, the old Beijing way. Mix well before eating.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the paste makes the whole sauce bitter; add water if it sticks.
  • Too much paste makes it very salty; taste before serving.

Authentic tips

  • Stir the sauce often so it does not catch.
  • Traditional toppings include blanched bean sprouts, shredded cucumber and radish, and edamame.

Substitutions

  • Ground beef or chicken replaces the pork.
  • Hoisin mixed with miso replaces yellow soybean paste.

Storage

Leftovers: Store sauce and noodles separately; the sauce keeps 5 days refrigerated.

Freezing: The sauce freezes for 3 months; cook fresh noodles.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Shredded cucumber, radish and bean sprouts
  • Soup: Not needed; the noodles are the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot jasmine tea
