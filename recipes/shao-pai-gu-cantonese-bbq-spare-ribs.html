Shāo Pái Gǔ (Cantonese BBQ Spare Ribs) 烧排骨

▾

Surprise me with

Spare ribs in a char siu-style marinade, roasted until sticky and red, hang alongside char siu and roast duck in Cantonese siu mei shops.

  • Prep 20 min (plus 4 hr marinating)
  • Cook 45 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Method

  1. Cut shallow slits between the bones and along the thick edge of the rack, so the seasoning gets in and the meat cooks evenly. Pat dry and rub the garlic salt all over, working it into the slits.
  2. Mix the hoisin, ketchup, soy sauce, oyster sauce, wine, sugar, bean curd, five-spice, pepper and garlic and spread it thinly over both sides. Wrap and refrigerate for 4 to 8 hours; much longer and the salt firms up the meat.
  3. Take the ribs out 1 hour before cooking. Heat the oven to 200 C (400 F). Line a tray with foil, add a little water, and set the ribs on a rack over it, meat-side up.
  4. Roast uncovered for 30 to 35 minutes, until the edges start to char. Leaving them uncovered is what gives the charred barbecue edges.
  5. Stir the honey with the hot water until it coats a spoon. Brush it over the ribs and roast for 10 minutes more, until sticky and glossy and the meat has shrunk back from the bone tips. Watch closely: the glaze burns quickly.
  6. Rest for 10 minutes, then cut between the bones and serve with any pan juices.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • High heat from the start burns the sugary marinade before the ribs are tender.
  • Skipping the water in the tray lets the drippings smoke.

Authentic tips

  • Remove the membrane on the back of the rack so the marinade penetrates.
  • Hanging ribs vertically in a hot oven is the siu mei shop method; a rack works fine at home.

Substitutions

  • Pork shoulder strips make char siu with the same marinade.
  • Air fryer: cook rib sections at 180 C (350 F) for 25 minutes, glazing at the end.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days in the refrigerator; reheat in a hot oven.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and blanched gai lan
  • Soup: Winter melon soup
  • Dessert: Egg tarts
  • Beverage: Cold beer
