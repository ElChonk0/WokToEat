Shāo Pái Gǔ (Cantonese BBQ Spare Ribs) 烧排骨

Spare ribs in a char siu-style marinade, roasted until sticky and red, hang alongside char siu and roast duck in Cantonese siu mei shops.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 1 hr 15 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Method

  1. Mix everything except the honey and hot water, rub it over the ribs and refrigerate at least 4 hours or overnight.
  2. Heat the oven to 160 C (325 F). Put the ribs on a rack over a foil-lined tray with a little water, cover with foil and roast 50 minutes.
  3. Uncover, raise the oven to 200 C (400 F) and roast 15 minutes, brushing with the leftover marinade.
  4. Brush with the honey thinned with the hot water and roast 5 to 10 minutes more until sticky and charred at the edges, or finish under the broiler, watching closely.
  5. Rest 5 minutes and cut between the bones.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • High heat from the start burns the sugary marinade before the ribs are tender.
  • Skipping the water in the tray lets the drippings smoke.

Authentic tips

  • Remove the membrane on the back of the rack so the marinade penetrates.
  • Hanging ribs vertically in a hot oven is the siu mei shop method; a rack works fine at home.

Substitutions

  • Pork shoulder strips make char siu with the same marinade.
  • Air fryer: cook rib sections at 180 C (350 F) for 25 minutes, glazing at the end.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days in the refrigerator; reheat in a hot oven.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and blanched gai lan
  • Soup: Winter melon soup
  • Dessert: Egg tarts
  • Beverage: Cold beer
