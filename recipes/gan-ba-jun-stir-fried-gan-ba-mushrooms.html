Gān Ba Jūn (Stir-Fried Dried Gan Ba Mushrooms) 干巴菌

▾

Surprise me with

Gan ba jun, a prized wild mushroom with a meaty texture and smoky flavor, is a symbol of Yunnan's wild mushroom season and sells at high prices in Kunming.

  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Ingredients

  • 300 g10 ozGan ba mushrooms (or mixed wild mushrooms)
  • 60 g2 ozGreen and red chilies
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 60 ml4 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Tear the mushrooms into strips by hand and rinse carefully to remove grit; drain well.
  2. Dry-fry the mushrooms in a wok over medium heat for 3 minutes to evaporate moisture.
  3. Add the oil, garlic and chilies and stir-fry 2 minutes.
  4. Add the mushrooms back with the soy sauce and toss 3 minutes until they are dark and aromatic.
  5. Serve at once.

Common mistakes

  • Wild mushrooms must be cooked thoroughly.
  • Washing too long makes them soggy.

Authentic tips

  • Gan ba jun is a prized wild mushroom; substitute a mix of wild mushrooms if you cannot find it.
  • Never eat foraged mushrooms you cannot identify.

Substitutions

  • Mixed wild or cultivated mushrooms replace gan ba jun.
  • Black trumpet mushrooms are the closest flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Pu'er tea
