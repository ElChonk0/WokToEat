Meen Kuzhambu (Tamil Tamarind Fish Curry)

▾

Surprise me with

Meen kuzhambu is a tangy, spicy tamarind fish curry cooked across Tamil Nadu, flavored with shallots and tempered with mustard seeds, fenugreek and curry leaves.

Method

  1. Dissolve the tamarind in the water and strain.
  2. Heat the oil in a wide pan over medium heat, add the mustard and fenugreek seeds until they pop, then the shallots, garlic and curry leaves for 5 minutes until golden.
  3. Add the tomatoes and cook 4 minutes, then the coriander, chili powder and turmeric for 30 seconds.
  4. Pour in the tamarind water, salt and jaggery and simmer 10 minutes until the oil floats on top.
  5. Slide in the fish, simmer gently 6 to 8 minutes without stirring, and rest 10 minutes before serving with rice.

Cook’s notes

Written for cod loin. Tamil cooks use seer fish, sardines or mackerel.

Common mistakes

  • Stirring breaks the cod; shake the pan.
  • Burnt fenugreek turns the curry bitter.

Authentic tips

  • The curry tastes best the next day.
  • Sesame oil gives the traditional Tamil flavor.

Substitutions

  • Haddock replaces the cod.
  • Coconut paste (2 tablespoons) added with the tomato makes a milder version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.
