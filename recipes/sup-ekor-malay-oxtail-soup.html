Sup Ekor (Malay Oxtail Soup)

Sup ekor, oxtail soup spiced with star anise, cinnamon and cloves, is a Malay and Singaporean favorite served with rice and bread.

  • Cook 3 hr

  • 1 kg2.2 lbOxtail

  • 100 g3.5 ozShallot, garlic, ginger
  • 8 g3 tspStar anise, cinnamon, cloves
  • 150 g5 ozCelery and tomato
  • 15 g1/2 ozFried shallots
  • 10 g2 tspSalt
  • 2.5 L10 cupsWater

Method

  1. Cover the oxtail with cold water, boil 5 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  2. Return to a clean pot with 2.5 L water, the shallot, garlic, ginger and spices and bring to a boil; skim.
  3. Cover and simmer on low for 2.5 hours until the meat falls off the bone.
  4. Add the potatoes and carrots and simmer 25 minutes.
  5. Season with the salt, add the celery and tomato, and serve with fried shallots.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the blanch gives a cloudy broth.
  • Adding vegetables too early makes them mushy.

Authentic tips

  • Skim the fat for a clearer soup.
  • Serve with rice or bread.

Substitutions

  • Beef short ribs replace oxtail.
  • A pressure cooker cuts the simmer to 90 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; skim the fat.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or bread
  • Soup: Not needed; the soup is the main
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Teh tarik
