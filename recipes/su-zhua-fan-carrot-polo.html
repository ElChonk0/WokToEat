Sù Zhuā Fàn (Vegetarian Carrot Polo) 素抓饭

▾

Surprise me with

Polo (zhua fan), rice cooked with carrots and raisins, is the festive dish of the Uyghurs of Xinjiang, traditionally made with lamb; the vegetarian version highlights the sweet carrots and spices.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 40 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Ingredients

  • 320 g11 ozBasmati or long-grain rice
  • 500 g1.1 lbCarrots, julienned
  • 150 g5 ozOnion
  • 50 g2 ozRaisins
  • 3 g1 tspCumin
  • 60 ml4 tbspNeutral oil
  • 6 g1 tspSalt
  • 600 ml2.5 cupsWater

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a heavy pot and fry the onion until golden, 6 minutes.
  2. Add the carrots and cumin and cook 10 minutes until softened.
  3. Add the salt and water and bring to a boil.
  4. Spread the rinsed rice over the top without stirring, cover and cook on low 25 minutes.
  5. Fold in the raisins and fluff.

Common mistakes

  • Stirring the rice makes it gummy.
  • Lifting the lid too often lets steam escape.

Authentic tips

  • Soak the rice 30 minutes for fluffier grains.
  • Add chickpeas for protein.

Substitutions

  • Brown basmati replaces white; add 10 minutes.
  • Chickpeas add protein.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat with a splash of water.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Cucumber and tomato salad
  • Soup: Clear broth
  • Dessert: Dried apricots and nuts
  • Beverage: Black tea
