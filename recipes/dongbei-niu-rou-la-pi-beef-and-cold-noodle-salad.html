Dōngběi Niú Ròu Bàn Miàn (Dongbei Cold Noodles with Beef) 东北牛肉冷面

▾

Surprise me with

Cold noodles with sliced sauced beef and a sweet-sour broth are a summer staple of Dongbei, shared with Korean-Chinese communities.

  • Cook 10 min

  • 400 g14 ozCold noodles (buckwheat or wheat)
  • 300 g10 ozSauced beef, sliced
  • 200 g7 ozCucumber and pear
  • 600 ml2.5 cupsBeef broth
  • 45 ml3 tbspVinegar and sugar
  • 22Boiled egg
  • 5 g1 tspMustard

Method

  1. Cook the noodles until just tender, rinse under cold water and chill.
  2. Mix the beef broth with the vinegar and sugar and chill until very cold.
  3. Slice the sauced beef thinly; slice the cucumber and pear into matchsticks.
  4. Divide the noodles among bowls and arrange the beef, cucumber, pear and halved eggs on top.
  5. Pour over the icy broth and add mustard to taste.

Common mistakes

  • Warm noodles or broth ruins the dish; chill both.
  • Too sweet a broth; keep it balanced and tangy.

Authentic tips

  • A little ice in the broth helps.
  • Serve with kimchi for the Korean-Chinese style.

Substitutions

  • Soba replaces the cold noodles.
  • Sliced deli roast beef is a quick shortcut for sauced beef.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated; dress just before serving.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Kimchi on the side
  • Soup: Chilled broth
  • Dessert: Pear slices
  • Beverage: Iced barley tea
