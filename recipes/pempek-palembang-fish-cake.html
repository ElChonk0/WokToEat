Pempek (Palembang Fish Cake with Cuko Sauce)

Pempek, chewy fish and tapioca cakes served in a dark sweet-sour vinegar sauce (cuko), is the signature dish of Palembang in South Sumatra.

  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Method

  1. Mix the ground fish, tapioca starch, garlic, salt and water into a smooth, firm dough; knead 3 minutes.
  2. Shape into logs about 15 cm long.
  3. Boil in a pot of water for 10-15 minutes until they float; drain and cool.
  4. Slice and pan-fry in a little oil until golden.
  5. Simmer the palm sugar, vinegar, chilies and 200 ml water for the cuko sauce 10 minutes; pour over.

Common mistakes

  • Too much starch makes them rubbery.
  • Over-boiling cracks them.

Authentic tips

  • Fresh wolf-herring is traditional; mackerel works.
  • Add a quail egg inside for kapal selam.

Substitutions

  • Mackerel replaces wolf herring.
  • Pan-fried or boiled both work.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; re-fry.

Freezing: Freezes boiled pempek for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Cuko sauce and noodles
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Es teh
