Hunan Bacon Stir-Fry 腊肉炒蒜苗

▾

Surprise me with

La rou (cured, smoked pork) is a Hunanese preservation tradition dating back generations; this dish stir-fries the smoky cured pork with leeks or dried bean curd for a distinctly savory, chewy home-style dish.

Method

  1. If using la rou, steam or boil briefly to soften and rinse away excess salt, then slice thin.
  2. Sear the sliced pork in a dry pan over medium heat until the fat renders and edges crisp, 4–5 minutes.
  3. Add garlic and dried chili, stir-frying until fragrant.
  4. Add leek and stir-fry 2–3 minutes until just wilted.
  5. Add soy sauce and wine, tossing everything together over high heat before serving.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the pre-steaming step for true la rou, which can leave the dish overly salty and tough.
  • Adding extra oil unnecessarily — the cured pork renders plenty of fat on its own.

Authentic tips

  • True la rou (available at Chinese grocers) has a distinctly different, more intensely smoky flavor than regular bacon, worth seeking out.
  • This is a very quick, high-heat dish — have all ingredients prepped before you start cooking.

Substitutions

  • Thick-cut smoked bacon is a widely available substitute for la rou.
  • Dried tofu strips can replace or supplement the leek.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

  • Dessert: Sliced apple
