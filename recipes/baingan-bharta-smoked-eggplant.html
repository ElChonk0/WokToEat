Baingan Bharta (Smoked Eggplant)

▾

Surprise me with

Baingan bharta, roasted and mashed eggplant cooked with onion and tomato, is a Punjabi classic with a smoky flavor from the open-flame char.

Method

  1. Rub the eggplants with a little oil, prick them, and roast them directly over a gas flame, on a grill or under a broiler for 15 to 20 minutes, turning, until the skin is blackened and the flesh completely soft. The smoke is the essential flavor of bharta.
  2. Cool in a covered bowl for 10 minutes, then peel off the charred skin and mash the flesh roughly with a fork.
  3. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium heat and fry the cumin seeds for 20 seconds, then the onions for 8 minutes until golden. Add the ginger, garlic and chilies for 1 minute.
  4. Add the tomatoes, turmeric, coriander, chili and salt and cook for 6 to 8 minutes until the tomatoes break down and the oil separates. Add the peas for 2 minutes.
  5. Stir in the mashed eggplant and cook for 8 to 10 minutes, stirring, until thick and the flavors meld. Finish with garam masala and cilantro and serve with roti or paratha.

Common mistakes

  • Underchared eggplant lacks smoke.
  • Not cooking down the masala leaves it raw.

Authentic tips

  • Charring directly over a gas flame or charcoal gives the most smoke; a broiler is the equal-effort route on electric or induction.
  • Serve with roti.

Substitutions

  • A grill pan or outdoor grill also works.
  • Peas are optional.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.
