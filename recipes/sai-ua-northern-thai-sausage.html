Sai Ua (Northern Thai Herb Sausage) ไส้อั่ว

▾

Surprise me with

Sai ua, a coarse pork sausage packed with lemongrass, galangal, kaffir lime and red curry paste, is a specialty of Chiang Mai and Lanna cooking.

  • Cook 25 min

  • 1 m3 ftCasings
  • 40 g1.5 ozCilantro and scallion

Method

  1. Pound the lemongrass, galangal and lime leaves with the red curry paste and fish sauce.
  2. Mix with the pork until sticky, then fold in the cilantro and scallion.
  3. Soak the casings, rinse and stuff loosely; twist into coils and prick with a pin.
  4. Grill over low heat, or bake at 180 C (350 F), for 20-25 minutes, turning, until browned and cooked through.
  5. Slice and serve with sticky rice, cabbage and chilies.

Common mistakes

  • Overstuffing bursts the casing.
  • High heat burns the outside before the inside cooks.

Authentic tips

  • Do not skip the prick; steam needs an escape.
  • Chiang Mai sai ua is traditionally served with sticky rice.

Substitutions

  • Ground pork wrapped in foil and grilled replaces casings.
  • Reduce the curry paste for a milder sausage.
  • In the oven: bake at 180°C (350°F) for 20 to 25 minutes, turning once, then broil 2 minutes to brown.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; reheat in a pan.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months, raw or cooked.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sticky rice, raw cabbage and chilies
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
