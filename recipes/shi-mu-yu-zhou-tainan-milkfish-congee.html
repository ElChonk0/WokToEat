Shī Mù Yú Zhōu (Tainan Milkfish Congee) 虱目魚粥

▾

Surprise me with

Milkfish (virgin fish) is Tainan's signature seafood, and a bowl of congee with fresh milkfish, ginger and fried shallots is a classic Tainan breakfast.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 40 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Taiwan Seafood BreakfastLunchSoupRice

  • 200 g1 cupCooked rice or rice
  • 400 g14 ozMilkfish fillets, sliced
  • 1.2 L5 cupsFish stock
  • 30 g2 tbspGinger, julienned
  • 15 g1 tbspFried shallots
  • 15 g1/2 ozCilantro
  • 5 g1 tspSalt
  • 1 g1/4 tspWhite pepper

Method

  1. Rinse the rice and simmer in the stock for 30 minutes until soft and creamy.
  2. Add the ginger and season with the salt.
  3. Slide in the milkfish slices and cook 3 minutes until opaque.
  4. Remove from the heat.
  5. Ladle into bowls and top with shallots, cilantro and white pepper.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the fish makes it dry.
  • Stirring breaks the fish.

Authentic tips

  • Milkfish has many fine bones; buy deboned fillets.
  • Eat at breakfast with fried dough.

Substitutions

  • Cod or tilapia replaces milkfish.
  • Leftover rice shortens the cooking.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; loosen with water.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Fried dough sticks
  • Soup: Not needed; the congee is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
