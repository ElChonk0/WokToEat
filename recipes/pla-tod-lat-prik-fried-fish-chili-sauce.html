Pla Tod Lat Prik (Crispy Fried Fish with Chili Sauce)

▾

Surprise me with

Crispy fried whole fish topped with a sweet, sour and spicy chili sauce is a staple of Thai restaurants and homes, typically using sea bass or snapper.

  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 900 g2 lbWhole fish, scored
  • 45 g1/3 cupCornstarch
  • 40 g3 tbspGarlic
  • 30 g1 ozRed chilies
  • 30 g2 tbspTamarind paste
  • 30 ml2 tbspFish sauce
  • 30 g2 tbspPalm sugar
  • 600 ml2.5 cupsNeutral oil for frying

Method

  1. Pat the fish dry and score deeply on both sides.
  2. Coat with the cornstarch.
  3. Fry in oil at 180 C (350 F) for 8-10 minutes, turning once, until crisp.
  4. Simmer the garlic, chilies, tamarind, fish sauce, sugar and 100 ml water 3 minutes until syrupy.
  5. Pour over the fish.

Common mistakes

  • Oil that is too cool makes the fish greasy.
  • Overcooking dries it.

Authentic tips

  • Use a wide, deep pan or wok.
  • Serve with rice and herbs.

Substitutions

  • Fillets fry in 5 minutes.
  • Bake at 220 C (425 F) for a lighter dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the sauce keeps 4 days separately.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Jasmine rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
