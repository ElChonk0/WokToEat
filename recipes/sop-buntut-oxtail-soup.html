Sop Buntut (Indonesian Oxtail Soup)

▾

Surprise me with

Sop buntut, oxtail soup with potatoes, carrots and nutmeg, shows Dutch influence and is served at restaurants and hotels across Indonesia.

  • Cook 3.5 hr

Method

  1. Blanch the oxtail in boiling water for 10 minutes, drain and rinse. This keeps the broth clear.
  2. Put the oxtail, water, onion, fried garlic, ginger, cloves, nutmeg and cinnamon in a large pot. Bring to a boil, skim, and simmer gently, partly covered, for 3 hours, until the meat pulls easily from the bone.
  3. Chill the broth if you have time and lift off the fat, or skim it well. Season with salt and white pepper.
  4. Add the potatoes and carrots and simmer for 15 to 20 minutes until tender, then the tomatoes for 2 minutes.
  5. Ladle into bowls, scatter with celery leaves, green onion and fried shallots, and serve with rice, lime wedges and sambal.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the blanch gives a cloudy broth.
  • Adding the vegetables too early makes them mushy.

Authentic tips

  • Skim the fat after chilling for a clearer soup.
  • Serve with rice, lime and chili sambal.

Substitutions

  • Beef short ribs replace oxtail.
  • A pressure cooker cuts the simmer to 90 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; skim the fat.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and sambal
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Es jeruk
