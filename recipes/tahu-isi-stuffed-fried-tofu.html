Tahu Isi (Stuffed Fried Tofu)

▾

Surprise me with

Tahu isi, fried tofu pockets stuffed with vegetables and a spicy filling, is a popular Indonesian street snack, eaten with fresh chilies.

Method

  1. Stir-fry the carrots, bean sprouts, garlic, shallot and chilies for 3 minutes; season with salt and cool.
  2. Slit the tofu puffs and stuff with the filling.
  3. Whisk the flour with 100 ml water and salt to a thin batter.
  4. Dip the stuffed tofu and fry at 175 C (350 F) for 3-4 minutes until golden.
  5. Serve with fresh chilies.

Common mistakes

  • Overstuffing splits the tofu.
  • Thick batter makes a heavy coating.

Authentic tips

  • Eat with raw bird's eye chilies.
  • Serve hot.

Substitutions

  • Bean sprouts and carrot are the classic filling.
  • Air-fry at 200 C (400 F) for a lighter dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; recrisp in a hot oven.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Fresh bird's eye chilies
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
