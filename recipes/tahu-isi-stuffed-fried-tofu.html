Tahu Isi (Stuffed Fried Tofu)

▾

Surprise me with

Tahu isi, fried tofu pockets stuffed with vegetables and a spicy filling, is a popular Indonesian street snack, eaten with fresh chilies.

Method

  1. Fry the garlic and shallots in the oil for 1 minute, add the carrot and cabbage for 2 minutes and the bean sprouts for 30 seconds, and season with salt and pepper. Cool.
  2. Cut a slit in each tofu puff and stuff it generously with the vegetables.
  3. Whisk the flours, a pinch of salt and the cold water into a smooth batter.
  4. Heat the oil to 175 C (345 F) in a flat-bottom wok. Dip each stuffed tofu in batter and fry for 3 to 4 minutes, turning, until golden and crisp.
  5. Drain and serve hot with raw green chilies, bitten alongside each piece, the way street vendors serve gorengan.

Common mistakes

  • Overstuffing splits the tofu.
  • Thick batter makes a heavy coating.

Authentic tips

  • Eat with raw bird's eye chilies.
  • Serve hot.

Substitutions

  • Bean sprouts and carrot are the classic filling.
  • Air-fry at 200 C (400 F) for a lighter dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; recrisp in a hot oven.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Fresh bird's eye chilies
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Es teh
