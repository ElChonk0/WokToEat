Qing Zheng Yin Tai Yu (Steamed Black Cod) 清蒸银鳕鱼

▾

Surprise me with

Cantonese steaming with ginger, scallion and hot oil is the standard way to cook very fresh fish; black cod, sold in Hong Kong as silver cod, suits it because its richness stays moist.

  • Cook 10 min

Ingredients

  • 600 g1 lb 5 ozBlack cod (sablefish) fillets, about 150 g each
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 2 g1/3 tspSalt
  • 30 g1 ozGinger, half sliced, half cut in fine strips
  • 40 g1.5 ozScallions, greens in lengths, whites cut in fine strips
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 3 ml1/2 tspDark soy sauce
  • 3 g3/4 tspSugar
  • 15 ml1 tbspWater
  • 0.5 g1/4 tspWhite pepper
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil
  • 3 ml1/2 tspSesame oil
  • 10 g1/3 ozCilantro

Method

  1. Pat the fillets dry, rub them with the wine and salt and leave for 10 minutes. Mix the soy sauces, sugar, water and pepper in a small pan and warm it gently.
  2. Lay the scallion greens on a heatproof plate as a rack, put the fish on top and lay the ginger slices over it. Raising the fish lets the steam reach the underside.
  3. Set a rack in a flat-bottom wok or steamer and bring the water to a full boil before the plate goes in. Steam covered over high heat for 7 to 9 minutes for fillets 2.5 cm (1 in) thick, until the flesh flakes when pressed and is opaque in the center. Black cod is rich and forgiving, but it still turns mushy if overcooked.
  4. Pour off the steaming liquid and discard the cooked ginger and scallion greens. Pile the ginger strips and scallion whites over the fish.
  5. Heat the oil until it shimmers and pour it over the aromatics so they sizzle. Spoon the warm sauce around the fish, add the sesame oil and cilantro and serve at once.

Cook’s notes

Written for black cod fillet (sablefish). Cantonese restaurants sell it as silver cod.

Common mistakes

  • Over-steaming dries the fish; start checking at 7 minutes.
  • Leaving the steaming liquid on the plate makes the dish taste fishy.

Authentic tips

  • Water must be at a full boil before the fish goes in.
  • Pour the oil while it is hot enough to hiss.

Substitutions

  • Cod loin or Chilean sea bass replaces the black cod.
  • Add 1 tablespoon fermented black beans for a stronger flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day in the refrigerator; reheat by steaming 2 minutes.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and blanched choy sum
  • Soup: Pork and watercress soup
  • Dessert: Tong sui sweet soup
  • Beverage: Hot oolong tea
