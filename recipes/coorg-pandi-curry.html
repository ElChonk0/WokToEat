Coorg Pandi Curry

Pandi curry, pork cooked with kachampuli (black vinegar) and a roasted spice blend, is the signature dish of the Kodava people of Coorg in Karnataka.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 90 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 700 g1.5 lbPork shoulder, cubed
  • 45 ml3 tbspKachampuli or vinegar
  • 20 g3/4 ozCoorg curry powder
  • 150 g5 ozOnion and garlic
  • 40 g1.5 ozGinger
  • 15 g1/2 ozGreen chilies
  • 3 g1 tspTurmeric
  • 6 g1 tspSalt
  • 30 ml2 tbspOil

Method

  1. Marinate the pork with the curry powder, turmeric, salt, ginger and half the kachampuli for 30 minutes.
  2. Fry the onion and garlic in the oil until golden, 8 minutes.
  3. Add the pork and chilies and brown 10 minutes.
  4. Add 300 ml water, cover and simmer 60 minutes until tender.
  5. Uncover, add the remaining kachampuli and reduce the gravy.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking on high heat toughens the pork.
  • Adding all the vinegar at the start loses its tang.

Authentic tips

  • Kachampuli is made from garcinia fruit; use vinegar if you can't find it.
  • Serve with kadumbuttu (rice dumplings).

Substitutions

  • Lime juice plus rice vinegar replaces kachampuli.
  • Pork belly adds richness.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Kadumbuttu (rice dumplings) or rice
  • Soup: Cucumber salad
  • Dessert: Payasam
  • Beverage: Black tea
