Yachae-jeon (Korean Vegetable Pancakes) 야채전

▾

Surprise me with

Yachae-jeon, thin crisp pancakes packed with shredded vegetables, is a popular Korean home snack and a staple at holiday tables.

Method

  1. Cut all the vegetables into thin matchsticks of similar length, so the pancake is even and holds together. Mix the dipping soy sauce and vinegar.
  2. Whisk the flour, potato starch and salt with the ice water into a thin batter, just until combined; the cold water and starch make a crisp, lacy edge.
  3. Fold the vegetables into the batter so they are lightly coated rather than swimming in it.
  4. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium heat. Spread half the mixture into a thin round, pressing it flat. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes until the underside is golden and crisp.
  5. Flip, add another tablespoon of oil around the edge, press flat and cook 2 to 3 minutes more. Repeat with the rest. Cut into wedges and serve hot with the dipping sauce.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Too much batter makes a doughy pancake; it should be mostly vegetables.
  • Too little oil gives pale, soft pancakes.

Authentic tips

  • Ice-cold water keeps the batter light and crisp.
  • Leftover vegetables of any kind work.

Substitutions

  • Korean pancake mix (buchim garu) replaces flour and starch.
  • Add a beaten egg for a richer, softer pancake.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days; re-crisp in a dry skillet.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month.
