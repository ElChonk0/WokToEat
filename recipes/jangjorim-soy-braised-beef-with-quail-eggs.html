Jangjorim (Soy-Braised Beef with Quail Eggs) 메추리알 장조림

▾

Surprise me with

Jangjorim, beef braised in soy sauce until it can be pulled into shreds, is a long-keeping Korean banchan; quail eggs and shishito peppers are common additions.

Method

  1. Soak the beef in cold water for 20 minutes, then cut it into 4 chunks.
  2. Simmer the beef in the water with the onion and ginger for 40 minutes, skimming, until tender. Remove the onion and ginger.
  3. Add the soy sauce, sugar, syrup and garlic and simmer 20 minutes.
  4. Add the quail eggs and simmer 10 minutes, then the peppers for 3 minutes.
  5. Cool in the liquid, then shred the beef along the grain and store it all in the braising liquid.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Adding soy at the start toughens the beef; cook it until tender first.
  • Overcooking the peppers makes them drab.

Authentic tips

  • Spoon a little of the liquid over rice with butter for a childhood favorite.
  • Lean cuts are traditional so it keeps well cold.

Substitutions

  • Chicken eggs, halved, replace quail eggs.
  • Pork shoulder for dwaeji jangjorim.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 weeks in the refrigerator in its liquid.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Rice and other banchan
  • Soup: Miyeokguk
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
