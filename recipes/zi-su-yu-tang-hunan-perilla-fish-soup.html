Zi Su Yu Tang (Perilla Fish Soup) 紫苏鱼汤

Perilla leaves are a common Hunan seasoning for fish, and a milky fish soup, made by frying fish and boiling it hard, is a staple of home cooking.

  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 500 g1 lb 2 ozCod loin, in 4 cm pieces
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil
  • 30 g1 ozGinger, sliced
  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 1.2 L5 cupsBoiling water
  • 300 g10 ozFirm tofu, in cubes
  • 30 g1 ozPerilla leaves, shredded
  • 2 g3/4 tspWhite pepper
  • 8 g1.5 tspSalt, for the soup
  • 20 g3/4 ozScallions, sliced

Method

  1. Pat the cod dry and season with 3 g salt.
  2. Heat the oil in a pot over medium-high heat, fry the ginger for 30 seconds, then sear the cod 1 minute per side until lightly golden.
  3. Add the wine, then pour in the boiling water and bring to a hard boil; boil with the lid on for 10 minutes until the broth turns milky.
  4. Add the tofu, salt and white pepper and simmer 5 minutes.
  5. Stir in the perilla and scallions off the heat and serve at once.

Cook’s notes

Written for cod loin.

Common mistakes

  • Cold water gives a thin, clear broth; use boiling water straight from the kettle.
  • Perilla loses its fragrance if cooked, so add it last.

Authentic tips

  • A hard boil emulsifies the oil and gelatin into the milky color.
  • Mint with extra ginger works if you cannot find perilla.

Substitutions

  • Haddock replaces the cod.
  • Mint or Thai basil replaces the perilla.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and stir-fried greens
  • Soup: The recipe is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
