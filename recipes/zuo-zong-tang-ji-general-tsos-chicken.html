General Tso's Chicken 左宗棠雞

▾

Surprise me with

General Tso's chicken was created in Taipei in the 1950s by the Hunanese chef Peng Chang-kuei and named after a Qing-dynasty general; a sweeter version became an American Chinese takeout icon.

Method

  1. Toss the chicken with the marinade soy sauce, Shaoxing wine and egg, then with the coating cornstarch until each piece is well covered.
  2. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven to 170 C (340 F) and fry the chicken in batches for 4 minutes until cooked. Raise the oil to 190 C (375 F) and fry it all again for 1 to 2 minutes until crisp and golden. Drain.
  3. Mix the sauce soy sauce, dark soy sauce, vinegar, sugar, water and sauce cornstarch.
  4. Heat 2 tablespoons of the oil in a flat-bottom wok over low heat and fry the chilies for 30 seconds until dark red, then the garlic and ginger for 20 seconds.
  5. Stir the sauce and pour it in. When it boils and turns glossy, add the chicken and sesame oil and toss quickly to coat. Scatter with green onion and serve. The dish was created in Taiwan by chef Peng Chang-kuei in the 1950s and named after a Hunanese general.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Letting the chicken sit in the sauce turns the crust soggy; toss and serve.
  • Burnt chilies make the whole dish bitter; pull them as soon as they darken.

Authentic tips

  • Peng's original was savory, sour and hot rather than sweet; cut the sugar by half for that style.
  • Fry the chicken twice, the second time at 190 C (375 F) for 1 minute, for an extra-crisp crust.

Substitutions

  • Air fryer: spray the coated chicken with oil and cook at 200 C (400 F) for 15 minutes, shaking twice.
  • Chili flakes replace whole dried chilies.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; reheat in a hot oven or air fryer to re-crisp.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months; reheat in a hot oven.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and garlic broccoli
  • Soup: Hot and sour soup
  • Dessert: Fresh pineapple
  • Beverage: Oolong tea
