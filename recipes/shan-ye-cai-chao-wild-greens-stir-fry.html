Shan Ye Cai Chao Dan (Wild Greens and Egg Stir-Fry) 山野菜炒蛋

Wild mountain greens such as shepherd's purse, fern and bracken feature in Anhui's mountain cooking, often stir-fried with egg.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 8 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Anhui Tofu & Vegetarian LunchDinnerStir-fryDeep-friedVegetarian30 min or lessEgg

Ingredients

  • 400 g14 ozWild greens or spinach
  • 55Eggs
  • 15 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Blanch the greens in boiling water for 20 seconds, plunge into cold water, squeeze dry and chop.
  2. Beat the eggs with half the salt.
  3. Heat half the oil in a wok over medium-high heat and scramble the eggs into large curds; remove.
  4. Add the remaining oil and garlic for 10 seconds, then the greens for 1 minute.
  5. Return the eggs, season with the remaining salt and toss 20 seconds.

Common mistakes

  • Not squeezing the greens makes the dish watery.
  • Overcooking the eggs makes them dry.

Authentic tips

  • Use young, tender greens.
  • Spinach and chive work well.

Substitutions

  • Spinach or chives replace wild greens.
  • Omit the egg for a vegan dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Tofu soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
