Sòng Sǎo Yú Gēng (Hangzhou Fish Chowder) 宋嫂鱼羹

▾

Surprise me with

Song Sao fish chowder is a thick Hangzhou soup of shredded fish, bamboo shoots and mushrooms, named for a Song-dynasty cook who, according to legend, made it for an emperor.

  • 400 g14 ozFish fillets (sea bass or mandarin fish)
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine and ginger juice, for steaming
  • 44Dried shiitake, soaked and finely shredded
  • 50 g2 ozBamboo shoots, finely shredded
  • 30 g1 ozJinhua ham, finely shredded
  • 900 ml3.75 cupsChicken stock
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt
  • 25 g3 tbspCornstarch, mixed with 60 ml water
  • 22Egg yolks, beaten
  • 30 ml2 tbspChinkiang vinegar
  • 1 g1/2 tspWhite pepper
  • 10 g1/3 ozGinger, finely julienned
  • 10 g1/3 ozCilantro or green onion

Method

  1. Put the fish on a plate with the Shaoxing wine and ginger juice and steam for 6 to 8 minutes until just cooked. Cool slightly, then flake it into large pieces, removing any bones and skin.
  2. Bring the stock to a boil with the shiitake, bamboo shoots and half the ham. Season with soy sauce and salt.
  3. Stir in the cornstarch slurry gradually until the soup is thick enough to coat a spoon. The soup is named for 'Sister-in-law Song', a Song dynasty Hangzhou cook.
  4. Gently fold in the fish with its juices, then drizzle in the egg yolks in a thin stream, stirring slowly so they form fine golden threads.
  5. Turn off the heat, add the vinegar and white pepper, and top with the remaining ham, ginger and cilantro. It should taste savory, lightly sour and peppery.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the vinegar before the soup comes off the heat dulls its aroma.
  • Stirring too hard breaks the fish into a paste.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with extra vinegar and white pepper at the table.
  • Mandarin fish is traditional; sea bass is a fine substitute.

Substitutions

  • Cod or tilapia replaces the mandarin fish or bass.
  • Julienned ham is traditional; omit for a lighter version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat gently.

Freezing: Freezes the broth only, for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
