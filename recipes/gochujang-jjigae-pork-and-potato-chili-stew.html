Gochujang Jjigae (Gochujang Stew with Pork and Potato) 고추장찌개

▾

Surprise me with

Gochujang jjigae, a thick red stew of pork, potato and zucchini in chili paste, is everyday Korean home cooking and a cousin of doenjang jjigae.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 25 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Fry the pork in a pot over medium heat for 4 minutes until browned and some fat renders.
  2. Add the gochujang, gochugaru, doenjang and garlic and stir 1 minute.
  3. Add the water, potatoes and onion, bring to a boil and simmer 12 minutes until the potatoes are nearly tender.
  4. Add the zucchini, tofu and soy sauce and simmer 5 minutes more.
  5. Add the green onion and chili and serve bubbling.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the zucchini at the start turns it to mush.
  • Too much gochujang makes it cloying; balance with doenjang.

Authentic tips

  • Frying the paste in the pork fat deepens the flavor.
  • Canned tuna is a popular quick swap for pork.

Substitutions

  • Canned tuna or spam replace pork.
  • Mushrooms and extra tofu for a vegetarian version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months; the potatoes soften.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice, kimchi and gyeran-mari
  • Soup: This is the stew
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
