Xiāng Gū Ròu Piàn (Pork with Shiitake in a Clay Pot) 香菇肉片煲

▾

Surprise me with

Huangshan and Dabie mountain shiitake are celebrated, and stewed with pork in a clay pot they make a rich, mushroomy dish.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 1 hr
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

  • 400 g14 ozPork shoulder, sliced
  • 40 g1.5 ozDried shiitake, soaked (soaking water kept)
  • 15 ml1 tbspNeutral oil
  • 40 g1.5 ozGinger and garlic, sliced
  • 10 g2 tspSugar
  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 20 ml4 tspLight soy sauce
  • 10 ml2 tspDark soy sauce
  • 300 ml1.25 cupsMushroom soaking water
  • 15 g1/2 ozGreen onion

Method

  1. Fry the pork in the oil in a clay pot or Dutch oven over medium heat for 5 minutes until it renders and browns.
  2. Add the ginger, garlic and sugar and stir until the sugar caramelizes slightly.
  3. Add the shiitake, Shaoxing wine, soy sauces and the strained soaking water.
  4. Cover and simmer over low heat for 40 minutes, until the pork is tender and the mushrooms have absorbed the rich sauce.
  5. Uncover, reduce for a few minutes, scatter with green onion and serve from the pot.

Common mistakes

  • Not browning the pork leaves a pale, greasy dish.
  • Discarding the soaking liquid loses flavor.

Authentic tips

  • Skim the fat if you prefer a lighter dish.
  • Serve in the pot at the table.

Substitutions

  • Pork shoulder replaces the belly.
  • Fresh shiitake replace dried.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
