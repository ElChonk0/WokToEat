Xian Dan Huang Xia (Salted Egg Yolk Shrimp) 咸蛋黄虾

Salted egg yolk shrimp is a Hong Kong and Cantonese tze char style dish in which fried shrimp are tossed in a crumbly, buttery salted egg yolk coating.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 500 g1.1 lbShrimp, peeled
  • 44Salted egg yolks, steamed and mashed
  • 45 g1/3 cupCornstarch
  • 30 g2 tbspButter
  • 10 g1/3 ozCurry leaves or scallion
  • 10 g2 tspChilies
  • 300 ml1.25 cupsNeutral oil for frying

Method

  1. Steam the salted egg yolks for 10 minutes until firm, then mash with a fork into fine crumbs.
  2. Pat the shrimp dry and toss with the cornstarch. Fry in 180 C (350 F) oil in two batches for 1-2 minutes until golden; drain.
  3. Melt the butter in a clean wok over medium heat, add the mashed yolk and stir 1-2 minutes until it foams and turns sandy.
  4. Add the chilies and curry leaves for 10 seconds, then the shrimp, and toss to coat.

Common mistakes

  • Letting the yolk brown makes it bitter; keep the heat at medium and move quickly.
  • Wet shrimp make the coating gummy, so dry them thoroughly before starching.

Authentic tips

  • The yolk should froth into fine bubbles; that is what gives the sandy texture.
  • Fry the curry leaves in the oil for a few seconds first for extra fragrance.

Substitutions

  • Squid or chicken pieces take the same sauce.
  • Unsalted cooked egg yolks mashed with 1/2 teaspoon salt stand in for salted yolks.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the coating softens within an hour.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Winter melon soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
