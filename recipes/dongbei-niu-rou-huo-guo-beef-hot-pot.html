Dōngběi Niú Ròu Huǒ Guō (Northeastern Beef Hot Pot) 东北牛肉火锅

▾

Surprise me with

Hot pot is a communal winter meal across northern China; the Dongbei version features beef, sauerkraut and glass noodles in a rich broth.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 600 g1.3 lbBeef, thin slices
  • 250 g9 ozSauerkraut
  • 100 g3.5 ozGlass noodles
  • 350 g12 ozTofu and cabbage
  • 90 ml6 tbspSesame sauce
  • 40 g3 tbspGinger and scallion
  • 2 L8 cupsStock

Method

  1. Soak the glass noodles for 15 minutes. Rinse the sauerkraut and slice finely.
  2. Simmer the stock with the sauerkraut and ginger in a hot pot for 15 minutes.
  3. Add the tofu and cabbage and cook 5 minutes.
  4. At the table, swish the thin beef slices in the broth 10-15 seconds until they turn pale; dip in the sesame sauce.
  5. Add the noodles toward the end.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the beef makes it tough.
  • Cutting the beef too thick.

Authentic tips

  • Freeze the beef for 30 minutes before slicing very thin.
  • Garlic and chili oil in the dipping sauce are classic.

Substitutions

  • Lamb slices are the classic alternative.
  • Frozen tofu soaks up the broth.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; use leftover broth for noodle soup.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sesame buns
  • Soup: Not needed; the pot broth serves as soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
