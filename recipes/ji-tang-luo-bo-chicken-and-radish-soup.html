Jī Tāng Luó Bo (Chicken and Radish Soup) 鸡汤萝卜

▾

Surprise me with

Chicken and radish soup is a Shandong winter soup, the sweet radish absorbing the savory chicken broth.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 60 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 800 g1.75 lbChicken, chopped, bone-in
  • 500 g1.1 lbDaikon, chunked
  • 30 g2 tbspGinger
  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 2 L8 cupsWater
  • 8 g1.5 tspSalt
  • 20 g2 tbspScallion

Method

  1. Cover the chicken with cold water, bring to a boil for 3 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  2. Return the chicken to the pot with the water, ginger and wine, bring to a boil and skim.
  3. Reduce to a low simmer, cover and cook 40 minutes.
  4. Add the daikon and simmer 20 minutes more until translucent and tender.
  5. Season with salt and scatter over scallion.

Common mistakes

  • A hard boil clouds the soup.
  • Adding the salt early toughens the chicken.

Authentic tips

  • Skim for a clear, golden broth.
  • Add the daikon after the chicken is nearly done so it keeps its shape.

Substitutions

  • Bone-in thighs replace the whole chicken.
  • Turnip replaces daikon.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; the broth improves.

Freezing: Freezes the broth well for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed buns
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
