Yáng Yù Niú Ròu (Yunnan Beef and Potato Stew) 洋芋炖牛肉

▾

Surprise me with

Beef and potatoes stewed together are a highland Yunnan staple, a hearty dish for cold mountain evenings.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 90 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 650 g1.4 lbBeef brisket, cubed
  • 500 g1.1 lbPotatoes
  • 8 g1/4 ozDried chilies
  • 40 g3 tbspGinger and garlic
  • 45 ml3 tbspSoy sauce
  • 800 ml3.5 cupsWater
  • 15 g1/2 ozCilantro

Method

  1. Cover the beef with cold water, boil 5 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  2. Brown the beef in a heavy pot over medium-high heat 6 minutes.
  3. Add the ginger, garlic, chilies, soy sauce and water, bring to a boil, cover and simmer 60 minutes.
  4. Add the potato chunks and cook 25 minutes more until tender.
  5. Garnish with cilantro.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the potatoes too early makes them dissolve.
  • Boiling hard toughens the beef.

Authentic tips

  • Better the next day.
  • Serve in bowls with chili oil.

Substitutions

  • Chuck replaces brisket.
  • Waxy potatoes hold their shape best.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months without the potatoes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Pu'er tea
