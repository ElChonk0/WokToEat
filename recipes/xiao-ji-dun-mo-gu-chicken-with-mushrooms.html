Xiǎo Jī Dùn Mó Gū (Dongbei Chicken Stewed with Mushrooms) 小鸡炖蘑菇

▾

Surprise me with

Chicken stewed with dried wild mushrooms and sweet potato noodles is the signature dish of Dongbei (northeast) cooking, a warming winter stew.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 50 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 10 g2 tspSugar
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Soak the dried mushrooms in warm water for 30 minutes, rinse, trim stems and keep the liquid; soak the noodles in warm water 20 minutes.
  2. Heat the oil with the sugar in a heavy pot over medium heat until it turns amber, add the chicken and brown 8 minutes.
  3. Add the ginger, scallion, garlic, both soy sauces and the mushrooms and stir 2 minutes.
  4. Add 600 ml water (including the mushroom liquid), bring to a boil, then cover and simmer 35 minutes.
  5. Add the noodles for the last 10 minutes, stirring once, until they are tender and have absorbed the sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the browning gives a pale, flat stew.
  • Overcooked noodles turn mushy; add them near the end.

Authentic tips

  • Strain and use the mushroom soaking water for deeper flavor.
  • Fresh hazel mushrooms are hard to find; shiitake is a good substitute.

Substitutions

  • Dried shiitake replace hazel mushrooms.
  • Bone-in thighs and drumsticks work well.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; the flavor improves.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months without the noodles.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or cornbread
  • Soup: Not needed; the stew is its own soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
