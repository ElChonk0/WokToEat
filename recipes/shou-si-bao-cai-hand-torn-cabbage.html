Shǒu Sī Bāo Cài (Hand-Torn Cabbage) 手撕包菜

▾

Surprise me with

Hand-torn cabbage is a Hunan home staple, in which cabbage is torn into rough pieces and stir-fried with dried chilies and garlic over fierce heat for a smoky, slightly charred flavor.

  • Cook 8 min

  • 600 g1.3 lbGreen cabbage, torn
  • 8 g1/4 ozDried chilies
  • 30 g2 tbspGarlic
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 10 ml2 tspChinkiang vinegar
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Tear the cabbage into 4 cm pieces by hand, discarding the thick core; dry the pieces well.
  2. Heat the wok until it smokes, add the oil, and fry the dried chilies and garlic for 10 seconds.
  3. Add the cabbage and stir-fry hard for 3 minutes, until the edges char and the leaves wilt but the stems stay crisp.
  4. Add the soy sauce, vinegar, sugar and salt around the edge of the wok and toss 1 minute.

Common mistakes

  • A crowded or cool wok steams the cabbage instead of charring it; cook in two batches if needed.
  • Cooking longer than 4 minutes makes it limp.

Authentic tips

  • Tearing rather than cutting gives more surface area for charring.
  • Add the vinegar around the wok edge so it sizzles and flavors the cabbage.

Substitutions

  • Napa cabbage is more tender but wilts faster.
  • Smoked tofu strips add protein and smokiness.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day but loses its crunch.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Tofu soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
