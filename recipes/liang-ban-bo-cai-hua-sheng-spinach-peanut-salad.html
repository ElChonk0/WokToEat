Liáng Bàn Bō Cài Huā Shēng (Spinach and Peanut Salad) 凉拌菠菜花生

▾

Surprise me with

Blanched spinach tossed with peanuts, vinegar and sesame oil is a standard Beijing cold dish, a fixture of the starter platters served before northern banquets.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 3 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 400 g14 ozSpinach
  • 80 g3 ozRoasted peanuts
  • 15 g1 tbspGarlic, minced
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspRice vinegar
  • 15 ml1 tbspSesame oil
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt

Method

  1. Blanch the spinach in boiling water for 30 seconds and plunge into ice water; drain.
  2. Squeeze out as much water as you can, then cut into 5 cm lengths.
  3. Mix the garlic, soy sauce, vinegar, sesame oil and salt.
  4. Toss the spinach with the dressing and top with the peanuts.

Common mistakes

  • Not squeezing the spinach makes the salad watery.
  • Dressing too early wilts it.

Authentic tips

  • Add the peanuts last for crunch.
  • Serve chilled.

Substitutions

  • Cashews replace peanuts.
  • Baby bok choy replaces spinach.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the spinach waters out.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or noodles
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
