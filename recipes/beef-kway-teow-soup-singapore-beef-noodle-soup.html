Singapore Beef Kway Teow Soup

Beef kway teow soup, flat rice noodles in a clear beef broth with offal and slices of meat, is a Teochew hawker dish in Singapore.

  • Cook 2 hr
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 300 g10.5 ozFlat rice noodles, fresh
  • 400 g14 ozBeef brisket
  • 600 g1.3 lbBeef bones
  • 150 g5 ozBeef balls
  • 150 g5 ozBean sprouts
  • 30 g1 ozGarlic oil and shallots
  • 100 g3.5 ozGinger and daikon
  • 20 g3/4 ozCilantro
  • 8 g1.5 tspSalt
  • 2 L8 cupsWater

Method

  1. Blanch the bones and brisket in boiling water for 5 minutes; drain and rinse.
  2. Simmer in 2 L water with the ginger and daikon for 2 hours; lift out the brisket and slice thinly.
  3. Season the broth with the salt and add the beef balls for the last 5 minutes.
  4. Blanch the noodles and sprouts and divide among bowls.
  5. Add the brisket and balls, ladle on the hot broth and top with garlic oil, shallots and cilantro.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the blanch gives a murky broth.
  • Overcooked noodles go soft.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with chili and dark soy.
  • Add tripe for the full hawker experience.

Substitutions

  • Chuck replaces brisket.
  • Tripe and tendon are traditional additions.

Storage

Leftovers: Store noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 4 days.

Freezing: Freeze the broth for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sambal and pickled chilies
  • Soup: Not needed; the soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Kopi
