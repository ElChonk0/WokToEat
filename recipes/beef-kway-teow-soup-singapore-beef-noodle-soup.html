Singapore Beef Kway Teow Soup

▾

Surprise me with

Beef kway teow soup, flat rice noodles in a clear beef broth with offal and slices of meat, is a Teochew hawker dish in Singapore.

Method

  1. Blanch the bones and brisket in boiling water for 5 minutes, then drain and rinse. This keeps the broth clear.
  2. Put them back in a clean pot with the water, ginger, daikon, star anise and cinnamon. Bring to a boil, skim, then simmer gently for 2 hours, until the brisket is tender. Lift out the brisket and slice it; strain the broth.
  3. Season the broth with the soy sauce, fish sauce, rock sugar and salt. It should be clean, beefy and lightly sweet from the daikon.
  4. Just before serving, bring the broth to a boil and add the beef balls for 3 minutes.
  5. Blanch the noodles and bean sprouts for 30 seconds and divide among bowls. Lay the raw sirloin slices on top and ladle the boiling broth over; it cooks the thin beef to pink in seconds.
  6. Add the brisket and beef balls and top with garlic oil, fried shallots, salted vegetable, cilantro and green onion. Serve with the chili-vinegar dip.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the blanch gives a murky broth.
  • Overcooked noodles go soft.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with chili and dark soy.
  • Add tripe for the full hawker experience.

Substitutions

  • Chuck replaces brisket.
  • Tripe and tendon are traditional additions.

Storage

Leftovers: Store noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 4 days.

Freezing: Freeze the broth for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sambal and pickled chilies
  • Soup: Not needed; the soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Kopi
