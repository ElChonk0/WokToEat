Beef Nilaga

▾

Surprise me with

A simple, clear beef and vegetable soup, nilaga is one of the most basic and beloved Filipino comfort foods, valued for its clean, restorative broth and hearty combination of beef and root vegetables.

Method

  1. Put the beef in a pot with cold water, bring to a boil and boil for 5 minutes. Drain and rinse, then return to the clean pot with the measured water. This keeps the broth clear.
  2. Add the onion and peppercorns, bring to a boil, skim, then cover and simmer gently for 2 hours, until the beef is tender.
  3. Season with fish sauce. Add the potatoes, corn and saba and simmer for 15 minutes.
  4. Add the green beans and cabbage for 5 minutes, then the pechay for 1 minute, until just wilted. Nilaga simply means 'boiled'; the clean broth is the point.
  5. Taste and add salt. Serve in bowls with rice and fish sauce with calamansi on the side.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the cabbage too early, which overcooks it and dulls its flavor and color.
  • Rushing the initial beef simmer, which doesn't give it enough time to become properly tender.

Authentic tips

  • This is an intentionally simple, clean-tasting soup — resist the urge to over-season the broth.
  • Skimming the foam thoroughly during the initial simmer keeps the broth clear rather than cloudy.

Substitutions

  • Short rib adds richness.
  • A pressure cooker cuts the simmer to 45 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes the broth and beef for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and fish sauce with calamansi
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Calamansi juice
