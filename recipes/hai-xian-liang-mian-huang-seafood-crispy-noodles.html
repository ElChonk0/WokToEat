Hǎi Xiān Liǎng Miàn Huáng (Seafood over Crispy Pan-Fried Noodles) 海鲜两面黄

▾

Surprise me with

Hong Kong-style chow mein is a cake of noodles fried golden on both sides (liang mian huang) and topped with a seafood and vegetable gravy, a banquet and restaurant classic.

Method

  1. Steam the noodles in a loose nest over boiling water for 10 minutes, or dip pre-steamed noodles in boiling water for 20 seconds. Drain, fluff with chopsticks and let them cool and dry for 5 minutes. Toss the seafood with the cornstarch and pepper. Mix the slurry.
  2. Heat a flat-bottom wok or large skillet over medium-high heat with 2 tablespoons of oil. Spread the noodles into an even cake and fry without stirring for 5 to 7 minutes, shaking the pan now and then, until golden and crisp underneath. Flip, add 1 tablespoon of oil around the rim and crisp the other side. Slide onto a platter.
  3. Blanch the greens and carrot in boiling water for 30 seconds and drain well.
  4. Heat the remaining oil over high heat and sear the scallops for 30 seconds per side, then add the shrimp and squid and stir-fry for about 1 minute, until the shrimp turn pink and the squid curls. Lift everything out; it will finish in the gravy.
  5. Fry the garlic, ginger and green onion for 20 seconds, then the mushrooms for 1 minute. Splash in the wine, add the stock, oyster sauce, soy sauce, sugar and salt and bring to a boil.
  6. Return the seafood and vegetables, stir the slurry in gradually until the gravy is glossy and thick enough to coat a spoon, and add the sesame oil. Pour over the crisp noodles at the table and serve at once.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Flipping too early breaks the noodle cake; let it set and brown first.
  • A thin gravy soaks the noodles; make it thick and glossy.

Authentic tips

  • Diners add Chinkiang vinegar at the table to cut the richness.
  • Use a plate to flip the noodle cake, like a tortilla.

Substitutions

  • Chicken or char siu replace seafood.
  • Use frozen seafood mix.

Storage

Leftovers: Store topping separately for 2 days; re-crisp the noodles in a pan.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Red vinegar and chili oil
  • Soup: Winter melon soup
  • Dessert: Almond jelly
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
