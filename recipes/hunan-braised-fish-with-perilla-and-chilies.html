Hóng Shāo Yú Kuài (Hunan Braised Fish with Perilla) 红烧鱼块

▾

Surprise me with

Hunan home cooks braise fish chunks with fermented black beans, ginger and fresh perilla for a pungent, savory, spicy dish that is quintessential everyday Hunan fare.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 25 min
  • Difficulty Medium
  • Draft Hunan Seafood LunchDinnerBraise / stewSpicy

Ingredients

  • 900 g2 lbCarp or catfish, cut into chunks
  • 15 g1/2 ozDried chilies
  • 15 g1 tbspFermented black beans
  • 40 g3 tbspGarlic and ginger
  • 20 g2/3 ozPerilla leaves
  • 15 ml1 tbspDark soy sauce
  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 60 ml4 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Pat the fish chunks dry and sear in the oil over medium-high heat for 2 minutes per side until golden; lift out.
  2. In the same pan fry the garlic, ginger, dried chilies and black beans for 1 minute.
  3. Add the wine, dark soy sauce and 250 ml water, return the fish, cover and simmer 12 minutes.
  4. Uncover and reduce the sauce for 3 minutes until glossy.
  5. Stir in the perilla off the heat and serve.

Common mistakes

  • Stirring the fish breaks the chunks; swirl the pan instead.
  • Overcrowding the pan while searing steams the fish.

Authentic tips

  • Perilla gives a mint-like, slightly anise aroma.
  • Thai basil is the closest substitute.

Substitutions

  • Thick cod steaks replace carp.
  • Thai basil replaces perilla.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
