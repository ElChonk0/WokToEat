Sambal Stingray (Grilled Stingray with Sambal)

Sambal stingray, grilled over charcoal and topped with spicy sambal, is a Singapore hawker favorite popularized at the Newton and Lau Pa Sat food centers.

  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 30 ml2 tbspLime juice

  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Pound the shallot, garlic and belacan and fry with the sambal in the oil for 8 minutes until thick and fragrant.
  2. Season the stingray lightly with salt and place on a banana leaf.
  3. Spread the sambal generously over the top.
  4. Grill over medium-high heat, or roast at 220 C (425 F), for 15 minutes until the flesh is opaque.
  5. Finish with the lime juice and sugar.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking makes the ray rubbery.
  • Burning the sambal makes it bitter.

Authentic tips

  • Skate wing is a good substitute.
  • Serve with lime and rice.

Substitutions

  • Skate wing replaces stingray.
  • Foil replaces banana leaf.

Storage
