Tandoori Chicken

▾

Surprise me with

Tandoori chicken, marinated in yogurt and spices and cooked in a clay oven, was popularized in Delhi by the Punjabi restaurant Moti Mahal after the 1947 Partition.

Method

  1. Score the chicken legs to the bone in two or three places.
  2. Mix the yogurt, chili powder, ginger-garlic paste, garam masala, lemon juice and mustard oil, with a teaspoon of salt, and rub it in; marinate at least 4 hours or overnight.
  3. Heat the oven to 230 C (450 F) with a rack over a tray.
  4. Roast the legs for 30 minutes, turning once, until the edges char and the juices run clear.
  5. Broil 2-3 minutes for extra char; rest 5 minutes and serve with lemon, onion rings and mint chutney.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the scoring leaves the inside bland.
  • Rinsing the yogurt off before cooking loses the marinade.

Authentic tips

  • Overnight marination gives the best flavor.
  • Kashmiri chili gives the red color without heat.

Substitutions

  • Chicken breast works but dries; roast 8 minutes less.
  • A grill or broiler replaces the tandoor.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; serve cold in wraps.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Naan and onion salad with mint chutney

  • Dessert: Kulfi
  • Beverage: Masala chai
