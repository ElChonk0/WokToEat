Jiàng Pái Gǔ (Beijing Sauce-Braised Ribs) 酱排骨

▾

Surprise me with

Jiang pai gu, ribs braised in soy sauce and sweet bean paste, are a Beijing deli and home dish eaten with steamed buns.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 75 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Method

  1. Cut the ribs into 4 cm pieces, cover with cold water, boil 5 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  2. Melt the rock sugar in the pot over low heat until amber, add the ribs and turn for 2 minutes.
  3. Add the aromatics, sweet bean sauce, soy sauce and star anise, then 700 ml water.
  4. Bring to a boil, cover and simmer on low for 50 minutes.
  5. Uncover and boil 8-10 minutes, stirring, until the sauce is thick and the ribs are glazed.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the blanching makes the sauce murky.
  • Reducing the sauce too far makes it salty.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with steamed buns to mop up the sauce.
  • A pressure cooker does the braise in 25 minutes.

Substitutions

  • Baby back ribs shorten the braise by 15 minutes.
  • Hoisin and soy sauce replace sweet bean sauce.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; reheat in the sauce.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
