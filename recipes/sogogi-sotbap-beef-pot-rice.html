Sogogi Sotbap (Beef and Mushroom Pot Rice) 소고기 솥밥

▾

Surprise me with

Sotbap, rice cooked to order in an individual stone or iron pot with toppings, is a revived traditional Korean style now popular in Seoul restaurants.

Method

  1. Mix the beef with the soy sauce, sugar, garlic and half the sesame oil.
  2. Heat the rest of the sesame oil in a heavy pot or Dutch oven and fry the mushrooms 2 minutes, then add the drained rice and stir 1 minute.
  3. Add the water, bring to a boil, cover and cook on low 13 minutes.
  4. Lay the beef over the rice, add the butter, cover and cook 5 minutes more, then rest off the heat 10 minutes.
  5. Mix the soy sauce, green onion and gochugaru as a seasoning sauce. Serve the rice with an egg yolk on each portion and the sauce to mix in.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Lifting the lid during cooking lets steam escape and leaves the rice hard.
  • Too much water makes it mushy.

Authentic tips

  • Pour hot water into the pot after serving to make sungnyung (scorched rice tea).
  • A rice cooker works: add the beef for the last 10 minutes.

Substitutions

  • Chicken thigh or salmon replace beef.
  • Add soybean sprouts for kongnamul-bap.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Kimchi and namul
  • Soup: Doenjang jjigae
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
