Kǎo Fū (Shanghai Braised Wheat Gluten) 烤麸

▾

Surprise me with

Kao fu, spongy wheat gluten braised in sweet soy sauce with wood ear and peanuts, is a Shanghai cold dish on the New Year table.

  • 300 g10 ozKao fu (wheat gluten), torn
  • 20 g2/3 ozDried wood ear mushrooms
  • 20 g2/3 ozDried shiitake mushrooms
  • 40 g1.5 ozPeanuts
  • 45 ml3 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspDark soy sauce
  • 30 g2 tbspSugar
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Soak the wood ear and shiitake in warm water for 30 minutes; slice or tear them, keeping the soaking liquid.
  2. Soak the kao fu in water, squeeze out the water and tear into 3 cm pieces.
  3. Fry the kao fu in the oil over medium-high heat for 4-5 minutes until golden; lift out.
  4. Add the mushrooms and peanuts and stir-fry 2 minutes.
  5. Return the kao fu with the soy sauces, sugar and 250 ml of the mushroom soaking liquid; simmer 30 minutes until the liquid is absorbed.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the squeeze makes the kao fu bland and watery.
  • Under-braising leaves it bland inside.

Authentic tips

  • Better chilled; it is traditionally served cold on the New Year table.
  • Add extra sugar to taste; Shanghai cooking leans sweet.

Substitutions

  • Roasted peanuts can be omitted for a nut-free version.
  • Fresh wheat gluten from an Asian market gives the best texture.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; served cold.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or congee
  • Soup: Tofu soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
