Yukgaejang (Spicy Beef Soup) 육개장

▾

Surprise me with

A hearty, spicy shredded beef soup loaded with vegetables and gochugaru, yukgaejang was traditionally made for royal households and is now a beloved comfort food, especially popular for its warming, restorative qualities.

Method

  1. Simmer beef in water, covered, 1 hour until tender; remove, reserving the broth, and shred once cool enough to handle.
  2. Whisk gochugaru, garlic, soy sauce, and sesame oil together, then toss with the shredded beef to coat.
  3. Return the seasoned beef to the reserved broth and bring to a simmer.
  4. Add bean sprouts, green onion, and fernbrake if using; simmer 20–25 minutes until the vegetables are tender.
  5. Taste and adjust seasoning with more soy sauce or gochugaru before serving.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the step of seasoning the shredded beef separately before returning it to the broth, which results in a less flavorful final soup.
  • Rushing the initial beef simmer, which doesn't give it enough time to become tender enough to shred easily.

Authentic tips

  • Seasoning the shredded beef directly with the gochugaru mixture before returning it to the broth builds much deeper flavor than seasoning the broth alone.
  • This soup freezes and reheats exceptionally well, making it a great batch-cooking candidate.

Substitutions

  • Dried bracken can be skipped; add extra bean sprouts.
  • Shredded chicken replaces beef.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; the soup improves.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.
