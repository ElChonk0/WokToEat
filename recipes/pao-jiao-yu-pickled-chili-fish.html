Pào Jiāo Yú (Fish with Pickled Chilies) 泡椒鱼

▾

Surprise me with

Pao jiao yu is a Sichuan home-style dish built on pao jiao, the pickled red chilies fermented in a salt brine jar found in nearly every Sichuan kitchen, which give the fish a bright, sour heat.

  • 700 g1.5 lbFish fillets (tilapia or grass carp), sliced 6 mm (1/4 in)
  • 10 ml2 tspSalt and Shaoxing wine, for the fish
  • 11Egg white
  • 15 g2 tbspCornstarch
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 80 g3 ozPickled red chilies (pao la jiao), chopped
  • 30 g1 ozPickled ginger, sliced
  • 30 g1 ozGarlic and ginger, sliced
  • 500 ml2 cupsStock
  • 3 g1/2 tspSugar
  • 1 g1/2 tspWhite pepper
  • 20 g3/4 ozGreen onion, sliced

Method

  1. Toss the fish slices with a pinch of salt, the Shaoxing wine (or a splash of water), the egg white and the cornstarch until coated in a thin film. Velveting keeps the delicate fish whole and silky in the broth.
  2. Heat the oil in a pot or flat-bottom wok over medium heat and fry the pickled chilies and pickled ginger for 2 minutes until the oil turns orange and smells tangy. Add the garlic and ginger for 30 seconds.
  3. Pour in the stock, add the sugar and white pepper and simmer for 5 minutes so the pickles flavor the broth.
  4. Lower the heat to a gentle simmer and add the fish a slice at a time. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes without stirring, just until opaque.
  5. Pour into a deep bowl and scatter with green onion. The broth is sour, salty and bright from the pickled chilies.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling the fish breaks the delicate slices; keep the broth just below a simmer.
  • Skipping the pickled ginger loses the sourness that defines the dish.

Authentic tips

  • A splash of the pickling brine adds tang.
  • Use the bones to make the stock if you have them.

Substitutions

  • Cod or snapper fillets replace the tilapia.
  • A little sugar balances very sour pickles.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
