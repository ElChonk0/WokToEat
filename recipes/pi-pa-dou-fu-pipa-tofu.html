Pi Pa Dou Fu (Pipa Tofu with Shrimp) 琵琶豆腐

Pipa tofu, deep-fried dumplings of mashed tofu and shrimp shaped in spoons to look like a pipa lute, is a Cantonese banquet dish served in a light oyster sauce gravy.

  • Prep 30 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Method

  1. Mash the tofu and squeeze out the water in a cloth. Mix it with the shrimp, egg white, cornstarch, salt, pepper, sesame oil and green onion into a smooth paste.
  2. Oil a dozen Chinese soup spoons, fill each with paste and smooth the top.
  3. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven to 170 C (340 F). Slide the paste off the spoons into the oil and fry in batches for 4 to 5 minutes until golden and puffed.
  4. Blanch the bok choy and arrange it on a plate with the tofu.
  5. Bring the stock and oyster sauce to a boil, thicken with the cornstarch mixed with a little water and pour over.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Wet tofu makes a loose paste that breaks in the oil; squeeze it well.
  • Too hot oil browns them before the center sets.

Authentic tips

  • Dip the spoons in oil so the paste slides off cleanly.
  • Serve with sweet chili or Worcestershire as a snack.

Substitutions

  • Fish paste replaces shrimp.
  • Use ground pork for a meatier version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator; re-crisp in a hot oven.

Freezing: The fried dumplings freeze for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Winter melon soup
  • Dessert: Mango pudding
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
