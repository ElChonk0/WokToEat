Cōng Yóu Bàn Miàn (Shanghai Scallion Oil Noodles) 葱油拌面

▾

Surprise me with

Cong you ban mian, thin noodles tossed in slowly fried scallion oil and soy sauce, is a Shanghai classic that turns the simplest ingredients into a deeply fragrant dish.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 400 g14 ozThin wheat noodles
  • 200 g7 ozScallions, cut into lengths
  • 90 ml6 tbspNeutral oil
  • 45 ml3 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspDark soy sauce
  • 15 g1 tbspSugar
  • 15 g1 tbspFried shallots

Method

  1. Cut the scallions into 5 cm lengths, keeping whites and greens apart.
  2. Heat the oil in a pan over low heat, add the whites and fry 5 minutes, then the greens, and fry another 5 minutes until dark golden and crisp.
  3. Add both soy sauces and the sugar to the pan and simmer 1 minute, then remove from the heat.
  4. Boil the noodles until just tender, drain well and toss with the scallion oil.
  5. Top with the fried shallots and serve at once.

Common mistakes

  • Frying the scallions too fast burns them; low heat is essential.
  • Overcooking the noodles makes them mushy.

Authentic tips

  • Make extra oil to keep in the fridge for a week; it improves everything from rice to eggs.
  • A fried egg on top makes it a meal.

Substitutions

  • Spaghetti or any thin noodle works.
  • Add shrimp for a non-vegetarian version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated; stir in a little extra oil when reheating.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Blanched greens
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
