Chicken Xacuti (Goan Roasted Coconut Curry)

▾

Surprise me with

Xacuti, a Goan curry of roasted coconut and whole spices including poppy seed, is a dish of the state's Catholic cuisine, shaped by Portuguese contact.

Method

  1. Rub the chicken with turmeric and half the salt.
  2. Dry-roast the coconut in a skillet over medium-low heat for 8 to 10 minutes, stirring constantly, until deep brown and fragrant. Lift it out, then roast the chilies and all the whole spices for 2 to 3 minutes. Grind them with the coconut and one sliced onion, roasted until brown, into a thick paste. This deeply roasted coconut is what makes xacuti dark and complex.
  3. Heat the oil in a pot over medium heat and fry the remaining onion for 8 minutes until golden, then the garlic and ginger for 1 minute and the tomato for 3 minutes.
  4. Add the chicken and cook for 5 minutes, then the xacuti paste, and fry for 3 to 4 minutes until fragrant.
  5. Add the water, tamarind and remaining salt, cover and simmer for 25 to 30 minutes until the chicken is tender and the gravy thick. Serve with rice or pao.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the spices makes the curry bitter.
  • Under-roasting loses depth.

Authentic tips

  • Poppy seeds thicken and enrich the gravy.
  • Serve with rice or pao.

Substitutions

  • Desiccated coconut roasted in a dry pan replaces fresh.
  • Poppy seeds can be omitted; add extra coconut.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; the flavor improves.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pao (Goan bread) or rice
  • Soup: Kachumber salad
  • Dessert: Bebinca
  • Beverage: Lime soda
