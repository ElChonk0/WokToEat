Hēi Hú Jiāo Tiě Bǎn Niú (Iron-Plate Black Pepper Beef) 黑胡椒鐵板牛

▾

Surprise me with

Sizzling iron-plate dishes with black pepper sauce are a Taiwanese restaurant staple, served with noodles and a fried egg.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

  • 500 g1.1 lbBeef steak or sirloin, sliced
  • 6 g2 tspBlack pepper, crushed
  • 200 g7 ozOnion and bell pepper
  • 30 g2 tbspButter
  • 45 ml3 tbspSoy sauce
  • 30 g2 tbspKetchup
  • 15 ml1 tbspCornstarch slurry

Method

  1. Heat a cast-iron plate or skillet until smoking hot.
  2. Sear the sliced beef in a little oil 1 minute; remove.
  3. Fry the onion and pepper 2 minutes, then add the butter, crushed pepper, soy sauce and ketchup.
  4. Stir in the slurry and simmer 1 minute until thick.
  5. Return the beef and serve sizzling on the hot plate.

Common mistakes

  • A cool pan steams the beef.
  • Overcooking makes the beef tough.

Authentic tips

  • Black pepper should be coarsely cracked.
  • Serve with noodles and a fried egg.

Substitutions

  • Ribeye or strip steak replaces sirloin.
  • Serve on a cast-iron skillet if no iron plate.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; reheat gently.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Noodles with a fried egg
  • Soup: Corn soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Black tea
