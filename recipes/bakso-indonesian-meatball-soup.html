Bakso (Indonesian Beef Meatball Soup)

▾

Surprise me with

Bakso, bouncy beef meatballs in a clear broth with noodles, is Indonesia's most popular street food, sold from carts across the country.

Method

  1. Process the beef with the starch, garlic, ice and salt for 2 minutes to a sticky paste.
  2. Bring a pot of water to a bare simmer and shape the paste into balls with wet hands, dropping them in.
  3. Simmer 8-10 minutes until they float and are firm.
  4. Heat the broth, cook the noodles and greens in it for 2 minutes.
  5. Divide among bowls, add the meatballs and top with fried shallots, celery, sambal and soy sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard makes the meatballs tough.
  • Warm beef gives a dense, rubbery texture; keep everything cold.

Authentic tips

  • Ice keeps the mix cold and bouncy.
  • Add a boiled egg or fried tofu.

Substitutions

  • Store-bought beef meatballs save time.
  • Chicken stock replaces beef broth.

Storage

Leftovers: Store meatballs and broth separately; the broth keeps 4 days.

Freezing: Freeze meatballs for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sambal and fried shallots
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Es teh
