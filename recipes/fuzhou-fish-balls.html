Fuzhou Stuffed Fish Balls in Soup 福州鱼丸

▾

Surprise me with

Fuzhou fish balls hide a small pork filling inside a springy shell of fish and sweet potato starch; served in clear broth with vinegar and pepper, they are among the city’s best-known street foods.

  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Method

  1. Mix pork, soy and green onion for the filling; chill.
  2. Blend the fish with salt, adding the iced water gradually, until a smooth paste; beat in the starch until very sticky.
  3. With wet hands, flatten a spoonful of paste, add a small ball of filling, close and roll smooth.
  4. Drop into barely simmering water and cook until they float, 4–5 minutes.
  5. Heat the stock, add the fish balls, and serve with celery leaves, pepper, a few drops of sesame oil and vinegar to taste.

Common mistakes

  • Letting the paste warm up; keep everything cold or it won’t turn springy.

Authentic tips

  • Slapping the paste against the bowl for a few minutes builds the bounce Fuzhou fish balls are known for.

Substitutions

  • Frozen Fuzhou fish balls from Chinese grocers are a fine weeknight shortcut.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the fridge.

Freezing: Freeze cooked fish balls for up to 2 months; drop straight into simmering broth.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is a soup
