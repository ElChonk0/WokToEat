Shī Mù Yú Dù Tāng (Milkfish Belly Soup) 虱目魚肚湯

Milkfish belly, the fattiest and most prized part, is simmered with ginger in a clear broth and is another Tainan breakfast staple.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Taiwan Seafood BreakfastLunchDinnerSoup30 min or less

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 500 g1.1 lbMilkfish belly
  • 40 g1.5 ozGinger, sliced
  • 1 L4 cupsWater or stock
  • 6 g1 tspSalt
  • 15 ml1 tbspRice wine
  • 15 g1/2 ozCilantro
  • 1 g1/4 tspWhite pepper

Method

  1. Bring the water or stock to a boil with the ginger and simmer 5 minutes.
  2. Add the milkfish belly in a single layer.
  3. Simmer gently 5-6 minutes until just opaque.
  4. Season with the salt, wine and pepper.
  5. Garnish with cilantro and serve at once.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard breaks the delicate belly.
  • Overcooking makes it dry.

Authentic tips

  • The belly is fatty and sweet.
  • Serve with congee or rice.

Substitutions

  • Fish belly from tilapia or sea bass replaces milkfish belly.
  • Add ginger and a splash of rice wine.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
