Jiāo Yán Pái Gǔ (Salt-and-Pepper Pork Chops) 椒盐排骨

▾

Surprise me with

Salt-and-pepper pork chops are a Cantonese restaurant and dai pai dong classic, in which fried pork is tossed with toasted pepper salt, chilies and garlic.

Method

  1. Cut the pork into palm-size pieces, then slice each horizontally into thinner pieces, across the grain. Pound both sides with the back of a knife to tenderize them.
  2. Mix the marinade cornstarch, baking soda, wine, oyster sauce, soy sauce, water, sugar and salt, work it into the pork by hand for 1 minute and leave for 30 to 60 minutes. The baking soda keeps lean pork tender. Mix the garlic salt, seasoning salt and white pepper in a small bowl.
  3. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven or flat-bottom wok to 190 C (375 F). Mix the egg into the pork, then coat each piece in the coating cornstarch and shake off the excess.
  4. Fry in small batches for about 1.5 minutes, until pale golden and cooked through, so the pieces do not stick together. Drain on a rack.
  5. Raise the oil to 220 C (425 F) and fry all the pieces again in batches for 20 to 30 seconds, until deep golden and crisp. The first fry cooks the meat; the second dries the crust so it stays crunchy.
  6. Pour off all but 1 tablespoon of oil. Over medium heat fry the garlic and chili until fragrant, then the bell pepper for 30 seconds over high heat, then the green onion.
  7. Return the pork and toss for 30 seconds, sprinkling in the seasoning salt as you go. Turn off the heat, toss 20 seconds more and serve at once, while the crust is crisp.

Common mistakes

  • Oil that is not hot enough makes the coating greasy so that it slides off; test with a pinch of cornstarch, which should sizzle at once.
  • Tossing the pork with the seasoning long before serving softens the crust.

Authentic tips

  • Toast the pepper salt in a dry pan for 1 minute to deepen the flavor.
  • The second fry is what makes the crust shatter.

Substitutions

  • Chicken wings or thighs take the same coating and cook in about the same time.
  • Extra-firm tofu cubes make a vegetarian version; fry until deeply golden.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days; recrisp in a 200 C (400 F) oven or air fryer for 6 minutes.

Freezing: Freeze fried pieces for 1 month; reheat from frozen in a hot oven, not the microwave.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice

  • Dessert: Fresh orange slices
