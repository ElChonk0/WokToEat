Gaeng Pa Neua (Thai Jungle Curry with Beef) แกงป่าเนื้อ

▾

Surprise me with

Jungle curry, a coconut-free curry of herbs and chilies, was cooked in the forests of northern Thailand and is among the spiciest of Thai curries.

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a pot over medium heat and fry the curry paste for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring, until fragrant and darker. Jungle curry has no coconut milk, so the paste is fried in oil.
  2. Add the beef and stir for 1 minute to coat it in the paste.
  3. Pour in the water and bring to a boil, then add the krachai, eggplants, long beans, baby corn and peppercorns. Simmer for 5 to 7 minutes until the eggplant is tender.
  4. Season with fish sauce and the small pinch of sugar; the curry should be hot, salty and herbal rather than sweet.
  5. Turn off the heat and stir in the lime leaves, basil and chilies. Serve with rice; it is meant to be fiery and thin.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the beef makes it tough.
  • Adding coconut milk would turn it into a different curry.

Authentic tips

  • Add extra chilies for authentic heat.
  • Krachai is key; use galangal if you can't find it.

Substitutions

  • Pork or chicken take the same paste.
  • Fresh fingerroot (krachai) can be replaced by galangal.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Jasmine rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
