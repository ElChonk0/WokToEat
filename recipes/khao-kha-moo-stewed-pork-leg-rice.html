Khao Kha Moo (Stewed Pork Leg Rice) ข้าวขาหมู

▾

Surprise me with

Khao kha moo, pork leg stewed in sweet soy broth and served over rice with a boiled egg, is a Thai-Chinese street-food staple of Bangkok.

Method

  1. Blanch the pork in boiling water for 10 minutes; drain and rinse.
  2. Fry the garlic and coriander root until golden, then add the soy sauce, sugar, five-spice and water.
  3. Add the pork, cover and simmer on low for 2 hours until the skin is soft and sticky.
  4. Add the peeled boiled eggs for the last 20 minutes.
  5. Slice the pork and serve over rice with the egg, sauce and pickled mustard greens.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard toughens the meat.
  • Skipping the blanch gives a cloudy, greasy sauce.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with a sharp chili-garlic-vinegar sauce.
  • Hock gives the most gelatinous texture.

Substitutions

  • Pork hock is the easiest cut to find.
  • A pressure cooker cuts the braise to 50 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; the sauce sets.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled mustard greens and chili-garlic sauce
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
