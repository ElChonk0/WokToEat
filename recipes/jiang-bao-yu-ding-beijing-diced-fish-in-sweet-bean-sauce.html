Jiàng Bào Yú Dīng (Diced Fish in Sweet Bean Sauce) 酱爆鱼丁

▾

Surprise me with

Jiang bao, quick frying in sweet bean sauce (tian mian jiang), is a Beijing technique best known with diced chicken; the same sauce suits firm fish.

Method

  1. Mix the cod with the egg white, cornstarch and salt and rest 10 minutes.
  2. Heat 60 ml of the oil in a wok over medium-high heat and fry the fish for 1 minute, stirring gently, until just set; lift out.
  3. Pour off the oil, add the remaining 30 ml, and fry the sweet bean sauce over low heat for 1 minute until fragrant.
  4. Add the sugar, wine and water and simmer 1 minute until the sauce is thick.
  5. Add the carrot, cucumber and scallions, return the fish, and toss for 30 seconds. Finish with the sesame oil.

Cook’s notes

Written for cod loin. The classic jiang bao dish uses diced chicken or pork.

Common mistakes

  • High heat burns the bean sauce; keep it at low to medium.
  • Overcooking the cucumber turns it soft.

Authentic tips

  • Add the vegetables last so they stay crisp.
  • Serve with thin pancakes or steamed buns.

Substitutions

  • Haddock replaces the cod.
  • Hoisin plus a pinch of salt replaces sweet bean sauce.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or thin pancakes
  • Soup: Clear tomato egg soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
