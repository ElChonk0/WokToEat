Lumpiang Shanghai (Filipino Pork Spring Rolls)

Lumpiang Shanghai, thin crisp pork spring rolls, are a Filipino party staple with Chinese roots, served with sweet chili sauce.

  • Prep 40 min
  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Finely mince the carrot, onion and garlic.
  2. Mix with the pork, egg and soy sauce until sticky.
  3. Place a tablespoon of filling at the edge of a wrapper, roll tightly into a thin cigar shape and seal with water.
  4. Fry in oil at 170 C (340 F) in batches for 3-4 minutes until deep golden and cooked through.
  5. Drain and serve with the sweet chili sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Loose rolls burst in the oil.
  • Oil that is too hot browns the wrapper before the pork cooks.

Authentic tips

  • Thin rolls (finger-size) cook fastest.
  • Freeze uncooked rolls on a tray.

Substitutions

  • Ground chicken replaces pork.
  • Air-fry at 200 C (400 F) for a lighter version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; recrisp in a hot oven.

Freezing: Freeze uncooked rolls for 2 months; fry from frozen.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sweet chili sauce
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
