Dendeng Balado (Padang Crispy Beef with Chili Sauce)

▾

Surprise me with

Dendeng balado, thin beef fried crisp and topped with red chili sauce, is a classic of Padang (West Sumatra) cooking.

  • 30 ml2 tbspLime juice
  • 100 ml6 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Pound the beef thin and rub with the coriander and salt; dry on paper towels.
  2. Heat the oil in a wide pan to medium-high and fry the beef in batches for 3-4 minutes per side until crisp and brown.
  3. Pour off all but 2 tablespoons of oil and fry the blended chilies, shallot, garlic and tomato for 8 minutes into a thick sambal.
  4. Add the lime juice.
  5. Spoon the sambal over the beef and serve with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Wet beef doesn't crisp.
  • Too little frying leaves the sambal raw.

Authentic tips

  • Slice the beef thin against the grain.
  • Dendeng is traditionally smoked and dried first.

Substitutions

  • Beef jerky replaces fresh dendeng.
  • Reduce the chilies for a milder dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; recrisp in a pan.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Es teh
