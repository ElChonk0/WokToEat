Beef Keema (Spiced Ground Beef)

▾

Surprise me with

A versatile spiced ground meat dish found across South Asia, keema simmers ground beef with onion, tomato, and warming spices — often served with peas, alongside rice, naan, or stuffed into pastries.

Method

  1. Fry onion in oil until golden, 6–8 minutes.
  2. Add ginger-garlic paste and cook 1–2 minutes until fragrant.
  3. Add cumin, coriander, and chili powder, stirring briefly, then add tomato.
  4. Cook until the tomato breaks down, 4–5 minutes.
  5. Add ground beef, breaking it up, and cook until browned and no longer pink, 8–10 minutes.
  6. Add peas and a splash of water; simmer 8–10 minutes until the peas are tender. Stir in garam masala and garnish with cilantro.

Common mistakes

  • Not breaking up the ground beef finely enough while cooking, which leaves large clumps instead of the desired loose, spiced texture.
  • Adding the garam masala too early, which cooks off some of its aroma — stir it in near the end.

Authentic tips

  • This dish is very versatile — it can be served over rice, with naan, or used as a filling for samosas or stuffed parathas.
  • A splash of water while simmering helps the peas cook through without the mixture drying out too much.

Substitutions

  • Ground lamb is traditional.
  • Peas can be replaced by diced potatoes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; better the next day.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with
