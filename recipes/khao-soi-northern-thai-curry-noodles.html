Khao Soi (Northern Thai Curry Noodles)

▾

Surprise me with

Khao soi comes from Thailand's northern Lanna region, reflecting influence from neighboring Myanmar and Yunnan — a rich coconut curry broth poured over egg noodles and topped with a tangle of crispy fried noodles for contrast.

Method

  1. Heat the oil and the thick top of the coconut milk in a Dutch oven over medium heat until the cream splits. Stir in the curry paste, curry powder, turmeric, coriander and cardamom and fry for 3 minutes until dark and fragrant. The dry spices give khao soi its distinctive northern, Burmese-influenced flavor.
  2. Add the chicken and turn to coat, then pour in the remaining coconut milk and stock. Simmer gently, partly covered, for 35 to 40 minutes, until the chicken is very tender.
  3. Season with fish sauce, soy sauce and palm sugar. The broth should be rich, salty and slightly sweet.
  4. Heat the frying oil in a small pan to 180 C (350 F) and fry a handful of the noodles for 30 to 60 seconds until golden and crisp. Drain.
  5. Boil the remaining noodles for 1 to 2 minutes and divide among bowls. Add a piece of chicken, ladle the curry over and top with the crispy noodles.
  6. Serve with shallots, pickled mustard greens, lime and chili oil for each person to add.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the crispy fried noodle topping, which is the dish's signature textural contrast, not an optional garnish.
  • Using only red curry paste without any khao soi-specific spice blend, which misses the dish's characteristic curry-powder warmth.

Authentic tips

  • Dedicated khao soi paste (sold at Southeast Asian grocers) gives a more authentic, complex flavor than plain red curry paste alone.
  • Frying the noodle topping in the same oil you'll use for the curry paste saves a pan and adds flavor.

Substitutions

  • Beef or tofu can replace chicken with a similar method.
  • Dried egg noodles, boiled, work if fresh aren't available.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; store fried noodle topping separately to keep it crisp.

Freezing: The curry and chicken freeze well for up to 2 months; fry noodles fresh.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled mustard greens, shallots and lime
  • Soup: Not needed; the curry noodles are the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
