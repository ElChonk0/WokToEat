Shandong Cong Shao Niu Rou (Shandong Scallion-Braised Beef) 山东葱烧牛肉

Cong shao, scallion braising, is a signature Shandong technique, in which large scallions are fried and braised with meat for a sweet, savory sauce.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 90 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 800 g1.75 lbBeef brisket, cubed
  • 250 g9 ozLarge scallions
  • 45 ml3 tbspSoy sauce
  • 20 g4 tspRock sugar
  • 30 g2 tbspGinger
  • 45 ml3 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 600 ml2.5 cupsWater

Method

  1. Cut the brisket into 4 cm cubes, blanch 5 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  2. Heat a little oil in a heavy pot and fry half the scallions until golden and fragrant, about 3 minutes; set aside the rest.
  3. Add the beef, ginger, soy sauce, sugar, wine and 600 ml water, bring to a boil, cover and simmer 75 minutes.
  4. Add the remaining scallions and cook 10 minutes with the lid off to reduce the sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the scallions makes the sauce bitter.
  • Boiling hard toughens the beef.

Authentic tips

  • Use large, thick scallions; the sweetness comes from slow cooking.
  • Serve over rice.

Substitutions

  • Chuck replaces brisket.
  • Leeks can replace some scallions.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed buns or rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
