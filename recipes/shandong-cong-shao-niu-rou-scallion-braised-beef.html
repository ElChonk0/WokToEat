Shāndōng Cōng Shāo Niú Ròu (Shandong Scallion-Braised Beef) 山东葱烧牛肉

▾

Surprise me with

Cong shao, scallion braising, is a signature Shandong technique, in which large scallions are fried and braised with meat for a sweet, savory sauce.

Method

  1. Blanch the beef for 5 minutes and rinse.
  2. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven over medium-low heat and fry half the scallions for 8 to 10 minutes, until deep golden and fragrant, making a scallion oil (cong you). Shandong's thick da cong scallions are the star of cong shao dishes.
  3. Add the ginger and beef and fry for 3 minutes, then the rock sugar until it melts.
  4. Add the Shaoxing wine, soy sauces, star anise and hot water, bring to a boil, cover and simmer for 1.5 to 2 hours, until tender.
  5. Add the remaining scallions and reduce over medium-high heat for 10 minutes, until the sauce is glossy and thick and the fresh scallions are soft and sweet.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the scallions makes the sauce bitter.
  • Boiling hard toughens the beef.

Authentic tips

  • Use large, thick scallions; the sweetness comes from slow cooking.
  • Serve over rice.

Substitutions

  • Chuck replaces brisket.
  • Leeks can replace some scallions.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed buns or rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
