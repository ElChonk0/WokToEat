Bái Cài Mèn Dōng Gū (Braised Shiitake with Bok Choy) 白菜焖冬菇

▾

Surprise me with

Glossy braised shiitake ringed with blanched bok choy is a Cantonese banquet and Lunar New Year dish; the mushrooms symbolize good fortune.

  • Cook 35 min

Method

  1. Rinse the mushrooms twice, then soak them in warm water (40 to 50 C / 105 to 120 F) for at least 30 minutes, weighted with a plate so they stay under, or overnight in cold water. They should be soft right through. Cut off the stems, squeeze them gently and keep the soaking water, leaving any grit at the bottom.
  2. Rinse the bok choy well, as grit hides at the base. Bring a pot of water to a rolling boil with the blanching salt and oil and blanch the bok choy for about 2 minutes, until bright green and just tender. Dip it in cold water, drain and arrange it in a ring on a platter.
  3. Heat the oil in a flat-bottom wok over low heat and fry the ginger and garlic for 40 to 50 seconds until fragrant. Add the mushrooms and fry for 1 minute, then splash in the wine.
  4. Add the soaking water, vegetarian oyster sauce, soy sauces, sugar and salt. Bring to a boil, then cover and braise over low heat for 15 to 20 minutes, until a chopstick slides easily through the thickest mushroom and the liquid has reduced by about half.
  5. Mix the cornstarch with 2 tablespoons of the braising liquid and stir it in over low heat, then bring back to a boil until glossy. Add the sesame oil.
  6. Pile the mushrooms in the center of the bok choy ring and spoon the sauce over.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Short soaking leaves the mushrooms tough in the middle.
  • Overcooking the bok choy loses its bright color.

Authentic tips

  • Thick-capped flower mushrooms (hua gu) are the prized grade for this dish.
  • Oil in the blanching water keeps the greens glossy.

Substitutions

  • Regular oyster sauce if it does not need to be vegetarian.
  • Broccoli or gai lan replace bok choy.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; the mushrooms improve, the greens do not.

Freezing: The braised mushrooms freeze for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and steamed fish
  • Soup: Winter melon soup
  • Dessert: Sweet tangyuan
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
