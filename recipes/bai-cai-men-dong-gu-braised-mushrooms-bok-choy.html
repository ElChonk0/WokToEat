Bái Cài Mèn Dōng Gū (Braised Shiitake with Bok Choy) 白菜焖冬菇

Glossy braised shiitake ringed with blanched bok choy is a Cantonese banquet and Lunar New Year dish; the mushrooms symbolize good fortune.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 30 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 1616Dried shiitake mushrooms, soaked overnight
  • 300 ml1 1/4 cupsMushroom soaking water
  • 500 g1.1 lbBaby bok choy, halved
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil
  • 15 g1/2 ozGinger, sliced
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 30 ml2 tbspVegetarian oyster sauce (mushroom sauce)
  • 10 ml2 tspLight soy sauce
  • 5 ml1 tspDark soy sauce
  • 8 g2 tspRock sugar or brown sugar
  • 5 g2 tspCornstarch
  • 5 ml1 tspSesame oil

Method

  1. Squeeze the mushrooms, trim the stems and keep the strained soaking water.
  2. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a pot over medium heat, fry the ginger 30 seconds, add the mushrooms and fry 2 minutes, then add the wine, soaking water, vegetarian oyster sauce, soy sauces and sugar. Cover and simmer gently 25 minutes until the mushrooms are tender and plump.
  3. Meanwhile blanch the bok choy in boiling water with the remaining oil and a pinch of salt for 1 to 2 minutes, drain and arrange around a plate.
  4. Thicken the mushroom sauce with the cornstarch mixed with a spoon of water and stir in the sesame oil.
  5. Pile the mushrooms in the center of the bok choy and pour the sauce over.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Short soaking leaves the mushrooms tough in the middle.
  • Overcooking the bok choy loses its bright color.

Authentic tips

  • Thick-capped flower mushrooms (hua gu) are the prized grade for this dish.
  • Oil in the blanching water keeps the greens glossy.

Substitutions

  • Regular oyster sauce if it does not need to be vegetarian.
  • Broccoli or gai lan replace bok choy.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; the mushrooms improve, the greens do not.

Freezing: The braised mushrooms freeze for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and steamed fish
  • Soup: Winter melon soup
  • Dessert: Sweet tangyuan
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
