Xinghua Fried Rice Vermicelli 兴化炒米粉

▾

Surprise me with

Xinghua is the old name for Putian, whose hair-thin, sun-dried rice vermicelli is a regional pride; fried with pork, oysters and vegetables, it is served at almost every Putian celebration.

  • 50 g1 cupGarlic chives, cut

Method

  1. Soak the vermicelli in warm water 5 minutes until pliable; drain well.
  2. Fry the shallots in oil until golden, add the pork and shiitake and stir-fry until the pork browns.
  3. Add cabbage and carrot and stir-fry 2 minutes; add the oysters and cook 30 seconds.
  4. Pour in the stock and soy sauce and bring to a boil.
  5. Add the noodles and toss with chopsticks over medium-high heat until they absorb the liquid, 3–4 minutes.
  6. Fold in the chives and serve.

Common mistakes

  • Over-soaking the vermicelli; it finishes cooking in the stock and turns mushy if already soft.

Authentic tips

  • Fry the shallots slowly until golden; the shallot oil is the main flavour in the noodles.

Substitutions

  • Thin dried rice noodles replace Xinghua vermicelli.
  • Shrimp replaces oysters.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot pan with a splash of water.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Fish ball soup
  • Soup: Not needed; the noodles are the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
