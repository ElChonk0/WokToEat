Dǎi Kǎo Niú Ròu (Dai Lemongrass Grilled Beef) 傣味烤牛肉

▾

Surprise me with

Dai cooks in Xishuangbanna grill beef marinated in lemongrass, herbs and chilies, a technique that shares flavors with Laos and Thailand.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 600 g1.3 lbBeef sirloin or flank
  • 60 g2 ozLemongrass
  • 50 g3 tbspGarlic and shallot
  • 20 g2/3 ozChilies
  • 40 g1.5 ozCilantro and mint
  • 30 ml2 tbspFish sauce
  • 22Lime

Method

  1. Pound the lemongrass, garlic, shallot, chilies and fish sauce to a coarse paste.
  2. Rub over the beef and marinate 1 hour.
  3. Grill over high heat (or in a hot ridged pan) for 3 minutes per side for medium-rare.
  4. Rest 5 minutes, then slice across the grain.
  5. Serve with herbs and lime wedges.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking too long makes the beef tough.
  • Slicing with the grain leaves it chewy.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with sticky rice and a chili dip.
  • Flank steak takes the marinade best.

Substitutions

  • Flank steak takes the marinade best.
  • No grill: broil on a rack about 8 cm (3 in) from the element for 3 to 4 minutes per side for medium-rare, then rest 5 minutes before slicing; a hot ridged pan also works.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; serve cold in salad.

Freezing: Freezes the cooked beef for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sticky rice and fresh herbs
  • Soup: Sour bamboo shoot soup
  • Dessert: Fresh pineapple
  • Beverage: Chilled tea
