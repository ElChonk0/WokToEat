Kozhi Varutharacha Curry (Kerala Roasted Chicken Curry)

▾

Surprise me with

Kozhi varutharacha, chicken in a dark roasted-coconut and spice gravy, is a Kerala specialty, particularly popular with the state's Muslim and Christian communities.

  • 250 g9 ozOnion and tomato
  • 15 g1/2 ozCurry leaves
  • 40 g3 tbspGinger-garlic paste
  • 45 ml3 tbspCoconut oil
  • 6 g1 tspSalt

Method

  1. Roast the coconut and spices in a dry pan until dark brown, about 8 minutes; grind to a paste.
  2. Fry the onion and curry leaves in coconut oil until golden.
  3. Add the ginger-garlic paste, tomato and the roasted paste and fry 5 minutes.
  4. Add the chicken, salt and 250 ml water, cover and simmer 30 minutes.
  5. Reduce the gravy to your liking.

Common mistakes

  • Under-roasting gives a pale, flat curry.
  • Burning the coconut makes it bitter.

Authentic tips

  • Coconut oil gives the Kerala flavor.
  • Serve with appam or rice.

Substitutions

  • Desiccated coconut roasted in a dry pan replaces fresh.
  • Bone-in chicken gives the best flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Appam, parotta or rice
  • Soup: Cucumber raita
  • Dessert: Payasam
  • Beverage: Black tea
