Chicken Karaage 唐揚げ

▾

Surprise me with

Karaage is Japan's beloved bite-size fried chicken, marinated in soy, ginger, and garlic before a double-fry that produces a light, craggy crust — a fixture of izakayas and home bento boxes alike.

  • Prep 20 min (plus 30 min marinating)
  • Cook 15 min

Method

  1. Combine chicken with soy sauce, sake, ginger, and garlic; marinate at least 30 minutes.
  2. Drain chicken lightly and toss in potato starch to coat.
  3. Heat oil to 170°C (340°F) and fry chicken in batches 3–4 minutes until light golden; drain.
  4. Let the chicken rest 2–3 minutes, then increase oil heat to 190°C (375°F).
  5. Fry all the chicken again together for 1–2 minutes until deeply golden and extra crisp.
  6. Drain and serve immediately with lemon wedges.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the double-fry, which is what gives karaage its signature light, shattering crust.
  • Over-marinating past a few hours, which can make the texture slightly mushy.

Authentic tips

  • Potato starch produces a lighter, crispier crust than cornstarch or flour, if you can find it.
  • Letting the fried chicken rest between fries lets residual heat finish cooking the inside gently.

Substitutions

  • Chicken breast works but dries out faster — watch fry times closely if substituting.
  • No sake: dry sherry or a splash of extra soy sauce with water works as a substitute.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in an air fryer or oven to restore crispness.

Freezing: Freezes well, cooked, for up to a month; reheat from frozen in a hot oven.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and shredded cabbage with lemon
  • Soup: Miso soup
  • Dessert: Matcha ice cream
  • Beverage: Cold beer or barley tea
