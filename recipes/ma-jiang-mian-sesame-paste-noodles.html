Má Jiàng Miàn (Beijing Sesame Paste Noodles) 麻酱面

▾

Surprise me with

Noodles with sesame paste are a Beijing summer staple, served cold or warm with cucumber and garlic and eaten at lunch in homes and small restaurants.

  • Cook 8 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

  • 400 g14 ozWheat noodles
  • 90 g6 tbspSesame paste
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspRice vinegar
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic, grated
  • 150 g5 ozCucumber, julienned
  • 10 ml2 tspSesame oil
  • 90 ml6 tbspWarm water

Method

  1. Whisk the sesame paste with the warm water, adding a little at a time, until smooth and pourable.
  2. Stir in the soy sauce, vinegar, garlic and sesame oil.
  3. Boil the noodles until just tender, drain and rinse briefly under cold water.
  4. Toss the noodles with the sauce and top with the cucumber.

Common mistakes

  • Adding all the water at once makes the paste seize.
  • A thick sauce that does not coat the noodles.

Authentic tips

  • Add the water slowly while whisking.
  • A pinch of sugar rounds the flavor.

Substitutions

  • Tahini replaces Chinese sesame paste.
  • Peanut butter thinned with water works in a pinch.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated; loosen with water.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Smashed cucumber
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
