La Jiao Chao He Xia (Stir-Fried River Shrimp with Chilies) 辣椒炒河虾

Small freshwater shrimp from Hunan's lakes and rivers are quickly stir-fried with green chilies, ginger and garlic; the dish is a staple of Dongting Lake home cooking.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 500 g1.1 lbSmall whole freshwater shrimp
  • 150 g5 ozLong green chilies, sliced
  • 30 g2 tbspGarlic and ginger
  • 5 g2 tspDried chili flakes
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 15 g1 tbspCornstarch
  • 60 ml4 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Pat the shrimp very dry and toss with the cornstarch.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok over high heat until shimmering and fry the shrimp 2-3 minutes until crisp; lift out.
  3. Reduce to medium-high, fry the garlic, ginger and chilies 1 minute.
  4. Return the shrimp with the chili flakes, soy sauce and wine and toss 30 seconds.

Common mistakes

  • Wet shrimp steam instead of crisping; dry them completely.
  • Crowding the pan drops the heat.

Authentic tips

  • Eat the shrimp whole, shells and all, for crunch and calcium.
  • Fry in two batches for the crispest result.

Substitutions

  • Peeled prawns cut into pieces replace small river shrimp.
  • Peanuts add crunch.

Storage

Leftovers: Best the same day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Light vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
