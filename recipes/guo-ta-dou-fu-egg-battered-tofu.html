Guō Tā Dòu Fu (Shandong Egg-Battered Tofu) 锅塌豆腐

▾

Surprise me with

Guo ta, pan-frying in egg then simmering in stock, is a Shandong technique from Jinan; guo ta dou fu is a classic with a soft, custardy, savory finish.

  • 60 ml4 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Cut the tofu into 1.5 cm slabs and pat dry.
  2. Dredge in the flour and dip in the beaten eggs, coating both sides.
  3. Heat the oil in a nonstick pan over medium heat and fry 2-3 minutes per side until golden.
  4. Add the ginger, scallion, stock, soy sauce and wine and simmer 4 minutes until the sauce is absorbed.

Common mistakes

  • A hot pan burns the egg before the tofu heats through.
  • Turning too early breaks the coating.

Authentic tips

  • Use a nonstick pan.
  • Serve at once while the egg coating is soft.

Substitutions

  • Extra-firm tofu holds its shape best.
  • Bake the battered slices at 220 C (425 F) for a lighter version.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the batter softens.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
