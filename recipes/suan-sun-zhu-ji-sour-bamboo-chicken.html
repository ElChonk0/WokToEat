Suān Sǔn Zhǔ Jī (Dai Sour Bamboo Shoot Chicken) 酸笋煮鸡

▾

Sour bamboo shoots, fermented in brine, add a funky tang to Dai chicken soups and stews.

Method

  1. Rinse the sour bamboo shoots well; their fermented smell is strong but mellows with cooking. Dai and other southern Yunnan peoples ferment shoots for this tang.
  2. Heat the oil in a pot and fry the garlic, ginger, lemongrass and chilies for 1 minute, then the chicken for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the bamboo shoots and water, bring to a boil and skim.
  4. Simmer, covered, for 35 to 40 minutes until the chicken is tender and the broth sour and fragrant.
  5. Season with salt and finish with the herbs.

Common mistakes

  • Not rinsing the shoots makes the soup too sour and harsh.
  • Over-salting: the shoots already add salt.

Authentic tips

  • Buy sour bamboo shoots from Southeast Asian or Chinese markets.
  • Serve with sticky rice, as the Dai do.

Substitutions

  • Rinsed sauerkraut plus a little lime replaces sour bamboo shoots.
  • Boneless thigh shortens the cooking by 10 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; the sourness mellows.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sticky rice
  • Soup: Not needed; the stew is its own soup
  • Dessert: Fresh pineapple
  • Beverage: Pu'er tea
