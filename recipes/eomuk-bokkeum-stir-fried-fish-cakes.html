Eomuk Bokkeum (Stir-Fried Fish Cakes) 어묵볶음

Eomuk bokkeum, sheets of fish cake stir-fried in a sweet soy or spicy glaze, is one of the most common Korean banchan and a lunchbox (dosirak) standard.

  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Pour boiling water over the fish cake strips, drain; this removes excess oil.
  2. Heat the oil in a flat-bottom wok or skillet over medium-high heat and stir-fry the onion and carrot for 2 minutes.
  3. Add the fish cake and garlic and stir-fry 2 minutes until lightly browned.
  4. Add the soy sauce, syrup, gochugaru and water and toss for 1 to 2 minutes until the liquid reduces to a glaze.
  5. Add the green onion, sesame oil and sesame seeds and toss to finish.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the hot-water rinse leaves the dish greasy.
  • Cooking too long makes the fish cake tough and dry.

Authentic tips

  • Leave out the gochugaru for a mild version for children.
  • It keeps well, so make it for several days of banchan.

Substitutions

  • Japanese satsuma-age or chikuwa replace Korean eomuk.
  • Add sliced bell pepper for color.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days in the refrigerator; eat cold or warm.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and kimchi
  • Soup: Miyeok guk (seaweed soup)
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
