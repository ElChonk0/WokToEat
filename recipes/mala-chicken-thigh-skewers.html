Mala Chicken Thigh Skewers 麻辣鸡串

Inspired by Sichuan street-food skewer stalls, these thighs are marinated in a numbing chili oil blend and grilled, echoing the mala (numbing-spicy) flavor profile found throughout Sichuan barbecue culture.

  • Prep 20 min (plus 1 hr marinating)
  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Combine chicken with Sichuan peppercorn, chili flakes, cumin, garlic, soy sauce, oil, and sugar; marinate at least 1 hour.
  2. Thread chicken onto skewers (soak wooden skewers in water first if using).
  3. Grill or pan-sear over medium-high heat, turning occasionally, 10–12 minutes until cooked through and lightly charred.
  4. Sprinkle with extra ground Sichuan peppercorn and chili flakes to taste while still hot.
  5. Garnish with cilantro and serve immediately.

Common mistakes

  • Skewering pieces of uneven size, which cooks unevenly on the grill.
  • Not marinating long enough, since the numbing-spicy flavor needs time to penetrate the meat.

Authentic tips

  • A final dusting of extra spice mix right off the grill mimics the way street vendors finish these skewers.
  • Grinding whole Sichuan peppercorns fresh gives noticeably more aroma than pre-ground.

Substitutions

  • Boneless chicken breast works but grill briefly less to avoid drying out.
  • No grill: lay the skewers on a wire rack over a foil-lined sheet pan and broil on high about 10 cm (4 in) from the element for 4 to 5 minutes per side, or roast at 230°C (450°F) for 12 to 15 minutes, turning once, until charred at the edges and cooked through.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot pan to re-crisp.

Freezing: Freezes well, raw and marinated, for up to a month.
