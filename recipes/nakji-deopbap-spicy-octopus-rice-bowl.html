Nakji Deopbap (Spicy Octopus Rice Bowl) 낙지덮밥

Nakji deopbap is a Korean rice bowl of small octopus stir-fried in a gochujang sauce with vegetables, served over steamed rice.

Method

  1. Mix the gochujang, gochugaru, soy sauce, sugar, garlic, ginger and sesame oil into a sauce.
  2. Heat the neutral oil in a wok over high heat until smoking, add the onion, carrot and cabbage and stir-fry for 2 minutes.
  3. Add the octopus and half the sauce and stir-fry for 2 to 3 minutes until the octopus curls and turns opaque.
  4. Add the remaining sauce and green onion and toss for 30 seconds, without cooking further, so the octopus stays tender.
  5. Divide the rice into bowls, spoon over the octopus and vegetables, and sprinkle with the sesame seeds.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking the octopus longer than 3 minutes makes it rubbery.
  • A wok that is not very hot steams the vegetables instead of searing them.

Authentic tips

  • Rinse the octopus with salt to remove grit before cutting.
  • Serve with a fried egg for extra richness.

Substitutions

  • Squid replaces octopus.
  • Chicken thigh replaces octopus for a milder bowl.

Storage

Leftovers: Eat at once; the octopus turns tough on reheating.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Kimchi and bean sprout banchan
  • Soup: Doenjang or seaweed soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold barley tea
