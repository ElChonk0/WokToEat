Yi Pin Guo (Hui Layered Chicken Pot) 一品锅

Yi pin guo, a layered pot of chicken, pork, egg dumplings and tofu simmered together, is a celebration dish of Huizhou, traditionally served at New Year and weddings.

  • Cook 90 min
  • Difficulty Hard

Method

  1. Soak the shiitake in warm water for 30 minutes, squeeze and slice; keep the liquid. Cut the pork belly into 3 cm pieces.
  2. Blanch the pork and chicken together for 5 minutes in boiling water; drain and rinse.
  3. Brown the pork belly in a dry pot for 5 minutes, then add the ginger, scallion, soy sauce and sugar and stir 1 minute.
  4. Layer in order: the pork, chicken, mushrooms, tofu and egg dumplings on top; add the mushroom liquid and enough water to reach halfway.
  5. Cover and simmer on very low heat for 80 minutes without stirring; serve straight from the pot.

Common mistakes

  • Stirring destroys the layers that define the dish.
  • Cooking too hard breaks the egg dumplings.

Authentic tips

  • The layered presentation is traditional; scoop vertically so every portion has all the layers.
  • Egg dumplings are sold in Chinese groceries or made by frying thin omelets around meat.

Substitutions

  • Omit the egg dumplings if you can't find them; extra tofu fills the layer.
  • Pork shoulder replaces the belly.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; reheat gently without stirring.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; the pot is its own soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
