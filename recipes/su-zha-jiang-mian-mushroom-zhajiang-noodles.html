Su Zha Jiang Mian (Vegetarian Zhajiang Noodles) 素炸酱面

Zhajiang mian is Beijing's signature noodle dish of fried soybean paste over noodles; this meatless version uses mushrooms and pressed tofu in place of pork.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

  • 400 g14 ozWheat noodles
  • 90 g6 tbspYellow soybean paste (huangjiang)
  • 30 g2 tbspSweet bean sauce
  • 150 g5 ozShiitake mushrooms, diced
  • 150 g5 ozPressed tofu, diced
  • 30 g2 tbspGinger and scallion
  • 250 g9 ozCucumber, bean sprouts
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Brown the mushrooms and tofu in the oil over medium-high heat for 5-6 minutes until the mushrooms release their water and caramelize.
  2. Add the ginger, scallion and both pastes and stir for 1 minute, then add 100 ml water.
  3. Simmer 10 minutes over low heat, stirring often, until thick and glossy.
  4. Cook the noodles until tender, drain and divide among bowls.
  5. Top with the sauce, cucumber and bean sprouts and mix at the table.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the paste makes the sauce bitter; stir often.
  • Under-browning the mushrooms gives a flat sauce.

Authentic tips

  • Brown the mushrooms deeply for meaty flavor.
  • Add edamame for color.

Substitutions

  • Miso mixed with sweet bean sauce replaces yellow soybean paste.
  • Chopped walnuts add richness.

Storage

Leftovers: Store sauce and noodles separately; the sauce keeps 5 days.

Freezing: The sauce freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Shredded cucumber and radish
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot jasmine tea
