Nikuman (Steamed Pork Buns) 肉まん

▾

Surprise me with

Nikuman, steamed buns filled with seasoned pork, are a Japanese convenience-store and winter street snack adapted from Chinese baozi.

Method

  1. Mix the flour, yeast, 3 tablespoons of sugar, a pinch of salt and about 170 ml warm water into a smooth dough; knead 8 minutes and rise, covered, until doubled, 1 hour.
  2. Mix the pork, onion, ginger, soy sauce, oyster sauce and sesame oil, stirring until sticky.
  3. Divide the dough into 8, flatten into discs, fill and pleat closed.
  4. Rest the buns on parchment squares for 20 minutes.
  5. Steam over high heat for 15 minutes without lifting the lid.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the second rest makes dense buns.
  • Opening the lid wrinkles the buns.

Authentic tips

  • Low-protein flour gives whiter buns.
  • Freeze cooked buns and re-steam.

Substitutions

  • Ground chicken replaces some pork.
  • Store-bought frozen buns are a quick shortcut.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; re-steam for 8 minutes.

Freezing: Freeze steamed buns for 2 months; steam from frozen for 15 minutes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickles
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
