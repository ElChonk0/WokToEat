Kòu Ròu Bāo (Hakka Pork Belly Buns) 扣肉包

▾

Kou rou bao fills a steamed bun with braised Hakka pork belly, a handheld way to enjoy the banquet dish.

Method

  1. Simmer the pork belly with both soy sauces, the wine, sugar, star anise, ginger and water, covered, for 1 hour until a skewer slides in, then slice it 4 mm thick.
  2. Stir the yeast and sugar into the warm water, then mix with the flour and oil and knead for 8 minutes to a smooth dough.
  3. Cover and rest the dough for 1 hour until doubled, then divide it into 8 pieces and roll each into a 10 cm circle.
  4. Place 2 slices of pork and a spoonful of the mustard greens on each, fold over into a half-moon and set each bun on a square of parchment.
  5. Rest the buns for 20 minutes, then steam over high heat for 15 minutes without lifting the lid.

Common mistakes

  • Steaming over water that is not yet boiling gives dense buns.
  • Overfilling makes the buns split; keep to 2 slices.

Authentic tips

  • Reduce the braising liquid to a glaze and brush it on the pork slices.
  • Let the buns rest before steaming for a lighter bite.

Substitutions

  • Pork shoulder replaces belly.
  • Pickled cucumber replaces preserved mustard greens.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; resteam for 8 minutes.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months; steam from frozen for 18 minutes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled mustard greens
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot jasmine tea
