Jinmichae Bokkeum (Spicy Seasoned Dried Squid) 진미채볶음

Jinmichae bokkeum, shredded dried squid coated in a sweet gochujang glaze, is a chewy, savory banchan that keeps for weeks.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 5 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Korea Seafood LunchDinnerStir-frySpicy30 min or lessShellfishEgg

  • 200 g7 ozShredded dried squid (jinmichae)
  • 15 ml1 tbspMayonnaise
  • 30 g2 tbspGochujang
  • 2 g1 tspGochugaru (Korean chili flakes)
  • 45 ml3 tbspRice syrup or honey
  • 5 ml1 tspLight soy sauce
  • 1 clove1 cloveGarlic, minced
  • 15 ml1 tbspNeutral oil
  • 10 ml2 tspSesame oil
  • 5 g2 tspToasted sesame seeds

Method

  1. Rinse the squid briefly, squeeze dry and cut into bite-size lengths. Toss with the mayonnaise to keep it soft.
  2. Heat the oil, gochujang, gochugaru, syrup, soy sauce and garlic in a skillet over low heat until it just bubbles.
  3. Turn off the heat and add the squid.
  4. Toss until every strand is coated.
  5. Mix in the sesame oil and seeds and cool.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking the squid in the sauce makes it hard.
  • Dry, brittle squid stays tough; the rinse and mayonnaise soften it.

Authentic tips

  • It is even better the next day.
  • A soy-only version without chili suits children.

Substitutions

  • Dried shredded pollock works.
  • Honey replaces rice syrup.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 weeks in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Rice and other banchan
  • Soup: Miyeokguk
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
