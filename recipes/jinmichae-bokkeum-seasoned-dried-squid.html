Jinmichae Bokkeum (Spicy Seasoned Dried Squid) 진미채볶음

▾

Jinmichae bokkeum, shredded dried squid coated in a sweet gochujang glaze, is a chewy, savory banchan that keeps for weeks.

  • 120 g4 ozShredded dried squid (jinmichae)
  • 120 ml1/2 cupWater, for soaking
  • 20 ml1.5 tbspNeutral oil
  • 4 g2 tspGochugaru (Korean chili flakes)
  • 30 g2 tbspGochujang
  • 30 ml2 tbspRice or corn syrup
  • 5 ml1 tspLight soy sauce
  • 1 clove1 cloveGarlic, minced
  • 15 ml1 tbspMayonnaise (optional, for softness)
  • 5 ml1 tspSesame oil
  • 3 g1 tspToasted sesame seeds

Method

  1. Snip the squid into shorter pieces, rinse it and soak it in the water for about 20 minutes, turning once, until slightly pliable. Squeeze it dry. Soaking keeps it soft and chewy rather than hard.
  2. Warm the oil in a small pan over low heat, add the gochugaru and stir for 10 to 15 seconds until the oil turns red and the flakes look pasty, then take it off the heat before they darken.
  3. Add the gochujang, syrup, soy sauce and garlic and bring to a boil over medium heat, stirring, until glossy.
  4. Turn off the heat, stir in the sesame oil and seeds and let the sauce cool completely. Adding the squid to hot sauce makes it rubbery.
  5. Massage the squid with the mayonnaise, then toss it in the cooled sauce with your hands until every strand is coated. It keeps in the refrigerator for about 2 weeks as a banchan.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking the squid in the sauce makes it hard.
  • Dry, brittle squid stays tough; the rinse and mayonnaise soften it.

Authentic tips

  • It is even better the next day.
  • A soy-only version without chili suits children.

Substitutions

  • Dried shredded pollock works.
  • Honey replaces rice syrup.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 weeks in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Rice and other banchan
  • Soup: Miyeokguk
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
