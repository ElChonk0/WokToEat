Jī Ròu Dùn Fěn Tiáo (Chicken Stewed with Sweet Potato Noodles) 鸡肉炖粉条

▾

Sweet potato vermicelli stewed with meat is a northern Chinese winter dish; the noodles soak up the rich braising sauce.

  • Cook 35 min

  • 50 g4 tbspGinger, scallion, garlic

  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Soak the vermicelli in warm water for 20 minutes until pliable; cut into 15 cm lengths. Chop the cabbage.
  2. Heat the oil in a pot over medium-high heat and brown the chicken for 8 minutes.
  3. Add the ginger, scallion, garlic, star anise and soy sauce, stir 1 minute, then pour in 600 ml water.
  4. Cover and simmer 20 minutes until the chicken is nearly tender.
  5. Add the cabbage and noodles and cook 10 minutes more, stirring once, until the noodles are soft and glossy.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the noodles too soon makes them mushy.
  • Not enough liquid: the noodles soak up a lot, so keep an eye on the pot.

Authentic tips

  • Use sweet potato noodles, which stay chewy.
  • Serve in the pot at the table.

Substitutions

  • Glass noodles replace sweet potato vermicelli; add them in the last 5 minutes.
  • Add dried mushrooms for depth.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; the noodles absorb the broth.

Freezing: Freeze without the noodles for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; the stew is its own soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
