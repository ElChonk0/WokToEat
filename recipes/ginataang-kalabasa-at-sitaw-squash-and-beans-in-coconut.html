Ginataang Kalabasa at Sitaw (Squash and String Beans in Coconut Milk)

▾

Surprise me with

Ginataang kalabasa at sitaw, squash and long beans in coconut milk, is an everyday Filipino ginataan, a staple of Bicol and Tagalog kitchens.

  • Cook 20 min

  • 500 g1.1 lbKabocha squash, cubed
  • 200 g7 ozString beans
  • 400 ml1.75 cupsCoconut milk
  • 60 g4 tbspGarlic, onion, ginger
  • 15 g1/2 ozChilies
  • 200 g7 ozTofu, fried
  • 5 g1 tspSalt

Method

  1. Fry the garlic, onion and ginger in a little oil for 3 minutes.
  2. Add the squash and coconut milk and simmer gently 10 minutes.
  3. Add the beans, tofu and chilies and simmer 6 minutes.
  4. Season with the salt.
  5. Serve with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard splits the coconut milk.
  • Overcooked squash dissolves.

Authentic tips

  • Add shrimp for a non-vegetarian version.
  • Kabocha holds its shape well.

Substitutions

  • Butternut squash replaces kabocha.
  • Shrimp is the classic addition for non-vegetarians.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Calamansi juice
