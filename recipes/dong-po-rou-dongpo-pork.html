Dōng Pō Ròu (Dongpo Braised Pork) 东坡肉

▾

Surprise me with

Dongpo pork, squares of pork belly slow-braised in soy sauce and Shaoxing wine, is named for the Song-dynasty poet Su Dongpo and is a celebrated dish of Hangzhou.

Method

  1. Singe off any hairs from the skin and blanch the pork in boiling water for 5 minutes, then rinse. Tie each square with string like a parcel so it keeps its shape through the long braise.
  2. Line the bottom of a small, heavy Dutch oven or clay pot with the green onion and ginger, which stops the pork from sticking. Set the pork on top skin-side down in one snug layer.
  3. Add the Shaoxing wine, soy sauces, rock sugar, star anise and water. Dongpo pork uses wine rather than water as its main liquid; this is its signature.
  4. Bring to a boil, cover tightly and simmer over the lowest heat for 1.5 hours. Turn the pork skin-side up and simmer for 1 hour more.
  5. Transfer the pork and sauce to heatproof bowls and steam for 30 minutes, until the fat is translucent and quivering and the meat melts under a chopstick.
  6. Reduce the braising liquid in a small pan until syrupy, cut the strings and spoon the glaze over the pork. Named after the Song dynasty poet Su Dongpo, governor of Hangzhou.

Common mistakes

  • Opening the lid often lets out steam and dries the pork.
  • Boiling hard makes the fat greasy and the meat stringy.

Authentic tips

  • Keep the heat very low; the original method was a covered earthenware pot over embers. On an electric or induction stove use the lowest setting, or finish the braise in a 150°C/300°F oven.
  • Serve with a bit of the sauce and rice, or steamed buns.

Substitutions

  • A pressure cooker cuts the braise to 50 minutes.
  • Pork belly with less skin makes a lighter dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; reheat by steaming so the fat stays tender.

Freezing: Freezes well for 3 months in its sauce.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or steamed buns
  • Soup: Blanched greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot jasmine tea
