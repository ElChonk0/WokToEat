Rùn Bǐng Juǎn (Minnan Vegetable Popiah) 润饼卷

▾

Surprise me with

Popiah, a thin crepe rolled with braised vegetables, egg and peanut powder, is a Minnan (Xiamen and Quanzhou) specialty, eaten at Qingming and the Tomb Sweeping Festival.

  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 88Popiah wrappers
  • 400 g14 ozJicama and carrot, shredded
  • 33Eggs, omelet strips
  • 200 g7 ozBean sprouts and lettuce
  • 45 ml3 tbspSweet sauce
  • 40 g1.5 ozGround peanut and sugar
  • 20 g2/3 ozCilantro
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Heat 1 tablespoon of the oil in a pan and fry the jicama and carrot for 10 minutes until soft; season and drain very well.
  2. Beat the eggs with a pinch of salt and fry into a thin omelet; slice into strips.
  3. Warm the wrappers briefly. On each wrapper spread a little sweet sauce, then add lettuce, sprouts, the vegetable filling and egg.
  4. Sprinkle with the ground peanut and sugar and the cilantro.
  5. Fold in the sides and roll up tightly.

Common mistakes

  • A wet filling makes the wrapper tear; drain it well.
  • Overfilling makes rolling difficult.

Authentic tips

  • Roll tightly like a burrito.
  • Serve with a little chili sauce.

Substitutions

  • Spring roll wrappers or lettuce replace the wrappers.
  • Omit the egg for a vegan version.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day unwrapped.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Not needed; the rolls are the meal
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
