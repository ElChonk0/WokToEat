Rùn Bǐng Juǎn (Minnan Vegetable Popiah) 润饼卷

▾

Surprise me with

Popiah, a thin crepe rolled with braised vegetables, egg and peanut powder, is a Minnan (Xiamen and Quanzhou) specialty, eaten at Qingming and the Tomb Sweeping Festival.

Method

  1. Fry the garlic in the oil, then add the jicama, carrot and cabbage with the soy sauce and salt and braise, covered, over medium-low heat for 15 to 20 minutes until soft and sweet. Drain off excess liquid; wet filling tears the wrapper. Run bing is a Qingming (tomb-sweeping) festival food in Xiamen and Quanzhou.
  2. Lay a wrapper flat, spread it with a little sweet sauce and lay a lettuce leaf on top to protect it.
  3. Add a line of the braised vegetables, bean sprouts and shredded egg.
  4. Sprinkle with peanut sugar, cilantro and seaweed flakes.
  5. Fold in the sides and roll up tightly. Eat at once.

Common mistakes

  • A wet filling makes the wrapper tear; drain it well.
  • Overfilling makes rolling difficult.

Authentic tips

  • Roll tightly like a burrito.
  • Serve with a little chili sauce.

Substitutions

  • Spring roll wrappers or lettuce replace the wrappers.
  • Omit the egg for a vegan version.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day unwrapped.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Not needed; the rolls are the meal
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
