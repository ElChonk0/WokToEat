Shāndōng Niú Ròu Shuǐ Jiǎo (Shandong Beef Dumplings) 山东牛肉水饺

▾

Dumplings are the New Year food of northern China; beef versions are made by Shandong's Hui communities.

  • Cook 8 min

Method

  1. Mix the beef with the ginger, soy sauce, sesame oil and salt, stirring in one direction and adding 3 tablespoons of water gradually.
  2. Fold in the chopped scallions just before filling.
  3. Place a tablespoon of filling in each wrapper, fold and pleat.
  4. Boil 6-7 minutes until they float.
  5. Serve with vinegar dip.

Common mistakes

  • Overmixing makes the filling tough.
  • Adding scallions early makes it watery.

Authentic tips

  • Add water gradually for a juicy filling.
  • Lamb is a common alternative.

Substitutions

  • Ground lamb is a traditional alternative.
  • Chopped celery lightens the filling.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; pan-fry leftovers.

Freezing: Freeze uncooked on a tray for 2 months; boil from frozen for 8 minutes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Black vinegar and chili oil dip
  • Soup: Hot and sour soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
