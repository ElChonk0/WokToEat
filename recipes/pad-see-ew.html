Pad See Ew

▾

Surprise me with

Pad See Ew ("stir-fried soy sauce") reflects Thailand's Chinese immigrant culinary influence, built on wide, flat rice noodles char-fried in dark soy sauce — a close cousin of Chinese flat-noodle stir-fries adapted with Thai seasoning.

Method

  1. If using fresh noodles, gently separate them by hand — they're delicate and tear easily when cold.
  2. Mix dark soy sauce, light soy sauce, oyster sauce, and sugar in a small bowl.
  3. Heat 2 tbsp oil in a wok over high heat; cook the chicken until just cooked through, then push to one side.
  4. Push chicken aside, add remaining oil and garlic, and crack in the eggs, scrambling briefly.
  5. Add the gai lan stems first, then the leaves, and stir-fry 1–2 minutes.
  6. Add the noodles and sauce mixture, tossing gently but constantly to coat without tearing the noodles.
  7. Let the noodles sit against the hot wok surface for 20–30 seconds at a time between tosses to pick up a light char, then toss again.
  8. Serve immediately while hot.

Common mistakes

  • Stirring fresh rice noodles too roughly, which shreds them instead of keeping the wide, silky ribbons that define the dish.
  • Using only light soy sauce — the dark soy sauce gives pad see ew its signature deep color and slightly different sweetness.

Authentic tips

  • A blazing hot wok and minimal tossing (let the noodles sit against the surface) creates the slightly charred bits people look for in good pad see ew.
  • If noodles are stuck together straight from the package, microwave 30–60 seconds to loosen before separating by hand.

Substitutions

  • No gai lan? Broccolini or regular broccoli work, though slightly milder.
  • Shrimp or beef can replace chicken with no other changes to the method.

Storage

Leftovers: Reheats reasonably well in a hot pan with a splash of water; microwaving makes the noodles gummy.

Freezing: Not recommended — rice noodles turn mushy and break apart after freezing.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Chili vinegar and sugar at the table
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
