Yáng Zhōu Chǎo Fàn (Yeung Chow Fried Rice) 扬州炒饭

Despite the name, Yeung Chow fried rice with char siu, shrimp and egg is the house fried rice of Cantonese restaurants worldwide, distinct from the Yangzhou original.

  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 800 g5 cupsCooked jasmine rice, cold
  • 200 g7 ozChar siu (Chinese BBQ pork), diced
  • 200 g7 ozShrimp, peeled and diced
  • 33Eggs, beaten
  • 100 g2/3 cupFrozen peas
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 4 stalks4 stalksGreen onion, chopped
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt
  • 2 g1/2 tspSugar
  • 1 g1/2 tspGround white pepper

Method

  1. Break the rice into separate grains with your hands. Thaw the peas in hot water and drain.
  2. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a flat-bottom wok over high heat and stir-fry the shrimp for 1 minute until pink, then add the char siu and peas for 1 minute. Move to a plate.
  3. Add the remaining oil, pour in the eggs and stir once; while they are still half-liquid add the rice and toss for 3 to 4 minutes until hot and every grain is coated.
  4. Return the shrimp mixture, add the salt, sugar and pepper and pour the soy sauce around the hot edge of the wok. Toss for 1 minute.
  5. Add the green onion, toss 30 seconds and serve.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Too much soy turns it brown and soggy; Yeung Chow fried rice should be pale and golden.
  • Overloading a small wok steams the rice; cook in two batches.

Authentic tips

  • On induction, keep the wok on the surface and toss with a spatula rather than lifting it.
  • Day-old rice gives separate grains; spread fresh rice on a tray and chill for an hour if needed.

Substitutions

  • Ham or Chinese sausage replace char siu.
  • Add diced lettuce at the end for crunch.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Garlic gai lan
  • Soup: West Lake beef soup
  • Dessert: Mango pudding
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
