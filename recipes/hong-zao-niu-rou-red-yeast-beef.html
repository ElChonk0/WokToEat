Hóng Zāo Niú Ròu (Red Yeast Rice Beef) 红糟牛肉

▾

Surprise me with

Red yeast rice lees, the signature Fuzhou seasoning, flavor beef as well as pork and fish, giving a winey color and sweetness.

  • Cook 15 min

  • 500 g1.1 lbBeef sirloin, sliced
  • 60 g4 tbspRed yeast rice lees
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 10 g2 tspSugar
  • 15 g1 tbspCornstarch
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Slice the beef 4 mm thick across the grain and toss with the lees, wine, half the sugar and a spoonful of water; marinate 1 hour.
  2. Wipe off excess lees and dust the slices lightly with the cornstarch.
  3. Heat the oil in a wok over high heat until shimmering and sear the beef in two batches for 45 seconds per batch; remove.
  4. Add the garlic and remaining sugar for 15 seconds.
  5. Return the beef and toss 20 seconds until glossy.

Common mistakes

  • Leaving excess lees burns in the pan.
  • Overcooking makes the beef tough.

Authentic tips

  • Red yeast rice lees are sold in Chinese groceries; red rice wine is a rough substitute.
  • The dish should be savory with a touch of sweetness.

Substitutions

  • Red fermented bean curd plus a pinch of paprika approximates red yeast lees.
  • Pork takes the same marinade.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot oolong tea
