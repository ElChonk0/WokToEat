Gai Yang (Thai Grilled Chicken)

▾

Surprise me with

A specialty of Thailand's Isaan region, gai yang marinates chicken in fish sauce, garlic, and cilantro root before slow-grilling — traditionally served alongside sticky rice and a tangy dipping sauce.

Method

  1. Pound the cilantro roots, garlic, peppercorns and lemongrass into a fine paste, then mix in the fish sauce, soy sauce, oyster sauce, sugar and turmeric.
  2. Slash the chicken to the bone in a few places so the marinade penetrates. Rub the paste in well and refrigerate for at least 4 hours, or overnight.
  3. Set up a grill for indirect heat, or heat the oven to 200 C (400 F). Grill the chicken skin-side up over indirect, medium heat, covered, for 25 to 30 minutes, turning occasionally. Gai yang is grilled slowly so the inside cooks before the sugary skin burns.
  4. Brush with coconut milk and move to direct heat for the last 5 to 10 minutes, turning, until the skin is deeply browned and the thickest part reaches 74 C (165 F). In the oven, finish under the broiler.
  5. Rest for 5 minutes, then chop and serve with nam jim jaew, sticky rice and som tam.

Common mistakes

  • Grilling over too high a direct heat, which chars the outside before the inside cooks through.
  • Skipping the cilantro root — it has a more intense, earthy flavor than the leaves and is central to the marinade.

Authentic tips

  • If cilantro root isn't available, the lower stems are the closest substitute — avoid using just the leafy tops.
  • Moving pieces to indirect heat once charred lets them finish cooking through without burning.

Substitutions

  • No grill: a hot grill pan or broiler both work well as substitutes.
  • Boneless thighs work but reduce grilling time by about a third.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; great shredded into a salad.

Freezing: Freezes well, marinated and raw, for up to a month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sticky rice and green papaya salad
  • Soup: Tom yum or clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh mango
  • Beverage: Singha beer or Thai iced tea
