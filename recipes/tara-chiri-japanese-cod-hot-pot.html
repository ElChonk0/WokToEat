Tara Chiri (Cod Hot Pot) 鱈ちり

Tara chiri is a winter Japanese nabe in which cod, tofu and vegetables are poached in kombu water and dipped in ponzu, a style that keeps the flavor of the fish clean.

  • Cook 20 min

  • 600 g1 lb 5 ozIcelandic cod fillet, skinless, in 5 cm pieces
  • 8 g1.5 tspSalt, to cure the cod
  • 10 g1/3 ozKombu, 15 cm piece
  • 1.2 L5 cupsWater
  • 60 ml1/4 cupSake
  • 300 g10 ozNapa cabbage, in large pieces
  • 300 g10 ozSilken or firm tofu, in cubes
  • 100 g3.5 ozShiitake mushrooms, stems removed
  • 100 g3.5 ozLeek or negi, sliced on a slant
  • 100 g3.5 ozSpinach or shungiku
  • 120 ml1/2 cupPonzu
  • 100 g3.5 ozDaikon, grated
  • 20 g3/4 ozScallions, finely sliced

Method

  1. Salt the cod and leave it 15 minutes, then pour boiling water over it in a bowl and rinse off any scum to remove the fishy smell; pat dry.
  2. Soak the kombu in the water in a wide pot for 20 minutes, then warm over medium heat until small bubbles form and lift out the kombu before it boils. Add the sake.
  3. Add the cabbage, leek and shiitake and simmer 5 minutes until tender.
  4. Add the cod and tofu and simmer gently for 5 to 7 minutes until the cod is opaque and flakes at its thickest point, skimming foam.
  5. Add the spinach for 30 seconds, then serve at once with ponzu, grated daikon and scallions as the dip.

Cook’s notes

Written for Icelandic cod fillet; any thick white cod fillet works.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling the kombu makes the broth slimy and bitter; remove it as bubbles form.
  • Skipping the salting and rinse leaves a fishy, cloudy broth.

Authentic tips

  • Eat it as soon as the cod is cooked because thick cod firms up quickly.
  • Finish with rice or udon cooked in the leftover broth.

Substitutions

  • Haddock or hake replaces the cod.
  • Lemon juice and soy sauce (1:1) replaces the ponzu.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day in the refrigerator; reheat gently.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: The broth is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
