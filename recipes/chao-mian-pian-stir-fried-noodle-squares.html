Chǎo Miàn Piàn (Xinjiang Stir-Fried Noodle Squares) 炒面片

▾

Surprise me with

Mian pian, hand-torn squares of wheat dough, are a staple of Uyghur and Hui home cooking; stir-fried with tomato, peppers and egg they make a quick everyday meal.

  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Method

  1. Mix the flour, water and a pinch of salt into a firm dough, knead 5 minutes, and rest it, covered, for 30 minutes.
  2. Roll the dough thin and tear or cut into 4 cm squares; boil in plenty of water for 3-4 minutes until they float and are tender; drain.
  3. Scramble the eggs in half the oil in a wok; remove.
  4. Fry the onion, garlic and cumin in the remaining oil for 2 minutes, then add the peppers and tomatoes and cook 6 minutes into a thick sauce.
  5. Add the noodle squares, eggs, soy sauce and salt and toss 2 minutes.

Common mistakes

  • Undercooked dough squares stay chewy and raw inside; boil until they float.
  • A watery sauce makes the squares soggy; cook the tomato down.

Authentic tips

  • Tear the dough by hand into irregular squares; the uneven edges hold sauce.
  • Add a spoonful of black vinegar at the table.

Substitutions

  • Torn lasagna sheets replace homemade dough.
  • Lamb is the traditional addition.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot pan with a splash of water.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled cucumbers
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Hami melon
  • Beverage: Black tea
