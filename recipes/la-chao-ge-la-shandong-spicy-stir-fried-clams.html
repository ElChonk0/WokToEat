La Chao Ge La (Shandong Spicy Stir-Fried Clams) 辣炒蛤蜊

▾

Surprise me with

Spicy stir-fried clams (la chao gala) are a Qingdao beer-garden classic eaten with cold beer by the sea.

  • Cook 8 min

  • 1.2 kg2.6 lbClams, purged
  • 10 g1/3 ozDried chilies
  • 60 g4 tbspScallion, ginger and garlic
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 15 g1 tbspDoubanjiang
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Soak the clams in salted water for 30 minutes to purge sand; rinse and drain.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok over high heat and fry the dried chilies and aromatics for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the doubanjiang and fry 20 seconds.
  4. Add the clams and wine, toss, cover and cook 3-4 minutes, until the shells open.
  5. Remove any that stay closed, toss with the soy sauce and serve.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the soak leaves sand in the sauce.
  • Overcooking makes the clams rubbery.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with cold beer in Qingdao style.
  • Mussels work too.

Substitutions

  • Mussels replace clams.
  • Reduce the chilies for a milder dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Eat immediately.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed buns or rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
