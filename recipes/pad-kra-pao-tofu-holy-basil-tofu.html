Pad Kra Pao Tofu (Holy Basil Tofu Stir-Fry)

▾

Surprise me with

Pad kra pao, stir-fried holy basil with chilies, is Thailand's most ordered everyday dish; the vegetarian version uses tofu and mushroom sauce.

Method

  1. Press the tofu for 15 minutes, then crumble or cube it. Pound the garlic and chilies into a coarse paste. Mix the soy sauces, mushroom sauce, sugar and a tablespoon of water.
  2. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a flat-bottom wok over medium-high heat and fry the tofu for 5 to 7 minutes, stirring now and then, until golden and slightly crisp. Browned tofu soaks up the sauce like minced meat does.
  3. Push the tofu aside, add the remaining oil and fry the garlic-chili paste for 20 seconds until fragrant.
  4. Add the long beans and stir-fry for 2 minutes, then pour in the sauce and toss for 1 minute until it coats everything.
  5. Turn off the heat, fold in the holy basil until just wilted and serve over jasmine rice.

Common mistakes

  • Wet tofu doesn't crisp.
  • Cooking the basil too long makes it black.

Authentic tips

  • Holy basil is essential; Thai basil is a substitute.
  • Top with a fried egg.

Substitutions

  • Thai basil replaces holy basil.
  • Top with a fried egg.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Jasmine rice topped with a fried egg
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
