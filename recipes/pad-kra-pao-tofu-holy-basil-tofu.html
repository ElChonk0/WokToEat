Pad Kra Pao Tofu (Holy Basil Tofu Stir-Fry)

Pad kra pao, stir-fried holy basil with chilies, is Thailand's most ordered everyday dish; the vegetarian version uses tofu and mushroom sauce.

  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 500 g1.1 lbFirm tofu, crumbled or cubed
  • 60 g2 ozHoly basil
  • 50 g3 tbspGarlic and chilies
  • 30 ml2 tbspSoy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspVegetarian oyster sauce
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 100 g3.5 ozLong beans
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Press the tofu 15 minutes and crumble or cube it.
  2. Pound the garlic and chilies.
  3. Fry the tofu in the oil in a hot wok 5 minutes until crisp; remove.
  4. Fry the paste 15 seconds, then add the beans, sauces and sugar and toss 2 minutes.
  5. Return the tofu and fold in the basil.

Common mistakes

  • Wet tofu doesn't crisp.
  • Cooking the basil too long makes it black.

Authentic tips

  • Holy basil is essential; Thai basil is a substitute.
  • Top with a fried egg.

Substitutions

  • Thai basil replaces holy basil.
  • Top with a fried egg.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Jasmine rice topped with a fried egg
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
