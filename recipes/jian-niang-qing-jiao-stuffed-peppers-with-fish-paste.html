Jiān Niàng Qīng Jiāo (Stuffed Peppers with Fish Paste in Black Bean Sauce) 煎酿青椒

▾

Surprise me with

Peppers stuffed with fish paste, pan-fried and sauced with black beans, are part of the Cantonese "three stuffed treasures" (jian niang san bao) alongside eggplant and tofu.

  • Prep 30 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Ingredients

  • 44Green bell peppers or Anaheim peppers
  • 400 g14 ozFish paste (or minced white fish, as for fish cakes)
  • 10 g1 tbspCornstarch, for dusting
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 15 g1 tbspFermented black beans, rinsed and mashed
  • 3 cloves3 clovesGarlic, minced
  • 15 ml1 tbspOyster sauce
  • 10 ml2 tspLight soy sauce
  • 4 g1 tspSugar
  • 150 ml2/3 cupWater
  • 5 g2 tspCornstarch, for the sauce

Method

  1. Cut the peppers into large pieces and remove the seeds. Dust the insides lightly with cornstarch and press in a layer of fish paste, smoothing the top.
  2. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium heat and fry the pieces paste-side down for 4 minutes until golden, then turn and fry the skin side 2 minutes.
  3. Push the peppers aside and fry the black beans and garlic for 30 seconds.
  4. Add the oyster sauce, soy sauce, sugar and water, cover and simmer 3 minutes until the fish is cooked through.
  5. Thicken with the cornstarch mixed with a little water and spoon the sauce over.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Without the cornstarch dusting the paste falls off the peppers.
  • High heat burns the paste before it cooks.

Authentic tips

  • Eggplant slices and tofu squares can be stuffed and cooked in the same pan.
  • Hong Kong street stalls deep-fry them instead.

Substitutions

  • Shrimp paste or seasoned ground pork replace fish paste.
  • Mild chilies give a slight heat.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Tomato tofu soup
  • Dessert: Sweet red bean soup
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
