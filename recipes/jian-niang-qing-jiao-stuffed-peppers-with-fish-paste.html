Jiān Niàng Qīng Jiāo (Stuffed Peppers with Fish Paste in Black Bean Sauce) 煎酿青椒

▾

Surprise me with

Peppers stuffed with fish paste, pan-fried and sauced with black beans, are part of the Cantonese "three stuffed treasures" (jian niang san bao) alongside eggplant and tofu.

  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 5 g2 tspCornstarch, for dusting

  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 15 ml1 tbspBlack bean garlic sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspOyster sauce
  • 5 ml1 tspLight soy sauce
  • 3 ml1/2 tspDark soy sauce

Method

  1. Pat the fish and shrimp dry. Chop each with a cleaver and smash it with the flat of the blade into a paste, then combine and chop together again. Hand-chopped paste is bouncier than one made in a food processor.
  2. Mix in the salt, sugar, pepper, cornstarch and water, stirring hard in one direction for 2 to 3 minutes until the paste turns sticky and springy. Stir in the sesame oil and chill for 20 minutes.
  3. Core the peppers and cut them into large curved wedges. Dust the insides with the dusting cornstarch, then press about 1 tablespoon of filling firmly onto each, smoothing it into a dome. The cornstarch is the glue that stops the filling sliding off.
  4. Stir the sauce ingredients together. Heat a large skillet or flat-bottom wok over high heat, then lower to medium and add the oil. Lay the peppers in filling-side down, cover and fry for 2 to 3 minutes, until the filling is golden.
  5. Turn the pieces pepper-side down, lower the heat, cover for 1 minute, then splash in the wine, cover again and turn off the heat for 1 minute. The filling is done when a chopstick pushed in meets firm resistance.
  6. Lift the peppers onto a plate. Stir the sauce into the hot pan over high heat until it boils and thickens, thinning with water if needed, and spoon it over.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Without the cornstarch dusting the paste falls off the peppers.
  • High heat burns the paste before it cooks.

Authentic tips

  • Eggplant slices and tofu squares can be stuffed and cooked in the same pan.
  • Hong Kong street stalls deep-fry them instead.

Substitutions

  • Shrimp paste or seasoned ground pork replace fish paste.
  • Mild chilies give a slight heat.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Tomato tofu soup
  • Dessert: Sweet red bean soup
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
