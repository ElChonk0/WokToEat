Shāndōng Niú Ròu Miàn (Shandong Beef Noodle Soup) 山东牛肉面

▾

Surprise me with

Beef noodle soup is a hearty Shandong staple, especially among its Hui communities, eaten at breakfast and lunch.

  • Cook 2.5 hr

Method

  1. Blanch the beef for 5 minutes and rinse.
  2. Put the beef in a pot with the water, ginger, green onion, garlic and spices. Bring to a boil, skim and simmer, covered, for 2 hours until tender.
  3. Season the broth with the soy sauces, rock sugar and salt.
  4. Cook the noodles and blanch the greens.
  5. Divide among bowls, add sliced beef, ladle over the broth and finish with cilantro, green onion and chili oil.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the blanch leaves the broth cloudy.
  • Overcooking the noodles in the broth makes them mushy.

Authentic tips

  • Hand-pulled noodles are traditional; fresh wheat noodles work well.
  • A spoonful of vinegar at the table lifts the broth.

Substitutions

  • Fresh wheat noodles replace hand-pulled.
  • Ready-made beef stock saves an hour.

Storage

Leftovers: Store noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 4 days.

Freezing: Freeze the broth and beef for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled vegetables
  • Soup: Not needed; the noodle soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
