Ikan Rica-Rica (Manado Chili Fish)

▾

Surprise me with

Rica-rica is a fiery Manadonese seasoning of chilies, shallots, lemongrass, tomato and basil, spooned over fried fish, chicken or pork.

  • Cook 20 min

Method

  1. Rub the fish with the salt and turmeric and leave for 10 minutes. Fry it in the oil over medium-high heat for 3 to 4 minutes per side until golden. Drain and pour off all but 3 tablespoons of oil.
  2. Coarsely grind the shallots, garlic and both kinds of chilies.
  3. Fry the paste in the reserved oil with the lemongrass and lime leaves over medium heat for 6 to 8 minutes, until fragrant and the oil separates.
  4. Add the tomato and sauce salt and cook for 2 minutes, then the lime juice.
  5. Add the fish and toss gently to coat, then stir in the basil. Serve with rice; it is fiery, in Manado style.

Cook’s notes

Written for Chilean sea bass. Manado cooks use tuna, skipjack or snapper.

Common mistakes

  • A weak fry gives a pale, flat sauce; cook the paste until the oil rises.
  • Adding the basil early loses its aroma.

Authentic tips

  • Rica-rica should be hot and bright; use fresh chilies, not dried.
  • Reduce the bird's eye chilies for a milder dish.

Substitutions

  • Cod loin or haddock replaces the sea bass.
  • Chicken thigh takes the same sauce.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and sautéed greens
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Es teh
