Galmaegisal Gui (Grilled Pork Skirt) 갈매기살구이

▾

Surprise me with

Galmaegisal is the pork skirt cut, a thin, chewy muscle from the diaphragm that is a favourite at Korean barbecue restaurants, grilled and dipped in sesame oil and salt.

  • Cook 15 min

Method

  1. Trim the thin membrane from the pork skirt if your butcher has not, then cut it into strips about 1.5 cm (1/2 in) thick. Galmaegisal is lean, dense and chewy-tender, so it cooks fast and needs no marinade; salt and pepper bring out its rich, beefy pork flavor.
  2. Mix the dipping sesame oil with a pinch of salt and pepper in small dishes. Arrange the lettuce, perilla leaves, pa muchim, garlic, kimchi and ssamjang on the table.
  3. Heat a cast-iron grill pan or tabletop grill over medium-high heat until very hot, and rub it with a little of the sesame oil. Toss the pork with the rest of the sesame oil, salt and pepper.
  4. Lay the strips on the grill without crowding and cook for 2 to 3 minutes per side, until browned with a little char and just cooked through, with no pink in the center. Grill the garlic slices and kimchi alongside in the rendered fat until softened and caramelized.
  5. Snip the pork into bite-size pieces with kitchen scissors. Dip each piece in the sesame-oil salt and wrap it in lettuce with garlic, kimchi, green onion salad and a dab of ssamjang.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • A pan that is not hot enough stews the meat; preheat for 5 minutes.
  • Overcooking dries out this lean cut; 2 minutes per side is enough.

Authentic tips

  • Cut against the grain after grilling for the most tender bite.
  • Serve with a cold soju or beer.

Substitutions

  • Pork collar or pork belly replaces skirt.
  • Add gochujang for a spicy marinade.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator; reheat quickly in a hot pan.

Freezing: Freeze the raw marinated pork for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and kimchi
  • Soup: Doenjang jjigae
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold soju or beer
