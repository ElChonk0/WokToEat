Pāi Huáng Guā (Smashed Cucumber Salad) 拍黄瓜

▾

Surprise me with

Smashed cucumber salad, cucumbers hit with a cleaver and dressed with garlic and vinegar, is a cold dish found across northern and Sichuan kitchens; the smashing creates rough edges that absorb the dressing.

  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt

Method

  1. Lay the cucumbers on a board and smash them with the flat of a cleaver or a rolling pin until they crack open, then cut into bite-size chunks. Smashing gives rough edges that soak up the dressing far better than slicing.
  2. Toss the cucumber with the salt and leave in a colander for 15 to 20 minutes, then shake off the liquid. This draws out water so the dressing is not diluted.
  3. Mix the garlic, vinegar, soy sauce, sugar, sesame oil and chili oil until the sugar dissolves.
  4. Toss the cucumber with the dressing just before serving.
  5. Serve chilled as a cool side dish to spicy Sichuan dishes.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the salting leaves the dressing diluted by cucumber water.
  • Dressing too early makes the salad soggy.

Authentic tips

  • Chill for crispness.
  • Smaller, firmer cucumbers hold their crunch better.

Substitutions

  • Persian cucumbers need no peeling.
  • Crushed peanuts add crunch.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated; drain the liquid before serving.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Any rich mala main
  • Soup: Hot soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
