Pāi Huáng Guā (Smashed Cucumber Salad) 拍黄瓜

Smashed cucumber salad, cucumbers hit with a cleaver and dressed with garlic and vinegar, is a cold dish found across northern and Sichuan kitchens; the smashing creates rough edges that absorb the dressing.

  • 500 g1.1 lbEnglish cucumbers
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic, minced
  • 30 ml2 tbspChinkiang vinegar
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 10 ml2 tspSesame oil
  • 10 ml2 tspChili oil
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt

Method

  1. Smash the cucumbers with the flat of a cleaver or a rolling pin until they split, then cut into 3 cm pieces.
  2. Toss with the salt and let drain in a colander for 10 minutes; squeeze lightly and pat dry.
  3. Stir the garlic, vinegar, soy sauce, sesame oil, chili oil and sugar together.
  4. Toss with the cucumber and chill 10 minutes before serving.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the salting leaves the dressing diluted by cucumber water.
  • Dressing too early makes the salad soggy.

Authentic tips

  • Chill for crispness.
  • Smaller, firmer cucumbers hold their crunch better.

Substitutions

  • Persian cucumbers need no peeling.
  • Crushed peanuts add crunch.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated; drain the liquid before serving.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Any rich mala main
  • Soup: Hot soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
