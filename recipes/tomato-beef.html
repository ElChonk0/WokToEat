Tomato Beef

▾

Surprise me with

Tomato beef stir-fry reflects the Western influence that shaped much of Hong Kong's "soy sauce Western" (xi can) cuisine in the 20th century, pairing the distinctly un-Chinese tomato with classic Cantonese stir-fry technique.

  • Prep 15 min (plus 15 min marinating)
  • Cook 12 min

Method

  1. Marinate the sliced beef in 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine, 1 tbsp cornstarch, and the baking soda for 15 minutes (the velveting technique from Part 2).
  2. Mix the sauce: remaining soy sauce and Shaoxing wine, ketchup, sugar, and stock.
  3. Heat oil in a wok over high heat and sear the beef quickly, 1–2 minutes until just browned but not fully cooked through; remove and set aside.
  4. In the same wok, stir-fry the onion 1–2 minutes, then add garlic and tomato wedges; cook another 1–2 minutes until the tomatoes just begin to soften and release juice.
  5. Return the beef to the wok along with the sauce mixture; toss to combine.
  6. Stir in the cornstarch slurry and toss until the sauce thickens and clings to the beef and tomatoes.
  7. Serve immediately over steamed rice.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the tomatoes into mush — they should soften and release juice while still holding their wedge shape.
  • Skipping the velveting marinade, which results in noticeably tougher beef than the silky texture this dish is known for.

Authentic tips

  • Searing the beef separately and adding it back at the end keeps it from overcooking while the vegetables catch up.
  • Ripe, slightly soft tomatoes actually work better here than firm ones, since they break down into more sauce.

Substitutions

  • Chicken thigh or firm tofu can replace beef with the same velveting technique.
  • No ketchup — a mix of tomato paste and a touch of sugar and vinegar gets close.

Storage

Leftovers: Reheats well; the sauce continues to mellow and meld overnight.

Freezing: Not ideal — tomatoes turn mushy after freezing and reheating, though the flavor remains fine.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot jasmine tea
