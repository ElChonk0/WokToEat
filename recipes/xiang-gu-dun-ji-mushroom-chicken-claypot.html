Xiāng Gū Dùn Jī (Fujian Mushroom Chicken Clay Pot) 香菇炖鸡

▾

Surprise me with

Fujian's mountains grow shiitake, which are stewed with chicken in a clay pot for a deeply savory soup.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 50 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 30 g2 tbspGinger
  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 1 L4 cupsWater
  • 6 g1 tspSalt

Method

  1. Soak the dried shiitake in hot water for 30 minutes, squeeze, trim the stems and slice; keep the soaking liquid.
  2. Cover the chicken with cold water, bring to a boil for 3 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  3. Place the chicken, mushrooms, ginger, wine, soy sauce and 1 L water (including the mushroom liquid) in a Dutch oven or heavy pot.
  4. Bring to a boil, skim, then reduce to a low simmer and cook 45 minutes with the lid slightly ajar.
  5. Season with the salt and serve in the pot.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the blanching leaves a murky broth.
  • Boiling hard toughens the chicken and clouds the soup.

Authentic tips

  • The mushroom soaking liquid is full of flavor.
  • Add goji berries or red dates for sweetness.

Substitutions

  • Fresh shiitake replace dried; use the soaking liquid sparingly.
  • Add goji berries for the last 10 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; the broth improves.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
