Chirashi Zushi (Scattered Sushi) ちらし寿司

▾

Surprise me with

Chirashi zushi, sushi rice topped with sashimi and vegetables, is a festive Japanese dish especially for Hinamatsuri, the Girls' Day festival in March.

Method

  1. Rinse the rice until the water runs nearly clear and cook it with the kombu and slightly less water than usual, for firm grains. Warm the vinegar, sugar and salt until dissolved.
  2. Turn the hot rice into a wide bowl, ideally a wooden hangiri, and pour the sushi vinegar over a rice paddle. Fold with slicing motions while fanning it for 3 to 5 minutes, until glossy and at body temperature. Fanning drives off steam and gives the shine; stirring makes it mushy.
  3. Beat the eggs with the sugar and a pinch of salt and cook paper-thin sheets in a lightly oiled nonstick pan over low heat, about 30 seconds each, without browning. Roll and slice them into fine threads.
  4. Fold the cucumber and sliced shiitake into the rice and spread it in a shallow dish or bowls. Cover with the egg threads.
  5. Arrange the fish, shrimp or ikura and snow peas on top, scatter with nori and serve with pickled ginger, soy sauce and wasabi.

Common mistakes

  • Warm rice cooks the fish; cool it first.
  • Sauce poured over the rice makes it soggy.

Authentic tips

  • Use only sashimi-grade fish.
  • Fold in cooked shiitake and lotus root for the traditional version.

Substitutions

  • Cooked shrimp and smoked salmon replace raw fish.
  • Pre-made sushi vinegar saves time.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled ginger
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot sencha
