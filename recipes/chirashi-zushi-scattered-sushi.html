Chirashi Zushi (Scattered Sushi) ちらし寿司

▾

Surprise me with

Chirashi zushi, sushi rice topped with sashimi and vegetables, is a festive Japanese dish especially for Hinamatsuri, the Girls' Day festival in March.

Method

  1. Cook the rice and fold in the sushi vinegar mixture while hot; fan until cool.
  2. Make thin omelet crepes with the beaten eggs and slice into strips.
  3. Slice the fish into bite-size pieces and the cucumber thinly.
  4. Spread the rice in bowls.
  5. Scatter the egg, fish and cucumber on top; garnish with nori and pickled ginger and serve with soy sauce and wasabi.

Common mistakes

  • Warm rice cooks the fish; cool it first.
  • Sauce poured over the rice makes it soggy.

Authentic tips

  • Use only sashimi-grade fish.
  • Fold in cooked shiitake and lotus root for the traditional version.

Substitutions

  • Cooked shrimp and smoked salmon replace raw fish.
  • Pre-made sushi vinegar saves time.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled ginger
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot sencha
