Kiam Chye Ark Tng (Salted Vegetable Duck Soup) 咸菜鸭汤

▾

Surprise me with

Salted vegetable duck soup is a Teochew soup of duck simmered with salted mustard greens, tomatoes and sour plums for a tangy, clear broth, popular at Singapore Chinese New Year and family dinners.

Method

  1. Taste the salted mustard greens: if very salty, soak them for 5 to 10 minutes at most, then squeeze and tear them into pieces. Over-soaking leaves them bland, and the salt is what flavors the soup.
  2. Trim the fat and most of the skin from the duck and blanch the pieces in boiling water for 5 minutes. Drain and rinse.
  3. Put the duck, salted greens, sour plums, ginger, whole garlic, white peppercorns (crushed and tied in a muslin bag) and water in a large pot. Bring to a boil, skim and lower to a gentle simmer.
  4. Simmer, partly covered, for 2 hours, until the duck falls from the bone, topping up with boiling water if needed. Add the tomatoes for the last 15 minutes.
  5. Taste before adding any salt, as the greens and plums are salty; adjust with sugar if too sour. Serve with rice and sliced chili in soy sauce.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the soak leaves a soup too salty to drink.
  • Boiling hard makes the broth cloudy and the duck tough.

Authentic tips

  • Press a few tomatoes against the side of the pot to thicken the soup slightly.
  • Chill and skim the fat for a lighter soup.

Substitutions

  • Pickled mustard greens replace salted greens.
  • Chicken replaces duck.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days in the refrigerator; the flavor improves.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and chili-lime dip
  • Soup: The dish is a soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot Chinese tea
