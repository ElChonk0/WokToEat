Niú Ròu Zhōu (Fujian Beef Congee) 牛肉粥

▾

Surprise me with

Congee is the breakfast of southern China, and Fujian cooks add ground beef, ginger and scallion for a savory version.

  • Cook 50 min

  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 2 L8 cupsWater or stock
  • 40 g1.5 ozScallion and cilantro
  • 5 ml1 tspSesame oil

Method

  1. Rinse the rice and simmer in the water or stock with the ginger, partly covered, for 40 minutes, stirring occasionally, until it breaks down into a creamy porridge.
  2. Mix the ground beef with the soy sauce and a spoonful of water.
  3. Stir the beef into the bubbling congee, breaking it up, and cook 3 minutes until just cooked through.
  4. Season and add a little water if it is too thick.
  5. Ladle into bowls and top with scallion, cilantro and sesame oil.

Common mistakes

  • Not stirring lets the rice scorch on the bottom.
  • Overcooking the beef toughens it.

Authentic tips

  • Marinate the beef with a pinch of cornstarch for a silky texture.
  • Add pickles at the table.

Substitutions

  • Ground pork or chicken replaces beef.
  • Leftover cooked rice shortens the cooking to 30 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; loosen with water when reheating.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Fried dough sticks
  • Soup: Not needed; the congee is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
