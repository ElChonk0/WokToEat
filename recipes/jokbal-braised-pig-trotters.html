Jokbal (Braised Pig's Trotters) 족발

Jokbal, pig's trotters braised in soy sauce and rice wine and sliced, is a Korean drinking food that became popular in Seoul in the 1960s.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 2 hr
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 80 g5 tbspGinger and garlic
  • 8 g3 tspCinnamon and star anise

Method

  1. Soak the trotters in cold water for 1 hour to draw out blood; blanch in boiling water for 10 minutes, then rinse.
  2. In a pot, bring the soy sauce, wine, sugar, aromatics, spices and water to a boil.
  3. Add the trotters, cover and simmer for 90 minutes until the skin is soft and sticky.
  4. Rest in the liquid 15 minutes, lift out and slice.
  5. Wrap in lettuce with saeujeot (salted shrimp) and garlic.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the blanch leaves a muddy smell.
  • Boiling hard toughens the skin.

Authentic tips

  • Serve cold or warm with soju.
  • The liquid can be reused for the next batch.

Substitutions

  • Pork hock replaces trotters.
  • A pressure cooker cuts the braise to 50 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; slice cold.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Lettuce wraps with ssamjang and salted shrimp
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Soju
