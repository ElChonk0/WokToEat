Yán Sū Xiā (Taiwanese Salt-and-Pepper Shrimp) 鹽酥蝦

Yan su ji (salt-crispy chicken) is a Taiwan night-market icon; the same seasoning, with fried basil and garlic, is applied to shrimp.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 8 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Taiwan Seafood LunchDinnerDeep-fried30 min or lessShellfish
230 kcal13 g protein17 g carbs12 g fat900 mg sodiumper serving

Ingredients

  • 600 g1.3 lbShrimp, shell on
  • 60 g1/2 cupSweet potato starch
  • 30 g2 tbspGarlic
  • 30 g1 ozThai basil
  • 8 g1.5 tspSalt and white pepper
  • 2 g1/2 tspFive-spice powder
  • 400 ml1.75 cupsNeutral oil for frying

Method

  1. Pat the shrimp very dry and toss with the starch.
  2. Heat the oil to 190 C (375 F).
  3. Fry the shrimp 1-2 minutes until crisp; drain.
  4. Fry the garlic and basil 10 seconds.
  5. Toss all with the salt, white pepper and five-spice and serve at once.

Common mistakes

  • Wet shrimp spatter and fry soggy.
  • Overcooking makes them rubbery.

Authentic tips

  • Eat the shells too if the shrimp are small.
  • Serve hot with beer.

Substitutions

  • Squid or tofu take the same coating.
  • An air fryer at 200 C (400 F) for 6 minutes works.

Storage

Leftovers: Best within minutes.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Corn soup
  • Dessert: Shaved ice
  • Beverage: Cold beer
