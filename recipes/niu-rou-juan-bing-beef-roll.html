Niú Ròu Juǎn Bǐng (Beef Roll Pancake) 牛肉捲餅

▾

Surprise me with

Beef rolls, beef braised or sliced and wrapped in a scallion pancake with hoisin and cucumber, are a Taiwanese street and restaurant food of mainland Chinese origin.

  • Prep 25 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 44Scallion pancakes
  • 400 g14 ozBraised beef shank, sliced
  • 60 g4 tbspHoisin or sweet bean sauce
  • 150 g5 ozCucumber
  • 40 g1.5 ozScallion
  • 20 g2/3 ozCilantro
  • 10 ml2 tspSesame oil

Method

  1. Slice the braised beef shank thinly (see the braised beef shank recipe).
  2. Warm the scallion pancakes in a dry pan until pliable.
  3. Spread each with hoisin sauce.
  4. Layer on the beef, cucumber, scallion and cilantro.
  5. Roll up tightly, drizzle with sesame oil and slice in half.

Common mistakes

  • Cold pancakes crack when rolled.
  • Wet cucumber makes the roll soggy.

Authentic tips

  • Make the beef a day ahead.
  • Serve with a bowl of soup.

Substitutions

  • Store-bought scallion pancakes replace homemade.
  • Deli roast beef is a shortcut.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day wrapped.

Freezing: Freeze the braised beef for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Clear beef broth
  • Soup: Pickled vegetables
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Oolong tea
