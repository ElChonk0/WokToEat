Xiāng Shì Hóng Shāo Dòu Fu (Hunan Red-Braised Tofu) 湘式红烧豆腐

▾

Surprise me with

Red-braised tofu in Hunan style is pan-fried tofu braised with dried chilies, fermented black beans and soy sauce, giving a deep, smoky, spicy flavor.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

  • 500 g1.1 lbFirm tofu, cubed
  • 10 g1/3 ozDried chilies
  • 15 g1 tbspFermented black beans
  • 30 g2 tbspGarlic and ginger
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 10 ml2 tspDark soy sauce
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Pat the tofu dry and cut into 3 cm cubes.
  2. Heat half the oil in a nonstick pan over medium heat and fry the tofu 8 minutes, turning, until golden on all sides; remove.
  3. Add the remaining oil, chilies, black beans, garlic and ginger and fry 30 seconds.
  4. Add the soy sauces, sugar and 150 ml water and bring to a simmer.
  5. Return the tofu and simmer 8 minutes until the sauce is thick and clinging.

Common mistakes

  • Breaking the tofu by stirring too much; use gentle turns.
  • Over-salting: the black beans and soy are salty.

Authentic tips

  • Pan-fry the tofu well so it holds its shape in the braise.
  • Dark soy gives the deep color.

Substitutions

  • Smoked tofu adds depth.
  • Mushrooms make it heartier.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
