Beef Chilli (Indo-Chinese Chilli Beef)

▾

Surprise me with

Indo-Chinese food, born in Kolkata's Chinese community, produced chilli dishes such as chilli beef that are popular across India, especially in Kerala and the Northeast.

Method

  1. Toss the beef with the marinade soy sauce, egg, cornstarch, flour, pepper and a pinch of salt until each strip is coated.
  2. Heat the oil in a flat-bottom wok to 180 C (350 F) and fry the beef in batches for 2 to 3 minutes until crisp and golden. Drain. Pour off all but 2 tablespoons of oil.
  3. Over high heat, stir-fry the garlic, ginger and green chilies for 30 seconds, then the onion and capsicum for 1 minute; they should stay crunchy.
  4. Add the soy sauce, chili sauce, ketchup, vinegar and sugar and bubble for 30 seconds, then stir in the slurry until glossy. For a gravy version, add 120 ml (1/2 cup) more water.
  5. Toss in the beef and spring onion greens for 30 seconds and serve at once with fried rice or Hakka noodles. Indo-Chinese food grew out of Kolkata's Hakka Chinese community.

Common mistakes

  • Crowding the pan steams the beef.
  • Overcooking the peppers makes them limp.

Authentic tips

  • Slice the beef thin against the grain.
  • Serve with fried rice.

Substitutions

  • Paneer or chicken take the same sauce.
  • Air-fry the beef at 200 C (400 F) before tossing.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Chinese-style fried rice
  • Soup: Hot and sour soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
