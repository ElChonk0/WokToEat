Sān Xiān Shuǐ Jiǎo (Egg, Chive and Mushroom Dumplings) 三鲜素饺子

▾

Surprise me with

Vegetarian dumplings with egg, chives and mushrooms are a staple of northern Chinese households, especially at New Year when dumplings are eaten at midnight.

  • Cook 10 min

Method

  1. Scramble the eggs in the oil into fine curds and cool them.
  2. Mix the chives with the sesame oil first; coating them in oil before salting stops them weeping water into the filling.
  3. Add the eggs, shiitake, glass noodles, ginger, soy sauce, salt and pepper and mix gently. Season the filling just before wrapping.
  4. Put a heaping teaspoon of filling in each wrapper, fold and pleat to seal.
  5. Boil in plenty of water, adding a cup of cold water each time it returns to a boil, twice, until the dumplings float and look puffed, 5 to 6 minutes. Serve with black vinegar and garlic.

Common mistakes

  • Adding salt early makes the chives release water; mix just before filling.
  • Overfilling splits the wrappers.

Authentic tips

  • Toss the chives with oil to keep them green and prevent water loss.
  • Fry leftover dumplings in a pan.

Substitutions

  • Spinach with chives replaces the glass noodles.
  • Add pressed tofu for protein.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; pan-fry leftovers.

Freezing: Freeze uncooked on a tray for 2 months; boil from frozen for 8 minutes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Black vinegar dip
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
