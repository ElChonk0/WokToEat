San Xian Shui Jiao (Egg, Chive and Mushroom Dumplings) 三鲜素饺子

Vegetarian dumplings with egg, chives and mushrooms are a staple of northern Chinese households, especially at New Year when dumplings are eaten at midnight.

  • Prep 45 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 200 g7 ozChinese chives
  • 30 g1 ozDried shiitake, chopped
  • 60 g2 ozGlass noodles, cooked and chopped
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspSesame oil
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt

Method

  1. Soak the mushrooms until soft, squeeze and chop; scramble the eggs and chop finely.
  2. Toss the chives with the sesame oil first to coat them, then mix with the eggs, mushrooms, glass noodles, soy sauce and salt.
  3. Place a tablespoon of filling in each wrapper, fold and seal.
  4. Boil in plenty of water 5-6 minutes until the dumplings float.
  5. Serve with black vinegar and chili oil.

Common mistakes

  • Adding salt early makes the chives release water; mix just before filling.
  • Overfilling splits the wrappers.

Authentic tips

  • Toss the chives with oil to keep them green and prevent water loss.
  • Fry leftover dumplings in a pan.

Substitutions

  • Spinach with chives replaces the glass noodles.
  • Add pressed tofu for protein.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; pan-fry leftovers.

Freezing: Freeze uncooked on a tray for 2 months; boil from frozen for 8 minutes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Black vinegar dip
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
