Singapore Fish Head Curry

▾

Surprise me with

Fish head curry, a Singapore dish created by Indian cooks adapting Kerala flavors for Chinese customers, uses a large red snapper head in a tamarind curry with okra.

  • Cook 40 min

Method

  1. Rinse the fish head, scrape out the gills and rub it with the salt and turmeric. Blend the shallots, garlic and ginger into a paste, and mix the curry and chili powders with a little water into a second paste.
  2. Heat the oil in a wide Dutch oven over medium heat and fry the mustard, fenugreek and fennel seeds until the mustard seeds pop, then the curry leaves. Add the shallot paste for 3 minutes, then the curry paste for 5 to 8 minutes until the oil separates.
  3. Pour in the tamarind water, add the tomatoes and bring to a boil, then simmer for 10 minutes so the gravy develops.
  4. Add the eggplant and okra and simmer for 5 minutes, then lay in the fish head and spoon the gravy over it. Simmer gently, without stirring, for 10 to 12 minutes, until the flesh at the thickest part pulls away from the bone.
  5. Stir in the coconut milk, sugar and salt and bring back to a gentle simmer. Taste: it should be tangy, spicy and rich. Serve in the pot with rice, and enjoy the tender cheeks and collar meat.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard splits the coconut milk and breaks up the fish.
  • Overcooked okra turns slimy.

Authentic tips

  • The cheeks are the best part.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • Fish fillets replace the head.
  • Okra, eggplant and tomato are traditional.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with
