Inari Zushi (Tofu Pouch Sushi) いなり寿司

▾

Surprise me with

Inari zushi, sushi rice stuffed in sweet fried tofu pouches, is named for the fox deity Inari, whose messengers are said to love fried tofu; it's a picnic and bento staple.

  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 88Aburaage (fried tofu pouches)
  • 450 g16 ozSushi rice
  • 45 ml3 tbspSoy sauce
  • 45 g3 tbspSugar
  • 30 ml2 tbspMirin
  • 15 g1 tbspSesame seeds
  • 250 ml1 cupKombu dashi

Method

  1. Blanch the aburaage in boiling water for 1 minute to remove oil; squeeze gently.
  2. Simmer in the dashi, soy sauce, sugar and mirin for 15 minutes until the liquid is nearly gone; cool.
  3. Mix the sushi rice with the sesame seeds.
  4. Squeeze the pouches gently and open them.
  5. Fill them with rice, shaping and tucking the edges in.

Common mistakes

  • Overfilling splits the pouches.
  • Hot pouches tear; cool them first.

Authentic tips

  • Pre-seasoned pouches are sold in packets.
  • Add shredded ginger to the rice.

Substitutions

  • Pre-seasoned aburaage pouches save time.
  • Add shredded ginger to the rice.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Miso soup
  • Soup: Pickled ginger
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot sencha
