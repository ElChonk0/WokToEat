Yúnnán Jūn Zi Yú Tāng (Wild Mushroom Fish Soup) 野生菌鱼汤

▾

Yunnan's wet season brings wild mushrooms, and local cooks simmer them with freshwater fish in a clear broth.

  • 1.2 L5 cupsWater or stock

Method

  1. Toss the fish with the cornstarch and a pinch of salt.
  2. Wild mushrooms must be cooked through: stir-fry them for at least 8 to 10 minutes with plenty of garlic, and buy only from trusted sources. Cultivated king oyster, shiitake and oyster mushrooms are a safe substitute.
  3. Fry the mushrooms with the ginger, garlic and chili in the oil over medium-high heat for 8 to 10 minutes until cooked through and browned.
  4. Add the stock, bring to a boil and simmer for 10 minutes until fragrant, then add the fish and simmer for 2 minutes until opaque.
  5. Season with salt and scatter with green onion.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard breaks the fish.
  • Wild mushrooms must be cooked thoroughly.

Authentic tips

  • Cultivated mushrooms work if wild ones are not available.
  • Never eat foraged mushrooms you cannot identify.

Substitutions

  • Cultivated mixed mushrooms replace wild ones.
  • Fish bones plus fillets enrich the broth.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated.

Freezing: Freeze the broth only.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Pu'er tea
