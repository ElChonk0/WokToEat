Yúnnán Jūn Zi Yú Tāng (Wild Mushroom Fish Soup) 野生菌鱼汤

▾

Surprise me with

Yunnan's wet season brings wild mushrooms, and local cooks simmer them with freshwater fish in a clear broth.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 25 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 600 g1.3 lbFish fillets
  • 250 g9 ozMixed mushrooms
  • 30 g2 tbspGinger
  • 30 g2 tbspScallion
  • 1 L4 cupsWater or stock
  • 6 g1 tspSalt
  • 10 g2 tspChili
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Fry the ginger and mushrooms in the oil in a pot over medium-high heat for 5 minutes.
  2. Add the stock and chili, bring to a boil and simmer 15 minutes.
  3. Season with the salt.
  4. Slide in the fish fillets and poach gently 3 minutes until just opaque.
  5. Add the scallion and serve.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard breaks the fish.
  • Wild mushrooms must be cooked thoroughly.

Authentic tips

  • Cultivated mushrooms work if wild ones are not available.
  • Never eat foraged mushrooms you cannot identify.

Substitutions

  • Cultivated mixed mushrooms replace wild ones.
  • Fish bones plus fillets enrich the broth.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated.

Freezing: Freeze the broth only.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Pu'er tea
