Chǎo Mǐ Fěn (Taiwanese Fried Rice Vermicelli) 炒米粉

Taiwanese fried rice vermicelli, from Hsinchu's famous thin rice noodles, is stir-fried with pork, cabbage and mushrooms and is a staple of banquets and night markets.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 180 g6 ozDried rice vermicelli
  • 250 g9 ozPork, shredded
  • 250 g9 ozCabbage and carrots
  • 50 g2 ozDried shiitake and shallots
  • 45 ml3 tbspSoy sauce
  • 1 g1/4 tspWhite pepper
  • 150 ml2/3 cupStock
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Soak the vermicelli in hot water for 5 minutes until flexible; drain. Soak the shiitake, then slice.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok and fry the shallots and mushrooms 2 minutes until fragrant.
  3. Add the pork and stir 2 minutes, then the cabbage and carrots for 3 minutes.
  4. Add the noodles, soy sauce, pepper and stock and toss on medium heat 4 minutes until absorbed.
  5. Serve hot.

Common mistakes

  • Over-soaked vermicelli turns to mush.
  • Too much stock makes the noodles gummy.

Authentic tips

  • Hsinchu vermicelli is the classic.
  • Add dried shrimp for extra flavor.

Substitutions

  • Thin dried rice noodles replace Hsinchu vermicelli.
  • Add dried shrimp for extra flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot pan with a splash of water.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Fish ball soup
  • Soup: Not needed; the noodles are the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Oolong tea
