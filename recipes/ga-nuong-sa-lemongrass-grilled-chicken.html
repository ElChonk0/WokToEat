Gà Nướng Sả (Lemongrass Grilled Chicken)

▾

Surprise me with

A fragrant grilled chicken marinated in lemongrass, garlic, and fish sauce, this dish is a staple of Vietnamese barbecue and street food, often served over rice or stuffed into a banh mi.

  • Prep 20 min (plus 2 hr marinating)
  • Cook 18 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 4 cloves4 clovesGarlic, minced

  • 1 g1/2 tspBlack pepper
  • 1 g1/2 tspFive-spice powder (optional)

Method

  1. Pound or blend the lemongrass, garlic and shallots into a fine paste, then mix with the fish sauce, oyster sauce, sugar, oil, pepper and five-spice. Massage it into the chicken and marinate for at least 1 hour, or overnight. The sugar caramelizes over the heat into the smoky, sticky crust.
  2. Heat a grill to medium-high, or the oven to 220 C (425 F) with a rack under the broiler.
  3. Grill the chicken skin-side down for 6 to 7 minutes until charred and crisp, then turn and cook for 4 to 5 minutes until cooked through (74 C / 165 F). In the oven, roast for 18 to 20 minutes and finish under the broiler.
  4. Rest for 5 minutes, then slice.
  5. Serve over broken rice or vermicelli with pickles, herbs and nuoc cham.

Common mistakes

  • Mincing lemongrass too coarsely, which leaves tough, fibrous pieces in the finished dish.
  • Grilling over too high heat, which burns the sugar in the marinade before the chicken cooks through.

Authentic tips

  • Use only the tender inner portion of the lemongrass stalk, near the base, and mince it very finely for the best texture.
  • A food processor makes quick work of finely mincing the lemongrass, garlic, and shallot together.

Substitutions

  • Bone-in thighs work with a slightly longer cooking time.
  • No grill: roast on a wire rack over a sheet pan at 230°C (450°F) for 25 to 30 minutes, basting with the marinade every 10 minutes, then broil 2 to 3 minutes for char; boneless thighs take about 18 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; great sliced into a banh mi.

Freezing: Freezes well, marinated and raw, for up to a month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or banh mi, with pickled carrot and cucumber
  • Soup: Canh
  • Dessert: Che (sweet bean dessert)
  • Beverage: Iced tea
