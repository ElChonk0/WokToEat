Dōng Gū Dòu Fu Bāo (Mushroom and Tofu Pot) 冬菇豆腐煲

▾

Fried tofu braised with shiitake in a savory sauce and finished in a sizzling pot is a standard Cantonese restaurant and home dish, often made vegetarian.

Method

  1. Soak the mushrooms in warm water for at least 20 minutes until soft through, keeping the liquid. Pat the tofu very dry and dust it lightly with cornstarch, which gives it a thin crust that holds the sauce.
  2. Heat a flat-bottom wok over high heat until it smokes, add the oil and lower to medium. Fry the tofu for 2 to 3 minutes per side, without moving it, until golden on both sides. Lift it out.
  3. Fry the ginger and garlic for 30 seconds, then the mushrooms for 1 minute, until fragrant. Add the napa cabbage stems and stir-fry 1 minute, then splash in the wine.
  4. Transfer to a Dutch oven or clay pot, add the soaking water, mushroom sauce, soy sauces and sugar and bring to a boil. Add the cabbage leaves and lay the tofu on top. Cover and braise over low heat for 8 to 10 minutes, until the cabbage is soft and the tofu has absorbed the sauce.
  5. Stir in the cornstarch mixed with 1 tablespoon of water until the sauce turns glossy. Add the sesame oil, scatter with green onion, cover for 30 seconds and serve bubbling in the pot.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Wet tofu spatters and will not brown; pat it dry.
  • Stirring hard breaks the tofu; nudge it gently.

Authentic tips

  • The strained mushroom soaking water is the backbone of the sauce.
  • A clay pot is traditional; a Dutch oven holds the sizzle on electric and induction hobs.

Substitutions

  • Regular oyster sauce if it does not need to be vegetarian.
  • Fried tofu puffs replace pan-fried tofu.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended; the tofu turns spongy.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and garlic choy sum
  • Soup: Spinach tofu soup
  • Dessert: Sweet tofu pudding
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
