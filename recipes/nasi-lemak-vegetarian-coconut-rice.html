Nasi Lemak (Vegetarian Coconut Rice Plate)

Nasi lemak, coconut rice with sambal, anchovies, peanuts and egg, is Malaysia's unofficial national dish and a standard breakfast; this version replaces the anchovies with tempeh.

  • Cook 30 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Rinse the rice and cook it with the coconut milk, pandan, a pinch of salt and enough water to cover by 1 cm; about 20 minutes.
  2. Warm the vegetarian sambal.
  3. Fry the tempeh until crisp; boil or fry the eggs.
  4. Slice the cucumber.
  5. Plate the rice with the sambal, tempeh, eggs, peanuts and cucumber.

Common mistakes

  • Too much coconut milk makes the rice greasy.
  • Skipping the pandan loses the fragrance.

Authentic tips

  • The classic version uses fried anchovies.
  • Banana-leaf packets are the street style.

Substitutions

  • Anchovies are the traditional topping.
  • Coconut milk powder replaces canned.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; the rice firms up.

Freezing: Freezes the rice for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sambal and fried peanuts
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Kuih
  • Beverage: Teh tarik
