Wú Wéi Xūn Jī (Wuwei Smoked Chicken) 无为熏鸡

▾

Surprise me with

Wuwei in Anhui is known for its smoked poultry, which is braised in spiced stock and smoked over sugar and tea until deep amber.

  • Cook 1.5 hr

  • 45 ml3 tbspLight soy sauce

  • 15 g1 tbspSugar, for the brine
  • 8 g1.5 tspSalt
  • 40 g1.5 ozStar anise, cinnamon, ginger and green onion

  • 30 g2 tbspUncooked rice, for smoking
  • 20 g1/4 cupTea leaves, for smoking
  • 40 g3 tbspBrown sugar, for smoking
  • 15 ml1 tbspSesame oil

Method

  1. Rub the chicken inside and out with salt and leave it for 2 hours.
  2. Simmer the soy sauces, sugar, spices, ginger, green onion and water for 10 minutes, add the chicken and poach at a bare simmer for 35 minutes, then leave it in the liquid for 20 minutes until the thigh reaches 74 C (165 F).
  3. Pat the chicken dry. Line a flat-bottom wok and lid with foil, scatter the rice, tea and brown sugar in the bottom and set a rack above.
  4. Smoke the chicken, covered, over medium heat for 12 to 15 minutes, then leave covered off the heat for 5 minutes. The rice and tea smoke give Wuwei chicken its amber skin.
  5. Brush with sesame oil and serve sliced, cold or warm.

Common mistakes

  • Smoking too long makes it bitter.
  • Poor ventilation: open a window and use the extractor fan.

Authentic tips

  • Cool the poached chicken so the skin dries and takes the smoke.
  • Wuwei is famous for smoked duck as well.

Substitutions

  • Duck is the other famous Wuwei smoked meat and takes the same method.
  • Smoking in a foil-lined covered pot works on the stovetop.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; serve cold.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed buns
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
