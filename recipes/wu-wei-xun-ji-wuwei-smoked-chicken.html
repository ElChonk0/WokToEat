Wú Wéi Xūn Jī (Wuwei Smoked Chicken) 无为熏鸡

▾

Surprise me with

Wuwei in Anhui is known for its smoked poultry, which is braised in spiced stock and smoked over sugar and tea until deep amber.

  • Cook 70 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 25 g1 ozStar anise, cinnamon, ginger
  • 60 g2 ozTea leaves and sugar for smoking
  • 15 ml1 tbspSesame oil
  • 30 g1 ozRice

Method

  1. Simmer the chicken in water with the soy sauce, sugar, star anise, cinnamon and ginger for 40 minutes until just cooked; lift out and cool 20 minutes.
  2. Line a wok and its lid with foil and spread the tea leaves, sugar and rice in the bottom; set a rack above.
  3. Set the chicken on the rack, cover tightly and smoke over medium-high heat 10 minutes until the skin turns deep amber.
  4. Brush with the sesame oil, rest 10 minutes and chop.

Common mistakes

  • Smoking too long makes it bitter.
  • Poor ventilation: open a window and use the extractor fan.

Authentic tips

  • Cool the poached chicken so the skin dries and takes the smoke.
  • Wuwei is famous for smoked duck as well.

Substitutions

  • Duck is the other famous Wuwei smoked meat and takes the same method.
  • Smoking in a foil-lined covered pot works on the stovetop.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; serve cold.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed buns
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
