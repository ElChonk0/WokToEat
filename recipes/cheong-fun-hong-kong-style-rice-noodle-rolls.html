Cheong Fun (Hong Kong-Style Rice Noodle Rolls) 肠粉

▾

Surprise me with

Cheong fun, steamed rice noodle rolls, are a Cantonese dim sum and breakfast staple served plain with a sweet soy sauce, or filled with shrimp, beef or fried dough.

  • Cook 30 min

Method

  1. Whisk the rice flour, wheat starch, tapioca starch, water, 15 ml of the oil and the salt into a thin batter, and rest for 30 minutes.
  2. Heat the soy sauce, sugar and water in a small pot until the sugar dissolves, then stir in the sesame oil.
  3. Oil a 20 cm square heatproof tray and set it over a steamer of boiling water.
  4. Stir the batter, pour a thin layer (about 60 ml) into the tray, cover and steam for 3 minutes until translucent and set.
  5. Use a bench scraper to roll the sheet into a loose roll, then lift it onto a plate. Repeat, oiling the tray between sheets.
  6. Cut the rolls into pieces, pour over the sweet soy sauce and sprinkle with the sesame seeds and fried shallots.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • A batter that has settled makes uneven rolls; stir it before each pour.
  • A thick layer stays doughy; keep the layer thin.

Authentic tips

  • A steady, rolling boil keeps the sheets smooth.
  • Fill with fried tofu puffs or dried shrimp if you do not need it vegetarian.

Substitutions

  • Store-bought fresh rice noodle sheets replace the homemade batter.
  • Hoisin and peanut sauce are common additions.

Storage

Leftovers: Eat at once; chilled rolls harden and need steaming for 5 minutes.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Chili sauce and hoisin
  • Soup: Clear congee
  • Dessert: Not needed
  • Beverage: Hot Chinese tea
