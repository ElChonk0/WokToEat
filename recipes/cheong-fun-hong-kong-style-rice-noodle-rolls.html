Cheong Fun (Hong Kong-Style Rice Noodle Rolls) 肠粉

▾

Surprise me with

Cheong fun, steamed rice noodle rolls, are a Cantonese dim sum and breakfast staple served plain with a sweet soy sauce, or filled with shrimp, beef or fried dough.

  • Prep 15 min (plus 45 min resting)
  • Cook 35 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Method

  1. Whisk the rice flour, tapioca starch, wheat starch and salt with the batter oil and lukewarm water until smooth, then whisk in the boiling water. Rest for 45 minutes; a rested batter makes thin, supple sheets.
  2. Make the sauce: dissolve the sugar in the sauce water in a small pan, add the soy sauces and mushroom sauce and simmer very gently for 3 to 4 minutes until it lightly coats a spoon. Stir in the sesame oil.
  3. Bring 5 cm (2 in) of water to a simmer in a wide pot or flat-bottom wok with a tight lid and a small rack. Oil a 25 cm (10 in) square metal pan and warm it in the steamer.
  4. Stir the batter well, as it separates every time it sits. Pour in about 80 ml (1/3 cup) and swirl it into a thin, even layer, about 3 mm (1/8 in). Scatter with a little green onion, cover and steam for 2 to 3 minutes, until the sheet turns translucent and bubbles.
  5. Lift the pan out, loosen the edge with a spatula and roll the sheet up tightly. Oil the pan again before each sheet and keep the water at a steady simmer.
  6. Cut the rolls into 3 to 5 cm (1 to 2 in) pieces with an oiled knife and serve warm, drizzled with the sweet soy and scattered with sesame seeds and fried shallots.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • A batter that has settled makes uneven rolls; stir it before each pour.
  • A thick layer stays doughy; keep the layer thin.

Authentic tips

  • A steady, rolling boil keeps the sheets smooth.
  • Fill with fried tofu puffs or dried shrimp if you do not need it vegetarian.

Substitutions

  • Store-bought fresh rice noodle sheets replace the homemade batter.
  • Hoisin and peanut sauce are common additions.

Storage

Leftovers: Eat at once; chilled rolls harden and need steaming for 5 minutes.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Chili sauce and hoisin
  • Soup: Clear congee
  • Dessert: Not needed
  • Beverage: Hot Chinese tea
