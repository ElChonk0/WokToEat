Xiǎo Chǎo Jī (Hunan Small-Wok Chicken) 小炒鸡

▾

Surprise me with

Xiao chao, "small stir-fry," is Hunan's fast, fiery home-cooking style, usually built on green chilies and fermented black beans.

  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 500 g1.1 lbChicken thighs, sliced
  • 200 g7 ozLong green chilies
  • 15 g1 tbspFermented black beans
  • 30 g2 tbspGarlic and ginger
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Slice the chicken thin and toss with half the wine and a pinch of salt. Cut the chilies into 3 cm pieces.
  2. Heat half the oil in a wok over high heat and sear the chicken 2 minutes until golden; remove.
  3. Add the remaining oil, black beans, garlic and ginger and fry 20 seconds until fragrant.
  4. Add the chilies and stir-fry 2-3 minutes until blistered and lightly charred.
  5. Return the chicken with the soy sauce and remaining wine and toss 1 minute.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking in a crowded or cool pan steams the chicken; use a very hot wok.
  • Burning the black beans turns the dish bitter.

Authentic tips

  • Char the chilies until they blister; the smokiness is the signature.
  • Rinse the black beans briefly if they taste very salty.

Substitutions

  • Pork shoulder takes the same method.
  • Bell peppers replace long chilies for a milder dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day and reheats in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
