Beef Satay (Sate Sapi)

A beef version of Indonesia's iconic grilled skewer, sate sapi marinates beef in a sweet soy and spice mixture before grilling, traditionally served with a rich peanut sauce alongside rice cakes.

  • Prep 20 min (plus 2 hr marinating)
  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Combine kecap manis, garlic, coriander, turmeric, and oil for the marinade.
  2. Coat beef cubes thoroughly and marinate at least 2 hours, or overnight for best flavor.
  3. Thread beef onto skewers.
  4. Grill over medium-high heat, turning occasionally, 10–12 minutes until charred and cooked to preference.
  5. Serve with peanut sauce and rice cakes (lontong) or steamed rice.

Common mistakes

  • Grilling over too high heat, which chars the sugary marinade before the beef cooks through.
  • Cutting the beef cubes unevenly, which cooks unevenly on the skewer.

Authentic tips

  • Basting with a little extra kecap manis while grilling builds up a beautifully lacquered exterior.
  • A good peanut sauce is central to this dish's appeal — worth making from scratch or seeking out a quality jarred version.

Substitutions

  • Chicken or lamb both work well with the same marinade and method.
  • No grill: lay the skewers on a wire rack over a foil-lined sheet pan and broil on high about 10 cm (4 in) from the element for 4 to 5 minutes per side, or roast at 230°C (450°F) for 12 to 15 minutes, turning once, until charred at the edges and cooked through.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes well, marinated and raw, for up to a month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Lontong and peanut sauce
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Es campur
  • Beverage: Es teh
