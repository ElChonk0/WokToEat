Goan Pork Chilli Fry

▾

Surprise me with

Goan pork chilli fry, a spicy dry-fried pork dish with vinegar and Goan red chilies, reflects the state's Portuguese-influenced Catholic cooking.

Method

  1. Simmer the pork with the salt, turmeric, pork vinegar and 250 ml (1 cup) water, covered, for 30 to 40 minutes until tender and most of the liquid has gone.
  2. Grind the chilies, cumin, pepper, garlic and ginger with the paste vinegar into a smooth red paste.
  3. Heat the oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat and fry the onions for 8 to 10 minutes until golden. Add the paste and fry for 3 minutes until it darkens and the oil separates.
  4. Add the tomato and green chilies for 2 minutes, then the cooked pork and any liquid. Fry over medium-high heat, stirring, for 8 to 10 minutes, until the pork is browned and coated in a dry, glossy masala.
  5. Add the bell pepper and sugar for the last 2 minutes. Serve with pao or as a bar snack, the Goan way.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the paste makes it bitter.
  • Not reducing leaves a watery curry.

Authentic tips

  • Goan vinegar (palm toddy vinegar) is traditional.
  • Better the next day.

Substitutions

  • Coconut vinegar is the traditional acid.
  • Beef takes the same method.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.
