Goan Pork Chilli Fry

Goan pork chilli fry, a spicy dry-fried pork dish with vinegar and Goan red chilies, reflects the state's Portuguese-influenced Catholic cooking.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 40 min
  • Difficulty Medium
  • Draft India Pork LunchDinnerDeep-friedSpicy

  • 600 g1.3 lbPork shoulder, cubed
  • 20 g3/4 ozGoan red chilies
  • 45 ml3 tbspVinegar
  • 250 g9 ozOnion and tomato
  • 50 g2 ozGarlic and ginger
  • 8 g3 tspCumin and turmeric
  • 6 g1 tspSalt
  • 45 ml3 tbspOil

Method

  1. Soak the dried chilies in hot water for 15 minutes and grind with the vinegar, garlic, ginger, cumin and turmeric to a paste.
  2. Fry the onion in the oil until golden, 8 minutes; add the paste and fry 5 minutes.
  3. Add the pork, tomato and salt and cook 5 minutes, then add 200 ml water, cover and simmer 35 minutes until tender.
  4. Uncover and dry-fry until the oil separates and the masala clings, 8-10 minutes.
  5. Serve with rice or pao.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the paste makes it bitter.
  • Not reducing leaves a watery curry.

Authentic tips

  • Goan vinegar (palm toddy vinegar) is traditional.
  • Better the next day.

Substitutions

  • Coconut vinegar is the traditional acid.
  • Beef takes the same method.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pao or steamed rice
  • Soup: Kachumber salad
  • Dessert: Bebinca
  • Beverage: Cold beer
