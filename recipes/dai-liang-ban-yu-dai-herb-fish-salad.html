Dǎi Liáng Bàn Yú (Dai Herb Fish Salad) 傣味凉拌鱼

▾

Surprise me with

Cold fish salads with lime, mint, cilantro, chili and lemongrass are typical of Dai cooking in Xishuangbanna, which shares flavors with neighboring Laos and Thailand.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 600 g1.3 lbSteamed fish, flaked
  • 60 ml4 tbspLime juice
  • 40 g1.5 ozMint and cilantro
  • 30 g1 ozLemongrass, minced
  • 20 g2/3 ozFresh chilies
  • 40 g1.5 ozShallot
  • 15 ml1 tbspFish sauce
  • 30 g1 ozPeanuts, crushed

Method

  1. Steam the fish for 10 minutes until just cooked; cool and flake into chunks.
  2. Mix the lime juice, fish sauce, lemongrass, chilies and shallot.
  3. Toss gently with the fish so it does not break up.
  4. Add the herbs.
  5. Top with crushed peanuts and serve at room temperature.

Common mistakes

  • Over-mixing mashes the fish.
  • Dressing too early turns the herbs limp.

Authentic tips

  • Use a firm white fish.
  • Serve with sticky rice.

Substitutions

  • Poached white fish works.
  • Crushed toasted rice powder adds a Lao-style note.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sticky rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh pineapple
  • Beverage: Chilled tea
