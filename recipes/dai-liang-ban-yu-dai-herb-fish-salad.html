Dǎi Liáng Bàn Yú (Dai Herb Fish Salad) 傣味凉拌鱼

▾

Surprise me with

Cold fish salads with lime, mint, cilantro, chili and lemongrass are typical of Dai cooking in Xishuangbanna, which shares flavors with neighboring Laos and Thailand.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Ingredients

  • 600 g1.3 lbWhite fish fillets
  • 15 g1/2 ozGinger, sliced, for steaming
  • 60 ml1/4 cupLime juice
  • 15 ml1 tbspFish sauce
  • 2 g1/3 tspSalt
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 30 g1 ozLemongrass, very finely minced
  • 20 g3/4 ozBird's eye chilies, minced
  • 40 g1.5 ozShallots, thinly sliced
  • 40 g1.5 ozMint, cilantro and sawtooth coriander
  • 30 g1/4 cupRoasted peanuts, crushed

Method

  1. Steam the fish with the ginger over boiling water for 8 minutes until just cooked. Cool and flake it into large pieces, removing any bones.
  2. Mix the lime juice, fish sauce, salt and sugar until dissolved, then stir in the lemongrass and chilies.
  3. Gently toss the fish with the shallots and dressing.
  4. Fold in the herbs.
  5. Top with peanuts and serve at room temperature with sticky rice.

Common mistakes

  • Over-mixing mashes the fish.
  • Dressing too early turns the herbs limp.

Authentic tips

  • Use a firm white fish.
  • Serve with sticky rice.

Substitutions

  • Poached white fish works.
  • Crushed toasted rice powder adds a Lao-style note.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sticky rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh pineapple
  • Beverage: Chilled tea
