Hyderabadi Chicken Biryani

▾

Surprise me with

Hyderabadi biryani, layered rice and marinated chicken sealed and cooked on dum, was developed in the kitchens of the Nizams of Hyderabad.

Method

  1. Fry the onions in the oil over medium heat for 20 to 25 minutes until deep golden and crisp, then drain. Mix the chicken with the yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, chili, turmeric, biryani masala, green chilies, lemon juice, marinade salt, half the fried onions, half the herbs and 2 tablespoons of the onion oil. Marinate for at least 4 hours.
  2. Spread the marinated raw chicken in an even layer in a heavy, wide Dutch oven. This is kacchi (raw) biryani, the Hyderabadi style, in which the chicken cooks in the steam of the rice.
  3. Bring 3 L (12 cups) of water to a rolling boil with the whole spices and rice salt; it should taste like seawater. Add the drained rice and boil for 4 to 5 minutes, until it is about 70% cooked and breaks with a hard center.
  4. Drain the rice and spread it evenly over the chicken. Top with the remaining fried onions and herbs, drizzle with the saffron milk and ghee.
  5. Seal the lid with foil or a rope of dough. Cook over medium-high heat for 5 minutes until you hear steam, then lower to the lowest heat, ideally on a heat diffuser or a cast-iron pan, and cook for 35 to 40 minutes (dum).
  6. Rest for 10 minutes before opening, then serve by scooping down through all the layers, with raita and mirchi ka salan.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooked parboiled rice turns mushy.
  • A loose seal lets the steam escape.

Authentic tips

  • Bone-in chicken gives the best flavor.
  • Use a heavy pot.

Substitutions

  • Bone-in thighs and drumsticks give the best flavor.
  • A pressure cooker cuts the dum to 20 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat with a splash of water, covered.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Raita and mirchi ka salan
  • Soup: Cucumber salad
  • Dessert: Double ka meetha
  • Beverage: Masala chai
