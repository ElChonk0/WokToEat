Hyderabadi Chicken Biryani

Hyderabadi biryani, layered rice and marinated chicken sealed and cooked on dum, was developed in the kitchens of the Nizams of Hyderabad.

  • Prep 40 min (plus 2 hr marinating)
  • Cook 60 min
  • Difficulty Hard

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken with the yogurt, ginger-garlic paste, spices, half the fried onions and half the herbs for at least 2 hours.
  2. Parboil the rice in salted water until 70 percent cooked, about 6 minutes; drain.
  3. Layer the chicken in a heavy pot, then the rice, then the remaining onions, herbs, saffron milk and ghee.
  4. Seal the lid with dough or foil and cook on very low heat (dum) for 40 minutes.
  5. Rest 10 minutes, then gently mix from the bottom and serve with raita.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooked parboiled rice turns mushy.
  • A loose seal lets the steam escape.

Authentic tips

  • Bone-in chicken gives the best flavor.
  • Use a heavy pot.

Substitutions

  • Bone-in thighs and drumsticks give the best flavor.
  • A pressure cooker cuts the dum to 20 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat with a splash of water, covered.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Raita and mirchi ka salan
  • Soup: Cucumber salad
  • Dessert: Double ka meetha
  • Beverage: Masala chai
