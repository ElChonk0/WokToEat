Cù Liū Tǔ Dòu Sī (Dongbei Vinegar Potato Strips) 醋溜土豆丝

▾

Surprise me with

Vinegar-glazed potato strips are one of the most common home dishes in northern and northeastern China, a quick and inexpensive way to enjoy the crisp texture of potatoes.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 6 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

  • 600 g1.3 lbPotatoes, julienned
  • 30 ml2 tbspChinkiang vinegar
  • 5 g1/6 ozDried chilies
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Cut the potatoes into fine matchsticks and rinse in cold water until it runs clear; soak 10 minutes and drain well.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok over high heat and fry the chilies and garlic for 15 seconds.
  3. Add the potato and stir-fry 3 minutes until translucent but crisp.
  4. Add the vinegar and salt, toss 20 seconds and serve.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the rinse leaves the starch on, so the strips stick.
  • Overcooking turns the potato soft.

Authentic tips

  • Add the vinegar at the end for the best aroma.
  • Cut the strips as evenly as possible.

Substitutions

  • Rice vinegar replaces Chinkiang for a lighter flavor.
  • Green pepper adds color.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or buns
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
