Hoedeopbap (Raw Fish Rice Bowl) 회덮밥

▾

Hoedeopbap is Korea's raw fish rice bowl, topped with sashimi, vegetables and spicy chogochujang sauce.

Method

  1. Make the chojang (vinegared gochujang): stir the gochujang, vinegar, sugar, lemon juice and garlic until smooth and glossy. Chill it; the sweet-tangy-spicy sauce is what ties the bowl together.
  2. Keep the fish very cold until the last moment. Slice it across the grain into thin bite-size pieces with a sharp knife, wiping the blade between cuts.
  3. Shred the lettuce and cut the cucumber, carrot and perilla leaves into fine strips. Chill them so they are crisp.
  4. Put the rice in wide bowls; it should be room temperature, not hot, or it will start to cook the fish. Pile the vegetables over it and arrange the fish on top.
  5. Drizzle with sesame oil, scatter with sesame seeds and seaweed and serve with the chojang. Each person adds sauce to taste and mixes everything together.

Common mistakes

  • Cutting the fish thick makes it chewy.
  • Warm rice cooks the fish; use cool rice.

Authentic tips

  • Use only sashimi-grade fish.
  • Add roe for a premium bowl.

Substitutions

  • Smoked salmon replaces raw fish.
  • Doenjang-based ssamjang is an alternative to gochujang.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Miso or maeuntang on the side
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
