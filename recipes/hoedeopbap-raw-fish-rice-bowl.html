Hoedeopbap (Raw Fish Rice Bowl) 회덮밥

Hoedeopbap is Korea's raw fish rice bowl, topped with sashimi, vegetables and spicy chogochujang sauce.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 5 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Korea Seafood LunchDinnerRiceSpicy30 min or less

  • 400 g14 ozSashimi-grade fish
  • 600 g21 ozCooked rice
  • 200 g7 ozMixed lettuce and cucumber
  • 20 g2/3 ozPerilla leaves
  • 45 g3 tbspGochujang
  • 30 ml2 tbspRice vinegar
  • 10 ml2 tspSesame oil
  • 10 g1 tbspSesame seeds

Method

  1. Mix the gochujang, vinegar, sugar and sesame oil.
  2. Spread the rice in bowls.
  3. Arrange the lettuce, cucumber and perilla over the rice.
  4. Slice the fish and place on top.
  5. Spoon over the sauce, sprinkle with sesame and mix before eating.

Common mistakes

  • Cutting the fish thick makes it chewy.
  • Warm rice cooks the fish; use cool rice.

Authentic tips

  • Use only sashimi-grade fish.
  • Add roe for a premium bowl.

Substitutions

  • Smoked salmon replaces raw fish.
  • Doenjang-based ssamjang is an alternative to gochujang.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Miso or maeuntang on the side
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
