Nakji Bokkeum (Spicy Stir-Fried Octopus) 낙지볶음

▾

Nakji bokkeum is a fiery stir-fry of small octopus, famous in Seoul's Mugyo-dong, and traditionally served with rice and sprouts.

Method

  1. Rub the octopus with the coarse salt or flour for 2 to 3 minutes to clean off the slime, then rinse it thoroughly and cut it into 5 cm (2 in) pieces. Mix the gochujang, gochugaru, soy sauce, sugar, syrup, garlic and ginger and toss the octopus in half of it.
  2. Cut the vegetables into thin pieces about 5 cm (2 in) long, so they cook as fast as the octopus.
  3. Heat a large skillet or flat-bottom wok over high heat with the oil and stir-fry the onion, carrot and cabbage for about 2 minutes until they start to wilt.
  4. Push the vegetables aside, add the octopus and the remaining sauce to the center and stir-fry for 2 to 3 minutes, until the tentacles curl and turn opaque. Octopus turns rubbery if overcooked, so work fast over high heat.
  5. Add the green onion, toss everything together for 30 seconds, drizzle with sesame oil and seeds and serve at once with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking makes the octopus tough.
  • Not cleaning leaves grit.

Authentic tips

  • Mugyo-dong in Seoul is famous for this dish.
  • Serve with somen noodles.

Substitutions

  • Baby squid replaces octopus.
  • Somen noodles added at the end are traditional.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and bean sprouts, or somen noodles
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
