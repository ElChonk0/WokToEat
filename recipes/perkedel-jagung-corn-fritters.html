Perkedel Jagung (Corn Fritters)

▾

Surprise me with

Perkedel, fried patties, are a Dutch-influenced Indonesian staple; corn fritters are a popular side or snack.

  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Pound the garlic, shallots, candlenut and coriander into a paste.
  2. Pound or pulse half the corn roughly so it releases its milk, then mix it with the whole kernels. The crushed corn binds the fritters.
  3. Mix the corn, paste, flours, eggs, celery, green onion, salt and pepper into a thick, spoonable batter.
  4. Heat 1 cm (1/2 in) of oil in a skillet over medium heat to about 170 C (340 F). Drop in heaped tablespoons of batter, flattening them slightly, and fry for 2 to 3 minutes per side until golden and crisp.
  5. Drain on a rack and serve hot with sambal.

Common mistakes

  • Too wet a batter spreads and absorbs oil.
  • Oil too hot browns before cooking through.

Authentic tips

  • Fry in small batches.
  • Serve as a snack.

Substitutions

  • Frozen corn replaces fresh.
  • Air-fry at 200 C (400 F) for a lighter version.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; recrisp in a hot oven.

Freezing: Freeze cooked fritters for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sambal
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Es teh
