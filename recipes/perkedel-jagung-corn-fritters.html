Perkedel Jagung (Corn Fritters)

▾

Surprise me with

Perkedel, fried patties, are a Dutch-influenced Indonesian staple; corn fritters are a popular side or snack.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 400 g14 ozSweet corn kernels
  • 60 g1/2 cupFlour
  • 22Eggs
  • 60 g2 ozCelery and scallion
  • 15 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 5 g1 tspSalt and white pepper
  • 200 ml3/4 cupNeutral oil for frying

Method

  1. Mix the corn, flour, eggs, celery, scallion, garlic and seasonings into a thick batter.
  2. Heat the oil to 170 C (340 F).
  3. Spoon in tablespoon-size portions and fry 2 minutes per side until golden.
  4. Drain on paper towels.
  5. Serve hot with chili sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Too wet a batter spreads and absorbs oil.
  • Oil too hot browns before cooking through.

Authentic tips

  • Fry in small batches.
  • Serve as a snack.

Substitutions

  • Frozen corn replaces fresh.
  • Air-fry at 200 C (400 F) for a lighter version.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; recrisp in a hot oven.

Freezing: Freeze cooked fritters for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sambal
