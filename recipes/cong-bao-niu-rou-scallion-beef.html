Cōng Bào Niú Ròu (Scallion Flash-Fried Beef) 葱爆牛肉

▾

Surprise me with

Cong bao niu rou, beef flash-fried with a heap of scallions, is a Beijing classic and one of the capital's best-known beef dishes.

  • Cook 6 min

  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Toss the beef with the marinade soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, cornstarch and a tablespoon of water and leave for 15 minutes. Cut the scallions on an angle, whites and greens separate.
  2. Mix the sauce soy sauce, vinegar, sugar and sesame oil in a bowl.
  3. Heat the oil in a flat-bottom wok over the highest heat until smoking. Add the beef and toss for 1 minute until it just changes color. Cong bao means 'scallion explode-fried': fierce heat and speed.
  4. Add the garlic and scallion whites and toss for 20 seconds, then the greens.
  5. Pour the sauce around the edge of the wok and toss for 15 to 20 seconds, until the scallions just wilt and everything is glossy. Serve at once; it is done in under 3 minutes from start to finish.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking in a crowded or cool wok steams the beef.
  • Overcooking makes it chewy; it should stay slightly pink in the center.

Authentic tips

  • Large scallions give the sweetest flavor.
  • Serve over rice or with steamed buns.

Substitutions

  • Lamb is the traditional alternative.
  • Chicken thigh takes the same method.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the dish is meant to be eaten at once.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
