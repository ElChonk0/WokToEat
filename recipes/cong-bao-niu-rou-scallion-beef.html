Cōng Bào Niú Ròu (Scallion Flash-Fried Beef) 葱爆牛肉

▾

Surprise me with

Cong bao niu rou, beef flash-fried with a heap of scallions, is a Beijing classic and one of the capital's best-known beef dishes.

  • Cook 6 min

  • 500 g1.1 lbBeef sirloin, sliced
  • 200 g7 ozScallions, in lengths
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 15 g1 tbspCornstarch
  • 5 ml1 tspSesame oil
  • 60 ml4 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Slice the beef 4 mm thick across the grain and toss with half the soy sauce and the cornstarch and 1 tablespoon of water; leave 15 minutes.
  2. Heat a wok until smoking, add 2 tablespoons of the oil and sear the beef for 45 seconds, stirring; remove.
  3. Add the remaining oil and the scallions and fry 30 seconds over high heat until charred at the edges.
  4. Return the beef with the wine and remaining soy sauce and toss 30 seconds.
  5. Finish with the sesame oil and serve immediately.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking in a crowded or cool wok steams the beef.
  • Overcooking makes it chewy; it should stay slightly pink in the center.

Authentic tips

  • Large scallions give the sweetest flavor.
  • Serve over rice or with steamed buns.

Substitutions

  • Lamb is the traditional alternative.
  • Chicken thigh takes the same method.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the dish is meant to be eaten at once.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
