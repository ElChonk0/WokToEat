Kare Udon (Curry Udon) カレーうどん

▾

Surprise me with

Curry udon, udon in a dashi broth thickened with Japanese curry, appeared in Tokyo noodle shops around 1910 and is a favorite way to use leftover curry.

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a pot and fry the pork and onion 4 minutes.
  2. Add the dashi, soy sauce and mirin and simmer 5 minutes.
  3. Turn off the heat, dissolve the curry roux and simmer 3 minutes, stirring; thicken with the potato starch mixed with water if you like it thicker.
  4. Cook the udon according to the packet and drain.
  5. Divide into bowls, ladle the curry broth over and top with green onion.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Adding roux to boiling broth makes lumps; dissolve it off the heat.
  • Wearing a white shirt; it splashes.

Authentic tips

  • Leftover Japanese curry thinned with dashi makes it in minutes.
  • Top with a fried tofu pouch (aburaage).

Substitutions

  • Chicken or beef replace pork.
  • Use hot curry roux for more heat.

Storage

Leftovers: Store broth separately for 3 days.

Freezing: The curry broth freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled vegetables
  • Soup: This is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold barley tea
