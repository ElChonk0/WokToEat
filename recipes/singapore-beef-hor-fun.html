Singapore Beef Hor Fun

▾

Surprise me with

A wok-fried noodle dish combining the smoky char of dry-fried noodles with a glossy, egg-thickened gravy poured over top, beef hor fun is a hawker-stall favorite in Singapore and Malaysia.

Method

  1. Marinate beef in a little soy sauce and cornstarch for 15 minutes.
  2. Stir-fry the rice noodles in a very hot wok with garlic and a splash of dark soy sauce until lightly charred; divide among plates.
  3. In the same wok, stir-fry beef over high heat until just browned; remove and set aside.
  4. Add stock and oyster sauce, bringing to a simmer, then stir in cornstarch slurry to thicken.
  5. Add choy sum and bean sprouts, simmering 1–2 minutes, then stir in beaten egg in a thin stream until just set.
  6. Return beef to the gravy, then pour everything over the charred noodles to serve.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the beef before adding it back to the gravy, which toughens it further as the gravy simmers.
  • Not achieving enough char on the noodles first, which misses the smoky wok-hei contrast that defines this dish.

Authentic tips

  • The contrast between smoky, charred noodles and silky egg gravy is the whole point of this dish — don't skip the high-heat noodle stir-fry.
  • Stir the egg in slowly, in a thin stream, for delicate ribbons rather than large clumps.

Substitutions

  • Chicken replaces beef.
  • Dried rice noodles, soaked, replace fresh.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated; best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.
