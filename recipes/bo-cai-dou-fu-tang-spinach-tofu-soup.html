Bō Cài Dòu Fu Tāng (Spinach and Tofu Soup) 菠菜豆腐汤

▾

Spinach and tofu soup is a gentle, everyday Chinese home soup served alongside rice dishes.

Ingredients

  • 400 g14 ozSpinach, stems and leaves separated
  • 400 g14 ozMedium-firm or soft tofu, cut into 2.5 cm (1 in) cubes
  • 80 g3 ozCarrot, thinly sliced on an angle
  • 1.2 L5 cupsVegetable stock
  • 5 ml1 tspNeutral oil
  • 10 g1/3 ozGinger, julienned
  • 44Dried shiitake mushrooms, soaked and sliced
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt
  • 5 ml1 tspLight soy sauce
  • 1 g1/2 tspGround white pepper
  • 5 ml1 tspSesame oil

Method

  1. Wash the spinach well and separate the stems from the leaves, so the stems can go in first. Cube the tofu and slice the carrot.
  2. Bring the stock to a boil in a covered pot over high heat. Uncover and add the oil, ginger, mushrooms, tofu and carrot and return to a boil, then simmer for 3 minutes so the mushrooms flavor the broth.
  3. Season with the salt and soy sauce, then add the spinach stems. After about 10 seconds add the leaves, pushing them under the broth, and cover for 1 minute, just until they wilt and turn bright green.
  4. Turn off the heat, add the white pepper and sesame oil and serve straight from the pot. Use medium-firm tofu if you want the cubes to hold their shape; soft tofu is silkier but more fragile.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the spinach turns it dark and bitter.
  • Stirring hard breaks the tofu.

Authentic tips

  • Blanch older spinach for 20 seconds first to remove bitterness.
  • Goji berries add color and a little sweetness.

Substitutions

  • Chicken stock if it does not need to be vegetarian.
  • Watercress or pea shoots replace spinach.

Storage

Leftovers: Best eaten the same day; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and steamed chicken
  • Soup: This is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh orange
  • Beverage: Hot tea
