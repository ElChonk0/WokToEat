Yang Yu Men Rou (Yunnan Pork and Potato Stew) 洋芋焖肉

Potatoes and pork stewed together are a mountain staple of Yunnan, where potatoes (yangyu) are central to the diet.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 50 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Yunnan Pork LunchDinnerBraise / stewSpicy

  • 400 g14 ozPork belly or shoulder, cubed
  • 500 g1.1 lbPotatoes
  • 10 g1/3 ozDried chilies
  • 40 g3 tbspGarlic and ginger
  • 45 ml3 tbspSoy sauce
  • 500 ml2 cupsWater
  • 15 ml1 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Brown the pork in the oil in a heavy pot over medium-high heat for 8 minutes.
  2. Add the garlic, ginger and chilies and stir 1 minute.
  3. Add the soy sauce and water, bring to a boil, cover and simmer 25 minutes.
  4. Add the potato chunks and cook 20 minutes more until tender.
  5. Uncover and boil 3-5 minutes to thicken; serve from the pot.

Common mistakes

  • Cutting the potatoes too small makes them dissolve.
  • Using only belly makes the stew greasy; mix it with shoulder.

Authentic tips

  • Skim the fat before serving if you prefer a lighter stew.
  • Waxy potatoes hold their shape best.

Substitutions

  • Waxy potatoes hold their shape best.
  • Pork shoulder gives a leaner stew.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months without the potatoes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
