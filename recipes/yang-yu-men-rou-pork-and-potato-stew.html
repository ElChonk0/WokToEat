Yáng Yù Mèn Ròu (Yunnan Pork and Potato Stew) 洋芋焖肉

▾

Surprise me with

Potatoes and pork stewed together are a mountain staple of Yunnan, where potatoes (yangyu) are central to the diet.

  • Cook 1.5 hr

Method

  1. Blanch the pork for 3 to 5 minutes and rinse.
  2. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven over medium heat and brown the pork for 5 minutes with the ginger, garlic and dried chilies.
  3. Add the soy sauce and water, bring to a boil, cover and simmer over low heat.
  4. After 40 minutes, add the potatoes and cook for 20 to 25 minutes more, until soft; some potato breaks down and thickens the sauce. Yunnan's mountain potatoes are prized for their dense, buttery flesh.
  5. Uncover, reduce for a few minutes, season with salt and scatter with cilantro.

Common mistakes

  • Cutting the potatoes too small makes them dissolve.
  • Using only belly makes the stew greasy; mix it with shoulder.

Authentic tips

  • Skim the fat before serving if you prefer a lighter stew.
  • Waxy potatoes hold their shape best.

Substitutions

  • Waxy potatoes hold their shape best.
  • Pork shoulder gives a leaner stew.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months without the potatoes.

  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
