Ikan Masak Lemak Cili Padi (Fish in Coconut and Bird's Eye Chili)

▾

Surprise me with

Masak lemak cili padi, a turmeric-yellow coconut gravy with a heavy dose of bird's eye chilies, is a dish of Negeri Sembilan and Malay home cooking, cooked with fish, beef or vegetables.

Method

  1. Rub the fish with the salt. Pound or blend the shallots, garlic, ginger, turmeric and bird's eye chilies into a coarse paste.
  2. Heat the oil in a wide pan over medium heat and fry the paste with the lemongrass for 3 to 4 minutes until fragrant, but not dark; masak lemak is pale and golden, unlike a fried sambal.
  3. Add the water and tamarind and bring to a simmer, then stir in the coconut milk and lime leaves. Bring back to a gentle simmer, stirring so the coconut milk does not split.
  4. Lay in the fish and simmer gently for 6 to 8 minutes, spooning the gravy over and shaking the pan rather than stirring, until the fish is opaque and flakes easily.
  5. Season with the gravy salt; it should be creamy, rich and fiery from the bird's eye chilies. Serve at once with rice.

Cook’s notes

Written for haddock fillet.

Common mistakes

  • A hard boil splits the coconut milk and breaks the fish.
  • Stirring flakes the haddock; swirl the pan instead.

Authentic tips

  • The gravy should be fiery as well as rich; add more chilies if you like.
  • Tamarind adds a light sourness that balances the coconut.

Substitutions

  • Cod loin replaces the haddock.
  • Fresh young jackfruit or eggplant can be added.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
