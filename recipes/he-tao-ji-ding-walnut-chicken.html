Hé Táo Jī Dīng (Walnut Chicken) 核桃鸡丁

▾

Surprise me with

Walnut chicken, diced chicken with caramelized walnuts in a savory sauce, is a northern Chinese banquet dish that reflects the region's walnut growing.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

  • 500 g1.1 lbChicken thighs, diced
  • 80 g3 ozWalnut halves, toasted
  • 30 g2 tbspSweet bean sauce
  • 40 g3 tbspScallion and ginger
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 g1 tbspCornstarch
  • 10 g2 tspSugar
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Toss the diced chicken with half the soy sauce and the cornstarch and 1 tablespoon of water; leave 10 minutes. Toast the walnuts in a dry pan for 5 minutes until fragrant.
  2. Heat half the oil in a wok over high heat and stir-fry the chicken 2-3 minutes until golden; remove.
  3. Add the remaining oil, scallion and ginger and fry 15 seconds, then the sweet bean sauce and sugar for 30 seconds until bubbling.
  4. Return the chicken and the remaining soy sauce and toss 1 minute until glazed and cooked through.
  5. Fold in the walnuts off the heat and serve immediately.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the nuts early makes them soggy.
  • Burning the sweet bean sauce makes it bitter; keep it moving.

Authentic tips

  • Blanch raw walnuts 2 minutes first if you find them bitter.
  • Cashews or pecans work if walnuts are unavailable.

Substitutions

  • Cashews or almonds replace walnuts; walnuts should be blanched to remove bitterness.
  • Chicken breast works if velveted with cornstarch.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; the nuts soften.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
