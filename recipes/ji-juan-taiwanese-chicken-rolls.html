Ji Juan (Tainan Chicken Rolls) 雞捲

Ji juan, a roll of minced meat and vegetables wrapped in tofu skin and fried, is a Tainan banquet and street snack, often served with sweet chili sauce.

  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Method

  1. Mix the chicken, finely diced water chestnuts and carrot, scallion, ginger and soy sauce until sticky.
  2. Wipe the tofu skin sheets with a damp cloth to soften and cut into rectangles.
  3. Place a line of filling on each sheet, roll up tightly, folding in the sides, and seal with the egg white.
  4. Fry at 170 C (340 F) for 5-6 minutes until golden and cooked through.
  5. Drain, slice on the diagonal and serve with sweet chili sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Loose rolls open in the oil.
  • Oil that is too hot browns the skin before the filling cooks.

Authentic tips

  • Steam the rolls first for 10 minutes for a more tender filling.
  • Sweet chili and pickled radish are the classic dips.

Substitutions

  • Spring roll wrappers replace tofu skin.
  • Steam first, then pan-fry, for a lighter roll.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; recrisp in a hot oven.

Freezing: Freeze uncooked rolls for 2 months; fry from frozen.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Oolong tea
