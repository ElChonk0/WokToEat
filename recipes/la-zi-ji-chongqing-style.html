La Zi Ji — Chongqing-Style 辣子鸡

▾

Surprise me with

A fiery Chongqing specialty where fried chicken is nearly buried under a mountain of dried chilies and toasted Sichuan peppercorns — eaten by digging through the chilies to find the chicken, prized as much for aroma as heat.

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken with soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, salt and grated ginger for 20 minutes, then toss in the cornstarch until coated.
  2. Heat the oil in a flat-bottom wok or Dutch oven to 170 C (340 F) and fry the chicken in batches for 4 to 5 minutes until golden. Drain.
  3. Raise the oil to 190 C (375 F) and fry all the chicken again for 1 to 2 minutes until deep golden and very crisp. The double fry dries the surface so it stays crunchy among the chilies.
  4. Pour off all but 3 tablespoons of oil. Over low heat, fry the garlic, sliced ginger and peppercorns for 30 seconds, then add the chilies and stir for 30 to 60 seconds until they darken and smell toasty.
  5. Return the chicken with the green onion and sugar and toss over high heat for 1 minute until coated.
  6. Sprinkle with sesame seeds and serve piled in the chilies, the Chongqing way. Pick out the chicken and leave the chilies.

Common mistakes

  • Frying oil too hot initially, which burns the coating before the chicken cooks through.
  • Burning the chilies during the toasting step, which turns the whole dish acrid instead of aromatic.

Authentic tips

  • The double-fry technique (fry, rest, fry again) is what gives la zi ji its signature shattering crunch.
  • This dish is meant to be eaten by picking through the chilies for chicken pieces — the chilies themselves are for aroma, not necessarily eating whole.

Substitutions

  • Chicken wings are a very traditional alternative cut for this dish.
  • Reduce dried chilies to about half for a considerably milder version.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 2 days refrigerated but loses its crispness.

Freezing: Not recommended — fried texture doesn't survive freezing.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Cold cucumber salad to cool the palate
  • Soup: Light clear soup
  • Dessert: Chilled watermelon
  • Beverage: Cold lager
