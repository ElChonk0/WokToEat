Masala Dosa (Potato-Filled Rice Crepe)

Masala dosa, a crisp fermented rice-lentil crepe filled with spiced potato, comes from Karnataka and is South India's best-known breakfast, served with sambar and chutney.

  • 400 g14 ozDosa batter (rice and urad dal)
  • 400 g14 ozPotatoes, boiled
  • 150 g5 ozOnion
  • 6 g2 tspMustard seeds and curry leaves
  • 10 g1/3 ozTurmeric and green chilies
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 120 ml1/2 cupCoconut chutney
  • 250 ml1 cupSambar

Method

  1. Fry the mustard seeds and curry leaves in 1 tablespoon of the oil until they pop, then add the onion and chilies and cook 5 minutes.
  2. Add the turmeric and potatoes, mash lightly and cook 3 minutes.
  3. Heat a griddle, rub with oil and pour on a ladle of batter, spreading it thin in circles.
  4. Drizzle with oil and cook 2-3 minutes until crisp and golden.
  5. Fill with the potato, fold and serve with chutney and sambar.

Common mistakes

  • A cool griddle makes the dosa stick.
  • Thick batter doesn't crisp.

Authentic tips

  • Ferment the batter 8-12 hours.
  • Wipe the pan with a cut onion between dosas.

Substitutions

  • Store-bought dosa batter saves 8 hours.
  • Cook on a cast-iron skillet.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the potato filling keeps 3 days.

Freezing: The filling freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sambar and coconut chutney
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Filter coffee
