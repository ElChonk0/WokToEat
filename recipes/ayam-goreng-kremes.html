Ayam Goreng Kremes

▾

Surprise me with

A beloved Indonesian fried chicken topped with kremesan — crispy shards of fried batter made from the leftover marinating liquid — giving the dish an extra layer of crunch alongside the chicken itself.

Method

  1. Blend the shallots, garlic, turmeric, ginger, galangal, candlenuts and coriander into a paste.
  2. Put the chicken, paste, lemongrass, bay leaves, salt and coconut milk in a pot and simmer, covered, for 30 minutes, until the chicken is cooked and has absorbed the spices. Lift out the chicken and keep 250 ml (1 cup) of the strained broth.
  3. Make the kremes batter: whisk the cooled broth with the rice flour, tapioca starch and egg yolk until thin and runny, like milk.
  4. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven to 180 C (350 F) and fry the chicken for 4 to 5 minutes until golden and crisp. Drain.
  5. Drizzle the batter into the hot oil from a height with your fingers or a spoon, in a circle, so it spreads into a lacy net. Fry for 2 to 3 minutes until golden, then gather it into a clump with a slotted spoon and drain.
  6. Pile the crisp kremes over the chicken and serve with rice and sambal.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the simmering step and frying raw chicken directly, which risks an undercooked interior with a burnt exterior.
  • Making the kremesan batter too thick, which produces clumps rather than delicate, lacy shards.

Authentic tips

  • Simmering the chicken in coconut milk first ensures it's fully seasoned and tender before frying, which only needs to crisp the surface.
  • Drizzle the kremesan batter from height in a thin, steady stream for the best lacy texture.

Substitutions

  • Chicken drumsticks work well with the same method.
  • No rice flour: all-purpose flour works for the kremesan, though texture is slightly less crisp.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; the kremesan loses crispness on reheating but the chicken remains good.

Freezing: Freezes acceptably; add fresh kremesan when serving.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice, sambal terasi and fresh lalapan
  • Soup: Sayur asem
  • Dessert: Pisang goreng
  • Beverage: Es teh manis
