Fēng Mì Huǒ Tuǐ (Honey-Glazed Yunnan Ham) 蜜汁火腿

▾

Surprise me with

Honey ham, dry-cured ham steamed with honey and rock sugar, is a Yunnan banquet dish, balancing the ham's salt with sweetness.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 90 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 400 g14 ozYunnan or country ham, thick slices
  • 90 g6 tbspHoney
  • 30 g2 tbspRock sugar
  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 20 g1 tbspGinger
  • 44Bread or steamed buns

Method

  1. Soak the ham slices in warm water for 30 minutes to remove excess salt; drain and pat dry.
  2. Arrange them in overlapping slices on a heatproof plate and top with the ginger, honey, rock sugar and wine.
  3. Steam over high heat for 80 minutes until the fat is translucent and the honey has melted into a syrup.
  4. Skim off any liquid and spoon the syrup over the ham.
  5. Serve in thin slices sandwiched in warm steamed buns.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the soak makes the ham unpleasantly salty.
  • Steaming too hard dries the ham.

Authentic tips

  • Prosciutto-style cured ham can stand in if you shorten the steaming to 30 minutes.
  • The buns balance the salt and sweet.

Substitutions

  • Thick prosciutto, steamed 30 minutes, replaces Yunnan ham.
  • Steamed buns are traditional with it; plain bread works.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; serve cold or re-steam.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed buns (mantou)
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Pu'er tea
