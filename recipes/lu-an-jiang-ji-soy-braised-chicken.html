Lù Ān Jiàng Jī (Lu'an Soy-Braised Chicken) 六安酱鸡

Lu'an, in western Anhui, is known for Lu'an Guapian tea and for sauce-braised poultry in the Hui style.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 40 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Anhui Chicken LunchDinnerBraise / stew

  • 1 kg2.2 lbChicken legs
  • 60 ml4 tbspSoy sauce
  • 20 g4 tspSugar
  • 8 g3 tspStar anise and cinnamon
  • 50 g4 tbspGinger and scallion
  • 45 ml3 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 500 ml2 cupsWater

Method

  1. Cover the chicken with cold water, bring to a boil for 3 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  2. In a pot just large enough, bring 500 ml water, the soy sauce, sugar, wine, ginger, scallion and spices to a boil and simmer 10 minutes.
  3. Add the chicken, cover and simmer 30 minutes, turning once.
  4. Turn off the heat and steep 10 minutes.
  5. Lift out, reduce the sauce over high heat for 5 minutes and brush it over the chicken; chop.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard tightens the meat.
  • Skipping the steep leaves it less juicy.

Authentic tips

  • The sauce can be reused as a master stock.
  • Serve warm or cold.

Substitutions

  • Whole chicken legs, bone in, replace small chickens.
  • Reuse the sauce as a master stock.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated in the sauce.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot Lu'an Guapian tea
