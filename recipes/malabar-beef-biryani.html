Malabar Beef Biryani

▾

Surprise me with

Malabar biryani, the Kerala version with short-grain kaima rice and tender beef cooked on dum, is a signature of Kozhikode's Mappila Muslim cooking.

Method

  1. Cook the beef with the ginger-garlic paste, spices, salt and a little water in a pressure cooker or covered pot until tender, about 45 minutes; reduce to a thick masala.
  2. Parboil the rice in salted water until 70 percent cooked; drain.
  3. Layer the masala and beef in a heavy pot with the rice, fried onions, ghee, yogurt and herbs.
  4. Seal and cook on dum over very low heat 25 minutes.
  5. Rest 10 minutes and mix gently.

Common mistakes

  • Mushy rice if boiled too long.
  • A poor seal lets the steam out.

Authentic tips

  • Kaima (jeerakasala) rice is the traditional grain.
  • Serve with raita and pickle.

Substitutions

  • Basmati replaces kaima rice.
  • A pressure cooker for the beef cuts 30 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat covered.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Raita and pickle
  • Soup: Cucumber salad
  • Dessert: Pudding
  • Beverage: Black tea
