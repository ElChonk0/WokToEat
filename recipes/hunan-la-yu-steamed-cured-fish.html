Zhēng Là Yú (Steamed Cured Fish) 蒸腊鱼

▾

Surprise me with

Winter-cured fish, air-dried with salt and spices, is a Hunan New Year specialty; steaming it with chilies and ginger turns the concentrated, savory flesh into a rich side dish.

Method

  1. Soak the cured fish in warm water for 30 minutes to soften it and draw out some salt, then scrub and drain. Hunan la yu is salted, air-dried and smoked over wood, so it needs little seasoning.
  2. Fry the black beans, ginger and chilies in the oil for 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Lay the fish in a heatproof bowl, top with the fried aromatics, and sprinkle with the Shaoxing wine and sugar.
  4. Steam over boiling water, covered, for 25 to 30 minutes, until the fish is tender and the oil and juices pool around it.
  5. Scatter with scallions and serve with rice; a small piece goes a long way.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the soak makes the fish far too salty.
  • Under-steaming leaves it leathery.

Authentic tips

  • Cured fish is intense; serve in small portions with rice.
  • Salt cod soaked overnight is a good substitute.

Substitutions

  • Salt cod soaked overnight replaces Hunan cured fish.
  • Add a few slices of pork belly for a richer dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes well for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
