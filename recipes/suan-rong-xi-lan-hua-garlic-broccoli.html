Suàn Róng Xī Lán Huā (Garlic Broccoli) 蒜蓉西兰花

▾

Surprise me with

Broccoli blanched then flash-fried with plenty of garlic is the standard Cantonese restaurant way of cooking green vegetables.

  • Cook 8 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 600 g1.3 lbBroccoli, cut into florets, stems peeled and sliced
  • 5 g1 tspSalt, for soaking
  • 5 g1 tspSalt and neutral oil, for blanching
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil
  • 5 cloves5 clovesGarlic, minced
  • 10 g1/3 ozGinger, minced
  • 5 ml1 tspShaoxing wine
  • 2 g1/2 tspSalt
  • 4 g1 tspSugar
  • 5 ml1 tspVegetarian oyster sauce (mushroom sauce)
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine, for the sauce
  • 6 g2 tspCornstarch
  • 30 ml2 tbspWater or vegetable stock

Method

  1. Soak the broccoli in cold salted water for 3 to 5 minutes to draw out any grit or insects, then drain. Cut the florets from the stem side so they break apart cleanly, and slice the peeled stems thinly. Mix the sauce salt, sugar, mushroom sauce, sauce wine, cornstarch and water.
  2. Bring a pot of water to a vigorous boil with the blanching salt and oil. Add the stems, then the florets, cover and cook for about 2 minutes, until bright green and just tender. Plunge into ice water for 1 minute and drain well; the cold stop keeps the color and crunch.
  3. Dry the wok, heat it over high heat, then lower to medium and add 1 tablespoon of oil. Fry the garlic for 15 to 20 seconds and the ginger for 20 seconds, until fragrant but not browned.
  4. Raise the heat to high, add the broccoli and toss for 40 to 50 seconds, then splash the teaspoon of wine around the edge.
  5. Stir the sauce and add it gradually, tossing for about 1 minute until it turns glossy and clings. Add the last tablespoon of oil, toss for 15 seconds and serve.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Burnt garlic turns bitter; keep the heat moderate for that step.
  • Stir-frying raw broccoli takes so long that the outside scorches.

Authentic tips

  • A little oil in the blanching water is the restaurant trick for glossy green broccoli.
  • Peel the stems; they are sweet and crisp.

Substitutions

  • Gai lan, choy sum or green beans in the same way.
  • Add a spoon of vegetarian oyster sauce for a richer sauce.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice with a main dish
  • Soup: Tomato tofu soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
