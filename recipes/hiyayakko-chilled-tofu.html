Hiyayakko (Chilled Tofu) 冷奴

Hiyayakko, cold tofu with toppings, is the simplest and most refreshing Japanese summer dish, served at home and in izakaya.

  • Cook 0 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Chill the tofu well.
  2. Drain and cut into 4 portions.
  3. Place each in a small dish.
  4. Top with the scallion, ginger and shiso.
  5. Drizzle with the soy sauce and sprinkle with sesame seeds.

Common mistakes

  • Watery tofu dilutes the soy sauce; drain it.
  • Warm tofu loses the refreshing quality.

Authentic tips

  • Use good soy sauce; there's nowhere to hide.
  • Add bonito flakes for extra flavor.

Substitutions

  • Add bonito flakes or sesame oil.
  • Soft tofu replaces silken.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day if kept cold and drained.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Miso soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold sencha
