Hui Zhou Dou Gan Chao Qin Cai (Huizhou Dried Tofu with Celery) 徽州豆干炒芹菜

Pressed dried tofu (dou gan) is a Huizhou staple, stir-fried with celery and chilies as a quick everyday dish.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 8 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Anhui Tofu & Vegetarian LunchDinnerStir-frySpicyVegetarianVegan30 min or less
250 kcal14 g protein10 g carbs17 g fat300 mg sodiumper serving

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

Both unit systems are shown; the toggle picks which one leads.

  • 300 g10 ozPressed tofu, sliced
  • 300 g10 ozCelery, sliced
  • 5 g1/6 ozDried chilies
  • 15 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 3 g1/2 tspSugar
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Cut the pressed tofu into thin strips and the celery on the diagonal into 4 cm lengths.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok over high heat and fry the dried chilies and garlic for 15 seconds.
  3. Add the tofu and fry 2 minutes until lightly browned.
  4. Add the celery, soy sauce and sugar and toss 2-3 minutes until crisp-tender.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the celery makes it limp.
  • Over-salting: pressed tofu is already seasoned.

Authentic tips

  • Cut the tofu and celery to the same size.
  • Slice thin for a quick cook.

Substitutions

  • Baked firm tofu replaces pressed tofu.
  • Reduce the chilies for a milder dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
