Huī Zhōu Dòu Gān Chǎo Qín Cài (Huizhou Dried Tofu with Celery) 徽州豆干炒芹菜

▾

Surprise me with

Pressed dried tofu (dou gan) is a Huizhou staple, stir-fried with celery and chilies as a quick everyday dish.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 8 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 300 g10 ozPressed tofu (dou gan), sliced into strips
  • 300 g10 ozCelery, sliced on an angle
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 15 g1/2 ozGarlic, sliced
  • 55Dried chilies, cut
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 2 g1/3 tspSalt
  • 3 g1/2 tspSugar

Method

  1. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a flat-bottom wok over medium-high heat and fry the tofu strips for 3 minutes, until golden at the edges. Lift them out.
  2. Add the remaining oil and fry the garlic and chilies for 15 seconds.
  3. Add the celery and stir-fry over high heat for 1 minute until bright green.
  4. Return the tofu with the soy sauce, salt and sugar and toss for 30 seconds.
  5. Serve hot with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the celery makes it limp.
  • Over-salting: pressed tofu is already seasoned.

Authentic tips

  • Cut the tofu and celery to the same size.
  • Slice thin for a quick cook.

Substitutions

  • Baked firm tofu replaces pressed tofu.
  • Reduce the chilies for a milder dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
