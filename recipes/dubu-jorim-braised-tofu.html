Dubu Jorim (Korean Braised Tofu) 두부조림

▾

Dubu jorim, pan-fried tofu simmered in a soy-gochugaru sauce, is one of the most common Korean banchan and home dishes.

Method

  1. Slice the tofu into pieces about 1.5 cm (1/2 in) thick, sprinkle with a little salt and pat dry with paper towels. Dry tofu browns well and does not spit.
  2. Mix the soy sauce, water, sugar, gochugaru, garlic, scallion, sesame oil and seeds into a sauce.
  3. Heat the oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium to medium-high heat and sear the tofu for 3 to 4 minutes per side, until golden. Turn it gently with a spatula, as it breaks easily.
  4. Lower the heat to medium-low, spoon the sauce over the tofu, lifting each piece so some sauce runs underneath, and simmer for 3 to 4 minutes.
  5. Turn the slices and simmer for 1 to 2 minutes more, spooning the sauce over, until it has reduced and coats the tofu. Serve warm or cold with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Unpressed tofu spatters.
  • Boiling the sauce dry burns it.

Authentic tips

  • A banchan staple.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • Extra-firm tofu holds its shape best.
  • Add sliced onions to the sauce.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; good cold.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Kimchi jjigae
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
