Pancit Guisado (Vegetable Stir-Fried Noodles)

▾

Surprise me with

Pancit, noodle dishes introduced by Chinese traders, are Philippine birthday staples symbolizing long life; the vegetable version is simple and filling.

  • Cook 15 min

  • 1 g1/2 tspBlack pepper
  • 44Calamansi, for serving

Method

  1. Fry the tofu in 2 tablespoons of oil in a flat-bottom wok over medium-high heat for 5 to 6 minutes until golden. Lift it out.
  2. Add the remaining oil and saute the garlic and onion for 2 minutes, then the carrot and green beans for 2 minutes and the cabbage for 1 minute. Lift out half the vegetables for topping.
  3. Add the stock, soy sauce, mushroom sauce and pepper and bring to a boil.
  4. Add the noodles and toss with tongs for 3 to 5 minutes, until they soften and absorb the liquid.
  5. Return the tofu and top with the reserved vegetables. Serve with calamansi to squeeze over.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooked noodles go soft.
  • Too little liquid leaves them hard.

Authentic tips

  • Pancit is eaten on birthdays for long life; don't cut the noodles.
  • Serve with calamansi.

Substitutions

  • Spaghetti replaces pancit canton.
  • Chicken or shrimp are the traditional additions.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a pan.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Calamansi wedges
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Calamansi juice
