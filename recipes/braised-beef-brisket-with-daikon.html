Braised Beef Brisket with Daikon 萝卜牛腩

▾

Surprise me with

A hearty northern Chinese braise pairing rich beef brisket with daikon radish, which soaks up the savory braising liquid while lightening the dish — a common cold-weather comfort food.

Method

  1. Blanch brisket in boiling water 5 minutes; drain and rinse.
  2. Brown brisket in a little oil, add ginger and star anise, stirring briefly.
  3. Add soy sauce, wine, and sugar, stirring to coat.
  4. Add water, bring to a boil, then reduce to a low simmer, cover, and braise 1.5 hours.
  5. Add daikon and continue simmering 25–30 minutes until the daikon and beef are both tender.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the daikon too early, which lets it turn mushy by the time the beef is fully tender.
  • Rushing the initial beef braise — brisket needs real time to become properly tender.

Authentic tips

  • Daikon absorbs the braising liquid's flavor beautifully — cut it into fairly large chunks so it holds its shape.
  • This dish, like most braises, tastes even better the next day.

Substitutions

  • Short rib replaces brisket.
  • A pressure cooker cuts the braise to 50 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; the flavor improves.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months without the daikon.
