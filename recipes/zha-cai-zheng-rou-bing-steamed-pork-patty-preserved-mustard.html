Zhà Cài Zhēng Ròu Bǐng (Steamed Pork Patty with Preserved Mustard Greens) 榨菜蒸肉饼

▾

Surprise me with

A flat patty of hand-chopped pork steamed on a plate is one of the most common Cantonese home dishes; preserved mustard stem (zha cai) is a favorite seasoning alongside salted egg or salted fish.

Method

  1. If using shoulder, dice it small. Rinse the pork in a bowl of water, massage it briefly, drain and squeeze firmly to press out the excess water. Use a fatty cut; lean pork steams into a dry, tough patty.
  2. Rinse the zha cai and cut it into thin strips; taste a piece, and soak it for 10 minutes if very salty. Crush the water chestnuts with the flat of a knife rather than dicing them, so they stay juicy and crunchy.
  3. Add the oyster sauce, soy sauce, sugar, pepper, cornstarch, water and sesame oil to the pork and mix with chopsticks for about 1 minute, until sticky. Add the green onion whites, water chestnuts and zha cai and mix for 1 to 2 minutes more. Stop there; overmixing makes the patty dense.
  4. Brush a rimmed heatproof plate with half the oil, spread the pork over it in an even layer about 2 cm (3/4 in) thick and flatten the top with the back of a spoon. Brush it with the rest of the oil.
  5. Set a rack in a flat-bottom wok or steamer and bring the water to a full boil. Steam the plate, covered, over high heat for 15 minutes (2 to 3 minutes less on a metal plate), until the patty is firm and the juices run clear. Do not steam much longer or it toughens.
  6. Scatter with the green onion greens and serve on the plate with rice, spooning the juices over.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Very lean pork gives a dry patty; use shoulder with some fat.
  • Not rinsing the mustard stem makes the dish too salty.

Authentic tips

  • Hand-chopping the pork gives a looser, juicier texture than machine-ground.
  • Steam it on top of rice for a classic one-pot dinner.

Substitutions

  • Salted egg yolk or a little salted fish replace the mustard stem.
  • Jicama replaces water chestnuts.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; re-steam for 5 minutes.

Freezing: Freezes raw, shaped on the plate, for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and stir-fried choy sum
  • Soup: Tomato tofu soup
  • Dessert: Ginger milk curd
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
