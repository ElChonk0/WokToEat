Zha Cai Zheng Rou Bing (Steamed Pork Patty with Preserved Mustard Greens) 榨菜蒸肉饼

A flat patty of hand-chopped pork steamed on a plate is one of the most common Cantonese home dishes; preserved mustard stem (zha cai) is a favorite seasoning alongside salted egg or salted fish.

  • 500 g1.1 lbPork shoulder, finely chopped, or ground pork
  • 60 g2 ozPreserved mustard stem (zha cai), rinsed and finely diced
  • 60 g2 ozWater chestnuts, diced
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspOyster sauce

  • 1 g1/2 tspGround white pepper

  • 45 ml3 tbspWater
  • 15 ml1 tbspNeutral oil
  • 2 stalks2 stalksGreen onion, chopped

Method

  1. Mix the pork with the soy sauce, oyster sauce, sugar and pepper, then stir in one direction while adding the water a spoon at a time until sticky. Mix in the cornstarch.
  2. Fold in the mustard stem and water chestnuts, then the oil.
  3. Spread the mixture into an even layer about 2 cm (3/4 in) thick on a rimmed heatproof plate and make a shallow dip in the center so it cooks evenly.
  4. Steam over high heat for 12 to 15 minutes until the juices run clear and the center reaches 71 C (160 F).
  5. Scatter with green onion and serve with the juices over rice.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Very lean pork gives a dry patty; use shoulder with some fat.
  • Not rinsing the mustard stem makes the dish too salty.

Authentic tips

  • Hand-chopping the pork gives a looser, juicier texture than machine-ground.
  • Steam it on top of rice for a classic one-pot dinner.

Substitutions

  • Salted egg yolk or a little salted fish replace the mustard stem.
  • Jicama replaces water chestnuts.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; re-steam for 5 minutes.

Freezing: Freezes raw, shaped on the plate, for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and stir-fried choy sum
  • Soup: Tomato tofu soup
  • Dessert: Ginger milk curd
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
