Chá Guǒ (Hakka Savory Tea Fruit) 茶果

▾

Surprise me with

Cha guo are Hakka glutinous rice dumplings made sweet or savory; the savory kind is filled with preserved vegetables or radish.

  • Prep 45 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 1 g1/4 tspWhite pepper
  • 5 ml1 tspSesame oil
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil, for the filling

Method

  1. Heat the filling oil in a wok, fry the garlic, mushrooms and preserved radish for 2 minutes, then add the tofu, soy sauce and white pepper and cook for 2 minutes. Stir in the green onion and sesame oil and cool completely.
  2. Stir the boiling water into the flour with the dough oil until shaggy, cool slightly, and knead until smooth and soft like clay.
  3. Divide the dough into 12 pieces, flatten each into a 7 cm round, add a spoonful of filling and pinch the edges closed into a half-moon.
  4. Set the dumplings on oiled parchment or banana leaf squares in a steamer, leaving 2 cm between them.
  5. Steam over high heat for 15 minutes, until the wrappers turn glossy and translucent.

Common mistakes

  • Using cold water makes a crumbly dough that cracks; use boiling water for the first mix.
  • Crowding the steamer makes the dumplings fuse together.

Authentic tips

  • Keep the dough covered while you shape, because it dries quickly.
  • Brush the cooked dumplings with a little oil so they do not stick.

Substitutions

  • Preserved mustard greens replace the radish.
  • Fried shallots add depth.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; resteam for 8 minutes.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months; steam from frozen for 20 minutes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Chili-soy dipping sauce
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot oolong tea
