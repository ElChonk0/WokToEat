Tortang Talong (Eggplant Omelet)

▾

Surprise me with

Tortang talong, a charred eggplant dipped in egg and pan-fried, is a Filipino home staple and a common breakfast.

Method

  1. Grill the whole eggplants over a gas flame or under a broiler for 8 to 10 minutes, turning, until the skin is charred and the flesh soft. Cool in a covered bowl for 5 minutes and peel, leaving the stem on as a handle.
  2. Lay each eggplant on a plate and flatten it with a fork into a fan shape about 1 cm (1/2 in) thick.
  3. Beat the eggs with the garlic, salt and pepper in a wide dish. Dip each flattened eggplant into the egg, coating both sides.
  4. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium heat. Lay in an eggplant, spoon a little extra egg over it and fry for 2 to 3 minutes per side until golden.
  5. Serve with banana ketchup and rice. A favorite Filipino breakfast and baon (packed lunch).
