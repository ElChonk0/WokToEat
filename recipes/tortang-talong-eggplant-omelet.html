Tortang Talong (Eggplant Omelet)

Tortang talong, a charred eggplant dipped in egg and pan-fried, is a Filipino home staple and a common breakfast.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 45 g3 tbspBanana ketchup

Method

  1. Char the eggplants under a broiler or on a grill pan for 10 minutes until soft; peel, leaving the stem.
  2. Flatten gently with a fork.
  3. Beat the eggs with the garlic and salt and dip in the eggplants.
  4. Pan-fry in the oil over medium heat 3 minutes per side until golden.
  5. Serve with banana ketchup.

Common mistakes

  • Underchared eggplant lacks flavor.
  • Egg that is too thin runs off.

Authentic tips

  • Add ground pork for a classic version.
  • A common breakfast.

Substitutions

  • Ground pork is the classic addition.
  • Zucchini replaces eggplant.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.
