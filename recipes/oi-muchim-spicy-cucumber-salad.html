Oi Muchim (Spicy Korean Cucumber Salad) 오이무침

▾

Surprise me with

Oi muchim, cucumbers quickly salted and tossed with chili flakes, vinegar and garlic, is a fresh Korean summer banchan made just before eating.

Method

  1. Slice the cucumbers 3 to 6 mm (1/8 to 1/4 in) thick, toss them with the salt and leave for about 15 minutes at room temperature.
  2. Drain off the liquid that collects, without squeezing the cucumber, which bruises it. Salting draws out water so the dressing does not dilute.
  3. Add the onion, garlic, gochugaru, vinegar, sugar, soy sauce, sesame oil and seeds and toss gently with your fingertips.
  4. Taste and adjust. It can look dry at first, but the cucumbers keep releasing juice; serve within an hour or two for the best crunch.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the salting makes the salad watery within minutes.
  • Making it far ahead softens the cucumbers.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with grilled meats like samgyeopsal to cut the richness.
  • A pinch of fish sauce is common if it does not need to be vegan.

Substitutions

  • English cucumber, seeded.
  • Use less gochugaru for a mild version.

Storage

Leftovers: Best the same day; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Grilled pork belly
  • Soup: Doenjang jjigae
  • Dessert: Watermelon
  • Beverage: Barley tea
