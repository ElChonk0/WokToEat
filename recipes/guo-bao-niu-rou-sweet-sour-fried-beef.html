Guo Bao Niu Rou (Dongbei Sweet-Sour Fried Beef) 锅包牛肉

Guo bao rou, a Dongbei dish of crisp battered pork in a tangy sauce, is adapted to beef in some restaurants for a chewier texture.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 90 g3/4 cupPotato starch
  • 45 g3 tbspSugar
  • 45 ml3 tbspChinkiang vinegar
  • 30 g2 tbspGinger and scallion
  • 60 g2 ozCarrot shreds
  • 500 ml2 cupsNeutral oil for frying

Method

  1. Slice the beef 3 mm thick across the grain and toss with a pinch of salt. Coat in a thick batter of the potato starch and 100 ml water.
  2. Heat the oil to 170 C (340 F) and fry in batches for 2-3 minutes until pale gold; drain. Refry at 190 C (375 F) for 30 seconds until crisp.
  3. Simmer the sugar and vinegar with 3 tablespoons of water and the ginger and scallion for 2 minutes until syrupy.
  4. Add the carrot shreds and the beef and toss quickly.
  5. Serve at once.

Common mistakes

  • Saucing early makes the crust soggy.
  • A thick batter on the beef makes it heavy.

Authentic tips

  • The sauce should be tangy; add a splash more vinegar if sweet.
  • Pork is the traditional version.

Substitutions

  • Pork tenderloin is the classic version.
  • Rice vinegar plus ketchup replaces Chinkiang vinegar.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the crust softens within minutes.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
