Náng Juǎn Jī Ròu (Chicken Naan Wrap) 馕卷鸡肉

▾

Surprise me with

Naan, the round baked bread of Xinjiang, is wrapped around grilled meat and vegetables as a quick street meal.

  • Cook 15 min

  • 44Naan or flatbread
  • 450 g1 lbChicken thighs, sliced
  • 8 g3 tspCumin and chili
  • 250 g9 ozOnion and tomato
  • 120 g1/2 cupYogurt
  • 6 g1 tspSalt
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Toss the chicken with the cumin, chili, salt and oil; marinate 20 minutes.
  2. Fry or grill the chicken in a hot pan 6-8 minutes until charred and cooked through.
  3. Warm the naan in a dry pan.
  4. Slice the onion and tomato.
  5. Fill the naan with chicken, onion, tomato and yogurt and roll up tightly.

Common mistakes

  • Cold bread cracks when rolled; warm it first.
  • Overfilling splits the wrap.

Authentic tips

  • Thick yogurt avoids soggy bread.
  • Add pickled chilies for extra heat.

Substitutions

  • Lamb is the traditional meat.
  • Store-bought naan or pita replaces nang.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the filling keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freeze the cooked chicken for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Cucumber yogurt salad
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Hami melon
  • Beverage: Black tea
