Dōng Guā Hǎi Xiān Gēng (Winter Melon Seafood Soup) 冬瓜海鲜羹

▾

Surprise me with

A thickened soup of finely diced winter melon with shrimp, scallops and crab is a Cantonese banquet soup that is also easy at home.

  • Cook 25 min

  • 600 g1.3 lbWinter melon, peeled and finely diced
  • 1.2 L5 cupsChicken stock
  • 15 g1/2 ozGinger, sliced
  • 200 g7 ozShrimp, peeled and diced
  • 150 g5 ozScallops, diced
  • 100 g3.5 ozCrab meat
  • 44Dried shiitake mushrooms, soaked and diced
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt
  • 1 g1/2 tspGround white pepper
  • 30 g3 tbspCornstarch
  • 60 ml1/4 cupWater, for the slurry
  • 22Egg whites
  • 10 g1/4 cupFresh cilantro, chopped

Method

  1. Simmer the winter melon, mushrooms and ginger in the stock for 15 minutes until the melon is translucent. Remove the ginger.
  2. Add the shrimp and scallops and simmer 1 to 2 minutes, then add the crab meat.
  3. Season with salt and pepper, then stir in the cornstarch mixed with the water and simmer 1 minute until it coats a spoon.
  4. Lower the heat and drizzle in the egg whites while stirring slowly so they form ribbons.
  5. Serve with cilantro.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the seafood with the melon overcooks it.
  • Cutting the melon in big chunks; it should be diced small for a geng.

Authentic tips

  • For banquets the soup is served inside a carved, steamed winter melon.
  • Dried scallops (conpoy), soaked and shredded, add depth.

Substitutions

  • Zucchini or fuzzy melon replace winter melon; simmer 8 minutes.
  • Any firm white fish replaces scallops.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator; reheat gently.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Garlic steamed shrimp
  • Soup: This is the soup course
  • Dessert: Almond jelly
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
