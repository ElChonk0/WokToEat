Dōng Guā Hǎi Xiān Gēng (Winter Melon Seafood Soup) 冬瓜海鲜羹

▾

Surprise me with

A thickened soup of finely diced winter melon with shrimp, scallops and crab is a Cantonese banquet soup that is also easy at home.

  • 8 g1 tbspCornstarch, for the seafood
  • 15 ml1 tbspWater, for the seafood
  • 1 g1/2 tspGround white pepper

Method

  1. Dice the winter melon, shrimp, scallops and fish to the same small size, about 1 cm (1/2 in), so every spoonful has a bit of everything. Toss the shrimp, scallops and fish with the seafood cornstarch, water and half the pepper. Mix the slurry, and beat the egg whites with a teaspoon of water.
  2. Bring the stock, water, ginger and mushrooms to a boil in a covered pot over high heat. Add the winter melon, cover and cook for 5 to 7 minutes until it turns translucent and tender.
  3. Re-stir the slurry and pour it in slowly over 30 to 45 seconds while stirring, until the soup thickens enough to coat a spoon. Adding it gradually prevents lumps.
  4. Add the seafood and crab, stir gently to separate the pieces, cover and simmer for 2 minutes until the shrimp turn pink.
  5. Turn the heat down so the soup barely bubbles and pour in the egg whites in a thin stream over 20 to 30 seconds, stirring slowly in one direction so they set into soft white strands.
  6. Season with salt, the remaining pepper and sesame oil, taste, and serve scattered with cilantro.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the seafood with the melon overcooks it.
  • Cutting the melon in big chunks; it should be diced small for a geng.

Authentic tips

  • For banquets the soup is served inside a carved, steamed winter melon.
  • Dried scallops (conpoy), soaked and shredded, add depth.

Substitutions

  • Zucchini or fuzzy melon replace winter melon; simmer 8 minutes.
  • Any firm white fish replaces scallops.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator; reheat gently.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Garlic steamed shrimp
  • Soup: This is the soup course
  • Dessert: Almond jelly
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
