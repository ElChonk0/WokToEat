Jiān Yú Bǐng (Pan-Fried Fish Cakes) 煎鱼饼

▾

Surprise me with

Springy fish paste patties pan-fried until golden are a Cantonese home and market food; the paste is made by slapping minced fish until it turns bouncy.

Method

  1. Squeeze the shrimp dry in a towel; excess water makes the cakes fall apart. Slice the fish, then smash and chop it with a cleaver into a sticky paste. Mash the shrimp the same way for 1 to 2 minutes, combine the two and fold in the scallops.
  2. Add the salt, sugar, pepper and cornstarch, then the egg, then the ice water in three additions, mixing hard in one direction for 1 to 2 minutes after each. Add the sesame oil, tangerine peel, green onion and cilantro and keep mixing for 2 to 3 minutes, until the paste pulls into springy strands.
  3. Stir the sauce ingredients until the sugar and cornstarch dissolve.
  4. Heat a heavy skillet, add the oil and turn off the heat. With oiled hands, shape the paste into balls about 60 g (2 oz) each and space them out in the pan, then turn the heat to high for 2 to 3 minutes and press them into patties about 1.5 cm (1/2 in) thick.
  5. Fry for about 5 minutes until golden and they release from the pan, then turn and fry over medium heat for about 5 minutes more, until firm and springy when pressed and 63 C (145 F) inside. Raise the heat for the last minute for extra color.
  6. Lift out the cakes, pour the sauce into the pan and stir over high heat until thick and glossy. Serve the cakes with the sauce spooned over, or sliced in noodle soup.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the slapping gives crumbly, soft cakes instead of bouncy ones.
  • Warm paste loses its spring; keep everything cold.

Authentic tips

  • Ready-made fish paste from an Asian grocer makes this a 15-minute dish.
  • The same paste stuffs peppers, eggplant and tofu.

Substitutions

  • Dace (ling yu), the traditional fish, if you can get it.
  • Add finely diced water chestnut for crunch.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; reheat in a pan.

Freezing: Freeze raw patties for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and garlic choy sum
  • Soup: Bok choy and pork soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
