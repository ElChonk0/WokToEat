Poha (Flattened Rice with Peanuts and Turmeric)

▾

Surprise me with

Poha, flattened rice tempered with mustard seeds, turmeric and peanuts, is the breakfast of Maharashtra and central India, especially popular in Indore.

Method

  1. Rinse the poha in a colander under running water for 20 to 30 seconds, tossing gently, just until soft, then drain and leave for 10 minutes. Do not soak it; it turns mushy. Toss it with the turmeric, salt and sugar.
  2. Heat the oil in a wide pan over medium heat and fry the peanuts for 2 to 3 minutes until golden and crisp. Lift them out.
  3. Splutter the mustard and cumin seeds, then add the curry leaves, chilies, onion and potato and cook, covered, for 5 to 6 minutes until the potato is tender.
  4. Add the poha and toss gently over low heat for 2 to 3 minutes, then cover and steam for 2 minutes so it is soft and fluffy.
  5. Turn off the heat, stir in the lemon juice and peanuts and top with cilantro and sev. A Maharashtrian and Indori breakfast staple.

Common mistakes

  • Soaking poha too long makes it mushy.
  • Stirring roughly breaks the flakes.

Authentic tips

  • Thick poha holds up best.
  • Serve with sev and jalebi in Indore.

Substitutions

  • Thin poha takes less rinsing.
  • Add a spoonful of sev on top.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.
