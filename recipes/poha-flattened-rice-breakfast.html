Poha (Flattened Rice with Peanuts and Turmeric)

▾

Surprise me with

Poha, flattened rice tempered with mustard seeds, turmeric and peanuts, is the breakfast of Maharashtra and central India, especially popular in Indore.

  • 250 g9 ozThick poha (flattened rice)
  • 200 g7 ozOnion and potato
  • 40 g1.5 ozPeanuts
  • 6 g2 tspMustard seeds and curry leaves
  • 3 g1 tspTurmeric
  • 10 g2 tspGreen chilies
  • 30 ml2 tbspLemon juice
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt

Method

  1. Rinse the poha in a colander until soft but not mushy; drain 5 minutes.
  2. Heat the oil and fry the mustard seeds, curry leaves, peanuts and chilies until the mustard pops.
  3. Add the onion and potato and cook 8 minutes until the potato is tender.
  4. Add the turmeric, salt and poha and toss gently 3 minutes.
  5. Finish with lemon juice and cilantro.

Common mistakes

  • Soaking poha too long makes it mushy.
  • Stirring roughly breaks the flakes.

Authentic tips

  • Thick poha holds up best.
  • Serve with sev and jalebi in Indore.

Substitutions

  • Thin poha takes less rinsing.
  • Add a spoonful of sev on top.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sev and lemon wedges
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Masala chai
