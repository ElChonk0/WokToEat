Kangkung Sambal (Water Spinach in Chili Sambal)

▾

Surprise me with

Kangkung belacan is a Malaysian tze char staple; this version uses a vegetarian chili paste in place of shrimp paste.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 5 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 500 g1.1 lbWater spinach
  • 40 g1.5 ozChilies
  • 40 g3 tbspGarlic and shallot
  • 15 ml1 tbspSoy sauce
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Cut the water spinach into 8 cm lengths and drain well.
  2. Pound the chilies, garlic and shallot.
  3. Heat the oil in a wok until smoking and fry the paste 20 seconds.
  4. Add the stems, then the leaves, with the soy sauce and sugar.
  5. Toss 2 minutes and serve at once.

Common mistakes

  • Wet greens spatter and steam.
  • Overcooking makes it limp.

Authentic tips

  • Stems first; they take longer.
  • Add belacan for the non-vegetarian version.

Substitutions

  • Spinach replaces kangkung.
  • Add belacan for a non-vegetarian version.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.
