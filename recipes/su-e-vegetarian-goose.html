Sù É (Vegetarian Goose) 素鹅

▾

Surprise me with

Su e, rolled bean curd skin braised in soy sauce, imitates goose meat and is a classic of Chinese Buddhist vegetarian cooking, popular in Shanghai and Jiangnan temples.

  • Cook 40 min

Method

  1. Simmer the soy sauces, sugar, Shaoxing wine, star anise and stock for 5 minutes, then stir in the sesame oil and cool slightly. This is the braising sauce.
  2. Lay a tofu skin sheet on a board, brush it generously with the sauce and lay the next sheet on top. Repeat with all the sheets, brushing each layer.
  3. Fold the stack in from the edges into a long, flat rectangle about 8 cm (3 in) wide, brushing as you go, and press it firmly.
  4. Steam the parcel for 15 minutes to set the layers.
  5. Heat the neutral oil in a skillet over medium heat and fry the parcel for 3 to 4 minutes per side until golden and crisp. Its crisp, layered skin resembles roast goose, hence the name.
  6. Cool, slice into strips and serve at room temperature with a drizzle of the remaining sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Not tying tightly makes the rolls fall apart.
  • Over-soaking the skins makes them tear.

Authentic tips

  • Slice thinly and serve at room temperature.
  • Dried tofu skin is sold in sheets at Asian markets.

Substitutions

  • Tofu skin sold as "yuba sheets" works.
  • Add shiitake for a meatier flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; served cold.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice

  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
