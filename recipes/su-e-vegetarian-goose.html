Sù É (Vegetarian Goose) 素鹅

▾

Surprise me with

Su e, rolled bean curd skin braised in soy sauce, imitates goose meat and is a classic of Chinese Buddhist vegetarian cooking, popular in Shanghai and Jiangnan temples.

  • Cook 25 min

Method

  1. Soak the tofu skins in warm water for 3 minutes until just soft; stack three and roll tightly, then tie with string; repeat to make two rolls.
  2. Heat the oil in a pan over medium heat and fry the rolls 3 minutes per side until golden.
  3. Add the soy sauces, sugar, wine, star anise and 300 ml water, bring to a boil and cover.
  4. Simmer 20 minutes, turning once, until the liquid is nearly gone.
  5. Cool slightly and slice 1 cm thick.

Common mistakes

  • Not tying tightly makes the rolls fall apart.
  • Over-soaking the skins makes them tear.

Authentic tips

  • Slice thinly and serve at room temperature.
  • Dried tofu skin is sold in sheets at Asian markets.

Substitutions

  • Tofu skin sold as "yuba sheets" works.
  • Add shiitake for a meatier flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; served cold.

Freezing: Not recommended.
