Gaeng Hung Lay (Northern Thai Pork Belly Curry) แกงฮังเล

Gaeng hung lay, a sweet-sour pork curry with ginger and tamarind, shows Burmese influence in northern Thailand and has no coconut milk.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 2 hr
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 250 g9 ozPork belly
  • 350 g12 ozPork shoulder
  • 30 g1 ozHung lay curry powder
  • 30 g1 ozTamarind
  • 60 g2 ozGinger
  • 40 g1.5 ozPickled garlic
  • 30 g2 tbspPalm sugar
  • 30 ml2 tbspSoy sauce
  • 40 g1.5 ozRoasted peanuts
  • 600 ml2.5 cupsWater

Method

  1. Toss the pork with the curry powder and marinate for 1 hour.
  2. Fry in a dry heavy pot over medium heat for 6 minutes, then add 600 ml water.
  3. Cover and simmer on low for 90 minutes until the pork is very tender.
  4. Add the julienned ginger, tamarind, sugar, soy sauce and pickled garlic and simmer 10 minutes.
  5. Uncover and reduce the sauce until it clings; top with peanuts.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking on high heat toughens the pork.
  • Adding the tamarind too early makes it bitter.

Authentic tips

  • A northern dish with Burmese roots; no coconut milk.
  • Serve with sticky rice.

Substitutions

  • Pork shoulder alone gives a leaner curry.
  • Ready-made hung lay powder is sold at Thai markets.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; the flavor improves.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sticky rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
