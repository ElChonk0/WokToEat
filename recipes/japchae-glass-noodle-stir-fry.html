Japchae (Glass Noodle Stir-Fry)

▾

Surprise me with

Japchae is a celebratory Korean dish of stir-fried sweet potato glass noodles and vegetables, traditionally served at holidays, valued for its glossy, slightly sweet, sesame-forward flavor.

Method

  1. Stir the soy sauce, sugar, sesame oil, garlic, sesame seeds and pepper together until the sugar dissolves. Cut the carrot, onion, mushrooms and beef into thin, even strips so they match the noodles.
  2. Boil the noodles for 6 to 8 minutes, until soft and translucent all the way through; they should be fully tender, not al dente. Rinse in cold water, drain, cut them a few times with scissors and toss with 3 tablespoons of the sauce.
  3. Blanch the spinach until just wilted, cool it in cold water, squeeze it dry, cut it into lengths and season with a pinch of salt.
  4. Heat a large skillet or flat-bottom wok over medium-high heat with a little oil and stir-fry the noodles for about 4 minutes, until they turn glossy and slightly sticky. Transfer to a large bowl.
  5. Stir-fry the carrot with a pinch of salt for 1 minute, then the onion until just translucent, then the green onion for 30 seconds, adding each to the bowl. Keep them crisp. Cook the beef and mushrooms with 2 tablespoons of sauce for 2 to 3 minutes until the beef is done, and add them too.
  6. Add the spinach and the remaining sauce and toss everything together with your hands or tongs. Taste and add a little more soy sauce or sugar if needed. Top with thin strips of egg omelet if you like. Japchae is good warm or at room temperature.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the noodles: they should be tender but still chewy — check a minute before the package time.

Authentic tips

  • Sautéing vegetables separately keeps colors and textures distinct in the final toss.

Substitutions

  • No glass noodles: thin rice noodles are the closest substitute, though the chewy texture differs.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2–3 days refrigerated; best served cold or at room temperature, since reheating can make noodles gummy.

Freezing: Not recommended; noodle texture suffers.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
