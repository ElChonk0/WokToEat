Yúnnán Suān Là Zhēng Yú (Yunnan Sour-Spicy Steamed Fish) 酸辣蒸鱼

▾

Surprise me with

Fresh chilies, lime and herbs are used across Yunnan for a bright, sour-spicy treatment of steamed fish.

  • Cook 15 min

  • 40 g1.5 ozFresh chilies
  • 30 g2 tbspGarlic
  • 45 ml3 tbspLime juice
  • 15 ml1 tbspFish sauce
  • 20 g2/3 ozCilantro
  • 5 g1 tspSugar

Method

  1. Score the fish on both sides and place it on a plate on a few ginger slices.
  2. Steam over high heat for 12-15 minutes depending on size, until the flesh at the thickest part is just opaque.
  3. Pour off the liquid.
  4. Mix the chilies, garlic, lime juice, fish sauce and sugar and pour over the fish.
  5. Top with cilantro and serve.

Common mistakes

  • Oversteaming dries the fish.
  • Adding the lime before steaming dulls its flavor.

Authentic tips

  • Use a whole fish of about 900 g so it cooks evenly.
  • Taste the dressing for balance of sour, salty, hot and sweet.

Substitutions

  • Fillets steam in 8 minutes.
  • Sea bass or snapper are good choices.

Storage

Leftovers: Eat within the hour.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sticky rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh pineapple
  • Beverage: Chilled tea
