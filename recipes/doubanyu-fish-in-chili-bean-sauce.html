Doubanyu (Fish in Chili Bean Sauce) 豆瓣鱼

Doubanyu is a quintessential Sichuan home dish: a whole fish pan-fried and simmered in Pixian chili bean paste with ginger, garlic and scallion, showcasing the province's signature fermented condiment.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 900 g2 lbWhole carp or sea bass, cleaned and scored
  • 45 g3 tbspPixian doubanjiang
  • 60 g4 tbspGinger, garlic and scallion
  • 300 ml1.25 cupsStock or water
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 15 ml1 tbspChinkiang vinegar
  • 15 ml1 tbspCornstarch slurry
  • 60 ml4 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Pat the fish dry and pan-fry in oil for 2 minutes per side.
  2. Remove the fish, add the doubanjiang and aromatics to the oil and stir until red.
  3. Add stock and sugar, return the fish, and simmer 8-10 minutes spooning sauce over.
  4. Thicken with slurry, finish with vinegar and scallion.

Common mistakes

  • Using too much doubanjiang, which makes the sauce salty.
  • Turning the fish more than once.

Authentic tips

  • Chop the doubanjiang finely to disperse it in the sauce.

Substitutions

  • Cod steaks or trout replace the whole fish.
  • A splash of rice wine adds depth.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat gently.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
