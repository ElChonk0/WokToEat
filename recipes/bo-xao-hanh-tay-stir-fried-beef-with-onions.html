Bò Xào Hành Tây (Vietnamese Beef and Onion Stir-Fry)

▾

Surprise me with

Beef stir-fried with onions is a quick Vietnamese home dish served with rice and fresh tomato and cucumber.

Method

  1. Marinate the beef with the fish sauce, half the garlic, cornstarch, pepper and 1 teaspoon of oil for 15 minutes.
  2. Heat half the oil in a flat-bottom wok over high heat and sear the beef in a single layer for 1 minute, then toss for 30 seconds until just browned. Lift it out.
  3. Add the remaining oil and garlic and stir-fry the onions for 2 minutes until they soften at the edges but keep some crunch.
  4. Return the beef with the oyster sauce, fish sauce and sugar and toss for 30 seconds.
  5. Add the green onion and plenty of black pepper and serve with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking makes the beef tough.
  • Crowding the wok steams the beef.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with rice and tomato slices.
  • Don't overcook the onions; keep some crunch.

Substitutions

  • Chicken takes the same sauce.
  • Add tomato wedges for a juicier dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and tomato slices
  • Soup: Canh
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
