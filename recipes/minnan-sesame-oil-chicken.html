Sesame Oil Chicken 麻油鸡

A southern Fujian (Minnan) home remedy carried across the strait to Taiwan, sesame oil chicken is the classic warming dish for new mothers and cold nights: old ginger fried slowly in black sesame oil, then simmered with rice wine.

  • Cook 30 min

  • 900 g2 lbChicken thighs, bone-in, cut into pieces
  • 45 ml3 tbspBlack (dark) sesame oil
  • 80 g3 ozOld ginger, thinly sliced (skin on)
  • 375 ml1.5 cupsRice wine (mi jiu or Shaoxing)
  • 250 ml1 cupWater
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 5 g1 tspRock sugar
  • 10 g1 tbspGoji berries (optional)

Method

  1. Put the sesame oil and ginger in a cold pot and heat gently over low-medium heat.
  2. Fry the ginger 6–8 minutes until shrivelled and golden at the edges but not dark.
  3. Raise the heat, add the chicken and sear until the skin colours, about 5 minutes.
  4. Pour in the rice wine (it will sizzle), then the water, soy sauce and rock sugar.
  5. Simmer covered 15–18 minutes until the chicken is tender; add goji berries for the last 2 minutes.

Common mistakes

  • Heating sesame oil hard; black sesame oil turns bitter at high heat, so start the ginger cold.

Authentic tips

  • Traditional versions use only rice wine and no water; dilute to taste, since much of the alcohol remains.
  • Ladle the broth over mee sua (thin wheat noodles) for a one-bowl meal.

Substitutions

  • Bone-in thighs give a richer broth; add 10 minutes.
  • Fresh young ginger is milder; use double the amount.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat gently, without boiling off the wine.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Rice vermicelli or steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Warm ginger tea
