Gamja Bokkeum (Stir-Fried Potato Strips) 감자볶음

Gamja bokkeum, thin potato strips stir-fried until just tender with onion and carrot, is an everyday Korean banchan.

Method

  1. Cut the potatoes into thin, even matchsticks and soak them in cold water with the soaking salt for 10 to 15 minutes. Soaking washes off the surface starch so the strands stay separate and slightly crisp. Drain and pat them dry.
  2. Heat the oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium heat and stir-fry the potatoes for 4 to 5 minutes, until they turn translucent at the edges but still have some bite.
  3. Add the onion and carrot and stir-fry for 2 minutes, then the bell pepper and garlic for 1 minute.
  4. Season with the salt, taste and cook for 1 minute more; the potatoes should be tender but not mushy or broken.
  5. Turn off the heat, toss with the sesame oil and seeds and serve warm or at room temperature as a banchan.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Unsoaked potatoes stick together and turn gummy.
  • Overcooking makes them break.

Authentic tips

  • Cut the strips evenly so they cook at the same rate.
  • It is good warm or cold.

Substitutions

  • Add a little soy sauce and syrup for a glazed version.
  • Add ham for a lunchbox favorite.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and other banchan

  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
