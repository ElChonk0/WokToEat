Gamja Bokkeum (Stir-Fried Potato Strips) 감자볶음

Gamja bokkeum, thin potato strips stir-fried until just tender with onion and carrot, is an everyday Korean banchan.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Soak the potato strips in cold water with the salt for 10 minutes, then drain and pat dry; this removes starch so they stay separate.
  2. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium heat and stir-fry the potatoes for 5 minutes.
  3. Add the onion, carrot and garlic and stir-fry 3 to 4 minutes until the potatoes are tender but still hold their shape.
  4. Add the green pepper and season with salt; toss 1 minute.
  5. Finish with sesame oil and sesame seeds.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Unsoaked potatoes stick together and turn gummy.
  • Overcooking makes them break.

Authentic tips

  • Cut the strips evenly so they cook at the same rate.
  • It is good warm or cold.

Substitutions

  • Add a little soy sauce and syrup for a glazed version.
  • Add ham for a lunchbox favorite.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and other banchan
  • Soup: Doenjang jjigae
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
