Suàn Pán Zi (Hakka Abacus Seeds with Pork) 算盘子

▾

Surprise me with

Abacus seeds are Hakka beads made from a dough of yam or taro and tapioca starch, boiled and then stir-fried with minced meat, dried shrimp and mushrooms.

Method

  1. Steam the taro for 25 minutes until very soft, mash it while hot, and knead in the tapioca starch and 15 ml of the oil until you have a smooth, slightly sticky dough.
  2. Roll the dough into 1.5 cm ropes, cut into 1.5 cm pieces and press each with your thumb into a flat bead with a dent in the middle.
  3. Boil the beads in a large pot of water for 4 to 5 minutes until they float, then drain.
  4. Heat the remaining oil in a wok, fry the garlic, dried shrimp and mushrooms for 1 minute, add the pork and stir-fry for 3 minutes until browned.
  5. Add the wine, soy sauce and water, then the beads and choy sum, and cook for 3 minutes, stirring, until the sauce clings and the greens are tender.

Common mistakes

  • Too much starch makes the beads tough; add it gradually until the dough just stops sticking.
  • Skipping the boil leaves the beads raw in the middle.

Authentic tips

  • Keep the beads even in size so they cook through together.
  • Season at the end, because the dried shrimp and soy sauce are salty.

Substitutions

  • Yam or sweet potato replaces taro.
  • Minced chicken replaces pork.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator; the beads firm up and reheat well in a pan.

Freezing: The uncooked beads freeze for 1 month on a tray before bagging.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled mustard greens
  • Soup: Clear pork rib soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot Chinese tea
