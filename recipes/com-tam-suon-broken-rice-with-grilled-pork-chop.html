Cơm Tấm Sườn (Broken Rice with Grilled Pork Chop)

Com tam suon, broken rice with a grilled lemongrass pork chop, is a Saigon street breakfast and lunch icon.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 80 g3 ozLemongrass, garlic, shallot
  • 45 ml3 tbspFish sauce
  • 30 g2 tbspSugar
  • 200 g7 ozCucumber and tomato
  • 80 g3 ozPickled vegetables

Method

  1. Pound the lemongrass, garlic and shallot and mix with half the fish sauce and the sugar; marinate the chops 1 hour.
  2. Cook the broken rice with a little less water than usual, about 20 minutes.
  3. Grill the chops over medium-high heat, 5 minutes per side, until charred.
  4. Mix the remaining fish sauce with water, lime and sugar for the dipping sauce.
  5. Serve the chops on the rice with the cucumber, tomato, pickles and sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the sugar on high heat.
  • Mushy rice; use less water.

Authentic tips

  • A fried egg and steamed egg meatloaf are classic additions.
  • Jasmine rice works if broken rice is unavailable.

Substitutions

  • Jasmine rice replaces broken rice.
  • Boneless pork shoulder slices replace chops.
  • No grill: broil on a rack about 10 cm (4 in) from the element for 5 to 6 minutes per side, or roast at 220°C (425°F) for 15 to 18 minutes, until caramelized and cooked through.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat the pork in a pan.

Freezing: Freezes the marinated raw pork for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Fried egg and fish sauce
  • Soup: Canh
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
