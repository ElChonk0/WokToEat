Roti Canai with Dhal (Flaky Flatbread with Lentil Curry)

Roti canai, a flaky flatbread of Indian-Muslim origin, is a Malaysian breakfast icon, served with dhal or curry at mamak stalls across the country.

  • Cook 30 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 15 g1/2 ozTurmeric and curry powder

  • 6 g1 tspSalt

Method

  1. Mix the flour, 20 g ghee, water and salt into a soft dough, knead 8 minutes, divide into 8 balls and coat in oil; rest at least 2 hours.
  2. Simmer the lentils with the turmeric in 700 ml water for 20 minutes until creamy.
  3. Fry the onion, garlic, ginger, tomato and curry powder in a little oil for 8 minutes and stir into the lentils.
  4. Stretch each dough ball very thin, fold into a square and cook on a hot griddle with ghee for 2 minutes per side until golden.
  5. Clap the roti to fluff it and serve with the dhal.

Common mistakes

  • Dough that isn't rested tears.
  • Insufficient oil makes it tough instead of flaky.

Authentic tips

  • Stretch rather than roll.
  • Serve with a fish or chicken curry too.

Substitutions

  • Store-bought frozen roti canai saves time.
  • Chickpea curry also works.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes the dhal for 3 months.
