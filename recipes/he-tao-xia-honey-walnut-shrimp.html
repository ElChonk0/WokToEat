Hé Táo Xiā (Honey Walnut Shrimp) 合桃虾

▾

Surprise me with

Crisp shrimp in a sweet, creamy mayonnaise sauce with candied walnuts is a Hong Kong banquet creation of the 1980s and 1990s that became a Chinese American restaurant favorite.

  • Cook 15 min

Method

  1. Toss the shrimp with the salt. Stir the mayonnaise, condensed milk and lemon juice into a smooth sauce in a large bowl. Whisk the flour, cornstarch, baking powder, egg and water into a thin pancake-like batter, then stir in the batter oil.
  2. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven or flat-bottom wok to 150 C (300 F) and fry the walnuts for 3 to 4 minutes until golden; drain. Warm the honey in a small pan, toss the walnuts in it and cook for 1 to 2 minutes until glazed, then spread them on parchment to cool and harden.
  3. Raise the oil to 175 C (350 F); a drop of batter should rise straight away without browning instantly. Coat the shrimp and drop them in one at a time so they do not clump. Fry in batches for 2 to 3 minutes until set and golden, then drain on a rack.
  4. Raise the oil to 190 to 200 C (375 to 390 F) and fry all the shrimp again for 60 to 90 seconds until deep golden and crisp. The second fry keeps them crunchy under the creamy sauce.
  5. Toss the hot shrimp in the sauce until coated, pile onto a plate, scatter the candied walnuts and sesame seeds over and serve at once.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Saucing the shrimp in a hot pan makes the mayonnaise split; toss in a bowl.
  • Under-coated walnuts burn in the oil; keep them moving.

Authentic tips

  • Serve on a bed of blanched broccoli, as restaurants do.
  • Japanese mayonnaise gives a richer sauce.

Substitutions

  • Pecans replace walnuts.
  • Air fryer: spray the coated shrimp and cook at 200 C (400 F) for 8 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Best eaten at once.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed broccoli and rice
  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
