Guō Bā Tāng (Sizzling Rice Soup with Shrimp) 锅巴汤

Sizzling rice soup, hot broth poured over fried rice crusts at the table so they crackle, is an old Chinese restaurant showpiece that is a favorite in Cantonese American restaurants.

Method

  1. To make rice crusts, press cooked rice in a thin layer on an oiled tray and dry in a 120 C (250 F) oven for 1 hour, then break into pieces.
  2. Toss the shrimp and chicken with cornstarch. Bring the stock to a boil with the mushrooms, carrot and bamboo shoots and simmer 5 minutes.
  3. Add the chicken and simmer 3 minutes, then the shrimp and snow peas for 2 minutes. Season with soy sauce, salt, pepper and sesame oil. Keep it very hot.
  4. Just before serving, heat the oil in a Dutch oven to 200 C (400 F) and fry the rice crusts for 30 seconds until puffed and golden. Drain and put them in a hot serving bowl.
  5. At the table, pour the boiling soup over the hot crusts so they sizzle loudly.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • If the crusts or soup are not both very hot, there is no sizzle.
  • Moist rice crusts will not puff; they must be fully dry.

Authentic tips

  • Store dried crusts in a sealed jar for weeks.
  • Toss in a few more crusts at the end for crunch.

Substitutions

  • Rice cakes or puffed rice crackers stand in for homemade crusts.
  • Make it vegetarian with tofu and vegetable stock.

Storage

Leftovers: Store the soup separately for 2 days; fry fresh crusts when serving.

Freezing: The soup freezes for 2 months without the snow peas.
