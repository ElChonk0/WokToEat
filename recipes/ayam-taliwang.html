Ayam Taliwang

▾

Surprise me with

Originating from Lombok, ayam taliwang is known for its fiery chili-tomato-shrimp paste marinade and grilled preparation, considered one of Indonesia's spiciest and most distinctive regional chicken dishes.

Method

  1. Flatten the chicken pieces slightly with a cleaver, rub with lime juice and leave for 10 minutes.
  2. Blend the chilies, shallots, garlic, kencur, tomato and shrimp paste into a paste. Fry it in the oil in a wide pan over medium heat for 8 minutes, until fragrant and the oil separates, and season with palm sugar and salt.
  3. Spoon out a third of the paste for basting. Add the chicken and water to the rest and simmer, covered, for 20 minutes, turning once, until the chicken is almost cooked and coated in sauce.
  4. Grill the chicken over charcoal or under a broiler for 4 to 6 minutes per side, basting with the reserved paste, until charred and cooked through.
  5. Squeeze over lime juice and serve with rice and plecing kangkung (water spinach with sambal), as in Lombok, where this dish comes from.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the shrimp paste toasting step, which leaves it tasting sharp and overly fishy instead of rounded.
  • Grilling over too high heat without moving pieces to indirect heat once charred, which risks burning the sugary marinade.

Authentic tips

  • This dish is meant to be seriously spicy — adjust chili quantity to taste, but expect real heat if following a traditional ratio.
  • Toasting the shrimp paste (wrapped in foil, in a dry pan) mellows its raw fishiness into a deep savory note.

Substitutions

  • No shrimp paste: a splash of fish sauce is a reasonable, if less traditional, substitute.
  • Boneless thighs work but reduce grilling time by about a third.
  • No grill: roast on a wire rack over a sheet pan at 230°C (450°F) for 25 to 30 minutes, basting with the reserved paste halfway through, then broil 2 to 3 minutes for char; boneless thighs take about 18 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes well, marinated and raw, for up to a month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and plecing kangkung
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Es teh
