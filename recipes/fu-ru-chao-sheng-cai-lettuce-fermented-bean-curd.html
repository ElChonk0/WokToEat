Fǔ Rǔ Chǎo Shēng Cài (Lettuce Stir-Fried with Fermented Bean Curd) 腐乳炒生菜

▾

Surprise me with

Cooked lettuce is a Cantonese favorite; stir-fried with white fermented bean curd (fu yu) and garlic it becomes a savory, creamy side in two minutes.

  • 600 g1.3 lbRomaine or iceberg lettuce, in large pieces
  • 2 cubes2 cubesWhite fermented bean curd (fu yu)
  • 15 ml1 tbspFermented bean curd liquid
  • 4 cloves4 clovesGarlic, minced
  • 2 g1/2 tspSugar
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Wash the lettuce and spin it very dry.
  2. Mash the bean curd with its liquid and the sugar.
  3. Heat the oil in a flat-bottom wok over medium heat and fry the garlic for 20 seconds, then stir in the bean curd for 10 seconds.
  4. Turn the heat to high, add the lettuce and toss for 1 to 2 minutes until just wilted but still crisp at the stems.
  5. Serve straight away.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Wet lettuce waters down the sauce and stews.
  • Overcooking makes it limp and watery.

Authentic tips

  • Fu yu is salty, so no extra salt is needed.
  • The same sauce is classic with water spinach (ong choy).

Substitutions

  • Oyster sauce and garlic replace the bean curd for the other classic version.
  • Bok choy or choy sum in place of lettuce.

Storage

Leftovers: Best eaten at once.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice with a main dish
  • Soup: Tomato tofu soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
