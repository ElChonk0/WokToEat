Tom Yum Goong (Hot and Sour Shrimp Soup)

▾

Surprise me with

Tom yum goong, a hot and sour soup of shrimp, lemongrass, galangal and kaffir lime, is one of Thailand's most famous dishes; the clear and creamy versions both appear in Thai kitchens.

  • 30 g1 ozNam prik pao
  • 1 L4 cupsStock

Method

  1. Peel the shrimp, keeping the shells, and simmer the shells in the stock for 10 minutes; strain.
  2. Bring the stock to a boil with the lemongrass, galangal and lime leaves and simmer 8 minutes.
  3. Add the mushrooms and chilies and cook 2 minutes.
  4. Add the shrimp and cook 2-3 minutes until pink.
  5. Take off the heat and stir in the nam prik pao, fish sauce and lime juice.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling after adding the lime juice makes it bitter.
  • Overcooking the shrimp makes them rubbery.

Authentic tips

  • Add evaporated milk for the creamy nam khon version.
  • Do not eat the lemongrass, galangal or leaves.

Substitutions

  • Add evaporated milk for the creamy nam khon version.
  • Chicken replaces shrimp.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat gently.

Freezing: Freeze the broth for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Jasmine rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
