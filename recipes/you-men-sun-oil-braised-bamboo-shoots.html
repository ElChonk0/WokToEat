Yóu Mèn Sǔn (Oil-Braised Bamboo Shoots) 油焖笋

▾

You men sun, spring bamboo shoots braised in soy sauce and sugar until glossy, is a classic Shanghai and Jiangnan spring dish.

  • 600 g1.3 lbFresh spring bamboo shoots, cut
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 10 ml2 tspDark soy sauce
  • 20 g4 tspSugar
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 120 ml1/2 cupWater

Method

  1. Cut the shoots into wedges or chunks and boil 10 minutes to remove bitterness; drain.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok over high heat and fry the shoots 4-5 minutes until browned at the edges.
  3. Add the light and dark soy sauces, sugar and the water.
  4. Cover and braise on medium-low for 10 minutes.
  5. Uncover and reduce over high heat until glossy and sticky.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the blanching leaves the shoots bitter.
  • Too much sauce prevents glazing.

Authentic tips

  • Fresh spring shoots make the best dish; canned works in winter.
  • Serve warm or cold.

Substitutions

  • Canned bamboo shoots work; rinse and blanch them.
  • Add a little rock sugar to deepen the sweetness.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; serve cold.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Tofu soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
