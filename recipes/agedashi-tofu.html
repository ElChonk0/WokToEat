Agedashi Tofu 揚げ出し豆腐

▾

Agedashi tofu, lightly fried tofu in a savory broth, is a classic Japanese izakaya and home dish, with the crisp coating softening in the dashi.

Method

  1. Wrap the tofu in paper towels and press it under a plate for 15 minutes to remove water; dry tofu fries crisp and does not spatter. Cut it into 8 cubes.
  2. Simmer the dashi, soy sauce and mirin for 1 minute to make the broth, and keep it warm.
  3. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven to 170 C (340 F).
  4. Coat each cube thickly in potato starch just before it goes in the oil, so the coating stays dry. Fry 3 or 4 at a time for 2 to 3 minutes, turning, until crisp and very pale gold. Potato starch gives a thin, delicate crust that turns silky in the broth.
  5. Drain on a rack, set in shallow bowls and pour the warm broth around (not over) the tofu so the top stays crisp. Top with daikon, ginger, green onion and shichimi and serve at once.

Common mistakes

  • Wet tofu spatters and the coating won't crisp.
  • Pouring the broth over the tofu makes the coating soggy.

Authentic tips

  • Eat at once while the coating is still crisp at the edges.
  • Silken tofu is traditional but fragile.

Substitutions

  • Extra-firm tofu holds its shape better.
  • Bake at 220 C (425 F) after coating for a lighter version.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the coating softens.

Freezing: Not recommended.
