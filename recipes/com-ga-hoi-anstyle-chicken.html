Cơm Gà Hội An–Style Chicken

▾

Surprise me with

Hoi An's signature chicken rice dish poaches and shreds chicken, then serves it over rice cooked with turmeric and the poaching fat, topped with fresh herbs and a sharp chili-lime dipping sauce.

Method

  1. Put the chicken in the water with the ginger, shallots and salt, bring to a bare simmer and poach for 20 to 25 minutes until cooked through. Cool it in ice water so the skin stays firm, and keep the broth.
  2. Heat the chicken fat in a pot and fry the minced shallot and garlic for 1 minute, then the turmeric for 10 seconds. Add the rice and stir for 2 minutes until golden and coated.
  3. Add 400 ml (1 2/3 cups) of the chicken broth and the pandan, bring to a boil, then cover and cook over very low heat for 15 minutes. Rest for 10 minutes. Turmeric gives Hoi An chicken rice its golden color.
  4. Shred the chicken by hand and toss it with the pickled onion, rau ram, lime juice, fish sauce, sugar and pepper.
  5. Serve the yellow rice with the chicken salad, shredded cabbage, herbs, a bowl of the broth and a spoonful of Hoi An chili sauce made from minced chili and lime.

Common mistakes

  • Shredding the chicken while too hot, which can make the pieces stringy rather than clean and tender.
  • Cooking the rice in plain water instead of the flavorful poaching liquid.

Authentic tips

  • The turmeric gives the rice its signature golden color and adds a subtle earthy flavor — don't skip it.
  • A little of the rendered chicken skin fat, sautéed with the shallot, adds real depth to the rice.

Substitutions

  • A whole chicken can replace thighs for a more traditional presentation.
  • No Vietnamese mint: regular mint or extra cilantro both work as a substitute.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; store components separately.

Freezing: The poaching stock freezes well; chicken and rice are best fresh.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled papaya, herbs and ginger fish sauce
  • Soup: Clear chicken broth
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
