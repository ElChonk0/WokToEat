Jiān Máo Dòu Fu (Pan-Fried Fuzzy Tofu) 煎毛豆腐

▾

Surprise me with

Mao doufu, "hairy tofu," is tofu fermented until it grows a fine white mold, then pan-fried; it is a signature of Huizhou in Anhui and a regional delicacy.

  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 60 ml4 tbspNeutral oil
  • 30 ml2 tbspChili sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 30 g2 tbspScallion
  • 15 g1 tbspGarlic

Method

  1. Mix the chili sauce, soy sauce and garlic in a small bowl.
  2. Pat the mao doufu dry with paper towels.
  3. Heat the oil in a nonstick pan over medium heat and fry 4 minutes per side until golden and crisp.
  4. Pour the sauce over or serve alongside; top with scallion.

Common mistakes

  • Wet tofu spatters in the oil.
  • Cooking too fast burns the outside before the inside is warm.

Authentic tips

  • Fresh mao doufu has a fine white fuzz; avoid any with dark or colored mold.
  • Mao doufu is sold at Chinese markets, or use stinky tofu as a stand-in.

Substitutions

  • Stinky tofu replaces mao doufu.
  • Firm tofu with fermented bean curd approximates the flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.
