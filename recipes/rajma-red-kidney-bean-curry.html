Rajma (Kidney Bean Curry)

▾

Surprise me with

Rajma chawal, kidney beans in tomato gravy with rice, is the comfort food of Punjab and Delhi and a Sunday lunch tradition.

Method

  1. Fry the onion in the ghee until golden, 8 minutes.
  2. Add the ginger, garlic and rajma masala and fry 1 minute.
  3. Add the tomato and salt and cook 8 minutes until the oil separates.
  4. Add the beans and 300 ml water and simmer 25 minutes.
  5. Mash a few beans to thicken and finish with the cream and cilantro.

Common mistakes

  • Under-cooked tomatoes leave the gravy sour.
  • Canned beans need less time; reduce the simmer.

Authentic tips

  • Soak and cook dried beans for the best texture.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • Canned kidney beans replace cooked; reduce the simmer.
  • A pressure cooker cuts dried-bean cooking to 30 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; better the next day.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice (rajma chawal)
  • Soup: Cucumber raita
  • Dessert: Kheer
  • Beverage: Masala chai
