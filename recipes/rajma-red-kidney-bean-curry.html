Rajma (Kidney Bean Curry)

▾

Surprise me with

Rajma chawal, kidney beans in tomato gravy with rice, is the comfort food of Punjab and Delhi and a Sunday lunch tradition.

Method

  1. If cooking dried beans, soak them overnight and pressure-cook or simmer until completely soft; undercooked kidney beans are unsafe, so boil them hard for at least 10 minutes. Keep 400 ml (1.75 cups) of the cooking liquid.
  2. Heat the ghee in a heavy pot over medium heat and fry the cumin and bay leaf for 20 seconds, then the onions for 12 to 15 minutes until deep golden.
  3. Add the ginger-garlic paste and chilies for 1 minute, then the tomato puree, coriander, chili, turmeric and salt. Cook for 8 to 10 minutes until the masala is thick and the oil separates.
  4. Add the beans and their liquid and mash a few beans against the pot to thicken. Simmer gently for 20 to 25 minutes, until creamy.
  5. Stir in the garam masala, crushed kasuri methi and cream and garnish with cilantro. Rajma chawal (with rice) is a beloved Punjabi Sunday lunch.

Common mistakes

  • Under-cooked tomatoes leave the gravy sour.
  • Canned beans need less time; reduce the simmer.

Authentic tips

  • Soak and cook dried beans for the best texture.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • Canned kidney beans replace cooked; reduce the simmer.
  • A pressure cooker cuts dried-bean cooking to 30 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; better the next day.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.
