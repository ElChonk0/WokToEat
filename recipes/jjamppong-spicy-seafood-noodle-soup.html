Jjamppong (Spicy Seafood Noodle Soup) 짬뽕

▾

Surprise me with

Jjamppong, a fiery red noodle soup full of seafood and stir-fried vegetables, is a Korean-Chinese restaurant staple developed in Korea from Chinese immigrants' cooking, and the usual partner to jjajangmyeon.

Method

  1. Cook the noodles according to the packet, rinse them under warm water and drain shortly before serving. Cut the squid into rings or strips no smaller than bite size, as it shrinks. Scrub the mussels.
  2. Heat the oil in a large Dutch oven over high heat and fry the ginger, garlic, green onion and gochugaru for about 1 minute, until the oil turns red and smells smoky. This chili oil is the backbone of jjamppong's broth.
  3. Add the pork and stir-fry for about 2 minutes until nearly cooked, then add the onion, cabbage, carrot and zucchini with a pinch of salt and stir-fry for 2 minutes until they start to soften and char at the edges.
  4. Pour in the stock, soy sauce and oyster sauce and bring to a boil. Boil for 3 to 4 minutes until the vegetables are just tender.
  5. Add the mussels or clams first, as they take longest, then the shrimp and squid. Boil for 2 to 3 minutes, until the shells open and the shrimp turn pink. Discard any shells that stay shut and season with salt.
  6. Divide the noodles among large bowls and ladle the piping hot soup and seafood over.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the chili flakes makes the soup bitter; keep the heat medium for that step.
  • Overcooking the squid makes it rubbery.

Authentic tips

  • The smoky "wok hei" comes from charring the vegetables over high heat before adding the stock.
  • On induction, fry the vegetables in two batches so the pot stays hot.

Substitutions

  • Frozen seafood mix works well.
  • Udon noodles replace jjajangmyeon noodles.

Storage

Leftovers: Keep the soup and noodles separate for up to 2 days.

Freezing: The soup base without seafood freezes for 2 months.
