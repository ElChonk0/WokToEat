Doenjang Dakgui (Soybean Paste Grilled Chicken) 된장 닭구이

Marinating chicken in doenjang, the Korean fermented soybean paste, before grilling is a homestyle alternative to soy or chili marinades.

  • Prep 1 hr
  • Cook 25 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Korea Chicken LunchDinnerGrilled / roasted

  • 800 g1.75 lbChicken thighs, boneless skin-on
  • 45 g3 tbspDoenjang (Korean soybean paste)
  • 30 ml2 tbspRice syrup or honey
  • 30 ml2 tbspMirin
  • 4 cloves4 clovesGarlic, minced
  • 10 g2 tspGinger, grated
  • 15 ml1 tbspSesame oil
  • 1 g1/2 tspGround black pepper
  • 2 stalks2 stalksGreen onion, sliced

Method

  1. Mix the doenjang, syrup, mirin, garlic, ginger, sesame oil and pepper, coat the chicken and marinate 1 hour or overnight.
  2. Heat a cast-iron skillet or grill pan over medium heat.
  3. Cook the chicken skin-side down for 8 to 10 minutes until the skin is crisp and deep brown, pressing it flat.
  4. Turn and cook 5 to 6 minutes more until it reaches 74 C (165 F). Lower the heat if the marinade darkens too fast.
  5. Rest 3 minutes, slice and scatter with green onion.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • High heat burns the doenjang and syrup before the chicken cooks.
  • Moving the chicken too soon tears the skin.

Authentic tips

  • Oven or broiler: roast at 220 C (425 F) for 20 to 25 minutes on a rack.
  • Wrap in lettuce with rice and ssamjang.

Substitutions

  • Pork shoulder slices work with the same marinade.
  • Miso replaces doenjang for a milder flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freezes raw in the marinade for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Lettuce wraps and rice
  • Soup: Doenjang jjigae
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
