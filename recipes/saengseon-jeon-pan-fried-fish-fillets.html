Saengseon Jeon (Pan-Fried Fish Fillets) 생선전

Saengseon jeon, egg-battered white fish fillets, are a staple of Korean holiday tables during Chuseok and Seollal.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Korea Seafood LunchDinnerBread & pancakes30 min or lessEgg

  • 600 g1.3 lbWhite fish fillets
  • 60 g1/2 cupFlour
  • 33Eggs
  • 5 g1 tspSalt
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 60 ml4 tbspSoy-vinegar dip

Method

  1. Cut the fillets into 6 cm pieces and pat dry; season with the salt.
  2. Dredge in flour and shake off the excess.
  3. Dip in the beaten eggs.
  4. Pan-fry in the oil over medium heat for 2-3 minutes per side until golden.
  5. Serve with the dip.

Common mistakes

  • Wet fish sheds the batter.
  • High heat burns the egg before the fish cooks.

Authentic tips

  • Cod or flounder work well.
  • Eaten at Chuseok and Seollal.

Substitutions

  • Cod or flounder are traditional.
  • Zucchini slices add variety.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a pan.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Sikhye
