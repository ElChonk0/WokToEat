Gado-Gado (Indonesian Salad with Peanut Sauce)

▾

Surprise me with

Gado-gado, "mix-mix," is a salad of blanched vegetables, tofu, tempeh and egg under a peanut sauce, popular across Indonesia and often called the country's national salad.

Method

  1. Make the peanut sauce: blend or pound the peanuts, garlic and chilies, then mix with the palm sugar, tamarind, kecap manis, salt and warm water until it is thick and smooth. Add lime juice to taste. In Jakarta it is ground to order on a stone mortar.
  2. Blanch the green beans for 2 minutes, the cabbage and spinach for 1 minute and the bean sprouts for 20 seconds, cooling each in cold water so they stay crisp and bright.
  3. Fry the tofu and tempeh in the oil over medium-high heat for 3 to 4 minutes per side until golden. Drain.
  4. Arrange the potatoes, vegetables, cucumber, tofu, tempeh and halved eggs on a platter.
  5. Pour the peanut sauce over just before serving and top with fried shallots and krupuk.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooked vegetables go limp.
  • Thick sauce is hard to pour; thin with water.

Authentic tips

  • Serve at room temperature.
  • Add lontong rice cakes.

Substitutions

  • Peanut butter plus coconut milk replaces homemade sauce.
  • Omit the egg for a vegan dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the sauce keeps 4 days separately.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Lontong and krupuk
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Es teh
