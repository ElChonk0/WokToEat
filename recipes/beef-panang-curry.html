Beef Panang Curry

▾

Surprise me with

The beef version of Thailand's thick, nutty panang curry pairs well with beef's heartier flavor, simmered until tender in a rich curry paste and coconut cream reduction.

  • 60 g1/4 cupPanang curry paste
  • 30 g3 tbspRoasted peanuts, finely ground
  • 20 ml4 tspFish sauce
  • 20 g1.5 tbspPalm sugar
  • 66Kaffir lime leaves, very finely shredded

Method

  1. Simmer the beef in half the coconut milk and 250 ml (1 cup) water in a covered Dutch oven over low heat for 1 to 1.5 hours, until tender. Chuck needs this slow cook; reserve the liquid.
  2. Heat the remaining coconut milk, thickest part first, in a flat-bottom wok or skillet over medium heat and simmer, stirring, for 3 to 5 minutes, until it thickens and the oil starts to separate ('cracking'). Fry the curry paste in it for 2 to 3 minutes, stirring constantly, until deeply fragrant and the oil turns red. Frying the paste in separated coconut fat is what develops a Thai curry's flavor.
  3. Stir in the ground peanuts, then the fish sauce and palm sugar, and cook for 1 minute until the sauce is thick, glossy and sweet-salty.
  4. Add the beef with a splash of its cooking liquid and simmer for 3 to 5 minutes. Panang should be thick and clinging, not soupy.
  5. Stir in most of the shredded lime leaves, spoon a little coconut cream over the top and garnish with the rest of the lime leaves, red chili and basil. Serve with jasmine rice.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling the curry hard, which can cause the coconut milk to separate into an oily texture.
  • Rushing the beef braise, since chuck needs real time to become properly tender.

Authentic tips

  • Panang should be noticeably thicker than red or green curry — don't thin it out with extra liquid.
  • This curry, like many braises, tastes even better the next day.

Substitutions

  • Short rib adds richness.
  • A pressure cooker cuts the simmer to 45 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; thin with coconut milk.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Jasmine rice
  • Soup: Cucumber salad
  • Dessert: Mango sticky rice
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
