Haw Mok Pla (Steamed Fish Curry Custard)

▾

Surprise me with

Haw mok is a Thai steamed curry custard of fish, red curry paste and coconut milk, traditionally cooked in banana-leaf cups.

Method

  1. Whisk the curry paste, coconut milk, eggs, fish sauce and rice flour until smooth.
  2. Fold in the fish and chopped lime leaves.
  3. Line small bowls or banana-leaf cups with cabbage or basil leaves.
  4. Fill with the mixture.
  5. Steam over medium heat for 20 minutes until the custard is just set.

Common mistakes

  • Steaming too hard makes it curdled and spongy.
  • Too much liquid keeps it from setting.

Authentic tips

  • Gentle steam gives a smooth texture.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • Ramekins replace banana leaf cups.
  • Shrimp and fish both work.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; re-steam gently.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Jasmine rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
