Fish Soup Bee Hoon (Sliced Fish or Fish Head)

▾

Surprise me with

Fish soup bee hoon, rice vermicelli in a milky ginger fish broth, is a Singapore hawker and zi char staple descended from Teochew fish soup; stalls offer it with sliced fish or with fried fish head.

Method

  1. Toss the fish slices with the cornstarch and salt. If using the fish head, blanch it in boiling water for 2 minutes and rinse.
  2. Heat the oil in a large pot over medium heat and fry the ginger and garlic for 1 minute, then the fish head for 2 to 3 minutes until lightly golden. Splash in the wine and add the stock, salted mustard greens and tomato. Boil hard for 15 to 20 minutes; a vigorous boil emulsifies the fish fat and turns the broth milky and rich.
  3. Soak the bee hoon in warm water for 15 minutes, then blanch it with the choy sum and divide among bowls.
  4. Strain the broth if you like, stir in the evaporated milk and season with the salt and pepper. Keep it at a gentle simmer from now on.
  5. Slide the fish slices into the simmering broth and poach for 1 to 2 minutes, just until opaque. Ladle the soup and fish over the noodles and top with fried shallots and scallions.

Cook’s notes

Written for cod loin; the fish head is optional. Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling after the milk goes in can split it; keep it just below a boil from then on.
  • Overcooking the fish slices makes them tough; they need only a minute or two.

Authentic tips

  • A hard boil before the milk goes in, and browning the ginger (and fish head), is what turns the broth milky.
  • Ask the fishmonger to split the fish head and remove the gills; fish bones simmered in the stock do almost as well.

Substitutions

  • Haddock, snapper or grouper replace the cod; Singapore hawkers use batang (Spanish mackerel).
  • Ordinary milk or a little coconut milk replaces evaporated milk.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day in the refrigerator with the broth and noodles stored separately.

Freezing: The broth freezes for 1 month; cook the fish and noodles fresh.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sliced chilies in soy sauce
  • Soup: The dish is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot Chinese tea
