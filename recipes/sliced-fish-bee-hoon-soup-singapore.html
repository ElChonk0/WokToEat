Sliced Fish Bee Hoon Soup

Sliced fish bee hoon is a Singapore hawker dish of thin rice vermicelli and fish slices in a milky ginger broth, descended from Teochew fish soup.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 25 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

  • 400 g14 ozCod loin, sliced 5 mm thick
  • 10 g1 tbspCornstarch
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt, for the fish
  • 200 g7 ozRice vermicelli (bee hoon)
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil
  • 30 g1 ozGinger, sliced
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 1.4 L6 cupsFish or chicken stock
  • 100 ml1/3 cupEvaporated milk
  • 150 g5 ozChoy sum, in lengths
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt, for the soup
  • 1 g1/4 tspWhite pepper
  • 15 g1/2 ozFried shallots
  • 20 g3/4 ozScallions, sliced

Method

  1. Soak the vermicelli in hot water 10 minutes, drain, and divide among 4 bowls. Toss the fish slices with the cornstarch and 3 g salt.
  2. Heat the oil in a pot over medium-high heat and fry the ginger for 1 minute until golden, then add the wine and stock.
  3. Bring to a boil, simmer 10 minutes, then add the evaporated milk, soup salt and white pepper.
  4. Add the choy sum for 1 minute, then slide in the fish and cook 1 to 2 minutes until opaque.
  5. Ladle over the noodles, top with the fried shallots and scallions, and serve with sliced chilies in soy sauce.

Cook’s notes

Written for cod loin. Singapore hawkers use batang or grouper.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling after the milk goes in can curdle it; keep it just below a boil.
  • Overcooking the fish makes it tough.

Authentic tips

  • Fry the ginger until golden for the characteristic flavor.
  • Fresh fish bones simmered in the stock make it richer.

Substitutions

  • Haddock replaces the cod.
  • Ordinary milk replaces evaporated milk.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day in the refrigerator without the noodles.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sliced chilies in soy sauce
  • Soup: The recipe is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot Chinese tea
