Shā Guō Yú Tóu Dòu Fu (Claypot Fish Head and Tofu) 砂锅鱼头豆腐

▾

Surprise me with

Fish head simmered with tofu in a clay casserole is a winter comfort dish across the Huizhou and Yangtze region, valued for its milky, collagen-rich broth.

  • Cook 35 min

Method

  1. Cut the fish head in half and pat dry. Cut the tofu into 3 cm cubes.
  2. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven or heavy pot over high heat and sear the fish head 2 minutes per side until golden.
  3. Add the ginger, wine, ham and 1.2 L boiling water; bring to a boil and simmer hard 20 minutes until the broth turns milky.
  4. Add the tofu and simmer 10 minutes.
  5. Season with the salt and garnish with scallion.

Common mistakes

  • Using cold water prevents the milky color.
  • Skipping the sear gives a thin, fishy broth.

Authentic tips

  • A vigorous simmer emulsifies the oil into the broth.
  • Ham adds sweetness.

Substitutions

  • Fish bones plus fillets replace the head.
  • Prosciutto replaces ham.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freeze the broth only, for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; the casserole is its own soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
