Shā Guō Yú Tóu Dòu Fu (Claypot Fish Head and Tofu) 砂锅鱼头豆腐

▾

Fish head simmered with tofu in a clay casserole is a winter comfort dish across the Huizhou and Yangtze region, valued for its milky, collagen-rich broth.

  • Cook 45 min

  • 1 g1/2 tspWhite pepper
  • 10 g1/3 ozCilantro

Method

  1. Rinse the fish head, scrape away the dark membrane, rub with salt and pat dry.
  2. Fry the head in the oil over medium-high heat for 3 minutes per side until golden. Add the ginger, green onion and ham.
  3. Pour the Shaoxing wine around the edge, then the boiling water, and boil hard for 15 minutes until milky.
  4. Transfer to a clay pot (sha guo) if you have one, add the tofu and shiitake and simmer gently for 20 minutes.
  5. Season with salt and white pepper and serve bubbling in the pot with cilantro.

Common mistakes

  • Using cold water prevents the milky color.
  • Skipping the sear gives a thin, fishy broth.

Authentic tips

  • A vigorous simmer emulsifies the oil into the broth.
  • Ham adds sweetness.

Substitutions

  • Fish bones plus fillets replace the head.
  • Prosciutto replaces ham.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freeze the broth only, for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; the casserole is its own soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
