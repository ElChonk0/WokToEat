Chengdu-Style Beef with Celery 芹菜炒牛肉

▾

Surprise me with

A simple, everyday Chengdu stir-fry that pairs quickly seared beef with celery's clean crunch, seasoned lightly to let both ingredients shine — a common home-cooking dish rather than a restaurant showpiece.

Method

  1. Toss the beef with the marinade soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, cornstarch and 1 tablespoon of water and leave for 15 minutes.
  2. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a flat-bottom wok over high heat until shimmering. Stir-fry the beef for 1 minute, until it changes color but is still slightly pink, and lift it out.
  3. Add the remaining oil and lower the heat to medium. Fry the doubanjiang for 30 seconds until the oil turns red, then the garlic, ginger and dried chilies for 20 seconds.
  4. Raise the heat to high, add the celery and stir-fry for 1 minute until bright green and still crisp.
  5. Return the beef with the sauce soy sauce and sugar and toss for 30 seconds until glossy. Sprinkle with ground Sichuan pepper and serve with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the celery, which loses its characteristic crisp bite.
  • Overcooking the beef, which turns it tough — sear it quickly and remove promptly.

Authentic tips

  • This dish is intentionally simple — resist the urge to add extra sauces or seasonings beyond the core ingredients.
  • Slicing the celery diagonally exposes more surface area for quick, even cooking.

Substitutions

  • Chicken or pork can replace the beef.
  • Bell pepper adds color alongside the celery.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat quickly so the celery stays crisp.

Freezing: Not recommended; celery texture suffers.
