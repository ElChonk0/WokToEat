Húnán Niú Ròu Mǐ Fěn (Hunan Beef Rice Noodles) 湖南牛肉米粉

▾

Surprise me with

Mi fen, rice noodles in a spicy beef broth, are the breakfast of Hunan; beef versions are particularly popular in Changsha and Changde.

  • Cook 3 hr

  • 500 g1.1 lbBeef brisket
  • 800 g1.75 lbBeef bones
  • 3 L12 cupsWater
  • 40 g1.5 ozGinger and green onion
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil
  • 12 g1/2 ozStar anise, cinnamon, bay leaves and dried chilies
  • 20 g1 tbspDoubanjiang
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 10 ml2 tspDark soy sauce
  • 10 g2 tspRock sugar
  • 6 g1 tspSalt
  • 600 g1.3 lbFresh round rice noodles (mi fen)
  • 30 ml2 tbspChili oil
  • 60 g2 ozPickled long beans, chopped
  • 40 g1.5 ozGreen onion and cilantro

Method

  1. Blanch the brisket and bones for 10 minutes, rinse, and simmer the bones in the water with the ginger and green onion for 2 hours to make a clear stock. Strain.
  2. Meanwhile, heat the oil in a Dutch oven and fry the spices for 1 minute, then the doubanjiang until red. Add the brisket, soy sauces, sugar and 1 L (4 cups) of the stock and braise, covered, for 1.5 to 2 hours until tender. This is the topping (ma zi).
  3. Season the remaining stock with salt and a ladle of the braising liquid.
  4. Blanch the rice noodles in boiling water for 30 seconds to 1 minute until hot and soft, and divide among bowls.
  5. Ladle over the hot stock, top with sliced brisket and its sauce, and finish with chili oil, pickled long beans, green onion and cilantro. Changsha's breakfast noodle.

Common mistakes

  • A weak broth makes a flat bowl; simmer the bones fully.
  • Overcooking the noodles turns them mushy.

Authentic tips

  • Add a few slices of ginger and a dried chili to the broth for depth.
  • Serve with extra chili oil, vinegar and pickles at the table.

Substitutions

  • Beef shank replaces the brisket with a longer braise.
  • Ready-made beef stock saves the first hour.

Storage

Leftovers: Store the noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 4 days.

Freezing: Freeze the broth and meat for 2 months; cook fresh noodles.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled long beans
  • Soup: Not needed; the noodle soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
