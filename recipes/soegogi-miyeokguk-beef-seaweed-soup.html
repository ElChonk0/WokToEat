Soegogi Miyeokguk (Beef Seaweed Soup) 소고기 미역국

Miyeokguk, seaweed soup with beef, is eaten by Korean mothers after giving birth and by everyone on their birthday morning, in memory of that first meal.

  • Cook 35 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 25 g1 ozDried miyeok (wakame) seaweed
  • 250 g9 ozBeef brisket or chuck, thinly sliced
  • 15 ml1 tbspSesame oil
  • 3 cloves3 clovesGarlic, minced
  • 30 ml2 tbspSoup soy sauce (guk-ganjang)
  • 1.8 L7.5 cupsWater
  • 5 ml1 tspFish sauce
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt

Method

  1. Soak the seaweed in cold water for 20 minutes until expanded, rinse, squeeze and cut into bite-size pieces.
  2. Heat the sesame oil in a pot over medium heat and fry the beef for 2 minutes until it loses its color.
  3. Add the seaweed, garlic and half the soup soy sauce and stir-fry 3 minutes until the seaweed smells nutty.
  4. Add the water, bring to a boil and simmer 25 to 30 minutes until the broth turns milky and rich.
  5. Season with the remaining soup soy sauce, fish sauce and salt.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the stir-fry step gives a thin, grassy soup.
  • Using regular soy sauce darkens the soup and makes it sweet.

Authentic tips

  • It tastes even better reheated the next day.
  • Korean families make a big pot on birthdays and serve it with rice and banchan.

Substitutions

  • Mussels, clams or dried anchovies replace beef for coastal versions.
  • Light soy sauce plus extra salt replaces soup soy sauce.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice, kimchi and japchae
  • Soup: This is the soup
  • Dessert: Sliced fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
