Soegogi Miyeokguk (Beef Seaweed Soup) 소고기 미역국

▾

Surprise me with

Miyeokguk, seaweed soup with beef, is eaten by Korean mothers after giving birth and by everyone on their birthday morning, in memory of that first meal.

Method

  1. Soak the seaweed in cold water for 10 to 20 minutes until soft and expanded, then rinse it twice, squeeze it and cut it into bite-size pieces. Do not soak it much longer, or it loses flavor.
  2. Mix the beef with the beef soup soy sauce, garlic and pepper.
  3. Heat the sesame oil in a large pot over medium-high heat and sauté the beef for 2 to 3 minutes until it is no longer red.
  4. Add the seaweed and the seaweed soup soy sauce and sauté for 4 to 5 minutes, stirring. Frying the seaweed in sesame oil with the beef builds the rich, nutty base of the soup.
  5. Add the water, bring to a boil and skim. Cover and simmer over medium-low heat for 20 to 30 minutes, until the beef is tender and the broth turns slightly milky. Season with the fish sauce and salt.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the stir-fry step gives a thin, grassy soup.
  • Using regular soy sauce darkens the soup and makes it sweet.

Authentic tips

  • It tastes even better reheated the next day.
  • Korean families make a big pot on birthdays and serve it with rice and banchan.

Substitutions

  • Mussels, clams or dried anchovies replace beef for coastal versions.
  • Light soy sauce plus extra salt replaces soup soy sauce.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.
