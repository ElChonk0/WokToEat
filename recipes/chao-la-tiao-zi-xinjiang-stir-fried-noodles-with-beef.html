Chǎo Lā Tiáo Zi (Xinjiang Stir-Fried Hand-Pulled Noodles with Beef) 炒拉条子

▾

Surprise me with

Chao la tiao zi, hand-pulled noodles stir-fried with beef and vegetables, is a Xinjiang noodle staple closely related to laghman but fried instead of served in sauce.

  • Cook 20 min

  • 350 g12 ozHand-pulled or wheat noodles, fresh
  • 350 g12 ozBeef, sliced
  • 400 g14 ozPeppers, tomatoes, cabbage
  • 100 g3.5 ozOnion and garlic
  • 3 g1 tspCumin
  • 30 ml2 tbspSoy sauce
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Boil the noodles 3 minutes until nearly done; drain and toss with a little oil.
  2. Heat half the oil in a wok over high heat and sear the beef 2 minutes; remove.
  3. Add the remaining oil, onion and garlic and stir 1 minute, then the vegetables and stir-fry 3 minutes.
  4. Add the noodles, beef, soy sauce and cumin and toss on high heat 2 minutes.
  5. Serve at once.

Common mistakes

  • Wet noodles stick and steam; drain and oil them.
  • A crowded wok steams the noodles.

Authentic tips

  • Cook in two batches for a good char.
  • Lamb is traditional.

Substitutions

  • Lamb is the traditional meat.
  • Fresh udon replaces hand-pulled noodles.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok with a splash of water.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled vegetables
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Hami melon
  • Beverage: Black tea
