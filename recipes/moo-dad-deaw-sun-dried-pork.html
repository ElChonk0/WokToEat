Moo Dad Deaw (Thai Sun-Dried Pork) หมูแดดเดียว

▾

Surprise me with

Moo dad deaw, pork marinated and dried in the sun for a few hours before frying, is a northeastern Thai (Isaan) specialty.

  • Prep 20 min (plus 4 hr marinating and drying)
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Pound the garlic, coriander root and pepper into a paste and mix with the soy sauce, oyster sauce and sugar.
  2. Marinate the pork slices 2 hours.
  3. Dry on a rack in the sun for 2 hours, or in an oven at 80 C (175 F) for 1 hour with the door ajar.
  4. Fry in oil at 170 C (340 F) for 3-4 minutes until golden and chewy.
  5. Serve with sticky rice and a spicy tamarind dipping sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Over-drying makes it leathery.
  • Hot oil burns the sugar; fry in small batches.

Authentic tips

  • Pork shoulder with a little fat is best.
  • Serve with jaew dipping sauce.

Substitutions

  • An oven at 80 C (175 F) for 1 hour replaces sun-drying.
  • Pork neck gives the best texture.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot oven.

Freezing: Freezes raw marinated slices for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sticky rice and jaew dipping sauce
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
