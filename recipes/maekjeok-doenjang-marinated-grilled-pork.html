Maekjeok (Doenjang-Marinated Grilled Pork) 맥적

Maekjeok, pork marinated in soybean paste and grilled, is described in old texts as a dish of the ancient Goguryeo kingdom and is considered an ancestor of Korean barbecue.

  • Cook 15 min

  • 700 g1.5 lbPork shoulder or collar, sliced 1 cm thick
  • 45 g3 tbspDoenjang (Korean soybean paste)
  • 30 ml2 tbspRice syrup or honey
  • 15 ml1 tbspMirin
  • 10 ml2 tspLight soy sauce
  • 4 cloves4 clovesGarlic, minced
  • 2 stalks2 stalksGreen onion, finely chopped
  • 15 ml1 tbspSesame oil
  • 1 g1/2 tspGround black pepper
  • 15 ml1 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Score the pork lightly on both sides.
  2. Mix all the marinade ingredients and coat the pork; marinate at least 1 hour.
  3. Heat the oil in a cast-iron skillet or grill pan over medium heat.
  4. Cook the pork 3 to 4 minutes per side until caramelized and cooked through, lowering the heat if the paste starts to burn.
  5. Rest 2 minutes, slice and serve with lettuce, rice and green onion salad.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Too much heat chars the doenjang black.
  • Thick slices stay pink inside before the outside is done.

Authentic tips

  • Oven or broiler: broil 4 to 5 minutes per side on a rack.
  • Perilla leaves are the classic wrap.

Substitutions

  • Pork belly slices make a richer version.
  • Chicken thighs work.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freezes raw in the marinade for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Lettuce, perilla leaves and pa-muchim (green onion salad)
  • Soup: Doenjang jjigae
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Makgeolli
