Maekjeok (Doenjang-Marinated Grilled Pork) 맥적

▾

Surprise me with

Maekjeok, pork marinated in soybean paste and grilled, is described in old texts as a dish of the ancient Goguryeo kingdom and is considered an ancestor of Korean barbecue.

  • Prep 15 min (plus 30 min marinating)
  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 25 g1.5 tbspDoenjang (Korean soybean paste)
  • 15 ml1 tbspSoy sauce
  • 30 ml2 tbspRice wine or mirin

  • 30 g2 tbspOnion, grated

  • 15 ml1 tbspSesame oil

Method

  1. Slice the pork thinly and evenly, partly freezing it first if slicing at home. Mix the doenjang, soy sauce, rice wine, sugar, syrup, onion, garlic, ginger, vinegar, sesame oil and pepper until smooth.
  2. Coat the pork in the marinade and leave it for at least 30 minutes, or overnight. The doenjang tenderizes and gives a deep, savory, slightly smoky flavor once grilled.
  3. Stir-fry the chives in a little of the oil over high heat for 30 seconds, until just wilted, season with a pinch of salt and spread them on a platter.
  4. Heat a grill pan or heavy skillet over medium-high heat with the remaining oil. Add the pork in a single layer, then turn the heat down to medium. The doenjang and sugar scorch easily at high heat.
  5. Cook for 1 to 2 minutes per side, turning a few times, until cooked through and lightly caramelized at the edges. Pile the pork on the chives and serve with lettuce, perilla leaves and ssamjang.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Too much heat chars the doenjang black.
  • Thick slices stay pink inside before the outside is done.

Authentic tips

  • Oven or broiler: broil 4 to 5 minutes per side on a rack.
  • Perilla leaves are the classic wrap.

Substitutions

  • Pork belly slices make a richer version.
  • Chicken thighs work.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freezes raw in the marinade for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Lettuce, perilla leaves and pa-muchim (green onion salad)
  • Soup: Doenjang jjigae
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Makgeolli
