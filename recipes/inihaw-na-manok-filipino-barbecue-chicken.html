Inihaw na Manok (Filipino Barbecued Chicken)

▾

Surprise me with

Inihaw na manok, chicken grilled over charcoal with a calamansi, soy and banana ketchup marinade, is a staple Filipino street food and weekend dish.

  • Prep 15 min (plus overnight marinating)
  • Cook 40 min

Method

  1. Mix the soy sauce, calamansi, banana ketchup, soda, garlic, sugar and pepper. Slash the chicken to the bone, coat it in the marinade and refrigerate overnight.
  2. Pour a third of the marinade into a small pan with the basting ketchup and oil and simmer for 5 minutes until slightly thick. This is the basting sauce; boiling makes it safe after contact with raw chicken.
  3. Grill the chicken over medium charcoal or a medium grill for 30 to 40 minutes, turning every 5 minutes, until cooked through (74 C / 165 F).
  4. Baste generously in the last 10 minutes, turning, until sticky, red and charred at the edges. Basting earlier burns the sugary sauce.
  5. Serve with rice, atchara (pickled papaya) and spiced vinegar.

Common mistakes

  • Basting with raw marinade is unsafe; boil it first.
  • Grilling over high heat burns the sugars.

Authentic tips

  • Lemongrass in the marinade is the Bacolod style.
  • Serve with atchara (pickled papaya).

Substitutions

  • Boneless thighs shorten the grilling.
  • Lemongrass in the marinade is the Bacolod style.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat in an oven.

Freezing: Freezes the marinated raw chicken for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Garlic rice and atchara
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh mango
  • Beverage: Calamansi juice
