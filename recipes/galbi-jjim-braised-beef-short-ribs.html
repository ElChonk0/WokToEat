Galbi-jjim (Braised Beef Short Ribs) 갈비찜

▾

Surprise me with

Galbi-jjim, short ribs braised in soy, pear and garlic with chestnuts and jujubes, is a Korean celebration dish served at Chuseok, Lunar New Year and family birthdays.

Method

  1. Soak the ribs in cold water for 30 minutes to draw out the blood, then drain. Trim off the thick outer fat.
  2. Bring 1.2 L (5 cups) of water to a boil with the parboil aromatics, add the ribs and skim off the foam, then boil for 10 minutes over medium-high heat. Lift out the ribs and strain the stock. Chill or skim it to remove the fat and keep 600 ml (2.5 cups). Parboiling rather than searing is the traditional way and gives a clean, glossy sauce.
  3. Mix the soy sauce, sugar, honey, rice wine, pear, grated onion, garlic, ginger and pepper and toss the ribs in it.
  4. Put the ribs, sauce and reserved stock in a Dutch oven, bring to a boil, then cover and boil gently over medium heat for 25 to 30 minutes.
  5. Add the radish, carrot, mushrooms, chestnuts and jujubes, cover and cook for about 20 minutes more. Adding the vegetables halfway keeps them whole rather than mushy.
  6. Uncover and boil for about 10 minutes, spooning the sauce over, until it has reduced to a glossy glaze and the meat is tender enough to pull from the bone but still holds together. Turn off the heat and stir in the sesame oil and green onion.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the soak and blanch gives a murky, greasy sauce.
  • Adding the vegetables at the start turns them to mush.

Authentic tips

  • Make it a day ahead and chill it; the fat sets on top and lifts off easily.
  • Pressure cooker: 35 minutes on high, then add the vegetables for 5 minutes more.

Substitutions

  • Beef chuck chunks work if you cannot get short ribs.
  • No pear: grated apple, or a little extra sugar plus kiwi for tenderizing.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days in the refrigerator; it improves overnight.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice, kimchi and japchae
  • Soup: Miyeok guk (seaweed soup)
  • Dessert: Sikhye (rice punch)
  • Beverage: Makgeolli
