Dotorimuk Muchim (Acorn Jelly Salad) 도토리묵무침

Dotorimuk, a jelly made from acorn starch, is a traditional Korean mountain food seasoned with soy sauce and sesame and served as a side.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 0 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 400 g14 ozAcorn jelly, sliced
  • 150 g5 ozLettuce and cucumber
  • 30 ml2 tbspSoy sauce
  • 10 g2 tspGochugaru
  • 15 ml1 tbspSesame oil
  • 15 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 10 g1 tbspSesame seeds

Method

  1. Cut the acorn jelly into 1 cm strips.
  2. Mix the soy sauce, gochugaru, sesame oil and garlic.
  3. Toss with the jelly gently.
  4. Add the lettuce and cucumber.
  5. Sprinkle with sesame seeds.

Common mistakes

  • Rough tossing breaks the jelly.
  • Dressing too early makes it soggy.

Authentic tips

  • Serve cold as a side.
  • Acorn jelly is sold at Korean markets.

Substitutions

  • Buckwheat jelly replaces acorn.
  • Add a few drops of vinegar.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Makgeolli or barley tea
