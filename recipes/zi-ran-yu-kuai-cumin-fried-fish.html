Zī Rán Yú Kuài (Xinjiang Cumin Fried Fish) 孜然鱼块

Cumin is the signature spice of Xinjiang's grilled and fried dishes, usually applied to lamb but equally good on firm river fish.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Xinjiang Seafood LunchDinnerBraise / stewSpicy30 min or less

Ingredients

  • 700 g1.5 lbFirm fish, cut into chunks
  • 10 g1 tbspCumin seeds, ground
  • 6 g2 tspChili flakes
  • 6 g1 tspSalt
  • 40 g1/3 cupCornstarch
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 20 g2 tbspScallion
  • 60 ml4 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Pat the fish dry and toss with the salt, then coat in the cornstarch.
  2. Heat the oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat.
  3. Fry the fish 2-3 minutes per side until golden and crisp.
  4. Add the garlic and scallion for 15 seconds.
  5. Toss with the cumin and chili flakes and serve at once.

Common mistakes

  • Wet fish does not crisp.
  • Cumin burns if it goes in too early.

Authentic tips

  • Use a firm fish such as catfish.
  • Serve with lemon.

Substitutions

  • Catfish or cod are good choices.
  • Bake at 220 C (425 F) for a lighter version.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; recrisp in a hot oven.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Nang flatbread
  • Soup: Cucumber salad
  • Dessert: Lemon wedges
  • Beverage: Black tea
