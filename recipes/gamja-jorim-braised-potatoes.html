Gamja Jorim (Soy-Braised Potatoes) 감자조림

▾

Surprise me with

Gamja jorim, potatoes braised in sweet soy sauce, is a classic banchan on Korean tables.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 600 g1.3 lbPotatoes, cubed
  • 45 ml3 tbspSoy sauce
  • 20 g4 tspSugar
  • 15 ml1 tbspCorn syrup
  • 15 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 10 ml2 tspSesame oil
  • 250 ml1 cupWater

Method

  1. Cut the potatoes into 3 cm chunks and rinse.
  2. Bring the soy sauce, sugar, syrup and water to a simmer.
  3. Add the potatoes and garlic, cover and simmer 15 minutes.
  4. Uncover and cook over medium heat until the sauce is glossy and nearly gone.
  5. Finish with the sesame oil.

Common mistakes

  • Stirring too much breaks the potatoes.
  • Burning the sugar at the end.

Authentic tips

  • Add green chilies for heat.
  • A staple banchan; serve at room temperature.

Substitutions

  • Honey replaces corn syrup.
  • Add a green chili for heat.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; good cold.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
