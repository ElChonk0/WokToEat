Khao Pad Jay (Vegetarian Fried Rice)

▾

Surprise me with

Khao pad, Thai fried rice, is a staple of home cooks and street vendors; the vegetarian version swaps fish sauce and meat for soy sauce and tofu, as eaten during Thailand's vegetarian festival.

Method

  1. Use cold, day-old rice and break up the clumps.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok and fry the garlic and tofu 2 minutes.
  3. Add the rice, soy sauce and sugar and toss 3 minutes.
  4. Push aside and scramble the eggs, then mix in.
  5. Serve with lime and cucumber.

Common mistakes

  • Fresh rice clumps and gets mushy.
  • An overcrowded wok steams the rice.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with sliced chilies in soy sauce rather than fish sauce to keep it vegetarian.
  • Serve with lime.

Substitutions

  • Day-old rice is essential.
  • Add pineapple for a sweeter version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot pan.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Cucumber slices and lime
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
