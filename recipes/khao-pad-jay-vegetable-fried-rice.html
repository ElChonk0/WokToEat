Khao Pad Jay (Vegetarian Fried Rice)

▾

Surprise me with

Khao pad, Thai fried rice, is a staple of home cooks and street vendors; the vegetarian version swaps fish sauce and meat for soy sauce and tofu, as eaten during Thailand's vegetarian festival.

Method

  1. Break up the cold rice so no clumps remain. Day-old rice is drier and fries into separate grains. Mix the soy sauce, mushroom sauce and sugar.
  2. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a flat-bottom wok over high heat and fry the tofu for 3 to 4 minutes until golden. Push aside, add the garlic and onion for 30 seconds, then the broccoli or carrots for 1 minute.
  3. Push everything aside, add the remaining oil and the eggs and scramble until just set.
  4. Add the rice and toss over high heat for 2 to 3 minutes, pressing out clumps, until hot and lightly toasted. Pour the sauce around the hot edge of the wok so it sizzles, add the tomato and white pepper, and toss for 1 minute.
  5. Serve with cucumber slices, lime wedges and green onion, and chilies in soy sauce on the side.

Common mistakes

  • Fresh rice clumps and gets mushy.
  • An overcrowded wok steams the rice.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with sliced chilies in soy sauce rather than fish sauce to keep it vegetarian.
  • Serve with lime.

Substitutions

  • Day-old rice is essential.
  • Add pineapple for a sweeter version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot pan.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Cucumber slices and lime
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
