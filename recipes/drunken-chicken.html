Drunken Chicken 醉鸡

▾

Surprise me with

A cold Shanghainese dish where poached chicken is chilled and steeped in Shaoxing wine and goji berries, traditionally served at banquets and during Chinese New Year for its fragrant, mellow winey flavor.

  • Prep 15 min (plus 4 hr chilling)
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Poach chicken thighs in water with ginger and green onion, covered, 18–20 minutes until just cooked through.
  2. Transfer chicken immediately to an ice bath to cool completely and firm the skin.
  3. Whisk together Shaoxing wine, goji berries, salt, and sugar, plus 60 ml of the reserved poaching liquid, to make the steeping liquid.
  4. Pat the chicken dry, place in a container, and pour the wine mixture over top, ensuring it's well covered.
  5. Refrigerate at least 4 hours, or overnight for best flavor.
  6. Slice and serve cold, spooned with a little of the wine liquid.

Common mistakes

  • Steeping the chicken while still warm, which can make the dish taste flat rather than fragrant.
  • Using cooking wine instead of proper Shaoxing wine, which lacks the right aromatic character.

Authentic tips

  • The longer the chicken steeps (up to 2 days), the more pronounced and mellow the wine flavor becomes.
  • Use a good-quality Shaoxing wine meant for drinking, not the salted cooking version, for the best flavor.

Substitutions

  • Chicken wings are a traditional alternative cut for drunken chicken.
  • No goji berries: a few strips of dried orange peel add a similar fragrant note.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated, steeping in the wine liquid the whole time.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Cold as an appetizer, then rice or noodles
  • Soup: Clear pork bone soup
  • Dessert: Fresh pear
  • Beverage: Warm Shaoxing wine or tea
