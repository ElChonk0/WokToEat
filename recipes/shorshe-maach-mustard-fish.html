Shorshe Maach (Bengali Mustard Fish)

Shorshe maach, fish in a pungent mustard seed sauce, is a Bengali classic traditionally made with hilsa (ilish) and eaten with plain rice.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 800 g1.75 lbFish steaks (hilsa or rohu)
  • 45 g3 tbspBlack and yellow mustard seeds
  • 20 g2/3 ozGreen chilies
  • 5 g2 tspTurmeric
  • 60 ml4 tbspMustard oil
  • 8 g1.5 tspSalt
  • 250 ml1 cupWater

Method

  1. Grind the mustard seeds with the chilies and 100 ml water into a smooth paste.
  2. Marinate the fish with turmeric and salt for 10 minutes.
  3. Fry the fish lightly in mustard oil for 1-2 minutes per side; set aside.
  4. Add the mustard paste and 150 ml water to the pan and simmer 3 minutes.
  5. Return the fish and cook 8 minutes; finish with a drizzle of raw mustard oil.

Common mistakes

  • Over-grinding makes the mustard bitter.
  • Boiling hard for long turns it bitter too.

Authentic tips

  • Grind with cold water.
  • Hilsa is the classic fish.

Substitutions

  • Salmon or catfish replace hilsa.
  • Mustard paste should be cold-ground.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Cucumber salad
  • Dessert: Mishti doi
  • Beverage: Black tea
