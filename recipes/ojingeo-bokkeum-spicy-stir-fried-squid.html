Ojingeo Bokkeum (Spicy Stir-Fried Squid) 오징어볶음

▾

Ojingeo bokkeum is a popular Korean banchan and main of squid stir-fried with gochujang and vegetables.

  • Cook 10 min

  • 600 g1.3 lbSquid, cleaned and cut
  • 250 g9 ozOnion and cabbage
  • 45 g3 tbspGochujang
  • 15 g1 tbspGochugaru
  • 15 ml1 tbspSoy sauce
  • 30 g2 tbspGarlic
  • 15 g1 tbspSugar
  • 10 ml2 tspSesame oil

Method

  1. Cut the squid into rings or scored strips and pat dry.
  2. Mix the gochujang, gochugaru, soy sauce, garlic and sugar.
  3. Stir-fry the onion and cabbage in a hot wok 2 minutes.
  4. Add the squid and sauce and cook over high heat 2 minutes until the squid curls.
  5. Finish with sesame oil and serve at once.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking makes the squid rubbery.
  • Wet squid dilutes the sauce.

Authentic tips

  • Score the squid for sauce to cling.
  • Serve with rice and sprouts.

Substitutions

  • Shrimp or pork belly take the same sauce.
  • Reduce the gochujang for a milder dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot pan.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and bean sprouts
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
