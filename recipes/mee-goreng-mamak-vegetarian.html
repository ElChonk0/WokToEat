Mee Goreng Mamak (Vegetarian Spicy Fried Noodles)

▾

Surprise me with

Mee goreng mamak, spicy stir-fried yellow noodles, is a hallmark of Malaysia's Indian-Muslim (mamak) food stalls; the vegetarian version uses tofu and egg.

Method

  1. Loosen the noodles, rinsing them briefly if they are very oily, and drain. Mix the chili paste, ketchup, soy sauce, kecap manis and curry powder with the water into a sauce.
  2. Cook in two batches so the wok stays very hot. Heat half the oil in a flat-bottom wok over high heat and fry half the garlic and shallots for 30 seconds.
  3. Push them aside, crack in an egg and scramble it in chunks, then add half the tofu, potato, cabbage and tomato and toss for 1 minute.
  4. Add half the noodles and sauce and toss hard for 2 to 3 minutes, letting the noodles catch against the wok so they char and absorb the sauce. Mamak noodles should be smoky, dry and well coated, not wet.
  5. Add half the bean sprouts and greens for the last 30 seconds, then repeat with the second batch. Serve with lime wedges and fried shallots.

Common mistakes

  • Overcrowding the wok steams the noodles.
  • Overcooked noodles break.

Authentic tips

  • Cook in two batches.
  • Squeeze lime over just before eating.

Substitutions

  • Spaghetti replaces yellow noodles.
  • Squeeze lime over just before eating.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Fried egg and lime
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Teh tarik
