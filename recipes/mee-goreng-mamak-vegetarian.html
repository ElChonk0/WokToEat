Mee Goreng Mamak (Vegetarian Spicy Fried Noodles)

Mee goreng mamak, spicy stir-fried yellow noodles, is a hallmark of Malaysia's Indian-Muslim (mamak) food stalls; the vegetarian version uses tofu and egg.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 400 g14 ozYellow noodles, fresh
  • 200 g7 ozFried tofu
  • 22Eggs
  • 250 g9 ozBean sprouts and choy sum
  • 45 g1.5 ozChili paste
  • 30 g1 ozKetchup
  • 30 ml2 tbspSoy sauce
  • 100 g3.5 ozPotato, boiled and cubed
  • 60 ml4 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Fry the chili paste in the oil for 2 minutes over high heat.
  2. Add the tofu and potato and fry 2 minutes.
  3. Add the noodles, soy sauce and ketchup and toss 3 minutes.
  4. Push aside, scramble the eggs, then mix in.
  5. Add the sprouts and choy sum, toss 1 minute and serve with lime.

Common mistakes

  • Overcrowding the wok steams the noodles.
  • Overcooked noodles break.

Authentic tips

  • Cook in two batches.
  • Squeeze lime over just before eating.

Substitutions

  • Spaghetti replaces yellow noodles.
  • Squeeze lime over just before eating.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Fried egg and lime
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Teh tarik
