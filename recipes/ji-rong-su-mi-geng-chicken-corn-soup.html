Ji Rong Su Mi Geng (Chicken and Sweetcorn Soup) 鸡蓉粟米羹

Thick chicken and creamed-corn soup with egg ribbons is a Hong Kong home and restaurant favorite, made possible by canned creamed corn arriving in the colony in the mid-20th century.

Method

  1. Mix the chicken with the soy sauce, cornstarch and 2 tablespoons of water.
  2. Bring the stock, creamed corn and ginger to a boil, then simmer 5 minutes and remove the ginger.
  3. Stir in the chicken, breaking it up, and simmer 3 minutes until cooked. Season with salt and pepper.
  4. Stir in the cornstarch mixed with the water and simmer 1 minute until thick, then lower the heat and drizzle in the eggs while stirring slowly.
  5. Finish with sesame oil and green onion.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the chicken in a clump makes chewy lumps; stir it in so it disperses.
  • Boiling hard after the egg breaks up the ribbons.

Authentic tips

  • Mincing the chicken very finely by hand gives the silky "chicken velvet" texture.
  • Add a few kernels of whole corn for bite.

Substitutions

  • Crab meat replaces chicken for the banquet version.
  • Vegetable stock and tofu make it vegetarian.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator; reheat gently.

Freezing: Not recommended; the thickened soup turns watery.
