Ji Si Chao Mian (Cantonese Chicken Chow Mein) 鸡丝炒面

Cantonese chow mein pan-fries thin egg noodles until crisp in places, then tosses them with a light, savory stir-fry; it is the dish behind the takeout chow mein known worldwide.

  • Cook 15 min

Method

  1. Mix the chicken with the soy sauce, wine and cornstarch. Loosen the noodles, blanch them in boiling water for 30 to 60 seconds, drain very well and toss with 1 tablespoon of oil. Mix the oyster sauce, soy sauces and sugar in a cup.
  2. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a flat-bottom wok or large skillet over medium-high heat. Spread the noodles in an even layer and fry without stirring for 3 to 4 minutes per side until golden and crisp in patches, then move to a plate.
  3. Add 1 tablespoon of oil and stir-fry the chicken over high heat for 3 minutes until just cooked, then push it aside. Add the garlic, onion, carrot and cabbage and stir-fry for 2 minutes.
  4. Return the noodles, pour the sauce around them and toss with tongs or chopsticks for 1 to 2 minutes until evenly colored. Add the bean sprouts and green onion and toss for 30 seconds more.
  5. Drizzle with sesame oil and serve straight away while the sprouts are still crunchy.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Wet noodles steam instead of crisping; drain and dry them well.
  • Overcrowding a small pan stews everything; cook in two batches if your wok is under 30 cm (12 in).

Authentic tips

  • Toss with chopsticks or tongs rather than a spatula to keep the noodles long.
  • On induction, preheat the wok fully and keep it in contact with the surface instead of tossing it.

Substitutions

  • Dried thin egg noodles: boil 3 minutes, then dry and fry the same way.
  • Use char siu or shrimp in place of the chicken.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; reheat in a hot pan to re-crisp.

Freezing: Not recommended; the vegetables turn limp.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed gai lan with oyster sauce
  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Sweet red bean soup
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
