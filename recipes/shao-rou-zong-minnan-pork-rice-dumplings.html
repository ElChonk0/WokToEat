Shāo Ròu Zòng (Minnan Pork Rice Dumplings) 烧肉粽

▾

Surprise me with

Shao rou zong, rice dumplings filled with braised pork, mushrooms and chestnuts, are a Minnan specialty eaten at the Dragon Boat Festival, with a savory, glutinous filling.

  • 500 g1.1 lbGlutinous rice, soaked
  • 250 g9 ozPork belly, braised
  • 40 g1.5 ozDried shiitake
  • 100 g3.5 ozChestnuts
  • 60 ml4 tbspSoy sauce
  • 30 g1 ozDried shrimp
  • 3030Bamboo leaves

Method

  1. Soak the glutinous rice overnight and drain. Soak the bamboo leaves in hot water for 1 hour and rinse; soak the shiitake and shrimp 30 minutes.
  2. Braise the pork belly with half the soy sauce and the mushrooms for 30 minutes until tender; cut into cubes.
  3. Stir-fry the rice with the remaining soy sauce for 5 minutes until evenly colored.
  4. Fold two leaves into a cone, fill with a layer of rice, then pork, mushroom, chestnut and shrimp, top with more rice, fold closed and tie.
  5. Boil the dumplings, covered by water, for 3 hours (or 45 minutes in a pressure cooker) until the rice is soft and sticky.

Common mistakes

  • Under-cooked rice stays hard; keep the dumplings submerged and top up the water.
  • Loose wrapping lets the rice spill out.

Authentic tips

  • Soaking the rice overnight shortens the cooking time.
  • Serve with sweet sauce and chili.

Substitutions

  • Parchment paper and string replace bamboo leaves in a pinch.
  • Omit the chestnuts for a simpler filling.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; re-steam for 15 minutes.

Freezing: Freezes very well for 3 months; steam from frozen for 25 minutes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sweet chili sauce and pickled radish
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
