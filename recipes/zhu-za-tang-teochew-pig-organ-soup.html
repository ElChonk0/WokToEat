Zhū Zá Tāng (Teochew Pig Organ Soup) 猪杂汤

▾

Surprise me with

Pig organ soup is a Teochew hawker soup of pork offal in a clear, peppery broth with salted vegetables and tomato, served at supper spots in Singapore.

Method

  1. Clean the stomach and intestines thoroughly: rub them with coarse salt and cornstarch, rinse inside and out several times, then blanch them with the bones in boiling water for 10 minutes. Drain and rinse. Careful cleaning is what keeps the soup free of off-flavors.
  2. Put the bones, stomach and intestines in a clean pot with the water, ginger, garlic and crushed white peppercorns. Bring to a boil, skim, then simmer gently for 1 hour until the stomach is tender.
  3. Lift out the stomach and intestines and slice them. Rinse the salted mustard greens well and soak them for 10 minutes to remove excess salt, then add them and the tomatoes to the soup and simmer for 20 minutes.
  4. Return the sliced organs to the pot and season with salt and sugar; the soup should be peppery, gently sour and savory from the salted vegetable.
  5. Just before serving, add the liver slices and simmer for only 1 to 2 minutes, until just cooked; liver turns hard and grainy if overcooked. Serve with fried shallots and rice.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the liver makes it grainy and bitter; add it last for 1 minute.
  • Not cleaning the organs thoroughly leaves a strong smell.

Authentic tips

  • Rub the stomach with salt and flour before blanching to clean it.
  • Be generous with white pepper, which is the signature flavor.

Substitutions

  • Pork loin slices replace the organ meats.
  • Pickled mustard greens replace salted greens.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator; reheat gently.

Freezing: The broth freezes for 2 months; organs are best fresh.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and chili-soy dip
  • Soup: The dish is a soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot Chinese tea
