Xian Cai Men Xue Yu (Braised Cod with Pickled Greens) 咸菜焖鳕鱼

Salted mustard greens (xian cai) are a staple of southern Fujian home cooking, braised with fish for a sour, savory broth.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Pat the cod dry. Heat the oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat and sear the cod for 1 minute per side; lift out.
  2. Add the ginger, garlic and salted greens and fry 2 minutes, then add the tomatoes and cook 3 minutes until they collapse.
  3. Add the wine, water, sugar, soy sauce and white pepper and simmer 5 minutes.
  4. Return the cod, cover and simmer on low for 6 to 8 minutes until the fish is cooked and flakes.
  5. Scatter with the scallions and serve with rice.

Cook’s notes

Written for cod loin. Minnan home cooks pair local sea fish with salted greens in this style.

Common mistakes

  • Unrinsed salted greens make the dish far too salty.
  • Over-simmering dries the cod.

Authentic tips

  • Taste the broth before adding salt; the greens season it.
  • Sugar balances the sourness of the greens.

Substitutions

  • Haddock replaces the cod.
  • Suan cai replaces the salted greens.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
