Níng Méng Jī (Dai Lime Chicken Salad) 柠檬鸡

▾

Surprise me with

Lime chicken, cold shredded chicken dressed with lime, chilies and herbs, is a Dai specialty, sold at restaurants in Xishuangbanna and Kunming.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 40 g1.5 ozMint, cilantro and sawtooth coriander

Method

  1. Poach the chicken with the ginger and green onion at a bare simmer for 20 minutes. Cool in ice water and tear the meat into shreds.
  2. Mix the lime juice, chilies, garlic, fish sauce, salt and sugar into a sharp dressing.
  3. Toss the chicken with the dressing and shallots.
  4. Fold in the herbs just before serving.
  5. Serve at room temperature with sticky rice. Lime-dressed chicken is a Dai festival dish.

Common mistakes

  • Dressing too early turns the herbs black and limp; add them at the end.
  • Boiling the chicken makes it stringy.

Authentic tips

  • Use the cooling poaching liquid as a base for soup.
  • Roughly tear the herbs rather than chopping them.

Substitutions

  • Chicken breast poached gently works.
  • Add shredded lemongrass for a more traditional Dai flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sticky rice
  • Soup: Clear herb soup
  • Dessert: Fresh pineapple
  • Beverage: Chilled lemon tea
