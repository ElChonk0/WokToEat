Ning Meng Ji (Dai Lime Chicken Salad) 柠檬鸡

Lime chicken, cold shredded chicken dressed with lime, chilies and herbs, is a Dai specialty, sold at restaurants in Xishuangbanna and Kunming.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Yunnan Chicken LunchDinnerSalad / coldSpicy

  • 600 g1.3 lbChicken thighs, poached
  • 60 ml4 tbspLime juice
  • 20 g2/3 ozChilies
  • 40 g1.5 ozMint and cilantro
  • 40 g3 tbspGarlic and shallot
  • 15 ml1 tbspFish sauce
  • 5 g1 tspSugar

Method

  1. Poach the chicken thighs in simmering water for 18 minutes until cooked through, then plunge into cold water and chill.
  2. Shred the chicken by hand into thin strips.
  3. Mix the lime juice, minced chilies, garlic, shallot, fish sauce and sugar until the sugar dissolves.
  4. Toss the chicken with the dressing and herbs.
  5. Taste and adjust for sour, salty and hot; serve cold.

Common mistakes

  • Dressing too early turns the herbs black and limp; add them at the end.
  • Boiling the chicken makes it stringy.

Authentic tips

  • Use the cooling poaching liquid as a base for soup.
  • Roughly tear the herbs rather than chopping them.

Substitutions

  • Chicken breast poached gently works.
  • Add shredded lemongrass for a more traditional Dai flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sticky rice
  • Soup: Clear herb soup
  • Dessert: Fresh pineapple
  • Beverage: Chilled lemon tea
