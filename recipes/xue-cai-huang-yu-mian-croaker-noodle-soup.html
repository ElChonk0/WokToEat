Xuě Cài Huáng Yú Miàn (Yellow Croaker and Pickled Greens Noodle Soup) 雪菜黄鱼面

Yellow croaker with xue cai (pickled mustard greens) in noodle soup is a beloved Ningbo and Shanghai dish, eaten across the day in Jiangnan noodle shops.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 500 g1.1 lbYellow croaker fillets or whole small croaker
  • 400 g14 ozWheat noodles
  • 120 g4 ozXue cai, chopped
  • 20 g1 tbspGinger
  • 1.2 L5 cupsChicken or fish stock
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 30 g2 tbspScallions

Method

  1. Pat the fish dry and pan-fry in the oil over medium-high heat for 2 minutes per side until golden.
  2. Add the ginger and stock, bring to a boil and simmer 10 minutes until the broth turns milky white.
  3. Stir in the chopped xue cai and cook 3 minutes.
  4. Cook the noodles in a separate pot of boiling water until tender, drain and divide among bowls.
  5. Ladle the fish and broth over the noodles, season with the soy sauce and top with the scallions.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the pan-frying gives a thin, pale broth.
  • Cooking the noodles in the soup makes them mushy and clouds the broth.

Authentic tips

  • Pan-frying the fish is what makes the broth milky.
  • Rinse xue cai if it is very salty.

Substitutions

  • Cod or tilapia fillets replace the croaker.
  • Pickled mustard greens (not xue cai) are a more widely available substitute.

Storage

Leftovers: Store noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 2 days.

Freezing: Freeze the broth for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Stir-fried greens
  • Soup: Not needed; the noodle soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot jasmine tea
