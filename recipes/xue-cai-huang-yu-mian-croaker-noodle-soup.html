Xuě Cài Huáng Yú Miàn (Yellow Croaker and Pickled Greens Noodle Soup) 雪菜黄鱼面

▾

Yellow croaker with xue cai (pickled mustard greens) in noodle soup is a beloved Ningbo and Shanghai dish, eaten across the day in Jiangnan noodle shops.

  • 500 g1.1 lbYellow croaker fillets or whole small croaker
  • 300 g10 ozFish bones and heads (optional, for the broth)
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 20 g3/4 ozGinger, sliced
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 1.2 L5 cupsBoiling water or fish stock
  • 120 g4 ozXue cai (pickled mustard greens), rinsed and chopped
  • 50 g2 ozBamboo shoots, shredded (optional)
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt
  • 1 g1/2 tspWhite pepper
  • 400 g14 ozThin wheat noodles
  • 30 g1 ozGreen onion, sliced

Method

  1. Pat the fish dry. Heat the oil in a pot over medium-high heat and fry the fish bones and head with the ginger for 3 to 4 minutes, until golden. Frying the fish before adding water is what turns the broth milky.
  2. Add the Shaoxing wine, then the boiling water, and boil hard for 10 minutes until the broth is creamy white. Strain.
  3. Squeeze the xue cai dry and add it to the broth with the bamboo shoots. Simmer for 3 minutes, then add the croaker fillets and simmer gently for 3 to 4 minutes until opaque. Season with salt and white pepper, tasting first, since the xue cai is salty.
  4. Cook the noodles for 2 to 3 minutes, drain and divide among bowls.
  5. Ladle the broth, fish and xue cai over the noodles and top with green onion.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the pan-frying gives a thin, pale broth.
  • Cooking the noodles in the soup makes them mushy and clouds the broth.

Authentic tips

  • Pan-frying the fish is what makes the broth milky.
  • Rinse xue cai if it is very salty.

Substitutions

  • Cod or tilapia fillets replace the croaker.
  • Pickled mustard greens (not xue cai) are a more widely available substitute.

Storage

Leftovers: Store noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 2 days.

Freezing: Freeze the broth for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Stir-fried greens
  • Soup: Not needed; the noodle soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot jasmine tea
