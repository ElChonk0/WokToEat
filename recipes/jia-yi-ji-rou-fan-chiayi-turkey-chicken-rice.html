Jiā Yì Jī Ròu Fàn (Chiayi Shredded Chicken Rice) 嘉義雞肉飯

Chiayi's shredded chicken rice, rice topped with shredded poached chicken, chicken fat and fried shallots, is one of Taiwan's most beloved comfort foods.

  • Cook 30 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 500 g1.1 lbChicken thighs, skin-on
  • 500 g1.1 lbCooked rice
  • 30 g1 ozFried shallots
  • 45 ml3 tbspChicken fat or neutral oil
  • 30 ml2 tbspSoy sauce
  • 20 g3/4 ozGinger
  • 60 g2 ozPickled radish

Method

  1. Poach the chicken in simmering water with the ginger for 20 minutes until just cooked; lift out and cool.
  2. Shred the meat and skin by hand into fine strips.
  3. Warm the chicken fat or oil with the fried shallots, soy sauce and 4 tablespoons of the poaching liquid.
  4. Pile the rice into bowls and mound the shredded chicken on top.
  5. Spoon over the shallot sauce and serve with the pickled radish.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard toughens the chicken.
  • Cutting the chicken instead of shredding loses the characteristic texture.

Authentic tips

  • Taiwanese cooks traditionally use turkey in Chiayi; chicken is the common version.
  • Warm rice absorbs the sauce best.

Substitutions

  • Turkey breast with skin is the Chiayi original.
  • Rendered chicken skin plus oil replaces chicken fat.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat the rice and chicken separately.

Freezing: Freeze the shredded chicken for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled yellow radish and a braised egg
  • Soup: Clear pork bone soup
  • Dessert: Sweet tofu pudding
  • Beverage: Winter melon tea
