Kimchi Bokkeumbap (Kimchi Fried Rice) 김치볶음밥

▾

Surprise me with

Kimchi fried rice grew out of the Korean home habit of using up well-fermented, sour kimchi and leftover rice; topped with a fried egg it is one of the most common quick meals in Korea.

Method

  1. Break up the cold rice with your hands; if it is hard from the refrigerator, microwave it for a minute and fluff it. Use well-fermented, sour kimchi; fresh kimchi tastes flat when fried.
  2. Heat a large skillet or flat-bottom wok over medium-high heat with the oil and cook the pork for 3 to 4 minutes until it renders some fat and browns. Add the onion and green onion whites for 1 minute.
  3. Add the kimchi, kimchi juice, gochujang, gochugaru, soy sauce and sugar and stir-fry for 3 to 4 minutes, until the kimchi softens and darkens and most of the liquid has cooked off. Cooking the kimchi down concentrates its flavor.
  4. Lower the heat to medium, add the rice and butter and stir-fry for 3 to 4 minutes, pressing out clumps, until evenly red. Then raise the heat to high, press the rice into the pan and leave it for a minute so the bottom toasts, and toss again.
  5. Turn off the heat, stir in the sesame oil and green onion greens and taste for salt.
  6. Fry the eggs sunny-side up in a little oil so the yolks stay runny. Serve the rice topped with an egg, crumbled seaweed and sesame seeds.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Fresh, mild kimchi gives a flat result; use kimchi that has turned sour.
  • Freshly cooked warm rice turns mushy; use cold rice from the day before.

Authentic tips

  • Press the rice flat at the end and leave it 1 minute for crisp scorched bits on the bottom.
  • Spam is a very common alternative to pork belly in Korean homes.

Substitutions

  • Spam or tuna in place of the pork belly.
  • Make it vegetarian with tofu and vegan kimchi.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; reheat in a pan and add a fresh egg.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month without the egg.
