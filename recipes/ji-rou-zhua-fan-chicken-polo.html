Jī Ròu Zhuā Fàn (Chicken Polo) 鸡肉抓饭

▾

Surprise me with

Polo, rice cooked with carrots and meat, is Uyghur celebration food, usually made with lamb; chicken is a lighter, widely used alternative.

  • Cook 55 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 320 g11 ozRice
  • 600 g1.3 lbChicken thighs, chopped
  • 400 g14 ozCarrots
  • 150 g5 ozOnion
  • 50 g2 ozRaisins
  • 3 g1 tspCumin
  • 8 g1.5 tspSalt
  • 600 ml2.5 cupsWater
  • 60 ml4 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a heavy pot over medium-high heat and brown the chicken 6 minutes; remove.
  2. Fry the onion until golden, 5 minutes, then add the carrot strips and cumin and cook 8 minutes.
  3. Return the chicken, add the salt and water and bring to a boil; simmer 10 minutes.
  4. Rinse the rice, spread it over the top without stirring, cover and cook on low 25 minutes.
  5. Fluff, fold in the raisins and serve.

Common mistakes

  • Stirring the rice makes it gummy.
  • Lifting the lid too often lets steam escape.

Authentic tips

  • Soak the rice 30 minutes for fluffier grains.
  • Lamb is traditional; use bone-in shoulder.

Substitutions

  • Lamb is the traditional meat; add 30 minutes of braising.
  • Brown basmati rice replaces white; add 10 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat with a splash of water.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Cucumber and tomato salad
  • Soup: Clear broth
  • Dessert: Dried apricots and nuts
  • Beverage: Black tea
