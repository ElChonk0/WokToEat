Tiē Bǐng Zi Áo Xiǎo Yú (Dongbei Small Fish with Cornbread) 贴饼子熬小鱼

▾

Surprise me with

This Dongbei (northeast) rustic dish simmers small fish in soybean paste while cornmeal cakes steam against the pot wall; it comes from the cold-climate fishing villages of the northeast.

  • Cook 30 min

  • 700 g1.5 lbSmall whole fish, cleaned
  • 200 g1.5 cupsCornmeal
  • 100 g3/4 cupFlour
  • 45 g3 tbspYellow soybean paste
  • 50 g4 tbspGinger, garlic and scallion
  • 5 g2 tspDried chilies
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Mix the cornmeal and flour with warm water into a soft dough; rest 15 minutes.
  2. Heat the oil in a wide, heavy pot and fry the soybean paste, ginger, garlic, scallion and dried chilies for 1 minute.
  3. Add the fish, the soy sauce and 400 ml water and bring to a boil.
  4. Shape the dough into flat oval cakes and stick them to the inside wall of the pot above the liquid.
  5. Cover and cook over medium-low heat for 20 minutes, until the fish is tender and the cakes are cooked and crisp on the bottoms.

Common mistakes

  • Letting the pot run dry burns the bottom; keep an eye on the liquid.
  • Cakes that are too thick stay raw inside.

Authentic tips

  • The bottoms of the cakes crisp against the hot pot wall.
  • Use sardines if you cannot find small fish.

Substitutions

  • Small sardines or smelts replace the fish.
  • Add chopped tofu to the stew.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat the stew separately.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: The cornbread is included
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
