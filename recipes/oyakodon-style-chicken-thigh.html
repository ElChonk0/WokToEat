Oyakodon-Style Chicken Thigh 親子丼

Oyakodon ("parent-and-child bowl") simmers chicken and egg together in a sweet-savory dashi broth over rice — a beloved donburi (rice bowl) dish found in nearly every Japanese diner.

Method

  1. Combine dashi, soy sauce, mirin, and sugar in a wide, shallow pan; bring to a simmer.
  2. Add onion and chicken; simmer 6–7 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
  3. Pour half the beaten egg evenly over the top, cover, and cook 1 minute until just set.
  4. Pour in the remaining egg, cover again, and cook 30–60 seconds more until softly set but still a little runny.
  5. Slide the chicken, onion, and egg mixture over a bowl of hot rice.
  6. Garnish with green onion and serve immediately.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the egg, which loses the dish's signature soft, custardy texture.
  • Adding all the egg at once instead of in two additions, which makes it harder to achieve the right set.

Authentic tips

  • Using individual small pans (if making single portions) makes sliding the finished dish onto rice much easier.
  • The egg should still jiggle slightly when you take the pan off the heat — it continues to set from residual heat.

Substitutions

  • No dashi: a light chicken stock is a reasonable substitute, though it loses some traditional umami.
  • Mitsuba (Japanese parsley) is a traditional garnish if you can find it, in addition to or instead of green onion.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the egg texture doesn't hold up well after a day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled ginger
  • Soup: Miso soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot sencha
