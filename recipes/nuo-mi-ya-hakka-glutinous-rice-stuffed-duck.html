Nuò Mǐ Yā (Hakka Glutinous Rice-Stuffed Duck) 糯米鸭

Glutinous rice-stuffed duck is a Hakka banquet dish in which a whole duck is boned and filled with seasoned sticky rice, then steamed or braised and roasted.

  • 10 g2 tspSugar

  • 30 ml2 tbspHoney

Method

  1. Bone the duck through the back: cut along the backbone, work the knife around the rib cage and thigh bones, and lift out the carcass, leaving the wing and drumstick bones in place so the shape holds. Pat dry.
  2. Rub the duck inside and out with 30 ml of the light soy sauce, the wine and the white pepper, and marinate for 1 hour.
  3. Fry the sausage, mushrooms, shrimp and ginger in the oil for 3 minutes, add the drained rice, remaining soy sauces and sugar, stir for 2 minutes, and cool.
  4. Mix in the chestnuts, then loosely fill the duck, leaving room for the rice to expand. Sew or skewer the opening shut.
  5. Steam the duck over high heat for 1 hour 15 minutes, until the rice is cooked and the meat is tender.
  6. Brush with the honey and roast at 220 C (425 F) for 12 to 15 minutes until the skin is crisp and glossy. Rest for 10 minutes and slice.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Overfilling lets the rice burst the skin as it swells; leave a quarter of the cavity empty.
  • Skipping the pre-soak leaves the rice hard.

Authentic tips

  • Ask the butcher to bone the duck if you do not want to do it yourself.
  • Use the steaming liquid as a sauce.

Substitutions

  • Boneless duck legs filled and rolled replace the whole duck.
  • Bacon replaces Chinese sausage.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; resteam for 15 minutes.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months after steaming.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Blanched greens with oyster sauce
  • Soup: Clear winter melon soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot Chinese tea
