Dì Sān Xiān (Dongbei Three Earthly Treasures) 地三鲜

▾

Surprise me with

Di san xian, fried potato, eggplant and green pepper in a garlicky soy glaze, is the signature Dongbei (northeastern) dish, found on nearly every menu in the region.

  • 8 g1 tbspCornstarch, for the sauce, mixed with 2 tbsp water

Method

  1. Toss the eggplant lightly in cornstarch; the coating stops it absorbing too much oil.
  2. Heat the oil to 180 C (350 F) and fry the potatoes for 5 to 6 minutes until golden and cooked through, then the eggplant for 2 to 3 minutes until golden and soft, then the peppers for 10 seconds. Drain. Frying each separately is the Dongbei way; it gives every vegetable a crisp edge.
  3. Pour off all but 1 tablespoon of oil and fry the garlic and green onion for 15 seconds.
  4. Add the soy sauces, sugar and water, bring to a boil and thicken with the slurry.
  5. Toss in the vegetables quickly to glaze and serve. The 'three treasures of the earth' is a northeastern home classic.

Common mistakes

  • Eggplant soaks up oil; salting and drying it first reduces this.
  • An over-thick sauce becomes gluey.

Authentic tips

  • Fry each vegetable separately so each cooks to its own time.
  • The sauce should be glossy rather than thick.

Substitutions

  • Zucchini replaces the eggplant.
  • Bake the vegetables at 220 C (425 F) instead of frying.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
