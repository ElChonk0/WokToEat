Dì Sān Xiān (Dongbei Three Earthly Treasures) 地三鲜

▾

Surprise me with

Di san xian, fried potato, eggplant and green pepper in a garlicky soy glaze, is the signature Dongbei (northeastern) dish, found on nearly every menu in the region.

  • 400 g14 ozEggplant, cut in chunks
  • 300 g10 ozPotatoes, cut in chunks
  • 150 g5 ozGreen peppers
  • 30 g2 tbspGarlic
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 10 g2 tspSugar
  • 30 ml2 tbspCornstarch slurry
  • 90 ml6 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Cut the eggplant and potatoes into 3 cm chunks and the peppers into similar pieces. Soak the potato in cold water 10 minutes; dry well.
  2. Heat a third of the oil in a wok over medium-high heat and fry the potato 5 minutes until golden; remove.
  3. Fry the eggplant in another third of the oil 5 minutes until soft and golden; remove. Fry the peppers 1 minute and remove.
  4. Add the rest of the oil and the garlic, fry 15 seconds, then the soy sauce, sugar and 120 ml water.
  5. Return the vegetables, simmer 2 minutes and stir in the slurry until glossy.

Common mistakes

  • Eggplant soaks up oil; salting and drying it first reduces this.
  • An over-thick sauce becomes gluey.

Authentic tips

  • Fry each vegetable separately so each cooks to its own time.
  • The sauce should be glossy rather than thick.

Substitutions

  • Zucchini replaces the eggplant.
  • Bake the vegetables at 220 C (425 F) instead of frying.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
