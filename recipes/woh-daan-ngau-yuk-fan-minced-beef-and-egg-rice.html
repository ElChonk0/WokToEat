Woh Daan Ngau Yuk Fan (Minced Beef and Egg over Rice) 窝蛋免治牛肉饭

▾

Surprise me with

Minced beef in a light gravy with a raw egg yolk stirred in at the table is a Hong Kong cha chaan teng staple, often cooked in a clay pot over rice.

Method

  1. Mix the beef with the oyster sauce, cornstarch, pepper and water, then work in the beef oil; the oil keeps the mince from clumping in the wok. Mix the sauce oyster sauce, soy sauces and sugar in a cup, and the slurry separately.
  2. Heat a flat-bottom wok over high heat until it smokes, add the oil and lower the heat. Add the beef and break it up with a spatula, cooking for 1 to 1.5 minutes, then turn it over. Add the onion, green onion whites and ginger and cook 1 minute more, until the beef has just lost its pink. Overcooked mince turns grainy and tough.
  3. Splash the wine around the edge of the wok and stir for 15 seconds, then pour in the hot water slowly along the side. Add the sauce and peas and bring to a boil over high heat for 30 to 40 seconds.
  4. Lower to medium and stir in the slurry gradually until the gravy is thick and glossy. Add the sesame oil and turn off the heat.
  5. Divide the hot rice among wide bowls and make a well in the center of each. Ladle the beef around the rice, leaving the well clear, and drop a raw yolk or whole egg into each well. Stir the egg into the hot beef and rice at the table, where the heat cooks it into a silky sauce. Use only very fresh eggs, or poach them first if you prefer them cooked.
  6. Scatter with the green onion greens and serve at once.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Too thick a gravy makes it stodgy; it should flow over the rice.
  • Cooking the beef on low heat makes it grey and watery.

Authentic tips

  • Use very fresh eggs if serving the yolk raw, or poach the eggs briefly in the gravy.
  • A clay pot is traditional; a small Dutch oven of rice topped with the beef in the last 5 minutes gives the same crust.

Substitutions

  • Ground pork works the same way.
  • Add diced mushrooms with the onion.

Storage

Leftovers: The beef keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; add fresh eggs when reheating.

Freezing: The beef gravy freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Blanched lettuce with oyster sauce
  • Soup: Hong Kong borscht
  • Dessert: Egg tarts
  • Beverage: Hong Kong milk tea
