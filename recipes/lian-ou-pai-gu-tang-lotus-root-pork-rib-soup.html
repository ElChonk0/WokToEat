Lián Ǒu Pái Gǔ Tāng (Lotus Root and Pork Rib Soup) 莲藕排骨汤

▾

Surprise me with

Lotus root and pork ribs, simmered gently for a milky soup, are a staple of Hunan and Hubei home cooking and winter tonics.

  • 20 g2/3 ozRed dates

  • 10 g2 tspSalt
  • 10 g1 tbspGoji berries

Method

  1. Put the ribs in cold water, bring to a boil for 3 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  2. Return the ribs to a clean pot with the water, ginger and red dates and bring to a boil, then simmer gently for 45 minutes.
  3. Peel the lotus root and cut into thick chunks; add to the pot and simmer 45 minutes more, until the lotus root is soft and the broth slightly thick.
  4. Add the goji for the last 5 minutes and season with the salt.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard clouds the soup; keep it at a gentle simmer.
  • Adding the salt early toughens the meat.

Authentic tips

  • Choose starchy lotus root (the kind with seven holes) for a creamy broth.
  • Soak the cut lotus root in water with a splash of vinegar to stop it browning.

Substitutions

  • Pork neck bones replace ribs; skim a little more.
  • Corn on the cob can replace the lotus root.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; the broth improves.

Freezing: Freezes well for 3 months, though lotus root softens.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
