Niú Ròu Guō Tiē (Beef Pot Stickers) 牛肉锅贴

▾

Surprise me with

Pan-fried dumplings with crisp bases are a northern Anhui breakfast and street food, filled with beef and chives by the province's Hui bakers.

  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 20 g1 tbspGinger
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 10 ml2 tspSesame oil
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Mix the beef with the ginger, soy sauce and sesame oil, stirring in one direction and adding 3 tablespoons of water gradually; fold in the chives.
  2. Place a tablespoon of filling in each wrapper, fold in half and pleat.
  3. Heat the oil in a nonstick pan over medium-high heat, arrange the dumplings flat side down and fry 2-3 minutes until golden.
  4. Add 100 ml water, cover and steam 6-8 minutes until the water is gone; uncook 1 minute for a crisp base.

Common mistakes

  • Too much water leaves the bases soggy.
  • Overfilling splits the wrappers.

Authentic tips

  • Dip in black vinegar.
  • Fry leftover dumplings from frozen.

Substitutions

  • Garlic chives replace Chinese chives.
  • Ground lamb replaces beef.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; re-fry in a hot pan.

Freezing: Freeze uncooked on a tray for 2 months; pan-fry from frozen with extra water.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Black vinegar dip
  • Soup: Hot and sour soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
