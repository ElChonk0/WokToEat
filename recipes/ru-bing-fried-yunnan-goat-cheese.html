Rǔ Bǐng (Fried Yunnan Goat Cheese) 乳饼

▾

Surprise me with

Rubing, a firm fresh cheese made by the Bai and Sani peoples of Yunnan, is pan-fried until golden and eaten as a main or snack, a rarity in Chinese cuisine.

  • Prep 5 min
  • Cook 8 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 300 g10.5 ozRubing or halloumi, sliced
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil
  • 30 g2 tbspRose jam or sugar
  • 3 g1/2 tspChili salt

Method

  1. Cut the cheese into slabs about 1 cm thick and pat dry.
  2. Heat the oil in a nonstick pan over medium heat.
  3. Fry the cheese 2-3 minutes per side until deep golden.
  4. Drain briefly on paper.
  5. Serve with rose jam for sweet or chili salt for savory.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking on too high a heat burns the surface before it warms through.
  • Wet cheese spatters in the oil.

Authentic tips

  • Rubing is made from goat's milk curdled with sour whey or vinegar; halloumi is the closest substitute.
  • It is not vegan; skip it if you avoid dairy.

Substitutions

  • Halloumi is the closest widely available cheese.
  • Paneer also fries well.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; reheat in a hot pan.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Flatbread or steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Pu'er tea
