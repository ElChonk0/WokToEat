Huái Nán Niú Ròu Tāng (Huainan Beef Soup) 淮南牛肉汤

▾

Surprise me with

Huainan beef soup, a peppery, milky beef broth served with beef slices and flatbread, is Huainan's signature dish and a local breakfast.

Method

  1. Soak the bones and beef in cold water for 1 hour, blanch for 10 minutes and rinse.
  2. Simmer the bones and shank in the water with the ginger, green onion and spices for 4 hours, skimming; lift out the shank after 2 hours when tender. Hard boiling at the end turns the broth milky.
  3. Make the chili oil: heat the tallow and fry the dried chilies and flakes over low heat for 2 minutes until fragrant and red.
  4. Season the broth with salt and white pepper. Slice the beef thinly.
  5. Blanch the vermicelli and tofu in the broth and divide among bowls, top with beef, ladle over the boiling broth and finish with the chili oil and cilantro. Huainan beef soup is a northern Anhui breakfast, eaten with flatbread.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the blanch gives a dark, cloudy broth.
  • Under-seasoning: this broth needs generous salt and pepper.

Authentic tips

  • A rolling simmer helps produce the milky broth.
  • Break the flatbread into the soup.

Substitutions

  • Ready-made beef stock saves hours.
  • A pressure cooker cuts the simmer to 90 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; the broth sets when cold.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Flatbread torn into the soup
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
