Cháohú Yín Yú Chǎo Dàn (Chaohu Whitebait Omelette) 巢湖银鱼炒蛋

▾

Surprise me with

Chaohu Lake in Anhui is famous for its three "whites": whitebait, white fish and white shrimp; whitebait scrambled with eggs is a simple local favorite.

  • Cook 8 min

  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt
  • 1 g1/2 tspWhite pepper
  • 30 g1 ozGreen onion, chopped
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Rinse the whitebait gently and drain well on paper towels. Chaohu whitebait, tiny and translucent, are one of the lake's 'three treasures'.
  2. Beat the eggs with the Shaoxing wine, salt and pepper, then fold in the whitebait and green onion.
  3. Heat the oil in a nonstick skillet over medium-high heat until shimmering, pour in the mixture and let it set for 30 seconds.
  4. Lower the heat and cook for 2 minutes until the bottom is golden, then flip it in sections and cook for 1 minute more until just set.
  5. Serve hot; the whitebait should be just cooked and tender.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the eggs makes them dry.
  • Rough handling breaks the delicate fish.

Authentic tips

  • Fresh whitebait is silvery and translucent; frozen works in a pinch.
  • Serve over rice.

Substitutions

  • Small frozen whitebait work if thawed and dried.
  • Shrimp replaces whitebait.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the eggs turn rubbery when reheated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or congee
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
