Jié Guā Fěn Sī Xiā Mǐ Bāo (Fuzzy Melon and Glass Noodle Pot with Dried Shrimp) 节瓜粉丝虾米煲

Fuzzy melon braised with glass noodles and dried shrimp is a homey Cantonese pot dish; the dried shrimp season the broth that the noodles soak up.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Cantonese Seafood LunchDinnerBraise / stewNoodlesShellfish

Ingredients

  • 800 g1.75 lbFuzzy melon (jie gua), peeled and cut into batons
  • 100 g3.5 ozMung bean glass noodles, soaked
  • 40 g1.5 ozDried shrimp, soaked
  • 200 g7 ozShrimp, peeled
  • 4 cloves4 clovesGarlic, minced
  • 10 g1/3 ozGinger, sliced
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil
  • 500 ml2 cupsChicken stock
  • 15 ml1 tbspOyster sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 2 g1/2 tspSalt
  • 1 g1/2 tspGround white pepper
  • 2 stalks2 stalksGreen onion, chopped

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven over medium heat and fry the dried shrimp, garlic and ginger for 2 minutes until fragrant.
  2. Add the fuzzy melon and stir-fry for 2 minutes, then add the stock, oyster sauce and soy sauce. Cover and simmer for 10 minutes until the melon is tender.
  3. Add the drained noodles and fresh shrimp and simmer uncovered for 3 to 4 minutes, until the noodles are clear and the shrimp are cooked.
  4. Season with salt and pepper; the noodles will absorb most of the liquid.
  5. Scatter with green onion and serve in the pot.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the noodles too early turns them to mush.
  • Leaving the fuzz on the melon gives a prickly texture; peel it.

Authentic tips

  • A clay pot is traditional; a Dutch oven works on any hob.
  • Soak the dried shrimp in a little Shaoxing wine for extra aroma.

Substitutions

  • Zucchini replaces fuzzy melon; simmer 5 minutes.
  • Leave out fresh shrimp for a lighter side dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator; the noodles keep absorbing liquid, so add a splash of stock.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and steamed pork patty
  • Soup: Tomato tofu soup
  • Dessert: Red bean soup
  • Beverage: Hot tea
