Jié Guā Fěn Sī Xiā Mǐ Bāo (Fuzzy Melon and Glass Noodle Pot with Dried Shrimp) 节瓜粉丝虾米煲

▾

Fuzzy melon braised with glass noodles and dried shrimp is a homey Cantonese pot dish; the dried shrimp season the broth that the noodles soak up.

Method

  1. Soak the glass noodles in warm water until soft, about 10 minutes, then snip them shorter and drain well; wet noodles water down the sauce. Soak the dried shrimp for 10 minutes and keep the liquid.
  2. Peel the fuzzy melon and cut it on an angle into slices about 6 mm (1/4 in) thick so it cooks evenly and soaks up the broth.
  3. Heat a flat-bottom wok over high heat until it smokes, add the oil and lower to medium. Fry the garlic, ginger, shallot and dried shrimp for 15 to 20 seconds until fragrant. Add the fresh shrimp and sear 25 seconds per side over high heat, then the carrot for 20 seconds, and lift the shrimp out.
  4. Add the melon and toss for 30 seconds, then pour in the stock, water and shrimp soaking liquid and transfer to a Dutch oven or clay pot. Cover and bring to a boil, then simmer for 6 to 8 minutes until the melon is tender and translucent.
  5. Season with the oyster sauce, soy sauce, salt and sugar. Push the noodles under the melon, return the shrimp with the mushrooms on top, cover and simmer for 2 to 3 minutes until the noodles have drunk up much of the broth.
  6. Finish with the white pepper, sesame oil and green onion and serve bubbling in the pot.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the noodles too early turns them to mush.
  • Leaving the fuzz on the melon gives a prickly texture; peel it.

Authentic tips

  • A clay pot is traditional; a Dutch oven works on any hob.
  • Soak the dried shrimp in a little Shaoxing wine for extra aroma.

Substitutions

  • Zucchini replaces fuzzy melon; simmer 5 minutes.
  • Leave out fresh shrimp for a lighter side dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator; the noodles keep absorbing liquid, so add a splash of stock.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and steamed pork patty
  • Soup: Tomato tofu soup
  • Dessert: Red bean soup
  • Beverage: Hot tea
