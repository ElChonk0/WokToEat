Galchi Jorim (Braised Cutlassfish) 갈치조림

▾

Surprise me with

Cutlassfish braised with radish in a spicy sauce is a Jeju and southern Korean specialty, using the silvery fish prized there.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 25 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 800 g1.75 lbCutlassfish, cut
  • 250 g9 ozKorean radish
  • 20 g1.5 tbspGochugaru
  • 45 ml3 tbspSoy sauce
  • 40 g3 tbspGarlic
  • 30 g1 ozGreen chilies
  • 10 g2 tspSugar
  • 300 ml1.25 cupsWater

Method

  1. Cut the radish into thick half-moons and lay on the bottom of a wide pot.
  2. Mix the soy sauce, gochugaru, garlic, sugar and water and pour over; bring to a boil.
  3. Lay the cutlassfish on top.
  4. Cover and simmer 15 minutes, spooning the sauce over.
  5. Add the chilies and cook 5 minutes.

Common mistakes

  • Stirring breaks the delicate flesh.
  • Fishy smell: rinse the fish well first.

Authentic tips

  • Jeju is famous for the silvery fish.
  • Radish soaks up the sauce.

Substitutions

  • Mackerel replaces cutlassfish.
  • Potatoes added to the braise are common.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
