Niku Udon (Beef Udon Noodle Soup) 肉うどん

Niku udon, udon in dashi broth topped with sweet simmered beef, is a Kansai noodle-shop classic; in eastern Japan pork is more common.

Method

  1. Heat the dashi with the broth soy sauce, mirin and salt.
  2. In a skillet simmer the beef soy sauce, mirin, sugar and water with the onion for 3 minutes, then add the beef and cook 2 minutes until just done and glazed.
  3. Cook the udon according to the packet and drain.
  4. Divide the noodles among bowls and ladle the hot broth over.
  5. Top with the beef, green onion and shichimi.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the thin beef makes it tough.
  • Dark soy sauce makes the Kansai broth too dark; use light soy.

Authentic tips

  • Kansai broth is pale and dashi-forward; let the dashi lead.
  • Add a soft-boiled egg or tempura flakes.

Substitutions

  • Pork belly for buta udon.
  • Instant dashi granules make quick stock.

Storage

Leftovers: Store broth and beef separately for 3 days.

Freezing: The beef topping freezes for 2 months.
