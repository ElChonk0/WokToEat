Beef Tapa (Filipino Cured Beef, Tapsilog)

▾

Surprise me with

Tapa, thinly sliced beef cured in soy, vinegar and garlic, is the centerpiece of the Filipino breakfast "tapsilog" with garlic fried rice and egg.

Method

  1. Slice the beef thin and marinate with the soy sauce, calamansi, garlic, sugar and pepper overnight.
  2. Fry the garlic rice with a little oil until golden.
  3. Fry the beef in batches in a hot pan, 2 minutes per side, until browned and slightly caramelized.
  4. Fry the eggs sunny-side up.
  5. Serve with the garlic rice, egg and a vinegar-chili dip.

Common mistakes

  • Crowding the pan steams the beef.
  • Too much sugar burns.

Authentic tips

  • Pound the beef to tenderize.
  • A classic breakfast with a spiced vinegar dip.

Substitutions

  • Pork replaces beef for a porksilog.
  • Use thin, tenderized slices for the best texture.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot pan.

Freezing: Freezes the marinated raw beef for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Garlic rice, fried egg and spiced vinegar
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot coffee
