Yě Shēng Jūn Chǎo (Yunnan Mixed Wild Mushroom Stir-Fry) 野生菌炒

▾

Surprise me with

Yunnan has over 800 edible mushroom species, and in summer they appear in markets across Kunming to be stir-fried with garlic and chilies.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 500 g1.1 lbMixed mushrooms
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 20 g2/3 ozChilies
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 g1/2 ozCilantro
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Wipe the mushrooms clean and tear or slice them evenly.
  2. Dry-fry the mushrooms in a wok over high heat for 4 minutes until they release their water and begin to brown.
  3. Add the oil, garlic and chilies and stir-fry 2 minutes.
  4. Add the soy sauce and toss 1 minute.
  5. Finish with the cilantro.

Common mistakes

  • Crowding the pan steams the mushrooms.
  • Wild mushrooms must be cooked thoroughly.

Authentic tips

  • Cultivated mixed mushrooms work if wild ones are not available.
  • Never eat foraged mushrooms you cannot identify.

Substitutions

  • Cultivated mixed mushrooms replace wild ones.
  • Add green chilies for heat.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Pu'er tea
