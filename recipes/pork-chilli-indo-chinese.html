Pork Chilli (Indo-Chinese Chilli Pork)

Indo-Chinese cuisine, born in Kolkata's Chinese community, produced chilli dishes such as chilli pork that are a staple of Indian takeaway menus.

  • 40 g1.5 ozGarlic and ginger

Method

  1. Toss the pork with half the cornstarch, a splash of soy sauce and an egg white if you like; leave 15 minutes.
  2. Shallow-fry the pork in batches in 3 tablespoons of the oil until crisp, about 4 minutes; drain.
  3. In the same wok, fry the garlic and ginger 30 seconds, then the onion and peppers 2 minutes.
  4. Add the soy sauce, chili sauce and vinegar with 4 tablespoons water.
  5. Return the pork, toss until glazed and serve at once.

Common mistakes

  • Soggy pork: serve right away.
  • Overcooking the peppers makes them limp.

Authentic tips

  • Fry the pork twice for extra crispness.
  • Serve with fried rice.

Substitutions

  • Chicken or paneer take the same sauce.
  • Air-fry the pork at 200 C (400 F) before tossing.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Chinese-style fried rice
  • Soup: Hot and sour soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
