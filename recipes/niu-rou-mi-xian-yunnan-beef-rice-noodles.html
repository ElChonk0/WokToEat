Niú Ròu Mǐ Xiàn (Yunnan Beef Rice Noodles) 牛肉米线

▾

Surprise me with

Rice noodles (mi xian) are Yunnan's defining staple, and a bowl with braised beef and a spicy broth is a standard breakfast and lunch.

  • Cook 2 hr

  • 60 g4 tbspGinger and scallion
  • 8 g3 tspStar anise and Sichuan pepper
  • 30 ml2 tbspSoy sauce
  • 50 g2 ozChili oil and pickles
  • 20 g3/4 ozCilantro
  • 2 L8 cupsWater

Method

  1. Cover the brisket with cold water, boil 5 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  2. Return to a clean pot with the water, ginger, scallion, star anise and pepper and simmer gently for 90 minutes until tender.
  3. Lift out the beef, slice thinly and season the broth with the soy sauce.
  4. Blanch the noodles for 30 seconds and divide among bowls.
  5. Top with beef, pickles and cilantro, ladle over the hot broth and finish with chili oil.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking the noodles in the broth clouds it.
  • Skipping the blanching gives a murky broth.

Authentic tips

  • Pickled greens and chili are the table condiments.
  • Make the broth a day ahead and skim the fat.

Substitutions

  • Shank replaces brisket.
  • Ready-made stock saves an hour.

Storage

Leftovers: Store noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 4 days.

Freezing: Freeze the broth and beef for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled vegetables
  • Soup: Not needed; the noodle soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Pu'er tea
