Niú Ròu Mǐ Xiàn (Yunnan Beef Rice Noodles) 牛肉米线

▾

Surprise me with

Rice noodles (mi xian) are Yunnan's defining staple, and a bowl with braised beef and a spicy broth is a standard breakfast and lunch.

  • Cook 3 hr

  • 400 g14 ozFresh round rice noodles (mi xian)
  • 500 g1.1 lbBeef brisket
  • 800 g1.75 lbBeef bones
  • 3 L12 cupsWater
  • 60 g2 ozGinger and green onion
  • 8 g1/4 ozStar anise, cao guo and Sichuan pepper
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 6 g1 tspSalt
  • 30 ml2 tbspChili oil
  • 60 g2 ozPickled mustard greens, chopped
  • 40 g1.5 ozGarlic chives, mint and cilantro

Method

  1. Blanch the brisket and bones and rinse. Simmer them in the water with the ginger, green onion and spices for 2.5 hours, skimming; lift out the brisket when tender.
  2. Season the broth with soy sauce and salt. Slice the brisket.
  3. Blanch the rice noodles in boiling water for 30 seconds to 1 minute until hot.
  4. Divide the noodles among bowls, top with brisket and ladle over the broth.
  5. Add chili oil, pickled greens and herbs to taste. Mi xian is Yunnan's everyday breakfast.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking the noodles in the broth clouds it.
  • Skipping the blanching gives a murky broth.

Authentic tips

  • Pickled greens and chili are the table condiments.
  • Make the broth a day ahead and skim the fat.

Substitutions

  • Shank replaces brisket.
  • Ready-made stock saves an hour.

Storage

Leftovers: Store noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 4 days.

Freezing: Freeze the broth and beef for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled vegetables
  • Soup: Not needed; the noodle soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Pu'er tea
