Bak Chang (Nyonya Pork Rice Dumplings)

▾

Surprise me with

Bak chang, rice dumplings filled with seasoned pork and candied winter melon, are a Nyonya (Peranakan) version of the Chinese zongzi, eaten at the Dragon Boat Festival.

  • 60 g2 ozCandied winter melon
  • 40 g1.5 ozDried shiitake
  • 30 g1 ozDried shrimp
  • 60 ml4 tbspSoy sauce and five-spice
  • 3030Bamboo leaves

Method

  1. Soak the rice 4 hours and drain; soak the shiitake and bamboo leaves and simmer the leaves for 10 minutes.
  2. Marinate the pork with half the soy sauce and five-spice, then fry with the shiitake, dried shrimp and winter melon for 8 minutes.
  3. Season the rice with the remaining soy sauce.
  4. Fold two leaves into a cone, add rice, a spoonful of filling and more rice, fold shut and tie securely.
  5. Boil in plenty of water for 3 hours, topping up the water, until the rice is tender.

Common mistakes

  • Loose wrapping lets the rice escape.
  • Letting the water drop below the dumplings gives uneven cooking.

Authentic tips

  • Hang to dry briefly before eating.
  • Dumplings freeze well.

Substitutions

  • Parchment paper and string replace bamboo leaves.
  • Skip the winter melon for a simpler filling.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; re-steam for 15 minutes.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months; steam from frozen for 25 minutes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sambal belacan
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Chinese tea
