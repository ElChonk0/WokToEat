Yúnnán Suān Tāng Yú (Yunnan Sour Fish Soup) 云南酸汤鱼

▾

Surprise me with

Sour soups are common in Yunnan and neighboring Guizhou, where fermented tomato or rice-water broth gives a tangy base for freshwater fish.

  • Cook 20 min

  • 700 g1.5 lbFish fillets
  • 300 g10 ozTomatoes
  • 40 g3 tbspPickled chilies
  • 40 g3 tbspGinger and garlic
  • 20 g2/3 ozCilantro
  • 800 ml3.5 cupsStock
  • 6 g1 tspSalt
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Cook the tomatoes, pickled chilies, ginger and garlic in the oil for 8 minutes until they collapse into a saucy base.
  2. Add the stock and simmer 10 minutes.
  3. Season with the salt.
  4. Slide in the fish fillets and poach gently 3 minutes until just opaque.
  5. Add the cilantro and serve at once.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard breaks the fish.
  • Overcooking makes it dry.

Authentic tips

  • Use a firm white fish such as catfish or sea bass.
  • Add tofu and greens for a fuller pot.

Substitutions

  • Canned tomatoes plus a splash of vinegar approximate fermented tomato.
  • Catfish or tilapia are good choices.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated.

Freezing: Freeze the broth only.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
