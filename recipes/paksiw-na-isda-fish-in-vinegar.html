Paksiw na Isda (Fish in Vinegar)

Paksiw, fish simmered in vinegar with ginger, garlic and chilies, is an old Filipino preservation-minded dish that keeps for days.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Philippines Seafood LunchDinnerBraise / stewSpicy30 min or less

  • 800 g1.75 lbMackerel or milkfish, cut
  • 120 ml1/2 cupCane vinegar
  • 60 g4 tbspGinger and garlic
  • 150 g5 ozEggplant
  • 22Long green chilies
  • 3 g1 tspPeppercorns
  • 200 ml3/4 cupWater
  • 5 g1 tspSalt

Method

  1. Layer the ginger, garlic, eggplant and peppercorns in a pot and lay the fish on top.
  2. Pour in the vinegar and water; do not stir.
  3. Bring to a boil, then simmer 15 minutes, covered, until the fish is cooked.
  4. Add the chilies and season with the salt.
  5. Serve with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Stirring early leaves a raw vinegar taste.
  • Overcooking breaks the fish.

Authentic tips

  • Keeps for days and tastes better the next day.
  • Add bitter melon for the Ilocano variation.

Substitutions

  • Bonito or sardines replace mackerel.
  • Bitter melon is a classic addition.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; improves.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Calamansi juice
