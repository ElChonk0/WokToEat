Red-Braised Beef Shank 红烧牛肉

▾

Surprise me with

Shanghai's hong shao technique applied to beef shank produces a glossy, deeply savory-sweet braise, traditionally served over noodles or rice — a comforting dish across Jiangnan households.

Method

  1. Put the beef in a pot of cold water, bring it to a boil and boil for 5 minutes. Drain and rinse off the scum; starting in cold water draws out more of the blood.
  2. Heat a tablespoon of oil in a Dutch oven over medium heat and fry the ginger, green onion, star anise and cinnamon for 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Add the rock sugar and stir over medium-low heat until it melts into an amber caramel, then add the beef and turn it until glossy and evenly colored. Caramelized sugar, not just dark soy, gives Shanghai red-braising its lacquered color.
  4. Pour in the Shaoxing wine around the edge of the pot so it sizzles, then the soy sauces and enough hot water to almost cover. Bring to a boil, cover and simmer over low heat for 2 hours, until a chopstick slides into the shank easily.
  5. Uncover, raise the heat to medium-high and reduce the sauce for 5 to 10 minutes, turning and basting, until it is thick, glossy and clings to the meat. This final reduction (shou zhi) is what makes the dish 'nong you chi jiang': rich in oil, dark in sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Rushing the long braise, which is essential for breaking down shank's connective tissue into tenderness.
  • Letting the caramel burn before adding the beef, which makes the whole dish bitter.

Authentic tips

  • This dish, like most braises, tastes even better reheated the next day.
  • The broth freezes and reheats exceptionally well; make a double batch and freeze half.

Substitutions

  • Brisket replaces the shank with a similar braise.
  • A pressure cooker cuts the braise to 50 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; slice cold or reheat in the sauce.

Freezing: Freezes well for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Blanched greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
