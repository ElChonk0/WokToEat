Bái Cài Dùn Ròu (Pork and Cabbage Stew) 白菜炖肉

▾

Surprise me with

Napa cabbage stewed with pork belly and glass noodles is a winter staple of Beijing and northern China, built on the capital's famous winter cabbage.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 45 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 400 g14 ozPork belly, sliced
  • 600 g1.3 lbNapa cabbage
  • 100 g3.5 ozGlass noodles, soaked
  • 45 ml3 tbspSoy sauce
  • 40 g3 tbspGinger and scallion
  • 22Star anise
  • 500 ml2 cupsWater

Method

  1. Cut the pork belly into thin slices and soak the glass noodles in warm water for 10 minutes.
  2. Brown the pork belly in a dry pot for 5 minutes until the fat renders and the edges color.
  3. Add the ginger, scallion, star anise and soy sauce, stir 1 minute, then pour in 500 ml water.
  4. Add the cabbage, cover and simmer 25 minutes until the cabbage is soft and sweet.
  5. Add the noodles and cook 8 minutes until they absorb the broth.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the noodles makes them mushy.
  • Too much soy sauce makes it salty because the cabbage cooks down.

Authentic tips

  • Napa cabbage in winter is sweet; do not skip the long simmer.
  • Serve in the pot with rice or steamed buns.

Substitutions

  • Pork shoulder gives a leaner stew.
  • Frozen tofu (thawed) soaks up the broth beautifully.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; the flavor improves.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months without the glass noodles.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or buns
  • Soup: Not needed; the stew is its own soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
