Teriyaki Chicken Thigh 鶏ももの照り焼き

▾

Surprise me with

Teriyaki ("shine-grill") describes the glossy, mirin-sweetened glaze achieved by reducing the sauce directly in the pan with the chicken — among the most widely recognized Japanese home-cooking techniques abroad and at home.

Method

  1. Score the meat side of the thighs every 1 cm (1/2 in) without cutting through, so they lie flat and cook evenly. Season lightly with salt and pepper. Mix the soy sauce, mirin, sake and sugar until the sugar dissolves.
  2. Heat the oil in a skillet over medium heat and lay the thighs in skin-side down, pressing them flat. Cook for 5 to 6 minutes without moving, until the skin is golden and crisp.
  3. Turn the thighs, lower the heat, cover and cook for 3 to 4 minutes until almost cooked through.
  4. Wipe out the fat with a paper towel; otherwise the sauce will not cling. Pour in the sauce, raise the heat to medium and simmer, turning and basting the chicken, for 2 to 3 minutes until the sauce reduces to a thick, glossy glaze. No cornstarch is needed; the sugar and mirin thicken it.
  5. Slice the chicken, spoon the glaze over and sprinkle with sesame seeds. Serve with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the sauce too early, before the skin has properly rendered and browned, which can leave it soggy.
  • Not spooning the sauce over the chicken as it reduces, which leads to uneven glazing.

Authentic tips

  • Searing skin-side down first, undisturbed, is what gives teriyaki chicken its crisp base under the glaze.
  • The sauce should reduce until it coats the back of a spoon — too thin and it won't cling properly.

Substitutions

  • Chicken breast works but is more prone to drying out during the reduction; watch closely.
  • No mirin: a mix of sake and a touch more sugar approximates it reasonably well.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat gently to avoid drying out.

Freezing: Freezes well, cooked, for up to a month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and blanched broccoli
  • Soup: Miso soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot sencha
