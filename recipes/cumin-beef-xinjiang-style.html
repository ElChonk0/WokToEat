Cumin Beef, Xinjiang-Style 孜然牛肉

▾

Surprise me with

Reflecting the influence of China's northwestern Xinjiang region and its Uyghur culinary traditions, cumin beef pairs the earthy warmth of toasted cumin with chili for a fragrant, distinctly different flavor from eastern Chinese cooking.

Method

  1. Marinate beef in soy sauce and cornstarch for 15 minutes.
  2. Toast cumin seeds and chili flakes in a dry pan briefly until fragrant; set aside.
  3. Stir-fry beef in hot oil over high heat until just browned; remove and set aside.
  4. Fry garlic and onion until softened, then return beef to the pan with the toasted cumin and chili.
  5. Toss everything together over high heat until well combined; garnish with cilantro before serving.

Common mistakes

  • Using pre-ground cumin instead of whole seeds toasted fresh, which lacks the same aromatic punch.
  • Overcooking the beef, which turns it tough — sear quickly over very high heat.

Authentic tips

  • Toasting whole cumin seeds briefly before use dramatically improves their aroma compared to pre-ground cumin.
  • This dish is a signature of Xinjiang cuisine and tastes distinctly different from more familiar eastern Chinese flavors — worth trying if new to you.

Substitutions

  • Lamb is the traditional meat; slice it very thin.
  • Chicken thighs take the same method.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Nang or steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Hami melon
  • Beverage: Black tea
