Chai Tow Kway (Singapore Fried Carrot Cake) 菜头粿

▾

Surprise me with

Chai tow kway, called "carrot cake" in Singapore though it is made of white radish, is a steamed rice-flour cake cubed and fried with egg and preserved radish, in a white or a sweet black soy version.

Method

  1. Simmer the grated radish with a cup of the water, covered, for 5 minutes until soft and translucent. Fry the shallots and mushrooms in the 45 ml of oil until golden, then add them to the radish.
  2. Off the heat, whisk the rice flour with the remaining water, the salt, sugar and pepper, then stir it into the radish mixture and cook over low heat, stirring, until it turns into a thick, pasty batter.
  3. Spread it in a lined cake pan and steam over high heat for 50 to 60 minutes, until a chopstick comes out clean. Cool, then chill for at least 6 hours; it must be cold and firm, or the cubes fall apart in the wok.
  4. Cut the cake into 2 cm (3/4 in) cubes. Heat the frying oil in a flat-bottom wok over high heat and fry the cubes in one layer, without stirring, for 4 to 5 minutes until a golden crust forms, then turn them.
  5. Add the garlic and chai poh and fry for 1 minute, then pour the beaten eggs over the cake. Leave for 30 seconds until they set, then toss and break into pieces.
  6. For black carrot cake, add the kecap manis and soy sauce and toss until caramelized; for white, use only the light soy. Scatter with green onion and serve with chili sauce.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Cutting the cake while warm makes it crumble; chill it first.
  • Stirring constantly stops the cubes browning; let them sit to crisp.

Authentic tips

  • Use a hot, well-seasoned wok or non-stick pan to prevent sticking.
  • Skip the dark soy for the white version, or add more for the black version.

Substitutions

  • Dried shrimp and Chinese sausage are traditional additions.
  • Store-bought carrot cake blocks replace the homemade cake.

Storage

Leftovers: The steamed cake keeps 4 days in the refrigerator; fry fresh.

Freezing: The steamed cake freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Chili sauce and pickled green chilies
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Not needed
  • Beverage: Hot kopi
