Xian Hua Jian Dan (Yunnan Edible Flower Omelette) 鲜花煎蛋

Yunnan cooks use edible flowers such as jasmine, squash blossoms and rose in stir-fries and omelettes, a legacy of the province's mild climate and plant diversity.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 8 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Yunnan Tofu & Vegetarian LunchDinnerStir-fryVegetarian30 min or lessEgg

  • 66Eggs
  • 60 g2 ozEdible flowers (squash blossoms, jasmine)
  • 20 g2 tbspScallion
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Beat the eggs with the salt until just combined and fold in the flowers and scallion.
  2. Heat the oil in a nonstick pan over medium heat.
  3. Pour in the egg mixture and cook 2-3 minutes until the edges set.
  4. Flip carefully and cook 1-2 minutes more.
  5. Slide onto a plate and cut into wedges.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking on too high a heat burns the delicate flowers.
  • Using flowers not meant to be eaten can be harmful; use only edible varieties.

Authentic tips

  • Squash blossoms or jasmine work.
  • Do not use florist flowers; they may be sprayed.

Substitutions

  • Squash blossoms are the most widely available flowers.
  • Never use florist flowers.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Pu'er tea
