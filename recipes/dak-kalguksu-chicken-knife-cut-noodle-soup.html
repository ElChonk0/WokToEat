Dak Kalguksu (Chicken Knife-Cut Noodle Soup) 닭칼국수

▾

Surprise me with

Dak kalguksu, hand-cut wheat noodles in a rich chicken broth topped with shredded chicken, is a Korean comfort food eaten especially on rainy days.

Method

  1. Simmer the chicken in the water with the onion, garlic, ginger and green onion whites for 40 minutes, skimming. Lift out the chicken, shred the meat and discard skin and bones. Strain the broth.
  2. Season the shredded chicken with a pinch of salt, pepper and a few drops of sesame oil if you like.
  3. Bring the broth back to a boil, add the potato and simmer 5 minutes.
  4. Shake the excess flour off the noodles, add them with the zucchini and cook 4 to 5 minutes, stirring, until tender; the starch thickens the broth slightly.
  5. Season with soup soy sauce, salt and pepper, ladle into bowls and top with the chicken, green onion and a pinch of gochugaru.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the noodles with lots of flour on them turns the soup gluey.
  • Boiling the chicken hard makes a cloudy, greasy broth.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with fresh geotjeori (quick kimchi) as Korean noodle houses do.
  • A clear broth comes from skimming well in the first 10 minutes.

Substitutions

  • Udon or dried wide wheat noodles replace kalguksu noodles.
  • Anchovy and kelp stock is the other traditional broth, for a lighter version.

Storage

Leftovers: Store broth and chicken separately for 3 days; cook noodles fresh.

Freezing: The broth and chicken freeze for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Kimchi or geotjeori
  • Soup: This is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
