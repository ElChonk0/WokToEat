Pad Prik King Beef (Dry Red Curry Stir-Fry)

▾

Surprise me with

Pad prik king is a drier stir-fry version of Thai red curry, cooking the paste down with meat and long beans until concentrated and only lightly sauced, rather than the soupier consistency of a full curry.

  • Cook 10 min

  • 20 ml4 tspFish sauce
  • 15 g1 tbspPalm sugar
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Fry curry paste in oil over medium heat until fragrant and the oil separates slightly, 2–3 minutes.
  2. Add beef and stir-fry until just browned, 2–3 minutes.
  3. Add long beans and stir-fry 3–4 minutes until just tender-crisp.
  4. Add fish sauce and palm sugar, tossing to coat; the dish should remain fairly dry, not saucy.
  5. Stir in kaffir lime leaves just before serving.

Common mistakes

  • Adding extra liquid, which turns this into a soupier curry rather than the intended dry stir-fry.
  • Overcooking the long beans, which loses their characteristic crisp bite.

Authentic tips

  • This dish should end up mostly dry, with the paste clinging to the beef and beans rather than pooling as sauce.
  • Thinly sliced kaffir lime leaves add a bright, citrusy top note best added at the very end.

Substitutions

  • Pork or chicken take the same paste.
  • Green beans replace long beans.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Jasmine rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
