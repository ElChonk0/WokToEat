Jiā Cháng Dòu Fu (Home-Style Tofu) 家常豆腐

▾

Surprise me with

Home-style tofu, pan-fried slices braised with doubanjiang, leeks and peppers, is a Sichuan family dish that is as common in homes as mapo tofu is in restaurants.

  • 100 ml1/3 cupVegetable stock or water
  • 60 ml4 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Cut the tofu into 1 cm slabs and pat dry. Slice the peppers and leeks.
  2. Heat half the oil in a nonstick pan over medium heat and fry the tofu 3 minutes per side until golden; lift out.
  3. Add the remaining oil and fry the doubanjiang and garlic for 30 seconds until the oil turns red.
  4. Add the peppers and leeks and stir-fry 1 minute, then the soy sauce, sugar and stock.
  5. Return the tofu, simmer 3 minutes and serve.

Common mistakes

  • Turning the tofu too often breaks it; let each side brown fully before flipping.
  • Doubanjiang is salty, so taste before adding more salt or soy sauce.

Authentic tips

  • Patting the tofu dry gives a better sear.
  • Chop the doubanjiang finely so it disperses into the sauce.

Substitutions

  • Extra-firm tofu holds its shape best.
  • Wood ear mushrooms add crunch.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat gently.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
