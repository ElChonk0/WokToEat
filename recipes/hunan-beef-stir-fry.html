Hunan Beef Stir-Fry 辣椒牛肉

▾

Surprise me with

A fiery, garlic-and-chili-forward stir-fry that showcases Hunan's love of fresh green chilies, quickly cooked with thin-sliced beef for maximum heat and minimal fuss.

  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 500 g1 lbFlank steak, sliced thin against the grain
  • 66Green chilies (Hunan-style), sliced
  • 4 cloves4 clovesGarlic, sliced
  • 10 g2 tspGinger, minced
  • 10 g1 tbspFermented black beans, chopped
  • 15 ml1 tbspSoy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 10 ml2 tspCornstarch

Method

  1. Marinate beef in soy sauce and cornstarch for 15 minutes.
  2. Stir-fry beef in hot oil over high heat until just browned; remove and set aside.
  3. Fry garlic, ginger, and black beans until fragrant.
  4. Add green chilies and stir-fry 2–3 minutes until slightly blistered.
  5. Return beef to the wok with wine; toss over high heat until combined.

Common mistakes

  • Using bell peppers instead of genuinely spicy green chilies, which loses the dish's characteristic heat.
  • Overcooking the beef, which turns it tough — sear quickly over very high heat.

Authentic tips

  • This dish is meant to be quite spicy and simple — don't over-season beyond the core ingredients.
  • High heat and quick cooking are essential; the whole stir-fry should take only a few minutes.

Substitutions

  • Serrano or jalapeno peppers replace Hunan-style chilies.
  • Pork takes the same method.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

  • Soup: Simple egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Sliced watermelon
  • Beverage: Cold beer
