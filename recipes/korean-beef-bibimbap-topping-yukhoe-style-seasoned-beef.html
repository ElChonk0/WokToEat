Korean Beef Bibimbap Topping (Yukhoe-Style Seasoned Beef)

Bibimbap tops a bowl of rice with an array of seasoned vegetables and a portion of seasoned beef, traditionally mixed together with gochujang at the table — one of Korea's most iconic composed rice dishes.

  • Prep 15 min (plus 20 min marinating)
  • Cook 8 min

Method

  1. Combine beef with soy sauce, sesame oil, garlic, and sugar; marinate 20 minutes.
  2. Stir-fry the marinated beef in a hot pan until just cooked through, 3–4 minutes.
  3. Divide rice among bowls.
  4. Arrange the beef and an assortment of seasoned vegetables over the rice in separate sections.
  5. Top each bowl with a fried egg and a dollop of gochujang; mix everything together at the table before eating.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the thin beef slices, which toughens them quickly given how fast they cook.
  • Overcrowding the pan, which steams the beef instead of searing it properly.

Authentic tips

  • Bibimbap is meant to be a showcase of variety — aim for at least four or five different vegetable preparations alongside the beef.
  • Mix everything together thoroughly at the table just before eating, distributing the gochujang evenly.

Substitutions

  • Ground beef works as a quicker alternative to sliced beef.
  • A stone bowl (dolsot), if available, keeps the rice crisping at the bottom for a traditional textural contrast.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; store components separately and assemble fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Gochujang and assorted namul
  • Soup: Doenjang soup
  • Dessert: Sliced pear
  • Beverage: Barley tea
