Kimchi Jjigae with Pork 김치찌개

▾

Surprise me with

One of Korea's most iconic stews, kimchi jjigae simmers well-fermented kimchi with pork belly for a deeply sour, spicy, savory pot — a dish that improves with genuinely aged, sour kimchi.

Method

  1. Stir-fry pork belly in a pot over medium heat until lightly browned and some fat renders.
  2. Add kimchi and stir-fry 3–4 minutes until fragrant and slightly caramelized.
  3. Add kimchi juice, gochugaru, garlic, and onion, stirring briefly.
  4. Add water or stock, bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer, cover, and cook 15 minutes.
  5. Add tofu and simmer 5 minutes more; garnish with green onion before serving.

Common mistakes

  • Using fresh, unfermented kimchi, which lacks the sourness that gives this stew its depth — well-aged kimchi is genuinely better here.
  • Adding the tofu too early, which can cause it to fall apart by serving time.

Authentic tips

  • This is one of the best uses for kimchi that has fermented past its prime for eating plain — sourer kimchi makes a better jjigae.
  • Stir-frying the kimchi with the pork before adding liquid deepens the stew's flavor considerably.

Substitutions

  • Canned tuna replaces pork.
  • Aged, sour kimchi gives the best stew.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; the stew improves.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and a rolled omelet
  • Soup: Not needed; the stew is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
