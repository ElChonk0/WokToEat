Dou Chi Chao Ge Li (Clams in Black Bean Sauce) 豉汁炒蛤蜊

Clams stir-fried in fermented black bean sauce are a Cantonese seafood-restaurant standby.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 8 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

  • 1.2 kg2.6 lbClams, purged
  • 30 g2 tbspFermented black beans, mashed
  • 40 g3 tbspGarlic and ginger
  • 15 g1 tbspChilies
  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 15 ml1 tbspOyster sauce
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Soak the clams in salted water for 30 minutes; rinse and drain, discarding any that stay open when tapped.
  2. Rinse the black beans and mash them with a little of the wine into a rough paste.
  3. Heat the oil in a wok over high heat and fry the garlic, ginger, chilies and mashed beans for 20 seconds until fragrant.
  4. Add the clams and the remaining wine, toss, cover and cook 3-4 minutes, shaking the pan, until the shells open.
  5. Remove the opened clams as they pop, stir the oyster sauce into the pan and pour over; discard any that stay closed.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the soak leaves sand in every clam.
  • Cooking clams after they open makes them tough; take each batch off the heat as it opens.

Authentic tips

  • Rinse the fermented black beans briefly if they taste very salty.
  • Add a splash of stock if you want more sauce for rice.

Substitutions

  • Mussels replace the clams; cook until just open.
  • Halve the chilies for a milder dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Eat immediately; cooked clams turn rubbery on reheating.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Choy sum
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot jasmine tea
