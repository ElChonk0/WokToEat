Jiàohuā Jī (Beggar's Chicken) 叫花鸡

▾

Surprise me with

Beggar's chicken, chicken wrapped in lotus leaf and clay and baked, is a Jiangnan specialty associated with Hangzhou; legend says a beggar baked a stolen chicken in mud.

  • Prep 1 hr (plus 4 hr marinating)
  • Cook 3 hr

Method

  1. Rub the chicken inside and out with the soy sauces, Shaoxing wine, sugar and five-spice, put the ginger and green onion in the cavity and marinate for at least 4 hours.
  2. Stir-fry the pork belly in the oil for 3 minutes, add the shiitake, ham and bamboo shoots and a spoon of the marinade and cook for 3 minutes. Cool, then stuff the chicken with it and close the cavity with a skewer.
  3. Wrap the chicken tightly in the soaked lotus leaves, then in a layer of parchment, and tie with string.
  4. Mix the flour, salt and water into a stiff dough, roll it out 1 cm (1/2 in) thick and wrap it completely around the chicken, sealing every seam. Traditionally the bird was wrapped in mud and buried in embers; the crust seals in the juices.
  5. Bake at 200 C (400 F) for 30 minutes, then lower to 160 C (325 F) and bake for 2 to 2.5 hours more.
  6. Crack the crust open at the table with a mallet, unwrap the fragrant leaves and serve the chicken, which should be tender enough to pull apart with chopsticks.

Common mistakes

  • A leak in the casing lets the steam escape, which leaves the chicken dry.
  • Cutting the casing open too soon sends the fragrant steam and juices away; rest it first.

Authentic tips

  • Lotus leaves give a tea-like fragrance; parchment makes a poor but workable substitute.
  • Serve with the stuffing and the juices spooned over.

Substitutions

  • Foil plus parchment works if you can't find dried lotus leaves.
  • Chicken legs stuffed the same way cook in 75 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a 180 C (350 F) oven for 15 minutes.

Freezing: Freezes the cooked meat for 2 months; shred for soups.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or lotus-leaf buns
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot jasmine tea
