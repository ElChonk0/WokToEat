Tenshinhan (Crab Omelet over Rice) 天津飯

▾

Surprise me with

Tenshinhan, a fluffy crab omelet on rice under a glossy sweet-sour sauce, is a Japanese-Chinese (chuka) restaurant creation of the early 20th century.

Method

  1. Simmer the dashi, soy sauce, vinegar, sugar and ketchup, then thicken with the potato starch mixed with a little water. Keep warm.
  2. Beat 2 eggs with a quarter of the crab, green onion and salt.
  3. Heat 2 teaspoons of oil in a non-stick skillet over medium-high heat, pour in the egg and stir quickly for 20 seconds, then let it set into a soft round omelet.
  4. Slide it over a mound of rice in a bowl. Repeat for the other 3 portions.
  5. Pour the sauce over each omelet and scatter with green onion.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooked egg loses the fluffy texture.
  • A thin sauce runs off; it should be thick and glossy.

Authentic tips

  • Use a small 20 cm (8 in) pan so each omelet is the right size for a bowl.
  • Kansai shops make a soy-based sauce; Tokyo shops add ketchup and vinegar.

Substitutions

  • Shrimp or crab sticks.
  • Add shredded shiitake to the eggs.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Almond tofu
  • Beverage: Oolong tea
