Thai Beef Salad (Neua Nam Tok) เนื้อน้ำตก

▾

Surprise me with

Nam tok ("waterfall," referring to the juices that drip while grilling) is an Isaan-style grilled beef salad tossed with toasted rice powder, lime, and fish sauce — a close cousin of larb with a stronger grilled character.

Method

  1. Toast the sticky rice in a dry skillet over medium-low heat for 10 to 15 minutes until deep golden, then grind it coarsely.
  2. Season the steak and grill it over high heat for 3 to 4 minutes per side, until charred outside and medium-rare inside. Nam tok means 'waterfall', for the juices that drip from the grilling meat.
  3. Rest the steak for 5 minutes, then slice it thinly against the grain, keeping the juices.
  4. Toss the warm beef and its juices with the fish sauce, lime juice, sugar and chili flakes. The warm meat absorbs the dressing.
  5. Fold in the shallots, green onion, mint, cilantro and most of the rice powder. Sprinkle with the rest and serve with raw vegetables and sticky rice.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the beef, since this dish is traditionally made with medium-rare beef sliced thin.
  • Adding the herbs too early, which wilts them and dulls their bright, fresh flavor.

Authentic tips

  • Toasted rice powder is simple to make at home: toast raw sticky rice in a dry pan until deep golden, then grind fine.
  • This dish should taste assertively balanced between sour, salty, and spicy — taste and adjust before serving.

Substitutions

  • Pork or chicken can be prepared similarly, though beef is most traditional for nam tok specifically.
  • No sticky rice for the powder: any raw rice, toasted and ground, works similarly.
  • No grill: broil on a rack about 8 cm (3 in) from the element for 3 to 4 minutes per side for medium-rare, then rest 5 minutes before slicing; a hot ridged pan also works.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; the herbs lose some vibrancy and the beef firms up.

Freezing: Not recommended — fresh herbs and rare beef don't hold up.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sticky rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Singha beer
