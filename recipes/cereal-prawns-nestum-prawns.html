Cereal Prawns (Nestum Prawns)

▾

Surprise me with

Cereal prawns are a Malaysian and Singapore zi char dish in which fried prawns are tossed with butter, curry leaves and toasted cereal, usually Nestum, until the crumbs cling in a crunchy, buttery coating.

  • 10 g2 tspSugar
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt, for the cereal

Method

  1. Trim the prawn legs and feelers and devein them through the back, leaving the shells on. Pat them very dry and toss with the salt, then the cornstarch.
  2. Heat the frying oil in a flat-bottom wok or Dutch oven to 180 C (350 F) and fry the prawns in batches for 2 to 3 minutes, until pink, crisp and cooked through. Drain on a rack.
  3. Mix the cereal, milk powder, sugar and cereal salt in a bowl.
  4. Pour out the oil and wipe the wok. Melt the butter over low heat and fry the garlic, curry leaves and chilies for 1 to 2 minutes, until the curry leaves are crisp and fragrant.
  5. Keeping the heat low, add the cereal mixture and stir constantly for 1 to 2 minutes, until it turns golden and fluffy; the sugar and milk powder burn quickly over high heat.
  6. Add the prawns and toss until they are buried in the buttery cereal. Serve at once, while the cereal is crunchy.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the garlic or cereal turns the dish bitter; keep the heat at medium and stir constantly.
  • Frying the prawns in a crowded pan steams them; fry in batches.

Authentic tips

  • The shell protects the meat and adds crunch, so eat the prawns shell and all if you like.
  • Dry the prawns thoroughly so the oil does not spit.

Substitutions

  • Rolled oats toasted with milk powder replace Nestum.
  • Squid rings replace prawns.

Storage

Leftovers: Best eaten at once; the cereal softens within an hour.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and stir-fried greens
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh pineapple
  • Beverage: Iced lemon tea
