Yīng Zuǐ Dòu Tāng (Uyghur Chickpea and Vegetable Soup) 鹰嘴豆蔬菜汤

Chickpeas are a staple legume of Xinjiang and Central Asia, simmered in soups with root vegetables and spices.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 45 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 200 g7 ozDried chickpeas, soaked overnight
  • 200 g7 ozCarrots
  • 250 g9 ozPotatoes
  • 100 g3.5 ozOnion
  • 200 g7 ozTomatoes
  • 15 g3 clovesGarlic
  • 3 g1 tspCumin
  • 2 g3/4 tspBlack pepper
  • 8 g1.5 tspSalt
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil
  • 1.5 L6 cupsWater
  • 15 g1/2 ozCilantro

Method

  1. Drain the soaked chickpeas and simmer in the water for 30 minutes until nearly tender.
  2. Meanwhile fry the onion and garlic in the oil for 5 minutes; add the cumin and tomatoes and cook 5 minutes into a thick paste.
  3. Add the paste to the chickpeas along with the carrots and potatoes in chunks.
  4. Simmer 20 minutes until all the vegetables are tender.
  5. Season with the salt and pepper and garnish with cilantro.

Common mistakes

  • Under-soaked chickpeas stay hard; soak overnight.
  • Adding the salt too early toughens the chickpeas.

Authentic tips

  • Mash a few chickpeas into the broth to thicken it.
  • Serve with nang flatbread.

Substitutions

  • Use canned chickpeas (about 450 g drained) and reduce the first simmer to 5 minutes.
  • Add lamb ribs for the traditional version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; thickens, so thin with water.

Freezing: Freezes well for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Nang flatbread
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Dried fruit
  • Beverage: Black tea
