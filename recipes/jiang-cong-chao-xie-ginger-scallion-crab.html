Jiang Cong Chao Xie (Ginger-Scallion Crab) 姜葱炒蟹

Wok-fried crab with ginger and scallion is a Cantonese banquet dish whose generous ginger offsets the richness of the crab.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 1 kg2.2 lbCrab, cleaned and cut
  • 30 g2 tbspCornstarch
  • 60 g4 tbspGinger, sliced
  • 100 g3.5 ozScallions, in 5 cm lengths
  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 15 ml1 tbspOyster sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 60 ml4 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Clean the crab: remove the apron and gills, split the body in half and crack the claws with the back of a cleaver.
  2. Dust the cut surfaces lightly with the cornstarch.
  3. Heat the oil in a wok over high heat and fry the crab pieces 3-4 minutes, turning, until the shells turn bright red; remove.
  4. Fry the ginger 30 seconds, then the scallions 15 seconds, until fragrant.
  5. Return the crab, add the wine, oyster sauce, soy sauce and 60 ml water, cover and cook 4 minutes until the meat is opaque.
  6. Uncover, toss over high heat to thicken the sauce slightly and serve.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking dries out the sweet meat; 4 minutes covered is enough for a 1 kg crab.
  • Skipping the starch lets the juices leak out and the sauce will not cling.

Authentic tips

  • Cut the ginger into thick coins; plenty of it is the point.
  • Crack the claws first so they absorb the sauce.

Substitutions

  • Shrimp or lobster tails can replace the crab.
  • Toss cooked thick rice noodles in the leftover sauce for a second dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; leftover crab keeps 1 day and the meat can be picked into fried rice.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or e-fu noodles
  • Soup: Choy sum
  • Dessert: Sweet red bean soup
  • Beverage: Hot jasmine tea
