Zi Ran Ji Chi (Cumin Chicken Wings) 孜然鸡翅

Cumin and chili wings take the Xinjiang skewer seasoning to everyday chicken parts, common at barbecue stalls across China.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 35 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Xinjiang Chicken LunchDinnerGrilled / roastedSpicy

  • 1 kg2.2 lbChicken wings
  • 10 g1 tbspCumin, ground
  • 6 g2 tspChili flakes
  • 8 g1.5 tspSalt
  • 30 ml2 tbspSoy sauce
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 15 ml1 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Toss the wings with the soy sauce, garlic, oil and half the cumin; marinate 30 minutes.
  2. Heat the oven to 220 C (425 F) and arrange the wings on a rack over a tray.
  3. Roast 30-35 minutes, turning once, until deep golden and crisp.
  4. Toss with the remaining cumin, chili flakes and salt.
  5. Serve hot.

Common mistakes

  • Crowding the tray steams the wings.
  • Sprinkling spices too early burns them.

Authentic tips

  • Pat the wings dry for crisp skin.
  • A hot air fryer gives a similar result in 20 minutes.

Substitutions

  • Chicken drumettes work equally well.
  • An air fryer at 200 C (400 F) takes 22 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; recrisp in a hot oven.

Freezing: Freezes cooked wings for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Nang flatbread
  • Soup: Cucumber yogurt salad
  • Dessert: Hami melon
  • Beverage: Cold beer or black tea
