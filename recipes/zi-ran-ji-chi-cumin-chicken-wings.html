Zī Rán Jī Chì (Cumin Chicken Wings) 孜然鸡翅

▾

Surprise me with

Cumin and chili wings take the Xinjiang skewer seasoning to everyday chicken parts, common at barbecue stalls across China.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 35 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Xinjiang Chicken LunchDinnerGrilled / roastedSpicy

  • 1 kg2.2 lbChicken wings, split
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 20 g3/4 ozGarlic, grated
  • 15 ml1 tbspNeutral oil
  • 10 g1.5 tbspGround cumin, plus some whole seeds
  • 6 g2 tspChili flakes
  • 5 g1 tspSalt
  • 5 g2 tspSesame seeds (optional)

Method

  1. Toss the wings with the soy sauce, garlic and oil and marinate for at least 1 hour.
  2. Mix the cumin, chili flakes, salt and sesame seeds into a spice mix.
  3. Grill the wings over medium charcoal or roast them on a rack at 220 C (425 F) for 25 to 30 minutes, turning once, until crisp and cooked through.
  4. Sprinkle generously with the spice mix in the last 5 minutes so it toasts without burning.
  5. Dust with a little more spice mix and serve hot.

Common mistakes

  • Crowding the tray steams the wings.
  • Sprinkling spices too early burns them.

Authentic tips

  • Pat the wings dry for crisp skin.
  • A hot air fryer gives a similar result in 20 minutes.

Substitutions

  • Chicken drumettes work equally well.
  • An air fryer at 200 C (400 F) takes 22 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; recrisp in a hot oven.

Freezing: Freezes cooked wings for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Nang flatbread
  • Soup: Cucumber yogurt salad
  • Dessert: Hami melon
  • Beverage: Cold beer or black tea
