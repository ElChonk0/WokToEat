Soto Ayam (Turmeric Chicken Soup)

▾

Surprise me with

Soto ayam is a turmeric-yellow Indonesian chicken soup, fragrant with lemongrass and lime leaf, typically served with rice noodles, hard-boiled egg, and a scatter of fried shallots.

Method

  1. Blend or pound turmeric, garlic, shallot, and ginger into a paste.
  2. Fry the paste in a little oil in a large pot until fragrant, 3–4 minutes.
  3. Add chicken, lemongrass, kaffir lime leaves, and stock; bring to a simmer.
  4. Simmer uncovered 25–30 minutes until chicken is cooked through; remove, shred the meat, and return to the pot.
  5. Season the broth with salt to taste.
  6. Divide vermicelli among bowls, top with shredded chicken, ladle hot broth over, and garnish with hard-boiled egg, fried shallots, and cilantro.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the step of frying the spice paste before adding liquid: raw paste added directly to broth tastes flat and slightly bitter.

Authentic tips

  • A double batch of broth freezes well and turns this into an easy future weeknight soup.

Substitutions

  • No fresh turmeric: ground turmeric works, using about half the amount since it's more concentrated.

Storage

Leftovers: Store broth and noodles separately to avoid mushy noodles.

Freezing: Broth freezes excellently up to 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or lontong, sambal and lime
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Es teh
