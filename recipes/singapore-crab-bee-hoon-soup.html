Singapore Crab Bee Hoon Soup

▾

Surprise me with

Crab bee hoon is a Singapore zi char dish of thin rice vermicelli in a rich crab and evaporated-milk soup, served at seafood restaurants and now at hawker stalls.

Method

  1. Put a live crab in the freezer for 30 minutes, then clean it, cut it into pieces and crack the claws. Soak the bee hoon in warm water for 15 minutes and drain.
  2. Heat the oil in a large Dutch oven over medium-high heat and fry the ginger and garlic for 1 minute, then add the crab and stir-fry for 2 to 3 minutes until the shells start to redden. Splash in the wine.
  3. Pour in the stock, bring to a boil and simmer, covered, for 6 to 8 minutes, until the crab is cooked and its sweetness has gone into the broth.
  4. Add the bee hoon and simmer for 2 to 3 minutes until soft, then stir in the evaporated milk and season with the pepper and salt. The evaporated milk gives the broth its signature creamy, milky body; keep it at a gentle simmer once added.
  5. Serve in the pot, scattered with green onion and fried shallots.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling after adding the milk can make the soup split; keep it at a gentle simmer.
  • Over-soaking the vermicelli makes it fall apart.

Authentic tips

  • Crack the claws lightly with the back of a cleaver so the flavor goes into the soup.
  • Mud crab gives the sweetest broth, but blue swimmer crab works.

Substitutions

  • Large prawns replace crab.
  • Coconut milk replaces evaporated milk for a dairy-free version.

Storage

Leftovers: Best eaten fresh; the vermicelli soaks up the soup overnight.

Freezing: Not recommended, because the soup and noodles go soggy.
