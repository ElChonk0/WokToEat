You Yu Geng (Taiwanese Squid Thick Soup) 魷魚羹

Squid thick soup, a starchy broth with squid, bamboo shoots and mushrooms seasoned with black vinegar, is a Taiwanese street food.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 400 g14 ozSquid, cleaned and cut
  • 80 g3 ozBamboo shoots, shredded
  • 20 g2/3 ozDried shiitake, soaked
  • 1 L4 cupsStock
  • 60 ml4 tbspSweet potato starch slurry
  • 30 ml2 tbspBlack vinegar
  • 15 g1/2 ozCilantro
  • 1 g1/4 tspWhite pepper
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt

Method

  1. Soak the shiitake and slice; keep the liquid.
  2. Bring the stock with the bamboo and mushrooms to a simmer for 8 minutes.
  3. Stir in the slurry and simmer 1 minute until thick.
  4. Add the squid and cook 1 minute until curled.
  5. Season with salt, vinegar, pepper and cilantro.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the squid makes it rubbery.
  • Adding the slurry too fast makes lumps.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with noodles or rice.
  • Black vinegar is essential.

Substitutions

  • Cuttlefish replaces squid.
  • Stock with a splash of fish sauce enriches the broth.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated; thickens as it sits.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is a thick soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Winter melon tea
