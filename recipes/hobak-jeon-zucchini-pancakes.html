Hobak Jeon (Zucchini Pancakes) 호박전

▾

Hobak jeon, egg-battered zucchini rounds pan-fried until golden, is a staple of Korean holiday tables.

  • Cook 10 min

  • 500 g1.1 lbZucchini, cut into 8 mm (1/3 in) rounds
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt
  • 40 g1/3 cupFlour
  • 33Eggs, beaten
  • 11Red chili or parsley leaves, for garnish (optional)
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 15 ml1 tbspSoy sauce, for dipping
  • 15 ml1 tbspWater, for dipping
  • 5 ml1 tspRice vinegar, for dipping

Method

  1. Cut the zucchini into rounds, sprinkle lightly with salt on both sides and leave for 15 to 20 minutes. Pat them dry; salting seasons them and draws out water so they do not steam in the pan.
  2. Dredge each round in flour on both sides and shake off the excess.
  3. Heat a tablespoon of oil in a large nonstick skillet over medium heat. Dip each round in the beaten egg and lay it in the pan.
  4. Cook for about 1 minute until the underside is lightly golden. Press a small piece of chili or a parsley leaf onto each while the top is still wet, then turn and cook about 1 minute more. The zucchini should keep a slight bite and the egg should stay pale gold.
  5. Add a little oil for each batch. Mix the soy sauce, water and vinegar and serve the jeon warm with the dip.

Common mistakes

  • Wet zucchini sheds the batter.
  • Too high a heat burns the egg.

Authentic tips

  • Eaten at Chuseok.
  • Serve warm.

Substitutions

  • Eggplant or mushrooms also work.
  • Pat the zucchini dry for crisper results.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a pan.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Sikhye
