Hobak Jeon (Zucchini Pancakes) 호박전

▾

Surprise me with

Hobak jeon, egg-battered zucchini rounds pan-fried until golden, is a staple of Korean holiday tables.

  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 500 g1.1 lbZucchini, sliced
  • 60 g1/2 cupFlour
  • 33Eggs
  • 5 g1 tspSalt
  • 45 ml3 tbspSoy-vinegar dip
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Slice the zucchini 7 mm thick and salt for 10 minutes; pat dry.
  2. Dredge in the flour.
  3. Dip in the beaten egg.
  4. Pan-fry in the oil over medium heat 2 minutes per side until golden.
  5. Serve with the dip.

Common mistakes

  • Wet zucchini sheds the batter.
  • Too high a heat burns the egg.

Authentic tips

  • Eaten at Chuseok.
  • Serve warm.

Substitutions

  • Eggplant or mushrooms also work.
  • Pat the zucchini dry for crisper results.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a pan.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Sikhye
