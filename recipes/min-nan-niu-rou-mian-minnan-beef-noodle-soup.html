Mǐn Nán Niú Ròu Miàn (Minnan Beef Noodle Soup) 闽南牛肉面

▾

Surprise me with

Minnan beef noodles in a clear, sweet beef broth with ginger are a staple of Xiamen and Quanzhou noodle shops, shaped by the region's Hokkien cooking.

  • Cook 2.5 hr

Method

  1. Blanch the brisket for 5 minutes and rinse.
  2. Simmer it in the water with the ginger, green onion and star anise, covered, for 2 hours until tender. Lift it out and slice.
  3. Season the broth with soy sauce, fish sauce, rock sugar and salt. Minnan (southern Fujian) broths are clear and lightly sweet.
  4. Cook the noodles and blanch the bok choy.
  5. Divide among bowls, top with brisket, ladle over the broth and finish with cilantro and fried shallots, with shacha sauce on the side.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the blanch gives a cloudy broth.
  • Cooking the noodles in the broth makes it starchy.

Authentic tips

  • The broth should be clear and slightly sweet.
  • Add a splash of white pepper and a spoonful of fried garlic at the table.

Substitutions

  • Short rib adds richness.
  • Ready-made stock saves an hour.

Storage

Leftovers: Store noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 4 days.

Freezing: Freeze the broth and beef for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled vegetables
  • Soup: Not needed; the noodle soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
