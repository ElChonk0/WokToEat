Mǐn Nán Niú Ròu Miàn (Minnan Beef Noodle Soup) 闽南牛肉面

▾

Surprise me with

Minnan beef noodles in a clear, sweet beef broth with ginger are a staple of Xiamen and Quanzhou noodle shops, shaped by the region's Hokkien cooking.

  • 400 g14 ozRice or wheat noodles

  • 60 g4 tbspGinger and scallion
  • 45 ml3 tbspSoy sauce
  • 10 g2 tspRock sugar
  • 150 g5 ozBok choy and cilantro
  • 2 L8 cupsWater

Method

  1. Cover the brisket with cold water, boil 5 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  2. Return to the pot with 2 L water, the ginger, scallion and rock sugar and simmer gently for 90 minutes until tender.
  3. Lift out the beef, slice thinly across the grain and season the broth with the soy sauce.
  4. Cook the noodles in a separate pot, adding the bok choy for the last 30 seconds; drain and divide among bowls.
  5. Top with beef and cilantro and ladle over the hot broth.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the blanch gives a cloudy broth.
  • Cooking the noodles in the broth makes it starchy.

Authentic tips

  • The broth should be clear and slightly sweet.
  • Add a splash of white pepper and a spoonful of fried garlic at the table.

Substitutions

  • Short rib adds richness.
  • Ready-made stock saves an hour.

Storage

Leftovers: Store noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 4 days.

Freezing: Freeze the broth and beef for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled vegetables
  • Soup: Not needed; the noodle soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
