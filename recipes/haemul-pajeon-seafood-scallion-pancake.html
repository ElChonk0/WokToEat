Haemul Pajeon (Seafood Scallion Pancake) 해물파전

▾

Surprise me with

Pajeon, a scallion pancake, is a traditional Korean food, especially on rainy days when it is paired with makgeolli; haemul pajeon adds shrimp and squid.

  • Cook 15 min

Method

  1. Whisk the flours, water and a pinch of salt into a thin batter.
  2. Heat half the oil in a nonstick pan over medium-high heat.
  3. Lay half the scallions in the pan, pour over half the batter, scatter half the seafood on top and drizzle with half the egg.
  4. Cook 4 minutes until golden, flip and cook 3-4 minutes more, pressing down.
  5. Repeat for a second pancake and serve with the dipping sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Thick batter makes a doughy pancake.
  • Flipping too early tears it.

Authentic tips

  • Add more oil to crisp the edges.
  • Serve with makgeolli.

Substitutions

  • Shrimp alone replaces mixed seafood.
  • Chives replace some scallions.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day, reheated in a hot pan.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Soy-vinegar dip
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Makgeolli
