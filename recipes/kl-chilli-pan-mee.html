KL Chilli Pan Mee

▾

Surprise me with

Chilli pan mee is a Kuala Lumpur noodle dish of hand-made flat noodles tossed with minced pork, crispy anchovies, a poached egg and dried chili flakes fried in oil.

Method

  1. Mix the pork with the soy sauce, wine and cornstarch. Fry the anchovies in 2 tablespoons of the oil over medium-low heat for 4 to 5 minutes until golden and crisp; drain.
  2. Make the chili oil: heat the remaining oil with the garlic over low heat for 1 to 2 minutes until pale gold, take it off the heat and stir in the chili flakes. The residual heat toasts the chili without burning it. This dry, smoky chili is the heart of the dish.
  3. Stir-fry the pork in a little of the chili oil over medium-high heat for 3 to 4 minutes, breaking it up, until browned, then add the dark soy, light soy, vinegar and sugar and cook 1 minute.
  4. Poach the eggs in barely simmering water for 3 to 4 minutes so the yolks stay runny, then lift them out. Blanch the choy sum in the same water for 1 minute.
  5. Cook the noodles in boiling water for 1 to 2 minutes until just tender, drain well and divide among bowls.
  6. Top each bowl with the pork, a poached egg, choy sum and a handful of crispy anchovies. Add chili oil to taste and mix everything together so the yolk coats the noodles. Serve with a small bowl of clear soup.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Pouring boiling-hot oil over the chili flakes burns them; wait until the oil shimmers, not smokes.
  • Overcooking the noodles makes them mushy; they cook in 2 minutes.

Authentic tips

  • Toss the noodles in the sauce while hot so the oil coats every strand.
  • Add a spoonful of the fried anchovy oil for extra aroma.

Substitutions

  • Fresh udon or wide egg noodles replace pan mee noodles.
  • Ground chicken replaces pork.

Storage

Leftovers: Best eaten at once; the chili sauce keeps 1 week in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freeze the chili sauce for 3 months; do not freeze the noodles.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled green chilies
  • Soup: Clear anchovy soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced Chinese tea
