Bak Chor Mee (Singapore Minced Pork Noodles)

▾

Surprise me with

Bak chor mee, noodles dressed in black vinegar, chili and minced pork, is a Singapore hawker dish of Teochew origin.

  • Cook 20 min

Ingredients

  • 400 g14 ozEgg noodles (mee pok)
  • 300 g10 ozPork, minced
  • 45 ml3 tbspBlack vinegar
  • 30 ml2 tbspChili sauce
  • 30 ml2 tbspLard or oil
  • 15 g1 tbspFried shallots
  • 100 g3.5 ozMushrooms and pork liver
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt

Method

  1. Fry the pork and mushrooms in the lard or oil for 6 minutes until browned; season with the soy sauce.
  2. Blanch the pork liver 1 minute and slice thin.
  3. Boil the noodles 2 minutes, drain well and toss with the vinegar, chili sauce, salt and fat.
  4. Divide among bowls.
  5. Top with the pork, liver and fried shallots; serve soup on the side.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooked noodles go limp.
  • Drain the noodles well or the sauce dilutes.

Authentic tips

  • Mee pok (flat noodles) is traditional.
  • Lard gives the authentic flavor.

Substitutions

  • Chicken replaces pork for a halal-friendly version.
  • Spaghetti replaces mee pok.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Fishball soup
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Ais kacang
  • Beverage: Kopi
