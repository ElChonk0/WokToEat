Oi-sobagi (Stuffed Cucumber Kimchi) 오이소박이

Oi-sobagi, cucumbers split and stuffed with chili-seasoned chives, is a crisp spring and summer kimchi eaten young, within a few days of making. This version is vegetarian.

Method

  1. Cut each cucumber into two halves crosswise, then cut a deep cross into each piece from one end, leaving 1 cm (1/2 in) uncut at the base.
  2. Dissolve the salt in the boiling water, pour over the cucumbers and leave 30 minutes, then drain.
  3. Mix the chives, carrot, onion, gochugaru, garlic, ginger, soy sauce and sugar into a stuffing.
  4. Open each cucumber and push the stuffing into the cuts; rub any extra over the outside.
  5. Pack into a container. Eat after a few hours, or keep at room temperature for half a day, then refrigerate.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Cutting all the way through makes the cucumbers fall apart.
  • Keeping it too long softens the cucumbers; eat within a week.

Authentic tips

  • The hot brine keeps the cucumbers crunchy.
  • Add 15 ml of fish sauce in place of the soy sauce for the traditional version.

Substitutions

  • English cucumber, cut into 6 cm lengths.
  • Green onion replaces chives.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 week in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: With rice and grilled meats
  • Soup: Doenjang jjigae
  • Dessert: Watermelon
  • Beverage: Barley tea
