Oi-sobagi (Stuffed Cucumber Kimchi) 오이소박이

▾

Surprise me with

Oi-sobagi, cucumbers split and stuffed with chili-seasoned chives, is a crisp spring and summer kimchi eaten young, within a few days of making. This version is vegetarian.

Method

  1. Trim the ends of the cucumbers and cut them in half crosswise, or into thirds if long. Put them in a heatproof bowl.
  2. Dissolve the salt in the boiling water and pour it over the cucumbers, weighting them down so they stay under. Leave for 1 to 2 hours, until they bend without snapping. The hot brine seasons them and keeps them crisp without cooking them.
  3. Cut the chives into short pieces and mix them with the carrot, onion, gochugaru, garlic, ginger, soy sauce, sugar and 3 tablespoons of water. It should taste a little salty.
  4. Drain the cucumbers. Make two lengthwise cuts in a cross through each piece, leaving 1 cm (1/2 in) uncut at each end so it holds together.
  5. Gently open each piece and push 1 to 2 teaspoons of stuffing into the slits. Pack them upright in a jar, leave them at room temperature for a day, then refrigerate. They are best within a week or two, while crunchy.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Cutting all the way through makes the cucumbers fall apart.
  • Keeping it too long softens the cucumbers; eat within a week.

Authentic tips

  • The hot brine keeps the cucumbers crunchy.
  • Add 15 ml of fish sauce in place of the soy sauce for the traditional version.

Substitutions

  • English cucumber, cut into 6 cm lengths.
  • Green onion replaces chives.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 week in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: With rice and grilled meats
  • Soup: Doenjang jjigae
  • Dessert: Watermelon
  • Beverage: Barley tea
