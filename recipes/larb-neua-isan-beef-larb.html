Larb Neua (Isan Beef Larb) ลาบเนื้อ

Larb, a spicy minced meat salad, is the national dish of Laos and Isaan; beef larb is often made with beef and offal.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Thailand Beef LunchDinnerSalad / coldSpicy30 min or less

  • 500 g1.1 lbGround beef
  • 15 g1 tbspToasted rice powder
  • 60 ml4 tbspLime juice
  • 30 ml2 tbspFish sauce
  • 120 g4 ozShallot, mint, cilantro
  • 6 g2 tspChili flakes
  • 3 g1/2 tspSugar

Method

  1. Cook the ground beef in 3 tablespoons water over medium heat for 4-5 minutes, breaking it up, until just done.
  2. Cool slightly in a bowl.
  3. Toss with the lime juice, fish sauce, chili flakes and sugar.
  4. Stir in the toasted rice powder.
  5. Fold in the shallot, mint and cilantro; serve with lettuce and sticky rice.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking makes the beef dry.
  • Adding the herbs while hot wilts them.

Authentic tips

  • Taste for balance of salty, sour and hot.
  • Toast and grind sticky rice fresh.

Substitutions

  • Ground pork or chicken are common alternatives.
  • Toast and grind your own sticky rice powder.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sticky rice and raw vegetables
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Singha beer
