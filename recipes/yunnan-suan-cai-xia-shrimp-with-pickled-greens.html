Yúnnán Suān Cài Xiā (Shrimp with Pickled Greens) 酸菜炒虾

▾

Surprise me with

Yunnan cooks often stir-fry pickled greens with fresh chili and seafood to add a salty sour tang to rich or bland ingredients.

  • Cook 8 min

  • 500 g1.1 lbShrimp, peeled
  • 150 g5 ozPickled mustard greens, chopped
  • 20 g2/3 ozFresh chilies
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Rinse the pickled greens, squeeze dry and chop.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok over high heat and fry the garlic and chilies 15 seconds.
  3. Add the pickled greens and stir-fry 2 minutes.
  4. Add the shrimp and cook 2 minutes until pink.
  5. Add the wine and soy sauce and toss 1 minute.

Common mistakes

  • Not rinsing the pickles makes the dish too salty.
  • Overcooking the shrimp makes them rubbery.

Authentic tips

  • Taste before adding soy sauce.
  • Pat the shrimp dry for a good sear.

Substitutions

  • Rinsed sauerkraut replaces pickled greens.
  • Squid takes the same method.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
