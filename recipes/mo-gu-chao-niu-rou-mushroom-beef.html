Mo Gu Chao Niu Rou (Beef with Mushrooms) 蘑菇炒牛肉

Beef stir-fried with mushrooms is a Jiangnan home dish, where tender local mushrooms are cooked with meat in a light soy sauce.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 450 g1 lbBeef sirloin, sliced
  • 300 g10 ozMixed mushrooms
  • 30 g2 tbspGinger and garlic
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspOyster sauce
  • 10 g2 tspCornstarch
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Slice the beef 4 mm thick across the grain and toss with half the soy sauce, the cornstarch and 1 tablespoon of water; leave 15 minutes.
  2. Cut the larger mushrooms into slices and leave small ones whole.
  3. Heat half the oil in a wok over high heat and sear the beef for 45 seconds; remove.
  4. Add the remaining oil and fry the mushrooms 3 minutes until browned and their liquid has evaporated.
  5. Add the ginger and garlic for 15 seconds, then return the beef with the remaining soy sauce and the oyster sauce; toss 30 seconds.

Common mistakes

  • Salting the mushrooms early draws out water, so brown them before adding sauces.
  • Crowding the pan steams the beef.

Authentic tips

  • Use a mix of mushrooms for depth.
  • Wipe rather than wash mushrooms.

Substitutions

  • Chicken thigh takes the same sauce.
  • A mix of shiitake, button and oyster mushrooms gives the best flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
