Mó Gū Chǎo Niú Ròu (Beef with Mushrooms) 蘑菇炒牛肉

▾

Surprise me with

Beef stir-fried with mushrooms is a Jiangnan home dish, where tender local mushrooms are cooked with meat in a light soy sauce.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 450 g1 lbBeef sirloin, sliced thin
  • 10 ml2 tspLight soy sauce, for the marinade
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 10 g1 tbspCornstarch
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 300 g10 ozMixed mushrooms (shiitake, king oyster, button), sliced
  • 30 g1 ozGinger and garlic, sliced
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce, for the sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspOyster sauce
  • 3 g1/2 tspSugar
  • 2 stalks2 stalksGreen onion, cut into segments

Method

  1. Toss the beef with the marinade soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, cornstarch and a tablespoon of water and leave for 15 minutes.
  2. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a flat-bottom wok over high heat and sear the beef for 1 minute until just browned. Lift it out.
  3. Add the remaining oil and the mushrooms and cook over high heat for 3 to 4 minutes, without stirring at first, until they release their water and brown.
  4. Add the ginger and garlic for 20 seconds, then return the beef with the sauce soy sauce, oyster sauce and sugar.
  5. Toss for 30 seconds until glossy, add the green onion and serve with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Salting the mushrooms early draws out water, so brown them before adding sauces.
  • Crowding the pan steams the beef.

Authentic tips

  • Use a mix of mushrooms for depth.
  • Wipe rather than wash mushrooms.

Substitutions

  • Chicken thigh takes the same sauce.
  • A mix of shiitake, button and oyster mushrooms gives the best flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
