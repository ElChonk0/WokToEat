Rou Sui Zheng Dan (Steamed Egg with Minced Pork) 肉碎蒸蛋

Silky steamed egg topped with seasoned minced pork is a Cantonese home favorite, cooked on a plate in the steamer alongside the rice.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 5 ml1 tspSesame oil
  • 2 stalks2 stalksGreen onion, chopped

Method

  1. Mix the pork with the soy sauce, cornstarch, sugar and a tablespoon of water and spread it thinly over a shallow heatproof dish.
  2. Beat the eggs gently with the salt, then whisk in the warm water. Strain through a sieve over the pork and skim off any bubbles.
  3. Cover the dish with a plate or foil and steam over medium heat for 12 to 15 minutes, until just set with a slight wobble in the center.
  4. Drizzle with the soy sauce and sesame oil.
  5. Scatter with green onion and serve with rice.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Steaming on high heat makes the egg pocked and spongy.
  • Leaving it uncovered lets condensation drip and dimple the surface.

Authentic tips

  • A ratio of about 1 part egg to 1.5 parts water gives the silkiest custard.
  • Prop the steamer lid open a crack to keep the temperature gentle.

Substitutions

  • Ground chicken replaces pork.
  • Add a salted egg or century egg, diced, to the custard.

Storage

Leftovers: Best eaten at once; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and garlic gai lan
  • Soup: Spinach tofu soup
  • Dessert: Sweet red bean soup
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
