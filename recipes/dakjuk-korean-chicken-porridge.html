Dakjuk (Korean Chicken Rice Porridge) 닭죽

Dakjuk, rice porridge cooked in chicken broth with shredded chicken, is a Korean breakfast and the food made for anyone feeling unwell.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 1 hr
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Simmer the chicken with the water, garlic, ginger and onion for 35 minutes. Lift out the chicken, shred the meat and discard skin and bones. Strain the broth, keeping the garlic.
  2. Heat the sesame oil in a heavy pot, add the drained rice and stir 2 minutes until translucent at the edges.
  3. Add 1.5 L of the broth and the garlic, bring to a boil and simmer 20 to 25 minutes, stirring often, until the rice breaks down.
  4. Add the chicken and carrot and simmer 5 minutes, adding broth if it gets too thick.
  5. Season with salt and pepper and serve topped with green onion.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Not stirring lets the porridge catch on the bottom.
  • Adding salt early tightens the rice grains.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with kimchi and soy-braised beef on the side.
  • Glutinous rice makes it creamier.

Substitutions

  • Leftover cooked rice makes it in 15 minutes.
  • Add sliced shiitake with the carrot.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; loosen with water.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Kimchi
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
