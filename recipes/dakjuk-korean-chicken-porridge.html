Dakjuk (Korean Chicken Rice Porridge) 닭죽

▾

Surprise me with

Dakjuk, rice porridge cooked in chicken broth with shredded chicken, is a Korean breakfast and the food made for anyone feeling unwell.

Method

  1. Rinse the rice and soak it in water for about 1 hour, then drain well. Sweet rice gives a creamier porridge; short-grain gives more distinct grains.
  2. Simmer the chicken with the water, garlic, ginger and onion, covered, for 30 to 40 minutes until tender. Lift out the chicken, strain the broth and keep 1.5 L (6 cups). Shred the meat and season it with the chicken salt, minced garlic and a drizzle of the sesame oil.
  3. Heat the remaining sesame oil in a heavy pot over medium heat and stir-fry the drained rice for 2 to 3 minutes, until the grains turn translucent at the edges. Toasting the rice gives a nutty flavor and helps it release its starch.
  4. Add the broth, bring to a boil, then cook for 20 to 25 minutes, stirring often and lowering the heat as it thickens, until the rice is soft and creamy. Stir more as it thickens so it does not catch on the bottom.
  5. Stir in the carrot and mushrooms, cover and simmer for 10 to 15 minutes until soft, thinning with broth or water if it gets too thick.
  6. Add the shredded chicken for the last few minutes, season with salt and pepper, and serve hot topped with green onion and sesame seeds.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Not stirring lets the porridge catch on the bottom.
  • Adding salt early tightens the rice grains.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with kimchi and soy-braised beef on the side.
  • Glutinous rice makes it creamier.

Substitutions

  • Leftover cooked rice makes it in 15 minutes.
  • Add sliced shiitake with the carrot.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator; loosen with water.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Kimchi
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
