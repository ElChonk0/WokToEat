Agujjim (Spicy Braised Monkfish) 아귀찜

▾

Surprise me with

Agujjim, spicy braised monkfish with bean sprouts, originated in Masan on Korea's southern coast and is a restaurant staple.

  • Cook 30 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 700 g1.5 lbMonkfish pieces
  • 300 g10.5 ozBean sprouts
  • 30 g1 ozGochugaru
  • 45 ml3 tbspSoy sauce
  • 40 g1.5 ozGarlic
  • 45 ml3 tbspCornstarch slurry
  • 300 ml1.25 cupsWater
  • 60 g2 ozCrown daisy

Method

  1. Soak the monkfish in cold water for 30 minutes and drain.
  2. Bring the water, gochugaru, soy sauce and garlic to a boil in a wide pot.
  3. Add the monkfish, cover and simmer 12 minutes.
  4. Add the bean sprouts and cook 5 minutes.
  5. Stir in the slurry, simmer until glossy and finish with the crown daisy.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooked sprouts go limp.
  • Stirring breaks up the fish.

Authentic tips

  • Monkfish has a firm, lobster-like texture.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • Cod or halibut replaces monkfish.
  • Dried monkfish is the Masan original.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
