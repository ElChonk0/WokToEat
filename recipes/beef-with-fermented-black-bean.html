Beef with Fermented Black Bean 豆豉牛肉

▾

Surprise me with

A savory, deeply aromatic stir-fry built on fermented black beans, a Hunanese and broader southern Chinese pantry staple that adds umami depth to quickly cooked beef and vegetables.

  • Cook 10 min

  • 4 cloves4 clovesGarlic, minced
  • 10 g2 tspGinger, minced

  • 11Red chili, sliced

  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 10 ml2 tspCornstarch

Method

  1. Marinate beef in soy sauce and cornstarch for 15 minutes.
  2. Stir-fry beef in hot oil over high heat until just browned; remove and set aside.
  3. Fry black beans, garlic, and ginger until fragrant.
  4. Add bell pepper and chili, stir-frying 2 minutes until just tender-crisp.
  5. Return beef to the wok with wine, tossing everything together over high heat until combined.

Common mistakes

  • Not rinsing the fermented black beans, which can leave the dish overly salty.
  • Overcooking the beef, which turns it tough — sear quickly over very high heat.

Authentic tips

  • Rinsing the fermented black beans briefly softens their intensity without losing their signature umami.
  • This dish comes together very quickly — have all ingredients prepped before you start cooking.

Substitutions

  • Chicken or pork replace the beef.
  • Any bell pepper color works; a mix adds visual appeal.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Simple egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Sliced watermelon
  • Beverage: Cold beer
