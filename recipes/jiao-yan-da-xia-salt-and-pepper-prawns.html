Jiāo Yán Dà Xiā (Salt-and-Pepper Prawns) 椒盐大虾

▾

Surprise me with

Salt-and-pepper prawns, fried and tossed with toasted Sichuan pepper salt, are a standard northern restaurant dish that reached Beijing from Chinese banquet cooking.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 700 g1.5 lbLarge prawns, shell on
  • 45 g1/3 cupCornstarch
  • 5 g2 tspSichuan peppercorns
  • 10 g2 tspSalt
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic, minced
  • 10 g2 tspChili, minced
  • 400 ml1.75 cupsNeutral oil for frying

Method

  1. Toast the Sichuan peppercorns in a dry pan for 2 minutes, then grind with the salt.
  2. Pat the prawns dry and toss with the cornstarch.
  3. Heat the oil to 190 C (375 F) and fry the prawns in two batches for 2-3 minutes until crisp; drain.
  4. Pour off all but 1 tablespoon of oil and fry the garlic and chili for 20 seconds.
  5. Return the prawns with the pepper salt and toss 15 seconds; serve immediately.

Common mistakes

  • Wet prawns spatter and do not crisp.
  • Seasoning too early softens the coating.

Authentic tips

  • Eat the shells for crunch.
  • Add scallion at the end for color.

Substitutions

  • Squid or scallops take the same coating.
  • Roast at 220 C (425 F) for a lighter version.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; eat within minutes.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Lemon wedges
  • Beverage: Cold beer
