Hóng Shāo Shàn Duàn (Braised Eel Sections) 红烧鳝段

▾

Surprise me with

Freshwater eel braised in soy sauce, rice wine and ginger is a popular Anhui banquet dish, sourced from the Yangtze and its tributaries.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Medium
  • Draft Anhui Seafood LunchDinnerBraise / stew

  • 800 g1.75 lbEel, cleaned and cut into sections
  • 50 g4 tbspGinger, garlic and scallion
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspDark soy sauce
  • 15 g1 tbspSugar
  • 45 ml3 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 250 ml1 cupWater
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Rub the eel sections with salt to remove the slime, rinse and pat dry.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok over high heat and sear the eel 3 minutes until lightly golden.
  3. Add the ginger, garlic, scallion, wine, both soy sauces and sugar and stir 1 minute.
  4. Add 250 ml water, cover and simmer 15 minutes until the sauce is thick.
  5. Uncover and reduce 3 minutes until glossy.

Common mistakes

  • Not scrubbing off the slime leaves a muddy taste.
  • Overcooking makes the eel tough.

Authentic tips

  • Ask the fishmonger to clean the eel.
  • Serve over rice.

Substitutions

  • Ask the fishmonger to clean the eel.
  • Add garlic for extra flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
