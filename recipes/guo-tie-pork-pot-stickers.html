Guō Tiē (Pork Pot Stickers) 锅贴

▾

Surprise me with

Guo tie, pan-fried dumplings with a crisp base, are a northern Chinese staple and a common Beijing breakfast and snack.

  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Method

  1. Salt the cabbage, wait 10 minutes and squeeze out the water; mix with the pork, ginger, scallion, soy sauce and sesame oil.
  2. Place a tablespoon of filling in each wrapper, fold in half and pleat the edge.
  3. Heat the oil in a nonstick pan over medium-high heat, arrange the dumplings flat side down and fry 2-3 minutes until the bases are golden.
  4. Add 100 ml water, cover and steam 6-8 minutes until the water has gone.
  5. Uncover and cook 1 more minute for a crisp base.

Common mistakes

  • Too much water leaves the bases soggy.
  • Overfilling splits the wrappers.

Authentic tips

  • Dip in black vinegar with a little ginger.
  • Use a nonstick pan for easy release.

Substitutions

  • Ground chicken thigh replaces the pork.
  • Chopped shrimp can replace a third of the pork.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; recrisp in a hot pan with a splash of water.

Freezing: Freeze uncooked on a tray for 2 months; pan-fry from frozen with extra water.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Black vinegar and ginger dip
  • Soup: Hot and sour soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot jasmine tea
