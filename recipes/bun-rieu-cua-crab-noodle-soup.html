Bún Riêu Cua (Crab and Tomato Noodle Soup)

▾

Surprise me with

Bun rieu is a Vietnamese noodle soup built on freshwater crab paste and tomato, a popular breakfast across Hanoi and Saigon.

  • 300 g10 ozCrab paste in spice (jarred) or 200 g crab meat with minced shrimp
  • 150 g5 ozPork, ground
  • 22Eggs
  • 10 ml2 tspFish sauce, for the crab mixture
  • 1.8 L7.5 cupsPork or chicken stock
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil
  • 33Shallots, minced
  • 300 g10 ozTomatoes, cut into wedges
  • 15 g1 tbspTomato paste
  • 15 ml1 tbspAnnatto oil (optional, for color)
  • 100 g3.5 ozFried tofu, cubed
  • 30 ml2 tbspFish sauce, for the broth
  • 15 ml1 tbspTamarind paste
  • 10 g2 tspSugar
  • 300 g10 ozDried rice vermicelli
  • 15 g1 tbspFermented shrimp paste (mam tom), for serving
  • 100 g3.5 ozHerbs, bean sprouts, shredded banana blossom and lime

Method

  1. Mix the crab paste, ground pork, eggs and fish sauce into a loose mixture.
  2. Heat the oil in a pot and fry the shallots for 1 minute, then the tomatoes and tomato paste for 4 to 5 minutes until soft and red. Lift out half the tomatoes.
  3. Add the stock, bring to a simmer, and spoon in the crab mixture in large dollops without stirring. Simmer gently for 10 minutes; the crab floats to the top in fluffy, golden curds, the 'rieu'.
  4. Add the remaining tomatoes, the fried tofu, fish sauce, tamarind and sugar and simmer for 5 minutes. The broth should be tangy and savory.
  5. Cook the vermicelli, divide among bowls and ladle over the broth, crab curds, tomatoes and tofu. Serve with herbs, sprouts, banana blossom, lime and a little shrimp paste.

Common mistakes

  • Stirring early breaks up the crab curds.
  • Cooking the noodles in the broth makes it starchy.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with shrimp paste, lime and herbs.
  • Bun rieu is often a breakfast.

Substitutions

  • Canned crab paste replaces fresh.
  • Tomato paste deepens the broth.

Storage

Leftovers: Store noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 2 days.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Herbs, shrimp paste and lime
  • Soup: Not needed; the soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
