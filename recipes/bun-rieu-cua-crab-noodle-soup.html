Bún Riêu Cua (Crab and Tomato Noodle Soup)

▾

Surprise me with

Bun rieu is a Vietnamese noodle soup built on freshwater crab paste and tomato, a popular breakfast across Hanoi and Saigon.

  • 300 g10.5 ozCrab paste or minced shrimp and crab
  • 300 g10.5 ozDried rice vermicelli
  • 300 g10.5 ozTomatoes
  • 22Eggs
  • 100 g3.5 ozFried tofu
  • 1.5 L6 cupsPork or fish stock
  • 30 ml2 tbspFish sauce
  • 60 g2 ozHerbs

Method

  1. Mix the crab paste with the eggs and a spoon of fish sauce.
  2. Simmer the stock with the tomatoes for 10 minutes.
  3. Drop in the crab mixture in spoonfuls; do not stir; simmer 10 minutes until it floats.
  4. Add the tofu and cook 5 minutes.
  5. Cook the vermicelli separately, divide among bowls, ladle on the broth and top with herbs.

Common mistakes

  • Stirring early breaks up the crab curds.
  • Cooking the noodles in the broth makes it starchy.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with shrimp paste, lime and herbs.
  • Bun rieu is often a breakfast.

Substitutions

  • Canned crab paste replaces fresh.
  • Tomato paste deepens the broth.

Storage

Leftovers: Store noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 2 days.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Herbs, shrimp paste and lime
  • Soup: Not needed; the soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
