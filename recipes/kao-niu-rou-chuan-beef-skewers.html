Kǎo Niú Ròu Chuàn (Xinjiang Beef Skewers) 烤牛肉串

▾

Surprise me with

Skewers of beef grilled with cumin, chili and salt are a Xinjiang street-food staple; lamb is the original, with beef a popular alternative.

  • Cook 10 min

  • 1212Skewers

Method

  1. Toss the beef with the soy sauce, oil and half the cumin; marinate at least 30 minutes.
  2. Soak bamboo skewers 20 minutes and thread the beef, alternating with small pieces of fat if using.
  3. Grill over high heat for 6-8 minutes, turning every 2 minutes.
  4. Sprinkle with the remaining cumin, chili flakes and salt.
  5. Serve straight off the grill.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking makes the beef tough.
  • A low heat steams rather than chars.

Authentic tips

  • Toast and grind the cumin fresh.
  • Lamb is the original; use lamb shoulder with the same method.

Substitutions

  • Lamb is the original; shoulder with some fat is best.
  • Cut the beef into small pieces so they char before drying out.
  • No grill: lay the skewers on a wire rack over a foil-lined sheet pan and broil on high about 10 cm (4 in) from the element for 3 to 4 minutes per side, or roast at 230°C (450°F) for 12 to 15 minutes, turning once, until charred at the edges and cooked through.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat in a hot pan.

Freezing: Freeze raw marinated skewers for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Nang flatbread
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Hami melon
  • Beverage: Black tea
