Bistek Tagalog (Filipino Beef Steak)

▾

Surprise me with

A Filipino comfort food staple, bistek Tagalog marinates thin beef slices in calamansi and soy sauce before pan-frying with caramelized onions — simple, tangy, and found on nearly every Filipino family's dinner table.

  • Prep 15 min (plus 30 min marinating)
  • Cook 15 min

  • 700 g1.5 lbBeef sirloin, sliced thin
  • 60 ml1/4 cupCalamansi juice (or lime and lemon mix)
  • 60 ml1/4 cupSoy sauce
  • 4 cloves4 clovesGarlic, minced
  • 2 g1 tspBlack pepper
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 22Onions, sliced into rings
  • 120 ml1/2 cupWater
  • 5 g1 tspSugar

Method

  1. Pound the beef slices thin and marinate them in the calamansi, soy sauce, garlic and pepper for at least 1 hour.
  2. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a skillet over medium-high heat and fry the onion rings for 1 to 2 minutes, just until softened but still crisp. Lift them out.
  3. Lift the beef from the marinade, keeping the liquid. Add the remaining oil and sear the beef in batches for 1 minute per side until browned. Lift it out.
  4. Pour the marinade and water into the pan with the sugar and bring to a boil, then return the beef, cover and simmer over low heat for 10 to 15 minutes until tender.
  5. Uncover and simmer for 2 to 3 minutes to reduce the sauce slightly, then top with the onion rings. Serve with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the thin beef slices, which toughens them quickly — they need only a couple minutes per side.
  • Rushing the onion caramelization, which is central to this dish's sweet-tangy balance.

Authentic tips

  • Calamansi's distinct tart-sweet flavor is central to bistek; a mix of lime and a touch of orange juice approximates it well.
  • The onions should be genuinely soft and sweet, not just lightly cooked — give them real time.

Substitutions

  • Pork can be prepared the same way for a variation.
  • No calamansi: a mix of lime and lemon juice with a pinch of sugar works well.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended — texture of thin beef slices suffers.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Calamansi juice
