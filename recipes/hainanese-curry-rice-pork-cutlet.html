Hainanese Curry Rice (Pork Cutlet with Curry Gravy)

▾

Surprise me with

Hainanese curry rice is a Singapore hawker plate of steamed rice with a fried pork cutlet, stewed cabbage and a thick, mild curry gravy, associated with Hainanese cooks who worked in colonial kitchens.

Method

  1. Pound the pork chops to about 1 cm (1/2 in) thick and season with the salt and pepper. Dredge in flour, dip in beaten egg and press into the breadcrumbs.
  2. Heat the frying oil in a skillet to 170 C (340 F) and shallow-fry the cutlets for 3 to 4 minutes per side, until golden and cooked through. Drain on a rack, then slice into strips.
  3. For the curry, heat the gravy oil in a pot over medium heat and fry the onion for 3 minutes, then the garlic and ginger for 1 minute. Add the curry powder mixed with a little water and fry for 3 to 4 minutes until fragrant.
  4. Add the potatoes, carrot and stock and simmer for 15 minutes until the potatoes are soft. Stir in the coconut milk, salt and sugar and simmer until the gravy is thick and starts to break down the potatoes slightly; Hainanese curry is a loose, mild, comforting gravy.
  5. Stir-fry the cabbage with a little oil and the soy sauce over high heat for 2 minutes until just tender.
  6. Pile rice on each plate with the sliced cutlet and cabbage and flood it with the curry gravy. Many stalls also add braised pork gravy and a fried egg.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Too hot oil browns the crumbs before the pork cooks; keep it near 175 C.
  • Skipping the pounding gives uneven, tough cutlets.

Authentic tips

  • The gravy thickens as it stands, so thin it with stock when reheating.
  • A fried egg on top is a common add-on.

Substitutions

  • Chicken thigh cutlets replace pork.
  • Japanese curry roux replaces curry powder and thickens faster.

Storage

Leftovers: The gravy keeps 4 days in the refrigerator; fry cutlets fresh.

Freezing: The gravy freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled chilies and stewed cabbage
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced lemon tea
