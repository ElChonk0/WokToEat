Xūn Yú (Shanghai Smoked Fish) 熏鱼

▾

Surprise me with

Xun yu is a Shanghai cold dish of fried fish steeped in a sweet-savory marinade, "smoked" in name only; it is a fixture of the Lunar New Year table.

  • Cook 20 min

  • 800 g1.75 lbGrass carp or mackerel, cut into 1.5 cm (1/2 in) steaks
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce, for the marinade
  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine, for the marinade
  • 40 g1.5 ozGinger and green onion, for the marinade
  • 1 L4 cupsNeutral oil, for frying
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce, for the sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspDark soy sauce
  • 60 g1/4 cupSugar
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine, for the sauce
  • 5 g1/6 ozStar anise and cinnamon
  • 1 g1/2 tspFive-spice powder
  • 200 ml3/4 cupWater

Method

  1. Marinate the fish steaks with the marinade soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, ginger and green onion for at least 3 hours, turning once. Pat them very dry; wet fish spatters and will not crisp.
  2. Make the sauce: simmer the soy sauces, sugar, Shaoxing wine, star anise, cinnamon, five-spice and water for 10 minutes, until syrupy. Keep it warm.
  3. Heat the oil to 180 C (350 F) and fry the fish in batches for 5 to 6 minutes, until deep brown and crisp right through to the edges.
  4. Lift each piece straight from the oil into the warm sauce for 30 seconds, turning to coat. The hot, dry fish soaks up the sauce. Despite the name, Shanghai 'smoked' fish is not smoked; it is fried and soaked.
  5. Lay the pieces on a rack, brush with more sauce and let them cool. Serve at room temperature as a cold appetizer.

Common mistakes

  • Under-frying leaves the fish soft; it should be well browned.
  • Steeping too long makes it mushy.

Authentic tips

  • Despite the name, no smoke is used; the sweet-savory flavor is what makes it "smoked" in style.
  • Better the next day.

Substitutions

  • Mackerel replaces grass carp.
  • Skip the frying and bake at 220 C (425 F) for a lighter version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; eat cold or at room temperature.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
