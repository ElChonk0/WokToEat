Xūn Yú (Shanghai Smoked Fish) 熏鱼

▾

Surprise me with

Xun yu is a Shanghai cold dish of fried fish steeped in a sweet-savory marinade, "smoked" in name only; it is a fixture of the Lunar New Year table.

Method

  1. Cut the fish into 1.5 cm slices and marinate with the wine, ginger and scallion for 30 minutes; pat dry.
  2. Heat the oil to 180 C (350 F) and fry the fish in batches for 4-5 minutes until deep golden and crisp; drain.
  3. Simmer the soy sauces, sugar, star anise, cinnamon and 200 ml water for 5 minutes until syrupy.
  4. Dip the hot fried fish into the sauce and steep 10 minutes.
  5. Serve at room temperature or chilled.

Common mistakes

  • Under-frying leaves the fish soft; it should be well browned.
  • Steeping too long makes it mushy.

Authentic tips

  • Despite the name, no smoke is used; the sweet-savory flavor is what makes it "smoked" in style.
  • Better the next day.

Substitutions

  • Mackerel replaces grass carp.
  • Skip the frying and bake at 220 C (425 F) for a lighter version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; eat cold or at room temperature.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
