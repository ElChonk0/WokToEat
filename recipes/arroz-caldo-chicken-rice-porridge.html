Arroz Caldo (Filipino Chicken Rice Porridge)

▾

Surprise me with

Arroz caldo, chicken rice porridge with ginger and saffron-colored turmeric or safflower, is a Filipino comfort food derived from Chinese congee and Spanish rice dishes; it is a typical breakfast and rainy-day meal.

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a heavy pot over medium heat and saute the garlic until golden, then the onion and ginger for 3 minutes. Be generous with ginger; it is the main flavor.
  2. Add the chicken and fish sauce and cook for 5 minutes, stirring, until the chicken is lightly browned and the fish sauce has been absorbed.
  3. Add the rice and stir for 2 minutes to coat it, then the stock. Bring to a boil.
  4. Simmer over low heat, partly covered, for 40 to 45 minutes, stirring often so it does not stick, until the rice has broken down into a thick, creamy porridge. Add water if it gets too thick. Stir in the kasubha for golden color.
  5. Season with pepper and more fish sauce. Serve topped with a halved egg, plenty of fried garlic, green onion and a squeeze of calamansi.

Common mistakes

  • Not stirring causes sticking.
  • Under-seasoning: the porridge needs plenty of fish sauce.

Authentic tips

  • Glutinous rice gives the stickiest texture.
  • Add black pepper at the table.

Substitutions

  • Leftover rice shortens the cooking to 20 minutes.
  • Saffron replaces kasubha.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; loosen with water.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Fried garlic, calamansi and a boiled egg
  • Soup: Not needed; the porridge is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot coffee
