Bō Luó Chǎo Fàn (Cantonese Pineapple Fried Rice) 菠萝炒饭

▾

Surprise me with

Cantonese pineapple fried rice, with diced chicken, shrimp and pineapple and no curry, is a Hong Kong restaurant dish distinct from the Thai version.

Method

  1. Break up the cold rice with wet hands so no clumps remain. Toss the chicken with half the cornstarch, a pinch of the salt and pepper and 1 tablespoon of water; toss the shrimp with the rest of the cornstarch and a pinch of pepper. Drop the pineapple into boiling water for 40 seconds to soften its sharpness, then drain it well and pat it dry; wet fruit is what makes fried rice soggy.
  2. Heat a flat-bottom wok over high heat until it just begins to smoke. Add 1 tablespoon of oil, pour in the eggs and stir for about 20 seconds until barely set in soft curds, then tip them into a bowl.
  3. Lower the heat to medium-high and add 1 teaspoon of oil. Spread the chicken in one layer and leave it 30 seconds to brown, then turn and cook 1 to 2 minutes until white all the way through. Add the shrimp and stir-fry about 1 minute, just until they turn pink and curl. Lift both out.
  4. Return the wok to high heat with 1 tablespoon of oil and the onion for 30 seconds. Add the rice, spread it over the surface and leave it alone for 30 seconds so it toasts, then flip and leave it another 30 seconds. Stir-fry 2 to 3 minutes, pressing out any clumps, until every grain is hot and separate and you hear it hop in the pan.
  5. Drizzle the remaining oil around the edge of the wok. Add the pineapple and peas and toss 1 minute, then season with the soy sauce, fish sauce, sugar and remaining salt and pepper and toss 1 minute more so the seasoning colors the rice evenly.
  6. Return the chicken, shrimp and egg, breaking the egg into small pieces, with the green onion. Toss over high heat for 1 minute, turn off the heat, stir in the sesame oil and serve at once.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Wet pineapple makes soggy rice; dry and sear it first.
  • Adding the pineapple too early turns it mushy.

Authentic tips

  • Serve it in a hollowed pineapple half for a party.
  • A spoon of the pineapple juice in the sauce boosts the flavor.

Substitutions

  • Canned pineapple, well drained.
  • Char siu replaces chicken.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Garlic choy sum
  • Soup: Chicken and sweetcorn soup
  • Dessert: Mango pudding
  • Beverage: Iced lemon tea
