Bo Luo Chao Fan (Cantonese Pineapple Fried Rice) 菠萝炒饭

Cantonese pineapple fried rice, with diced chicken, shrimp and pineapple and no curry, is a Hong Kong restaurant dish distinct from the Thai version.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Pat the pineapple dry and sear it in a dry flat-bottom wok over high heat for 2 minutes until lightly caramelized. Set aside.
  2. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil and stir-fry the chicken 3 minutes, then the shrimp 1 minute, until cooked. Lift out.
  3. Add the remaining oil and the onion, then the eggs; stir once and add the rice before they set. Toss 3 minutes until hot and separate.
  4. Add the peas, soy sauce, fish sauce, salt and pepper and toss 1 minute.
  5. Return the chicken, shrimp and pineapple with the green onion, toss 30 seconds and serve.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Wet pineapple makes soggy rice; dry and sear it first.
  • Adding the pineapple too early turns it mushy.

Authentic tips

  • Serve it in a hollowed pineapple half for a party.
  • A spoon of the pineapple juice in the sauce boosts the flavor.

Substitutions

  • Canned pineapple, well drained.
  • Char siu replaces chicken.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Garlic choy sum
  • Soup: Chicken and sweetcorn soup
  • Dessert: Mango pudding
  • Beverage: Iced lemon tea
