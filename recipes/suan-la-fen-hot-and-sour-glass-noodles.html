Suān Là Fěn (Hot and Sour Sweet Potato Noodles) 酸辣粉

Suan la fen, chewy sweet potato noodles in a spicy, sour broth, is one of Chongqing and Chengdu's best-known street foods.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 800 ml3.5 cupsVegetable broth

Method

  1. Soak the sweet potato noodles in cold water for 30 minutes, or overnight for the best chew.
  2. Boil the noodles 4-5 minutes until translucent and tender; drain and divide among four bowls.
  3. Heat the vegetable broth until steaming.
  4. Into each bowl put the vinegar, soy sauce, chili oil, garlic and Sichuan pepper.
  5. Ladle in the hot broth, stir well and top with peanuts, scallion and cilantro.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the noodles makes them mushy and ruins the chew.
  • Boiling the vinegar in the broth dulls its sharpness; add it to the bowl.

Authentic tips

  • Taste the broth; it should be tangy and spicy.
  • Use thin noodles for the real street-stall experience.

Substitutions

  • Rice noodles replace the sweet potato noodles.
  • Pickled mustard greens add a savory tang.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the noodles swell and soften.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled vegetables
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
