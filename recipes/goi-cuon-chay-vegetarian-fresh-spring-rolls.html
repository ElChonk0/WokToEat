Gỏi Cuốn Chay (Vegetarian Fresh Spring Rolls)

▾

Surprise me with

Fresh spring rolls of rice paper wrapped around herbs, noodles and vegetables are a Vietnamese summer classic, and the chay version with tofu and peanut sauce is on every vegetarian menu.

  • Prep 25 min
  • Cook 5 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 1212Rice paper

  • 150 g5 ozRice vermicelli
  • 150 g5 ozLettuce, mint, cilantro
  • 200 g7 ozCucumber and carrot
  • 60 g4 tbspPeanut butter
  • 30 ml2 tbspHoisin sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspLime juice

Method

  1. Cook the vermicelli, rinse and drain.
  2. Soften one rice paper at a time in warm water for 10 seconds.
  3. Fill with the lettuce, herbs, vermicelli, vegetables and tofu; fold in the sides and roll tightly.
  4. Whisk the peanut butter, hoisin, lime and 3 tablespoons water for the sauce.
  5. Serve the rolls with the sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Over-soaking the paper makes it tear.
  • Overfilling splits the rolls.

Authentic tips

  • Cover the rolls with a damp cloth.
  • Add chili sauce.

Substitutions

  • Almond butter replaces peanut butter.
  • Add mango or avocado.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day wrapped in a damp towel.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Peanut-hoisin dipping sauce
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
