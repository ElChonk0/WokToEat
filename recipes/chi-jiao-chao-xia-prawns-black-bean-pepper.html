Chǐ Jiāo Chǎo Xiā (Prawns with Black Bean and Peppers) 豉椒炒虾

Black bean and pepper (si jiu) sauce is a Cantonese foundation used with seafood, beef and chicken; prawns are among its most popular partners.

  • Cook 8 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Toss the shrimp with cornstarch. Mix the oyster sauce, soy sauce, sugar, water and cornstarch.
  2. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a flat-bottom wok over high heat and cook the shrimp 1 to 2 minutes until pink. Lift out.
  3. Add the remaining oil and stir-fry the peppers and onion 2 minutes until lightly charred.
  4. Add the black beans, garlic, ginger and chili and fry 30 seconds, then splash in the wine.
  5. Return the shrimp, stir in the sauce and toss until glossy.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Unrinsed black beans make the dish very salty.
  • Adding the shrimp back too early overcooks them.

Authentic tips

  • Mash the black beans with the garlic to release their aroma.
  • The same sauce is excellent with clams or beef.

Substitutions

  • Squid or scallops replace shrimp.
  • Leave out the chili for a mild version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Winter melon soup
  • Dessert: Sweet red bean soup
  • Beverage: Cold beer
