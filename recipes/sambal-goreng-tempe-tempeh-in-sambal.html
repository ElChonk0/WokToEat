Sambal Goreng Tempe (Tempeh in Chili Sambal)

Sambal goreng, fried dishes in a spicy chili sauce, are Indonesian staples; tempeh and potato versions are common vegetarian dishes.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 300 g10 ozTempeh, cubed
  • 200 g7 ozPotatoes, cubed
  • 60 g2 ozChilies
  • 60 g4 tbspShallot and garlic
  • 100 g3.5 ozTomato
  • 120 ml1/2 cupCoconut milk
  • 10 g2 tspPalm sugar
  • 60 ml4 tbspNeutral oil
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt

Method

  1. Fry the tempeh and potatoes in a little oil until golden, 8 minutes; drain.
  2. Fry the blended chilies, shallot, garlic and tomato for 8 minutes.
  3. Add the coconut milk and palm sugar and simmer 5 minutes.
  4. Toss with the tempeh and potato, season with salt.
  5. Cook 3 minutes until the sauce clings.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the paste makes it bitter.
  • Boiling hard splits the coconut milk.

Authentic tips

  • Add long beans or peas.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • Reduce the chilies for a milder dish.
  • Add long beans.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Sayur lodeh
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Es teh
