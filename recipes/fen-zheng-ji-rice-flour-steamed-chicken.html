Fěn Zhēng Jī (Sichuan Rice-Flour Steamed Chicken) 粉蒸鸡

▾

Surprise me with

Fen zheng, steaming meat coated in toasted rice flour, is a classic Sichuan method; the rice flour absorbs the juices and gives a soft, fragrant texture.

  • 3 g1 tspSichuan peppercorns and star anise, for the rice flour
  • 30 g2 tbspPixian doubanjiang
  • 30 ml2 tbspFermented rice wine (jiuniang) or Shaoxing wine
  • 40 g1.5 ozGinger and garlic, minced
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 2 g1 tspGround Sichuan pepper
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil
  • 60 ml1/4 cupWater
  • 250 g9 ozSweet potato or pumpkin, cubed
  • to tasteto tasteGreen onion and cilantro, for garnish

Method

  1. Make the steaming rice flour: toast the rice with the peppercorns and star anise in a dry skillet over medium-low heat for 10 to 12 minutes, until golden. Grind it coarsely, like fine couscous. Coarse grains give the dish its texture; packaged rice flour for steaming also works.
  2. Mix the chicken with the doubanjiang, rice wine, ginger, garlic, soy sauce, sugar, Sichuan pepper and oil and marinate for 30 minutes.
  3. Add the rice flour and the water and mix until every piece is evenly coated and slightly moist. The water lets the rice cook through.
  4. Line a steaming bowl or bamboo steamer with the sweet potato and pile the chicken on top.
  5. Steam over boiling water in a flat-bottom wok, covered, for 40 to 50 minutes, topping up the water, until the chicken is tender and the rice coating is soft and sticky.
  6. Turn out onto a plate and scatter with green onion and cilantro.

Common mistakes

  • Too much rice flour makes the dish pasty instead of fragrant; use just enough to coat.
  • Under-steaming leaves the chicken firm and the flour gritty.

Authentic tips

  • Toasted rice flour with star anise and cloves (sold as steamed-meat flour) gives the traditional aroma.
  • The sweet potato soaks up the juices and is the best part.

Substitutions

  • Pork belly gives a richer version; steam 40 minutes longer.
  • Bone-in thighs give the best flavor; boneless will dry out if you shorten nothing.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; re-steam for 10 minutes.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months; thaw overnight and re-steam.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Stir-fried greens
  • Soup: Not needed; the steamed dish is rich
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
