Pào Jiāo Jī Dīng (Pickled Chili Chicken) 泡椒鸡丁

Pao jiao, red chilies pickled in brine, is a signature Sichuan pantry ingredient, and chicken stir-fried with it is a bright, tangy, spicy dish.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 500 g1.1 lbChicken thighs, diced
  • 80 g3 ozPickled red chilies
  • 30 g1 ozPickled ginger
  • 100 g3.5 ozCelery
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 g1 tbspCornstarch
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Toss the diced chicken with the soy sauce, cornstarch and 1 tablespoon of water; leave 10 minutes. Chop the pickled chilies and ginger coarsely and cut the celery into 1 cm pieces.
  2. Heat 2 tablespoons of the oil in a wok over high heat until shimmering and stir-fry the chicken for 2 minutes, until golden and about 80 percent cooked; remove.
  3. Add the remaining oil, then the pickled chilies, pickled ginger and garlic, and fry 1 minute until the kitchen smells sharp and tangy.
  4. Return the chicken, add the celery and a splash of the pickle brine and toss 1 minute.

Common mistakes

  • Over-salting: the pickles carry a lot of salt already, so taste before adding more.
  • Cooking the chicken through before it returns to the sauce dries out the thighs.

Authentic tips

  • The pickle brine is a free seasoning; add a tablespoon for tang.
  • Use bright red pickled chilies for color and a clean sour flavor.

Substitutions

  • Fresh red chilies plus an extra spoonful of vinegar replace pickled chilies.
  • Pork loin works with the same method.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day refrigerated; reheat in a hot wok.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Pickled mustard greens soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
