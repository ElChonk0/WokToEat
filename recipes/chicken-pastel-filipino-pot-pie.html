Pastel de Pollo (Filipino Chicken Pastel)

▾

Surprise me with

Pastel, a stew of chicken and vegetables baked under a pastry crust, is a Spanish-influenced Filipino festive dish, especially popular in Pampanga.

Method

  1. Make the pastry: rub the cold butter into the flour with a pinch of salt until it looks like coarse crumbs, then add the ice water and press into a dough. Wrap and chill for 30 minutes; cold butter makes a flaky crust.
  2. Marinate the chicken in the soy sauce and calamansi for 15 minutes.
  3. Melt the butter in a pan over medium heat and saute the garlic and onion for 3 minutes, then the chicken and sausage for 5 minutes until lightly browned.
  4. Add the stock, potatoes and carrots and simmer for 15 minutes until tender. Add the mushrooms, peas and cream, sprinkle in the flour and simmer for 3 minutes until thick. Season and cool slightly.
  5. Heat the oven to 200 C (400 F). Pour the filling into a baking dish, roll out the pastry and lay it over the top, crimping the edges. Cut a steam vent and brush with egg yolk.
  6. Bake for 25 to 30 minutes, until the crust is deep golden. Rest for 10 minutes before serving.

Common mistakes

  • Hot filling melts the pastry; cool it first.
  • Overworking the dough makes it tough.

Authentic tips

  • Brush with egg for a glossy crust.
  • Traditionally made at Christmas.

Substitutions

  • Store-bought puff pastry replaces homemade.
  • Chicken sausage can be replaced by ham or omitted.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a 180 C (350 F) oven.

Freezing: Freeze unbaked pies for 2 months.
