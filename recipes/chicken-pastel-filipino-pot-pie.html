Pastel de Pollo (Filipino Chicken Pastel)

▾

Surprise me with

Pastel, a stew of chicken and vegetables baked under a pastry crust, is a Spanish-influenced Filipino festive dish, especially popular in Pampanga.

  • Cook 45 min

  • 500 g1.1 lbChicken thighs, skinless boneless
  • 350 g12 ozPotatoes, carrots, peas
  • 100 g3.5 ozChicken sausage or ham
  • 80 g3 ozOnion and garlic
  • 60 ml1/4 cupCream
  • 200 g7 ozFlour, for pastry
  • 60 g2 ozCold butter, for pastry
  • 15 g1 tbspButter, for the filling
  • 60 ml1/4 cupWater
  • 5 g1 tspSalt

Method

  1. Rub the cold butter into the flour with the salt, bind with the water to a dough, wrap and chill 30 minutes.
  2. Melt the filling butter and brown the chicken 5 minutes; add the onion and garlic and cook 3 minutes.
  3. Add the potatoes, carrots, sausage and 250 ml water, simmer 15 minutes, then add the cream and peas and simmer 5 minutes; cool.
  4. Spoon into a baking dish and cover with the rolled pastry, sealing the edges; cut a vent.
  5. Bake at 190 C (375 F) for 30 minutes until golden.

Common mistakes

  • Hot filling melts the pastry; cool it first.
  • Overworking the dough makes it tough.

Authentic tips

  • Brush with egg for a glossy crust.
  • Traditionally made at Christmas.

Substitutions

  • Store-bought puff pastry replaces homemade.
  • Chicken sausage can be replaced by ham or omitted.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in a 180 C (350 F) oven.

Freezing: Freeze unbaked pies for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Green salad
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Leche flan
  • Beverage: Hot coffee
