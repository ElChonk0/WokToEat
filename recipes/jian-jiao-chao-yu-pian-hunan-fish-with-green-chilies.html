Jiān Jiāo Chǎo Yú Piàn (Stir-Fried Fish Slices with Green Chilies) 尖椒炒鱼片

▾

Surprise me with

Fresh green chilies stir-fried with fermented black beans are typical of Hunan home cooking, which favors the heat of fresh over dried chilies.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 450 g1 lbHaddock fillet, sliced 6 mm thick
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt
  • 10 g1 tbspCornstarch
  • 11Egg white
  • 200 g7 ozGreen chilies (jian jiao), sliced
  • 15 g1 tbspFermented black beans, rinsed
  • 4 cloves4 clovesGarlic, sliced
  • 10 g1/3 ozGinger, shredded
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 20 ml4 tspLight soy sauce
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 3 g1/2 tspSugar
  • 1 g1/4 tspWhite pepper

Method

  1. Mix the fish with the salt, egg white and cornstarch and rest 10 minutes.
  2. Heat half the oil in a wide pan over medium-high heat until shimmering, slide the fish in a single layer and cook 1 minute per side without moving it; lift out while still slightly underdone.
  3. Add the remaining oil and stir-fry the black beans, garlic and ginger for 20 seconds.
  4. Add the chilies and stir-fry over high heat for 2 minutes until blistered and softened.
  5. Add the wine, soy sauce, sugar and white pepper, return the fish, and toss gently for 30 seconds until cooked through.

Cook’s notes

Written for haddock fillet.

Common mistakes

  • Tossing the fish vigorously breaks it; fold it in with a spatula.
  • A pan that is not hot enough steams the chilies.

Authentic tips

  • Remove the chili seeds for less heat.
  • Cook the fish only until just under done and it finishes with the chilies.

Substitutions

  • Cod loin replaces the haddock.
  • Sweet green peppers replace part of the chilies for a milder dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh orange
  • Beverage: Hot tea
