Shǔ Zǎi Mèn Zhū Ròu (Cantonese Braised Pork with Potatoes) 薯仔焖猪肉

Pork belly or shoulder braised with potatoes in soy, oyster sauce and a little fermented bean curd is a thrifty everyday Cantonese family dish.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 1 hr
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Put the pork in cold water, bring to a boil, simmer 3 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  2. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven over medium-high heat and brown the pork for 5 minutes. Add the ginger, garlic and shallots and fry 1 minute, then mash in the red bean curd.
  3. Add the wine, oyster sauce, both soy sauces, sugar, star anise and water. Bring to a boil, cover and simmer for 35 minutes.
  4. Add the potatoes and simmer covered for 20 minutes more, until both are tender.
  5. Uncover and boil for 3 to 5 minutes to reduce the sauce until it coats the potatoes. Scatter with green onion.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the potatoes at the start turns them to mush.
  • Skipping the blanch leaves a cloudy, scummy sauce.

Authentic tips

  • Red fermented bean curd gives the reddish color and a savory, almost cheesy depth.
  • Pork belly makes it richer; shoulder keeps it meaty.

Substitutions

  • Taro replaces the potatoes for a more festive version.
  • No nam yu: add 1 extra tablespoon of oyster sauce.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months; potatoes soften slightly.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and garlic bok choy
  • Soup: Spinach tofu soup
  • Dessert: Orange segments
  • Beverage: Hot tea
