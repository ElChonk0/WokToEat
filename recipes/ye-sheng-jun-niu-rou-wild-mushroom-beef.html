Yě Shēng Jūn Niú Ròu (Wild Mushroom Beef) 野生菌牛肉

▾

Yunnan's wild mushroom season brings stir-fries of beef and mushrooms with garlic and chilies.

  • 400 g14 ozBeef sirloin, sliced thin
  • 10 ml2 tspLight soy sauce, for the marinade
  • 10 g1 tbspCornstarch
  • 300 g10 ozMixed mushrooms (wild or king oyster, shiitake), sliced
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 25 g1 ozGarlic, sliced
  • 15 g1/2 ozFresh chilies, sliced
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 10 ml2 tspLight soy sauce, for the sauce
  • 2 g1/3 tspSalt

Method

  1. Toss the beef with the marinade soy sauce, cornstarch and a tablespoon of water.
  2. Wild mushrooms must be cooked through: Yunnan cooks stir-fry them for at least 8 to 10 minutes with plenty of garlic, and only buy from trusted sources. Cultivated mushrooms such as king oyster, shiitake and oyster are a safe substitute.
  3. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil and stir-fry the mushrooms with the garlic and chilies over medium-high heat for 8 to 10 minutes until tender, browned and their water has evaporated.
  4. Push them aside, add the remaining oil and the beef and stir-fry for 1 to 2 minutes until just cooked.
  5. Add the Shaoxing wine, soy sauce and salt and toss for 30 seconds. Serve with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Wild mushrooms must be cooked thoroughly.
  • Crowding the pan steams the mushrooms.

Authentic tips

  • Cultivated mixed mushrooms work if wild ones are not available.
  • Never eat foraged mushrooms you cannot identify.

Substitutions

  • Cultivated mixed mushrooms replace wild ones.
  • Pork takes the same method.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Pu'er tea
