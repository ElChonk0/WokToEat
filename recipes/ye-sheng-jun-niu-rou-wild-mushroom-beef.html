Yě Shēng Jūn Niú Ròu (Wild Mushroom Beef) 野生菌牛肉

▾

Yunnan's wild mushroom season brings stir-fries of beef and mushrooms with garlic and chilies.

  • Cook 12 min

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 400 g14 ozBeef sirloin, sliced
  • 300 g10 ozMixed mushrooms
  • 40 g3 tbspGarlic and chilies
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 10 g2 tspCornstarch
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Slice the beef thin against the grain and marinate with half the soy sauce, wine and cornstarch for 10 minutes.
  2. Heat half the oil in a wok over high heat and sear the beef 1 minute; remove.
  3. Add the remaining oil, then the mushrooms and cook 5 minutes until browned and their liquid evaporates.
  4. Add the garlic and chilies for 30 seconds.
  5. Return the beef and the remaining soy sauce and toss 1 minute.

Common mistakes

  • Wild mushrooms must be cooked thoroughly.
  • Crowding the pan steams the mushrooms.

Authentic tips

  • Cultivated mixed mushrooms work if wild ones are not available.
  • Never eat foraged mushrooms you cannot identify.

Substitutions

  • Cultivated mixed mushrooms replace wild ones.
  • Pork takes the same method.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Pu'er tea
