Mǐn Nán Dòu Fu Wán (Fried Tofu Balls in Broth) 闽南豆腐丸

Fried tofu balls mixed with vegetables and starch are a Minnan specialty simmered in a clear broth, served at festivals and family gatherings.

  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 400 g14 ozFirm tofu, mashed
  • 100 g3.5 ozCarrot and celery, minced
  • 45 g1/3 cupSweet potato starch
  • 20 g2/3 ozDried shiitake, minced
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 800 ml3.5 cupsVegetable stock
  • 400 ml1.75 cupsNeutral oil for frying

Method

  1. Press the tofu in a clean cloth to remove as much water as possible; mash with a fork.
  2. Mix the tofu with the carrot, celery, shiitake, starch and soy sauce until it holds together.
  3. Roll into 3 cm balls.
  4. Heat the oil to 175 C (350 F) and fry in batches for 3-4 minutes until deep golden; drain.
  5. Bring the stock to a simmer, add the balls and simmer 5 minutes; serve with scallion.

Common mistakes

  • A wet mixture falls apart in the oil; squeeze the tofu dry.
  • Oil that is too cool makes greasy balls.

Authentic tips

  • Chill the formed balls for 15 minutes before frying.
  • Add the starch gradually until the mix holds.

Substitutions

  • Bake the balls at 220 C (425 F) for a lighter version.
  • Add cabbage to the broth.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freeze fried balls for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; the balls sit in broth
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
