Mǐn Nán Dòu Fu Wán (Fried Tofu Balls in Broth) 闽南豆腐丸

▾

Surprise me with

Fried tofu balls mixed with vegetables and starch are a Minnan specialty simmered in a clear broth, served at festivals and family gatherings.

  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 400 g14 ozFirm tofu, pressed and mashed
  • 100 g3.5 ozCarrot and celery, minced
  • 44Dried shiitake, soaked and minced
  • 45 g1/3 cupSweet potato starch
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt
  • 1 g1/2 tspWhite pepper
  • 500 ml2 cupsNeutral oil, for frying
  • 800 ml3.5 cupsVegetable stock
  • 20 g3/4 ozCelery leaves and fried shallots

Method

  1. Wrap the tofu in cloth and squeeze out as much water as possible, then mash it. Dry tofu holds its shape.
  2. Mix with the carrot, celery, shiitake, starch, soy sauce, salt and pepper until it holds together.
  3. Roll into walnut-size balls and fry at 170 C (340 F) for 4 to 5 minutes until golden. Drain.
  4. Bring the stock to a boil, season with salt and add the tofu balls for 3 minutes so they soak up the broth.
  5. Serve in bowls topped with celery leaves and fried shallots.

Common mistakes

  • A wet mixture falls apart in the oil; squeeze the tofu dry.
  • Oil that is too cool makes greasy balls.

Authentic tips

  • Chill the formed balls for 15 minutes before frying.
  • Add the starch gradually until the mix holds.

Substitutions

  • Bake the balls at 220 C (425 F) for a lighter version.
  • Add cabbage to the broth.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freeze fried balls for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; the balls sit in broth
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
