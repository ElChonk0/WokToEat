Kerala Beef Stew (with Appam)

▾

Surprise me with

Kerala stew, a mild coconut-milk stew with whole spices, is a Syrian Christian specialty eaten with appam at breakfast and Easter.

  • Cook 90 min

  • 150 g5 ozOnion, ginger, green chilies
  • 6 g2 tspCinnamon, cardamom, cloves
  • 3 g1 tspBlack pepper
  • 30 ml2 tbspCoconut oil and curry leaves
  • 6 g1 tspSalt

Method

  1. Pressure cook the beef with half the onion, ginger, chilies, salt and 200 ml water for 25 minutes (or simmer 1.5 hours).
  2. Heat the coconut oil, fry the whole spices and remaining onion 5 minutes.
  3. Add the beef with its liquid and the potatoes and carrots; simmer 15 minutes.
  4. Pour in the coconut milk and simmer gently 10 minutes without boiling.
  5. Temper with curry leaves and serve with appam.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling after adding the coconut milk splits it.
  • Browning the onion makes it too dark; the stew is pale.

Authentic tips

  • Serve for breakfast with appam.
  • Add a splash of vinegar for authenticity.

Substitutions

  • Chicken or lamb replace beef.
  • Instant appam mix saves time.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months without the potatoes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Appam
  • Soup: Not needed; the stew is its own soup
  • Dessert: Payasam
  • Beverage: Black tea
