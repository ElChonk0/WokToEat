Dan Zai Mian (Tainan Danzai Noodles) 擔仔麵

Danzai noodles originated in Tainan in 1895 as a fisherman's off-season street food: a small bowl of noodles with a shrimp-head broth and minced pork.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 300 g10 ozYellow wheat noodles
  • 300 g10 ozShrimp with heads
  • 100 g3.5 ozMinced pork
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 1 L4 cupsShrimp or pork stock
  • 15 g1 tbspFried shallots
  • 15 g1/2 ozCilantro
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce

Method

  1. Simmer the shrimp heads (or whole shrimp) in the stock for 15 minutes; strain.
  2. Fry the pork with the garlic and soy sauce until browned.
  3. Blanch the shrimp in the broth for 1 minute and peel.
  4. Cook the noodles 1 minute and divide among small bowls.
  5. Add broth, pork, shrimp, shallots and cilantro.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooked noodles go soft.
  • Cooking the shrimp too long makes them rubbery.

Authentic tips

  • Traditional bowls are small, a snack, not a meal.
  • The shrimp-head broth is the heart.

Substitutions

  • Chicken stock plus shrimp shells replaces shrimp-head broth.
  • Cooked pork belly slices replace minced pork.

Storage

Leftovers: Store noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 3 days.

Freezing: Freeze the broth for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: A second small bowl is typical
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Oolong tea
