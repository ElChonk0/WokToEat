Jiàng Mèn Huáng Yú (Soybean Paste Braised Yellow Croaker) 酱焖黄花鱼

Jiang men, braising in fermented soybean paste, is a Shandong and Dongbei home technique that suits oily or strongly flavored fish.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Shandong Seafood LunchDinnerBraise / stew30 min or less

  • 800 g1.75 lbYellow croaker, cleaned
  • 45 g3 tbspYellow soybean paste
  • 50 g4 tbspGinger, garlic and scallion
  • 10 g2 tspSugar
  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 250 ml1 cupWater
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Pat the croaker dry and fry in the oil over medium-high heat 2 minutes per side until lightly golden; lift out.
  2. Add the soybean paste, ginger, garlic and scallion to the pan and fry 1 minute until fragrant.
  3. Add the sugar, wine and water and bring to a simmer.
  4. Return the fish, cover and braise 10 minutes, spooning the sauce over.
  5. Uncover and reduce the sauce for 2-3 minutes until it clings.

Common mistakes

  • Using too much paste makes the sauce too salty.
  • Burning the paste turns it bitter; keep it moving.

Authentic tips

  • Taste the sauce before adding salt.
  • Mackerel and other oily fish also work well.

Substitutions

  • Whole sea bream or cod steaks replace the croaker.
  • A little sugar balances the salty paste.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or buns
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
