Yào Cái Jī Tāng (Chinese Herbal Chicken Soup) 药材鸡汤

▾

Surprise me with

Herbal chicken soup is a Chinese home tonic soup in which chicken is simmered with dried herbs such as dang shen, goji berries and red dates, drunk for warmth and recovery.

Method

  1. Put the chicken in a pot of cold water, bring it to a boil and cook for 3 minutes, until gray scum rises. Drain and rinse each piece under warm water; starting clean is what gives a clear, golden soup.
  2. Rinse the dried herbs, red dates, longan and goji briefly in cold water to wash off dust; do not soak them long, or they lose flavor before they reach the pot. Halve the red dates so they release their sweetness. Keep the goji aside.
  3. Put the chicken, codonopsis, Chinese yam, red dates, longan, ginger, wine and water in a Dutch oven or large pot. Bring it to a boil over high heat and skim off any foam that rises.
  4. Turn the heat down until the surface barely trembles, cover with the lid slightly ajar and simmer for 1 hour 15 minutes. A hard boil clouds the broth and toughens the chicken; a gentle simmer gives a clean, sweet, lightly herbal soup and meat that comes off the bone.
  5. Add the goji for the last 10 minutes, so they plump without turning mushy. Season with the salt, tasting as you go: the dates and longan make the broth naturally sweet, so it needs less salt than you would expect.
  6. Serve the broth first in small bowls, then the chicken and herbs. The codonopsis and yam are edible; the ginger slices can be left aside.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard makes the broth cloudy and the chicken stringy; keep it at a gentle simmer.
  • Over-salting early hides the herbs; season at the end.

Authentic tips

  • Skin the chicken for a clearer, lighter broth.
  • Dip the chicken in light soy sauce and ginger to eat it.

Substitutions

  • A ready-mixed herbal soup pack from a Chinese market replaces the individual herbs.
  • Pork ribs replace chicken.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and blanched greens
  • Soup: The dish is a soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot water or chrysanthemum tea
