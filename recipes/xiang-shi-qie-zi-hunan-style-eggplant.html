Xiāng Shì Qié Zi (Hunan Smoky Chili Eggplant) 湘式烧茄子

▾

Surprise me with

Hunan home cooks char or fry eggplant until silky and dress it with fresh chilies, garlic and soy sauce for a smoky, spicy dish.

  • Cook 15 min

  • 600 g1.3 lbLong eggplant
  • 60 g2 ozFresh chilies
  • 30 g2 tbspGarlic
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspChinkiang vinegar
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 60 ml4 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Cut the eggplant into 6 cm lengths and halve lengthwise; salt for 10 minutes and pat dry.
  2. Heat half the oil in a wok over medium-high heat and fry the eggplant 6-7 minutes until soft and golden; lift out.
  3. Add the remaining oil, chilies and garlic and fry 1 minute until blistered.
  4. Add the soy sauce, vinegar, sugar and a splash of water, return the eggplant and toss 1 minute.

Common mistakes

  • Eggplant soaks up oil; salting and drying it first helps.
  • Undercooked eggplant is rubbery; cook until completely soft.

Authentic tips

  • Charring the eggplant over a gas flame or charcoal gives a smokier version.
  • Season while it is hot so it absorbs the sauce.

Substitutions

  • Zucchini replaces eggplant.
  • Soft tofu cubes make it heartier.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Tofu soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
