Peking-Style Crispy Chicken Thigh 北京香酥鸡

▾

Surprise me with

Echoing the technique behind Peking duck — marinating, air-drying, then frying for a shatteringly crisp skin — this dish applies the same principle to chicken thighs for a faster home version.

  • Prep 15 min (plus overnight drying)
  • Cook 45 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 800 g1.75 lbChicken thighs, bone-in, skin-on
  • 2 g1 tspFive-spice powder
  • 6 g1 tspSalt
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 15 ml1 tbspMaltose or honey, for glazing
  • 15 ml1 tbspRice vinegar, for glazing
  • 30 ml2 tbspHot water, for glazing
  • 15 ml1 tbspNeutral oil, for brushing
  • 60 ml1/4 cupHoisin or sweet bean sauce, for serving
  • to tasteto tasteMandarin pancakes or scallion pancakes, cucumber and green onion

Method

  1. Pour boiling water over the chicken skin to tighten it, then pat dry. Rub the meat side with the five-spice, salt and Shaoxing wine, keeping the skin clean.
  2. Dissolve the maltose in the hot water with the vinegar and brush it over the skin. Refrigerate the thighs uncovered, skin-side up, overnight. Drying the skin like Peking duck is what makes it shatter.
  3. Heat the oven to 200 C (400 F) and set the thighs skin-side up on a rack over a tray of water.
  4. Roast for 35 to 40 minutes, brushing once with oil, until the skin is lacquered deep mahogany and the meat reaches 74 C (165 F).
  5. Rest for 5 minutes, then slice and serve with pancakes, hoisin, cucumber and green onion, Peking-duck style.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the air-drying step, which is essential for achieving genuinely crackling skin.
  • Frying at too high heat, which browns the skin before the interior is properly cooked.

Authentic tips

  • The air-drying step (uncovered, in the fridge) removes surface moisture and is the real secret to crispy skin — don't rush it.
  • A wire rack while air-drying lets air circulate on all sides for more even crisping.

Substitutions

  • Air-drying overnight in the refrigerator is more forgiving than the 4 hours.
  • An air fryer at 200 C (400 F) for 18 minutes replaces deep-frying.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 2 days and recrisps in a 200 C (400 F) oven for 8 minutes.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Thin pancakes with hoisin, scallion and cucumber
  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Hawthorn candy
  • Beverage: Hot jasmine tea
