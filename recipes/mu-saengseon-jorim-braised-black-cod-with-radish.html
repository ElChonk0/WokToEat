Mu Saengseon Jorim (Braised Black Cod with Radish) 무생선조림

▾

Surprise me with

Jorim, braising in a seasoned soy-chili sauce until the liquid is nearly gone, is a staple Korean method for fish, usually over sliced radish that absorbs the sauce.

  • 600 g1 lb 5 ozBlack cod, in 5 cm pieces
  • 300 g10 ozKorean radish (mu), in thick half-moons
  • 100 g3.5 ozOnion, sliced
  • 400 ml1 3/4 cupsWater
  • 45 ml3 tbspSoy sauce
  • 15 g2 tbspGochugaru (Korean chili flakes)
  • 15 g1 tbspGochujang
  • 4 cloves4 clovesGarlic, minced
  • 10 g2 tspGinger, grated
  • 15 g1 tbspSugar
  • 30 ml2 tbspRice wine
  • 40 g1.5 ozGreen chilies and scallions, sliced

Method

  1. Pat the black cod dry. Mix the soy sauce, gochugaru, gochujang, garlic, ginger, sugar and rice wine into a sauce.
  2. Lay the radish in the bottom of a wide, shallow pan, add the water and half the sauce and boil over medium-high heat for about 10 minutes, until the radish is soft and the liquid has thickened a little. Cooking the radish first lets the delicate fish spend only a few minutes in the pot.
  3. Add the onion, then lay the fish on top in a single layer and spoon the rest of the sauce over.
  4. Simmer over medium heat for 4 to 5 minutes, carefully turn the pieces and cook 4 to 5 minutes more, spooning the sauce over again and again, until the fish is opaque and flakes and the sauce is glossy.
  5. Scatter the chilies and scallions over for the last minute and serve with rice and the sauce-soaked radish.

Cook’s notes

Written for black cod. Korean cooks braise mackerel, cutlassfish or cod this way.

Common mistakes

  • Stirring breaks the fish; baste instead.
  • Skipping the radish head start leaves it hard in the center.

Authentic tips

  • The radish is as important as the fish, so cut it thick.
  • Baste until the sauce is almost gone for a concentrated flavor.

Substitutions

  • Cod loin or mackerel replaces the black cod.
  • Daikon replaces Korean radish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and kimchi
  • Soup: Soybean paste soup
  • Dessert: Sliced pear
  • Beverage: Barley tea (boricha)
