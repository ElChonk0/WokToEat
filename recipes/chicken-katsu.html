Chicken Katsu チキンカツ

▾

Surprise me with

Katsu describes Japan's take on breaded, deep-fried cutlets, adapted from Western influences in the late 19th century and now a Japanese comfort food staple, typically served with a tangy tonkatsu sauce.

Method

  1. Pound the thighs to an even 1 cm (1/2 in) thickness so they cook through by the time the crumb is golden. Season both sides with salt and pepper.
  2. Set up three plates: flour, beaten egg mixed with the tablespoon of oil, and panko. The oil in the egg helps the panko cling and fry up crisp.
  3. Dredge each piece in flour and shake off the excess, dip it in egg, then press it firmly into the panko on both sides.
  4. Heat the oil in a deep skillet or Dutch oven to 170 C (340 F), about 4 cm (1.5 in) deep. Fry one or two cutlets at a time for 2 to 3 minutes per side, until deep golden and the center reads 74 C (165 F).
  5. Drain upright on a rack for a minute so the bottom does not steam, then slice into 2.5 cm (1 in) strips. Serve with shredded cabbage, rice and tonkatsu sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the pounding step, which leaves the chicken uneven and prone to overcooking in thick spots.
  • Overcrowding the pan, which drops the oil temperature and produces a greasy, soggy crust.

Authentic tips

  • Panko's larger, airier crumbs give katsu its distinctively light, crunchy crust compared to regular breadcrumbs.
  • Draining on a wire rack instead of paper towels keeps the bottom crust from steaming and going soft.

Substitutions

  • Chicken breast, pounded thin, is the more traditional cut and works equally well.
  • No tonkatsu sauce: a mix of ketchup, Worcestershire sauce, and a little soy sauce approximates it.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat in an oven or air fryer to restore crispness.

Freezing: Freezes well, breaded and uncooked, for up to a month; fry directly from frozen with extra time.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and shredded cabbage with tonkatsu sauce
  • Soup: Miso soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot sencha
