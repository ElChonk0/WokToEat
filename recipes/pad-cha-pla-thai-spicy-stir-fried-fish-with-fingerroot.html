Pad Cha Pla (Spicy Stir-Fried Fish with Fingerroot)

▾

Surprise me with

Pad cha, a hot stir-fry with fresh green peppercorns, fingerroot (krachai) and holy basil, is a Thai dish made with fish, seafood or meat.

  • Cook 10 min

  • 500 g1 lb 2 ozCod loin, in 3 cm pieces
  • 20 g2 tbspCornstarch
  • 60 ml4 tbspNeutral oil
  • 60 g2 ozFingerroot (krachai), shredded
  • 20 g3/4 ozFresh green peppercorns
  • 5 cloves5 clovesGarlic, crushed
  • 25 g1 ozRed chilies, crushed
  • 44Kaffir lime leaves
  • 20 ml4 tspOyster sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspFish sauce
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 60 ml1/4 cupWater or stock
  • 30 g1 ozThai holy basil

Method

  1. Pat the fish dry and toss it in the cornstarch. Fry in the oil in a flat-bottom wok over medium-high heat for 3 to 4 minutes, turning gently, until golden and just cooked. Lift out and pour off all but 2 tablespoons of oil.
  2. Pound the garlic and chilies in a mortar into a coarse paste.
  3. Fry the paste with the fingerroot over medium heat for 1 minute, until fragrant; fingerroot gives pad cha its distinctive peppery, earthy perfume.
  4. Add the green peppercorns, lime leaves, oyster sauce, fish sauce, sugar and water and bubble for 30 seconds.
  5. Return the fish and toss gently to coat without breaking it. Turn off the heat, fold in the holy basil and serve with rice.

Cook’s notes

Written for cod loin.

Common mistakes

  • Too much stirring flakes the fish; fold, do not stir.
  • Fingerroot burns easily; keep the heat steady.

Authentic tips

  • Frozen fingerroot in jars works if fresh is not available.
  • Add more chilies for heat.

Substitutions

  • Haddock replaces the cod.
  • Fresh ginger and extra peppercorns replace fingerroot.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Jasmine rice
  • Soup: Clear gaeng jued
  • Dessert: Fresh pineapple
  • Beverage: Iced Thai tea
