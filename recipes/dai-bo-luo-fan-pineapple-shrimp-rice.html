Bō Luó Fàn (Dai Pineapple Shrimp Rice) 傣味菠萝饭

▾

Surprise me with

Pineapple rice, served in a hollowed pineapple, is a Dai festival dish from Xishuangbanna combining sticky rice with the sweetness of fresh fruit.

  • 400 g14 ozShrimp, peeled
  • 450 g1 lbCooked jasmine or sticky rice
  • 200 g7 ozPineapple, diced
  • 22Eggs
  • 40 g1.5 ozCashews or raisins
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Spread the cooked rice on a tray to dry slightly; use day-old rice if possible.
  2. Heat half the oil in a wok over high heat and scramble the eggs; remove.
  3. Add the remaining oil, garlic and shrimp and stir-fry 1-2 minutes until pink.
  4. Add the rice, soy sauce and pineapple and toss 3 minutes until hot; return the egg and cashews.
  5. Pack into a hollowed pineapple half to serve.

Common mistakes

  • Fresh, wet rice turns mushy.
  • Cooking the pineapple too long makes the rice soggy.

Authentic tips

  • Short-grain or sticky rice gives the traditional texture.
  • Hollow the pineapple with a spoon and keep the shell to serve.

Substitutions

  • A bowl replaces the hollowed pineapple.
  • Chicken replaces shrimp.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat with a splash of water.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Not needed; the rice is the meal
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh pineapple
  • Beverage: Chilled tea
