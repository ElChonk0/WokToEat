Chettinad Prawn Masala

▾

Surprise me with

Chettinad cuisine from Tamil Nadu is known for its fiery roasted spices; the prawn masala showcases it in a thick dry gravy.

  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Ingredients

  • 700 g1.5 lbPrawns
  • 300 g10 ozOnion and tomato
  • 20 g2 tbspChettinad masala
  • 15 g1/2 ozCurry leaves
  • 30 g2 tbspGinger and garlic paste
  • 6 g2 tspFennel and pepper
  • 40 g1.5 ozCoconut
  • 45 ml3 tbspSesame oil

Method

  1. Fry the onion, curry leaves and Chettinad masala in the sesame oil for 8 minutes until golden.
  2. Add the ginger-garlic paste, fennel and pepper for 1 minute.
  3. Add the tomato and cook 6 minutes until soft.
  4. Add the prawns and simmer 6 minutes.
  5. Finish with the coconut.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking makes the prawns rubbery.
  • Burning the spices makes it bitter.

Authentic tips

  • Dry roast the spices for authentic flavor.
  • Serve with rice and rasam.

Substitutions

  • Large shrimp replace prawns.
  • Ground Chettinad masala from a market saves effort.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or parotta
  • Soup: Cucumber raita
  • Dessert: Payasam
  • Beverage: Filter coffee
