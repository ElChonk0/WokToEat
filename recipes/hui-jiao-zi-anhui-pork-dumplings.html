Huī Shì Zhū Ròu Jiǎo Zi (Anhui Pork and Chive Dumplings) 徽式猪肉韭菜饺

▾

Surprise me with

Dumplings are a northern staple that also spread into Anhui's northern plains, where pork and chive fillings are common at the New Year.

  • Cook 8 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 4040Dumpling wrappers
  • 400 g14 ozPork, ground
  • 150 g5 ozChinese chives
  • 20 g1 tbspGinger
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspSesame oil
  • 5 g1 tspSalt

Method

  1. Mix the pork with the ginger, soy sauce, sesame oil and salt, stirring in one direction and adding 3 tablespoons of water gradually until sticky.
  2. Fold in the chopped chives just before filling.
  3. Place a tablespoon of filling in each wrapper, fold and pleat.
  4. Boil in plenty of water 6 minutes until the dumplings float.
  5. Serve with black vinegar.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the chives early makes the filling watery.
  • Overfilling splits the wrappers.

Authentic tips

  • Toss the chives with a little oil first.
  • Leftover dumplings fry well.

Substitutions

  • Garlic chives replace Chinese chives.
  • Ground chicken thigh replaces some of the pork.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; pan-fry leftovers.

Freezing: Freeze uncooked on a tray for 2 months; boil from frozen for 8 minutes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Black vinegar dip
  • Soup: Hot and sour soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
