Dhal (Malaysian-Indian Lentil Curry)

Dhal, spiced lentil curry, came to Malaysia with Indian immigrants and is a mamak and rice-shop staple.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 30 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Malaysia / Singapore Tofu & Vegetarian LunchDinnerCurrySpicyVegetarianVegan

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 250 g9 ozRed lentils
  • 200 g7 ozOnion and tomato
  • 12 g1.5 tbspTurmeric and curry powder
  • 30 g2 tbspGarlic and ginger
  • 10 g1/3 ozCurry leaves
  • 3 g1 tspMustard seeds
  • 800 ml3.5 cupsWater
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil
  • 5 g1 tspSalt

Method

  1. Simmer the lentils with the turmeric in 800 ml water for 20 minutes until soft and creamy.
  2. Fry the mustard seeds and curry leaves in the oil until they pop.
  3. Add the onion, garlic and ginger and cook 5 minutes.
  4. Add the tomato and curry powder and cook 5 minutes.
  5. Stir into the lentils with the salt and simmer 5 minutes.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the spices makes it bitter.
  • Too little water makes it stodgy.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with roti or rice.
  • Add spinach for greens.

Substitutions

  • Yellow split peas replace red lentils.
  • Spinach adds greens.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Roti canai or rice
  • Soup: Acar
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Teh tarik
