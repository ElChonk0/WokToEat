Adobong Kangkong (Water Spinach Adobo)

▾

Surprise me with

Adobong kangkong, water spinach cooked in vinegar, soy sauce and garlic, applies the Philippines' national adobo method to a cheap everyday vegetable.

  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 500 g1.1 lbWater spinach

  • 45 ml3 tbspCane vinegar
  • 30 ml2 tbspSoy sauce
  • 2 g1 tspBlack pepper
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Cut the water spinach into 8 cm lengths and drain well.
  2. Fry the garlic in the oil until golden.
  3. Add the vinegar and soy sauce and simmer 1 minute.
  4. Add the water spinach, pepper and sugar.
  5. Toss 3 minutes until just wilted.

Common mistakes

  • Wet greens dilute the sauce.
  • Overcooking makes it limp.

Authentic tips

  • Stems first.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • Spinach replaces kangkong.
  • Add crispy tofu for protein.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Calamansi juice
