Adobong Kangkong (Water Spinach Adobo)

▾

Surprise me with

Adobong kangkong, water spinach cooked in vinegar, soy sauce and garlic, applies the Philippines' national adobo method to a cheap everyday vegetable.

  • Cook 10 min

  • 500 g1.1 lbWater spinach (kangkong), cut into 8 cm (3 in) lengths, stems and leaves separate
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil
  • 8 cloves8 clovesGarlic, minced
  • 1/21/2Onion, sliced
  • 150 g5 ozFirm tofu, cubed and fried (optional)
  • 30 ml2 tbspSoy sauce
  • 45 ml3 tbspCane vinegar
  • 2 g1 tspBlack pepper
  • 5 g1 tspSugar

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a flat-bottom wok over medium heat and fry half the garlic for 1 to 2 minutes until golden and crisp. Lift it out for garnish.
  2. Saute the remaining garlic and the onion for 1 minute.
  3. Add the kangkong stems and the tofu and stir-fry over high heat for 1 minute.
  4. Add the soy sauce and vinegar and let them bubble for 30 seconds without stirring, then add the leaves, pepper and sugar and toss for 1 minute until just wilted.
  5. Top with the crisp garlic and serve at once with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Wet greens dilute the sauce.
  • Overcooking makes it limp.

Authentic tips

  • Stems first.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • Spinach replaces kangkong.
  • Add crispy tofu for protein.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Calamansi juice
