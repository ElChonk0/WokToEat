Chana Masala (Spiced Chickpea Curry)

▾

Surprise me with

Chana masala, spiced chickpeas in a tangy tomato gravy, is a Punjabi staple eaten with bhature, puri or rice.

Method

  1. If cooking dried chickpeas, simmer them with a black tea bag for the dark color of Punjabi chole. Keep 250 ml (1 cup) of the cooking liquid.
  2. Heat the oil in a heavy pot over medium heat and fry the cumin, bay leaf and black cardamom for 30 seconds, then the onions for 12 to 15 minutes until deep brown. Deeply browned onions give chana masala its rich color.
  3. Add the ginger-garlic paste and chilies for 1 minute, then the tomato puree, chana masala, chili, turmeric and salt. Cook for 8 to 10 minutes, stirring, until the masala is thick and the oil separates.
  4. Add the chickpeas and the reserved liquid, then mash a few chickpeas against the pot to thicken the gravy. Simmer for 15 to 20 minutes.
  5. Stir in the amchur and simmer for 2 minutes. Garnish with cilantro, ginger and onion and serve with bhatura, puri or rice.

Common mistakes

  • Under-browned onions give a thin flavor.
  • Too much water makes it soupy.

Authentic tips

  • Use canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed.
  • Serve with bhature or rice.

Substitutions

  • Canned chickpeas replace cooked.
  • Add a teaspoon of pomegranate powder.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; better the next day.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Bhature or basmati rice
  • Soup: Cucumber raita
  • Dessert: Gulab jamun
  • Beverage: Masala chai
