Chana Masala (Spiced Chickpea Curry)

▾

Surprise me with

Chana masala, spiced chickpeas in a tangy tomato gravy, is a Punjabi staple eaten with bhature, puri or rice.

  • Cook 30 min

  • 20 g2 tbspChana masala spice blend
  • 10 g2 tspTamarind or amchur
  • 20 g2/3 ozCilantro
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 6 g1 tspSalt

Method

  1. Fry the onion in the oil until well browned, 10 minutes.
  2. Add the ginger, garlic and spice blend and fry 1 minute.
  3. Add the tomato and cook 8 minutes until the oil separates.
  4. Add the chickpeas, salt and 200 ml water and simmer 15 minutes, mashing a few chickpeas to thicken.
  5. Finish with the tamarind and cilantro.

Common mistakes

  • Under-browned onions give a thin flavor.
  • Too much water makes it soupy.

Authentic tips

  • Use canned chickpeas, drained and rinsed.
  • Serve with bhature or rice.

Substitutions

  • Canned chickpeas replace cooked.
  • Add a teaspoon of pomegranate powder.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; better the next day.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Bhature or basmati rice
  • Soup: Cucumber raita
  • Dessert: Gulab jamun
  • Beverage: Masala chai
