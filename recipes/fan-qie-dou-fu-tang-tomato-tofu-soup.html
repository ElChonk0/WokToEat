Fān Qié Dòu Fu Tāng (Tomato Tofu Soup) 番茄豆腐汤

▾

Surprise me with

A light soup of tomatoes and soft tofu, sometimes with egg, is one of the quickest everyday soups in Cantonese home cooking.

  • Cook 15 min

Method

  1. Dice a third of the tomatoes finely, which melt into the broth, and cut the rest into wedges, which keep their shape. Cut the tofu into small cubes. Beat the eggs lightly, leaving some streaks, and mix the slurry.
  2. Heat a flat-bottom wok or pot over high heat, add the oil and, when it ripples, fry the ginger and green onion whites for 15 seconds. Lower the heat, add the finely diced tomato for 30 seconds, then the wedges for 30 seconds and the mushrooms for 30 seconds, until the tomato starts to break down and colors the oil.
  3. Add the stock, cover and bring to a boil, then add the tofu and simmer gently for 8 to 10 minutes, so the tomato flavors the broth.
  4. Turn the heat to low and stir in the slurry slowly until the soup is lightly thick, then bring it back up to a simmer.
  5. Pour in the eggs in a thin stream while stirring gently, without letting the soup boil hard, until the egg floats in soft clouds.
  6. Season with the ketchup, sugar, salt, pepper and sesame oil, taste for balance between sweet and tangy, and serve scattered with the green onion greens.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the tomatoes straight to the stock gives a thin, pale soup; fry them first.
  • Boiling hard breaks the soft tofu.

Authentic tips

  • A pinch of sugar balances sharp tomatoes.
  • Ripe, soft tomatoes give the best color.

Substitutions

  • Chicken stock is common if it does not need to be vegetarian.
  • Add sliced pork or fish for a heartier soup.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and stir-fried greens
  • Soup: This is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
