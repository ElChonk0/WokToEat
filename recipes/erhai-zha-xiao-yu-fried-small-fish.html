Erhai Zha Xiao Yu (Erhai Fried Small Fish) 洱海炸小鱼

Small fish from Erhai Lake are fried crisp and tossed with chili salt, a Dali market snack and beer food.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Yunnan Seafood LunchDinnerStir-fryDeep-friedSpicy30 min or less
230 kcal18 g protein10 g carbs13 g fat700 mg sodiumper serving

Draft. This recipe has been written but not yet cooked or checked by a person. Treat the amounts, times and nutrition figures as a starting point.

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 600 g1.3 lbSmall whole fish
  • 45 g1/3 cupCornstarch
  • 6 g1 tspSalt
  • 5 g2 tspChili powder
  • 2 g1 tspSichuan pepper
  • 400 ml1.75 cupsNeutral oil for frying
  • 15 g1/2 ozCilantro

Method

  1. Pat the fish very dry and toss with the cornstarch and a pinch of the salt.
  2. Heat the oil to 180 C (350 F) in a wok.
  3. Fry the fish in small batches for 3-4 minutes until crisp; drain on a rack.
  4. Toss with the remaining salt, chili powder and Sichuan pepper.
  5. Garnish with cilantro and serve immediately.

Common mistakes

  • Wet fish spatters and fries soggy.
  • Crowding the pan drops the oil temperature.

Authentic tips

  • Eat the fish whole, bones and all, as they are small.
  • Good with beer.

Substitutions

  • Whitebait or smelts replace small fish.
  • Air-fry at 200 C (400 F) for 8 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; eat while crisp.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
