Japchae (Glass Noodle Stir-Fry) 잡채

Japchae, sweet potato glass noodles stir-fried with vegetables, was created for a royal feast in the 17th century and is now a Korean celebration staple.

  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 200 g7 ozDried sweet potato noodles (dangmyeon)
  • 150 g5 ozSpinach
  • 200 g7 ozCarrot and onion
  • 100 g3.5 ozShiitake mushrooms
  • 45 ml3 tbspSoy sauce
  • 20 g1.5 tbspSugar
  • 30 ml2 tbspSesame oil
  • 20 g4 clovesGarlic
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Boil the noodles 6-7 minutes until soft and chewy; drain, rinse and cut into shorter lengths.
  2. Blanch the spinach 30 seconds, squeeze dry and season with a little soy sauce and sesame oil.
  3. Stir-fry the onion, carrot and mushrooms separately in the neutral oil, 2 minutes each.
  4. Toss the noodles with the soy sauce, sugar, garlic and sesame oil in the pan for 2 minutes.
  5. Combine with the vegetables and sprinkle with sesame seeds.

Common mistakes

  • Undercooked noodles are hard; cook until springy.
  • Stir-frying everything together makes the vegetables mushy.

Authentic tips

  • Cook each vegetable separately for color.
  • Serve warm or at room temperature.

Substitutions

  • Add thin beef strips for a traditional version.
  • Cook each vegetable separately for color.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; microwave with a splash of water.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
