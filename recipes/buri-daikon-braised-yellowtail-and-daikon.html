Buri Daikon (Braised Yellowtail and Daikon) ぶり大根

Buri daikon, winter yellowtail simmered with daikon in a sweet soy sauce, is a classic Japanese home dish that makes the most of fatty fish collars and cheap daikon.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 40 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 300 ml1.25 cupsWater

Method

  1. Cut the fish collar or steaks into pieces and pour boiling water over them; rinse in cold water to remove scum and scales.
  2. Simmer the daikon in the water for 15 minutes until nearly tender.
  3. Add the fish, ginger, soy sauce, mirin, sake and sugar.
  4. Simmer, partly covered, for 20 minutes, spooning the sauce over, until glazed.
  5. Serve with a little of the sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the blanching leaves a fishy sauce.
  • Boiling hard breaks up the fish.

Authentic tips

  • Fatty yellowtail collar is the classic cut.
  • Leftovers taste better the next day.

Substitutions

  • Salmon collars or mackerel replace yellowtail.
  • Add ginger for a cleaner flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; the daikon improves.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Miso soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot sencha
