Yóu Bào Dà Xiā (Shandong Oil-Burst Prawns) 油爆大虾

▾

Surprise me with

Shandong's bao technique flash-fries prawns in hot oil and finishes with a quick sauce; you bao da xia is a classic of Jiaodong coastal cooking.

  • 700 g1.5 lbLarge prawns, shell on
  • 40 g3 tbspGinger, garlic and scallion
  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 15 ml1 tbspChinkiang vinegar
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 10 g2 tspSugar
  • 200 ml3/4 cupNeutral oil, for frying

Method

  1. Snip the legs and whiskers, slit the backs to devein, and pat the prawns completely dry.
  2. Heat the oil to 200 C (390 F) in a wok until almost smoking.
  3. Flash-fry the prawns in two batches for 30-40 seconds until bright red; drain.
  4. Pour off all but 1 tablespoon of oil and fry the ginger, garlic and scallion 15 seconds.
  5. Add the wine, vinegar, soy sauce and sugar, return the prawns and toss over high heat for 20 seconds.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking longer than a minute makes the prawns tough.
  • Adding the sauce too early steams them.

Authentic tips

  • The shell should be crisp and red.
  • Wet prawns spatter badly; dry them well.

Substitutions

  • Large shrimp replace prawns.
  • Shell-on prawns give more flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; eat while hot.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
