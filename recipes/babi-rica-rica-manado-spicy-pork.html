Babi Rica-Rica (Manado Spicy Pork)

Rica-rica, a fiery Manado chili and lemongrass sauté, is a North Sulawesi specialty used for pork, chicken and fish.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 40 min
  • Difficulty Medium
  • Draft Indonesia Pork LunchDinnerBraise / stewSpicy

  • 600 g1.3 lbPork shoulder, cubed
  • 60 g2 ozChilies
  • 80 g3 ozShallot and garlic
  • 60 g2 ozLemongrass, ginger
  • 150 g5 ozTomato
  • 30 g1 ozLime leaves and basil
  • 30 ml2 tbspLime juice
  • 6 g1 tspSalt
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Blend the chilies, shallot, garlic, ginger and lemongrass to a coarse paste.
  2. Fry the paste in the oil for 5 minutes until fragrant and the oil separates.
  3. Add the pork and cook, stirring, 8 minutes.
  4. Add the tomato, salt and 100 ml water, cover and simmer 25 minutes until tender.
  5. Fold in the lime leaves, basil and lime juice and serve.

Common mistakes

  • Under-frying the paste leaves it raw and sharp.
  • Adding the basil too early blackens it.

Authentic tips

  • Fiery by tradition; reduce the chilies if needed.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • Chicken replaces pork for a halal version.
  • Reduce the chilies for a milder dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Es jeruk
