Babi Rica-Rica (Manado Spicy Pork)

▾

Surprise me with

Rica-rica, a fiery Manado chili and lemongrass sauté, is a North Sulawesi specialty used for pork, chicken and fish.

Method

  1. Rub the pork with lime juice and a pinch of salt and leave for 10 minutes.
  2. Coarsely grind the chilies, shallots, garlic and ginger.
  3. Heat the oil in a flat-bottom wok or Dutch oven over medium heat and fry the spice paste (bumbu) with the lemongrass, lime leaves and pandan, stirring often, for 6 to 8 minutes, until it darkens, smells cooked rather than raw and the oil starts to separate. Under-fried bumbu tastes sharp and grassy; this step is the base of Indonesian cooking.
  4. Add the pork and stir-fry for 5 minutes until it browns, then add the tomatoes, water, salt and sugar. Cover and simmer over low heat for 40 to 50 minutes, until the pork is tender and the sauce thick.
  5. Turn off the heat, stir in the basil and lime juice and serve with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Under-frying the paste leaves it raw and sharp.
  • Adding the basil too early blackens it.

Authentic tips

  • Fiery by tradition; reduce the chilies if needed.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • Chicken replaces pork for a halal version.
  • Reduce the chilies for a milder dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.
