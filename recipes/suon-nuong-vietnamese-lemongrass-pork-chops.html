Suon Nuong (Vietnamese Lemongrass Pork Chops)

A staple of Vietnamese com tam (broken rice) plates, suon nuong grills pork chops in a lemongrass-honey marinade until caramelized and smoky — a dish found at nearly every com tam stall in southern Vietnam.

  • Prep 20 min (plus 2 hr marinating)
  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Pound the lemongrass, garlic and shallot to a paste and mix with the fish sauce, honey, soy sauce and oyster sauce.
  2. Pound the chops lightly to an even thickness and coat in the marinade; refrigerate at least 2 hours.
  3. Grill over medium heat for 12-15 minutes, turning occasionally, until caramelized and cooked through.
  4. Rest 3 minutes.
  5. Serve with rice, cucumber and fish sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the honey on high heat.
  • Skipping the marinating time leaves the chops bland.

Authentic tips

  • Thin chops cook evenly and char well.
  • Serve over broken rice for com tam.

Substitutions

  • Chicken thighs work well with the same marinade and method.
  • No grill: broil on a rack about 10 cm (4 in) from the element for 5 to 6 minutes per side, or roast at 220°C (425°F) for 15 to 18 minutes, until caramelized and cooked through.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes well, marinated and raw, for up to a month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice, cucumber and fish sauce
  • Soup: Canh
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
