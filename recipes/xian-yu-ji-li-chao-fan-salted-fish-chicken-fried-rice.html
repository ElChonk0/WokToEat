Xián Yú Jī Lì Chǎo Fàn (Salted Fish and Chicken Fried Rice) 咸鱼鸡粒炒饭

▾

Surprise me with

Fried rice with diced chicken and salted fish is a Hong Kong restaurant staple; a little pungent salted fish seasons the whole wok.

  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 1 g1/2 tspGround white pepper

Method

  1. Mix the chicken with the soy sauce and cornstarch. Break up the rice with your hands so there are no clumps.
  2. Heat 1 tablespoon of oil in a flat-bottom wok over medium heat and fry the salted fish for 2 minutes until crisp and golden, then lift it out. Stir-fry the chicken in the same oil for 3 minutes until cooked, and set aside.
  3. Add the remaining oil, turn the heat to high, pour in the eggs and stir once; before they set add the rice and toss for 3 to 4 minutes until every grain is separate and hot.
  4. Return the fish and chicken with the ginger, salt and pepper and toss for 1 minute.
  5. Add the lettuce and green onion, toss for 30 seconds until just wilted, and serve.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Too much salted fish overwhelms the dish; it is a seasoning.
  • Warm, fresh rice clumps and turns mushy.

Authentic tips

  • Lettuce added at the end gives the crunch Hong Kong diners expect.
  • Salted fish keeps for months refrigerated; fry a batch and store it in oil.

Substitutions

  • Anchovy fillets or a spoon of fish sauce stand in for salted fish.
  • Use diced char siu instead of chicken.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Garlic gai lan
  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Mango sago
  • Beverage: Iced lemon tea
