Xián Yú Jī Lì Chǎo Fàn (Salted Fish and Chicken Fried Rice) 咸鱼鸡粒炒饭

▾

Surprise me with

Fried rice with diced chicken and salted fish is a Hong Kong restaurant staple; a little pungent salted fish seasons the whole wok.

Method

  1. Break up the rice with wet hands so no clumps remain. Mix the chicken with the oyster sauce, pepper, cornstarch and water.
  2. Trim the fins from the salted fish, rinse it and pat dry, then pick out every bone; even small ones are unpleasant in fried rice. Cut it into thin strips and then into pea-size dice. Salted fish is very salty, so use only as much as you like; the rice should taste of it, not be overwhelmed.
  3. Heat a flat-bottom wok over high heat until it just smokes, add 1 teaspoon of oil and scramble the eggs for 20 to 30 seconds into soft curds. Lift them out.
  4. Add 1 tablespoon of oil, lower the heat to medium-high and stir-fry the chicken for about 1 minute, until white outside and nearly cooked. Lift it out.
  5. Add the remaining oil around the edge of the wok and fry the ginger and salted fish for about 1 minute over medium heat, until the fish is golden and fragrant. Add the rice, raise the heat to high and stir-fry for 2 minutes, pressing out any clumps, until every grain is hot and coated in the fish oil.
  6. Return the eggs and chicken and toss for 1 minute, breaking the egg into small pieces, then add the green onion and white pepper for 30 seconds. Taste before adding the fish sauce, because the salted fish varies, then toss it in.
  7. Turn off the heat, fold in the lettuce so it just wilts but stays crisp, and serve at once.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Too much salted fish overwhelms the dish; it is a seasoning.
  • Warm, fresh rice clumps and turns mushy.

Authentic tips

  • Lettuce added at the end gives the crunch Hong Kong diners expect.
  • Salted fish keeps for months refrigerated; fry a batch and store it in oil.

Substitutions

  • Anchovy fillets or a spoon of fish sauce stand in for salted fish.
  • Use diced char siu instead of chicken.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Garlic gai lan
  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Mango sago
