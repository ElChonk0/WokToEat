Huī Shì Zhēng Jī (Hui-Style Steamed Chicken) 徽式蒸鸡

▾

Steaming is a common Hui technique that preserves the tenderness of chicken and the flavor of local mushrooms and ham.

  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine

  • 5 g1 tspSalt

Method

  1. Soak the shiitake in warm water for 30 minutes; squeeze and slice.
  2. Toss the chicken with the wine, soy sauce, salt and half the ginger; marinate 20 minutes.
  3. Arrange the chicken on a heatproof plate or bowl with the mushrooms and ham on top.
  4. Steam over high heat for 30 minutes until the chicken is cooked through.
  5. Pour off the juices into a small pan, simmer 2 minutes and pour over; garnish with scallion.

Common mistakes

  • Oversteaming dries the chicken.
  • Too much ham makes the dish salty.

Authentic tips

  • Use the rich juices as the sauce.
  • Bone-in thighs stay juiciest.

Substitutions

  • Prosciutto or country ham replaces Jinhua ham.
  • Boneless thighs steam in 20 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; re-steam for 8 minutes.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
