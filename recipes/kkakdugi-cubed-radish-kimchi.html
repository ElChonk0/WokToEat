Kkakdugi (Cubed Radish Kimchi) 깍두기

▾

Surprise me with

Kkakdugi, crunchy cubes of Korean radish in chili paste, is the kimchi served with seolleongtang and gukbap soups. This version is vegetarian.

  • 1.5 kg3.3 lbKorean radish (mu) or daikon, cut into 2 cm cubes
  • 30 g2 tbspCoarse sea salt
  • 30 g2 tbspSugar
  • 40 g1/2 cupGochugaru (Korean chili flakes)
  • 6 cloves6 clovesGarlic, minced
  • 10 g2 tspGinger, minced
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 4 stalks4 stalksGreen onion, chopped

Method

  1. Scrub the radish, keep the skin on and cut it into 2 cm (3/4 in) cubes.
  2. Toss the cubes with the salt and sugar and leave them for about 1 hour, turning once or twice, until slightly softened and sitting in their own liquid.
  3. Drain off the liquid but do not rinse the radish; rinsing washes away seasoning and leaves it bland and watery.
  4. Wearing gloves, add the gochugaru and rub it into the radish until every cube is red. Add the garlic, ginger and soy sauce, taste (it should be slightly saltier than you want to eat) and fold in the green onion.
  5. Pack the kkakdugi tightly into a clean jar, pressing out air pockets, and leave it at room temperature for 1 to 2 days (2 to 3 in cold weather), until it smells tangy and bubbles appear. Then refrigerate; it is best after one to two weeks.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Rinsing the salted radish washes away flavor; just drain it.
  • Cutting cubes unevenly makes some go soft while others stay raw.

Authentic tips

  • Add 30 ml of fish sauce and a spoon of salted shrimp in place of the soy sauce for the traditional version.
  • Korean radish is denser and sweeter than daikon; look for it at Korean grocers.

Substitutions

  • Daikon works; salt it 10 minutes less.
  • Use less gochugaru for a milder kimchi.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 month in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: With seolleongtang or gukbap
  • Soup: Seolleongtang
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
