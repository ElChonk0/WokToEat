Ba Gong Shan Dou Fu Tang (Bagong Mountain Tofu Soup) 八公山豆腐汤

Tofu is said to have been invented in Huainan, at Bagong Mountain, by Liu An, a Han-dynasty prince; the town's tofu is still prized for its smooth texture.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 500 g1.1 lbSilken tofu
  • 30 g1 ozDried shiitake
  • 60 g2 ozBamboo shoots
  • 800 ml3.5 cupsVegetable stock
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 1 g1/4 tspWhite pepper
  • 20 g2 tbspScallion

Method

  1. Soak the shiitake in warm water for 30 minutes; slice and keep the liquid.
  2. Simmer the stock with the mushrooms, mushroom liquid and bamboo shoots for 8 minutes.
  3. Slide in the tofu in large pieces and heat gently for 3 minutes without boiling.
  4. Season with the soy sauce and pepper and garnish with scallion.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling the tofu makes it spongy.
  • Over-seasoning hides the delicate tofu.

Authentic tips

  • Keep the broth light so the tofu's flavor shines.
  • Slide the tofu in gently.

Substitutions

  • Soft tofu replaces silken tofu.
  • Add napa cabbage.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
