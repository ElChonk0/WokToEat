Bā Gōng Shān Dòu Fu Tāng (Bagong Mountain Tofu Soup) 八公山豆腐汤

▾

Surprise me with

Tofu is said to have been invented in Huainan, at Bagong Mountain, by Liu An, a Han-dynasty prince; the town's tofu is still prized for its smooth texture.

Method

  1. Bagong Mountain near Huainan is said to be where tofu was first made, by Liu An, prince of Huainan, in the 2nd century BCE. Buy the freshest tofu you can.
  2. Blanch the tofu in salted water for 1 minute to firm it and remove the beany taste. Drain gently.
  3. Bring the stock to a boil with the shiitake and bamboo shoots and simmer for 5 minutes.
  4. Slide in the tofu and simmer gently for 5 minutes. Season with soy sauce, salt and white pepper, and thicken lightly with the slurry if you like.
  5. Finish with sesame oil and green onion.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling the tofu makes it spongy.
  • Over-seasoning hides the delicate tofu.

Authentic tips

  • Keep the broth light so the tofu's flavor shines.
  • Slide the tofu in gently.

Substitutions

  • Soft tofu replaces silken tofu.
  • Add napa cabbage.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
