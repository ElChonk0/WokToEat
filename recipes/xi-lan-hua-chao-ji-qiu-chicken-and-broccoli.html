Xi Lan Hua Chao Ji Qiu (Chicken and Broccoli Stir-Fry) 西兰花炒鸡球

Chicken and broccoli in a light oyster-garlic sauce is a Cantonese restaurant staple and the model for the takeout dish of the same name.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 5 ml1 tspSesame oil

Method

  1. Marinate the chicken with the soy sauce, wine, cornstarch and a teaspoon of oil for 15 minutes. Mix the oyster sauce, sugar, stock and cornstarch.
  2. Blanch the broccoli in boiling salted water with a teaspoon of oil for 1.5 minutes, then drain.
  3. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a flat-bottom wok over high heat and stir-fry the chicken 3 to 4 minutes until just cooked. Lift out.
  4. Add the remaining oil and fry the garlic and ginger 20 seconds, then add the broccoli and toss 1 minute.
  5. Return the chicken, stir in the sauce until glossy and finish with sesame oil.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Stir-frying raw broccoli takes too long and burns the garlic; blanch it first.
  • A thick, gloopy sauce; it should just coat.

Authentic tips

  • A little oil in the blanching water keeps the broccoli glossy green.
  • Peel and slice the stems; they are as good as the florets.

Substitutions

  • Gai lan replaces broccoli.
  • Beef flank or shrimp replace chicken.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Almond jelly
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
