Ānhuī Niú Ròu Miàn (Anhui Beef Noodles) 安徽牛肉面

▾

Surprise me with

Beef noodles in a savory, lightly spiced broth are a staple of northern Anhui, shaped by the province's Hui communities.

  • Cook 2.5 hr

  • 60 g2 ozGinger and green onion
  • 10 g1/3 ozStar anise, Sichuan pepper and cinnamon

  • 150 g5 ozBok choy
  • 40 g1.5 ozChili oil, cilantro and green onion

Method

  1. Blanch the beef for 5 minutes and rinse.
  2. Fry the chili paste in the oil in a Dutch oven for 1 minute, then add the beef, spices, ginger and green onion and stir for 2 minutes.
  3. Add the water and soy sauces, bring to a boil, skim and simmer, covered, for 2 hours until the beef is tender. Season the broth with salt.
  4. Cook the noodles and blanch the bok choy.
  5. Divide among bowls, add sliced beef and ladle over the broth. Finish with chili oil, cilantro and green onion.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the blanch clouds the broth.
  • Cooking the noodles in the broth makes it starchy.

Authentic tips

  • Hand-pulled noodles are traditional.
  • Add a spoonful of vinegar at the table.

Substitutions

  • Brisket replaces shank.
  • Ready-made stock saves an hour.

Storage

Leftovers: Store noodles and broth separately; the broth keeps 4 days.

Freezing: Freeze the broth and beef for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled vegetables
  • Soup: Not needed; the noodle soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
