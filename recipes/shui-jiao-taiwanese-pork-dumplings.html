Shuǐ Jiǎo (Taiwanese Pork and Cabbage Dumplings) 水餃

▾

Surprise me with

Boiled dumplings are a staple of Taiwan's mainland-descended northern communities and a common breakfast and snack.

  • Cook 8 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 4040Dumpling wrappers
  • 400 g14 ozPork, ground (about 30% fat)
  • 250 g9 ozCabbage, minced
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt, for the cabbage
  • 10 g2 tspGinger, grated
  • 20 g3/4 ozGreen onion, minced
  • 20 ml4 tspLight soy sauce
  • 10 ml2 tspRice wine
  • 1 g1/2 tspWhite pepper
  • 3 g1/2 tspSugar
  • 60 ml1/4 cupScallion-ginger water
  • 15 ml1 tbspSesame oil
  • 60 ml1/4 cupSoy sauce, black vinegar, garlic and chili oil, for dipping

Method

  1. Toss the cabbage with the salt and leave for 15 minutes, then squeeze it dry.
  2. Mix the pork with the ginger, soy sauce, rice wine, pepper and sugar. Add the scallion-ginger water a little at a time, stirring in one direction, until the filling absorbs it and becomes springy. Fold in the cabbage, green onion and sesame oil.
  3. Put a heaping teaspoon of filling in the center of a wrapper, wet the edge, fold it in half and pleat or pinch to seal tightly.
  4. Bring a large pot of water to a boil and add 12 to 15 dumplings. When it returns to a boil, add 250 ml (1 cup) of cold water; repeat twice. The dumplings are done when they float and the wrappers look slightly translucent, about 6 to 7 minutes.
  5. Lift them out with a slotted spoon and serve with soy sauce, black vinegar, garlic and chili oil for dipping.

Common mistakes

  • Unsqueezed cabbage makes a watery filling.
  • Overfilling splits the wrappers.

Authentic tips

  • Freeze uncooked dumplings on a tray.
  • Dip in soy, vinegar and chili oil.

Substitutions

  • Ground chicken replaces some pork.
  • Chives replace cabbage for a stronger flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; pan-fry leftovers.

Freezing: Freeze uncooked on a tray for 2 months; boil from frozen.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Hot and sour soup
  • Soup: Soy milk
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Oolong tea
