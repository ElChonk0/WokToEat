Yú Piàn Zhōu (Fish Slice Congee) 鱼片粥

▾

Surprise me with

Fish slice congee is a Cantonese breakfast: thin slices of raw fish are cooked by ladling boiling congee over them in the bowl.

  • Cook 80 min

  • 150 g3/4 cupJasmine rice
  • 1.8 L7 1/2 cupsWater
  • 20 g3/4 ozGinger, cut in fine strips
  • 6 g1 tspSalt
  • 400 g14 ozHaddock fillet, sliced 4 mm thick
  • 5 g2 tspCornstarch
  • 5 ml1 tspSesame oil
  • 1 g1/4 tspWhite pepper
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 30 g1 ozScallions, finely sliced
  • 10 g1/3 ozCilantro
  • 40 g1.5 ozFried youtiao or fried shallots, to serve
  • 22Century egg, peeled and chopped (optional)

Method

  1. Rinse the rice, drain, and stir in 1 teaspoon of oil if you like; combine with the water, half the ginger and the salt in a pot.
  2. Bring to a boil over high heat, then simmer on low, partly covered and stirring from the bottom every 15 minutes, for 60 to 70 minutes until the rice has broken down into a thick, creamy congee.
  3. Toss the fish slices with the cornstarch, sesame oil, white pepper and soy sauce and divide among 4 bowls with the remaining ginger.
  4. Bring the congee to a hard boil and ladle it over the fish so it cooks in the bowl, about 1 minute.
  5. Top with the scallions, cilantro and youtiao and serve at once.

Cook’s notes

Written for haddock fillet. Cantonese cooks use grass carp or dace; any mild white fish works.

Common mistakes

  • Congee that is not boiling hard leaves the fish raw in the middle.
  • Cutting the slices too thick means they cook unevenly.

Authentic tips

  • Freeze the fish for 20 minutes to slice it thin.
  • Century egg, chopped and stirred in for the last 10 minutes, is the other classic Cantonese pairing (pei daan yu pin juk).

Substitutions

  • Cod, tilapia or any firm white fish replaces the haddock.
  • Leftover cooked rice and extra water make a quicker version in 20 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Congee keeps 2 days in the refrigerator without the fish; thin with water when reheating.

Freezing: Freeze plain congee for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Fried dough sticks
  • Soup: The congee is the soup
  • Dessert: Soy milk
  • Beverage: Hot Chinese tea
