Là Chǎo Gé Lí (Hunan Spicy Stir-Fried Clams) 辣炒蛤蜊

▾

Surprise me with

Clams stir-fried with chilies, black beans and perilla are a quick Hunan restaurant classic, showing the province's preference for pairing seafood with fermented seasonings and fresh heat.

  • Cook 8 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 1.2 kg2.6 lbClams, purged and cleaned
  • 80 g3 ozFresh red and green chilies, sliced
  • 15 g1 tbspFermented black beans
  • 40 g3 tbspGarlic and ginger
  • 15 g1/2 ozPerilla leaves, torn
  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Soak the clams in salted water for 1 hour to purge their sand, then scrub and drain.
  2. Heat the oil in a flat-bottom wok over high heat and fry the ginger, garlic and black beans for 30 seconds, then the chilies for 30 seconds.
  3. Add the clams and Shaoxing wine, cover and cook for 3 to 4 minutes, shaking the wok, until the shells open. Discard any that stay closed.
  4. Add the soy sauce and toss for 30 seconds so the juices mix into a sauce.
  5. Toss in the perilla, which gives Hunan clam dishes their fragrance, and serve at once.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the soak leaves grit in the sauce.
  • Overcooking the clams makes them rubbery.

Authentic tips

  • Salt the soaking water to mimic seawater so the clams spit their sand.
  • Mussels work equally well.

Substitutions

  • Mussels replace the clams.
  • Thai basil replaces perilla.

Storage

Leftovers: Eat immediately.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Light vegetable soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
