Masak Lemak Labu (Pumpkin in Turmeric Coconut Curry)

▾

Surprise me with

Masak lemak, vegetables cooked in coconut milk, turmeric and chilies, is a Malay home dish especially associated with Negeri Sembilan; pumpkin makes a sweet, mild version.

  • Cook 20 min

  • 500 g1.1 lbPumpkin, cubed
  • 300 ml1.25 cupsCoconut milk
  • 3 g1 tspTurmeric
  • 15 g1/2 ozBird's eye chilies
  • 40 g3 tbspShallot and garlic
  • 20 g2/3 ozLemongrass
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt
  • 100 g3.5 ozLong beans

Method

  1. Pound the chilies, shallot, garlic and turmeric.
  2. Simmer the paste with the lemongrass, pumpkin and 200 ml water for 10 minutes.
  3. Add the coconut milk and long beans.
  4. Simmer gently for 6 minutes until the pumpkin is just soft.
  5. Season with the salt.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard splits the coconut milk.
  • Overcooked pumpkin turns to mush.

Authentic tips

  • Add dried anchovies for the traditional version.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • Butternut squash replaces pumpkin.
  • Dried anchovies are traditional.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and sambal
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
