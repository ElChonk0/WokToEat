Masak Lemak Labu (Pumpkin in Turmeric Coconut Curry)

▾

Surprise me with

Masak lemak, vegetables cooked in coconut milk, turmeric and chilies, is a Malay home dish especially associated with Negeri Sembilan; pumpkin makes a sweet, mild version.

Method

  1. Pound or blend the shallots, garlic, turmeric and chilies into a coarse paste.
  2. Heat the oil in a pot over medium heat and fry the paste with the lemongrass for 2 to 3 minutes until fragrant but not browned; masak lemak stays golden and fresh-tasting.
  3. Add the water and pumpkin, bring to a boil and simmer, covered, for 8 to 10 minutes, until the pumpkin is just tender.
  4. Stir in the coconut milk, lime leaves and long beans and simmer gently for 5 minutes, until the beans are tender and some pumpkin has melted into the sauce. Keep it at a gentle simmer so the coconut milk does not split.
  5. Season with the salt and sugar and serve with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard splits the coconut milk.
  • Overcooked pumpkin turns to mush.

Authentic tips

  • Add dried anchovies for the traditional version.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • Butternut squash replaces pumpkin.
  • Dried anchovies are traditional.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.
