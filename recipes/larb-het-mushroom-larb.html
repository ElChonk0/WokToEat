Larb Het (Isaan Mushroom Larb)

▾

Surprise me with

Larb, a chilled herby minced meat salad, is a national dish of Laos and Isaan; mushrooms make a lively vegetarian version dressed with toasted rice powder.

Method

  1. Toast the sticky rice in a dry skillet over medium-low heat for 10 to 15 minutes until deep golden, then grind coarsely.
  2. Heat the oil in a large skillet over high heat and cook the mushrooms in two batches for 4 to 5 minutes each, without stirring at first, until they release their water and brown. Browning gives them a meaty savoriness.
  3. Take the pan off the heat and season with soy sauce, lime juice, chili flakes and sugar. It should be sour, salty and hot.
  4. Toss in the shallots, green onion, mint, cilantro and most of the rice powder.
  5. Sprinkle with the remaining rice powder and serve with raw vegetables and sticky rice.

Common mistakes

  • Wet mushrooms make a soggy larb.
  • Adding herbs while hot wilts them.

Authentic tips

  • Mix several mushroom varieties.
  • Eaten with sticky rice.

Substitutions

  • Tofu crumbles add protein.
  • Toast and grind your own sticky rice powder.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sticky rice and raw vegetables
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
