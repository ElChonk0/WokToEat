Larb Het (Isaan Mushroom Larb)

Larb, a chilled herby minced meat salad, is a national dish of Laos and Isaan; mushrooms make a lively vegetarian version dressed with toasted rice powder.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 500 g1.1 lbMixed mushrooms, chopped
  • 15 g1 tbspToasted rice powder
  • 60 ml4 tbspLime juice
  • 30 ml2 tbspSoy sauce
  • 100 g3.5 ozShallot, mint, cilantro
  • 5 g2 tspChili flakes
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 15 ml1 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Chop the mushrooms coarsely.
  2. Dry-fry the mushrooms in a wok with the oil over high heat for 6 minutes until browned and their water has evaporated.
  3. Toss with the lime juice, soy sauce, chili and sugar.
  4. Stir in the toasted rice powder.
  5. Fold in the shallot, mint and cilantro and serve with lettuce.

Common mistakes

  • Wet mushrooms make a soggy larb.
  • Adding herbs while hot wilts them.

Authentic tips

  • Mix several mushroom varieties.
  • Eaten with sticky rice.

Substitutions

  • Tofu crumbles add protein.
  • Toast and grind your own sticky rice powder.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sticky rice and raw vegetables
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
