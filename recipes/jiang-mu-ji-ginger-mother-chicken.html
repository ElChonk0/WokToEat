Jiāng Mǔ Jī (Ginger Mother Chicken) 姜母鸡

▾

Surprise me with

Jiang mu, mature ginger, is stewed in rice wine and sesame oil with chicken in Minnan and Taiwanese cooking as a warming winter dish.

  • Cook 40 min

Method

  1. Heat the sesame oil in a Dutch oven or heavy pot over medium heat and fry the ginger for 4-5 minutes until the edges brown and the oil smells strongly of ginger.
  2. Add the chicken and sear 5 minutes until the skin is golden.
  3. Add the wine, water, mushrooms and salt and bring to a boil.
  4. Cover and simmer gently for 30 minutes until the chicken is tender.
  5. Serve in the pot with the broth.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the ginger turns the whole dish bitter; stir often.
  • Using too much wine makes it overpowering; keep to the amounts given.

Authentic tips

  • Use mature ginger, which is spicier and more fibrous than young ginger.
  • Traditionally eaten in winter and by new mothers.

Substitutions

  • Add extra rice wine for a more traditional tonic.
  • Boneless thighs shorten the simmer by 10 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Rice vermicelli or steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Warm ginger tea
