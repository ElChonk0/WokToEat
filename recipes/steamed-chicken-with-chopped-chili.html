Steamed Chicken with Chopped Chili 剁椒蒸鸡

▾

Surprise me with

A vivid, fiery Hunan steaming technique that piles a thick layer of fermented red and green chopped chilies over the protein, letting the steam carry the chili's aroma directly into the meat.

Method

  1. Toss the chicken with the soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, cornstarch and a tablespoon of water and spread it in a shallow heatproof dish.
  2. Mix the red and green chopped chilies (duo jiao) with the garlic and ginger and spoon them evenly over the top. Duo jiao, salted and fermented chopped chilies, are Hunan's signature condiment and season the dish by themselves.
  3. Steam over boiling water in a flat-bottom wok, covered, for 12 to 15 minutes, until the chicken is cooked through and the juices run clear.
  4. Drizzle with the sesame oil and scatter with green onion.
  5. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil until smoking and pour it over the chilies so they sizzle. Serve with rice to soak up the juices.

Common mistakes

  • Steaming at too low a heat, which extends cooking time and leaves the chicken less tender.
  • Using only red chili and skipping the green, which loses the visual and flavor contrast the dish is known for.

Authentic tips

  • Slicing the chicken thin ensures fast, even cooking under the piled-on chili topping.
  • Both red and green duo jiao are usually sold together at Chinese grocers specifically for this dish.

Substitutions

  • Fresh minced red and green chilies with a splash of rice vinegar approximate duo jiao.
  • Chicken breast works but dries quickly; steam 3 minutes less.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat by re-steaming for 6 minutes.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Plain steamed rice to soak up the chili oil
  • Soup: Winter melon soup
  • Dessert: Dragon fruit
  • Beverage: Iced green tea
