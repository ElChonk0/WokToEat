Yáo Zhù Dàn Bái Chǎo Fàn (Dried Scallop and Egg White Fried Rice) 瑶柱蛋白炒饭

▾

Surprise me with

Fried rice with shredded dried scallops and egg white is a Hong Kong banquet and restaurant favorite, prized for its delicate color and the savory depth of conpoy.

  • Cook 12 min

  • 800 g5 cupsCooked jasmine rice, cold
  • 30 g1 ozDried scallops (conpoy)
  • 44Egg whites
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 15 g1/2 ozGinger, minced
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt
  • 2 g1/2 tspSugar
  • 3 ml1/2 tspFish sauce
  • 0.5 g1/4 tspGround white pepper
  • 5 ml1 tspSesame oil
  • 100 g3.5 ozLettuce, shredded (optional)
  • 3 stalks3 stalksGreen onion, chopped

Method

  1. Soak the scallops in hot water for at least 20 minutes until soft, then drain and shred them finely with your fingers. Break up the rice with wet hands so no clumps remain.
  2. Heat a flat-bottom wok over high heat until nearly smoking, add 2 tablespoons of oil and lower to medium. Add the egg whites and stir gently for about 15 seconds until just set in soft white curds, then lift them out.
  3. Add the remaining oil over medium-high heat and fry half the scallop shreds for 50 to 60 seconds until golden and crisp. Lift them out to drain; they become the crunchy topping.
  4. Lower to medium, add the ginger and the rest of the scallops for 15 seconds, then add the rice. Press and toss for about 1.5 minutes until every grain is separate and hot.
  5. Season with the salt, sugar, fish sauce and pepper, return the egg whites, breaking them up, and stir-fry for 1.5 minutes. Add the lettuce and sesame oil for 30 seconds, then the green onion for 10 seconds.
  6. Pile into bowls and scatter the crisp fried scallops on top.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Adding soy sauce defeats the dish; it should stay pale.
  • Burning the scallops makes them bitter; fry gently.

Authentic tips

  • Save the scallop soaking water and sprinkle it on the rice for more flavor.
  • Use the leftover yolks for steamed egg.

Substitutions

  • Crab meat replaces dried scallops.
  • Whole eggs if you do not want to separate them.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Garlic pea shoots
  • Soup: Winter melon seafood soup
  • Dessert: Mango sago
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
