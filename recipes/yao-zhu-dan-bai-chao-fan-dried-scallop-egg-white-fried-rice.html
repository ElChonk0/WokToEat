Yáo Zhù Dàn Bái Chǎo Fàn (Dried Scallop and Egg White Fried Rice) 瑶柱蛋白炒饭

Fried rice with shredded dried scallops and egg white is a Hong Kong banquet and restaurant favorite, prized for its delicate color and the savory depth of conpoy.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Cantonese Seafood LunchDinnerStir-fryRice30 min or lessShellfishEgg

  • 800 g5 cupsCooked jasmine rice, cold
  • 40 g1.5 ozDried scallops (conpoy), soaked and shredded
  • 55Egg whites
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 10 g2 tspGinger, minced
  • 4 g3/4 tspSalt
  • 1 g1/2 tspGround white pepper
  • 100 g3.5 ozLettuce, shredded
  • 3 stalks3 stalksGreen onion, chopped

Method

  1. Fry the shredded scallops in 1 tablespoon of oil over medium-low heat for 3 to 4 minutes until golden and crisp. Lift out.
  2. Beat the egg whites with a pinch of salt.
  3. Heat the remaining oil in a flat-bottom wok over high heat, add the rice and ginger and toss 2 minutes until hot.
  4. Pour the egg whites over the rice and toss quickly so they coat the grains in white flecks; add the salt and pepper.
  5. Toss in half the scallops, the lettuce and green onion, and serve topped with the rest of the scallops.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Adding soy sauce defeats the dish; it should stay pale.
  • Burning the scallops makes them bitter; fry gently.

Authentic tips

  • Save the scallop soaking water and sprinkle it on the rice for more flavor.
  • Use the leftover yolks for steamed egg.

Substitutions

  • Crab meat replaces dried scallops.
  • Whole eggs if you do not want to separate them.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Garlic pea shoots
  • Soup: Winter melon seafood soup
  • Dessert: Mango sago
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
