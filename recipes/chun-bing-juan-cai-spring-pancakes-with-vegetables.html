Chūn Bǐng Juǎn Cài (Spring Pancakes with Vegetables) 春饼卷菜

▾

Surprise me with

Chun bing, thin wheat pancakes eaten on the first day of spring, are traditionally served with stir-fried vegetables, eggs and sauces in Beijing.

  • Difficulty Medium

  • 1212Thin wheat pancakes (chun bing)
  • 44Eggs, beaten
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 200 g7 ozPotato, finely julienned and rinsed
  • 150 g5 ozBean sprouts
  • 60 g2 ozGarlic chives, cut into lengths
  • 50 g2 ozGlass noodles, soaked (optional)
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 10 ml2 tspRice vinegar
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt
  • 45 g3 tbspSweet bean sauce
  • 150 g5 ozCucumber and green onion, julienned

Method

  1. Steam the pancakes for 5 minutes until soft and warm, and keep them covered.
  2. Scramble the eggs in 1 tablespoon of oil until just set and lift them out in large pieces.
  3. Stir-fry the potato in the remaining oil over high heat for 2 minutes until translucent but crisp, then the bean sprouts, chives and glass noodles for 1 minute. Season with soy sauce, vinegar and salt, then toss in the eggs.
  4. Fry the sweet bean sauce in a little oil for 1 minute to make it glossy.
  5. Spread a pancake with sweet bean sauce, add the vegetables, cucumber and green onion, and roll up. Eaten on the first day of spring (li chun), 'biting the spring'.

Common mistakes

  • Cooking all the vegetables together turns them to mush.
  • Dry pancakes crack; keep them wrapped.

Authentic tips

  • Roll like a burrito, tucking in the ends.
  • Pancakes keep well frozen.

Substitutions

  • Store-bought thin pancakes (spring roll wrappers) replace homemade ones.
  • Add braised tofu for protein.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; steam pancakes to soften.

Freezing: Freeze the pancakes alone for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: The platter of fillings is the meal
  • Soup: Millet porridge
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot jasmine tea
