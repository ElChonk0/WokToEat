Zhà Lì Huáng (Fried Oysters) 炸蛎黄

▾

Surprise me with

Zha li huang is a Shandong coastal dish of oysters fried in a light batter and eaten dipped in pepper salt.

Method

  1. Pat the oysters very dry with paper towels.
  2. Whisk the flour, egg, cornstarch and 90 ml cold water into a thin batter.
  3. Heat the oil to 180 C (350 F) and dip the oysters in the batter in small batches.
  4. Fry for 1-2 minutes until golden; drain on a rack.
  5. Serve immediately with pepper salt and lemon wedges.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking makes the oysters rubbery and dry.
  • Wet oysters spatter and dilute the batter.

Authentic tips

  • Fry in small batches to keep the oil hot.
  • Cold batter gives a crisper coating.

Substitutions

  • Clams or scallops take the same batter.
  • Bake at 230 C (450 F) for a lighter version.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; eat within minutes.

Freezing: Not recommended.
