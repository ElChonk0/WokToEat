Yakisoba (Japanese Stir-Fried Noodles) 焼きそば

▾

Surprise me with

Yakisoba, steamed wheat noodles stir-fried with pork and cabbage in a fruity Worcestershire-style sauce, spread across Japan after the war and is a festival-stall staple.

Method

  1. Mix the Worcestershire sauce, oyster sauce, ketchup, soy sauce and sugar.
  2. Loosen the noodles under warm water and drain.
  3. Heat the oil in a large skillet or flat-bottom wok over high heat and fry the pork 3 minutes until browned, then the onion, carrot and cabbage for 3 minutes.
  4. Add the noodles and 2 tablespoons of water and toss 2 minutes, then the sprouts and sauce and toss until evenly coated.
  5. Serve topped with aonori and pickled ginger.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Too much sauce makes the noodles soggy.
  • Overcrowding a small pan stews everything.

Authentic tips

  • Store-bought yakisoba sauce is fine; Otafuku is a popular brand.
  • Top with a fried egg for a fuller meal.

Substitutions

  • Ramen noodles, boiled 1 minute, replace yakisoba noodles.
  • Chicken or shrimp replace pork.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled ginger
  • Soup: Miso soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold barley tea
