Sù Fàn Tuán (Taiwanese Sticky Rice Roll) 素飯糰

▾

Surprise me with

Fan tuan, sticky rice wrapped around pickles, egg and fried dough, is a Taiwanese commuter breakfast.

  • Cook 20 min

  • 300 g10.5 ozSteamed sticky rice

  • 30 g1 ozVegetarian floss or sesame
  • 15 ml1 tbspSoy sauce
  • 5 ml1 tspSesame oil

Method

  1. Steam the glutinous rice until sticky and soft; keep warm.
  2. Spread the warm rice 1 cm thick on a damp cloth or plastic wrap.
  3. Add the pickles, omelet, floss and broken youtiao in a line down the center.
  4. Roll tightly using the cloth and press firmly into an oval.
  5. Slice and serve warm with the soy sauce and sesame oil.

Common mistakes

  • Cold rice won't stick; roll while warm.
  • Overstuffing makes it fall apart.

Authentic tips

  • Add sesame and peanut sugar for a sweet version.
  • Wrap in paper for a portable breakfast.

Substitutions

  • Tofu floss replaces pork floss.
  • Add pickled mustard greens for crunch.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day wrapped.

Freezing: Freezes wrapped for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Soy milk
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot soy milk
