Sù Fàn Tuán (Taiwanese Sticky Rice Roll) 素飯糰

▾

Surprise me with

Fan tuan, sticky rice wrapped around pickles, egg and fried dough, is a Taiwanese commuter breakfast.

  • Prep 15 min (plus 4 hr soaking)
  • Cook 35 min

  • 15 ml1 tbspSoy sauce
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 5 ml1 tspSesame oil

Method

  1. Drain the soaked glutinous rice and steam it on a cloth-lined steamer for 30 to 35 minutes, until translucent and chewy. Toss it with the soy sauce, sugar and sesame oil while hot.
  2. Beat the eggs and cook a thin omelet in the oil, then cut it into strips.
  3. Dry-fry the pickled radish and mustard greens in a skillet for 2 to 3 minutes each until fragrant; this drives off moisture so the roll stays firm.
  4. Lay a sheet of plastic wrap on a towel, spread a quarter of the warm rice into a rectangle, and arrange the youtiao, egg, pickles and floss down the center.
  5. Use the towel to fold the rice around the filling and squeeze it into a tight oval roll. Wrap in the plastic and eat warm; a Taipei breakfast classic.

Common mistakes

  • Cold rice won't stick; roll while warm.
  • Overstuffing makes it fall apart.

Authentic tips

  • Add sesame and peanut sugar for a sweet version.
  • Wrap in paper for a portable breakfast.

Substitutions

  • Tofu floss replaces pork floss.
  • Add pickled mustard greens for crunch.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day wrapped.

Freezing: Freezes wrapped for 1 month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Soy milk
  • Soup: Not needed
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot soy milk
