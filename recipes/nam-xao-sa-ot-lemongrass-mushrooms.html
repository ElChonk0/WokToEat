Nấm Xào Sả Ớt (Lemongrass Chili Mushrooms)

▾

Surprise me with

Mushrooms stir-fried with lemongrass and chili are a favorite across Vietnamese vegetarian kitchens, drawing on the country's love of herbs.

  • 500 g1.1 lbMixed mushrooms (king oyster, oyster, shiitake), torn or sliced
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil
  • 3 stalks (60 g)3 stalks (2 oz)Lemongrass, finely minced
  • 4 cloves4 clovesGarlic, minced
  • 33Bird's eye chilies, minced
  • 30 ml2 tbspSoy sauce

  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 1 g1/2 tspBlack pepper
  • 20 g3/4 ozGreen onion and cilantro

Method

  1. Tear the mushrooms into bite-size pieces and pat dry.
  2. Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in a flat-bottom wok over high heat and cook the mushrooms for 4 to 5 minutes, without stirring at first, until they brown and release their water. Lift them out.
  3. Add the remaining oil and fry the lemongrass over medium heat for 3 minutes until golden and crisp, then the garlic and chilies for 30 seconds.
  4. Return the mushrooms with the soy sauce, mushroom sauce, sugar and pepper and toss over high heat for 1 to 2 minutes until glazed.
  5. Finish with green onion and cilantro and serve with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Crowding the pan steams the mushrooms.
  • Burning the lemongrass.

Authentic tips

  • Brown the mushrooms well.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • King oyster mushrooms have a meaty texture.
  • Tofu cubes join the mushrooms.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Canh
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
