Nấm Xào Sả Ớt (Lemongrass Chili Mushrooms)

▾

Surprise me with

Mushrooms stir-fried with lemongrass and chili are a favorite across Vietnamese vegetarian kitchens, drawing on the country's love of herbs.

  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 500 g1.1 lbMixed mushrooms
  • 40 g1.5 ozLemongrass, minced
  • 15 g1/2 ozChilies
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 30 ml2 tbspSoy sauce
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Mince the lemongrass finely and slice the mushrooms.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok over high heat and fry the lemongrass, chilies and garlic 30 seconds.
  3. Add the mushrooms and cook 6 minutes until browned.
  4. Add the soy sauce and sugar.
  5. Toss and serve.

Common mistakes

  • Crowding the pan steams the mushrooms.
  • Burning the lemongrass.

Authentic tips

  • Brown the mushrooms well.
  • Serve with rice.

Substitutions

  • King oyster mushrooms have a meaty texture.
  • Tofu cubes join the mushrooms.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Canh
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
