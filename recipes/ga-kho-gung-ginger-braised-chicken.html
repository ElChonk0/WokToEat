Gà Kho Gừng (Caramel Ginger Chicken)

▾

Surprise me with

Gà kho is the chicken counterpart of thịt kho, braised in a dark caramel and fish sauce; the ginger-heavy gà kho gừng is the most common home version in Vietnam.

Method

  1. Melt the sugar in a heavy pot over medium heat, swirling, until deep amber, about 4 minutes.
  2. Add the chicken carefully (it will spit) and turn it in the caramel for 2 minutes, then add the fish sauce, ginger, garlic, shallot and pepper.
  3. Pour in the coconut water, add the eggs if using, bring to a simmer, cover and braise 20 minutes.
  4. Uncover and simmer 5 to 10 minutes more, spooning the sauce over the chicken, until it reduces to a glossy glaze.
  5. Garnish with green onion and chili and serve with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Letting the caramel burn, which makes the whole dish bitter.
  • Skimping on the ginger; this version should be assertively ginger-forward.

Authentic tips

  • Kho dishes are traditionally made in a clay pot; a heavy Dutch oven braises just as gently on gas, electric and induction.
  • Julienned rather than minced ginger lets you taste distinct pieces, and the dish is even better reheated.

Substitutions

  • Leave out the ginger and use 4 hard-boiled eggs for plain gà kho.
  • Pork belly can replace or join the chicken for a version close to thịt kho.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; the flavor deepens.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and boiled greens
  • Soup: Canh (clear vegetable soup)
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced jasmine tea
