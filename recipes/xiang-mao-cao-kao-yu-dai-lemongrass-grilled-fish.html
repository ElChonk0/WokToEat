Xiāng Máo Cǎo Kǎo Yú (Dai Lemongrass Grilled Fish) 香茅草烤鱼

Dai cooks in Xishuangbanna stuff whole fish with lemongrass, herbs and chili, then grill it over charcoal inside a bamboo clamp.

  • Prep 20 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Medium
  • Draft Yunnan Seafood LunchDinnerGrilled / roastedSpicy

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 1 kg2.2 lbWhole tilapia or carp, cleaned
  • 60 g2 ozLemongrass
  • 50 g3 tbspGarlic and shallot
  • 30 g1 ozFresh chilies
  • 30 g1 ozCilantro and mint
  • 8 g1.5 tspSalt
  • 22Lime
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Pound the lemongrass, garlic, shallot, chilies, herbs and salt to a coarse paste.
  2. Score the fish on both sides and stuff the paste into the cavity and slashes.
  3. Brush the skin with the oil.
  4. Roast at 220 C (425 F) or grill 18-20 minutes, turning once, until the flesh flakes at the thickest part.
  5. Serve with lime wedges.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the scoring gives uneven cooking.
  • Overcooking dries the fish.

Authentic tips

  • Dai cooks grill it in a bamboo clamp over charcoal.
  • Tilapia or carp both work.

Substitutions

  • Whole trout replaces tilapia.
  • Oven-roast at 220 C (425 F) for 20 minutes if no grill.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Sticky rice
  • Soup: Sour bamboo shoot soup
  • Dessert: Fresh pineapple
  • Beverage: Chilled tea
