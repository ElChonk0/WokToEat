Myeolchi Bokkeum (Sweet Stir-Fried Anchovies) 멸치볶음

▾

Myeolchi bokkeum, tiny dried anchovies toasted and glazed in sweet soy with nuts, is one of the most loved Korean banchan and a lunchbox staple.

  • Cook 10 min

  • 5 g2 tspToasted sesame seeds

Method

  1. Toast the anchovies in a dry skillet over medium-low heat for 3 minutes, shaking, then tip into a sieve and shake off the dust.
  2. Heat the oil in the skillet and fry the garlic 30 seconds.
  3. Add the anchovies and nuts and stir-fry 1 minute.
  4. Turn off the heat, add the soy sauce, syrup and sugar and toss until coated; the residual heat makes the glaze.
  5. Stir in the sesame oil and seeds and spread out to cool so they stay separate and crisp.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Adding syrup over high heat makes it harden into a lump.
  • Skipping the dry toast leaves a fishy smell.

Authentic tips

  • Store in a jar; it keeps for two weeks.
  • Add a little gochujang for a spicy version.

Substitutions

  • Walnuts replace peanuts.
  • Larger anchovies, heads and guts removed, for a chewier version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 weeks in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Rice and other banchan
  • Soup: Doenjang jjigae
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Barley tea
