Pesang Isda (Filipino Ginger Fish Soup)

▾

Surprise me with

Pesa is a Filipino soup of fish simmered in a clear, ginger-heavy broth, often eaten when someone is unwell.

Method

  1. Bring the water, ginger, onion, tomato and peppercorns to a boil in a pot, then simmer 8 minutes.
  2. Add the fish sauce and the cod, return to a gentle simmer, and cook 6 to 8 minutes without stirring.
  3. Add the cabbage and chilies for 3 minutes until tender.
  4. Taste, adjust with fish sauce, and serve with scallions and rice.

Cook’s notes

Written for cod loin.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard clouds the soup and breaks the fish.
  • Too little ginger makes the broth flat.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with a dipping bowl of fish sauce and calamansi.
  • Use plenty of ginger; it is the main flavor.

Substitutions

  • Haddock replaces the cod.
  • Bok choy replaces the napa cabbage.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: The recipe is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Calamansi juice
