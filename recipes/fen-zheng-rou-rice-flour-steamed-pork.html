Fěn Zhēng Ròu (Rice-Flour Steamed Pork) 粉蒸肉

▾

Surprise me with

Fen zheng rou, pork belly coated in spiced rice flour and steamed until melting, is a Sichuan banquet classic served on a bed of sweet potato.

  • Cook 1.5 hr

Ingredients

  • 450 g1 lbPork belly, skinless, sliced 5 mm (1/4 in) thick
  • 90 g1/2 cupLong-grain rice, for the steaming rice flour
  • 3 g1 tspSichuan peppercorns and star anise, for the rice flour
  • 30 g2 tbspPixian doubanjiang
  • 15 g1 tbspFermented bean curd (red), mashed
  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 40 g1.5 ozGinger and garlic, minced
  • 60 ml1/4 cupWater
  • 300 g10 ozSweet potato, cubed
  • 20 g3/4 ozGreen onion, sliced

Method

  1. Toast the rice with the peppercorns and star anise in a dry skillet over medium-low heat for 10 to 12 minutes until golden, then grind it coarsely.
  2. Mix the pork with the doubanjiang, fermented bean curd, Shaoxing wine, soy sauce, sugar, ginger and garlic and marinate for 30 minutes.
  3. Add the rice flour and water and mix until each slice is coated in a moist layer.
  4. Line a heatproof bowl with the sweet potato and lay the pork slices on top, overlapping them.
  5. Steam over boiling water, covered, for 1 to 1.5 hours, topping up the water, until the pork is meltingly tender and the rice coating soft. The long steam renders the belly fat into the rice.
  6. Invert onto a plate, or serve from the bowl, scattered with green onion.

Common mistakes

  • Under-steaming leaves the belly chewy; this dish needs the full time.
  • Using lean pork gives a dry result; belly with the skin removed is traditional.

Authentic tips

  • The fat renders into the rice flour and sweet potato, so do not drain it off.
  • Cut the sweet potato into thick pieces so it does not turn to paste.

Substitutions

  • Pork ribs can replace the belly; steam 30 minutes longer.
  • Pumpkin replaces the sweet potato for a sweeter, softer base.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; re-steam for 15 minutes.

Freezing: Freezes well for 2 months; re-steam from frozen for 25 minutes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Stir-fried greens
  • Soup: Not needed; the steamed dish is rich
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
