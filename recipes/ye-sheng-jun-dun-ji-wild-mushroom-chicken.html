Ye Sheng Jun Dun Ji (Wild Mushroom Chicken Soup) 野生菌炖鸡

In Yunnan's rainy season, wild mushrooms are simmered with chicken for a deeply fragrant soup.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 50 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Yunnan Chicken LunchDinnerBraise / stewSoup

Method

  1. Cover the chicken with cold water, boil 3 minutes, then drain and rinse.
  2. Return to a clean pot with the ginger, wine and 1.2 L water and bring to a boil; skim.
  3. Cover and simmer gently for 30 minutes.
  4. Add the cleaned, torn mushrooms and simmer 15 minutes until tender (never serve wild mushrooms undercooked).
  5. Season with the salt and garnish with scallion.

Common mistakes

  • Undercooked wild mushrooms can cause stomach upset; cook them thoroughly.
  • A rolling boil makes the broth cloudy.

Authentic tips

  • Buy only mushrooms from a trusted source; never eat foraged mushrooms you cannot identify.
  • Cultivated mixed mushrooms work if wild ones are not available.

Substitutions

  • Cultivated mixed mushrooms replace wild ones.
  • Dried porcini add depth.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat gently.

Freezing: Freezes the broth for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Pu'er tea
