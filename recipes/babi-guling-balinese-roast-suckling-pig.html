Babi Guling (Balinese Spit-Roast Pork)

▾

Surprise me with

Babi guling, pork stuffed with a base genep spice paste and spit-roasted until crisp, is Bali's signature festive dish for Hindu ceremonies.

  • Cook 2 hr

  • 1.2 kg2.6 lbPork belly roast, boned
  • 150 g5 ozBase genep (turmeric, ginger, galangal, chilies)
  • 60 g2 ozLemongrass and kaffir lime
  • 100 g3.5 ozGarlic and shallot
  • 100 g3.5 ozCassava leaves or spinach
  • 15 g1 tbspSalt
  • 30 ml2 tbspCoconut oil

Method

  1. Pound the base genep spices, lemongrass, garlic, shallot, salt and coconut oil into a coarse paste.
  2. Score the skin of the pork belly, rub the paste into the meat side and spread the leaves over it.
  3. Roll tightly and tie with string at 3 cm intervals; air-dry uncovered in the refrigerator overnight for crisp skin.
  4. Roast at 190 C (375 F) for 2 hours on a rack, until the skin blisters and the meat is tender; finish at 230 C (450 F) for 10 minutes.
  5. Rest 15 minutes, slice and serve with rice, lawar and crackling.

Common mistakes

  • Wet skin does not crisp.
  • Too little salt on the skin gives chewy crackling.

Authentic tips

  • A real suckling pig is whole spit-roasted; belly gives the same flavors at home.
  • Serve with lawar (a coconut-vegetable salad).

Substitutions

  • Pork belly plus the spice paste is a home-friendly alternative.
  • Cassava leaves or spinach work for the stuffing.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; crisp the skin in a hot oven.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice, lawar and sambal matah
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Es teh or arak
