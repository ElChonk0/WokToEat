Yuringi (Crispy Chicken in Sweet Soy Chili Sauce) 유린기

▾

Surprise me with

Yuringi is a Korean-Chinese restaurant dish of fried chicken thigh over shredded lettuce with a tangy soy, vinegar and chili sauce, adapted from the Chinese youlinji (oil-drizzled chicken).

Method

  1. Pound the thighs to an even thickness of about 1.5 cm (1/2 in) and season with the salt, pepper and rice wine. Even pieces fry evenly and stay juicy.
  2. Stir the soy sauce, vinegar, sugar, water, lemon juice and sesame oil until the sugar dissolves, then add the green onion, chilies and garlic. Let the sauce stand while you fry so the aromatics flavor it.
  3. Coat the chicken in the beaten egg, then press it into the potato starch on both sides and shake off the excess.
  4. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven to 170 C (340 F). Fry the pieces one or two at a time for 5 to 6 minutes, turning once, until cooked through and lightly golden. Drain on a rack.
  5. Raise the oil to 185 C (365 F) and fry again for 1 minute until deep golden and shatteringly crisp.
  6. Pile the lettuce on a platter, slice the chicken into strips and lay it on top. Spoon the sauce over just before serving, so the crust is still crunchy when it reaches the table.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Saucing too early makes the crust soggy.
  • Skipping the refry leaves the chicken soft.

Authentic tips

  • The lettuce soaks up the sauce and is part of the dish.
  • Cheongyang chilies give the authentic sharp heat.

Substitutions

  • Air fryer: cook the coated chicken at 200 C (400 F) for 18 minutes.
  • Cornstarch replaces potato starch.

Storage

Leftovers: Store chicken and sauce separately for 2 days; re-crisp in the oven.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Jjamppong
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
