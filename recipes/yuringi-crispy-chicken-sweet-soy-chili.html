Yuringi (Crispy Chicken in Sweet Soy Chili Sauce) 유린기

Yuringi is a Korean-Chinese restaurant dish of fried chicken thigh over shredded lettuce with a tangy soy, vinegar and chili sauce, adapted from the Chinese youlinji (oil-drizzled chicken).

Method

  1. Flatten the chicken slightly and season with salt and pepper. Coat with the egg, then the potato starch.
  2. Heat the oil in a Dutch oven to 170 C (340 F) and fry the chicken 6 minutes, then rest and refry at 185 C (365 F) for 2 minutes until crisp.
  3. Mix the soy sauce, vinegar, sugar and water until the sugar dissolves, then add the green onion, chilies and garlic.
  4. Spread the lettuce on a platter, slice the chicken and lay it on top.
  5. Spoon the sauce over just before serving.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Aaron and Claire YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Saucing too early makes the crust soggy.
  • Skipping the refry leaves the chicken soft.

Authentic tips

  • The lettuce soaks up the sauce and is part of the dish.
  • Cheongyang chilies give the authentic sharp heat.

Substitutions

  • Air fryer: cook the coated chicken at 200 C (400 F) for 18 minutes.
  • Cornstarch replaces potato starch.

Storage

Leftovers: Store chicken and sauce separately for 2 days; re-crisp in the oven.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Jjamppong
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer
