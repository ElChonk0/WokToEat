Tau Yew Bak (Hokkien Soy-Braised Pork) 豆油肉

Tau yew bak, pork belly braised in dark soy sauce with garlic, is a Hokkien dish brought to Malaysia and Singapore, eaten with rice or in buns.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 90 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Malaysia / Singapore Pork LunchDinnerBraise / stew

  • 500 g1.1 lbPork belly
  • 45 ml3 tbspDark soy sauce
  • 30 ml2 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 30 g2 tbspRock sugar
  • 40 g1.5 ozGarlic
  • 22Star anise
  • 600 ml2.5 cupsWater

Method

  1. Cut the pork belly into 4 cm pieces and brown in a dry pot over medium heat for 8 minutes.
  2. Add the garlic and star anise and fry 1 minute.
  3. Add both soy sauces, the sugar and water, bring to a boil and cover.
  4. Simmer on low for 75 minutes until very tender.
  5. Uncover and reduce the sauce 5-8 minutes until glossy.

Common mistakes

  • Burning the sugar makes it bitter.
  • Boiling hard toughens the pork.

Authentic tips

  • Add boiled eggs for the last 20 minutes.
  • Serve with rice or in steamed buns.

Substitutions

  • Pork shoulder gives a leaner dish.
  • Hard-boiled eggs added for the last 20 minutes soak up the sauce.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or buns
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Chinese tea
