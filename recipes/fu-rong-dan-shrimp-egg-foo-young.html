Fú Róng Dàn (Shrimp Egg Foo Young) 芙蓉蛋

▾

Surprise me with

Fu yung egg, an omelet of egg with shrimp, bean sprouts and onion, is a Cantonese home dish that became the Chinese American "egg foo young" with brown gravy.

Method

  1. Crack the eggs into a bowl with the salt, sugar and pepper but do not beat them yet. Whisk the gravy oyster sauce, soy sauces, sugar, water and cornstarch together, and the egg cornstarch with its water in another cup.
  2. Bring a small pot of water to a boil and blanch the shrimp for 15 to 20 seconds, then add the bean sprouts for 10 seconds and the onion for 30 seconds. Drain well. Blanching takes the raw edge off the vegetables so they stay crunchy and release no water into the eggs.
  3. Heat a flat-bottom wok or skillet over high heat. Beat the eggs now and whisk in the cornstarch mixture, which helps the omelets hold together and stay fluffy. Add the oil and swirl, then mix the shrimp and vegetables into the eggs.
  4. Pour in a quarter of the mixture to make a round omelet, or all of it for one large one. Fry over high heat for about 1 minute without moving it until the bottom is golden, then lower the heat. Flip and fry the second side for 2 to 3 minutes, then flip again for 30 seconds. Keep warm and repeat.
  5. Wipe out the pan, pour in the gravy and stir over low heat until it boils and turns glossy, adding a splash of water if it gets too thick. Add the sesame oil.
  6. Pour the gravy over the omelets and scatter with the green onion.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by a dish featured on the Made With Lau YouTube channel.

Common mistakes

  • Too much filling makes the omelet fall apart; keep it mostly egg.
  • High heat burns the outside before the middle sets.

Authentic tips

  • Hong Kong home cooks often skip the gravy and eat it with soy sauce and rice.
  • Char siu is a classic alternative filling.

Substitutions

  • Crab meat or char siu replace shrimp.
  • Add shredded shiitake.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Egg drop soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Jasmine tea
