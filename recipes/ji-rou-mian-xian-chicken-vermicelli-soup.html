Jī Ròu Miàn Xiàn (Chicken Vermicelli Soup) 雞肉麵線

▾

Surprise me with

Mian xian, fine wheat vermicelli in a thick broth, is a Taiwanese breakfast and snack; the chicken version is lighter than the oyster-intestine classic.

  • Cook 25 min

Method

  1. Poach the chicken in the stock for 12 to 15 minutes until cooked, then lift it out and shred it.
  2. Add the bonito flakes to the stock, simmer for 2 minutes and strain. The bonito gives the broth its savory depth.
  3. Add the shiitake, bamboo shoots, garlic and fried shallots and simmer for 5 minutes. Season with soy sauce and salt.
  4. Rinse the vermicelli briefly and add it to the pot; it cooks in 2 to 3 minutes. Stir in the starch slurry gradually until the soup is thick and glossy.
  5. Return the chicken and ladle into bowls. Finish with black vinegar, cilantro, white pepper and chili garlic.

Common mistakes

  • Adding the slurry all at once makes lumps.
  • Overcooked vermicelli dissolves.

Authentic tips

  • The soup should be thick enough to coat the noodles.
  • Sweet potato starch gives the best texture.

Substitutions

  • Rice vermicelli replaces wheat vermicelli.
  • Chicken stock replaces bonito.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the vermicelli thickens as it sits, so add water when reheating.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled radish
  • Soup: Not needed; the soup is the meal
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Winter melon tea
