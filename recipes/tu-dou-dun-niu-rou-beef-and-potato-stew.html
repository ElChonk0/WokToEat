Tǔ Dòu Dùn Niú Ròu (Beef and Potato Stew) 土豆炖牛肉

▾

Surprise me with

Beef and potatoes stewed in soy sauce is a common northern home dish, warming and filling in the cold Beijing winter.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 90 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 800 ml3.5 cupsWater

Method

  1. Cut the brisket into 4 cm cubes, blanch 5 minutes in boiling water, then drain and rinse.
  2. In a heavy pot, melt the sugar over low heat until amber, add the beef and turn to coat.
  3. Add the ginger, scallion, garlic, star anise and soy sauce, then pour in 800 ml water and bring to a boil.
  4. Cover, reduce to a gentle simmer and cook 60 minutes.
  5. Add the potatoes in large chunks and cook 25 minutes until tender, uncovering for the last 10 to thicken the sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Cutting the potatoes small makes them dissolve into the sauce.
  • Boiling hard toughens the beef.

Authentic tips

  • Add the potatoes late so they hold their shape.
  • Serve over rice with the sauce.

Substitutions

  • Chuck replaces brisket with a shorter stew.
  • Carrots can join the potatoes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; the flavor improves.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months without the potatoes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; the stew is its own soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
