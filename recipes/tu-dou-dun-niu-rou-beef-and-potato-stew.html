Tǔ Dòu Dùn Niú Ròu (Beef and Potato Stew) 土豆炖牛肉

▾

Surprise me with

Beef and potatoes stewed in soy sauce is a common northern home dish, warming and filling in the cold Beijing winter.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 2.5 hr
  • Difficulty Easy

Method

  1. Blanch the beef for 5 minutes, drain and rinse.
  2. Heat the oil and rock sugar in a Dutch oven over low heat until it caramelizes, then turn the beef in it. Add the ginger, green onion, garlic and spices and stir for 1 minute.
  3. Add the Shaoxing wine, soy sauces, tomato and hot water. Bring to a boil, cover and simmer over low heat for 1.5 hours until the beef is nearly tender. Hot water, not cold, keeps the meat from seizing.
  4. Add the potatoes and simmer for 25 to 30 minutes until soft; some break down and thicken the sauce.
  5. Season with salt, reduce the sauce slightly and scatter with cilantro. A northern home-cooking staple.

Common mistakes

  • Cutting the potatoes small makes them dissolve into the sauce.
  • Boiling hard toughens the beef.

Authentic tips

  • Add the potatoes late so they hold their shape.
  • Serve over rice with the sauce.

Substitutions

  • Chuck replaces brisket with a shorter stew.
  • Carrots can join the potatoes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; the flavor improves.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months without the potatoes.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Not needed; the stew is its own soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
