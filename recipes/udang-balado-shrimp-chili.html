Udang Balado (Padang Chili Shrimp)

▾

Surprise me with

Balado, a chili-tomato sauce, is a West Sumatran (Minangkabau) specialty from Padang; udang balado coats shrimp in the red, fiery sauce.

  • Cook 12 min

  • 15 ml1 tbspLime juice, to finish

Method

  1. Toss the shrimp with lime juice and salt and leave for 10 minutes. Fry them in 2 tablespoons of oil in a flat-bottom wok over high heat for 1 to 2 minutes until just pink, and lift them out.
  2. Coarsely grind the chilies, shallots, garlic and tomatoes; balado should be chunky, not smooth.
  3. Add the remaining oil and fry the paste with the lime leaves over medium heat for 8 to 10 minutes, until the oil separates and the chilies turn glossy and deep red.
  4. Season with sugar, salt and lime juice.
  5. Return the shrimp and toss for 1 minute to coat. Serve with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the shrimp makes them tough.
  • Under-frying the paste leaves it raw.

Authentic tips

  • Leave the shells on for flavor.
  • Add green chilies for extra heat.

Substitutions

  • Squid replaces shrimp.
  • Reduce the chilies for a milder dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
