Udang Balado (Padang Chili Shrimp)

▾

Surprise me with

Balado, a chili-tomato sauce, is a West Sumatran (Minangkabau) specialty from Padang; udang balado coats shrimp in the red, fiery sauce.

  • Cook 12 min

  • 600 g1.3 lbShrimp, shell on
  • 80 g3 ozRed chilies
  • 150 g5 ozTomatoes
  • 60 g4 tbspShallot and garlic
  • 30 ml2 tbspLime juice
  • 10 g2 tspSugar
  • 5 g1 tspSalt
  • 60 ml4 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Blend the chilies, tomatoes, shallot and garlic to a coarse paste.
  2. Fry the paste in the oil over medium heat for 8 minutes until thick and the oil separates.
  3. Add the shrimp and cook 3 minutes until pink.
  4. Season with the lime juice, sugar and salt.
  5. Toss and serve with rice.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the shrimp makes them tough.
  • Under-frying the paste leaves it raw.

Authentic tips

  • Leave the shells on for flavor.
  • Add green chilies for extra heat.

Substitutions

  • Squid replaces shrimp.
  • Reduce the chilies for a milder dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
