Ròu Zào Miàn (Taiwanese Pork Sauce Noodles) 肉燥麵

▾

Surprise me with

Rou zao, a minced pork sauce braised with shallots and soy sauce, is a pillar of Taiwanese home cooking and is spooned over rice or noodles.

  • Cook 1 hr

Method

  1. Fry the shallots in the oil in a Dutch oven over medium-low heat for 8 minutes until golden. Lift them out.
  2. Add the pork and cook over medium heat for 5 to 6 minutes until it renders and browns, then the garlic for 30 seconds.
  3. Melt the rock sugar into the pork, then add the rice wine, soy sauces, five-spice, pepper, fried shallots and water.
  4. Simmer, uncovered, over low heat for 45 minutes, stirring occasionally, until the sauce is thick and glossy. This rou zao keeps a week in the refrigerator.
  5. Cook the noodles, drain and divide among bowls with a little hot stock. Top with a generous spoon of rou zao and the bean sprouts and chives.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard makes the sauce greasy.
  • Burning the sugar makes it bitter.

Authentic tips

  • Make a big batch; it freezes well.
  • Serve over rice for lu rou fan.

Substitutions

  • Ground pork replaces belly for a lighter sauce.
  • Serve over rice for lu rou fan.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; the sauce improves.

Freezing: Freezes the sauce well for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Blanched greens
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Oolong tea
