Ròu Zào Miàn (Taiwanese Pork Sauce Noodles) 肉燥麵

Rou zao, a minced pork sauce braised with shallots and soy sauce, is a pillar of Taiwanese home cooking and is spooned over rice or noodles.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 60 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft Taiwan Pork LunchDinnerBraise / stewNoodles

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 320 g11 ozWheat noodles, fresh
  • 250 g9 ozPork belly, minced
  • 40 g1.5 ozFried shallots
  • 60 ml4 tbspSoy sauce
  • 20 g2 tbspRock sugar
  • 45 ml3 tbspRice wine
  • 3 g1 tspFive-spice
  • 600 ml2.5 cupsWater

Method

  1. Fry the pork in a dry pot over medium heat for 6 minutes until browned.
  2. Add the shallots, soy sauce, sugar, wine and five-spice and stir 1 minute.
  3. Add the water, cover and simmer 45 minutes until thick.
  4. Boil the noodles 3 minutes and drain.
  5. Spoon over the sauce and serve with greens.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard makes the sauce greasy.
  • Burning the sugar makes it bitter.

Authentic tips

  • Make a big batch; it freezes well.
  • Serve over rice for lu rou fan.

Substitutions

  • Ground pork replaces belly for a lighter sauce.
  • Serve over rice for lu rou fan.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated; the sauce improves.

Freezing: Freezes the sauce well for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Blanched greens
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Oolong tea
