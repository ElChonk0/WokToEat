Bún Gà Nướng (Grilled Chicken Vermicelli Bowl)

▾

Surprise me with

A refreshing noodle salad bowl pairing grilled lemongrass chicken with rice vermicelli, fresh herbs, pickled vegetables, and nuoc cham — a common lunch dish designed to be light yet satisfying.

Method

  1. Pound or blend the lemongrass, garlic and shallots into a fine paste, then mix with the fish sauce, sugar and oil. Massage it into the chicken and marinate for at least 1 hour, or overnight. The sugar caramelizes over the heat into the smoky, sticky crust.
  2. Make the nuoc cham: stir the sugar into the warm water until dissolved, then add the fish sauce, lime juice, garlic and chili. Taste; it should be sweet, sour and salty in balance, and light enough to pour over noodles.
  3. Grill the chicken skin-side down over medium-high charcoal or in a grill pan for 6 to 7 minutes until charred and crisp, then turn and cook for 4 to 5 minutes until cooked through (74 C / 165 F). Rest for 5 minutes and slice.
  4. Fill bowls with lettuce, herbs, sprouts and cucumber, then the vermicelli, and top with the chicken and pickles.
  5. Drizzle with scallion oil, scatter with peanuts and serve with plenty of nuoc cham to pour over.

Common mistakes

  • Not rinsing the noodles after cooking, which leaves them starchy and prone to clumping.
  • Skimping on the nuoc cham — this dish depends on it for most of its seasoning, since the components themselves are fairly neutral.

Authentic tips

  • Make the nuoc cham ahead; it keeps well refrigerated for over a week and is useful for many other dishes.
  • A mix of fresh herbs, not just one, gives this bowl its characteristic bright, complex flavor.

Substitutions

  • Grilled shrimp or pork can replace or supplement chicken for variety.
  • No rice vermicelli: any thin rice noodle works as a substitute.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; store noodles, chicken, and sauce separately.

Freezing: Not recommended.
