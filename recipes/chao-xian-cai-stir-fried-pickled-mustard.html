Chǎo Xián Cài (Stir-Fried Pickled Mustard Greens) 炒咸菜

▾

Surprise me with

Pickled mustard greens, stir-fried with garlic and a little sugar, are a Minnan pantry staple served with plain congee at breakfast.

  • 300 g10 ozPickled mustard greens (xian cai), rinsed and chopped
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil
  • 20 g3/4 ozGarlic, minced
  • 15 g1/2 ozFresh chilies, sliced
  • 10 g2 tspSugar
  • 5 ml1 tspSesame oil

Method

  1. Rinse the pickled greens and soak them in fresh water for 10 minutes to reduce the salt, then squeeze dry.
  2. Dry-fry the greens in the wok over medium heat for 2 to 3 minutes until most of the moisture is gone; this concentrates their flavor.
  3. Push them aside, add the oil, garlic and chilies and fry for 20 seconds.
  4. Toss everything together with the sugar for 1 minute. The sugar balances the salty, sour pickle.
  5. Finish with sesame oil. Serve as a side with congee or rice.

Common mistakes

  • Not rinsing makes it far too salty.
  • Wet greens spatter and steam instead of frying.

Authentic tips

  • Squeeze very dry before frying.
  • Serve with plain rice or congee.

Substitutions

  • Sauerkraut rinsed well replaces pickled mustard.
  • Tofu strips make it heartier.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 5 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Congee or plain rice
  • Soup: Tofu soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
