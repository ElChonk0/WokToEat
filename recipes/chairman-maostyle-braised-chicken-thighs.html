Chairman Mao–Style Braised Chicken Thighs 毛家鸡

In the style of the famous Mao-inspired braised pork dish, this chicken version leans on dried chilies, fermented black beans, and a touch of sugar for a smoky, mildly sweet-spicy glaze typical of Hunanese home braises.

  • Cook 30 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Method

  1. Brown chicken pieces in oil over medium-high heat until golden; set aside.
  2. In the same pot, fry dried chilies, black beans, garlic, and ginger until fragrant.
  3. Return chicken to the pot; add both soy sauces, sugar, and wine, stirring to coat.
  4. Add stock, bring to a boil, then reduce to a simmer, cover, and braise 20 minutes.
  5. Uncover and simmer 5–10 more minutes to reduce the sauce to a glossy glaze.
  6. Serve hot over rice.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the initial browning step, which forfeits a good deal of flavor and color.
  • Letting the sauce reduce too far uncovered, which can turn it overly salty and sticky.

Authentic tips

  • This braise tastes even better the next day, once the flavors have had time to meld.
  • Rinsing the fermented black beans briefly softens their intensity without losing their signature umami.

Substitutions

  • Bone-in drumsticks work with a similar braising time.
  • Reduce the dried chilies for a milder, family-style version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated; the flavor deepens.

Freezing: Freezes well for up to 2 months; thaw overnight and reheat gently.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice with blanched greens
  • Soup: Tomato egg soup
  • Dessert: Sliced pear
  • Beverage: Hot green tea
