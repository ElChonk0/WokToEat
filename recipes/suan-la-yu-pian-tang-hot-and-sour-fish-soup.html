Suān Là Yú Piàn Tāng (Hot and Sour Fish Slice Soup) 酸辣鱼片汤

▾

Surprise me with

Suan la tang, hot and sour soup, is a Sichuan favorite thickened with starch and sharpened with black vinegar and white pepper; fish slices make a lighter version of the classic.

Method

  1. Toss the fish slices with the cornstarch and salt and set aside.
  2. Bring the stock to a boil, add the wood ear, bamboo shoots, tofu and soy sauce, and simmer 5 minutes.
  3. Stir the cornstarch with 80 ml cold water and pour it in, stirring, until the soup thickens slightly, about 1 minute.
  4. Reduce to a simmer, slide in the fish and cook 2 minutes without stirring until opaque.
  5. Drizzle in the egg in a thin stream, stir once, then add the vinegar, white pepper and sesame oil and serve with the scallions and cilantro.

Cook’s notes

Written for Icelandic cod fillet.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling the soup after adding the fish breaks it up.
  • Adding vinegar too early cooks off its sharpness.

Authentic tips

  • White pepper supplies the heat; add more to taste.
  • Add the vinegar off the heat for the best aroma.

Substitutions

  • Haddock or cod loin replaces the cod.
  • Mushrooms replace the wood ear.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day in the refrigerator; reheat gently.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or flower rolls
  • Soup: The recipe is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot jasmine tea
