Táinán Niú Ròu Tāng (Tainan Fresh Beef Soup) 台南牛肉湯

▾

Surprise me with

Tainan's fresh beef soup, thin slices of just-slaughtered beef poured over with scalding beef bone broth, is a unique Tainan breakfast and is eaten at dawn near the old slaughterhouses.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 500 g1.1 lbFresh beef, paper-thin slices
  • 1.2 L5 cupsBeef bone broth, boiling
  • 40 g3 tbspGinger, julienned
  • 40 g1.5 ozScallion and cilantro
  • 60 ml4 tbspGinger-soy dip
  • 1 g1/4 tspWhite pepper
  • 400 g14 ozCooked rice
  • 6 g1 tspSalt

Method

  1. Bring the broth with the salt to a rolling boil.
  2. Place the paper-thin raw beef slices in preheated bowls with the ginger.
  3. Pour the scalding broth over; the beef cooks in 15-20 seconds.
  4. Add the scallion, cilantro and white pepper.
  5. Serve with the ginger-soy dip and a bowl of rice.

Common mistakes

  • Lukewarm broth leaves the beef raw.
  • Thick slices stay raw inside; cut paper-thin.

Authentic tips

  • Freeze the beef for 30 minutes to make it easier to slice thin.
  • Use very fresh beef.

Substitutions

  • Very thin beef from a good butcher replaces just-slaughtered beef.
  • Beef bones plus ginger make the broth in 2 hours.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; the broth keeps 4 days, but slice and cook beef fresh.

Freezing: Freeze the broth for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice with soy-ginger dip
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Oolong tea
