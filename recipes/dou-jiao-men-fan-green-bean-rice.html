Dòu Jiǎo Chǎo Mǐ Fàn (Xinjiang Green Bean Rice) 豆角焖饭

▾

Surprise me with

Green bean rice, rice steamed in the pot with beans, potato and spices, is a one-pot meal of the Hui and Han communities of Xinjiang and northwest China.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 30 min
  • Difficulty Easy

Ingredients

4servings (recipe serves 4)

  • 300 g1.5 cupsRice
  • 300 g10 ozGreen beans
  • 200 g7 ozPotatoes
  • 100 g3.5 ozOnion and garlic
  • 3 g1 tspCumin
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 450 ml1.75 cupsWater
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Rinse the rice and trim and cut the green beans into 4 cm lengths; dice the potatoes.
  2. Fry the onion and garlic in the oil 2 minutes, then the beans and potatoes 5 minutes.
  3. Add the rice, soy sauce and cumin and stir 1 minute.
  4. Add the water, bring to a boil, cover and cook on low 20 minutes.
  5. Rest 10 minutes and fluff.

Common mistakes

  • Raw green beans stay tough if cut too thick.
  • Lifting the lid lets steam escape.

Authentic tips

  • Add cured pork for a non-vegetarian version.
  • A crust at the bottom is prized.

Substitutions

  • Add cured meat for non-vegetarians.
  • Brown rice needs more water and time.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated; reheat with a splash of water.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled vegetables
  • Soup: Clear soup
  • Dessert: Hami melon
  • Beverage: Black tea
