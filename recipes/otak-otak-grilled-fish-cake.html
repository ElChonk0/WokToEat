Otak-Otak (Grilled Fish Cake in Banana Leaf)

Otak-otak, spiced fish paste wrapped in banana leaf and grilled, is a snack along Indonesia's coasts, from Palembang to Java.

  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Medium

Method

  1. Pound the shallot, garlic, turmeric and chilies into a paste.
  2. Mix with the ground fish, coconut milk, tapioca starch, egg and salt until sticky.
  3. Soften the banana leaves in boiling water for 30 seconds or briefly in a hot dry skillet, then spoon the mixture onto each; fold into flat packets and secure with toothpicks.
  4. Grill over medium heat for 12-15 minutes, turning, until the leaves char and the fish sets.
  5. Serve with peanut sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Too wet a mixture won't hold in the packet.
  • Cooking on high heat burns the leaves.

Authentic tips

  • Wrap tightly so it doesn't leak.
  • Steaming first prevents drying.

Substitutions

  • Foil replaces banana leaves.
  • Steaming first keeps it moist.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat under a broiler.

Freezing: Freezes uncooked for 2 months.
