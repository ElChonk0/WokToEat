Otak-Otak (Grilled Fish Cake in Banana Leaf)

▾

Surprise me with

Otak-otak, spiced fish paste wrapped in banana leaf and grilled, is a snack along Indonesia's coasts, from Palembang to Java.

Method

  1. Blend the shallots, garlic, chilies, turmeric and lemongrass into a fine paste.
  2. Mix the fish with the paste, coconut milk, egg, salt and sugar, then the tapioca starch and lime leaves, beating with a spoon for 2 to 3 minutes until smooth and slightly elastic. Beating develops the fish proteins so the cakes are springy.
  3. Soften the banana leaves by passing them over a flame or dipping them in hot water so they fold without cracking.
  4. Spread 2 tablespoons of the mixture along the center of each leaf, fold the long sides over and secure the ends with toothpicks or staples.
  5. Grill over medium charcoal or in a dry grill pan for 6 to 8 minutes, turning once, until the leaves are charred and the filling is firm.
  6. Unwrap at the table and eat with peanut or chili sauce.

Common mistakes

  • Too wet a mixture won't hold in the packet.
  • Cooking on high heat burns the leaves.

Authentic tips

  • Wrap tightly so it doesn't leak.
  • Steaming first prevents drying.

Substitutions

  • Foil replaces banana leaves.
  • Steaming first keeps it moist.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat under a broiler.

Freezing: Freezes uncooked for 2 months.
