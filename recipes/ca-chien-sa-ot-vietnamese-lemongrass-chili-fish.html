Cá Chiên Sả Ớt (Lemongrass-Chili Fried Fish)

▾

Surprise me with

Fried fish topped with fragrant lemongrass and chili is a common southern Vietnamese home dish eaten with rice and herbs.

  • Cook 15 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 600 g1 lb 5 ozHaddock fillet, in 4 pieces
  • 3 g1/2 tspSalt
  • 2 g1 tspTurmeric
  • 30 g1/4 cupCornstarch
  • 100 ml1/3 cupNeutral oil
  • 60 g2 ozLemongrass, finely minced
  • 4 cloves4 clovesGarlic, minced
  • 20 g2 tbspRed chilies, minced
  • 20 ml4 tspFish sauce
  • 10 g2 tspSugar
  • 11Lime wedges

Method

  1. Season the haddock with the salt and turmeric, rest 10 minutes, pat dry and dust with the cornstarch.
  2. Heat half the oil in a non-stick pan over medium-high heat and fry the fish 3 to 4 minutes per side until golden; transfer to a plate.
  3. Wipe the pan, add the remaining oil, and fry the lemongrass, garlic and chilies over medium heat for 2 minutes until fragrant and lightly golden.
  4. Add the fish sauce and sugar and stir 20 seconds.
  5. Spoon the topping over the fish and serve with lime and rice.

Cook’s notes

Written for haddock fillet.

Common mistakes

  • Haddock is delicate, so avoid turning it more than once.
  • Burnt lemongrass turns bitter; stop when it is golden.

Authentic tips

  • Use only the tender lower stalk of the lemongrass.
  • A non-stick pan prevents the fillet from sticking.

Substitutions

  • Cod loin replaces the haddock.
  • Rainbow trout fillets also work.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day in the refrigerator.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice and cucumber
  • Soup: Canh chua
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
