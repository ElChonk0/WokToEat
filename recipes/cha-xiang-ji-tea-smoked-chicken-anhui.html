Chá Xiāng Jī (Anhui Tea-Scented Chicken) 茶香鸡

▾

Surprise me with

Anhui is a major tea region, and Huangshan Maofeng and Qimen black tea scent chicken dishes in the cooking of the province's tea villages.

Method

  1. Simmer the chicken in the brewed tea with the soy sauces, rock sugar, ginger, green onion, star anise, Shaoxing wine and salt, covered, for 25 minutes until cooked. Leave it in the liquid for 15 minutes. Anhui grows some of China's most famous teas, including Huangshan Maofeng and Keemun.
  2. Lift the chicken out and pat dry.
  3. Line a flat-bottom wok and lid with foil, scatter the tea leaves and brown sugar in the bottom and set a rack above. Put the chicken on the rack.
  4. Cover tightly, heat until smoking, then smoke over medium heat for 8 to 10 minutes. Leave covered off the heat for 5 minutes.
  5. Brush with sesame oil, chop and serve warm or cold.

Common mistakes

  • Over-steeped tea turns the sauce bitter; brew 5 minutes and strain.
  • Too much tea overpowers the chicken.

Authentic tips

  • Black tea gives a deeper, sweeter flavor than green tea.
  • Serve with steamed rice.

Substitutions

  • Black tea gives a deeper flavor than green tea.
  • Bone-in drumsticks add flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot Huangshan Maofeng tea
