Cha Xiang Ji (Anhui Tea-Scented Chicken) 茶香鸡

Anhui is a major tea region, and Huangshan Maofeng and Qimen black tea scent chicken dishes in the cooking of the province's tea villages.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 45 min
  • Difficulty Medium

  • 45 ml3 tbspSoy sauce
  • 15 g1 tbspRock sugar
  • 40 g3 tbspGinger, scallion
  • 22Star anise
  • 30 ml2 tbspShaoxing wine

Method

  1. Brew the tea strongly in 300 ml boiling water for 5 minutes and strain; keep the liquid.
  2. Brown the chicken in a dry heavy pot over medium-high heat 8 minutes until the skin is golden.
  3. Add the ginger, scallion, star anise, soy sauce, sugar and wine and stir 2 minutes.
  4. Pour in the tea, cover and simmer 30 minutes until tender.
  5. Uncover and reduce the sauce for 5 minutes until glossy.

Common mistakes

  • Over-steeped tea turns the sauce bitter; brew 5 minutes and strain.
  • Too much tea overpowers the chicken.

Authentic tips

  • Black tea gives a deeper, sweeter flavor than green tea.
  • Serve with steamed rice.

Substitutions

  • Black tea gives a deeper flavor than green tea.
  • Bone-in drumsticks add flavor.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 4 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes for 2 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Stir-fried greens
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot Huangshan Maofeng tea
