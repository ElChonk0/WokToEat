Daegutang (Spicy Cod Stew) 대구탕

Daegutang is a Korean winter stew of cod simmered with radish and tofu in a clear or chili-red broth; daegu (cod) is closely associated with the southern coast around Geoje.

Method

  1. Simmer the anchovies and kombu in the water over medium heat for 10 minutes, removing the kombu after 5, then strain the stock into a wide pot.
  2. Add the radish, gochugaru, garlic, ginger and soup soy sauce and simmer for 10 minutes until the radish turns translucent.
  3. Salt the cod lightly and rest for 5 minutes, then slide it into the broth with the tofu and simmer gently for 6 to 8 minutes, skimming any foam, until the cod is opaque and flakes at the thickest point.
  4. Add the scallions and chili for 1 minute, then the crown daisy for 30 seconds until just wilted.
  5. Taste, adjust with salt or a splash more soup soy sauce, and serve in the pot with rice.

Cook’s notes

Written for cod loin; Pacific cod is the traditional fish and Atlantic cod loin is a close match.

Common mistakes

  • A hard boil breaks the cod apart and clouds the broth; keep it at a gentle simmer.
  • Adding the fish before the radish has softened overcooks it; build the broth first.

Authentic tips

  • Use cod loin, the thick center cut, because it stays in large flakes.
  • The stew tastes better once the radish has sweetened the broth, so do not shorten step 2.

Substitutions

  • Pollock or haddock replaces the cod.
  • Omit the gochugaru and add extra garlic and salt for the clear, mild version.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 1 day in the refrigerator; reheat gently without boiling.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Kimchi and steamed rice
  • Soup: The stew is the soup
  • Dessert: Sliced pear
  • Beverage: Barley tea (boricha)
