Chǎo Sǔn (Fujian Stir-Fried Bamboo Shoots) 炒鲜笋

▾

Surprise me with

Fujian's mountains, including the Wuyi range, are famous for bamboo, and fresh shoots are stir-fried simply with garlic and a touch of soy sauce.

  • Prep 10 min
  • Cook 12 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 500 g1.1 lbFresh bamboo shoots, sliced
  • 20 g1 tbspGarlic
  • 15 ml1 tbspLight soy sauce
  • 15 ml1 tbspShaoxing wine
  • 5 g1 tspSugar
  • 10 g2 tspChili (optional)
  • 30 ml2 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Slice the fresh bamboo shoots and boil in water for 8 minutes to remove bitterness; drain.
  2. Heat the oil in a wok over high heat and fry the garlic and chili for 15 seconds.
  3. Add the shoots, soy sauce, wine and sugar and toss 3-4 minutes until they are tender-crisp and lightly browned.

Common mistakes

  • Skipping the blanching leaves the shoots bitter.
  • Overcooking makes them tough and stringy.

Authentic tips

  • Fresh shoots need blanching; canned ones just a quick rinse.
  • Slice thinly and evenly.

Substitutions

  • Canned bamboo shoots work; rinse and blanch them.
  • Add mushrooms for a heartier dish.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Tofu soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Hot tea
