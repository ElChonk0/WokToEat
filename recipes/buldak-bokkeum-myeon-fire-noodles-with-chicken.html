Buldak Bokkeum Myeon (Fire Noodles with Chicken) 불닭볶음면

▾

Surprise me with

Popularized globally through instant noodle versions, buldak bokkeum myeon tosses stir-fried noodles in the same fiery gochujang-gochugaru sauce as classic buldak, here made from scratch with fresh noodles and chicken for a heartier, less processed version.

Method

  1. Whisk gochujang, gochugaru, soy sauce, brown sugar, garlic, and sesame oil into a thick sauce.
  2. Cook noodles according to package directions until just shy of fully tender; drain, reserving a little cooking water.
  3. Stir-fry chicken in a hot pan with a little oil until browned and cooked through, 6–7 minutes.
  4. Add the sauce to the pan and toss with the chicken until well coated and fragrant, 1–2 minutes.
  5. Add the noodles, tossing everything together over high heat, loosening with a splash of reserved noodle water if the sauce is too thick to coat evenly.
  6. Garnish with green onion, sesame seeds, and nori strips before serving.

Common mistakes

  • Overcooking the noodles before stir-frying, since they'll continue cooking briefly once tossed with the hot sauce and chicken.
  • Making the sauce too thick without a splash of noodle water to loosen it, which leaves it clumping rather than coating evenly.

Authentic tips

  • A splash of the starchy noodle cooking water helps the sauce cling evenly to every strand — don't skip reserving some.
  • This dish is genuinely very spicy; start with less gochugaru if serving a heat-sensitive crowd.

Substitutions

  • Regular ramen noodles work well if fresh wheat noodles aren't available.
  • Tofu or shrimp can replace chicken for a different protein.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; the noodles soften on reheating.

Freezing: Not recommended — noodle texture doesn't survive freezing.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Pickled radish
  • Soup: Not needed; the noodles are the meal
  • Dessert: Cold milk or fruit
  • Beverage: Cold barley tea
