Kerala Meen Moilee (Fish Molee)

▾

Surprise me with

Meen moilee, fish in a mild coconut milk stew with ginger, green chili and curry leaves, is a Kerala specialty associated with the Syrian Christian and Portuguese communities.

Method

  1. Rub the fish with half the turmeric, the pepper, a pinch of salt and the lime juice and leave for 15 minutes. Sear it in 1 tablespoon of the coconut oil for 1 minute per side, just to firm it. Lift it out.
  2. Heat the remaining oil, splutter the mustard seeds, then cook the onion, ginger, garlic, green chilies and curry leaves for 5 minutes until soft but not browned; moilee is a pale, mild curry.
  3. Add the remaining turmeric and the thin coconut milk and salt and simmer for 3 minutes.
  4. Lay in the fish and the tomato slices, spoon the sauce over and simmer gently for 5 to 6 minutes until the fish is cooked.
  5. Pour in the thick coconut milk and heat for 1 to 2 minutes without boiling. Serve with appam or rice.

Common mistakes

  • Browning the onion spoils the pale, delicate color.
  • Boiling hard splits the coconut milk.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with appam or rice.
  • Add sliced tomato for color.

Substitutions

  • Prawns also work.
  • Fish fillets cook in 6 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Appam or steamed rice
  • Soup: Cucumber salad
  • Dessert: Payasam
  • Beverage: Black tea
