Kerala Meen Moilee (Fish Molee)

Meen moilee, fish in a mild coconut milk stew with ginger, green chili and curry leaves, is a Kerala specialty associated with the Syrian Christian and Portuguese communities.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 20 min
  • Difficulty Easy
  • Draft India Seafood LunchDinnerBraise / stewCurrySpicy

  • 50 g3 tbspGinger and green chilies
  • 150 g5 ozOnion
  • 3 g1 tspTurmeric
  • 15 g1/2 ozCurry leaves
  • 15 ml1 tbspLime juice
  • 45 ml3 tbspCoconut oil
  • 5 g1 tspSalt

Method

  1. Fry the onion, ginger and chilies in the coconut oil over medium heat 5 minutes until soft but not brown.
  2. Add the turmeric and coconut milk and bring to a gentle simmer.
  3. Slide in the fish and simmer 8 minutes without boiling.
  4. Season with salt.
  5. Finish with curry leaves and lime juice.

Common mistakes

  • Browning the onion spoils the pale, delicate color.
  • Boiling hard splits the coconut milk.

Authentic tips

  • Serve with appam or rice.
  • Add sliced tomato for color.

Substitutions

  • Prawns also work.
  • Fish fillets cook in 6 minutes.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Appam or steamed rice
  • Soup: Cucumber salad
  • Dessert: Payasam
  • Beverage: Black tea
