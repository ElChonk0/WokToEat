Hǔ Pí Qīng Jiāo (Tiger-Skin Peppers) 虎皮青椒

▾

Surprise me with

Tiger-skin peppers are green peppers pan-blistered until wrinkled and braised in a savory sauce; the dish is a Sichuan and Hunan home staple.

  • Cook 10 min

Method

  1. Wash and dry the peppers thoroughly; leave them whole and prick each once with a knife tip so they do not burst.
  2. Heat a dry or lightly oiled heavy pan over high heat and cook the peppers 6-8 minutes, turning, until the skins blister and wrinkle.
  3. Press each pepper flat with a spatula so it chars on all sides.
  4. Add the garlic, black beans, soy sauce, vinegar and sugar and toss 1 minute until the peppers are coated.

Common mistakes

  • A low heat steams the peppers instead of blistering them.
  • Wet peppers spatter badly in hot oil; dry them well.

Authentic tips

  • Use thin, mild peppers (shishito or Anaheim work).
  • Serve warm or at room temperature.

Substitutions

  • Shishito peppers replace the long green peppers.
  • Add chunks of eggplant.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; good cold.

Freezing: Not recommended.
