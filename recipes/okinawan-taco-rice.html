Okinawan Taco Rice タコライス

▾

Surprise me with

Taco rice is an Okinawan dish of spiced ground beef served over rice with lettuce, tomato and cheese, created in the 1980s near American military bases.

Method

  1. Heat the oil in a large pan over medium-high heat and fry the onion for 4 minutes until soft, then the garlic for 30 seconds.
  2. Add the beef and cook for 6 to 8 minutes, breaking it up, until browned and no pink remains.
  3. Add the chili powder and cumin and stir for 1 minute, then the ketchup, Worcestershire sauce, soy sauce and salt.
  4. Simmer for 3 to 4 minutes until the sauce clings and the mixture is moist but not wet.
  5. Divide the rice into bowls and top with the lettuce, tomato and beef, then the cheese and hot sauce.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Leaving the meat in big lumps gives uneven texture; break it up as it browns.
  • Adding the lettuce while the beef is very hot wilts it; keep it cool.

Authentic tips

  • Use a mild chili powder for a family-friendly dish, or add more for heat.
  • A fried egg on top is common.

Substitutions

  • Ground pork or chicken replaces beef.
  • Corn chips replace rice for a taco salad.

Storage

Leftovers: The meat keeps 4 days in the refrigerator; assemble fresh.

Freezing: The cooked meat freezes for 3 months.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Avocado slices or salsa
  • Soup: Clear corn soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Iced tea
