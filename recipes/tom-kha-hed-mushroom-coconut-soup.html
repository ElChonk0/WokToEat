Tom Kha Hed (Mushroom Coconut Soup)

▾

Surprise me with

Tom kha, a coconut milk soup with galangal, is a central Thai favorite; the mushroom version is a staple at vegetarian restaurants.

Method

  1. Bring the stock and coconut milk to a gentle simmer in a pot with the galangal, lemongrass, lime leaves and shallots. Simmer for 5 minutes to infuse. Tom kha means 'galangal soup', so be generous with it.
  2. Add the mushrooms and simmer for 3 to 4 minutes until tender. Keep it at a gentle simmer; boiling hard makes coconut milk split.
  3. Season with soy sauce, salt and sugar.
  4. Turn off the heat and add the chilies and lime juice. The soup should be creamy and fragrant, sour first, then salty, with a little heat.
  5. Ladle into bowls and top with cilantro. The galangal and lemongrass are left in for aroma but not eaten.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard splits the coconut milk.
  • Adding the lime while boiling makes it bitter.

Authentic tips

  • Straw or oyster mushrooms are traditional.
  • Do not eat the aromatics.

Substitutions

  • Straw or oyster mushrooms are traditional.
  • Tofu cubes add protein.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat gently.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Jasmine rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
