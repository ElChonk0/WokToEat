Tom Kha Hed (Mushroom Coconut Soup)

▾

Surprise me with

Tom kha, a coconut milk soup with galangal, is a central Thai favorite; the mushroom version is a staple at vegetarian restaurants.

  • Cook 15 min

  • 300 g10 ozMixed mushrooms
  • 400 ml1.75 cupsCoconut milk
  • 60 g2 ozGalangal, lemongrass, lime leaves
  • 400 ml1.75 cupsVegetable stock
  • 15 g1/2 ozChilies
  • 15 ml1 tbspSoy sauce
  • 45 ml3 tbspLime juice
  • 15 g1/2 ozCilantro

Method

  1. Simmer the galangal, lemongrass and lime leaves in the stock for 8 minutes.
  2. Add the coconut milk and mushrooms and simmer gently 5 minutes.
  3. Season with the soy sauce.
  4. Take off the heat and stir in the lime juice.
  5. Garnish with chilies and cilantro.

Common mistakes

  • Boiling hard splits the coconut milk.
  • Adding the lime while boiling makes it bitter.

Authentic tips

  • Straw or oyster mushrooms are traditional.
  • Do not eat the aromatics.

Substitutions

  • Straw or oyster mushrooms are traditional.
  • Tofu cubes add protein.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days refrigerated; reheat gently.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Jasmine rice
  • Soup: Not needed; this is the soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Thai iced tea
