Okonomiyaki (Osaka-Style Pork Pancake) お好み焼き

▾

Surprise me with

Okonomiyaki is a savory Japanese pancake of flour batter, shredded cabbage and toppings such as pork belly, cooked on a griddle and finished with sauce, mayonnaise and bonito flakes; the Osaka style mixes everything into the batter.

  • 2 g2 tspAonori (green seaweed flakes)

Method

  1. Whisk the flour with a pinch of salt and baking powder, then whisk in the dashi until smooth. Rest the batter in the refrigerator for at least 1 hour; resting relaxes the gluten so the pancake stays tender. Grated nagaimo, if you have it, makes it extra fluffy.
  2. Shred the cabbage finely and pat it dry. Just before cooking, fold the eggs, tenkasu, pickled ginger and green onion into the batter, then the cabbage a third at a time, mixing in air.
  3. Heat a little oil in a large skillet or griddle over medium heat. Spoon in half the batter and shape it into a round about 2 cm (3/4 in) thick, without pressing it down. Lay half the pork slices on top.
  4. Cover and cook over medium-low heat for 5 minutes, until the bottom is golden. Flip it with two spatulas, cover again and cook 5 minutes until the pork is crisp, then flip once more and cook uncovered for 2 minutes. Never press it flat; that squeezes out the air and makes it dense.
  5. Brush with okonomiyaki sauce, zigzag with mayonnaise and scatter with bonito flakes and aonori. Repeat and serve hot.

Cook’s notes

Written independently, inspired by dishes featured on the Get Fed series (Overkill Singapore) and on Alderic's channel.

Common mistakes

  • Pressing the pancake flat squeezes out the air and makes it dense; shape it gently.
  • Cooking over high heat browns the outside before the center cooks; keep it at medium.

Authentic tips

  • Use a lid to steam the center so it cooks through.
  • Squeeze the mayonnaise in a lattice for the classic look.

Substitutions

  • Shrimp or squid replaces pork.
  • Bacon replaces pork belly.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 2 days in the refrigerator; reheat in a pan.

Freezing: Freezes for 1 month without toppings.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Simple cucumber salad
  • Soup: Miso soup
  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
  • Beverage: Cold beer or iced green tea
