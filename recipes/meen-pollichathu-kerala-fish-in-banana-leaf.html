Meen Pollichathu (Kerala Fish in Banana Leaf)

▾

Surprise me with

Meen pollichathu, fish with a spicy masala wrapped in banana leaf and pan-grilled, is a Kerala backwater specialty.

Method

  1. Fry the shallot, ginger, garlic, chili powder, turmeric and tomato in the coconut oil for 8 minutes into a thick masala; add the tamarind.
  2. Soften the banana leaves by dipping them in boiling water for 30 seconds or laying them briefly in a hot dry skillet.
  3. Score the fish and coat it with the masala inside and out.
  4. Wrap the fish with curry leaves in the leaves and tie.
  5. Pan-grill 10 minutes per side over medium heat.

Common mistakes

  • Unsoftened leaves tear.
  • High heat burns the packet.

Authentic tips

  • Foil is a substitute for leaves.
  • Pearl spot (karimeen) is traditional.

Substitutions

  • Foil replaces banana leaves.
  • Any whole fish, such as sea bream, works.
  • No grill: wrap the packets in foil and bake at 200°C (400°F) for 20 to 25 minutes, turning once.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh; keeps 1 day.

Freezing: Not recommended.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice
  • Soup: Cucumber salad
  • Dessert: Payasam
  • Beverage: Black tea
