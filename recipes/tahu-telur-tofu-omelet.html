Tahu Telur (Tofu Omelet with Peanut Sauce)

▾

Surprise me with

Tahu telur, a tofu and egg omelet with peanut sauce, is a street food of Surabaya and East Java.

  • Prep 15 min
  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 300 g10 ozFirm tofu, crumbled
  • 44Eggs
  • 100 g3.5 ozBean sprouts
  • 30 g2 tbspScallion
  • 120 ml1/2 cupPeanut sauce
  • 30 ml2 tbspKecap manis
  • 45 ml3 tbspNeutral oil

Method

  1. Mix the crumbled tofu, eggs, bean sprouts and scallion with a pinch of salt.
  2. Heat the oil in a nonstick pan over medium heat.
  3. Pour in the mixture and cook 4 minutes until golden underneath.
  4. Flip and cook 3 minutes more.
  5. Slice and top with the peanut sauce and kecap manis.

Common mistakes

  • High heat browns outside before the inside sets.
  • Wet tofu makes a soggy omelet; drain it.

Authentic tips

  • A Surabaya street food.
  • Serve with chili.

Substitutions

  • Peanut butter plus kecap manis replaces peanut sauce.
  • Add bean sprouts for crunch.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.
