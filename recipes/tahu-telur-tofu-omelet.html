Tahu Telur (Tofu Omelet with Peanut Sauce)

▾

Surprise me with

Tahu telur, a tofu and egg omelet with peanut sauce, is a street food of Surabaya and East Java.

  • Cook 10 min
  • Difficulty Easy

  • 15 g2 tbspFried shallots

Method

  1. Make the sauce: pound the garlic and chilies, then mix with the peanuts, kecap manis, hot water and lime juice into a thick, sweet peanut sauce.
  2. Beat the eggs with the salt, pepper and green onion, then fold in the tofu.
  3. Heat the oil in a small skillet over medium-high heat and pour in the mixture. Cook for 3 to 4 minutes, pushing in the edges, until the bottom is golden and the omelet puffs and turns crisp at the edges.
  4. Flip it with a plate and cook for 2 minutes more until golden.
  5. Slide onto a plate, top with bean sprouts and cucumber, pour the peanut sauce over and scatter with fried shallots. An East Javanese street dish.

Common mistakes

  • High heat browns outside before the inside sets.
  • Wet tofu makes a soggy omelet; drain it.

Authentic tips

  • A Surabaya street food.
  • Serve with chili.

Substitutions

  • Peanut butter plus kecap manis replaces peanut sauce.
  • Add bean sprouts for crunch.

Storage

Leftovers: Best fresh.

Freezing: Not recommended.

  • Dessert: Fresh fruit
