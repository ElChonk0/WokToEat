Bo Nuong Sa (Grilled Lemongrass Beef)

▾

Surprise me with

A staple of Vietnamese grilling, bo nuong sa marinates thin beef slices in lemongrass and fish sauce before quick grilling, often wrapped in rice paper with herbs and vermicelli or served over rice.

  • Prep 20 min (plus 2 hr marinating)
  • Cook 10 min

Method

  1. Combine lemongrass, garlic, shallot, fish sauce, sugar, and sesame oil for the marinade.
  2. Coat beef slices thoroughly and marinate at least 2 hours, or overnight for best flavor.
  3. Grill or pan-sear over high heat, 1–2 minutes per side, until charred and cooked through.
  4. Serve over rice or wrapped in rice paper with vermicelli and fresh herbs.

Common mistakes

  • Mincing lemongrass too coarsely, which leaves tough, fibrous pieces in the finished dish.
  • Overcooking the thin slices, which toughens them quickly given how fast they cook.

Authentic tips

  • Use only the tender inner portion of the lemongrass stalk, near the base, and mince it very finely for the best texture.
  • Very high heat and brief cooking are essential for thin slices — they only need a minute or two per side.

Substitutions

  • Pork works well with the same marinade and method.
  • No grill: a very hot pan or broiler both work as alternatives.

Storage

Leftovers: Keeps 3 days refrigerated.

Freezing: Freezes well, marinated and raw, for up to a month.

Serve with

  • Side dish: Steamed rice or vermicelli with herbs
  • Soup: Canh
  • Dessert: Che
  • Beverage: Cold beer
